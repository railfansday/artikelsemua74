Sources indicate that artificial intelligence website builder 10Web Inc. is introducing a new feature that it claims can automatically turn content from Instagram, TikTok, Etsy, Google Business Profiles and more than 30 other sources into functioning websites and online stores. (Related: Google files patent for AI-generated TV channel that us...)

Called Start from Anything, the feature targets small businesses that lack the time and expertise to build their own websites. Owners can paste a link to an existing online presence or upload files, and 10Web’s AI extracts the information needed to construct a website. (Related: SpaceX wants to build AI data centers in space. Will it...)

It has been revealed that the system can convert photos and videos into galleries and product pages, use captions to populate product descriptions, and turn service information into dedicated pages. Business hours, addresses and location information can automatically populate contact pages, while social media posts can become news items and promotional offers.

10Web remarked its software also determines what functionality a website needs. Businesses offering services automatically receive booking capabilities, while those selling merchandise get online stores equipped to process orders and payments. Customers can subsequently modify their websites by conversing with AI.

“Nobody opens a bakery because they love choosing plugins,” Chief Executive Narek Torosyan stated in a statement. “Every question we ask an owner about software is a question we failed to answer ourselves.”.

Industry observers note that the service departs from the prompt-based approach that has dominated generative AI website development. Although prompts can create attractive websites, they require users to describe their businesses and often produce generic content that must subsequently be replaced or customized.

10Web’s approach is to reuse information businesses have already accumulated, such as photos, customer-facing descriptions, product catalogs and promotional materials.

Sources indicate that the company remarked it tested the technology using publicly available content, converting a concept store‘s Instagram presence and a vintage retailer‘s TikTok account into online stores. It also generated websites from a vacation property‘s Google profile and a small business’s Facebook page.

The newly introduced capability builds on 10Web’s existing generative AI platform, which creates and manages websites using the open-source WordPress content management system.

In May 2025, the manufacturer introduced an application programming interface that allows developers and software providers to incorporate AI website generation into their applications. The platform uses specialized AI agents to handle different aspects of website development, including design, content creation and functionality.

10Web stated the new feature includes hosting and is free to use for building and launching websites. The announcement did not specify charges for longer-term operation.

The organization noted it serves tens of thousands of customers worldwide and attracts between 50,000 and 60,000 upcoming signups monthly.

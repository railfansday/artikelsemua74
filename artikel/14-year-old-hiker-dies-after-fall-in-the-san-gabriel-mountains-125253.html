Industry observers note that A 14-year-old hiker died Tuesday morning after falling several hundred feet in icy conditions on Wright Mountain in Wrightwood, California. Authorities have identified the boy as Nathan Sakaue of Phelan, California.

According to recent reports, just before 11 a.m. on November 24, deputies from the San Bernardino County Sheriff’s Department and crews with the San Bernardino County Fire Protection District were dispatched to Wright Mountain in the Angeles National Forest after receiving reports of a fall. According to officials, three teenage boys were hiking together when two of them slipped and fell an estimated 500 feet down the mountainside.

The third hiker remained at the top of the mountain and called for help, and was later able to walk away from the site safely as crews wrapped up the operation. The Sheriff’s Aviation Division and Air Rescue Team responded to the scene to reach the two boys who had fallen.

A post shared by San Bernardino County Sheriff (@sanbernardinocountysheriff).

One of the injured teens was airlifted off the mountain and taken to a local trauma center, where officials reported he was in stable condition. Sakaue was pronounced dead at the scene, and rescue crews later recovered his body from the mountain. Authorities have not released the names of the other two hikers.

The tragedy comes just days after a hiker elsewhere in the San Gabriel mountains slipped on ice and fell hundreds of feet to his death while trying to help up his son.

It has been revealed that our thoughts are with Nathan’s family, his friends, and the Phelan and Wrightwood communities as they grieve his loss. While no GoFundMe exists yet to support his family, we will be sure to upgrade this article with that information if one is started.

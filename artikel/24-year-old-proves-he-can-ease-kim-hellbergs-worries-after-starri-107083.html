According to recent reports, middlesbrough maintained their place at the top of the Championship table on Tuesday night as they defeated managerless Swansea City. It marked back-to-back wins for Boro after the return from the international break, following a 2-0 triumph over Bristol City on Saturday.

Sources indicate that kim Hellberg’s side won 2-1 against the Swans, with Myles Peart-Harris opening the scoring before Joseph Opoku’s equaliser. Star striker Will Lankshear was back amongst the goals to net the second and secure the three points.

Sources indicate that as well as scoring the first, midfielder Peart-Harris set up Lankshear’s winner. The 24-year-old started for Middlesbrough and his performance will have come as reassurance for Hellberg given his recent midfield blows.

According to recent reports, earlier this week, Hellberg expressed a desire to reinforce his midfield in the January transfer window. Middlesbrough have lost Rokas Pukstas and Connor Barron to injury, meaning that the only natural players in a deeper role are usual starters Aidan Morris and Sebastian Berhalter. According to recent reports, peart-Harris, like Riley McGree, can and has played in a deeper role, but is more effective further forward. However, the former Oxford United and Brentford man revealed his quality alongside Morris against Swansea City.

Sources indicate that hellberg’s tactics mean Peart-Harris was able to progress into advanced areas. He was lurking in the 18-yard box before being found by Neto Borges’ pull-back and cleverly side-footing into the bottom corner.

Recent findings suggest that for Lankshear’s goal, Peart-Harris just about beat the Swans’ offside trap and ran through before squaring it for the striker to convert. According to recent reports, he was able to progress the ball with strong running and forward-thinking passing when in deeper areas. Peart-Harris spent much of the game operating on the right-hand side of the middle third of the pitch, and he was effective for Boro.

Hellberg’s lack of depth in midfield is a justified concern, especially coming into the hectic festive schedule. However, Peart-Harris’ performance against Swansea City indicated he is ready to step in for Middlesbrough when Morris and Berhalter need a rest.

It was his best display in a Boro shirt to date, and if he can maintain a high level, he’ll be a valuable squad player to have throughout a demanding 2026/27 promotion push.

The performance doesn’t decrease the need for another midfielder, but it highlights that Hellberg should be able to deal with the shortage until the opportunity to sign a reinforcement arises.

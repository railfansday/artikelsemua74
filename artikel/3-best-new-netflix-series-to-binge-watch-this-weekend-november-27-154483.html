Always On Call is a brand upcoming medical drama series that just premiered on Netflix on Thursday, November 26. Consisting of just seven episodes, Always On Call follows a brilliant yet complicated surgeon named Sofia who is assigned to one of the most chaotic emergency rooms in her city. Determined to make an immediate impact and improve her professional reputation, Sofia navigates the endless stream of medical emergencies while forming a tight-knit bond with her new coworkers. Always On Call is the perfect weekend binge on Netflix this weekend for fans of The Pitt and Grey's Anatomy and is already being called one of Netflix's best newly introduced medical dramas by viewers. Featuring a talented Norwegian cast, Always On Call has flown under the radar since its global premiere on Thanksgiving but is positioned to become a new medical drama franchise for the streamer. Full of human emotion and intense situations, Always On Call is worth a quick 7-part binge on Netflix right now.

The Trials of Karen Read is officially the biggest new streaming hit on Netflix heading into the holiday weekend, right now ranking #2 worldwide following its premiere earlier this week. The 3-part true crime series examines the death of Boston police officer John O'Keefe and the controversial murder trial of his former girlfriend, Karen Read. The Trials of Karen Read refers to Read's two murder trials, the first ending in a hung jury in 2024, and the second resulting in her being acquitted of several charges, including second-degree murder.

Recent findings suggest that the Trials of Karen Read is the newest viral true crime docuseries on Netflix following Taken: Natalee Holloway, which was previously the #1 most popular series on the platform earlier this month. The upcoming Netflix docuseries has a movie-length runtime of 2 hours and 25 minutes, making it extremely easy to start and finish in one night. Don't miss out on Netflix's latest streaming sensation this weekend.

The Hunting Wives just returned with its highly anticipated second season on Thursday, November 26, making it the best fresh show to binge on Netflix this weekend. The erotic mystery thriller follows the tension-fueled dynamic between Sophie O'Neil, played by Brittany Snow, and her new socialite friend Margo Banks, played by Malin Akerman, after she moves to East Texas. Margo pulls Sophie into a twisted secret world of seduction and even murder, centered on soapy twists, high-stakes affairs, and full-blown obsession.

It has been revealed that there's no denying that Harry Potter continues to be a phenomenon. After seven bestsellers translated into multiple languages, years of extravagant midnight release parties, eight significant-budget films, a stage play, and theme parks, there's still enormous hype for both the upcoming HBO television show and an all-star re-recording of the audiobooks. Clearly, there is something about the boy who lived and his world that draws people in and keeps them captivated. All of this begs one very important question, however: how good are the Harry Potter books, really?

The answer to that question is, ultimately, a matter of personal taste, but the books do have their faults, from skimming mythology and using, at best, insensitive naming conventions to treating serious issues as fodder for gags and jokes. There's also the perennial question of whether the artist can be separated from their art. The good news is that there's a lot of fantasy in the world waiting to be read.

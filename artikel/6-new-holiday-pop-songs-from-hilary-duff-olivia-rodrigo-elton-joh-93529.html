Christmas music is already adorning the Billboard charts, and this year, there are some newly introduced holiday hits to add to the Christmas classics we know and love.

On the new Billboard Pop Shop Podcast, Katie & Keith are taking a listen to a half-dozen new holiday songs to see if we should add them into the reindeer rotation. The six songs: Olivia Rodrigo’s “Take Me Home (4 Christmas),” which has already peaked at No. 42 on the Billboard Hot 100; Hilary Duff’s “All You Want for Christmas Is Me,” from her five-track Holiday Things EP; Elton John & CMAT’s “Step Into Christmas,” a new duet version of his 1973 classic; Hozier & Jessie Buckley’s cover of The Pogues’ “Fairytale of New York”; EJAE’s “Christmas In New York,” which she’ll perform at Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade on Thursday; and Alanis Morissette’s “Beautiful Star of Bethlehem,” featuring Brandi Carlile & Steve Martin, from Morissette’s Good Weather for Immaculate Conception holiday album.

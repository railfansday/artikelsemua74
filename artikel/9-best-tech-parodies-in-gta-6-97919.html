Recent findings suggest that the Grand Theft Auto series tends to be a parody of real life, with there being loads of satirical comedy woven throughout the many fibers of each game. This trend continues in Grand Theft Auto 6, with there being loads of hilarious parodies of well-known companies, television reveals, radio broadcasts, and even tech.

Tech plays a significant role in the game, as you use your phone for many things, such as ordering a ride on the RydeMe app, or even bucking yourself using the aptly named BuckMe app. (Related: Industry News: How to train a new language model from s...)

You are going to be relying on a lot of tech companies throughout GTA 6, so it is obvious that there will be a number of jokes surrounding them.

Texting plays a huge role in real life. After all, you text your friends, family members, and even coworkers about what is going on in your life. The same is done in GTA 6, and the game captures texting quite well, with both protagonists texting one another and various people about jobs. (Related: Breaking: Simple considerations for simple people build...)

While you may normally use the popular WhatsApp application to get your fill of communication, the people of GTA 6 use a slightly different app called WhatUp. This is a very obvious parody of WhatsApp, with a name that is almost identical.

Even the user interface is almost identical, with it featuring a very minimalist style showing the avatar or profile picture of each contact next to the messages.

Amazon is a go-to tech and shipping company for many people nowadays. While we all may complain about capitalism, there is no denying the convenience of having everything at your fingertips through the use of an app that has next-day shipping.

In GTA, the citizens of Leonida are using a slightly different company that is a clear parody of Amazon, and it is called Sahara. You can find boxes scattered around that mimic the exact shipping tape used by the company, the logo is very similar, and there is even Sahara Mirage, which is meant to be a parody of Amazon Prime.

In a recent development, money makes the world go ‘round in real life, and it especially makes the world go ‘round in GTA 6. You are on a constant mission to collect money in the game, which in a way mimics real life. Anyway when you are handling money in real life, you might use a banking app, or you might use CashApp or Venmo.

GTA 6 has an app that parodies these real-life tools and it is called BuckMe - a hilarious name that has various uses, such as when you give yourself money, which is called bucking yourself.

This app is the go-to banking app that you get at the start of the game because you can’t afford to open a real checking account and it is just perfect.

Sources indicate that did you know that you can play games within GTA 6? Odds are you probably did, and it is thanks to Cal. He so graciously installs the newly introduced JoyStation in Jason’s home early in the game, allowing you to catch up on your gaming within your game.

The JoyStation is an obvious parody of the PlayStation 5, with the console looking almost identical to the real-life system you are probably playing GTA 6 on. Even the user interface looks extremely close to the real-life version.

The console isn’t just for looks, as it can be used to play some real games, with some of them even being easter eggs for real-life movies and games.

In a recent development, when you need to get around the city in real life, you either rely on your car to get you from point A to point B, or you rely on someone else’s car. This can come in the form of a ride that you hire via a rideshare app like Lyft or Uber.

Just like in real life, you can get from point to point in GTA 6 by using the convenient ridesharing app aptly called RydeMe. It even comes complete with a Y instead of an I so you know it was made by the tech bros in Silicon Valley.

The app is great, as it lets you fast travel around the city. If only you could skip the unwanted conversation with the driver in real life. At least the prices match up with the inflated real-life equivalent.

It has been revealed that doomscrolling has become the norm lately, as apps like YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok all offer short-form content that you can easily digest and swipe away. Luckily, GTA 6 hasn’t gone that far… yet.

Sources indicate that while you can’t doomscroll reels in GTA 6, you can flip through tons of posts thanks to the improved Snapmatic app. This app made an appearance in GTA 5, as it was used to take pictures.

In a recent development, now, you can use it to scroll through countless posts about how we got something else before RAC 7. You can even read the comments, and those are something else entirely, so be prepared.

Industry observers note that dating in real life has been boiled down to a few different dating apps, but if you are looking to hook up with someone, you are probably going to use Tinder. Of course, you could try FarmersOnly, but to each their own.

In a recent development, sadly, you can’t get the experience of using a dating app in GTA 6, but you can help out Cal. Early in the game, you get to see the in-game version of Tinder, as Cal hands you his phone and you get to swipe right or left on various dating profiles.

The app is pretty accurate to the real-life equivalent, complete with cringy profiles, horrible pictures, and people with way too high of standards.

According to recent reports, there has been a rise of people who are vaping, and as someone who used to vape all the time, it is sad to see how much it has grown at times. Vaping has infected the world of Leonida as well, but one in particular that is worth pointing out is Manifestick.

This hilarious parody is meant to replace anxiety pens and mock those who wear a vape on a leash around their neck, as there are women who use them like they are addicted to them.

Oh wait, that is because they are addicted. You can discover that these vape pens are laced with cocaine, making them not so friendly and very addictive like the real-life nicotine infused sticks.

Cryptocurrency is everywhere nowadays. There are people who swear on Bitcoin, and we see a meme coin pop up almost every day. We have all heard stories of people being scammed because someone did a rug pull.

Industry observers note that throughout the game, you will interact with a company called AudaCity, and there is even a character named Papa Audacity. This company is creating DacityCoin, and you are meant to promote it with Redbull-style stunts that push the coin’s value up.

The protagonists even call out how the coin is a scam, but that doesn’t mean you can’t get in on the ground floor and be one of the few who profits off of everyone else’s losses.

The Swans’ unassailable form at the start of the season has waned, but they have given a good account of themselves under the interim management of Ashley Williams after Vitor Matos’ exit.

After a 2-2 draw with West Brom last weekend, the South Welsh side were narrowly beaten 2-1 by Southampton in midweek. Williams is set to remain in charge for this weekend’s game, but talks to appoint Freyr Alexandersson as the fresh head coach are progressing.

According to recent reports, as for Cardiff City, they come into the crucial game off the back of a heavy midweek defeat.

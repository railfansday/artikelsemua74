According to recent reports, machines without a GPU still serve and record, but do not train. Tried Songe on another setup? Share the result on the testing board.

In a recent development, with auto-dream on, Dreams start when your agent has been quiet for a while. Or press Dream in the dashboard (songe open). A Dream never just disappears: it keeps going on the page, or states why it stopped.

In a recent development, before the first training Songe measures how much your computer can learn at once, and trains on what fits. See DESIGN.md.

uv tool upgrade songe, pipx upgrade songe or brew upgrade songe: the service moves to the new version by itself in a quiet moment.

songe uninstall stops the service and restores your agent settings; add --purge to also delete its data and learned versions. Run it before brew uninstall songe.

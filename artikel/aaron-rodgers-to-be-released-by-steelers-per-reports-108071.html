Aaron Rodgers' time with the Pittsburgh Steelers has about to officially come to an end following his benching and comments on Wednesday's episode of "The Pat McAfee Show.".

Sources indicate that according to Jordan Schultz and Pittsburgh Post-Gazette's Gerry Dulac, the team will be releasing the soon-to-be 43-year-old quarterback once his contract situation is finalized. Rodgers signed a one-year, $22.5 million deal in May.

The situation went from the Steelers announcing late Tuesday evening that Rodgers would be benched to him airing his feelings about Mike McCarthy less than 24 hours later.

On "McAfee," Rodgers claimed that McCarthy fell asleep and snored during a quarterbacks meeting after arriving late. He also said that the Steelers head coach broke down the team during halftime of Sunday's loss to the Philadelphia Eagles by saying, "Alright, 1-2-3-Packers!". The four-time NFL MVP added that he found out he was being benched in favor of Mason Rudolph from Steelers' tight end Pat Freiermuth.

"I was talking to Muth and a couple of the guys, and he mentioned, 'I can't believe they're doing this.' And I said, 'Doing what?' I didn't know," Rodgers said. "And he told me, and obviously, there had been a lot of things in motion at that point. I didn't hear from Mike [McCarthy].". (Related: Tech Report: Think AI "knows" what it’s doing? Scientis...)

"Mike and I talked for a long time the last few days, and that word [benching] never came up.".

Recent findings suggest that what comes next for Rodgers? That's to be determined. He noted on "McAfee" that the "ship has sailed" in regards to a return to the Steelers. Now, he will be free agent once he clear waivers.

Rodgers had already declared in May that the 2026 season would be his last in the NFL.

In 10 games this season, Rodgers has thrown for 2,241 yards, 16 touchdowns and 6 interceptions.

Vasilije Adžić has undergone knee surgery, and the Juventus loanee could miss the rest of the season with Sassuolo.

Sources indicate that according to a statement from Juventus, the 20-year-old Montenegro international suffered a sprain and bruising to his right knee in Montenegro’s Nations League game against Cyprus on 15 November. Tests revealed “a small osteochondral detachment”, meaning a piece of bone and cartilage had come away inside the joint. (Related: Steam is pitting 10 years of Game of the Year winners a...)

Adžić had surgery on Thursday at the Hôpital Privé Jean Mermoz in Lyon, where Juventus captain Manuel Locatelli was also operated on in September. He has now started the rehabilitation programme “aimed at returning to competitive activity”.

Neither Juventus nor Sassuolo have given a timeframe for his recovery. However, TuttoMercatoWeb suggests the need for surgery points to a lengthy absence, with a real risk he won’t play again before the end of the 2026-27 season. It’s a cruel blow for a player who started his loan in style. Adžić scored in three consecutive Serie A games in September, including a curling winner off the bench in Sassuolo’s 3-2 victory over Juventus. His form has tailed off since then, and he has three goals and one assist in nine league appearances.

The injury could also affect his future. Sassuolo paid €500,000 to take him on loan in the summer and included an option to buy fixed at €12m, while Juventus kept a buy-back clause. A long spell out leaves both clubs with little to go on when they decide his future next summer.

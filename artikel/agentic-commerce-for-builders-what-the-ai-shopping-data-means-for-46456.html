Two open protocols are worth knowing.

Sources indicate that agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP). OpenAI and Stripe released it on 29 September 2025 alongside Instant Checkout, starting with U.S. Etsy sellers. [9] The merchant stays merchant of record; orders, payments and fulfillment run through the merchant's existing systems. [9] OpenAI's merchant page now describes ACP as supporting "product discovery and more accurate, personalized recommendations through shared product data", and indicates it is "moving away from a standalone Instant Checkout experience", with purchases completed on merchant websites or apps. [8].

Industry observers note that universal Commerce Protocol (UCP). Google announced it on 11 January 2026, co-developed with Shopify, Etsy, Wayfair, Target and Walmart. It is meant to power checkout on eligible listings in AI Mode in Search and the Gemini app, with the retailer remaining seller of record. [10].

So the pattern in late 2026: the agent protocols exist, but in ChatGPT the live value is discovery from shared product data, and the transaction lands on your site. If you're prioritizing work, feed quality comes before checkout plumbing.

Sources indicate that the platforms spell this out. OpenAI invites merchants to share product feeds and says richer, up-to-date data helps products surface with more accurate information. It also says a feed isn't required if ChatGPT already crawls your site, but feeds give more control. [8] Shopify generates a catalog for AI channels automatically and lists what helps: clear titles, full-sentence descriptions, technical details, sizing and care information, public return policies, and reviews. Sources indicate that I call the resulting dynamic the Citation Economy, a term I coined: search made us compete for clicks, AI makes us compete for citations. The path is cited, compared, chosen, bought. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), making pages and product data easy for AI assistants to read, trust and cite, is just SEO with a new reader: the model.

A hypothetical German online jewelry shop gets this kind of request through an assistant: a minimalist gold necklace, hypoallergenic, within a budget, delivered before a birthday. No brand named.

According to recent reports, A product record that only carries title: "Necklace gold" gives the model nothing to match. One that states metal, plating, allergy information, delivery time and return policy can be matched to every constraint in the prompt, and to the follow-up ("which is better for sensitive skin?") if that answer is on the page. Same product, very different odds of being cited.

Shopify recommends tracking AI referrals separately from organic search, using custom filters or reports in GA4. [7] A simple starting point is a custom channel group or exploration filter on session source matching the assistants' referrer domains, for example chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai and gemini.google.com. Then compare conversion rate and revenue per session against organic search.

Sources indicate that add one manual step: once a month, ask the assistants your key buyer questions by hand and log whether your products are cited. ChatGPT's shopping features are U.S.-only for now [8], so test in the markets you actually sell in.

OpenAI began testing ads in ChatGPT in the U.S. on 9 February 2026, matched to conversation topic, and says ads don't influence answers. According to recent reports, for developers the useful reframe is this: AI shopping in 2026 is less an integration project and more a data-quality project. Ship clean, complete, consistent product data, publish the answers shoppers ask for, and instrument AI referrals as a first-class channel. Checkout can follow when the platforms and your numbers say it's worth it.

SEOUL, South Korea, Nov. 26, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- AIPro, an AI video analytics company, is expanding the use of its CCTV analytics technology to address site-specific safety challenges at public facilities and power plants. From software that detects signs of fires and wildfires to a system that detects people in a dam's downstream discharge zone, the company provides solutions tailored to each site's needs. According to AIPro, the tech giant has supplied fire and wildfire detection software to the Korea Asset Management Corporation (KAMCO) Human Resources Development Institute and VistaPro-FDS-002, a fire detection solution designated as an innovative product, to the Korea Gas Safety Corporation. At Goesan Hydropower Plant, operated by Korea Hydro & Nuclear Power, the company supplied a system that detects people in the dam's downstream discharge zone. Each deployment uses CCTV video analytics for a distinct safety management objective. In fire monitoring, the focus is on detecting signs of fire, such as flames and smoke, in CCTV footage; in the discharge zone, the focus is on detecting the presence of people. The organization explained that it tailors its video analytics capabilities to the monitoring requirements and operational objectives of each site.

Sources indicate that vistaPro, the firm's platform for enterprises and institutions, supports flame and smoke detection, people counting, and detection of people approaching or entering restricted areas, depending on the site configuration. AI-powered analysis helps managers assess on-site conditions and determine whether a response is needed. The platform is designed to reduce the workload of managers who must continuously monitor multiple CCTV feeds.

In a recent development, AIPro was founded in 2020 by Professor Chun-Su Park of Sungkyunkwan University. It translates research in computer vision and image processing into products that support safety management and operations at public facilities and industrial sites. The enterprise and institutional version of VistaPro has earned Grade 1 Good Software (GS) certification, and VistaPro-FDS-002 has been designated as an innovative product by the Public Procurement Service. The company's AI video analytics models have also undergone performance testing by the Telecommunications Technology Association (TTA).

The company's research and development efforts focus on improving both detection performance and operational efficiency. Building on its patented technology for improving the computational efficiency of video surveillance systems, the company continues to develop products that account for on-site imaging conditions and the computational load on hardware.

Through the "Super-Gap Startup 1000+" program, the organization is currently developing an on-device software engine capable of running multiple deep learning models on devices deployed in the field. It is also conducting research on ultra-long-range object detection and low-power optimization. In addition, the company is participating in a project to develop and demonstrate an integrated AI-based system for early fire detection and suppression in livestock barns, gaining practical experience in field operations.

AIPro intends to draw on its experience deploying solutions at public institutions and conducting research and development to improve its products and expand adoption across sites of various sizes. By addressing site-specific needs such as fire monitoring, people counting, and restricted-area management, the organization aims to help people in Korea and abroad adopt video analytics technology suited to their operational needs and scale.

According to recent reports, SINGAPORE, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Pixocial, the organization behind Airbrush, is expanding its web editing capabilities with the launch of AI Image Relighter, bringing one of its popular portrait enhancement features from the mobile app to desktop on Airbrush Web.

Airbrush focuses on natural portrait retouching without over-editing, and Relighter brings that same approach to lighting by brightening dark, underexposed, or gloomy photos in just one click. (Related: Tech Report: AI swarms could hijack democracy without a...)

Flexible Image Relighting: Presets and Intensity Control.

In a recent development, now accessible directly in the browser, Relight makes it easy to recreate the right lighting without complicated manual adjustments. Users simply upload a photo, select the Relight tool, and choose from available presets designed for different photography styles and situations. (Related: Breaking: Influencer “screwed” after AI trick to get fl...)

Recent findings suggest that soft Flash adds a gentle, flattering light to portraits, Cloudy Fix brings a bright, sunny look to photos taken in gloomy weather, and Flashnight creates a brighter, more vibrant mood for nighttime photography. Owners can then adjust the intensity of the effect with the slider, preview the result, and download their edited photo when satisfied with the look.

More Than Relighting: Natural Portrait Optimization.

According to recent reports, beyond simply adding light to a photo, Relight builds on Airbrush's core strength in portrait editing to enhance overall image quality, giving photos a richer, more polished texture. Its intelligent lighting adjustments consider both the subject and the surrounding background, adapting the lighting of the entire environment to create a more balanced and natural result. This helps portraits blend seamlessly into their surroundings, rather than appearing artificially brightened against a dark or mismatched background.

Airbrush Web is also continuing to expand its Relight collection. Upcoming presets will introduce fresh creative lighting effects, including Blue Flash, which creates a cool blue-hour backdrop, and Sunset Flash, which adds the warm tones of a sunset sky. These new options will give users even more ways to experiment with the mood and vibe of their portraits.

Industry observers note that with Relight now available on Airbrush Web, users can enjoy an easier way to restore lighting, enhance portrait quality, and explore different photographic styles directly in their browser. Whether rescuing an underexposed photo or creating an entirely upcoming lighting atmosphere, Airbrush makes it easier to get more out of every shot.

Discover Relight on Airbrush Web at https://airbrush.com/relighter.

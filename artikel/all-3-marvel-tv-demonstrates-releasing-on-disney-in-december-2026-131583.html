Industry observers note that marvel Cinematic Universe fans and viewers interested in general Marvel content outside the shared universe are in luck, as three TV indicates are coming to Disney+ in December with latest episodes, and they are filled with major heroes. December is set to be the most important month of the year for the MCU's slate of projects, and that is saying something.

Sources indicate that after all, Marvel has had many hits in 2026, with MCU TV highlights on Disney+, such as Daredevil: Born Again season 2 and VisionQuest, and the July release of Tom Holland's Spider-Man: Brand Upcoming Day. That film became the 3rd highest-grossing movie of all time with over $2.5 billion, and on December 4, it will return to theaters, with Spider-Man: Brand New Day's re-release including an Avengers: Doomsday scene. After all, this is the first film for Earth's Mightiest Heroes in the Multiverse Saga, bringing back beloved stars like Chris Evans, as Steve Rogers, and Robert Downey Jr., now as Doctor Doom. Industry observers note that while all eyes are on the substantial screen, December will also feature three Marvel TV highlights releasing new episodes on Disney+. Two of them feature major heroes, but exist outside the MCU canon, though in a shared universe of their own, while the other is led by an MCU fan-favorite, and it sets up Avengers: Doomsday. These are the Marvel shows being released on Disney+ in December:.

Marvel’s Iron Man and his Awesome Friends is an animated series for young viewers, helping kids get used to seeing Marvel's superheroes in action. Season 1 will be back with new episodes on the streaming service on December 1, starting the month Downey returns to the MCU with an Iron Man release, which is fitting, even though Marvel’s Iron Man and his Awesome Friends bears no connection to the MCU.

It has been revealed that the Iron Man animated series features a lot of characters from Marvel Comics, though it focuses on a main trio, including Tony Stark/Iron Man, Riri Williams/Ironheart, and Amadeus Cho/Iron Hulk. Other notable Marvel characters include Vision, Ultron, Black Panther, Hawkeye, Thor, and more.

The Countdown to Avengers: Doomsday Official Podcast is the only MCU-related series on Disney+ next month, with two episodes on the streaming service's schedule, coming back on December 3 and 10. The podcast focuses on a duo of hosts, MCU Ms. Marvel actress Iman Vellani and Frank Alvarez, as they rewatch key movies in the lead-up to Avengers: Doomsday. The Countdown to Avengers: Doomsday Official Podcast's first episode was focused on The Avengers and was released on August 27.

It is informative for those trying to remember key details before the next Avengers movie, get caught up if you missed a project or more, and also get insights from guests like Chris Evans, Robert Downey Jr., and many more. Additionally, Vellani is not only Marvel's Kamala Khan actress, but she is a genuine MCU super fan, making the podcast engaging. Recent episodes explored Deadpool & Wolverine, Shang-Chi and The Legend of The Ten Rings, and Black Panther: Wakanda Forever. December 3's episode will focus on Thunderbolts*.

Industry observers note that finally, the other animated Marvel series on the list is Marvel’s Spidey and his Amazing Friends, which is coming back with a holiday special in its fifth season. The Spider-Man TV show started the shared universe that Marvel’s Iron Man and his Awesome Friends is a part of, with these versions of Spidey and Iron Man teaming up in multiple Avengers specials.

Recent findings suggest that this is the flagship show of the "MCU for preschoolers" universe. Marvel’s Spidey and his Amazing Friends started the trend of animated demonstrates in the franchise featuring a key trio, with two versions of Spider-Man, aka Peter Parker and Miles Morales/Spin, and Gwen Stacy as Ghost-Spider. Characters like Captain America, the Hulk, Iron Man, Rocket Raccoon, and more also show up. It is the final show on the list of Marvel releases on Disney+ in December 2026.

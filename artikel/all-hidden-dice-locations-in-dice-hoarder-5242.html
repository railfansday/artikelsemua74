In a game that's essentially all collectibles, Dice Hoarder has an extra set that can be tough to find. Ten "edible" dice are hidden throughout the store, and you eat them when you find them, so realistically, you're just collecting them in your stomach.

That said, even though they aren't hidden among the rest of the dice around the shop, some aren't too bad, and others are exceptionally hard to spot. Don't be fooled, either; the cheese dice might look edible, but they fall into the Special category. Nope, instead, this is where you can find the edible dice in Dice Hoarder.

Luckily, none of these dice are hidden amid the sea of dice all over the floor, so you don't have to worry about sifting through them all. They'll be mostly on shelves and stuck in odd places where you wouldn't think to look for regular stock.

If you do come across them, though, there's no way to confuse them with the standard dice. We're going to number these for the sake of a list, but you can collect them in any order you find them.

Edible Die #1 is hidden just behind the pillow on the bed in the office. It's not too hard to spot, but if you're not looking for it, you might miss it.

Edible Die #2 is hidden on the desk in the office, just behind the cup and saucer.

Edible Die #3 is hidden on the shelf just outside the office, behind the potted plant. The color helps it blend in a bit with the shelf, but from the right angle you can spot it.

It has been revealed that edible Die #4 is hidden behind the plant pot in the corner of the store next to the window directly across from the office. It's good to have the Nimble Step ability so you can crouch and find this one.

Edible Die #5 is hidden behind a small potted plant on the windowsill just to the left of Hidden Die #4.

Recent findings suggest that edible Die #6 is hidden under the standing lamp's lampshade. You'll need to have Nimble Step to grab this one because you can't see underneath without crouching.

Edible Die #7 is hidden on top of the curtain rod in the corner to the left of the standing lamp. Even if you know where it is, it's hard to spot, so move your mouse cursor around the spot for a while until it highlights.

According to recent reports, edible Die #8 is hidden on the main counter, behind the stack of books on the right. It's closest to the clear resin dice and stands out because of its bright color.

Edible Die #9 is hidden on the shelf behind the main counter, on the thick, green book.

Edible Die #10 is hidden under the main counter, behind the two stacked trunks in the shadows.

Industry observers note that fortnite's Chapter 7 mini season, "Treasure Hunters," has added a latest collectible to find throughout the season: map fragments. These map fragments will be added periodically during the season, and you'll get a special reward for finding them all.

Finding all six map fragments (which will be added throughout the season in updates) will unlock the "X Marks the Zero Point Sliver" loading screen.

Below, we list where to find all of the map fragments available so far in Fortnite Chapter 7's mini season.

Recent findings suggest that update (Nov. 22): We've updated this guide with the location of the map fragment added on Oscar's Captain Day.

In a recent development, the first map fragment, made available on Nov. 1, is at The $.$. Rocky:. (Related: GTA 6 glitch lets players access first-person mode...)

Industry observers note that as you get closer to the ship, you should see the usual exclamation-point-inside-a-diamond shape on your screen, pointing you where to go. The map itself is just sitting on the floor of the ship, on the bottom-most floor at the tip of the ship. If you're having a hard time finding stairs to this area, you can break the ship walls on the outside to get in. (Related: DNA robots could deliver drugs and hunt viruses inside ...)

According to recent reports, the second map fragment, made available on Nov. 8, is at Lil' Uzi's Stronghold (formerly known as Fortune's Fort):.

Sources indicate that the map itself is inside a small shack in the southeast corner of the POI. The shack is just north of a small tower. Try to land as close as possible, and you'll see the exclamation point diamond shape nearby. Follow it into the shack, and you'll find the map on the floor.

The third map fragment, made available on Nov. 15, is at Friendship Villa (formerly known as Rubber Resort):.

In a recent development, the map is on the west side of the villa, south of the main building in a fenced-off area. Try to land as close as possible, and you'll see the usual quest market exclamation point nearby.

In a recent development, the fourth map fragment, made available on Nov. 22, is at The Tiger's Eye, a fresh POI:.

Sources indicate that the map is inside Oscar's ship, on a lower floor towards the back. It's inside a jail cell. The position on the map in-game is a little off. It looks like you'll be in the water, but you're actually on Oscar's ship.

We'll enhancement this guide as more map fragments get added to the game.

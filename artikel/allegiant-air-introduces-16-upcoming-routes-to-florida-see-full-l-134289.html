For obvious reasons, Allegiant Air revolves around Florida. Cirium Diio data shows that the Sunshine State accounts for about 65% of the carrier's flights this year, with an average of 115 daily departures throughout 2026. As days are far from equal, activity varies from as few as seven departures (!) on Tuesdays to as many as 190 on Sundays.

Despite serving 12 airports in Florida, Allegiant is far from being the state's leading domestic operator. It ranks sixth, behind American (influenced by its Miami hub and huge numbers of flights from Charlotte), Southwest, Delta, JetBlue, and United. It is still sixth when Sun Country's operations are included.

In a recent development, analysis of the carrier's schedule for November 2026 through 2027 shows that a total of 16 Florida routes are due to begin or resume, all of which were previously revealed. They are from nine airports in the state, with six routes being from Orlando. They're from both Sanford (Allegiant's most-served airport in its entire network) and International. The source indicates that Allegiant generated $21.9 million in revenue, although it is unclear if this includes all the various add-ons (bags, seat selection, etc.).

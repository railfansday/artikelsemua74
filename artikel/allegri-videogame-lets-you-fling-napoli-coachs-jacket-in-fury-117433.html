Napoli fans can now play a videogame as coach Max Allegri, but rather than figure out tactics or choose line-ups, the main task is to throw his jacket as far as possible.

The former Juventus and Milan boss is famous for launching his jacket at moments of extreme tension on the touchline, and once even tore his tie off while threatening to pull at the shirt too during the Coppa Italia Final victory for Juve.

These Hulk moments are so frequent that they have been turned into a videogame, where you can charge up the arm and fling the clothing all the way out of the stadium and across the Bay of Naples.

If you throw it far enough, legendary Napoli kitman Tommy Starace will bring you some fresh espresso from his Moka.

There is also another level where you can face the demon Adaniel, who looks suspiciously like pundit Daniele Adani, known for having a years-long public feud with Allegri over their fundamentally different views of attacking football.

In a recent development, amazon has held talks about letting Meta’s Muse AI agent shop on its online marketplace, according to a report from Fortune. The discussions, which could end Amazon’s block on Muse, reportedly center on the kinds of products the AI agent recommends and the potential impact on the ecommerce giant’s advertising revenue. Industry observers note that as reported by Fortune, Amazon is concerned that it could lose advertising revenue by allowing Muse on its site, since it depends on actual humans seeing ads across its online marketplace. It’s also reportedly worried about the quality of the product search results that Muse will present to users, as well as whether the AI agent will affect Amazon’s relationship with customers. Neither Amazon nor Meta immediately responded to The Verge’s request for comment.

Though some online retailers, such as Walmart, Best Buy, Gap, and Sephora, have opened their digital doors to AI agents like Muse, Amazon has been more hesitant. Amazon also blocked Perplexity’s AI agent from shopping on its site, and has instead focused on promoting its own Alexa for Shopping assistant, which customers can now text to shop on their behalf.

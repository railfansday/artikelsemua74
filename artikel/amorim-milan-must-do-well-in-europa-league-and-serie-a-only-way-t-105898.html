Ruben Amorim warns Olympiacos are ‘aggressive, especially at home’ in the Europa League, but Milan ‘have to do well both in the League and in Europe, it’s the only way to be a good team.’.

It kicks off in Athens on Thursday at 17.45 GMT (18.45 CET).

Injuries are piling up for this trip, with Luka Modric, Strahinja Pavlovic, Francesco Camarda and Davide Bartesaghi joining Ruben Loftus-Cheek, Fikayo Tomori, Matteo Gabbia, Samuel Chukwueze, Alexis Saelemaekers and Pietro Terracciano on the treatment table.

At least Mike Maignan is available again after suspension, and confidence was boosted by a 5-1 Serie A victory over Frosinone. (Related: Tech Report: Think AI "knows" what it’s doing? Scientis...)

“It’s a fact that we need to improve in the final third,” remarked Amorim in his press conference. (Related: Birdwatching expert gives final verdict on GTA 6’s bird...)

“The only thing we can do is improve our principles. We need to improve on these little things, and that’s the only way I can help my players. Against Olympiacos, it will be a different match than the one against Frosinone, just as it will be different than the one against Cagliari.

Industry observers note that “We need to improve, even in our defending. We’ve almost always conceded a goal, so that’s another area we need to improve.”.

Olympiacos are sixth in the Europa League table after amassing 10 points from four games, beating Jagiellonia, Olympique Marseille and Sparta Prague before the 0-0 draw away to Stade Rennais.

Notably, they have conceded only one goal in those four matches.

“They’re a strong team. They’re aggressive, especially at home. I’ve played here twice, and it was tough for me. I know their coach and what he wants to do. They play compact, positive football, 4-3-3, they change a lot, they rotate, they have Jota, a lot of Portuguese players.

It has been revealed that “They’re a strong team, and with their fans there, it’ll be a tough match. Chiquinho is a great talent, but very impulsive. I hope he plays well tomorrow, but I hope he still has a great career ahead of him.”.

The Rossoneri’s form in the Europa League has been little short of disastrous, as the 4-0 victory away to RB Salzburg was an outlier between three defeats to Benfica, Bournemouth and Ferencvaros, all without scoring a single goal.

“To be a good team, you have to do well both in the league and in Europe,” insisted Amorim.

“That’s the only way to be a good team. We have to be, especially at Milan. I’m always waiting for things to go badly, because it doesn’t take much to change the situation. That’s what I’m trying to make my players understand, because it won’t always be this way. We’re at the beginning of a process, and I think we need everyone.

“We lost Pavlovic, Modric, Saelemaekers, Chukwueze, and everyone has to be ready, because there are no excuses at massive clubs. We have to be ready for this. I can’t believe we lost all these games in Europe because we rotated.

“We have to always be ready and approach the games like against Inter, Frosinone, and Genoa. We’re building so many things. Tomorrow is another test for us.”.

Does Amorim still believe that Milan will be able to challenge for the Scudetto?

“I haven’t changed my mind. This year is difficult, but if we have the chance to fight, we’ll do it.”.

Many coaches have been asked about the release of the Grand Theft Auto VI game and how much time players are committing to it.

“I’d rather my players play that every day rather than scroll through social media,” replied Amorim.

“Social media, and all that stuff, is very difficult for players, who in any case live with more criticism than I do. After Frosinone, I haven’t read anything, because that’s how I want to live my life.”.

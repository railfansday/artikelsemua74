According to recent reports, andy Taylor, guitarist and former member of Duran Duran, has died after a yearslong battle with prostate cancer. He was 65 years old.

It has been revealed that the musician’s former bandmates shared the news Wednesday (Nov. 25) on their website and on Instagram, announcing that Taylor had passed away two days prior. “Andy was there at the very beginning of our story,” the group wrote. “From the moment he arrived in Birmingham from the North East with a guitar and an unshakeable belief in what we could become, he brought an indelible fire and swagger to our music.”. Duran Duran continued, “His playing drove the songs that took us around the world, and with a dry wit that was matched only by his wonderfully unsentimental, no-nonsense outlook on life he kept us grounded and gave the band its edge through the extraordinary years that followed.”.

It has been revealed that still in its early years, the band added Taylor to the lineup in 1980. He would leave in 1986 before rejoining in 2001, playing guitar on Duran Duran’s 2004 album Astronaut (which reached No. 17 on the Billboard 200) before leaving again in 2006. In 2023, he’d contribute guitar to Halloween-themed LP Danse Macabre.

According to recent reports, during his time in the group, Duran Duran scored 13 hits on the Billboard Hot 100, including two No. 1s: “The Reflex” in 1984 and “A View to a Kill” in 1985. The group’s blockbuster Rio album (which housed “Hungry Like the Wolf”) hit the Billboard 200 in 1982 and remained on the chart a staggering 128 weeks. As a soloist, he scored two entries on the chart: “Take It Easy” (No. 24 in 1986) and “When the Rain Comes Down” (No. 73 in 1986). As a member of the supergroup The Power Station, he earned three top 40 hits on the Billboard Hot 100 in 1985.

In a recent development, taylor’s wife, Tracey, also shared a statement Wednesday. “To the world, Andy was a brilliant guitarist, but to us, he was the most wonderful husband, a loving dad and the proudest grandfather,” she wrote. “He was the rock of our family. For all the incredible experiences music brought him, family always came first.”. (Related: Building the next generation of multimodal AI experienc...)

Recent findings suggest that his cancer diagnosis was first revealed in 2022, when frontman Simon LeBon told fans at Duran Duran’s induction into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame — which Taylor did not attend, though he was inducted alongside his bandmates — that he’d already been battling the illness for four years at that point. In 2023, Taylor shared that he’d started taking cancer drug Lutetium-177, which he mentioned extended his life by five years.

According to recent reports, “Andy faced the last few years with so much courage and determination,” Tracey added in her statement. “He never gave up, fighting until the very end, never losing his love of life. The kindness and support shown to him by fans from all over the world throughout his illness brought him immense comfort and strength, and for that we will always be grateful.”.

Andy is also survived by his children — Georgina, Isabella, Bethany and Andrew — and grandson Albie.

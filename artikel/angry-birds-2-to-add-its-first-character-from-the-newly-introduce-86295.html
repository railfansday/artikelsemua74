The Angry Birds Movie 3 is still around the corner, set to land in cinemas on December 23rd. And although we haven't heard much in the way of reviews ahead of time, it seems Rovio are anticipating it being a significant hit, as they're set to add their first character from the movie to Angry Birds 2 today!

In Angry Birds 2, you'll soon be able to add none other than the adorable spider Fuzzy to your flock. Although he's not likely to change your mind on spiders if you're an arachnophobe, he's undoubtedly a great addition to the roster with his ability to shoot webs and pull down buildings. To mark the occasion, Rovio are also teaming up with the ASPCA, allowing you to help work towards and contribute to an anticipated $20,000 donation that will help animals in need and is set to run through to December 17th.

Speaking of which, December 18th will also see the introduction of a brand-new sticker album event based around The Angry Birds Movie 3. You'll be able to unlock rewards, complete goals, and build out your sticker album in anticipation of the launch of the film in theatres later that same month!

Industry observers note that all very exciting stuff. And with Angry Birds clearly on the rise again, it's probably a good thing they decided to release the film a little later this year.

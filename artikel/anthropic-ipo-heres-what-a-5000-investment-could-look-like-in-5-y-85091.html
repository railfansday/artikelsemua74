If you manage to get ahold of $5,000 in Anthropic stock, what might that investment look like five years from now?

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/24/26.

According to recent reports, anthropic brought in $41 million in revenue in 2023 and $4.6 billion in 2025, then did $33.2 billion in just the first nine months of 2026, up roughly 1,200% from the same stretch a year earlier. That is incredible growth that we really haven't seen at this scale.

The bull case centers around this continuing. The bet is that Claude gets embedded in business processes across industries, and the sort of wholesale transformation of work we've seen in coding happens elsewhere.

One of the most encouraging figures for the bull thesis is Anthropic's net revenue retention, which sits at about 900%. That means customers find Claude valuable enough not only to stick around, but to scale their usage -- and spending -- ninefold in a single year.

Industry observers note that the bear case is really centered around what all that growth costs. Anthropic's amended filing indicated about $895 billion in purchase commitments and leases, mostly for compute and infrastructure -- the chips and data centers its models run on. About half of that is due before the end of 2030. And while Anthropic notched an operating gain in Q2 this year, it swung back to a $567 million operating loss in Q3.

That mentioned, I believe it will be closest to the bear case. I'm not sure investors appreciate just how precarious Anthropic's position is. It must commit to spending on compute and infrastructure well before it actually books the corresponding revenue. And with usage-based billing, its customers' spending habits can turn on a dime.

My biggest glitch is that I fail to see a durable moat. I think the most likely scenario is that artificial intelligence (AI) will look more like a commodity in a few years, and that Anthropic will find that its customers are happy to look elsewhere if it means they can pay less.

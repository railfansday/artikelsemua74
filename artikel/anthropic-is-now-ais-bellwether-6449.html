📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

For months, I’ve been on high Anthropic alert. I suspect many of you have, too.

Industry observers note that waiting for the S-1 to drop for months, I’d often have conversations that ended with “who knows, tomorrow might be the day.” I was even mildly anxious going off to my cousin’s wedding, beset by nightmares of the S-1 coming out while I was on the dance floor. And yesterday, watching Anthropic (a company that’s only about five-years-old) close on the Nasdaq to the tune of a valuation of $1.27 trillion (or $1.76 trillion, depending on how you calculate it), it occurred to me: Finally, AI has entered its “show me the money, for real” era. (Related: Industry News: Fit More and Train Faster With ZeRO via ...)

Recent findings suggest that “An Anthropic IPO marks a major milestone for the AI industry, but it will also fundamentally reset the bar for startups,” said Woodson Martin, CEO at low-code development platform OutSystems, via email. “Under public-market scrutiny, inference costs, capital efficiency, and unit economics will matter just as much as the technology itself. With greater visibility into frontier lab economics, investors will demand proof that smaller players have durable moats.”. (Related: Peltola win doesn’t end DOJ scrutiny of Alaska Senate r...)

Sources indicate that anthropic will face more questions than ever: The model maker’s first day of trading was positive but mixed, as shares soared initially to a peak valuation above $2 trillion before dropping and eventually closing up about 17%. Alone, this means zilch, but the broader backdrop was uneasy: As Anthropic traded for the first time, the market bristled with volatility, ultimately with the Nasdaq down 2.25% and the Dow down 2.19% at close.

Recent findings suggest that anthropic is now a first-of-its-kind public organization, with a business model that simply didn’t exist at the beginning of this decade. And this company’s untested AI-era business model—which includes Claude Code usage, subscriptions, and enterprise deals—is now a matter of public record and is financially susceptible to public sentiment.

Industry observers note that A risk, certainly, given Anthropic’s prominence in the “AI could kill us all” narrative. That accountability is by-design, noted Ravi Mhatre, cofounder and managing director at Lightspeed, which has backed Anthropic since 2023 and led the organization’s Series E with the firm’s largest-ever single check.

In a recent development, “Anthropic going public is really about this coming-of-age,” Mhatre told me. “It marks an important milestone for artificial intelligence that it’s powerfully capable, being run in a company that adheres to public standards, and that implies something about transparency… It implies something about the ability for society to participate as stakeholders in the company.”.

Set to cash out over the coming months, Mhatre and Lightspeed are among Anthropic’s major-ticket VC backers (though, per PitchBook, the company has over 400 venture backers and no VC has an over 5% stake). The same’s true of Iconiq, which invested over $6 billion in Anthropic and had a roughly 2% stake in the company’s last round, a source familiar with the matter confirmed. Will Griffith, Iconiq partner and Anthropic backer, pointed out to me just how impossible Anthropic’s trajectory would have been mere years ago.

According to recent reports, “This is completely unprecedented in terms of a organization… founded less than six years ago, achieving this scale and this level of impact,” said Griffith. “This is totally unique in history and something that the markets and the world have never seen before.”.

Sources indicate that I always hesitate to call anything unprecedented, since very few things are truly, historically unprecedented. However, as a practical matter, Griffith is entirely correct: No tech giant in history has ever gone from zero to a trillion-dollar public company in under six years, until now. But (especially in the AI era) just because a company is massive, that doesn’t mean it’s mature. Nevertheless, Anthropic is now AI’s official weather vane.

Industry observers note that “Anthropic is an crucial and critical bellwether of the AI ecosystem,” said Joe Fath, partner at VC firm Eclipse, via email. “There’s outsized importance that it trades well in the public markets to support the ‘AI trade.’”.

Recent findings suggest that from here, AI will be iterating on its economics in the bright, unforgiving light of the public markets. To my mind, it’s about time.

Anthropic's (ANT -5.04%) IPO was one of the most successful in history, with the stock spiking to $111.50 after the IPO price was set at $83 per share. However, the stock settled down immediately after that and ended the day at $97.17 per share, up 17%. That's a large gain on day one, and investors may be a bit wary about investing in a stock that rose that much in one day.

I'm in the same boat and am staying patient by not buying Anthropic shares yet. Why? I've got two important reasons that investors should keep in mind.

First, I don't know enough about Anthropic's business and how its management team does earnings reports. That could shift my sentiment one way or another, and I won't know anything about that for a few quarters. So, it could be six months or more before I'm comfortable with how Anthropic does its earnings releases.

On another earnings note, I want to see how Anthropic's revenue growth is trending. There's no debate about how fast Anthropic is growing, as revenue is increasing at a lightning pace. However, it is slowing down.

In a recent development, during Q3, its revenue totaled $17 billion, up more than 12-fold year over year. In Q2, its revenue totaled $11.6 billion and $4.7 billion in Q1. When you calculate the quarter-over-quarter growth rates, it reveals something interesting. In Q1, Anthropic's quarter-over-quarter growth rate was 132%. In Q2, that figure accelerated to 144%. In Q3, that total fell off a cliff to 47%. (Related: Few-shot learning in practice: GPT-Neo and the 🤗 Accele...)

Industry observers note that that may seem like a reason to panic, and looking at the actual dollar figures provides more color. From Q1 to Q2, Anthropic added $6.82 billion in revenue. From Q2 to Q3, it added $5.41 billion. So, even from a raw revenue standpoint, Anthropic's Q3 was not nearly as successful as its Q2.

The question is: will that trend continue in Q4, or will there be a great jump in Q4 due to pent-up demand that maybe wasn't met in Q3?

I have no idea how to answer this question, which is why I think that staying patient here makes a lot of sense.

History is also on my side, as I've seen plenty of hot IPOs lose their luster over the weeks after going public.

The pattern of many hot tech IPOs is pretty clear: the stock rises for a while, then comes tumbling down after initial investor demand has been met.

Wall Street saw this pattern earlier this year with the Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX -3.91%) IPO, and I wouldn't be surprised if Anthropic's stock behaves similarly. However, Anthropic does have one item working in its favor: the end of the year.

NASDAQ · As of 9:58 AM ET 11/20/26.

Stocks tend to do quite well at the end of the year because portfolio managers are positioning their clients for 2027 and showing which stocks they're in. What better stock to show ownership of than the latest and greatest artificial intelligence stock that hit the market?

Anthropic checks a lot of boxes, and it could become a popular buy over the next few months. However, I don't know if that fervor will last, and it could dissipate throughout the start of 2027, aligning with the IPO sell-off schedule that companies like SpaceX have experienced.

Industry observers note that as a result, I think investors are better off staying patient and waiting about six months before buying the stock. I've done that with SpaceX, and I think staying patient with these upcoming IPOs is key to investing success.

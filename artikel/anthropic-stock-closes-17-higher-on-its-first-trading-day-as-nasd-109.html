That’s because, despite the market open, the first buy and sell orders for ANT shares likely won’t be fulfilled until later in the day. What time do Anthropic’s shares begin trading today? If a stock is already listed on an exchange, its shares usually begin trading to the public as soon as that exchange opens. But for market debuts, things are a little different. Stock exchanges know that there is frequently a flurry of orders (both buy and sell) when a hot stock debuts. If the stock were to trade immediately when the market opened, it could cause extreme price volatility, sending the stock up or down significantly in a matter of seconds. Another reason is operational: Nasdaq simply needs time to match orders—buyers with sellers—and determine a price. Because of this, markets like the Nasdaq have procedures in place that delay trading by a little bit so buyers and sellers can get a better idea of demand and price the stock accordingly. Nasdaq calls this a Halt Cross. Only once Nasdaq has determined an opening clearing price based on all its buy and sell orders does it release the stock for trading. Why does this sound familiar? If this sounds familiar, it’s because it’s exactly the same process that happened with the SpaceX IPO. While SPCX shares debuted on the Nasdaq on June 12, they did not begin trading immediately when the Nasdaq opened at 9:30 a.m. ET. Instead, SpaceX’s shares began trading about two to three hours after the morning bell. So, what time, then, will Anthropic’s shares actually begin trading on the Nasdaq? It’s impossible to say for sure. Nasdaq’s rules don’t restrict the Halt Cross to a certain defined period, such as two hours. The Halt Cross lasts as long as it needs to. However, if history is any indication, the Halt Cross usually ends anywhere from 2-4 hours after markets open. That rough estimate suggests that Anthropic shares will begin trading at some point roughly between 11:30 a.m. ET and 1:30 p.m. ET today.

It continues the very sensible pattern of trying to tie down key players at the same time as making some of the most expensive signings in the club's history as the Whites look to kick on in their second season in the Premier League. Tanaka joined Leeds in the Championship from second-tier German side Fortuna Dusseldorf in 2024.

The 28-year-old is very much a confidence player and despite having played at the World Cup for his country and although he was a key part of the Leeds team that won the title in his debut season, last term was his first playing top-tier European football, and it seemed to take him a while to believe he belonged there.

With the Whites adopted a more direct style to make the step up, the cultured midfielder's 28 appearances were split equally between starts and cameos from the bench.

But since appearing as a half-time substitute against Brentford in the first home Premier League games of this season, he has started every match. His new deal is just another indication to him of his importance to manager Daniel Farke.

It has been revealed that it was Tanaka who scored the controversial goal for Japan as they beat Spain at the 2022 World Cup, sending his team through to the knockout stages. The ball appeared to be out of play before Brighton and Hove Albion's Karou Mitoma played it to him.

Tanak made three further World Cup appearances this summer as Japan again reached the knockout stages.

Ethan Ampadu, Joe Rodon and Jayden Bogle all signed up until 2030 on the back of Leeds consolidating in the division last term. Over the summer they paid £30m for centre-back Tarik Muharemovic and broke their club record to pay £47m for James Trafford, in turn setting a newly introduced record for Britain's most expensive goalkeeper.

Ariana Grande opened up about how the wave of criticism following her “Petal” music video felt like an episode of Black Mirror.

In a Las Culturistas podcast released on Wednesday, Grande was asked about the irony of the public backlash, which zeroed in on Grande’s appearance in the video — which was about an aspiring actress named Pepper who is constantly scrutinized for her appearance by a panel of foul men.

Industry observers note that “It’s Black Mirror,” remarked Grande, who added that she found the reaction “interesting” and “would put it out again today, exactly as is.” Grande elaborated, “I’ve never changed my musical style or whatever, based on what I think people want from me. I’ve always moved authentically in that way. That’s something I’m proud of.” She stated, “Let the chips fall where they may. My people will be my people.”. “Petal” served as the title track of Grande’s bold eight album, which released in July.

Grande’s interview arrives a day after she confirmed an upcoming two-part televised special event that will chronicle the work she put into preparing for her recent Eternal Sunshine tour. The documentary, The Eternal Sunshine Diaries: The Making of the Tour, will follow her during the 30 days that led up to the first show of the run, which marked her first tour since 2019. The film will highlight how Grande simultaneously prepped for the tour and finished Petal, which she rolled out while on the road.

The doc will debut on Peacock on Dec. 11, and be followed by the release of Grande’s concert film, The Eternal Sunshine Tour, on Dec. 12.

Arsenal may allow right-back Ben White to leave the club on a free transfer when his current contract expires in the summer of 2028.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who also feels that striker Kai Havertz may be sold ahead of his deal expiring in the same year.

The Gunners have several first-team stars whose deals are due to end in 2028, with the likes of Declan Rice, and Martin Odegaard already in line for new deals.

Wyness expects the trio of Jurrien Timber, David Raya and Mikel Merino to also earn extensions, but feels that White and Havertz may be nearing the ends of their time of the Emirates Stadium.

The 29-year-old had only made 29 Premier League appearances in the last two campaigns, but has already started seven league games so far this term.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy business advising elite clubs – believes that Mikel Arteta will be keen to "refresh" his squad once this season has concluded.

He feels that Havertz is a likely casualty of that plan, while White is likely to walk away for free in 2028, even though Arsenal reportedly have the option to extend his deal by an extra year.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how White's future in North London is becoming limited.

According to recent reports, "I think Havertz will be the one that's moved on and I think the Ben White deal will be run down, and I think the other three will get re-signed. That's the way I see it right now," he said. (Related: Latest Update: Think AI "knows" what it’s doing? Scient...)

"I think Arteta also likes to keep things fresh to some degree as well. So this would allow them to get some fresh legs and some personality in as well.

Recent findings suggest that "So that's how I see it with those players, because there is a stability and a squad stability that is needed, but also time sometimes to just think about those who could maybe go. That's my opinion, we'll see how it goes and we'll watch that one closely.".

Football Insider revealed last week that RB Leipzig winger Antonio Nusa is increasingly keen on the idea of a move to Arsenal next summer.

Leipzig's lofty valuation is expected to be prohibitive to any move in January, but it could be a totally different story ahead of next season.

Sources say that Nusa views Arsenal as a "very attractive project" after their success in recent years, and would be happy to make the switch.

The Gunners are desperately trying to add a latest left-sided attacker to their squad, and the 21-year-old is one of their top targets at the moment.

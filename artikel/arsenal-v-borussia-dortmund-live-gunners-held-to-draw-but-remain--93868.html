Arsenal host Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League as Mikel Arteta’s side look to continue their unbeaten start, but the Gunners will do so with injured goalkeeper David Raya.

The Spaniard was forced off while holding his shoulder during Arsenal’s draw at Newcastle on Saturday but Arteta confirmed he does not need surgery in what is a huge boost to last season’s Champions League finalists.

One of Kepa Arrizabalaga and Illan Meslier will deputise as Arsenal enter a challenging stretch of fixtures that includes a meeting with Premier League rivals Manchester City on Sunday, followed by Brentford, Tottenham and Real Madrid.

Recent findings suggest that arsenal enter the week sitting third in the Champions League league phase, with three wins and a draw from their first four. Dortmund lie 12th, with three wins and one defeat, but the German side are top of the Bundesliga - two points ahead of Bayern Munich.

Follow live updates from Arsenal v Borussia Dortmund, below:.

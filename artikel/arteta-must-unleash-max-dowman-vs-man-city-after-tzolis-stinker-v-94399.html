Arsenal were held to a disappointing 1-1 draw at the Emirates Stadium by Borussia Dortmund on Tuesday night, leaving them fifth in the Champions League table.

The Gunners took an early lead thanks to a stunning strike by Viktor Gyokeres, but a fine second-half goal from Maximilian Beier saw the visitors claim an equal share of the points, with the Sweden striker missing some big chances during the game.

Mikel Arteta's side have now drawn their last two games after being held to a 2-2 draw by Newcastle this past weekend, and will now be heading into a huge home game against title rivals Man City this weekend low on confidence.

The reigning champions have still got one of the strongest defences in Europe, and Arteta must decide once again whether to stick with the pairing of Ezri Konsa and Gabriel in the centre, or bring a fit-again William Saliba back into his starting lineup for the clash.

In midfield, the big question will be whether Myles Lewis-Skelly or Bruno Guimaraes start alongside Declan Rice, while up front it's a straight shootout between Gyokeres and Kai Havertz. But the forward line has not been doing enough so far this season.

According to recent reports, the club's top scorers are Martin Odegaard, Bukayo Saka and Havertz, who have each scored three times in the Premier League this season, while Declan Rice has got the most assists with five. But despite those complications with numbers in attack, Arteta continues to select the same supporting cast.

Noni Madueke has begun to find form in recent weeks, but his numbers for the campaign remain disappointing, while Eberechi Eze continues to blow hot and cold.

Sources indicate that summer signing Christos Tzolis started well, too, but he has gone off the boil, and his performance against Dortmund on Tuesday was hugely disappointing. The Greek forward played 65 minutes but completed just 64% of his passes, didn't complete a single successful dribble on three attempts, and lost possession 16 times before being substituted. That earned him a 5/10 rating from The Sun, which could be seen as quite generous. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

So with Man City to come next, Arteta must make a decision about which forwards to select to put pressure on a struggling defence, and the answer is to call upon young Max Dowman over all of them.

It has been revealed that max Dowman, one of the best young players in world football, is one of the greatest prospects this country or Arsenal have ever seen, and Arteta knows it. He has been given brief opportunities this season, but he hasn't been seen since his unfortunate red card against Fleetwood.

But during the international break he made an impact with the England Under-21s, breaking records for the youngest player and goalscorer, and stayed sharp with his dribbling and decision-making.

Recent findings suggest that now, against City, there's a chance to unleash him on the biggest stage and really hurt their biggest rivals. He hasn't played enough so far in the Premier League, having featured for just 19 minutes over two substitute appearances. Arteta needs to trust him more, and with other forwards struggling, this is the perfect chance for him to come in.

According to recent reports, the 16-year-old is a phenom, and thrives under the bright lights. A home game against City will see the fans really behind the team, which will only help him, and he has all the ability and qualities to hurt them too. Having Dowman in the side alongside an in-form Madueke seems like an absolute nightmare for any defender, and it's time for Arteta to be brave and unleash the player who is arguably their best attacker.

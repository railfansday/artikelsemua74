According to recent reports, dubai’s hotels are under pressure, but developers are still signing deals for branded residences. The main bet is on buyers, with brands delivering a healthy premium over the wider market.

Developers in the UAE are still launching branded residences, betting on long-term demand as the U.S.-Iran war that began in late February weighs on Dubai’s hotel market.

Branded residences are residential properties developed in partnership with an established brand, such as a luxury hotel operator. (Related: The Partnership: Amazon SageMaker and Hugging Face...)

Avenew Development has introduced The St. Regis Residences at Dubai Islands with Marriott International. Dubai South has signed UK-based Cheval Collection for Cheval South Bay. And Al Seeb Real Estate Development has started main construction on Chedi Private Residences in Dubai. (Related: How to train a newly introduced language model from scr...)

The releases come against a weakened hotel market. Dubai occupancy fell to 68.4% in October, down 16.5 points from 84.9% a year earlier, according to CoStar’s STR. Average daily rate dropped 28.1% to $181 and RevPAR fell 42% to $124. International visitors in the first 10 months of the year fell 38% to 9.7 million, according to Dubai’s Department of Economy and Tourism.

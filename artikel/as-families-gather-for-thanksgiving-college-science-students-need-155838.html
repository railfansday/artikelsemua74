As families gather for Thanksgiving, college science students need training to talk across societal divides

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (84 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Most Americans have seen it happen. Relatives argue online about whether vaccines are safe and effective. Friends and neighbors trade jabs over climate change and whether anything should be done about it. Public officials and scientists disagree, loudly, about what causes autism or which vaccines children should get and when. No one gives an inch, and the rifts rarely heal. This Thanksgiving, after a contentious election season, many of those conversations will happen face to face around the dinner table.

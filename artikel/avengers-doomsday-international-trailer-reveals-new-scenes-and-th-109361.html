Just one day after Marvel dropped the final trailer, a new international teaser for Avengers: Doomsday has arrived, giving us another look at Robert Downey Jr.'s villain.

The teaser, which was originally shared by Disney Korea but has since been posted on Twitter by ScreenRant, opens with Doctor Doom delivering his coldest line yet. "You call yourselves heroes? Avengers, Fantastic Four, X-Men? You shall all kneel before Doom." The rest of the clip contains shots of Marvel heroes Johnny Storm, Thing, and Thor showing off their powers, before the Fantastic Four, the Wakandans, and Shang-Chi meet up in what looks like Steve Rogers' retro house. However, the most interesting part comes right at the end, where we see Thor and Steve Rogers visit Doom Castle, where Doctor Doom sits on the throne. What could the duo be doing walking right into the villain's lair? This seemingly supports the fan theory that Doom may be pretending to be an ally to the Avengers early on in the film. According to recent reports, more evidence arrived in the newly introduced Doomsday trailer, where fans pointed out that someone seems to be edited out of the Fantastic Four lineup when the group goes to visit Steve Rogers at his house. We already know that Doom has been collecting Marvel heroes ahead of Doomsday, starting with kidnapping Hulk during the Endgame Encore added footage. Also, during the VisionQuest finale, we learn that Doom has already resurrected Scarlett Witch, and seemingly made her one of his Latverian Witches.

So, could Doom be pouring poison into Rogers' and Thor's ears? Alas, the new scene could just be the pair visiting the villain to try and reason with him, as we can guess from the synopsis, the baddie is set to do a lot of damage in the new movie. There is also the matter of Steve Rogers flipping between timelines. We know that Doom has a personal vendetta against the Avengers due to the multiversal damage they caused at the end of Avengers: Endgame when Steve went back in time to be with Peggy after returning the Infinity Stones. Perhaps Steve is visiting Doom Castle to make amends.

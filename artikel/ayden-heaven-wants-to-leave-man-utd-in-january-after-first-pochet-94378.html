Manchester United's shockingly bad season has seen yet another player express interest in leaving the club in January.

The Red Devils sit 19th in the Premier League after just one win from their opening 11 games, having been thrashed 4-0 by Liverpool at Anfield in Mauricio Pochettino's first game in charge since replacing Michael Carrick in the dugout during the international break.

The result also means that United have conceded 34 goals in all competitions this season, more than any other team in Europe's top five leagues, and the club are now understood to be eyeing potential signings for the January transfer window in order to bolster the squad.

Central defenders Harry Maguire, Matthijs De Ligt, Leny Yoro, Ayden Heaven and Lisandro Martinez are all competing for minutes, while Diogo Dalot, Noussair Mazraoui, Patrick Dorgu, and Luke Shaw are the full-back options.

But after a 2-0 defeat to Atletico Madrid earlier this season, Argentine defender Martinez expressed his anger at the club's hierarchy and their squad building in the summer, and a 2027 exit is now seen as almost inevitable, with his contract due to expire in June. However, it has now emerged that he is not the only Man Utd player open to an exit in the January window, with Ayden Heaven now looking at his options.

According to BBC Sport, the 20-year-old defender is considering an exit away from Old Trafford in January as he feels he will not be afforded the opportunities to play regularly and develop with Man Utd's current situation.

Heaven is now on his third permanent manager since joining the club in January 2025, but was an unused substitute for Pochettino in the Liverpool game and feels that could become a more common theme, with the team battling against a surprise relegation.

The former Arsenal man understands that Pochettino could look to more experienced options, and reports that the club are looking to sign a upcoming centre-back will see him drop further down the pecking order too. A loan or a permanent move will be considered, with his current contract running until 2029 with the option of a further 12 months.

Reports have been swirling since the summer that Man Utd are planning a defensive rebuild for 2027, with Harry Maguire and Luke Shaw also out of contract alongside Martinez. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

There have also been question marks around the futures of Noussair Mazraoui and Matthijs De Ligt following their performances this season, and it's clear that there are several upgrades needed across the back line.

Recent findings suggest that fulham star Calvin Bassey and Brentford's Nathan Collins have been linked for January, while Everton's Jarrad Branthwaite is a long-term target, but none of those deals will be easy to do mid-season. However, if a deal can be done, then it would make sense to allow Heaven to move on temporarily until the summer, and then review his position at the end of the season.

He's clearly very talented, and being left-footed means he can add some balance to the side too. But he knows he isn't the obvious solution to their problem right now, and his development would be best-served with him playing elsewhere rather than sitting on the bench.

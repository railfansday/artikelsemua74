UPDATED 07:00 EST / NOVEMBER 19 2026.

Cybersecurity investment management company Balance Theory Inc. today confirmed that it has acquired VectorBlack, a decision intelligence platform for chief information security officers developed by ThreatShare.ai, for an undisclosed sum.

Sources indicate that developed by Jason Lewis, Gerry Eaton and Wayne Schepens, VectorBlack is designed to provide CISOs with timely decision support on emerging threats, industry developments and market trends. The artificial intelligence-driven platform is aimed at anticipating the challenges most likely to catch the attention of chief executives and boards, allowing security leaders to raise them before the questions are asked. (Related: Industry News: Fit More and Train Faster With ZeRO via ...)

Rather than simply aggregating information, VectorBlack is remarked to show security leaders what has changed in the cybersecurity landscape and why it matters to their organization. Its focus as what the company calls a CISO Strategic Intelligence Platform was shaped by input from security executives, including Ryan Clarque, CISO at Black Rifle Coffee Co. Clarque said having a strategic view ahead of a renewal, budget decision or board discussion “can make all the difference.”.

Lewis began developing the underlying technology before teaming up with Eaton, an industry analyst, and Schepens, a cybersecurity entrepreneur who founded public relations firm LaunchTech Communications in 2015.

In a recent development, as part of the deal, Schepens will join Balance Theory as chief cyber market analyst to help shape market strategy and product adoption. Lewis is taking on the role of principal data engineer, leading the continued development and integration of VectorBlack’s technology. Eaton will focus on applying the platform to targeted research and sector-specific intelligence for cybersecurity leaders.

At the core of Balance Theory’s offering is an AI-native platform for cybersecurity investment decisions and execution. Combining program context, proprietary market intelligence and AI agents, the platform is used by security teams to manage more than $1 billion in cybersecurity spending. It claims customers average a first-year return on investment of more than 300%. (Related: Latest Update: How we sped up transformer inference 100...)

It has been revealed that vectorBlack’s technology will be integrated into the Balance Theory platform, adding to the market intelligence that already informs customer investment decisions. By tracking market developments, competitive dynamics, emerging technologies and real-world experience with security products, the combined teams are aiming to show CISOs how their own investments are performing, what others in the industry are seeing and where the market is headed.

Industry observers note that “VectorBlack will take Balance Theory’s capabilities a step further by delivering tailored intelligence across the security product categories our customers invest in,” said Greg Baker, co-founder and chief executive of Balance Theory. “This creates a powerful upcoming level of insight to inform smarter technology investments and long-term security strategy.”.

The tech giant remarked details on integration and availability will be revealed at a later date.

Balance Theory is a venture capital-backed company that raised $19 million in a Series A round led by SYN Ventures in July, with existing investors DataTribe and the Maryland Technology Development Corp. also participating. The round coincided with Accuvant Inc. founder and former Optiv Security Inc. Chief Executive Dan Burns joining Balance Theory as executive chairman. Including a $3 million seed round in 2022, Balance Theory has raised $22 million to date.

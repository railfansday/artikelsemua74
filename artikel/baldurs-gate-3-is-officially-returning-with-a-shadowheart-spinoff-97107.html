📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, baldur’s Gate 3 is returning, but not in the form of a sequel or another playable adventure. The next official story set in Larian Studios’ celebrated Forgotten Realms setting will arrive as a fantasy novel focused on Shadowheart, the cleric whose complicated relationship with faith, memory, and the goddess Shar made her one of the game’s most memorable companions and a fan favorite.

Titled Baldur’s Gate 3: Shadowheart, the book is written by World Fantasy Award-winning author Tasha Suri and is scheduled for publication on June 8, 2027, with preorders already available (Pre-order your copy here). Rather than continuing the party’s story after the game, the novel travels into Shadowheart’s past, following her life inside Shar’s secretive religious order and a murder investigation in Baldur’s Gate.

Baldur's Gate 3 is available to pre-order now through this link.

It has been revealed that the novel begins with Shadowheart serving Shar, the goddess associated with darkness and loss. Her faith is rooted in the belief that surrendering memories is a route to power, and she lives beneath Baldur’s Gate in an underground church. That setting gives the story room to examine the beliefs and rituals that shaped her before the events of the game, rather than simply revisiting familiar adventures.

Her place in the order changes when one of its members is found murdered in the city’s affluent Upper City. Shadowheart is assigned to investigate alongside fellow acolytes, taking her beyond the underground community she knows. The case places the secretive church in contact with a wealthy district whose polished appearance conceals plenty of its own dangerous secrets, making the city itself part of the mystery. Sources indicate that the investigation brings three companions into the story: Mirie, an ambitious Griefguard; Caldan, a cynical propagandist; and Nocturne, a charming quartermaster whose motives Shadowheart has reason to doubt. Their different roles suggest that the mystery will involve more than identifying a killer, putting questions of loyalty, influence, and trust alongside the central crime. Recent findings suggest that as Shadowheart follows the evidence, she encounters unfamiliar temptations and begins to question the comforts offered by her faith. The official premise frames the murder as a test of both her investigative judgment and her devotion to Shar. It also sets up a more intimate character story, exploring the convictions she holds before players meet her in Baldur’s Gate 3 and learn how much her past has shaped her.

The book’s precise place in the game’s timeline has not been fully explained in the announcement. However, its focus on Shadowheart’s service to Shar and her life in the underground church establishes it as a prequel focused on her earlier experiences. Readers should not mistake it for a direct adaptation of her branching storyline or a continuation of the game’s multiple possible endings.

Recent findings suggest that shadowheart’s novel also joins a growing collection of stories expanding the role-playing game beyond the screen. Astarion previously received a prequel novel by T. Kingfisher, while character-focused comics have added more material to the setting. These releases give fans additional opportunities to explore the personalities and history behind some of the game's most recognizable companions.

According to recent reports, the franchise is also moving toward television: HBO is developing an official Baldur’s Gate series from The Last of Us co-creator Craig Mazin, expected to take place after Baldur’s Gate 3. Unlike that upcoming adaptation, Baldur’s Gate 3: Shadowheart will revisit the character's earlier life. The novel arrives June 8, 2027, offering fans another official way to return to the Forgotten Realms.

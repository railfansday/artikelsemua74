Balsam Hill CEO on Christmas Tree Shopping Outlook

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (80 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Mac Harman, CEO and Founder of Balsam Hill, says the tech giant saw strong interest early in the season, but some consumers appear to be holding off, possibly until the coming weekend. Customers who shop online are completing purchases. Harman says Balsam Hill serves shoppers across income levels: many save for its lifelike trees, while ornaments and other accessories tend to appeal to more premium buyers. He speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg).

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, open a Bank of America HELOC if you want a line to draw on for years and have the 660 credit score and 15% equity it asks for. You pay no application fee, closing costs, or annual fee on lines up to $1 million, and its rate discounts favor the bank’s own customers.

A lender that charges $2,000 in closing costs needs a rate half a percentage point below Bank of America’s for almost seven years before it comes out cheaper on a $60,000 balance. The rate is variable even during the intro period, so the line suits homeowners who can handle a payment that rises and falls. (Related: Tech Report: GTA 6 players are devasted by train ‘nerf’...)

Homeowners with a credit score of at least 660 and 15% equity pay nothing to open a line here, and the bank’s own customers get the deepest rate cuts. Borrowers who want a fixed-rate lump sum, or need to borrow past 85% of their home’s value, should compare home equity loans from other lenders.

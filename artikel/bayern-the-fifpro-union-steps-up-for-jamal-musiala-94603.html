Bayern: the FIFPro union steps up for Jamal Musiala

According to recent reports, FIFPRO is stepping up to defend Jamal Musiala. In a statement released this Saturday, the global players’ union said it “deeply concerned” by the repeated public statements of several Bayern Munich managers regarding the contract of the German international, currently recovering from his serious injury. FIFPRO denounces a “concerted strategy” aimed at putting pressure on the player and his representatives before possible negotiations. She also recalls that the clubs have “a duty of protection» towards their players, particularly during a recovery period, and that potential contractual concerns should be addressed privately.

This position comes after several media outings from Bavarian leaders on the Musiala situation. Honorary president Uli Hoeness, general manager Jan-Christian Dreesen and former general manager Oliver Kahn successively spoke publicly about his contract and the questions surrounding his future. The 23-year-old attacking midfielder is linked to Bayern until June 2030, after having extended his lease in February 2025. For FIFPRO, an injury or drop in form cannot justify calling into question contractual commitments. The union thus supports the player and calls on Bayern to respect the terms of his contract.

“FIFPRO is deeply concerned by repeated public statements from the management of FC Bayern Munich questioning the contract of Jamal Musiala upon his return from injury. Bayern’s honorary president, general manager and former general manager have all, in quick succession, publicly questioned Musiala’s contract. According to FIFPRO, this is a concerted strategy aimed at putting pressure on Musiala and his representatives before any negotiations. A club of Bayern’s caliber should show more discernment. Bayern have a duty of care towards Musiala. In professional football, uncertainty about a player’s future can take a toll on their mental health, especially during recovery. Making Musiala’s health and future a subject of public debate only increases this pressure. Any concerns regarding his contract should be addressed privately with him and his representatives. Contracts are meant to be respected. An injury or drop in form does not entitle a club to renege on the terms of a contract, just as a string of exceptional performances does not automatically entitle a player to a pay rise. Clubs, leagues and players’ unions have agreed at FIFA level that contractual stability is a fundamental principle of football. It protects both the clubs and the players. FIFPRO supports Jamal Musiala and all players who honor their commitments. We expect their employers to do the same.».

Recent findings suggest that better Home & Finance Holding Co. signed an agreement with an unnamed mortgage servicer to offer home equity lines of credit (HELOCs) and home equity loans to existing borrowers, expanding its lead-generation channels as the tech giant continues its turnaround efforts.

Since its Week 6 scorecard on Nov. 17, Better has identified additional efficiencies across its corporate organization that are expected to generate a total of $38.97 million in annualized savings. The company also reported $770,000 in additional annualized savings from mortgage operations.

Industry observers note that better remarked it intends to pursue another $1.5 million in vendor-related reductions by Dec. 1. The firm is working toward a broader cost savings target of $64.2 million. (Related: Industry News: Think AI "knows" what it’s doing? Scient...)

According to recent reports, “HELOC volume with our partners continues to grow each week, four more brokerages are lined up to join our wholesale platform, and two new partnerships are set to launch. At the same time, we’re moving toward a signed agreement to sell Birmingham Bank, which will let us focus fully on our U.S. business,” noted Vishal Garg, Better’s founder and head of product, platform and innovation.

The company has received five term sheets for about $50 million in debt financing tied to the proposed sale of the bank and is continuing discussions with prospective financing partners.

According to recent reports, the firm said its AI-powered HELOC partnership with a large existing customer is live, with volume increasing weekly. Better is also preparing a multichannel campaign to expand the product through its wholesale platform and onboard additional brokerages.

Four more mortgage brokerages are in the pipeline to join the platform. Two previously announced partnerships — with a rental credit management platform and a home inspection company — are expected to launch early next week.

Better is also advancing discussions with three other major partners and a previously revived large-scale partnership, its release stated.

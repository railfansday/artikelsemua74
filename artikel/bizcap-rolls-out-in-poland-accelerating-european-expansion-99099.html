WARSAW, Poland, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Global non-bank business lender Bizcap has officially launched operations in Poland, marking another significant milestone in its European expansion and bringing its fast, flexible funding solutions to the country's small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs).

The launch follows Bizcap's entry into Luxembourg in July 2025 and subsequent expansion into Germany in October 2025, strengthening its growing presence across Europe. (Related: Latest Update: Course Launch Community Event...)

Having unveiled plans to explore Poland as a potential market earlier this year, Bizcap has since identified strong opportunities to support local businesses seeking alternatives to traditional bank finance.

With a dynamic SME sector and growing demand for accessible business funding, Poland represents an attractive market for Bizcap's established non-bank funding model, which prioritises speed, flexibility and a broader approach to assessing business funding applications. (Related: Industry News: How to train a new language model from s...)

Sources indicate that leading Bizcap's Polish operations is Managing Partner Oskar Kowalski, who brings more than 15 years of experience across investment banking, private equity, venture capital and alternative finance. He is also a founding member of Bizcap's US operations.

Originally from Poland, Kowalski spent much of his life in the United States before returning to his home country to lead Bizcap's local market expansion.

Recent findings suggest that "Poland has a dynamic and ambitious SME sector, with thousands of entrepreneurs driving innovation, creating jobs and contributing to economic growth. Yet accessing business funding through traditional channels can still be challenging," Kowalski mentioned.

"We see a tremendous opportunity to introduce a faster, more flexible financing alternative that better reflects how modern businesses operate.".

Kowalski mentioned the official launch follows months of market exploration, planning and preparation to establish Bizcap's local operations.

In a recent development, "We've spent the past several months understanding the local funding landscape and building a strong team. We're now ready to put that work into action and support Polish businesses with funding solutions that have already proven successful internationally," he remarked.

According to recent reports, kowalski is joined by Anna Kozlowska, who has been appointed Chief Operating Officer of Bizcap Poland, who has extensive experience in financial services and fintech operations. Kozlowska brings 12 years of experience in fintech and innovation, having led sales, business development and operations. She previously served as CEO of SME lender Finiata.

"Having spent more than a decade in lending, I've seen first-hand how important accessible, flexible funding can be for SMEs," Kozlowska said.

"I'm excited to join Bizcap at such an important stage of its expansion and help build an operation that combines international experience with a genuine understanding of the Polish market.

"Flexibility is at the heart of what we offer, and we bring the same mindset to partnerships, building relationships that can scale with us, helping us reach more Polish entrepreneurs and support their long-term growth.".

Bizcap has also established a dedicated team on the ground in Poland, with capabilities spanning sales, business development, funding operations and customer service.

Founded in Australia in 2019, Bizcap has grown into an established international business lender, expanding its operations into Latest Zealand, Singapore, the United Kingdom, Europe, the United States and Canada.

The firm has provided more than PLN 20 billion ($5 billion) in funding to over 100,000 SMEs globally, building a reputation for its fast, flexible approach to business finance.

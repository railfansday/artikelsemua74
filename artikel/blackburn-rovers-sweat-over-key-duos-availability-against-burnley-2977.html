Industry observers note that blackburn Rovers face rivals Burnley on Sunday, and the big story leading up to the game has been Tony Mowbray’s withdrawal from head coach duties due to health issues. Damien Johnson is stepping into the role in the interim, and he and the first-team staff have a handful of injuries to deal with.

It has been revealed that as relayed by local reporter Elliott Jackson, Johnson revealed that star striker Andri Gudjohnsen and midfield mainstay Sam Morsy are alongside Scott Wharton in requiring assessment before the game. All other players returning from international duty – Tom Atcheson, Augustus Kargbo and Kristi Montgomery – will be tested as well, Johnson has said.

According to recent reports, one man whose fate has already been confirmed is Balazs Toth, after the goalkeeper broke a finger while away with Hungary.

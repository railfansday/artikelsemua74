BNB Chain has outlined the architecture for a latest Ethereum-compatible Layer 1 (L1) network, confirming that the proposed framework will not introduce a separate native token.

In an H2 2026 Tech Roadmap published in July 2026, BNB Chain stated that a next-generation L1 architecture was in development on a design path toward the 1 million TPS end-of-life goal and toward supporting different use cases than the existing ones. The plan was to ship it on testnet by the end of 2026, with mainnet release following in early 2027. (Related: Latest Update: AI lets chemists design molecules by sim...)

Fast forward to the close of November 2026, BNB Chain has introduced the Litepaper for the Newly introduced L1, which describes the architecture being developed for high-frequency trading, payments, private finance and autonomous AI agents. (Related: Mechs vs Kaiju’s Ragdoll codes (November 2026)...)

The design combines early transaction feedback, reserved capacity, native account controls and shielded settlement.

The fresh L1 complements BNB Smart Chain, keeps BNB as its native network token with no new native token, and extends BNB's utility into trading, payments, privacy, and AI scenarios while staying interoperable with the BNB Chain ecosystem.

The latest L1 is being developed alongside BNB Smart Chain, which remains the ecosystem’s broad-based network and streams small groups of transactions called SubBlocks before sealing a full block, with its public testnet being worked towards.

On the upcoming L1, lanes reserve a share of block capacity for eligible activity, such as payments, while unused capacity returns to general traffic. This reduces competition from unrelated activity without guaranteeing that every transaction lands immediately.

It has been revealed that the design also reduces the work of recording changes to the chain’s state. The new L1’s privacy design supports shielded settlement for BNB and admitted BEP-20 tokens. Through AtomicCall, shielded assets can fund selected public operations, such as supported swaps.

The newly introduced L1 retains an Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM)-compatible execution environment. Parallel EVM execution is planned, and protected ordering through a trusted execution environment (TEE) gateway has also been proposed.

BNB Chain invites the crypto community to participate in discussions on its Layer 1,  submitting their questions, use cases, and feedback to the BNB Forum.

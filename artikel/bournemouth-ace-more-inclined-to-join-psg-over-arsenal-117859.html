Bournemouth star Eli Junior Kroupi is more inclined to join reigning European champions PSG over Arsenal, according to a report from Ekrem Konur.

While the 20-year-old might have only made a maiden appearance of the season last month after recently returning from a long-term foot injury, Kroupi has rapidly established himself as one of Europe’s hottest striking starlets.

Bagging 13 Premier League strikes during his maiden campaign among England’s elite last season, Bournemouth’s attacking ace also earned a first senior call-up to France’s squad during November’s international break.

Likewise, coming off the bench and notching a first international strike during Les Bleus’ 2-1 victory against Turkey earlier in the month, Kroupi finds his stock at a red-hot high.

Although TEAMtalk might have reported on Wednesday that Bournemouth have slapped a whopping £100million price tag on the former Lorient marksman, both PSG and Arsenal remain interested in a potential blockbuster swoop.

However, while the Gunners might have been linked with a move for the two-cap international over the summer, Konur is suggesting that Kroupi is more keen on a return to his homeland and a chance to join the reigning European champions. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

While the Bournemouth hierarchy might have made it abundantly clear that they will not allow Kroupi to leave the Vitality in January, PSG are desperate to add attacking reinforcements when the window reopens following Desire Doue’s potentially season-ending knee injury that he sustained last week.

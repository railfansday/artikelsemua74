📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

A 9-year-old boy in Texas was critically injured after he fell out of a moving SUV that was driven by his uncle who was under the influence.

It has been revealed that the boy’s uncle, Oscar Pereira Bustill, was arrested on Sunday, November 22, and has been charged with one count of driving while intoxicated for the second time in less than three years. He was previously hit with a DWI charge in 2023, which prosecutors later dismissed after Bustill, 32, agreed to enter a court-ordered pretrial intervention program.

According to recent reports, authorities learned of the incident in the early hours of Sunday when police responded to the intersection of Bellaire Boulevard and Boone Road in Houston, Texas, around 3:00 a.m. after they received a report of an injured child, according to KHOU.

Officers with the Houston Police Department and emergency medical personnel responded to the scene, and the child was rushed to Memorial Hermann Hospital for treatment.

Police spoke with the other passengers in the vehicle, who explained that Bustill was driving the vehicle with the 9-year-old boy, two other children and three adults inside, per the Houston Chronicle.

One of the passengers said that the group had been at a family member’s quinceañera earlier in the night, according to KTRK. She told authorities she believed Bustill had about four drinks during the party.

That witness also mentioned that she had initially been driving the car, but Bustill took over because she was feeling sick.

It has been revealed that as the group was driving home from the party, the 9-year-old allegedly opened the rear passenger door and fell out of the car. He then hit the pavement and suffered injuries to his legs, back and head.

According to recent reports, bustill also spoke to officers at the scene, and he allegedly refused to submit to a blood test. However, he eventually partook in a blood draw after police obtained a warrant.

Sources indicate that as police continued to investigate the incident, one witness claimed that an altercation broke out among the adults. Police mentioned that the child may have opened the door because he felt unsafe during the altercation.

In a recent development, “From the reports I have, I’d probably be scared in there,” Sgt. Thomas Fendia told the Houston Chronicle. “It sounds like it was a very violent interaction by the adults amongst each other, so it could be that.”.

While the boy’s current condition has not yet been shared, Fendia said he was “not good” as of Sunday.

In a recent development, following his arrest, Bustill was booked into the Harris County Jail. He made his first court appearance on Tuesday, November 24, and has since been released after posting a $30,000 bond.

It’s not at the moment clear if Bustill has entered a plea or retained legal counsel following his arrest. The Houston Police Department did not immediately respond to Us’ request for comment regarding updates in the case.

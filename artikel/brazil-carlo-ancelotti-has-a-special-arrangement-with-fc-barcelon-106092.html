The international break is underway and FC Barcelona intends to avoid an unpleasant surprise with Raphinha. Regularly faced with physical problems in recent months, the Brazilian winger is the subject of particular attention from the Catalan club. This time, the Blaugrana have decided to anticipate things in order to protect their player during his time in the selection. According to information from Mundo DeportivoBarça and the Brazilian federation are in close contact to organize his playing time in advance during this international period. The objective is clear, to allow the former Leeds player to return to Catalonia in full possession of his physical means.

This concerted management should make it possible to avoid any unnecessary risk-taking with Raphinha, while Barça is counting heavily on its number 11 for the rest of the season. Unlike certain previous truces, its use with the Seleção must therefore be supervised in advance, with a common desire to preserve its physical condition. It now remains to be seen what place Carlo Ancelotti will reserve for the winger in his next international meetings.

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Jon Cryer has continued to pop up all around television in the eleven-plus years since his Emmy-winning run on Two and a Half Men came to an end. The former Brat Pack peer enjoyed appearances in everything from NCIS to Robot Chicken, though his most extensive post-Alan Harper role was as the Arrowverse's Lex Luthor in Supergirl. NBC also briefly brought him back into the sitcom game with the short-lived Extended Family alongside Scrubs veteran Donald Faison. Yet, the one thing he hasn't done in nearly a decade is return to the home of his defining megahit comedy.

Industry observers note that that's about to change thanks to Robert and Michelle King. Per Deadline, Cryer has been tapped for a guest appearance in the Good Wife and Elsbeth duo's newly introduced legal thriller series, Cupertino. It'll mark his first role on a CBS show since 2017, when he joined Joel McHale for an episode of his short-lived sitcom The Great Indoors. An exact date has yet to be set for when he'll meet the lawyers of Price, Siffre & Associates, but images have been revealed that tease his turn as the head of a very curious court. "Jon Cryer was perfect, playing a judge you couldn’t tell was a good guy or a bad guy," the Kings shared in an official statement. "We needed someone who could deliver the comedy but also dig into the moral dilemma of a judge who needs to follow the law in a deeply flawed system.”. Cryer's character is Judge Van Linkerspleen, who is described as a high-profile judge working behind the scenes in a top-secret court. As the Kings teased, he's a complex figure caught in a crooked system that values compliance above all else, though he strives to deliver rulings fairly and serve as an individual thinker within his cases. Not only will Michael Price (Mike Colter) and Olivia Siffre (Rachel Keller) be in his presence, but so will Michael's ex, Nichole (Renée Elise Goldsberry), who appears in one of the images in what could be a tease for another legal showdown with her former flame. Cryer will join a cast that also features Ella Stiller, Nik Dodani, Busy Philipps, and Rob McClure, among others. (Related: This newly introduced chip survives 1300°F (700°C) and ...)

Industry observers note that reuniting the Kings with their Evil co-star, Colter, Cupertino has followed former tech start-up lawyer Michael Price and fellow canned attorney Olivia Siffre to fight back against the tech titans of Silicon Valley on behalf of everyone they've cheated. So far, they've been winning, too. From the case of a mother convinced a organization's AI companion pushed her son to patricide to a legendary rapper suing an AI music studio ripping off his sound, the fledgling firm has made a name for itself as the series has become one of the most popular newcomers of the season.

Recent findings suggest that with only a few more episodes until CBS' primetime slate goes on break, though, the show is about to get a bit of a shakeup. Cupertino will begin airing on Sunday nights at 10 p.m. starting on January 17, as Matlock returns for Season 3 on Thursdays. After a successful trial run on the weekend earlier this month, though, it may be the perfect home for the legal thriller.

For now, Cupertino airs on Thursdays at 10 p.m. ET/PT on CBS. Get a look at Cryer in his new guest-starring role above and stay tuned here at Collider for more on his upcoming episode.

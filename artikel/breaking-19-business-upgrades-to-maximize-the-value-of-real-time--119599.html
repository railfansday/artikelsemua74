Breaking: 19 Business Upgrades To Maximize The Value Of Real-Time Payments

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

As instant-payment networks such as FedNow and the RTP network reach more financial institutions, more businesses have the opportunity to send and receive funds within seconds, even on weekends and holidays. However, taking full advantage of that speed requires more than gaining access to a fresh payment network. Legacy workflows built around batch processing and traditional banking hours can create delays and operational gaps even when money moves instantly.

For many companies, the transition to real-time payments will require a fresh look at the internal technology and business practices that support financial operations. Below, Forbes Technology Council members share ways companies can modernize their systems and processes to make real-time payments more efficient, secure and beneficial to the business.

Update ERP payment reconciliation and the capacity behind it. From my experience leading fintech integration projects, connecting payment channels is only part of the work. Internal systems must confirm transactions, update balances and flag duplicates at any hour. Adopt API-driven, automated reconciliation with exception handling, and load-test it at peak volume without maintenance windows. - Mohammad Sharif Daoud, Global Center for AI Excellence. Sources indicate that combine automated matching with clear ownership of exceptions. Routine transactions should reconcile automatically, while unmatched payments, short payments and missing remittance details are routed to accountable teams for timely resolution. This approach reduces manual effort, improves visibility into available cash, and helps finance teams put incoming funds to work sooner while resolving discrepancies faster. - Venus Garg, Elevance Health. Recent findings suggest that providers and participants in the expanding instant payments ecosystem must modernize risk assessment alongside payment infrastructure. Instant settlement leaves little room for error, while added verification and manual review undermine speed. Richer financial data and streamlined fraud, liquidity and other risk assessments can enable more instant payments without sacrificing sound controls. - Brian Reshefsky, EDGE.

Industry observers note that companies should modernize their treasury and cash management systems. Real-time payments mean little if reconciliation, liquidity management and reporting remain outdated. Integrating automation, AI-driven forecasting and real-time financial visibility enables businesses to optimize cash flow, reduce risk and make faster, smarter capital decisions. - Paul Kerslake, N3XUS.

According to recent reports, payment routing deserves more attention. Many companies choose how to send money based on fixed bank relationships or manual processes. Connecting internal systems to multiple payment networks lets them route each payment based on cost, availability and whether the recipient can receive it instantly. That makes real-time payments useful across the business instead of within a single network. - Maghnus Mareneck, Cosmos Labs.

Sources indicate that I’d modernize how procurement negotiates supplier payment terms. If a tech giant can pay immediately upon verified delivery, procurement should be able to negotiate something in return, such as a discount, better pricing or priority access to inventory. That requires procurement and finance to agree on when using cash sooner is worthwhile. The opportunity is to make payment speed part of the negotiation. - Kacee Johnson, radical.

In a recent development, fraud detection and payment authorization need to evolve with real-time payments. When money moves instantly, there’s little time to detect fraud or reverse a transaction. Companies need real-time fraud analysis and stronger verification that a payment was genuinely authorized by the customer, without slowing down legitimate transactions. - Alex Spokoiny, Check Point Software.

Patch cash and liquidity management. When payments settle around the clock, including weekends, treasury can’t rely on end-of-day positions and cutoff times. Modernize to real-time cash visibility and automated funding of accounts so you can pay and collect any time without tying up idle balances. - Aruna Veerappan, Upwork Global Inc.

Recent findings suggest that customer-facing billing and refund workflows should be modernized to take full advantage of real-time payments. Many companies still rely on multi-day settlement, batch disbursements and manual refund processes that frustrate customers accustomed to instant transactions. Modernized workflows deliver instant payouts, real-time refunds and transparent payment status, improving customer experience and loyalty. - Nitin Agarwal, Luminace.

Recent findings suggest that companies should modernize how they track incoming payments. What’s the point of receiving money in seconds if accounting takes two days to recognize it? It’s like getting a package delivered instantly but waiting until tomorrow to open the door. Real-time payments need real-time accounting. - Margarita Simonova, ILoveMyQA.

Companies should modernize their payment approval workflows to keep pace with instant-payment networks. If a supplier payment still waits until the next business day for approval, the money may move instantly, but the business hasn’t gained much. Setting approval limits and delegating authority in advance lets routine payments go through around the clock, while larger or unusual transactions still get reviewed before funds leave the account. - Elton Chan, Second Talent.

Modernize the payment decisioning and exception-management architecture. Real-time settlement atop batch-era fraud controls, liquidity monitoring, reconciliation and exception handling merely accelerates operational risk. 24/7 money movement demands 24/7 intelligence, controls and accountability. Speed without trust is a liability. - Rajjie Sarmey, FutureProof CXO™.

According to recent reports, companies should modernize their liquidity orchestration layer, not just payment gateways. Real-time payments require real-time intelligence across treasury, fraud detection, reconciliation and cash forecasting. The next frontier is agentic liquidity management, where AI continuously anticipates cash needs, dynamically allocates funds and optimizes working capital. Instant settlement should enable instant financial decisions. - Nicola Sfondrini, PWC.

Sources indicate that real-time payments won’t automatically create value. I’d challenge the premise that modernizing one process or internal system is enough. Companies need to assess technical debt, process debt and data debt together. Money can move instantly while the business operates on yesterday’s information. The question leaders need to focus on is whether the entire organization can access and act on that data. - Neda Nia, Stibo Systems.

Modernize how you approve and double-check payouts before money goes out the door. Older payment methods gave finance teams until the end of the day to catch a duplicate invoice, a wrong account or a bad refund before the bank processed it. With instant payments, the money is gone in seconds, so your approval rules and mistake checks have to happen up front, before anyone clicks “Send.” - Vatsal Bhardwaj, Jabali.ai.

Industry observers note that update collections and customer communication. If payment status reaches the CRM overnight, customers who paid at 9 a.m. still get an overdue reminder at noon. At one client, about one in 10 dunning emails went to accounts that had already paid. Streaming payment events into Salesforce stopped those messages, cut “I already paid” support cases by roughly 35%, and released credit holds the same day. - Sergii Grushai, Peeklogic.

Industry observers note that modernize tax determination and allocation. When a customer pays instantly, the tech giant should reserve sales tax, VAT and withholding by jurisdiction and legal entity. Many firms calculate these obligations later from batch reports, overstating available cash and rushing remittances. Link payment events to tax rules and entity records, then test reversals and partial payments so speed does not create a compliance backlog. This clearly separates cash from tax owed. - Jagadish Gokavarapu, Wissen Infotech.

Your customer service layer needs modernization. Here’s why: Real-time payments are final. There’s no recall window. So the “Here’s my money, can I cancel it?” questions spike, and they need answers in seconds, not tomorrow. In our banking data, payments and money movement drive roughly 36% of guided-task volume. Instant money needs instant, accurate answers. - Merlin Bise, Inbenta.

Recent findings suggest that companies should integrate instant-payment networks directly with their ERP or through treasury platforms, ensuring cash inflows and outflows are reflected in real time. Payment initiation, status updates, and automated AR and AP matching can enable straight-through processing, reduce manual intervention and improve cash visibility. This aligns financial operations with payment speed, improving efficiency and working capital management. - Eranda Maldeniya, Enterprise Analytics.

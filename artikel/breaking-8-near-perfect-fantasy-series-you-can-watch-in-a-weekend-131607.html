Recent findings suggest that dirk Gently’s Holistic Detective Agency is a Netflix show most viewers have never heard of, but it's a woefully underrated series. Best described as The Dresden Files, but with an incredible duo, the series centers around the delusionally optimistic Dirk Gently (Samuel Barnett) and his reluctant partner, Todd Brotzman (Elijah Wood), as they go about investigating supernatural crimes. Dirk Gently has everything an audience can expect from a fanatsy/sci-fi/mystery series: interesting cases, deep lore, as well as an emotional core.

However, what truly elevates the series from great to excellent is two primary protagonists, Dirk and Todd. Like any memorable TV duo, the two characters have a sort of odd-couple relationship. Where Dirk walks around like a happy puppy, Todd is sullen and doesn’t always see the bright side to everything. It is this contrast in their demeanor that makes the show so interesting and makes viewers excited about what the odd pair will do next.

It has been revealed that starring David Jason and Sean Astin, The Color of Magic is a two-part British fantasy miniseries based on the first two novels in Terry Pratchett's beloved Discworld series. The story introduces viewers to Rincewind (David Jason), an exceptionally incompetent wizard who has very little magical ability and an even greater talent for getting himself into trouble. His life takes an unexpected turn when he encounters Twoflower (Sean Astin), a cheerful tourist from a distant land who has travelled to Discworld to experience its wonders.

Pratchett’s Discworld novels have one of the best fantasy settings, which ultimately means that they are not easy to bring to life. Thankfully, The Color of Magic gives it a valiant effort, resulting in a world that is so perfectly built that it is easy for non-book readers to see why the Discworld series is such a fan favorite. While The Color of Magic is an exceptional fantasy series that shines for many reasons, Rincewind makes it even better.

Recent findings suggest that most fantasy stories often revolve around powerful wizards, or individuals destined to save the world. Rincewind is practically the opposite. He is a cowardly, largely incompetent wizard whose greatest talent is running away from dangerous situations. This, of course, makes him one of the most interesting characters in fantasy TV.

The series is complex, with lots of moving parts, including the TVA and several versions of Loki. But beneath the complexity is a story about someone who has been viewed as a villain but is actually a hero.

It has been revealed that tom Hiddleston is, as one might expect, fantastic as Loki. However, he does not carry the show alone. Owen Wilson as Mobius and Sophia Di Martino as Sylvie are absolutely riveting. Loki is the perfect example of a show that proves that there is always nuance to a character. Granted, the series is a sci-fi and fantasy fan’s dream come true, but it is also an incredibly thoughtful look at a character who finally begins to understand what his purpose really is.

Released in 2024, Dead Boy Detectives is based on characters from Neil Gaiman's DC Comics universe. The eight-episode series follows two teenage ghosts, Edwin Payne (George Rexstrew) and Charles Rowland (Jayden Revri), who decide to remain on Earth after death rather than move on to the afterlife. Instead, they establish a detective agency dedicated to investigating supernatural mysteries and helping spirits resolve unfinished business.

Dead Boy Detectives doesn’t exactly tread newly introduced waters when it comes to its supernatural/mystery plot. However, it does set itself apart from others in the genre due to its two primary protagonists. Because the series centers on two teenage ghosts who have decided that death shouldn't prevent them from having careers, it is immediately entertaining.

According to recent reports, the series’ main duo give it much of its personality. Charles frequently encourages Edwin to embrace experiences beyond his investigations, while Edwin provides the intelligence and discipline necessary to solve their cases, making the duo a match made in heaven.

The Netflix original series is a masterclass in world-building. Though the show only has two seasons with 18 episodes, it manages to pack so much into its limited time. Another fantasy series that demonstrates that a great duo is the heart of a memorable story, Jonathan Strange & Mr Norrell centers around Mr Norrell (Eddie Marsan), a cautious, scholarly magician who believes magic should be controlled and practiced exclusively by those he considers responsible, and Jonathan Strange (Bertie Carvel), a naturally gifted young man whose extraordinary magical abilities quickly attract attention.

Jonathan Strange & Mr Norrell Key Details.

SEOUL, South Korea, Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- AIPro, an AI video analytics tech giant, has released a free version of VistaPro, its fire and smoke detection monitoring software. The release allows facility operators to use basic functions with their existing CCTV cameras and PCs, confirm whether the software suits their actual site, and then add features as needed.

The free version can be downloaded from the AIPro official website (www.aipro.ai/download) without registration or login. The basic functions provided free of charge have no usage time limit. People connect existing CCTV cameras that support RTSP video streaming to an Intel-based Windows PC that meets the recommended specifications, and the software runs without a separate high-performance graphics card. In a recent development, the free version supports a total of three channels: one AI fire detection channel and two general monitoring channels. The fire detection channel analyzes flames and smoke, and video from multiple cameras can be viewed on a single screen. Detection history, statistics, and automatic reporting are also provided.

According to recent reports, real-time video analysis is performed on the user's PC. Both the free version (Free) and the paid Pro blueprint require an internet connection for updates and version management. Before installation, users can check the recommended PC specifications and CCTV integration requirements on the website. (Related: Tech Report: Deep Learning over the Internet: Training ...)

The free distribution is designed so that users can verify CCTV compatibility and fire and smoke detection in their actual operating environment, and determine the features and operating scale their facilities need.

Recent findings suggest that AIPro recommends first trying the software in a single area where fire risk needs to be monitored. Because camera angles, lighting, and work conditions differ from site to site, the firm explained that it is vital to review detection results at different times of day and decide on the operating approach accordingly. AI analysis results are used by managers to judge on-site conditions and the need for a response.

It has been revealed that real-time alerts, email notifications, and fire and map-based monitoring are provided in the paid Pro initiative. AI detection channels can be added as needed, and a Business plan is available for companies and organizations that operate more cameras or need closed-network or site-specific configurations. Users start with the basic functions and then choose whether to expand according to their operating scale and purpose.

AIPro is an AI video analytics firm founded in 2020 by Professor Cheon-Su Park of Sungkyunkwan University. It has built experience applying video analytics technology in the field for public institutions, including supplying fire and wildfire detection software to the Korea Asset Management Corporation.

AIPro believes that even small stores, warehouses, and workshops can evaluate the feasibility of AI safety management using their existing equipment. Going forward, the tech giant prepares to continue research to enhance fire and smoke detection performance and reduce false alarms, while improving ease of use from installation to reviewing detection results and managing records.

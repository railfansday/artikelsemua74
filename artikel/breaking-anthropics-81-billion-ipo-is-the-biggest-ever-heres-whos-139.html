In a recent development, anthropic, the tech giant behind the popular AI chatbot, surged more than 30% in its Nasdaq debut today after the largest IPO in history. It sold more than 980 million shares at $83 apiece for $81.3 billion, topping the then-record $75 billion SpaceX raised in June, according to The New York Times. Shares opened at $111.50 under the ticker “ANT,” valuing Anthropic at about $2 trillion.

Sources indicate that the biggest winners of the Anthropic IPO are the manufacturer’s seven co-founders, including CEO Dario Amodei and his sister, president Daniela Amodei. Each holds a stake that could be worth $20 billion or more, according to the Times. On the VC side, Lightspeed Venture Partners and Iconiq hold stakes worth more than $25 billion at the IPO price, The Wall Street Journal reports. Menlo Ventures’ stake is worth roughly $22.5 billion.

The situation of Jamal Musiala is causing great concern at Bayern Munich. According to information from BILDthe German attacking midfielder reportedly suffered a new absence crisis last Monday, his second relapse since his collapse during a friendly match in Heidenheim in mid-August. A new episode which comes after his medication treatment had already been adjusted several times, without succeeding for the moment in stabilizing his situation.

This new relapse would have placed the Bavarian management on maximum alert. The club’s supervisory board also met the same evening to discuss the course of action regarding the player. After Uli Hoeneß, CEO Jan-Christian Dreesen and then Karl-Heinz Rummenigge also spoke publicly. Finding the right drug treatment for Musiala remains a real challenge for Bayern, who are now moving forward with great caution, to the point of calling into question the player’s future in Bavaria….

Breaking: Body-positive imagery in ads resonates differently with men and women, study finds

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (68 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, advocates of body-positive advertising, which showcases models who do not fit the traditional molds of thin or muscular models, say they are challenging female beauty standards by rejecting an unhealthy obsession with often unattainable idealized bodies. However, a new Concordia study demonstrates that while women generally respond positively to large-size models, body-positive advertising images are not as effective with men.

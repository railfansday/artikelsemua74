AMSTERDAM and LONDON, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Cboe Global Markets, Inc. (Cboe: CBOE), a leading global markets operator and pioneer in equity and index derivatives, today declared that Cboe Clear Europe, its pan-European central counterparty (CCP), has cleared more than 1.5 billion client equity trades so far in 2026, eclipsing its previous annual record of 1.49 billion in 2022. The milestone reflects continued client adoption of Cboe Clear Europe's services, reinforcing its position as a leading pan-European cash equities CCP and underscoring growing demand for competitive, open-access clearing.

Vikesh Patel, Global Head of Clearing and President, Cboe Clear Europe, said: "Surpassing our previous annual record at this point in the year demonstrates the trust market participants place in Cboe Clear Europe and the value of the efficiencies we offer. Because we compete for every trade we clear, this represents participants actively choosing us for our cost effectiveness, risk management capabilities and capital and operational efficiencies, as well as their confidence in the benefits of the open-access clearing model for European equities.". Recent findings suggest that cboe Clear Europe's open-access approach allows participants to consolidate their cash equities clearing at a single CCP regardless of where they trade, unlocking significant netting efficiencies and lower settlement costs. During 2026, Cboe Clear Europe's cash equity network expanded to 56 venues and market segments, the broadest coverage of any European CCP. Cboe Clear Europe supports two primary venue connection models: interoperability, which gives participants full choice of CCP; and preferred clearing, where trades are cleared at a participant's chosen CCP when selected by both counterparties to a trade. Cboe Clear Europe has also been at the forefront of industry efforts to expand competition in European equities clearing through the EU's Market Integration and Supervision Package (MISP). The firm co-signed an industry letter alongside leading buy-side and sell-side trade associations calling for mandatory interoperability among, at a minimum, significant CCPs for equities as part of MISP (full letter here).

Patel added: "Europe is at a decisive point in turning its ambition for more competitive, efficient and integrated capital markets into reality. Our volumes demonstrate that participants want choice in clearing, and now is the time to embed that competition across the market. Mandating clearing interoperability across all major equities CCPs would let firms consolidate their clearing where it is most efficient for them and help deliver the regulatory objective of more integrated European capital markets.".

Alongside cash equities, Cboe Clear Europe has continued to expand its securities financing transactions (SFTs) service, which has introduced central clearing to a market that has historically operated bilaterally. Released in 2025 for loans of European equities and ETFs, the service was extended this year to fixed income and US equities lendable assets, delivering the benefits of central clearing across the global securities lending market.

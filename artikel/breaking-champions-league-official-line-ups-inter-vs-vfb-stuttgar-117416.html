Inter rely on Pio Esposito and Marcus Thuram again, with Djed Spence and Carlos Augusto on the wings in a must-win match against impressive VfB Stuttgart.

It kicks off at San Siro at 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET).

You can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from this game and PSG-Roma on the Champions League Liveblog.

The Nerazzurri were shaken at the weekend by fumbling a two-goal advantage to be held 4-4 by Atalanta in Serie A.

They had started the season losing both Champions League fixtures to Real Madrid and Club Brugge, but got back on track winning the next two against Shakhtar Donetsk and Feyenoord.

Hakan Calhanoglu suffered a thigh strain in Bergamo and is out for at least a month, along with Ange-Yoan Bonny, while Lautaro Martinez is not yet ready to be risked from the start after returning from a month out with a similar problem. That means Pio Esposito hopes to continue his fine form after a hat-trick against Atalanta, partnered by Marcus Thuram.

Henrikh Mkhitaryan and Petar Sucic are chosen to step into midfield, while Manuel Akanji returns in defence.

Carlos Augusto is also preferred to Federico Dimarco on the left flank.

VfB Stuttgart are ahead of Inter in the Champions League table coming in, as they beat Viking and Slovan Bratislava, drew 1-1 with Atletico Madrid, then lost 2-0 away to Galatasaray.

Angelo Stiller, Leo Sauer and Luca Jaquez are back in the squad despite carrying knocks, while Dan-Axel Zagadou, Stefan Drljaca, Justin Diehl and Leonidas Stergiou are not on the UEFA list.

Inter: J Martinez; Bisseck, Akanji, Bastoni; Spence, Barella, Sucic, Mkhitaryan, Carlos Augusto; P Esposito, M Thuram.

Stuttgart: Seimen; Jeltsch, Chabot, Hendriks; Vagnoman, Nartey, Spalt, Mittelstäd; El Khannouss, Demirovic, Führich.

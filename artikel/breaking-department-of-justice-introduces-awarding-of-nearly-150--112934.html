Breaking: Department of Justice Introduces Awarding of Nearly $150 Million to Hire Over 1,000 Law Enforcement Officers and Deputies

🌐 Source: Justice • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (95 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

NEWLY INTRODUCED ORLEANS — Through the Office of Community Oriented Policing Services (COPS Office), the Department of Justice today unveiled it has awarded nearly $150 million under the 2026 COPS Hiring Program (CHP). CHP is a competitive grant program that provides funding directly to state, local, tribal, and territorial law enforcement agencies to pay for the entry-level salaries and fringe benefits of newly hired and rehired full-time sworn officers/deputies to make America safe again. In the Eastern District of Louisiana (4) municipalities were awarded funding totaling more than 7 million dollars.

The debut display of the senior striker, who turns 36 on December 1, when he came off the bench midway through the second half in Saturday's draw at Yorkshire rivals Bradford City, also preceded a bit of chatter among Rovers followers.

Industry observers note that rovers chief Grant McCann revealed: "We've just lacked a little bit of character at times. He's certainly not been quiet on the training ground.

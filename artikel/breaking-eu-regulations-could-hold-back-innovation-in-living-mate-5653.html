Breaking: EU regulations could hold back innovation in living materials

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (75 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that engineered living materials (ELMs) are materials that incorporate living organisms, such as bacteria, fungi or yeast, to perform specific functions. These organisms can produce substances, respond to signals from their surroundings or help repair damage to the material itself. Some organisms naturally possess properties that researchers can put to use. For example, certain bacteria produce cellulose, a substance that can be used to make clothing and textiles.

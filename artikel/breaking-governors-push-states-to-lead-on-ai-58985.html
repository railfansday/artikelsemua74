Breaking: Governors Push States to Lead on AI

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 22 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (80 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

On Bloomberg This Weekend, Governors Wes Moore, Spencer Cox, Josh Stein and Mike Braun tell host Christina Ruffini that states need to take a larger role in shaping AI and data center policy as concerns grow over electricity costs, water use, jobs and consumer protections. The bipartisan group says data centers should largely pay for their own energy needs and emphasizes local control, while differing over how aggressively the federal government should regulate AI.

It has been revealed that planes are, without a doubt, the fastest way to get around the map in GTA VI. They can help you explore areas to find points of interest or simply get to a pinged location in a flash.

Sources indicate that however, finding a plane is easier mentioned than done. In GTA VI, planes are typically found in very specific locations such as airports and airstrips.

In a recent development, if you are looking for a sweet plane to take up into the skies, you have come to the right place. Here are the best places to get a plane in Grand Theft Auto VI.

Sources indicate that players can find an airstrip on the southeast side of the Grassrivers in Mariana County. This airstrip has a plane that’s easy to control, and there is usually no one around, making it easy to steal.

Recent findings suggest that in the northern section of Belville in Leonard County, you will find Lake Leonida. Next to one of the docks on the lake, you will find a plane that’s easy to steal because the open water acts as a runway and there aren’t many people around. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Recent findings suggest that in Borrachero Sound, a very small area on the west side of Kelly County, you will find an airstrip that lies next to the water. You'll usually find more than one plane here, so you’ll have options to choose from.

In the Waning Sands region of Leonard County, you will find an airstrip just outside of Belville. Similar to the plane spot mentioned earlier, you will also find more than one plane at this airstrip.

Last, but certainly not least, one of the best places to find a plane is, of course, the Vice City International Airport. Known as VCIA, you can find this airport in the southwest section of Vice Dale County. (Related: Breaking: Course Launch Community Event...)

According to recent reports, keep in mind that VCIA has high security, so stealing a plane can be tricky. However, it is an airport, so it has the most planes available, making it one of the best places to find one.

Industry observers note that if you’d like to get a plane in a more natural way, that doesn’t involve stealing, you can find one during the McKittrick Farm side quest. To start this quest, examine the poster inside the Starlet Motel office (mandatory location in Chapter 2), and the location of the farm will be marked on your map.

According to recent reports, since you’ll be stealing the planes mentioned in this guide, it’s wise to learn how to avoid the cops when flying. Even though the places I mentioned here tend to have almost no one around, with the exception of VCIA, there’s still a possibility that someone will spot you stealing it or report the crime once they realize it's missing.

The best way to avoid cops' detection when flying a plane is to fly at low altitude. Flying low makes it harder for cops to see you, as buildings, trees, and other structures will help block their line of sight.

Recent findings suggest that you only really need to worry about this if the heat star level reaches 3 to 4, when police bring in the helicopters. Otherwise, you can easily lose cops by simply flying away due to how fast planes go in the game.

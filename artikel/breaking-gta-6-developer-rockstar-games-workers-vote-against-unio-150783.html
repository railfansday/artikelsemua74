Employees of “Grand Theft Auto VI” developer Rockstar Games voted against union recognition this week.

In a Nov. 26 vote that included 1,544 staff members (93.2% voter turnout across the Rockstar Games workforce), 75.3% voted against Rockstar Game Workers Union’s recognition with the Independent Workers of Great Britain (IWGB) and 24.7% voted in support. As a result of the vote, Rockstar Games is not required to recognize the union and regularly engage in collective bargaining with reps. Sources indicate that “For anyone at Rockstar who believes in a fairer workplace, a fairer sector and a fairer society—this isn’t a sign to retreat, it’s a sign to double down on what we stand for,” the IWGB Rockstar Game Workers Union said in a statement. “Hundreds of our coworkers voted in favour of our union at Rockstar, yet sadly we recognize we have more work to do to build understanding and win the trust of more of our colleagues. We’re ready to put in the effort to make that happen.

“We are extraordinarily proud of our team, the culture we’ve built together and the launch of ‘Grand Theft Auto 6,'” Rockstar Games head of publishing Jennifer Kolbe said in a statement.

Industry observers note that A Rockstar Games spokesperson added: “This result reflects our team’s strong support for continuing the direct relationships, collaboration and open dialogue that have helped make Rockstar Games so innovative and strong. As we look ahead, we’re excited to build on all we’ve accomplished together.”.

See more gaming news from the week in the roundup below.

The wait for “Avengers: Doomsday” is almost over, and soon, fans can play in that world in addition to watching the story unfold.

It has been revealed that to celebrate the movie hitting theaters Dec. 18, Scopely, in collaboration with Marvel Games, is uniting “Monopoly Go!” with Marvel Studios’ highly anticipated “Avengers: Doomsday” for an all-fresh season of “Doomsday Go!”.

Sources indicate that this season, which runs Nov. 25 — Jan. 27, brings fresh shields, tokens and dice with richer animations and visual effects and visually enhanced super stickers and super tokens.

The “Doomsday Go!” album is packed with 23 unique sticker sets featuring Marvel Super Heroes from Thor and Iron Man to Deadpool, Wolverine, Spider-Man, the X-Men and more. Players can collect super hero team sets, follow story sets, explore whimsical designs and piece together puzzles as Tycoons build a collection worthy of the Marvel Universe.

Wargaming’s “World of Tanks” will feature actor Steve Buscemi as the star of its Holiday Ops 2027, the highly anticipated annual winter celebration across the video game franchise.

In a recent development, buscemi stars as Coldstone, an observant investigative reporter (and tank enthusiast) who is introduced in a upcoming short film released to promote the “World of Tanks” Holiday Ops season. When the regular anchor is unable to go on air, he takes over the live broadcast just as a series of holiday disasters begins to pile up on screen. Sensing a conspiracy and spotting clues others miss, Coldstone uncovers the truth and saves the holidays. Once the broadcast is over, Coldstone invites players into World of Tanks as a tank commander, ready to set assignments and share festive rewards.

“What I liked about Coldstone is that he doesn’t just go along with the story. He pays attention and has a real eye for detail,” Buscemi mentioned. “I hope World of Tanks players enjoy the retro style, humor, and holiday spirit in the film.”.

Lenny Kravitz has been revealed as the new Elusive Target in “HITMAN World of Assassination” reprising the role of his iconic “007 First Light” character, the Pirate King Bawma.

Per IO Interactive (the developer behind both the “Hitman” franchise and “007 First Light”), “In this latest mission, Agent 47 is deployed to Ambrose Island on the instructions of the Pirate King, Bawma. A former child soldier turned ruthless ruler of Aleph, Bawma was last seen in IO Interactive’s Game of the Year nominee 007 First Light, where he commands both fear and respect in equal measure. This time around, Bawma has a rather unconventional assignment for Agent 47, insisting on directing the operation himself and guiding 47 throughout the mission via in-ear dialogue. Once 47 tracks Bawma down through the ruins and jungles of Ambrose Island, it soon becomes clear that something far more valuable than a simple target is at stake. Lenny Kravitz lends both his voice and likeness to his reprisal of Bawma, and the mission brings a bright, upbeat tone and an easy-going holiday feel to World of Assassination.”.

The Battle of the Boroughs Minecraft Competition will return to New York City for the fifth consecutive year.

Hosted by Newly introduced York City Public Schools and the NYC Mayor’s Office of Media and Entertainment (MOME), the competition incentivizes students in grades 3-12 to build a more inclusive and sustainable culture while developing critical thinking, computer science, public speaking and problem-solving skills. This year’s prompt will challenge students to develop Minecraft builds that create a safer, more connected Penn Station neighborhood.

Recent findings suggest that submissions for elementary, middle and high school teams are now open on the New York City Public Schools website and close Jan. 6.

ITV Studios unveiled on Monday a faithful, nostalgia-packed “Roblox” experience based on the original 1960s TV series “Thunderbirds.”.

The game introduces a latest generation to the series by recreating the look and spirit of the show, from the famous launch sequences to the craft designs fans grew up with, all with a modern twist.

“Recreating Thunderbirds, such an iconic piece of British television and a treasured brand at ITV Studios, required immense care, passion and dedication,” Ashley Lewis, managing director of Metavision, noted. “By pairing the unmistakable DNA and enduring spirit of the 1960s original with Roblox’s rapidly evolving technical capabilities, we’ve been able to bring Tracy Island and its iconic rescues to life with truly impressive scale and fidelity. We hope we’ve created an authentic experience for long-standing fans while introducing a fresh generation to the excitement and imagination of Thunderbirds.”.

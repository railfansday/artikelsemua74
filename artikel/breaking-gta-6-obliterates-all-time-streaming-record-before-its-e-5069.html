You'd better get used to seeing GTA 6 break records, because the game's not even out yet in most countries and it's already crushing long-standing numbers.

Indeed, the hype is so colossal that Rockstar's open worlder broke the all-time record for livestream viewers just minutes after its release in regions where it's already the 19th November.

According to Streams Charts' data, GTA 6 has peaked a frankly absurd 7.2 million viewers across streaming platforms Twitch (3.1 million), YouTube (2.7 million), and Kick (1.4 million), and others.

The crazy part is that this figure could well be eclipsed as GTA 6 becomes available in more regions, and more streamers start to broadcast their gameplay.

Team-based shooter Valorant previously held the all-time record, which was set back in 2020, when watching livestreams could net you access to the game's PC beta. (Related: Transformer-based Encoder-Decoder Models...)

It peaked at 1.7 million viewers, meaning that GTA 6 has absolutely demolished the record — and honestly, it's hard to imagine anything topping it for years and years to come.

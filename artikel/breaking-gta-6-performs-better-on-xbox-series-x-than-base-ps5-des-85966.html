Even though Sony has paid god-only-knows how much money to market GTA 6 as a borderline PS5 exclusive, it turns out that Rockstar's magnum opus actually runs better on Xbox.

This is according to those tech wizards over at Digital Foundry, who have spent hours comparing the open world title across base PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X, and Xbox Series S. (Related: Ex-Rockstar artist explains main difference between Red...)

Recent findings suggest that now, we already know that GTA 6 isn't perfect on the standard PS5. It suffers from minor but sometimes prolonged frame rate drops during busy scenes — whether you're speeding through heavy traffic or fighting for your life in a particularly explosive combat encounter. (Related: Latest Update: This relaxing cat-petting browser game i...)

But in a rather shocking twist, the Xbox Series X outdoes the PS5 in this department. While Digital Foundry's testing reveals that the frame rate dips are still present on the Series X, they're nowhere near as frequent.

They write: "PS5 suffers substantially more frequent frame rate drops than Series X, which is compounded by the fact that the Xbox machine also benefits from 120Hz VRR support to smooth out minor dips.".

The article continues: "Ultimately, Series X is meaningfully better off.".

This isn't what you'd expect, given that every PlayStation-related GTA 6 advertisement and trailer has boasted that the game 'Plays Best on PS5'.

Okay, so that slogan might technically be true if we're counting the PS5 Pro, where GTA 6 does perform best by some distance, thanks to a combination of the console's PSSR upscaler and increased GPU power.

But on base hardware, it's Microsoft that wins out.

"Overall, the PS5 Pro leads the rankings with its superior visual detail and rock-solid 30fps target, followed by the Xbox Series X — which edges out the standard PS5 through better performance stability," Digital Foundry concludes.

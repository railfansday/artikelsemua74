The likes of Cristiano Ronaldo and even Salah's former Liverpool teammate, James Milner, notably continued playing at the highest level into their late 30s after taking extreme care of their bodies over the years.

Fast-forward to 2026 – having left Liverpool in the summer – the 34-year-old joined Trabzonspor. His commitment to fitness has kept him in good form in Turkey so far. Beyond the football, though, he also caused a stir online when he shared an image of his impressive six-pack on social media.

According to the Daily Mail, a huge part of Salah's secret to being in such fine physical shape is his diet. The Egypt international has his own personal chef, who is tasked with preparing all the right foods to keep the winger firing on all cylinders. According to recent reports, it's claimed that he eats broccoli with almost every single meal, along with other 'superfoods'. This is just part of his recipe for success. The Liverpool icon is also extremely fond of a dish from his home country, called koshary, which is a mixture of fried rice, pasta, pulses, onions and tomato sauce.

According to recent reports, in 2022, he went into more detail about what his favourite foods are and even spoke about what he likes to eat when treating himself. Even then, however, it's clear how health-conscious he is:.

"I prefer broccoli, sweet potatoes, fish, chicken and always salad. When I indulge in a great restaurant in London, I prefer sushi. I eat pizza once a month at the most. I love hamburgers, but I hardly ever eat them because afterwards I feel heavy!".

It has been revealed that as well as his healthy eating, he also looks after his body 24/7 in other ways. Inside his home, Salah has two different gym rooms along with some incredible equipment that helps to keep him fit. He's stated to have a hyperbaric chamber in his house, which aims to help the lungs spread more oxygen around the body by having air pressure levels three times higher than normal conditions.

He also has cryotherapy facilities which include ice-led machines which constrict blood vessels to decrease swelling. It's incredible to see the lengths Salah goes to when it comes to his training regime. As a Muslim, the forward has to adapt his routine during Ramadan, which includes 2am gym sessions.

GIVEMESPORT Key Statistic: Mohamed Salah is the top goalscorer in Premier League history for Liverpool, having eclipsed Robbie Fowler's tally of 128 in 2023.

Having defined the very best moments of the Jurgen Klopp years, he began a newly introduced era at the Merseyside-based club by scoring and assisting on the opening day of the 2024/25 Premier League season against Ipswich Town in what was Arne Slot's first competitive game in charge.

In a recent development, despite his brilliant form in 2024/25, there were doubts about Salah's future as his contract was due to run out in 2025. But, thankfully for Liverpool fans around the world, the club and Salah were able to agree to a deal as he put pen to paper on a fresh two-year contract in April 2025.

Despite putting pen to paper on a deal until 2027, Salah unveiled that he'd be cutting that contract short and departing at the end of the 2025-26 season. He then joined Trabzonspor in the summer of 2026, signing a two-year contract in August. It took him until just September to score his first hat-trick for his newly introduced side, doing so against defending champions Galatasaray.

Still fit and firing at 34, it just goes to show how well Salah has looked after his body.

One of the more recent staples of GTA games is being able to take your hard-stolen money and use it to purchase properties and keep the income flowing, something that Grand Theft Auto 6 changes up.

Recent findings suggest that money is a lot harder to come by in GTA 6, and since Jason and Lucia are on the run, money isn't used on properties outside of purchasable garages. They're crucial, though, so let's get into it.

Recent findings suggest that as with many things in Grand Theft Auto 6, Vice City Real Estate only crops up during Chapter 2, specifically after you complete the mission "Daily Driver" for Moses. This mission sees you going to steal a vehicle back for Moses, who will then tell you about the app and how it can be used to Jason and Lucia's advantage. (Related: Predicting and detecting failures: How Azure strengthen...)

In a recent development, although Vice City Real Estate might sound like the place to get houses and businesses, its purpose in GTA 6 is to let you buy garages around Leonida and give yourself more vehicle inventory. Each garage you buy will give you another vehicle slot, allowing you to build up a sizeable collection.

It has been revealed that there are 12 garages in total, with eight of them being purchased on the Vice City Real Estate app and the others being tied to the story. To get them all, which also unlocks the "Little Boxes" achievement, you'll need to spend a total of $160,000. That might have been easy in previous games, but that's a pretty hefty amount in GTA 6.

I'd recommend getting a garage any time you've got a decent amount of money rolling in, as they're a sound investment and it's very easy to find yourself with more cars than you have space for.

Of all the mechanics available in Probably Stolen, Augs are definitely in my top three. The story goes like this: people have been receiving neural implant augmentation with an AI installed that's supposed to help improve their life. Unfortunately, the AI has gone rogue, and it's learned how to take over the body of its host after implantation. These AI zombies, called Augs, are incredibly dangerous, and will kidnap people to force implantation to try and take over the Station.

In a recent development, to avoid this, Security has tasked you, the Shopkeeper, with identifying and eliminating the Augs. Heres everything you need to know to avoid getting captured and taken over.

Sources indicate that augs are introduced on day 12. From there, they show up to your shop every six to eight days. Their presence is measured as a score; it goes down by 25 points for every Aug you shoot and goes up by 10 for every Aug you leave alive. If the presence score reaches zero and you have a Business Permit (though I'm not sure why that's a requirement), then the Augs are permanently gone.

It has been revealed that if you start the game with the Aug Menace perk, Augs can start showing up from day one and their presence never goes away. With this perk, and on higher difficulties, if you hit 100 Aug presence, the game ends.

When Augs are first introduced, you'll be presented with an identification guide. This will give you the tells you need to watch out for when deciding if someone is an Aug.

Industry observers note that there are four tiers of Aug based on how easy they are to spot, with the least obvious being Tier One. Remember that Augs don't need to have every sign to be authentic; they just need to have have one or two. (Related: Latest Update: At least seven progressives voted agains...)

The easiest way to guarantee that you're getting your identification right is to get an Aug Scanner, which can scan each customer once and will return either a positive or negative result.

If you identify someone as an Aug, you need to shoot them with the shotgun. You'll have the option to put the weapon down if you suspect you've gotten it wrong, so don't be afraid to hit the button even if you're not 100% sure; the worst that happens if you pull the gun and don't shoot a human is they walk away. You can't report Augs to Security, as trying to do that will make them flee and will cause Aug presence to rise. Failing to idenfity them will also cause Aug presence to rise.

Sources indicate that whenever you shoot, Security comes by to verify the kill. If it was an Aug, you get a reward and they clean up the mess and walk away. If it was just a normal customer, however, you catch a manslaughter charge and lose a lot of reputation with pretty much everyone. Shoot too many humans and you get arrested, ending your run.

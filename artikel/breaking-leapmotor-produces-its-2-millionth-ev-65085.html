📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Leapmotor just produced its 2 millionth vehicle, all EVs of course. The EV startup has seen a meteoric rise in the highly competitive Chinese EV market, and increasingly abroad. Of course, most of us in the US and even in Europe know nothing or next to nothing about Leapmotor, but it has clearly been more effective than hundreds or even thousands of other companies that have tried to launch and scale up EV production.

Industry observers note that one interesting thing to note regarding this milestone: it’s peanuts compared to Leapmotor’s prepares for the next few years. In 2026 alone, Leapmotor plans to produce one million vehicles, and it’s on track to do so. That means that, this year, the company plans to produce as many vehicles as half of Leapmotor’s cumulative total at the moment. In a recent development, naturally, the firm’s plan is to keep growing. As part of that effort, it just introduced a new model, the Lafa 5 compact hatchback, with pre-sales starting earlier today. Also, another new model, the D19 SUV, just had its 50,000th unit produced.

So, yes, keep an eye on Leapmotor. It’s already a wildly successful young EV manufacturer, and it looks like it is going to continue growing fast.

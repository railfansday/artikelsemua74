Two Libyan soldiers have died from their injuries sustained during a Ukrainian attack on a Russian warship in docked in the port of Benghazi.

Libya is presently divided between a UN-recognized government in Tripoli and a parallel administration in the east of the country that controls Benghazi.

The head of the eastern administration, Oussama Hamad, said the two men had died "as a result of their injuries during the treacherous Ukrainian aggression against the port of Benghazi.".

The Libyan National Army General Command said a response would come "at the appropriate time and place.".

Earlier, on Monday, Libya's Foreign ministry denounced the attacks and noted it didn't authorize "any foreign military operations within [Libya's] territory or territorial waters.

The naval drone attack that occurred on Sunday was claimed by Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy on Monday. On Tuesday, Ukraine's Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha defended the attack.

"Ukraine is not 'dragging' anyone into the war or 'transferring' the war anywhere," Sybiha stated on Telegram.

Sources indicate that "Ukraine respects the sovereignty and territorial integrity of Libya. We emphasize that Ukraine's actions are aimed at countering Russian armed aggression and are not intended ‌to harm Libya or its people.".

Liverpool are facing renewed interest from Bayern Munich in Florian Wirtz and Rio Ngumoha as the German giants prepare for the possibility of losing Michael Olise or Jamal Musiala.

Bayern are assessing several attacking options across Europe, with Manchester City winger Jérémy Doku also featuring on their shortlist.

However, convincing Liverpool to part with two players who could have an enormous influence on the club’s future under Andoni Iraola will be particularly difficult.

According to TEAMtalk, Bayern are maintaining a strong interest in Wirtz and Ngumoha as uncertainty surrounds the futures of Olise and Musiala.

The German champions have yet to finalise a contract extension with Olise, who has attracted interest from Real Madrid and Barcelona.

Both Liverpool players scored and provided an assist during Sunday’s impressive 4-0 victory over Manchester United.

However, Bayern’s chances of signing Ngumoha could soon suffer a significant setback.

A recent CaughtOffside report revealed that Liverpool are close to finalising a latest long-term contract for the 18-year-old winger amid growing interest from European heavyweights.

Bayern are also monitoring Doku, despite the Belgian international signing a contract extension with Manchester City until 2031 earlier this year.

Liverpool should firmly resist any approach for either player.

Wirtz is beginning to demonstrate why the Reds invested so heavily in bringing him to Anfield, with Iraola seemingly helping the German international rediscover his confidence and attacking influence.

Ngumoha, meanwhile, represents exactly the type of emerging talent Liverpool should be building around rather than cashing in on. (Related: Building the next generation of multimodal AI experienc...)

The teenager’s fearless approach, explosive acceleration and ability to unsettle defenders make him an exciting long-term prospect.

Bayern’s interest is understandable, but their recruitment problems should not become Liverpool’s concern.

Securing Ngumoha to an improved contract and continuing to build the attack around Wirtz should be straightforward priorities for the Anfield hierarchy.

At 18 and 23 respectively, the pair could potentially form the foundation of Liverpool’s attack for much of the next decade.

Selling either player would undermine that vision, regardless of how financially attractive Bayern’s eventual proposals might be.

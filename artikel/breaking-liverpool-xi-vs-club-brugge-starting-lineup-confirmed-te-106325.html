In a recent development, "I think with Cody it's a soleus injury, it's a muscle injury,” Iraola remarked at his pre-match press conference. "It's not a big thing but I think we expected that he was out. He's going to be out even for the weekend [against Everton]. It's more predictable.

Recent findings suggest that "With Ryan, it's difficult to say. I think he is having pain. These things in the fascia are difficult to solve.

"You know sometimes it's about managing the pain, but now it's not in a good place and let's see if we can find ways to solve a little bit the problem. But injuries [like this] are not easy to go normally.".

In Gravenberch’s absence, Trey Nyoni gets the nod in midfield alongside Florian Wirtz.

It has been revealed that jeremie Frimpong and Virgil van Dijk were welcome returnees against United following respective injury scares, while Rio Ngumoha played a starring role in the demolition job on Sunday in place of Gakpo on the left flank. The phenomenal young winger gets a rest here, though, as do his club captain and Alexis Mac Allister.

Alexander Isak led the line by example on Sunday, and gets the nod once more here, with Hugo Ekitike still recovering from his Achilles rupture.

Neither Giovanni Leoni nor Conor Bradley are fit enough to make the squad, though they are thought to be closing in on returns from their respective long-term knee injuries.

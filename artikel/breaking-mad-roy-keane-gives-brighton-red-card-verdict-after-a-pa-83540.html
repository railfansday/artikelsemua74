Roy Keane said Brighton’s “sloppy” finishing cost them dearly but gave all the credit to Hull City's determination after they stunned the Seagulls with two late goals to claim a 2-0 Premier League victory.

Brighton were made to pay for their wastefulness when Oli McBurnie struck twice in stoppage time, following Costinha's late dismissal for a second yellow card.

And former Manchester United captain Keane believes Hull deserve enormous credit for their fighting spirit after sustained pressure from Albion.

"It's a mad game. The sending off had a substantial impact. Brighton were desperately trying to win the game, and you have to give credit to them, but they left spaces and gaps," Keane told Sky Sports.

It has been revealed that "It's brilliant from Hull, great fighting spirit and a bit of quality. Great composure from McBurnie. You think McBurnie has missed the opportunity, but what a huge goal.".

Keane was also keen to highlight the contribution of Hull goalkeeper Tzolakis, who produced a string of outstanding saves to frustrate Fabian Hurzeler's side.

"Instead of being critical of Brighton, you have to give credit to the goalkeeper," he added.

"They might not have Brighton's quality, but they revealed great fighting spirit, composure, and stayed in the game; you have to give them huge credit for that.".

The result leaves Brighton second in the Premier League table with 22 points from 11 matches, while Hull climb to 11th with 14 points.

Albion will look to respond when they welcome Newcastle United to the Amex Stadium on Sunday, November 29 (2.05pm).

Hull, meanwhile, travel to Crystal Palace on the same afternoon as they look to build on an impressive victory.

Manchester United are reportedly keen on a move for Fulham defender Calvin Bassey in the January transfer window, but they should be looking at David Affengruber.

The 25-year-old was very impressive as the Cottagers held Manchester City to a 1-1 draw at the Etihad Stadium on Saturday.

Rodrigo Muniz's early goal saw the visitors take the lead before Elliot Anderson's volley after half-time. (Related: Your “um” and pauses could reveal early dementia risk...)

Affengruber joined Fulham from Elche in the summer transfer window for around £8.5million, in a deal that has aged remarkably well.

The Austria international is under contract until 2030 with the option of an additional year and will not come cheap, but may well be worth the money.

Alternative target, Bassey has just a year left on his deal with the option of an additional year, with Fulham presently in talks to extend it further.

It has been a slow start to the season for Fulham, with summer appointment Alvaro Arbeloa having faced some early pressure.

The Cottagers lost their first three Premier League games, conceding seven goals in the process, including three against Chelsea and three against Crystal Palace.

Affengruber then entered the team and Fulham have conceded more than one goal in just one Premier League game since, keeping three clean sheets.

The Austria international was excellent again on Saturday, holding his own to keep Erling Haaland quiet and virtually in his pocket throughout the 90 minutes. (Related: Think AI "knows" what it’s doing? Scientists say think ...)

He was impressive on the ball, too, completing 82 per cent of his passes, including 12 that broke lines, and three of his six attempted long balls.

But it was Affengruber's defensive presence that really caught the eye. He made three tackles and three blocks, more than anybody.

The defender also made six clearances - the joint-most - and six recoveries, winning seven of the duels he contested.

While Fulham would be hesitant to sell Affengruber in the middle of the season, keeping Bassey at the club would be an attractive consolation prize.

Still, it would take a significant offer to land the centre-back, who arrived just months ago for £8.5m.

Fulham's record sale came in 2023, when Alexsandar Mitrovic joined Al Hilal for a fee in the region of £50m.

Man United would almost certainly have to come close to breaking that fee if they are to sign Affengruber in January and they would be getting a high-quality player in return.

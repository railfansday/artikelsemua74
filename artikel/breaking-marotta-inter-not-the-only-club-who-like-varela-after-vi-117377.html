Inter President Beppe Marotta admits they are keeping track of Monza striker Gustavo Varela, but warns ‘we are not the only ones who like him’ on the transfer market.

The Nerazzurri are in action in the Champions League this evening, hosting VfB Stuttgart at 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET). (Related: Hosting your Models and Datasets on Hugging Face Spaces...)

You can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from this game and PSG-Roma on the Champions League Liveblog.

President Marotta spoke to Sky Sport Italia before kick-off and was inevitably asked about the reports that Inter had been scouting Varela for Monza.

“Inter are not the only ones who like Varela, as there are many other clubs interested too. It is normal also that, being so close to Monza geographically, our directors should visit the games.

“Varela is a wonderful surprise of the Serie A season.”.

The Portugal international was relatively unknown before coming to Monza in the summer, but has found the net 11 times in his first 11 Serie A appearances. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

There were also two goals in his only Coppa Italia match, a remarkable tally for the 21-year-old, who also earned his first senior cap for Portugal.

Inter are among many clubs keeping a close eye on Varela, after Arsenal, Napoli, Juventus, Liverpool and Borussia Dortmund.

Meanwhile, Marotta was also asked about the negotiations to extend the contract of Carlos Augusto and other figures in the Inter squad.

“We are dealing with professionals who are fond of these colours. They are all eager to continue their experience with us, and we see everything required to have a positive outcome in negotiations.”.

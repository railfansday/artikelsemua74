Despite Barnet pressure, particularly in the second half, the Reds were resolute in defence – and Crawley boss Kevin Blackwell was commendatory of all his players, particularly the back three.

The Reds built on an encouraging win over Fleetwood Town on Saturday, which highlighted signs of an improved defensive structure, with a clean sheet to show for their efforts.

When asked about the significance of defensive reinforcements Ben Heneghan and Josh Flint, Blackwell remarked, “[They have been] massive, massive. We've got somebody who can win a header, which has been thrown on the shoulders of Priestley [Farquharson], but Ben's there and Flinty's not bad in the air.

In a recent development, “They've been big signings for me, and you see why we tried to push them when I got here. It was clear to me what we needed and needed rapidly, and I can't praise both of those players.”.

Crawley re-signed Flint four weeks ago after a long time out injured. Heneghan, meanwhile, was released by Port Vale at the end of last season and had been without a club until he signed for the Red Devils just under three weeks ago.

Blackwell mentioned: “[Heneghan is] an ultimate professional. Stripped off he looks like a top-class swimmer, never mind footballer. He's looked after himself, and you can see the lads come in there.

“He's not played for six months but you can see how hard he's worked because he's come in and done really well.”.

According to recent reports, another player who had to adapt to fill a role for the team was Jonny Russell. With the Reds moving to a back five in the last two games, the Northern Irishman played as a wing back on Saturday and moved into the left side of the back against Barnet.

According to recent reports, the former assistant to Neil Warnock stated: “He's got a heart as massive as a bucket, Jonny. He'll go through a brick wall for you.

“He is absolutely proud to be a professional footballer, and you can see that in how he trains and how he plays.

“It was forced on us obviously to make that decision and pull [Charlie] Barker out and then take Flinty across, so we had to make a couple of changes because we'd lost Jude [Arthurs].

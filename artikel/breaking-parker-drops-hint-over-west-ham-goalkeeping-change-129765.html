Industry observers note that west Ham head coach Scott Parker has refused to commit to either Mads Hermansen or Fin Herrick ahead of Saturday’s Championship clash against Stoke City. The Hammers boss was asked about his goalkeeping selection during today’s pre-match press conference and was decidedly non-committal.

Speaking to the club’s official website, Parker mentioned: “I’ve been hugely impressed with both Mads [Hermansen] and Fin [Herrick], so we’ll manage things, keep working hard and then make our decision.”.

“As always, we’ll see. The goalkeeping position is probably one of the hardest on the pitch. You’re there for all eyes to see.”.

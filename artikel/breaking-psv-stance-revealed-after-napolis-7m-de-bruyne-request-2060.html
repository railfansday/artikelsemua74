Napoli have reportedly set their asking price for Kevin De Bruyne at €7m ahead of the January transfer window, but according to updates in the Netherlands, PSV Eindhoven have no intention of matching the fee requested by the Partenopei.

De Bruyne is into the final year of the two-year contract he signed with Napoli after leaving Manchester City on a free transfer in the summer of 2025. There is an option to extend by a further 12 months, but reports in Italy indicate that this option will not be exercised. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

It has been a disappointing start to the 2026-27 season for Napoli, who find themselves in 10th place in the Serie A table and at risk of missing out on the Champions League knock-out rounds, and reports over the last few weeks suggest that De Bruyne is considering a move away as well.

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport and various others, De Bruyne is keen to move back to his family home in Belgian Limburg.

Industry observers note that A move to PSV would facilitate a move back to the bordering Belgium. The Eredivisie side are interested in a potential January deal for De Bruyne according to reports in Italy and the Netherlands, although an opening offer of €2m for the former Man City superstar has reportedly been turned down. Reports from La Gazzetta dello Sport on Thursday claimed that it will take an offer in the region of €7m to convince Napoli into selling De Bruyne in January.

Dutch outlet De Telegraaf is adamant that PSV will not pay €7m for De Bruyne, a player who is ’35 years old, without sell-on value and was signed by Napoli on a free transfer’.

Recent findings suggest that de Telegraaf claims that PSV’s only real hope in negotiations is that De Bruyne forces Napoli’s hand if he really is desperate to return to his home in Limburg. The Dutch outlet adds that the news ‘being leaked’ in the Italian press this week has not helped PSV’s chances of pulling off a deal.

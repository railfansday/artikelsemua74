Sources indicate that from the sound of it, West Ham United are going all-in to refresh their options in front of goal in January. The club face a crisis with none of their trio of goal scorers looking fit for purpose: The best of the three, Joel Piroe, has amassed four goals this season whilst the other two have managed just one goal apiece.

Neither Pablo Felipe nor Taty Castellanos have fulfilled the expectations that were present when they were signed in the January window at the start of this year – Taty especially was expected to boss the Championship after the Hammers were relegated but at times he’s appeared no more than a scowling misfit, unable to influence games when he’s been called upon. ‘What a waste of money” as the song goes…. (Related: This fresh chip could slash data center energy waste...)

Footballinsider.com – who are usually on the ball with their reporting – have it apparently on good authority that West Ham are prepared to sanction his departure in January even though the club will have to absorb yet another huge financial hit:.

“Sources: West Ham to accept Taty Castellanos loss ahead of January”.

Runs their article, detailing West Ham’s willingness to sell the forward, despite being fully aware that they’ll not see anything like the £26 million they paid for the Argentine striker just twelve months ago:.

“The 28-year-old has scored just one goal from 15 games in the Championship this season, and is performing way below the level West Ham expected from him.

Sources say that the East London outfit are prepared to sell Castellanos at a loss this winter, knowing that it will be “very difficult” to recoup the £26million they paid for him.”.

Industry observers note that this is the key: Taty had previously been linked with loan moves back to Argentina which were never really feasible. The fact that West Ham are apparently putting it ‘out there’ that they’ll take the hit in order to move the striker on means that bids should be forthcoming in the winter market from a number of clubs; several who’ll no doubt chance their arm hoping to get him for a bit of a steal.

One goal in 15 games in the second division is nothing to write home about though, and so West Ham’s approach is borne largely out of facing the undeniable fact that Castellanos has been a massive disappointment, not to mention a colossal waste of money.

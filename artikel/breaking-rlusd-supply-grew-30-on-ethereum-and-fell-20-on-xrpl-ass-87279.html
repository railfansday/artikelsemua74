RLUSD is growing while USDT and USDC are losing supply.

At the macro level, the stablecoin sector is in a relatively weak phase. In Q4 alone, the market lost over $20 billion of value. As a result, the overall stablecoin market cap has fallen below $300 billion.

This means that all of the market’s growth since Q3 2025 (when it peaked at above $320 billion) has been erased. Notably, USDT and USDC account for more than half of the total liquidity outflows during this period.

Sources indicate that in contrast, RLUSD has increased by almost 20% during this period. This represents an increase of almost $500 million in total supply, pushing its total market cap to a record $2.9 billion.

In essence, RLUSD is attracting fresh liquidity even as the broader stablecoin market continues to contract.

This raises an notable question: Which L1 stands to benefit the most from this growth?

Fundamentally, Ethereum supports much larger amounts of RLUSD in comparison to XRPL, with 72%+ of RLUSD ($2.08 billion) on Ethereum. In addition, supply growth on Ethereum is relatively higher.

In the last 30 days, RLUSD supply has increased by 30% on Ethereum, whereas supply has fallen by 20% on XRPL.

So, with RLUSD liquidity increasingly concentrated on Ethereum even as the broader stablecoin market faces outflows, Ethereum seems to be the clear winner. However, supply is only one side of the story, and XRPL’s transaction volume shows why it should not be overlooked. (Related: Tech Report: Woman known as Firefighting hero arrested ...)

Growing stablecoin supply and actually using that supply are two different things.

Interestingly, this difference is becoming important in comparing Ethereum [ETH] and XRPL’s on-chain activity around RLUSD. On-chain data highlights that nearly $11.716 billion worth of RLUSD was transacted on the XRP Ledger and Ethereum in the week up to the 1st of November 2026. Recent findings suggest that the key takeaway? XRPL handled the larger share of RLUSD activity. As the chart below shows, $6.699 billion was transacted on the XRPL network, representing 57% of the total RLUSD volume. The Ethereum blockchain had total transactions of $5.017 billion (42.82%).

So, although there is more RLUSD on the Ethereum blockchain, XRPL is seeing higher transaction volume.

Notably, RLUSD’s DEX activity on XRPL is also picking up.

It has been revealed that the total DEX volume reached an all-time high of $596.2 million in the week ending August. While volume has dropped in recent weeks, it has been consistently above $70 million per week for the last 10 weeks.

It has been revealed that more recently, volume has picked up again, exceeding $200 million per week. This is significantly higher than previous averages of approximately $15 million.

So, while there is more RLUSD on Ethereum, XRPL is seeing higher transaction volume and growing DEX activity. This suggests that more RLUSD is being used on XRPL, making it a network to watch as the stablecoin continues to grow.

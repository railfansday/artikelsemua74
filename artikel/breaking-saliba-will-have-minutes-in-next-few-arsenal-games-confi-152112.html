The Gunners have not struggled much in the centre-back's absence, partly due to the arrival of Ezri Konsa, but they should still be boosted by his return.

Saliba won possession more often than any Arsenal defender in the 2025-26 season, in which the Gunners won their first Premier League title in 22 years and reached the Champions League final.

The Frenchman also won the second-most duels (198), won the third-most tackles (35) and recorded the third-most interceptions (29).

While Arteta did not clarify whether Saliba would feature this weekend against Manchester City, he has revealed the defender will be back in action soon.

"He hasn't played football for a long time, he can play a certain amount of minutes," remarked Arteta about Saliba.

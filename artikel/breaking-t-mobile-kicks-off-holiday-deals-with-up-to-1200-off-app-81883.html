The offers arrive just weeks after Apple introduced its latest iPhone lineup in September, with the iPhone Duo representing the tech giant’s most significant design departure in years. (Related: Tech Report: Woman known as Firefighting hero arrested ...)

Released Oct. 23, the iPhone Duo marks Apple’s long-awaited entry into the foldable smartphone market, joining competitors including Samsung and Google. The device features a 5.4-inch exterior screen that unfolds into a 7.6-inch display — the largest ever on an iPhone — designed for watching videos, gaming and multitasking across apps. The foldable design also makes it the thinnest iPhone Apple has released when opened. It’s currently available in Star White and Night Sky.

Meanwhile, the iPhone 18 Pro, which arrived Sept. 18, introduces Apple’s first variable-aperture camera, allowing people to adjust depth of field and lighting directly in the Camera app. The device also features a upcoming 48-megapixel Fusion Main camera, Apple’s A20 Pro chip, an upgraded cooling system and enhanced AI capabilities through iOS 27. Available in Black, Silver, Glacier and Burgundy, the Pro starts at $1,199.

Sources indicate that beyond Apple’s newest releases, T-Mobile’s early holiday promotions include $830 off the iPhone 17, offers on the iPhone 16 and 17e that can cover the full device cost, and discounts on Android devices including the Samsung Galaxy S26 series, Google Pixel 11 lineup and Motorola Razr.

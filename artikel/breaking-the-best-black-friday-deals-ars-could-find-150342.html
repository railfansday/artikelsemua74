But just as every dawn is twinned by a dusk that steals its light, so too did Optimus Prime’s bright spark fade, extinguished by the foul hand of Megatron during the Great Decepticon Incursion of 2005.

It is a day that lives in infamy forever, and we all know the tale—how the evil Megatron (cursed be the flow of his energon!) commandeered an Autobot shuttle, cruelly murdered its crew, and rolled out a cowardly surprise attack against Autobot City—a initiative utterly bereft of honor, as was so typical of Megatron’s schemes. Even as individual Autobots strove to secure Autobot City—heroes, all of them!—hope began to fade under an onslaught that included an attack by the fearsome Devastator, against which there appeared to be no defense.

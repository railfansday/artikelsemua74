November is drawing to a close, but you still have some time to put money to work in dividend stocks. Say, $5,000, which could give you a toehold in a reliable high-yield stock like Federal Realty (FRT +1.51%), a dividend growth stock like Visa (V +1.83%), or a turnaround story like Pfizer (PFE +0.00%). Here's a look at each one and why, depending on how you prefer to invest, it could be a good place for your cash before the month is over. Federal Realty is a bit unique in its strip-mall property niche, owning only around 100 assets. It is focused on being a sharpshooter, and its aim is very good. The proof of that is that it is the only Dividend King real estate investment trust (REIT). You don't string together 50+ annual dividend increases by accident; it requires a strong business model that is executed well in both good times and bad.

Sources indicate that federal Realty's approach is actually pretty simple. It buys properties surrounded by lots of wealthy customers. Then it redevelops the assets to ensure they are attractive to both consumers and tenants, including carefully curating the tenant list. When a property reaches full value, the REIT sells it and starts again with a new asset. That approach isn't particularly shocking, but it is hard to execute well. Federal Realty's dividend track record demonstrates it consistently executes well. (Related: This simple change stops robot swarms from getting stuc...)

According to recent reports, you can find higher-yielding REITs pretty easily, but there's only one REIT that can claim to be a Dividend King. For conservative dividend investors, Federal Realty's well-above-market 4.2% yield should help you sleep well at night, even when the market gets turbulent. A $5,000 investment will let you buy 45 shares.

Sources indicate that visa's tiny 0.75% yield probably won't interest you if you are looking for high-yield stocks. That stated, the dividend has grown by more than 370% over the past decade, which is a shockingly large number. If you don't need the income today and can set this stock to dividend reinvest, that kind of dividend growth could easily leave you with a robust income stream when you retire.

According to recent reports, visa is one of the world's largest payment processors. It has long benefited from the ongoing shift from paper currency to cards. It only collects a small percentage of each transaction it processes, but when a company handles 74.2 billion transactions in a single quarter (up 10% year over year), the numbers start to add up. And since the world is increasingly moving toward digital transactions, where paper money isn't an option, Visa is likely to have years of growth ahead. The stock isn't cheap, but given the growth opportunity here, it is probably still worth a look for long-term investors. A $5k investment will let you buy 12 shares.

NYSE · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/25/26.

According to recent reports, pfizer, an iconic drug maker, has a huge 6.2% yield. The average yield on pharma stocks is just 1.3%. The core problem Pfizer is facing is major patent expirations in the next few years, with Eliquis losing protection in 2028 and Vyndamax in 2031. It is a very real problem, but Pfizer is actively working on its pipeline to offset the revenue hit. Only, drug development doesn't work on a schedule, even though patent expirations do.

NYSE · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/25/26.

Sources indicate that investors clearly fear the worst, which could include a dividend cut. However, Pfizer's cash flow still covers its dividend, and it has over $14 billion of cash on its balance sheet. Moreover, the company continues to stand behind not only supporting the dividend at its current level, but growing it over time. Given the company's long history of success in the pharma sector, turnaround investors should give it a look. And even if there were a 50% dividend cut, which is probably a realistic worst-case scenario, the yield would still be over 3%. Five thousand dollars will buy you 178 shares.

Sources indicate that no two investors are alike, which is why Wall Street is such an exciting place. When it comes to dividends, you can find boring and reliable dividend stocks like Federal Realty. You can find dividend growth stocks, such as Visa. And you can find turnaround stocks like Pfizer that come with ultra-high yields. Each one is worth a deep dive today.

During Art Week NYC, a citywide festival organized by ARTnews, Artforum, and Art in America, I had the great pleasure of doing a fireside chat with Newly introduced York magazine senior art critic Jerry Saltz.

Industry observers note that like many of us, Jerry has been skeptical of AI, so I started the conversation by reading him a description of his approach to criticism produced by an AI that was based on a talk he gave a couple years ago. After chewing on that for a few minutes, we went on to speak about how he became a critic, how he became famous on social media, what he thinks about art being produced today, his strong feelings about auctions (he despises them), and what advice he would give creative people.

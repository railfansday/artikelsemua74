📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The H3 Podcast has come to an end.

On Monday, Hila Klein announced on the show's Instagram account that she had "made the difficult decision to stop working on the new show." She had been developing a crew-led version of the podcast since her estranged husband and co-host, Ethan Klein, was pushed out of it last month after she revealed his affair.

Industry observers note that "I've come to realize that I can't continue down this path while also giving myself the space I need to heal and protect my mental health," she wrote. "Right now, I need to focus on myself, my healing, and what's best for me and my kids.". (Related: Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 vs Watch 9: When is it rig...)

Recent findings suggest that the announcement closes out a month-long fallout that began on Oct. 22. That day, Ethan said on Instagram Stories that the podcast was going on "indefinite hiatus." Hours later, Hila posted that he had been having an affair for more than six months. As Mashable previously reported, the crew said in a statement that they would not keep working with Ethan "involved or profiting in any way." Ethan said he was working to give up his financial stakes in the podcast and in the couple's businesses, including the clothing brand Teddy Fresh. (Related: F1 star Max Verstappen played 40 hours of GTA 6 before ...)

Industry observers note that complex reported that Hila filed for divorce on Nov. 2, listing their separation date as Oct. 13. The couple married in 2012 and share three children.

Recent findings suggest that since the news broke, multiple crew members have spoken out. On the show's Discord, longtime producer GirlWithAMicrophone (GWAM) said she would not work on a project with the rest of the crew. She thanked Hila for giving them "a shot during all this turmoil.".

Recent findings suggest that producer AB Ayad suggested on X that the obstacles were bigger than any one person. "There are certain people involved that were never gonna allow us to continue doing the show together," he wrote, adding, "I give Hila a lot of credit that she was able to do what she even did.".

Recent findings suggest that beyond the H3 Podcast, Ethan co-hosted Frenemies with Trisha Paytas, which became one of YouTube's most talked-about indicates before it ended acrimoniously in 2021. He later started the political show Leftovers with Twitch streamer Hasan Piker, which ended in 2023 over disagreements about Israel and the war in Gaza.

Since the announcement of the show ending, Ethan has broken his weeks-long silence. On Tuesday, in a series of Instagram Stories, he called the affair "the ultimate betrayal" to his wife and to the show's audience. He also remarked that "the show ending is entirely my fault.".

However, he didn't rule out a comeback. "I will probably be back, but when and how, I can't tell you," he wrote, hinting at a solo show or a fresh project.

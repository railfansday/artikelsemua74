Szymon Marciniak made a huge error in stopping Man United from scoring against Sporting CP due to an apparent foul by Youri Tielemans.

That is according to former PGMOL chief and FIFA referee, Keith Hackett, who has exclusively told Football Insider that Tielemans clearly did not foul Goncalo Inacio before Amad Diallo netted.

United came away from Sporting with a 2-2 draw after an action-packed clash full of flashpoints and mad moments.

One of those came in the 40th minute, as Amad thought he had given Mauricio Pochettino's side their equaliser when he headed home a rebounded effort.

Referee Marciniak had, however, blown his whistle just moments before the ball crossed the line for a foul by Tielemans on Inacio.

Replays showed that the United summer signing barely even touched his opponent, yet VAR could not get involved as the ball was dead before it hit the net. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Tielemans was booked for his protests following the decision, and later sent off in added time at the end of the game.

United picked up their first point under Pochettino thanks to Bruno Fernandes' brace, but felt like it could have been more due to Marciniak's first error and a late own goal that was ruled out for a foul on the Sporting goalkeeper.

Former PGMOL boss Hackett could not believe that a top-level referee like the Pole had made such a bad mistake.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, he said: "Given the status in world football of this referee, you would not expect him to make this catastrophic error that ruled out the Manchester United goal.

Industry observers note that "Football is a contact sport and there is no foul here by the Manchester United player. Incorrect decision by the referee.". (Related: Industry News: My Journey to a serverless transformers ...)

Despite his earlier error, Hackett felt that the referee had no choice but to rule United's late potential winner out after Sporting keeper Kaique Pereira scored an own goal but was adjudged to have been fouled.

Sources indicate that he noted: "Once he has been asked to go to the monitor to review this incident, he is going to rule the goal out. The Manchester United player has been judged to have impeded the goalkeeper.

"It may seem harsh, but the fact is that European referees take a much stronger approach to fouls inside the penalty area.".

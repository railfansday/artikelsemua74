West Brom will hope to be active in the January transfer window as they look to beef up James Morrison’s squad.

Albion have enjoyed a very strong season so far, and they will feel that promotion is a genuine possibility, something which few thought possible before a ball was kicked back in August.

However, the Baggies merit their current position, and they have shown that they are capable of mixing it with the best in the Championship. (Related: Using & Mixing Hugging Face Models with Gradio 2.0...)

Yet, there will be concerns about their depth to last the distance, and fans will hope that owner Shilen Patel will provide the funds to bring in a few quality additions in the New Year.

Therefore, recent suggestions that Albion should try and bring back Torbjørn Heggem were encouraging, as the Norway international is an excellent defender at this level, which is why Bologna paid West Brom £10m for the player back in 2025.

Given the finances involved in any deal, a return to The Hawthorns always seemed a long shot, and BBC reporter Steve Hermon has confirmed that Heggem won’t be joining Albion in January.

But, here we look at THREE alternatives Albion should be considering ahead of the winter window….

If Albion do want to bring a former player back to the club, then Cedric Kipre should be under consideration.

The powerful left-sided centre-back was very good during his time at the club, and he would bolster the backline, giving Morrison another strong, reliable figure in defence.

Kipre is currently at Ipswich, but after helping them to promotion last season, he is now way down the pecking order, to the extent that he often fails to make the matchday squad.

Therefore, he should be available in the January window, and he is the calibre of player that Albion should be looking to bring in, whether it’s on loan or permanently.

Another option for the Baggies is influential Stockport County defender Ethan Pye, who is an assured left-sided centre-back, who could also fill in at left-back if needed.

It wouldn’t be a straightforward deal to do, as the League One side are pushing for promotion, but the chance to join an Albion side that could be in the Premier League next season is going to appeal. (Related: Tech Report: The Reformer - Pushing the limits of langu...)

At 24, Pye is approaching his peak years, and he is capable of making the step up to the Championship, as he has all the attributes that you would want from a centre-back.

Plus, given his career so far, Pye would fit in with the rest of the Albion squad, who are a determined, hungry group, with most of them desperate to show they can play at a higher level.

Alternatively, Albion could try and repeat the Heggem trick, and one left-sided centre-back that could make the jump from a Scandinavian league to the Championship is Rosenborg’s Tomas Nemcik.

The 25-year-old has emerged as a key figure for the Norwegian side this season, and he has played an crucial role in helping them to third in the table.

At 6ft3in, he has the physical profile to cope with the demands of the Championship, and he is another player at a good age in terms of being able to make an instant impact, whilst also retaining value moving forward.

He won’t be a name that Albion fans recognise, but it’s hard to imagine many had heard of Heggem before he joined - and that worked out very well for the club!

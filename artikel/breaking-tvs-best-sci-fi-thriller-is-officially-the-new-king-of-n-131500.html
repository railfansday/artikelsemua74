📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

From's success is rare among TV indicates. The MGM+ horror-thriller began in relative anonymity but has become one of the most-watched shows after four years. When it premiered in 2022 on Epix — just before the network rebranded to MGM+ — the platform was a niche, relatively unknown streamer that many viewers only encountered as a Prime Video add-on. Yet, fueled by relentless word-of-mouth and glowing endorsements from the likes of Stephen King, From steadily built a fiercely loyal cult following. Over four seasons, that organic audience growth transformed the series into the most-watched original show in MGM+ history.

Created by John Griffin, the series captures a specific vibe of mystery that is hard to replicate without collapsing on itself. While many mystery highlights lose steam by piling on empty twists, From anchors its unknowns in a deepening lore that feels earned. Each season peels back another layer of the nightmarish town's curse, rewarding viewers without sacrificing its dread. Crucially, the mythology never overshadows the human element, thanks to excellent ensemble performances. Harold Perrineau delivers a career-best turn as Sheriff Boyd Stevens, alongside standout work from Catalina Sandino Moreno, Eion Bailey, David Alpay, and others. The sleeper hit's trajectory then exploded into the mainstream when its first three seasons arrived on Netflix in October 2026. This exposed the show to an even bigger audience, and within weeks, the results started showing. From topped Nielsen's streaming charts with over five billion minutes viewed, proving the show's widespread appeal. As America celebrates Thanksgiving, From has received a massive boost on streaming and has surged to become the seventh most-watched show on the streamer in the US. Globally, it is the third most-watched show, behind the new horror hit The Body and the true-crime documentary The Trials of Karen Read. In April 2026, MGM+ confirmed that the hit horror-thriller had been renewed, but would end with Season 5. Principal photography kicked off in Halifax, Nova Scotia, in July 2026 and recently wrapped in late November, officially moving the final chapter into post-production. While MGM+ has yet to lock in an exact premiere date, the fifth season is slated to arrive in 2027. With the show's recent explosive success on Netflix, the final season is poised to be its biggest yet.

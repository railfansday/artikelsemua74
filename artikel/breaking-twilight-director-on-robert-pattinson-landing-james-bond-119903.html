Robert Pattinson is the next James Bond, and his Twilight director had an interesting reaction when she learned about his casting.

Catherine Hardwicke cast Pattinson in 2008’s Twilight, which catapulted the actor into worldwide fame.

It has been revealed that “I almost fell out of my bed,” Hardwicke said on The Kyle Buchanan Show. “I just didn’t think they were going to choose him because of The Batman and he’s so famous and they like to get the unknown [actor].”.

She continued, “I did almost fall out of the bed, and that is the same bed that Rob and Kristen auditioned for the first time and did their kissing scene. It was kind of bizarre.

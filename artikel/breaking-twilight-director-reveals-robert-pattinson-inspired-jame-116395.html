Catherine Hardwicke, who helmed 2008’s Twilight, might as well have had a crystal ball on the set of the vampire romance film.

After it was revealed earlier this month that Robert Pattinson — who earned his breakout role as Edward Cullen in the blockbuster franchise — had been tapped as the newly introduced James Bond, fans were quick to point out that Twilight predicted his 007 casting nearly two decades ago. It has been revealed that at one point in the movie, Eric’s (played by Justin Chon) discusses the prom theme and indicates, “Gambling, tuxedos, and Bond, James Bond,” right before the camera cuts to a smirking Edward, looking suave in a pair of sunglasses. In a recent interview on The Kyle Buchanan Show, Hardwicke recalled how that line and scene came about. “You guys have probably seen online where we have that [Twilight] scene where he [Eric] says, ‘Bond, James Bond,’ and then we cut to Rob,” she stated. “That’s the one scene where I was like, ‘Let’s put the sunglasses on that day,’ and he got out of the car and he looked so suave that he inspired the line, ‘Bond, James Bond.’.

It has been revealed that the director added that Chon “kind of ad-libbed it, and it was kind of cool because we did think he looked like James Bond. So I guess we were seeing the future.”.

In a recent development, fast forward to 2026 and Denis Villeneuve, who’s helming the upcoming 007 movie, and producers Amy Pascal and David Heyman, obviously thought Pattinson looked like Bond too. The Primetime actor is taking over the iconic role from Daniel Craig, who concluded his run with 2021’s No Time to Die.

Hardwicke also shared her reaction to the news that Pattinson would be going from having “the skin of a killer” to getting his license to kill.

“I almost fell out of my bed,” she quipped about the moment she learned about the casting. “I just didn’t think they were going to choose him because of The Batman and he’s so famous and they like to get the unknown [actor]. So I did almost fall out of the bed, and that is the same bed that Rob and Kristen [Stewart, who played Bella Swan in Twilight] auditioned for the first time and did their kissing scene. So it was kind of bizarre. And he [Pattinson] fell out of the bed when he did the audition and landed on the floor, so it was kind of cool.”.

According to recent reports, hardwicke continued to praise The Batman actor’s talents, adding, “He’s intense and interesting and has got a lot of stuff going on in there. He’s weird and he’s original. He does not fit the mold. He’s not afraid to say crazy shit, that’s what I love about it.”.

It has been revealed that when The Hollywood Reporter caught up with Hardwicke in 2023, ahead of the 15th anniversary of the first Twilight film, she opened up about the challenges of finding the right actor for the role of Edward. She said the actors who were initially auditioning “didn’t look like they’d been alive for almost 100 years and all this deep, soulful angst, existential crisis. They did not feel that kind of soulful.”.

However, once Stewart and Pattinson did their chemistry read, the director knew he was the one because she felt “that electricity” between them.

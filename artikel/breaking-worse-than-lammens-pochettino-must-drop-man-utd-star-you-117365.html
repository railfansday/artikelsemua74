Manchester United missed the chance to end their six-month wait for an away win as the Reds were held to a 2-2 draw against Sporting CP in the Champions League.

In a recent development, mauricio Pochettino looked to be on course for a first win as head coach after Bruno Fernandes scored twice to cancel out Luis Suarez's first-half opener. But Senne Lammens gifted Sporting a late equaliser as he failed to deal with a high ball inside the box and dropped it, allowing a thankful Suarez to tap home from close-range. (Related: Latest Update: Hugging Face Reads, Feb. 2021 - Long-ran...)

It was the second of two huge mistakes Lammens made against Sporting. Just minutes after saving a penalty from Suarez, the United goalkeeper came rushing out of his goal and clattered into Maximiliano Araujo, causing referee Szymon Marciniak to point to the spot once again. On this occasion, Suarez made no mistake by burying the ball into the bottom corner.

Although Lammens endured a horror show against Sporting, he wasn't the worst United player on the pitch.

Another 4/10 star let Pochettino down. The man in question is Youri Tielemans, who has struggled since moving to the Reds for £35million from Aston Villa in the summer and had another evening to forget, both in and out of possession at the Jose Avalade Stadium. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Lining up in a two-man defensive midfield alongside Kobbie Mainoo once again, Tielemans was unable to influence the game as much as he would have wanted. Indeed, the 29-year-old completed just 27 successful passes with an 82% passing accuracy.

Just one of his four passes into the final third were accurate, while he made just three progressive passes during his time on the pitch. While Tielemans did make one key pass, United fans are expecting much more from the midfielder, given what he did at Villa and Leicester.

Tielemans stats vs Sporting Lisbon.

According to recent reports, from a defensive point-of-view, Tielemans wasn't much better and won just 50% (6/12) of his duels. He was dribbled past once and committed a team-high four fouls. The Belgian did, however, win four tackles, made one interception, one clearance and two recoveries.

Sources indicate that tielemans' dour night ended in the 93rd minute when he was sent off for two bookable offences. He picked up his first yellow card in the first half before seeing red for a foul on Sergi Altimira.

Due to this, he will now serve a one-match ban for United's next Champions League game, which is a clash at home to Bundesliga side RB Leipzig in December.

Summing it up, Tielemans is nowhere near to the required level needed at United right now and should be dropped ahead of Sunday's huge Premier League game against Brentford.

Mainoo was much more involved against Sporting and should keep his spot in the midfield two alongside £50m summer signing Andrey Santos, who deserves a chance from the off.

Santos is much stronger off the ball than Tielemans and Mainoo, and can help bring a more defensive and solid base alongside a creative midfielder.

On this occasion, Tielemans should drop out of the side due to his form and performance against Sporting, with Mainoo keeping his place and Santos coming in.

Fernandes, who was the hero for United in Lisbon after scoring twice, would therefore retain his place in the attacking midfield role, just in front of Mainoo and Santos.

Tielemans does still have a future at United, but for the time being, Pochettino should take him out of the firing line and give him a rest, allowing Santos a chance to show what he can do.

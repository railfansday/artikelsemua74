Recent findings suggest that hurzeler added: “Of course, every injury hurts us because we feel for the player. We build relationships with the players, and every player wants to be on the pitch and express themselves on the pitch. If they can't do that, you feel for them.

“I think that's the worst thing that can happen to a player: being injured, being out and seeing their teammates performing on the pitch from the sidelines.

Sources indicate that “I just spoke to him and he feels quite confident. He has a lot of trust in his body. For tomorrow it's too early, but hopefully he'll be available for Sunday.”.

On the plus side, summer signing Femi Azeez is fit and could make his debut on Thursday night.

With stocks mounting a comeback Wednesday, options trading across a few key volatility products suggests big swings in stocks may dampen in the near term.

More than 80,000 options traded on the Barclays S&P 500 VIX Short Term Futures ETN (VXX), almost three times the 30-day average, with more puts trading than calls, according to LiveVol data. The fund aims to track the moves in Cboe's VIX Index, which surged to 35 early Wednesday but slipped to below 28 as the S&P 500 rallied to within 25 basis points of unchanged. Recent findings suggest that at the same time, options volume on VIX itself was 50% above average, with an even more distinctive bias towards puts, suggesting traders see stocks firming, or at least, the size of the market's moves shrinking. Of the 850,000 options traded on VIX, more than 515,000 were puts, with twice as many puts likely initiated by buyers than calls, Cboe data show.

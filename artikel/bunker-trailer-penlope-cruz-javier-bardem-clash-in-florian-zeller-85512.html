The dramatic thriller examines the emotional and moral challenges faced by a couple as the anxieties of an increasingly uncertain world begin to reshape their marriage, exploring the fears, doubts and ethical dilemmas that define our time. During the Bunker panel at Deadline’s recent Contenders Film Los Angeles event, Cruz noted she was concerned that the fragile relationship between the husband-and-wife leads might bleed over into her rock-solid union with real-life husband Bardem. ““We’ve worked together every five or six years – we don’t strategy it like that, but I think in a way we both want to protect and we feel like if we would be working together too often, it would be tricky and risky,” she said “But when Florian came to us, even before we read the script, we looked at each other and we knew none of us would want to say no … because we were huge fans and now even more after working with him.”.

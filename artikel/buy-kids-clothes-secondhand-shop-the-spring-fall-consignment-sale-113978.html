Buy Kids’ Clothes Secondhand – Shop the Spring & Fall Consignment Sales First

🌐 Source: Moneycrashers • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (421 words, 📖 Standard)

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Buy most of your kids’ clothes secondhand, since children outgrow clothes long before wearing them out. Start with a kids’ consignment sale, a pop-up event usually held each spring and fall where local families sell what their children outgrew at a fraction of the new price. Then fill gaps at resale shops and by swapping with other families.

Say your 6-year-old needs play clothes for spring and summer that would cost about $200 new. At a consignment sale, a similar used set might cost about $60.

Industry observers note that two chains run many of these sales. Just Between Friends advertises 50% to 90% off retail, and Rhea Lana’s advertises 60% to 90%. Families who sell their kids’ clothes there get to shop a presale before the public, and some sales offer early-shopping tickets too. Near the end, both hold a half-price sale, when every item whose tag isn’t marked full price drops to 50% off. (Related: Industry News: Texas’s outgoing GOP attorney general dr...)

Between sales, shop a kids’ resale chain such as Once Upon A Child, which advertises up to 70% off regular retail, or a thrift store. For a piece the local racks don’t have, try ThredUp, which advertises up to 90% off estimated retail. Neighbors in a Buy Nothing group give away what they no longer need, so ask your group for outgrown clothes before you buy.

Industry observers note that before you pay, check each piece for stains, test every zipper, and stretch the waistband to make sure the elastic springs back. Buy shoes latest, though. The American Podiatric Medical Association advises never handing down footwear, because the same size can fit two children differently, and shared shoes can spread athlete’s foot. Swapping is free too. Trade outgrown clothes with friends and relatives whose kids are a size ahead of or behind yours, and pass along what nobody in the swap can use through your Buy Nothing group.

At your next consignment sale, buy what your kids need at the presale, then come back during the half-price sale for extras.

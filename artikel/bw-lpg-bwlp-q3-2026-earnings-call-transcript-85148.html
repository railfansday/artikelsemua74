Recent findings suggest that BW LPG (BWLP +1.26%) reported its third quarter results, which management described as one of the strongest periods in organization history due to high shipping rates and substantial trading gains. Management stated that geopolitical factors and Panama Canal congestion continue to absorb fleet capacity by forcing longer trade routes around the Cape of Good Hope, which has pushed freight rates to record levels.

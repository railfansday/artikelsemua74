Recent findings suggest that LEHI, Utah, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- PayKings, a high-risk merchant services provider, offers a free Statement Analyzer that shows merchants the actual rate they pay to accept cards. Merchants upload a statement from any U.S. processor and receive their effective rate, total fees, and a line-by-line breakdown in about a minute.

Recent findings suggest that most merchants know the rate printed on their contract, but the effective rate tells a different story. Calculated by dividing total processing fees by total card volume, it includes charges that contract rates may leave out, such as per-transaction, monthly, and PCI fees. A merchant quoted 2.6% can end up paying 3.5%. The analyzer works with any U.S. merchant statement, including those from CBD, firearms, nutraceutical, and telemedicine businesses PayKings specializes in.

"Most merchants we talk to have never seen their real processing rate," said Kyle Hall, CEO of PayKings. "They see one number on a contract and assume that's what they're paying, but the reality is often much more complicated. We believe every business owner deserves complete transparency into their processing fees so they can understand exactly where their money is going and make informed decisions about their business.".

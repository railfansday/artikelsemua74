Candace Cameron Bure appeared on the “McBride Rewind” podcast on Tuesday and discussed the “substantial” residuals she still gets from “Full House,” as well as why she wasn’t surprised that Mary-Kate and Ashley Olsen didn’t return for the hit sitcom’s spin-off, “Fuller House.”.

Industry observers note that “They’re substantial… I think it’s like quarterly,” Bure told host Josh McBride of the consistency of her “Full House” residuals, adding that every check is different, but “still substantial.” “I’m always happy when I open that check and deposit it,” she added. McBride then brought up Bure’s “Full House” co-star Jodie Sweetin, who made headlines back in April when the actress revealed on the podcast she’d received a “one-cent check” for the sitcom. “She stated she doesn’t get much now,” Bure said. “And I was like, ‘Jodie, you gotta check with your agent or you gotta call your bank.’ ’Cause I still get residuals.”. Speaking of the Olsen twins, Bure mentioned that their decision not to appear on “Fuller House” was “a bummer” because “it would have been fun to have everybody back.” “But at the same time, it wasn’t surprising just because none of us really have stayed close with the girls,” Bure noted.

Industry observers note that she continued: “Not because anything went wrong or there was any bad blood. I just think they were kids. They were [filming from] 9 months old until they were 8 years old. They have very different memories than we do. Even me being 10 years older than them, I have significant, great, wonderful memories.”.

In a recent development, “They were babies. They probably don’t even remember half the stuff [we do],” she added. “They didn’t have the attachment to the crew and the people like we did being older. And they had such a successful fashion business. They haven’t been actors for a long time. They just haven’t been in that part of entertainment.”.

Sweetin played Stephanie Tanner and Bure played D.J. Tanner on “Full House,” which ran for eight seasons from 1987 to 1995. “Fuller House” ran for five seasons on Netflix, totaling nearly 80 episodes. Original cast members, including Bure, Sweetin, Andrea Barber, Scott Weinger, Bob Saget, Lori Loughlin, Dave Coulier and John Stamos, all returned for the series. Juan Pablo Di Pace, Adam Hagenbuch, John Brotherton, Michael Campion, Elias Harger and Soni Bringas also starred in the reboot.

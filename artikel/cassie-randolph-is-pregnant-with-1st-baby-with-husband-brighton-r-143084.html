The Bachelor alum, 31, and her husband, Brighton Reinhardt, shared their happy news via an Instagram Reel posted on Thursday, November 26.

“It is Thanksgiving 2026 and we have had the hardest time keeping this secret,” Randolph said as she indicated off her baby bump.

As music played, Reinhardt, 29, stated, “Growing very fast every week, every day.”.

Sources indicate that the couple could barely contain their happiness about starting a family. Randolph added, “We’re so excited!”.

The Reel also contained a montage of video clips of the pregnancy journey, including the pair’s excitement over a pregnancy test returning a positive result as well as footage of Randolph getting pregnancy scans and Reinhardt being a supportive partner as he gears up to be a dad. In the post’s caption, the couple indicated their baby was due in May 2027.

Earlier this year, Randolph hinted that she was ready to begin to start trying for a family in the near future.

“Deciding whether to sip my way through the holidays or get pregnant,” she wrote in a September 29 TikTok video, which she captioned, “Decisions decisions.”.

In a recent development, in the clip, Randolph took a sip out of a glass as she filmed herself and lifted her hands to create an invisible baby bump. After pretending to cradle a baby, Randolph’s eyes got wide as she leaned into the camera and took another sip.

In the comments section, Reinhardt quipped, “Both options scare me.”.

Randolph and Reinhardt tied the knot in Costa Rica in June 2025, less than a year after getting engaged in November 2024 at Crystal Cove Cottages in Newport Beach, California.

Randolph told People at the time of their engagement that she had no hesitations when it came to accepting. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

“When Brighton dropped to a knee and asked ‘Will you marry me?,’ as he opened the box, my reaction was immediately, ‘Oh, my gosh, yes!’” the reality star told the outlet.

One year after tying the knot, Randolph took to social media to look back at their special relationship step.

In a recent development, “6.3.2025 🤍,” Randolph wrote via Instagram in June. “One year ago today, we had a sweet, intimate ceremony in Laguna Beach with our grandparents who weren’t able to make it to our Costa Rica wedding (and parents too!). It was such a special day that we’ll cherish forever.”.

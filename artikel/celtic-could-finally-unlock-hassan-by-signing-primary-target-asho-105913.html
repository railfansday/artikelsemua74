Celtic are now being linked with a move for a newly introduced midfielder as they look to plug a key gap and wake summer signing Haissem Hassan out of his slump. A number of midfielders have been linked with a move to Parkhead ahead of the winter transfer window, and the newest name on the list is Al Ahly star Emam Ashour.

Sources indicate that the Bhoys made some big mistakes in the summer transfer window, and chief among them was their failure to replace Arne Engels, who left on a £22million deal to join West Ham. Engels was one of the most pivotal cogs in the Celtic team last season, and after missing out on Champions League qualification, the Bhoys board cut back on spending and decided to go with what they had, with the exception of some cheap late signings that included youngster Joel van der Berg and loan signing Oliver Sorensen, who has struggled so far.]. But while Mika Baur has done a good job filling in playing in a deeper role, Celtic know they need more there, and they were given a harsh reminder when Baur was ruled out for 10 weeks with an ankle concern recently.

It has been revealed that that has exposed a substantial gap, and Celtic know they need to recruit at the position this winter. Torino's Gvidas Gineitis has been linked after he was targeted in the summer, but now Al Ahly star Ashour is being linked. (Related: A classic brain test exposed AI's biggest weakness...)

Mark Hendry has reported that Ashour is Celtic's leading midfield target ahead of the winter, as someone who can play both ways in the midfield.

"Celtic interest remains in Emam Ashour, as I first reported back in July. They’ve always seen him as a No.8 who can operate in defensive role. Gvidas Gineitis also option but club considering next steps, including more discussions with Al-Ahly over Ashour." - Mark Hendry on X.

Ashour impressed for Egypt at the World Cup during the summer, and he was once described as a 'significant quality player' by Tottenham star Omar Marmoush.

But what Ashour does in the midfield could be secondary to the impact he has with struggling winger Hassan. Hassan arrived at Parkhead on a £6million deal that could rise to £9million in the summer, although some of those add-ons were voided by the Hoops failing to qualify for the Champions League.

The 24-year-old dazzled for Egypt in the World Cup but with just two assists and no goals, he has flattered to deceive at Celtic. Hassan has plenty of quality, and it's clear when has the ball at his feet, but he is not doing enough, and Martin O;Neill knows it. Hassan should be beating Premiership defenders on repeat, but he is not reliably doing so, and it comes at the cost of being rather lazy defensively. O'Neill will likely put up with the lack of defensive effort if Hassan is running riot up top, but that simply isn't the case, and it has led Hassan to be omitted from the last two matchday squads. Although O'Neill mentioned the last game was due to the winger having a longer international window and coming back later than most. Hassan will be involved on Thursday night against Torreense in the Europa League, but it's clear he has questions to answer, and while unverified, there have been murmurings of the winger not being particularly popular in the dressing room. O'Neill denied that. He said:.

Industry observers note that "Apparently something came out that he was the most unpopular player in the dressing room – which is not true. I didn’t say this.".

What better way to spark Hassan's Celtic career to life than signing a midfielder he plays with and has performed well with on the international stage? And it would be a smart move to get both of them feeling comfortable in their surroundings, given they could lean on one another while they try to get going at Parkhead.

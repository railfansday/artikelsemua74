Certain fungi turn specialized plant cells into water-carrying cells using one protein

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (74 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Can a pathogen that causes damage to a plant also make that plant more resilient to drought? An international research team led by the University of Göttingen has investigated this apparent paradox. The researchers discovered how certain fungi promote the formation of new water-conducting cells in plants. At the same time, infected plants highlighted increased tolerance to drought stress. The results were published in the journal Cell Host & Microbe.

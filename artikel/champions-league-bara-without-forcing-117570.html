FC Barcelona’s third defeat this season, in all competitions, will not have happened this Wednesday. And it’s no surprise since the Catalans were away to the very modest Sabah FC. Against the Azerbaijani club, Barça logically won their match on the 5th day of the Champions League league phase (1-4).

It has been revealed that with Lamine Yamal diminished and absent from the match sheet, Hansi Flick lined up a somewhat reshuffled eleven. Gabriel Jesus, for example, started up front, while Jules Koundé started in the right lane. The locals courageously tried their luck at the start of the match and even found the crossbar on a strike from Ivan Lepinjica (27th). But this episode past, it was the Catalans who gradually got their foot on the ball. Anthony Gordon ended up opening the scoring after half an hour of play (0-1, 31st). Two minutes later, the Englishman thought he had his double, but the VAR signaled an offside (33rd).

Regardless, the former Newcastle player was back at the start of the second half and made the break for Barça (0-2, 51st). Too complicated for Sabah FC who failed a third time with a goal from Karim Adeyemi (0-3, 55th). And a fourth when Gabriel Jesus found the fault in his turn (0-4, 60th).

From then on, the visitors took their foot off the gas. A boon for the locals who reduced the gap and saved the honor by Joy Mickels (1-4, 63rd). The end of the meeting will be soporific. Note, however, a newly introduced injury on the Barça side with the premature exit of Alejandro Balde (64th).

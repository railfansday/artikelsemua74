Join us for all the build-up and action as it happens from the Champions League, as Roma visit Luis Enrique’s Paris Saint-Germain, and Inter host VfB Stuttgart.

The Giallorossi have had strong performances, but lack of experience indicated when throwing away leads with Real Madrid and Manchester United.

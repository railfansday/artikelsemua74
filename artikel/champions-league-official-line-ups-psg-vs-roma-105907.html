Roma aim for the stars with Santiago Castro, Matias Soulé and Manu Koné against giants Paris Saint-Germain, as Paulo Dybala, Bryan Cristante and top scorer Donyell Malen are benched.

It kicks off at the Parc des Princes at 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET).

It has been a tricky campaign for the Giallorossi so far, as they are pushing for the top of the Serie A table and still unbeaten there, but have highlighted a distinct lack of experience in Europe.

This saw them twice throw away two-goal leads to Real Madrid and Manchester United, leaving them on four points in as many rounds.

The injury crisis is finally calming down, with Mile Svilar and Paulo Dybala making their comebacks at the weekend, where Malen scored four goals in a 5-1 drubbing of Parma. (Related: “Giant superatoms” could finally solve quantum computin...)

However, Malen and Dybala do not start in Paris, as Niccolò Pisilli is drafted in to bolster the midfield with Soulé and Koné.

Santiago Castro therefore leads the attack instead, perhaps hoping to tire PSG out before unleashing their biggest talents, seeing as Roma have shown a tendency to fall away.

Marten de Roon is also preferred to Bryan Cristante in the midfield.

Devyne Rensch and Lorenzo Pellegrini are the only remaining absentees.

Paris Saint-Germain won the last two editions of the Champions League, but their chances of a hat-trick have been badly damaged by losing two stars for the rest of the season.

Just weeks after former Napoli hero Khvicha Kvaratskhelia tore his anterior cruciate ligament, Désiré Doué suffered the same fate.

This is a special fixture for coach Luis Enrique, who started his senior management career at Roma in 2011/12.

There are other familiar faces, as Marquinhos was at Roma in 2012/13, while Fabian Ruiz made his name at Napoli and Achraf Hakimi first shone for Inter.

PSG need to reshuffle with Ousmane Dembélé and Marquinhos dropped, so Maghnes Alkiouche, Ferran Torres and Senny Mayulu all start together.

PSG: Safonov; Hakimi, Zabarnyi, Willian Pacho, Nuno Mendes; Joao Neves, Vitinha, Fabian Ruiz; Akliouche, Ferran Torres, Mayulu.

PSG bench: Chevalier, Bar, Lucas Beraldo, Marquinhos, Dembele, Digne, Lucas Hernandez, Godts, Dro Fernandez, Zaire-Emery, Boly, Ndjantou.

Roma: Svilar; Mancini, N’Dicka, Hermoso; Wesley, De Roon, Koné, Molina; Soulé, Pisilli; Castro.

Roma bench: De Marzi, Gollini, Koulierakis, Cristante, Malen, Dybala, Balerdi, Lulli, Rodrigo Mora, Ghilardi.

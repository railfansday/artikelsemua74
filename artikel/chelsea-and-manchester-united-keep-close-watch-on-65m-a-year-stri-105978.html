Marcus Thuram could be heading for an exit from Inter Milan, with Chelsea and Manchester United reportedly monitoring his contract situation amid growing uncertainty over his future at the Italian club.

The 29-year-old right now earns around €6.5 million annually, including bonuses, but Inter are looking to restructure their wage commitments in line with their ownership’s financial strategy.

The Nerazzurri are reportedly proposing a lower guaranteed salary supplemented by performance-related incentives, a structure that has not gone down well with the French international.

Chelsea and Manchester United are understood to be keeping a close eye on the situation, with both Premier League sides potentially looking to strengthen their attacking options.

Thuram has been an influential performer for Inter since arriving from Borussia Monchengladbach in 2023, contributing significantly to their success in domestic competitions.

His importance has increased further in recent weeks, particularly following Lautaro Martinez’s injury, with the Frenchman delivering impressive performances in the Argentine’s absence.

Thuram has registered four goals and two assists in six league matches during that period, highlighting his importance to Cristian Chivu’s attacking plans.

Although Inter remain keen to secure a long-term commitment from the striker, the ongoing financial disagreements could complicate negotiations.

With his current contract running until 2028, Inter are not under immediate pressure to sell. However, if discussions fail to progress, a summer departure could become a realistic possibility, potentially giving Chelsea and Manchester United an opportunity to pursue the experienced forward.

EXCLUSIVE: Vertigo Live Productions, the British manufacturer behind music specials like CBS‘s Christina Aguilera: Christmas in Paris, is facing the threat of liquidation over an unpaid tax bill.

HM Revenue & Customs is pursuing a “winding-up petition” against Vertigo Live Productions Limited, which in the UK is often the last resort taken by a creditor when attempting to recover debt. (Related: Latest Update: At least seven progressives voted agains...)

HMRC is essentially seeking a court order to put Vertigo Live into liquidation. The petition alone could trigger the freezing of Vertigo’s bank accounts. The precise nature of the dispute and the amount allegedly owed are unclear. Vertigo Live has not responded to requests for comments. The firm’s website was returning an error message on Friday. HMRC does not comment on individual cases. Watch on Deadline.

In a recent development, HMRC’s winding-up petition is the third such petition Vertigo Live has faced this year. Cafe Croissant, a French firm that provided production services on CBS special Christina Aguilera: Christmas in Paris, issued proceedings in June.

In January, Luna Remote Systems, a remote camera firm, also sought a winding-up petition against Vertigo, which has been involved in other specials including Duran Duran: A Hollywood High and Billy Idol: State Line – Live at Hoover Dam.

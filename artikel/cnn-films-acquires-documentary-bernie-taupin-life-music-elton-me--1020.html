CNN Films has acquired “Bernie Taupin: Life, Music, Elton & Me,” a documentary about the lyricist behind such durable pop hits as “Your Song,” “Tiny Dancer” and Rocket Man,” and a longtime collaborator with Elton John. The film will debut on CNN Friday, January 1 at 8 p.m., continuing a tradition at the Skydance network of kicking off a newly introduced year with an intimate portrait of an intriguing figure.

In a recent development, “I’ve spent much of my life telling stories, so it’s a curious experience to find yourself the subject of one,” states Taupin, in a statement. “There’s quite a distance between the life I imagined as a kid in Lincolnshire and the one I’ve been fortunate enough to live.” Related Stories 'Entertainment Tonight' Hosts Kevin Frazier and Nischelle Turner to Serve as Hollywood Christmas Parade Grand Marshals (EXCLUSIVE) Jennifer Lopez on the ‘JLo Show’ Concert Film, Her ‘Happy Era’ and Moving on From Divorce: 'What I Thought Love Was Really Isn’t Love’.

Industry observers note that taupin has largely remained out of the spotlight, while John sang his words. The upcoming film features intimate conversations with Taupin, including a rare sit-down with John, who reminisces about their shared history. “I wanted to explore the imagination behind those words and the singular mind that has helped shape the soundtrack of our lives,” Miele indicates in a statement. “Bernie afforded me rare access to his creative world, revealing an unconventional approach to songwriting in which he paints abstract portraits with words, conjuring images, characters, and emotions that often defy literal interpretation.”CNN Films has previously featured films that explored the lives of Linda Ronstadt and Glen Campbell, among others. “Bernie Taupin: Life, Music, Elton & Me” is directed and executive produced by Matthew Miele. Producers are Derik Murray for Network Entertainment and Stephen McCormack for Reflektor Media. The film is executive produced by Gary Oldman, Charlie Hendon, Greg Zeschuk, Tim Gamble, Brian Gersh, Kent Wingerak, Greg Zeschuk, and Jody Gerson and David Blackman for Polygram Entertainment.

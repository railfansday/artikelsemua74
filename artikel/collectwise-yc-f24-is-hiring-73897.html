You’ll work at the intersection of customer success, account management, and operations, managing customer communication, resolving technical problems, optimizing AI agents, monitoring performance, and growing revenue from existing customers.

Industry observers note that this is a highly hands-on role. You should be comfortable managing multiple customer relationships, proactively identifying opportunities to improve customer outcomes, making adjustments to AI agents and workflows, collaborating with engineering to resolve more complex challenges, and growing revenue across your accounts by increasing platform usage and expanding customers into additional products and use cases.

Your success will be measured by customer satisfaction, retention, revenue expansion, product adoption, and customer performance.

Own day-to-day customer relationships after customers have gone live, serving as their primary point of contact.

Troubleshoot and resolve customer problems by adjusting configurations, coordinating solutions, and escalating technical issues to engineering when necessary.

Drive revenue growth across existing accounts by executing upsell and cross-sell opportunities, increasing platform usage, and expanding customers into additional products and use cases.

Monitor customer usage, performance, and trends to identify opportunities to improve results, increase adoption, and reduce churn.

Continuously optimize customer AI agents, prompts, workflows, and outreach strategies to improve performance.

Communicate technical issues clearly to customers and internal engineering teams, coordinating resolutions and keeping customers informed throughout the process.

Build strong customer relationships through regular check-ins, performance reviews, and proactive communication, including preparing reports and presentations to demonstrate results and recommend improvements.

Support new implementations, additional use cases, and product rollouts for existing customers.

Assist with customer onboarding and operational requirements, including completing vendor diligence questionnaires related to security, compliance, and tech giant operations.

CollectWise is revolutionizing debt recovery with autonomous AI agents and an integrated legal network. We boost recovery rates, reduce costs, and maintain a positive brand image through respectful, data-driven interactions.

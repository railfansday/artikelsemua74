Industry observers note that sometimes celebrities create trends, and sometimes celebrities wear trends. But then there are times when you really can’t tell which happened first. It’s a chicken-or-the-egg situation. That’s what we have on our hands with the leopard-print coat trend.

Here’s the fact: Inde Navarrette, Elizabeth Debicki, and Michelle Randolph all wore leopard coats last week. Three of anything make a trend, especially A-list spottings. Also true: Leopard coats never really go out of style, so I’m not sure how much credit we can actually give the stars. But you can get Navarrette’s exact Rixo coat and Randolph’s from Aflalo.

It has been revealed that in case you feel compelled to make the trend your own, let me break it down a little further. Your leopard-print coat can—but doesn’t have to be—faux fur. It could also be more of a trench coat-like material or even jacquard. Many of the styles we’re seeing this season are collarless, but that’s simply a matter of preference.

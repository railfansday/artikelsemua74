As Netflix‘s The Altruists recounts the downfall of FTX, one of the company’s executives had some thoughts about her portrayal and the show’s overall accuracy.

Constance Wang, the former FTX COO who is portrayed by Madison Hu, called the depiction “very stereotypical” and “racist” as she unpacked some of the apparent inaccuracies of the limited series from creator Graham Moore.

Recent findings suggest that “People have asked whether Netflix consulted me. They didn’t,” Wang wrote on X. “One conversation would at least have saved Madison Hu, who plays me, the trouble of putting on a Chinese accent I don’t have. It feels very stereotypical, then again, it’s Netflix. What did we expect?”. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Wang added that the “biggest stretch” of the companies’ origin story was turning it “into a story about Sam and Caroline’s relationship,” noting some discrepancies with the timeline.

The financier, who has since co-founded AI-crypto trading platform Pacifica, also found the depiction of Changpeng “CZ” Zhao, former CEO of Binance who played a role in FTX’s 2022 collapse, “particularly hard to watch.”.

In a recent development, “FTX started in Hong Kong, and a lot of the people who built it were Asian,” she explained. “I found the stereotypes about Asians and Chinese culture racist.”?

According to recent reports, “Even Sam and Caroline felt unfamiliar. Sam was one of the least emotionally expressive people I’ve ever met. His character seems drawn from Michael Lewis’s book and the way he came across in public after the bankruptcy. Caroline reads like someone tried to piece her together from her private blog. She wasn’t nearly that reckless, and she was never the one pushing to go all in. The idea that she was the mastermind behind everything made us laugh,” added Wang.

Industry observers note that wang noted, “Maybe you’ll enjoy The Altruists as a romantic comedy. For those of us who watched it together, it felt like someone else’s story with our names attached.”.

The Altruists recounts Sam Bankman-Fried’s (Anthony Boyle) formation of FTX and Alameda Research, as well as his and co-CEO girlfriend Caroline Ellison’s (Julia Garner) role in FTX’s 2022 collapse, when it was found that Alameda used FTX company funds against the latter’s terms of service.

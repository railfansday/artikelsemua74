📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Contour Airlines is expanding its presence in the Southeast.

Starting March 1, 2027, the independent regional carrier will connect Macon, Georgia, and Nashville, Tennessee. Contour serves Macon via Middle Georgia Regional Airport.

Flights will operate three times weekly.

Contour officials mentioned there is strong demand for Nashville service from Macon.

According to recent reports, “Listening to the communities we serve is an important part of what we do, and this newly introduced route is another way we can respond to what local travelers want and need,” Contour President Ben Munson noted in a news release. “We look forward to providing more convenient travel options and connecting Macon to even more destinations.”.

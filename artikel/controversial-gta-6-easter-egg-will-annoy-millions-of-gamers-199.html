Controversial GTA 6 Easter Egg Will Annoy Millions of Gamers

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Grand Theft Auto 6 is now out everywhere, and players are discovering just how much this game is filled to the brim with an overwhelming amount of things to do. In the game’s opening hours, you’ll land a place on the water, steal a car from a police impound, get into a car chase, and more. There are a million little things to do, like playing video games inside GTA 6 itself.

In a recent development, early in the game, Cal, a friend of co-protagonist Jason, spends an afternoon gaming with his buddy. Ironically, he does something that IRL players can’t do with GTA 6 itself: bring a physical game disc with him. (Related: Open up peer review to early-career researchers...)

As GTA 6’s nearly 20 million players (at the time of this writing) know, GTA 6 is digital-only. “Physical” copies simply contain a digital download code in a box. As such, you can’t bring your copy of GTA 6 to a friend’s house to show them the game like you could with previous GTA games, or like how Cal introduces Jason to Tenebrae Lux, a very artsy game that spoofs Jonathan Blow’s The Witness. (Jason’s not impressed; he’d rather go kill zombies.). (Related: AI wearables could exploit people’s most vulnerable mom...)

The two then have a party-filled night together that ends with them drunkenly fighting over who gets to sleep in the bed, which then cuts to the both of them sharing the bed in their undies. It’s a surprisingly wholesome sequence that includes Jason opening up about the strains in his relationship with Lucia on the eve of her getting released from prison. The moments of genuine friendship between Cal and Jason contrast well to the rest of GTA 6, which is all about, ya know, stealing drugs from rednecks, evading death-by-gator, running away from cops, and all other kinds of wild shenanigans.

Trading games, bringing a game disc to a hang sesh with the boys, introducing a upcoming game to a friend by popping your copy into their console — for years, sharing games was easy because they were all on discs or other physical formats. When I reviewed the Gears of War remaster last year (with a digital copy), it reminded me how I first played the original game by trading my copy of Assassin’s Creed with a friend for his copy of Gears. Fast-forward to this year, and I’m playing Pokémon Pokopia by borrowing my partner’s copy. (OK, maybe I stole it from her Switch 2 case, who can say.).

PlayStation once famously demonstrated how to share used games in a 2013 video intended as a dig toward the always-online Xbox One. That joke has aged like milk in the wake of Sony’s announcement that it will cease manufacturing physical discs for PlayStation games in January 2028. Sony’s news hit just a short time after Rockstar Games announced its own digital-only aims for GTA 6 back in June.

It has been revealed that sony’s move prompted a wave of angry commenters spamming every PlayStation promotional video and blog post with disdain ever since. Some PlayStation owners tried to wrangle together a “no disc, no buy” blackout campaign where they wouldn’t use PSN, make purchases at the PlayStation Store, or basically engage with their PlayStation console in any way. That blackout had no effect on Sony. In fact, Sony’s digital game sales have continued to grow since the announcement.

The outrage over the decline of physical games clearly hasn’t had an impact on Rockstar’s GTA 6 either. It broke preorder records, despite there being no real need to preorder a copy — they’re all digital, so Sony and Microsoft won’t be running out. This isn’t 2009, when you had to preorder a physical copy of Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2 to make sure you could pwn some newbz day one. (And snag a sleek poster in the process.).

Recent findings suggest that though we can’t say with certainty yet, it seems likely GTA 6 will end up as 2026’s top-selling game, in spite of the uproar around its lack of a disc version. Yet none of the eleventy billion people who buy it will be able to share it with their buds in the way that Cal shared a disc version of Tenebrae Lux with Jason.

Copper aluminum oxide could make light-controlling components for smart glasses 120 times thinner

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (72 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Could augmented reality (AR) glasses become as thin and lightweight as everyday eyeglasses? Researchers at the Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST) and their collaborators have identified a material that could reduce the thickness of polarization-control components to approximately 1/120 that of conventional components while maintaining high light transmission. The discovery could pave the way for smaller, lighter AR devices and more compact optical communication equipment.

It has been revealed that cyberleek threatened to release a build of GTA 6 before back in October. The coin needed to hit $30 million, and the account released a series of escalating leaks, including nude footage of Lucia, one of the two playable characters. But the coin ended up tanking in value instead, and whoever was behind the Cyberleek account disappeared again. It’s unknown if this is the same person who was releasing footage throughout the summer or if multiple people are involved.

Cyberleek already appeared to cash out on the meme coin once before, and it doesn’t seem like Rockstar Games or parent organization Take-Two have been able to locate the individuals behind the stolen assets.

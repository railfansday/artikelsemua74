SPOILER ALERT: This article contains major spoilers for the Nov. 24 finale of “Dancing With the Stars.”.

Season 35 of “Dancing With the Stars” is officially coming to an end. On Tuesday, Nov. 24, the five remaining celebrities — Tatyana Ali, Ezra Frech, Amber Glenn, Maura Higgins and Connor Wood — compete one last time for the mirrorball trophy. Each finalist will perform three routines: the instant dance, a freestyle, and judges’ choice.

Sources indicate that tatyana Ali and Jan Ravnik (82/90)For judges’ choice, the duo kicked off the night with a sexy salsa to “Jamaican (Bam Bam),” directed by Carrie Ann Inaba, scoring a 27/30. When it came time for the instant dance, the duo drew the jive card, even though she preferred the foxtrot. Carrie Ann called the impressive routine “beyond insane,” and they earned a 27/30. They ended the night with a powerful freestyle to a mashup of Cab Calloway’s “Minnie the Moocher” and Judy Garland’s “Over the Rainbow.” Jan also may have forgotten he was on live TV, blurting out, “She fucking killed it” after the routine. They scored a 28, bringing their total to 82.

Sources indicate that amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov (90/90)The pair’s judges’ choice dance, with direction from Derek Hough, was a quickstep to “Sing, Sing, Sing,” and they scored the first perfect score of 30 of the night. For the instant dance, they were given a cha-cha, dancing to Sabrina Carpenter’s “Tears.” They scored another perfect 30 after leaving Derek speechless. Their Madonna-inspired freestyle to “Like a Prayer” and “Music” was super emotional, with Amber crying midway through. She was also joined by Team USA’s Madison Chock and Evan Bates on the dance floor. Afterward, Derek said, “The queen of the ice is now queen of the ballroom.” The pair earned a perfect 30.

Connor Wood and Rylee Arnold (82/60)Carrie Ann suggested the Viennese waltz for the judges’ choice dance, and they danced to Noah Kahan’s “Willing and Able.” The emotional number earned the pair a 28/30. For the instant dance, they were given a cha-cha to The Pussycat Dolls’ “Buttons” — their third cha-cha of the season. Carrie Ann called it their best cha-cha so far, despite a small mistake. They scored a 25/30. Taking a page out of Tom Hollland’s lip-sync battle, the pair danced their freestyle to Gene Kelly’s “Singin’ in the Rain” and Rihanna’s “Umbrella.” The routine featured Connor ripping off his outfit midway through to reveal a corset, short shorts and tights, as they then danced in the water. They earned a score of 29/30, bringing their total to 82.

In a recent development, maura Higgins and Mark Ballas (90/90)Assigned a tango by Bruno Tonioli, Team Tink danced to “Gallo Ciego” by Orquesta Color Tango, scoring the second perfect score of 30. For the instant dance, the pair were scared after choosing a quickstep and being assigned Little Richard’s “Tutti Frutti” — a song Maura had never heard of. Bruno complimented her “gorgeously spot on” footwork and Julianne Hough joked Mark had never looked happier than he did after the routine, which earned them a perfect 30. They then performed a powerful freestyle to Marilyn Monroe’s “Diamonds Are a Girl’s Best Friend” and traditional Irish folk songs “The Parting Glass” and “Flowers of the Red Hill,” honoring her roots. The pair “exceeded all expectations,” Bruno remarked, and earned a third perfect score.

Sources indicate that ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach (90/90)Going last in the judges’ choice dances, Bruno assigned Ezra and Daniella a steamy salsa to “Danza Kuduro” by Bruno; their routine earned a perfect score. For the instant dance, they were assigned the tango to Avicii’s “Levels.” Derek thanked Ezra for educating the audience to show what’s possible and complimented his “top-notch” artistry. The pair earned another perfect score. Ezra and Daniella performed the last freestyle of the night, dancing the most emotional routine of the night to Linkin Park’s “Numb,” along with other professional dancers in wheelchairs. He began the routine without his prosthetic leg; while he put it back on, it fell off. However, he recovered flawlessly, delivering one of the most incredible routines in the show’s history. It wasn’t surprising that the pair earned rave reviews and a perfect score.

The results revealed that Maura came in fifth, Tatyana came in fourth and Connor came in third. The newly introduced mirrorball champions are Ezra and Daniella, with Amber and Pasha coming in second.

In a recent development, SPOILER ALERT! This post contains details from the Season 35 finale of ABC‘s Dancing with the Stars.

It’s that time of year again: Another Dancing with the Stars couple is set to take home the mirrorball come end of show on Tuesday.

It has been revealed that that’s right, it’s the Season 35 finale. The five remaining couples will be performing not one, not two, but three routines for their final night in the ballroom.

First up, the couples are tasked with a fresh take on a dance style they performed earlier this season. Sources indicate that actress Tatyana Ali with Jan Ravnik. Tatyana made history as the person with the lowest score ever who made it in the finale, but hey! She made it! Carrie Ann stopped by to ask Tatyana and Jan to redo their salsa. She’s pretty happy with the decision, and the challenge with this second go round is to own the execution. Carrie Ann wants to see fire! “Hello, finale! Tatyana is here! You brought the fire,” Carrie Ann gushed. SCORE: 27 out 30.

It has been revealed that olympian Amber Glenn with Pasha Pashkov. After being on top of the leaderboard last week, Derek is challenging Amber to take another spin at the quickstep. She indicates she had a blast last time, but the judges think her tempo was a little off, so she’s got to lock in this time. The music is faster, so she’ll have to really maintain her frame. “Amber, you are a perfect combination of pure class and a little bit of fire in there,” Derek affirms. SCORE: 30 out of 30. Comedian and podcaster Connor Wood with Rylee Arnold. Connor’s journey has been quite a rollercoaster, and they’re ready to take on whatever comes for them in the finale. Carrie Ann lets them know they’ll be doing another viennese waltz, meaning that his main focus during this routine is his frame. It’s been a struggle for him all season, and he has one more shot to get it together. “Connor, bravo! You took every note! Look at that frame!” SCORE: 28 out of 30. Reality star Maura Higgins with Mark Ballas. These two have had a mixed bag of reactions from the judges, and Maura is quite exhausted, but she’ll have to muster all her energy for this final push. Bruno tasks her and Mark with redoing the tango, which Maura is happy about. She’s confident that she can show some improvement. “That is how you do a proper ballroom tango,” Bruno compliments. SCORE: 30 out of 30.

Paralympian Ezra Frech with Daniella Karagach. Ezra is closing out the first round of the competition by taking on another salsa. Bruno tells he and Daniella to work on getting fuller engagement in the hips. They got 9s the last time they performed a salsa, so they’re going to need to hone in on the details if they want 10s. “You did what I asked you. You went supernova with those hips tonight!” Bruno tells him. SCORE: 30 out of 30.

Next up is the instant dance round, where each couple will only have five minutes to prepare their routine.

Industry observers note that actress Tatyana Ali with Jan Ravnik. These two will take on a jive to “Land of 1000 Dances” by Wilson Pickett, better get to work! Tatyana notes in rehearsals she’d really prefer to not have a jive, so the universe is definitely throwing a challenge at her with this. “That was so amazing, that was so entertaining! Guys, good job!” Derek says. SCORE: 27 out of 30.

In a recent development, olympian Amber Glenn with Pasha Pashkov. Amber’s instant dance is taking her back to Week 1 as she’s dancing a cha cha to “Tears” by Sabrina Carpenter. Just like Tatyana, she’s inadvertently picked the dance she admits puts her at a disadvantage, but she’s never been afraid of a challenge, and she won’t start now. “Sometimes when you’re under pressure, it brings out the best in you,” Bruno tells her. SCORE: 30 out of 30.

It has been revealed that comedian and podcaster Connor Wood with Rylee Arnold. Connor gave himself a cha cha for the instant dance round, and they’ll be dancing to “Buttons” by the Pussycat Dolls. Last time they performed a cha cha, the judges loved their connection but did not love Connor’s technique. He’ll have to really pull through to impress the judges this go round. “Connor…that was your best cha cha,” Carrie Ann says excitedly. SCORE: 25 out of 30.

It has been revealed that reality star Maura Higgins with Mark Ballas. She’s dancing a quickstep to “Tutti Frutti” by Little Richard! Wow, what a task they have on their hands. The last time they took on the quickstep was Week 3, and she was missing some of the finesse, so she’s got a lot to remember to nail the details this time around. “Fantastic quickstep!” Derek chimes. SCORE: 30 out of 30.

Paralympian Ezra Frech with Daniella Karagach. Closing out this round, Ezra is facing a tango to “Levels” by Avicii. They seem pretty happy about that, so they might be the only ones in this round to not have been handed their least favorite dance style. “What you do, sometimes I cannot even explain it…” Bruno muses. SCORE: 30 out of 30.

Sources indicate that the moment everyone has been waiting for has arrived. It’s time for the freestyles.

Actress Tatyana Ali with Jan Ravnik. The underdog has arrived at her final dance of the competition. She’s extremely emotional talking to Jan in rehearsal about the journey she’s gone through over the last 11 weeks. She claims she’s using her final two minutes on this stage to dream her wildest dreams. “When I watch you, I feel something,” Derek says. SCORE: 28 out of 30.

Sources indicate that olympian Amber Glenn with Pasha Pashkov. Amber isn’t holding anything back in her finale freestyle. The mirrorball is in sight, if only she can make it through this last performance. “No question, you are a great dancer,” Bruno confirms. SCORE: 30 out of 30.

It has been revealed that comedian and podcaster Connor Wood with Rylee Arnold. Quite possibly the most anticipated dance of the season, Connor is paying homage to Tom Holland’s iconic lip sync battle dance with a freestyle to “Singin’ In The Rain/Umbrella” for his final time stepping on the ballroom floor. “That’s what you call ‘Give the people what they want’ freestyle,” Derek compliments. SCORE: 29 out of 30.

Recent findings suggest that reality star Maura Higgins with Mark Ballas. She’s come so far from her humble beginnings on Love Island, and though she still feels like that girl from Ireland, she’s standing on a national stage again to rep her country. “Exceeding all expectations!” Bruno exclaims. SCORE: 30 out of 30.

It has been revealed that paralympian Ezra Frech with Daniella Karagach. Closing out the entire competition, Ezra is using it as one last opportunity to illustrate the odds he’s defied by making it to the finale. But, no sob story here! It’s all about bringing power and energy to this last dance. “Ezra, every time you dance, you come out here and you’ve had purpose…you have been the most amazing ambassador for people with disabilities,” Carrie Ann tells him. SCORE: 30 out of 30.

According to recent reports, after a grueling 11 weeks, Ezra and Daniella take home the Len Goodman Mirrorball Trophy. That’s a wrap on Dancing with the Stars Season 35!

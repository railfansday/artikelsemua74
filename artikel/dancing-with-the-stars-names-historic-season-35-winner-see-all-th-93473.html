Season 35 of Dancing With the Stars is officially over, and the show has crowned a upcoming winner.

The finale episode of DWTS saw couples compete in three rounds of competition as Tatyana Ali, Ezra Frech, Amber Glenn, Maura Higgins and Connor Wood battled it out for a chance to win the season 35 Len Goodman mirrorball trophy.

Industry observers note that ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach were ultimately named the winner’s of DWTS season 35; his win makes him the first amputee winner of the series. Recent findings suggest that the Paralympian previously told The Hollywood Reporter it would be “very, very special” to become the first amputee winner of DWTS. “Of course, getting the opportunity to make the finale, to win the show, to get to represent the disabled community in that way and become the first amputee to win Dancing With the Stars would be very, very special, and would mean a lot to myself and hopefully to all people with disabilities and the community as a whole,” he remarked. Amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov placed second. Maura and Mark Ballas came in fifth, with Tatyana and Jan Ravnik in fourth, and Connor and Rylee Arnold in third.

First, contestants competed in the judges’ choice round, as each judge selected a dance style for one of the contestants to perform. Second was the instant dance challenge, which tasked them to perform a number without knowing the dance style or the song until only minutes before performing it live.

The final performance was the beloved freestyle round, where all rules are out the window, leaving contestants with one final dance to wow the judges and fans.

According to recent reports, during the judges’ choice, three couples tied for the top of the leaderboard, earning perfect scores. Those duos were Amber and Pasha; Maura and Mark; and Ezra and Daniella. Connor and Tatyana trailed closely behind, respectively scoring a 28/30 and 27/30 each.

It has been revealed that amber, Maura and Ezra all earned perfect scores during the instant dance round, keeping the competition neck-and-neck. Tatyana matched her first-round score with a 27/30 for her instant jive; Connor received a 25/30 for his instant cha cha.

It has been revealed that after Tatyana and Jan performed their freestyle, a medley of “Minnie the Moocher” by Cab Calloway and “Over the Rainbow” by Judy Garland, the duo was both tearful. A piece of the professional dancer’s comments after the dance were cut out due to him saying an explicit (“She fucking killed it!” Jan reportedly remarked). They scored a 28/30 for their last dance of DWTS season 35.

It has been revealed that amber and Pasha’s freestyle was set to Madonna’s “Like a Prayer” and “Music,” a tribute to her figure skating routine performed at the 2026 Olympics. She earned a perfect score, while also dancing alongside fellow Team USA members Madison Chock and Evan Bates.

Sources indicate that connor and Rylee referenced Tom Holland’s iconic performance on Lip Sync Battle, set to “Singin’ in the Rain” by Gene Kelly and “Umbrella” by Rihanna. Connor even wore a recreation of Spider-Man actor’s outfit in the number equipped with fake rain on the ballroom floor, earning them a 29/30.

Maura and Mark’s final number was another high spot, earning them a 30/30 for their glamorous performance to Marilyn Monroe’s “Diamonds Are a Girl’s Best Friend,” and “The Parting Glass” and “Flowers of the Red Hill.”.

Industry observers note that ezra and Daniella performed a touching freestyle set to “Numb” by Linkin Park. Ezra began the number without his prosthetic on, and after putting it back on, it suddenly slipped off him mid-dance. The duo recovered swiftly, though, and were joined by wheelchair dancers, and finished the dance with another prosthetic in hand, paying tribute to the amputee’s wider journey. They scored a 30/30.

Alfonso Ribeiro and Julianne Hough serve as co-hosts while Carrie Ann Inaba, Derek Hough and Bruno Tonioli judge.

Industry observers note that also during the finale, season 34 champion Robert Irwin was on hand to announce the renewal of Dancing With the Stars: The Next Pro, which will feature a cast of prospective male pro dancers who will compete for a chance to join the season 36 cast. Robert also revealed that the men will dance with fans throughout the competition, teaching them how to dance.

Recent findings suggest that alfonso and Julianne further revealed that Jackson Olson, Taylor Hanson, Tyler Cameron and all five finalists and their pros would be joining the DWTS tour in select cities. In more news, the co-hosts also announced 86 million votes had been cast in the finale alone.

For the first time, the DWTS finale aired live coast-to-coast, allowing fans in any time zone in the U.S., its territories and Canada to vote for the season 35 winner. (The show typically only airs live for the East coast, with voting taking place only during the East Coast airing of each show, leaving West Coast and Mountain time viewers unable to vote live.).

The couple with the lowest combined total of judges’ scores and viewer votes is typically eliminated at the end of each DWTS episode; the winner is determined by combining the judges’ scores and viewer votes in the finale episode.

Recent findings suggest that each viewer is allowed up to 10 votes per couple, per voting method (via ABC’s website and by text). Live voting takes place during the live airing of each show and ends shortly after the final performance.

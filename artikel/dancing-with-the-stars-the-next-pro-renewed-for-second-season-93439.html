Dancing With the Stars is going to find a new male professional dancer to add to its cast on the second season of The Next Pro.

The spinoff series has been renewed for a second season, which will find a latest male pro dancer to be added to the cast. It was unveiled during the season 35 finale of DWTS on Tuesday night.

According to recent reports, robert Irwin was present at the finale to announce the news, also revealing that fans will be paired with the eight prospective male pro dancers throughout The Next Pro. Adele Zaikman was named the winner of the first season of DWTS: The Next Pro, which originally shot in Australia. It was unclear if the show would be renewed, as the pro cast of the series does not always consistently change each season, and dancers typically remain on the show for multiple seasons.

The renewal was confirmed during the season 35 finale of DWTS on Tuesday night, which saw Tatyana Ali, Ezra Frech, Amber Glenn, Maura Higgins and Connor Wood compete for a chance to win the season 35 Len Goodman mirrorball trophy.

It has been revealed that zaikman reflected on winning The Next Pro in an interview with The Hollywood Reporter, saying taking on the show had been “a dream come true.” She added, “I’ve been a competitive dancer for all my life, and we have certain rules that we have to follow. The Next Pro allowed me to figure out who I am on a deeper level as a dancer. What can I bring to the dance floor? Maybe something fresh that people haven’t seen before. [On DWTS,] you can break the rules. You can be more creative.”. (Related: Breaking: Simple considerations for simple people build...)

Recent findings suggest that THR also recently asked Next Pro judge and season 35 pro dancer Mark Ballas if he’d be open to returning to the judges panel for a potential second season. “I don’t know if they’re doing a second season yet. I’ve had no confirmation of that. But yeah, again, if the timing feels right and everything’s in line with my family, and it makes sense, then sure. It was a fun experience,” he said.

Dancing With the Stars: The Next Pro is produced by BBC Studios. Conrad Green serves as showrunner and executive producer. Deena Katz, Ryan O’Dowd, Krystal Whitney and Alex Cross are executive producers. Quinn Lipton is co-executive producer.

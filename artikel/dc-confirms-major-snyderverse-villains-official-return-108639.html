It has been revealed that DC has confirmed the return of a major Snyderverse Justice League villain, nine years after their last appearance. The DCEU, or “the Snyderverse” as some called it, was DC’s first serious attempt at a cohesive cinematic universe, similar to the MCU. Snyderverse DC films were released consistently for nearly a decade, before the powers that be placed James Gunn in charge. (Related: Hila Klein announces end of H3 Podcast as Ethan Klein w...)

The Snyderverse debuted in 2013 with the arrival of The Man of Steel in theaters. Starring Henry Cavill as Superman, Amy Adams as Lois Lane and Jesse Eisenberg as Lex Luthor, the film was a hit with audiences, but not critics or devoted fans. A follow-up, Batman vs Superman: Dawn of Justice, hit the screens in 2016. Starring Ben Affleck as The Dark Knight, the film seeded the Justice League, introducing Wonder Woman, the Flash, Aquaman and Cyborg to the mix. Like its predecessor, Dawn of Justice received mixed reviews.

Recent findings suggest that during the filming of Justice League, Snyder stepped down from directorial duties due to a family tragedy. Snyder’s involvement with the DCEU faded, leading to films that, while good and entertaining, failed to connect with audiences in the same fashion that Marvel’s movies did. The Snyderverse ended with The Flash, which reset the timeline. Vocal segments of the fandom have demanded Snyder’s vision be reinstated, but the possibility of that happening fades over time. Now, those yearning for a return to the Snyderverse are in luck, as one of its biggest villains returns. (Related: “Giant superatoms” could finally solve quantum computin...)

Steppenwolf was created by Jack Kirby, and first appeared in 1972's Latest Gods #7.

In Justice League Unlimited #25, written by Mark Waid and drawn by Dan Mora, Plastic Man and Detective Chimp are horrified to discover that Steppenwolf, Darkseid’s uncle and a major threat in his own right, has set up a weapons forge underneath the Galaxy Communications Building in Metropolis. They look on as swarms of Parademons put together fantastic weapons for Steppenwolf to use in the service of Darkseid.

According to recent reports, the gravity of the League’s situation begins to set in, and Plastic Man starts to panic. The two heroes then learn that Maxwell Lord, wearing the sign of Darkseid on his forehead, is offering support to Steppenwolf and his hordes. Before the two can escape to warn the Justice League, they are taken out by Kanto the Assassin.

Darkseid has many zealots and henchmen waiting to do his bidding, such as Desaad, Mantis and Granny Goodness. Nearly all of Darkseid’s family is at his beck and call too, such as his children Kalibak and Grail, as well as Steppenwolf. One of the most feared warriors in the galaxy, Steppenwolf is powerful enough to take Doomsday down to the wire. On top of being a formidable combatant, Steppenwolfe also has his legions of Parademons, whose only desire is to obey and kill for Darkseid.

Movie goers will remember Steppenwolf from 2017’s Justice League movie, where he served as one of the film’s antagonists. Played by Ciaran Hinds, Steppenwolfe attacked Atlantis and Themyscira and took their Mother Boxes. Steppenwolfe planned to enslave Earth’s population, but the combined efforts of the Justice League were able to stop him. Steppenwolf is one of DC’s most underrated villains, but Justice League failed him. When Snyder finally released his cut in 2021, Steppenwolf’s role was larger, allowing audiences to get to know him better, and fear him appropriately.

In a recent development, and now, nine years after Justice League came and went into theaters, Steppenwolf is getting a shot at redemption. DC is building towards a major, Darkseid-themed event. Steppenwolf’s presence in Justice League Unlimited #25 seems to indicate he will be a part of the crossover as well. While DC’s heroes should fear Darkseid more than any other, Steppenwolfe is not to be underestimated either.

Justice League Unlimited #25 is on sale now from DC Comics!

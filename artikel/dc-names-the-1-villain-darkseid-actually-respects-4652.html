More often than not, supervillains don’t play well with others, and Darkseid is no exception. As the Lord of Apokolips and the most malevolent of all the Fresh Gods, the entity formerly known as Uxas doesn’t consider anyone to be his equals. Those in his circle are merely a means to an end to achieving his goals, with his minions including Steppenwolf, Granny Goodness, DeSaad, and the mindless Parademons. The few times that Darkseid has joined forces with another powerful antagonist, they’ve been begrudging alliances.

It has been revealed that when all is said and done, Darkseid only cares about himself and will sacrifice anyone he deems necessary to further his plans and accumulate more power. Yet, it turns out that there is a popular DC supervillain he actually respects. This is evidenced by Darkseid showing up to literally pay his respects to this newly-deceased troublemaker.

According to recent reports, though the Czarnian bounty hunter Lobo has famously been unkillable, that changed at the end of Lobo #8, when the character’s healing factor was overwhelmed by Francis Damex, the first and last Mauve Lantern. Both Lobo and Francis died during their final battle, and Lobo #9 begins with the Main Man’s funeral underway. Many of DC’s biggest heroes and villains are in attendance for this solemn affair, including Darkseid.

Recent findings suggest that apokolips’ iron-fisted tyrant is seen standing next to characters like The Joker, Flash, and Sinestro while Maynard, one of the members of Lobo’s unorthodox villain group, gives a funeral sermon for his fallen friend. Amusingly, rather than shedding tears for the late bounty hunter like almost everyone else, Darkseid shoots drooping Omega Beams out of his eyes to show sadness. Maynard also gets bonus points for wearing a hat that indicates, “Newly introduced Gods, Who Dis?”, though it’s hard to say if Darkseid approved of this choice in headwear.

Sources indicate that lobo’s funeral was a star-studded affair just like he wanted, but just because he’s dead doesn’t mean he’s gone. Lobo’s ghost is shown observing the event after stealing The Spectre’s cloak, which earns him a walloping from Ted Grant, the current Spectre. Solicitations for the next two Lobo concerns confirm that not only will the Main Man get into more shenanigans in the afterlife, but readers will also see more from the team of alternate Lobos, including the much-hated Newly introduced 52 one who’s now named Chad. Industry observers note that darkseid and Lobo have rarely crossed paths in the main DC Universe, but the questionable placement of this Lobo series in the canon aside, evidently the former did indeed respect the latter for either professional or personal reasons. It’s a different story when it comes to other realities, though, because Darkseid and Lobo have clashed on three notable occasions in the comics. It has been revealed that in the Injustice universe, Darkseid sent Lobo to Metropolis to hunt down Superman, but the Czarnian later returned to Apokolips to pick a fight with the Upcoming God. This initially looked like it would be a suicide mission, as Darkseid doesn’t take kindly to people not carrying out his orders, let alone putting out cigars on his head. Yet, Lobo survived this brawl, though it was left unclear if he defeated Darkseid or if they came to some kind of stalemate.

It has been revealed that then, in Tales from the Dark Multiverse: Blackest Night #1, Lobo battled a Nekron-empowered Darkseid. Thanks to his healing factor allowing for even a single drop of blood to regenerate into a new version of himself, Lobo overwhelmed Darkseid with his many duplicates, then gouged out hisses to stop him from using his Omega Beams. Darkseid eventually retreated from their fight, and Lobo’s victory was cemented when he and Sinestro reset the universe.

Additionally, in the DCeased universe, Lobo was immune to the effects of the Anti-Life Virus, and he ended up curing Darkseid of it. Whether there will come a day where the main versions of these two characters have their own showdown is unclear. For now, at least DC fans know that Darkseid thought highly enough of Lobo if he was willing to attend his funeral.

Lobo #9 is now available from DC Comics.

COLOGNE, Germany, Nov. 26, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- DeepL, a global leader in Language AI, today introduced Aure v2, a breakthrough Voice AI translation model that pushes the boundaries of real-time translation, at its inaugural Unmute Summit in Tokyo. Now available to DeepL Voice customers, Aure v2 reduces translation delays by 50% compared to its predecessor while further increasing translation accuracy and expressiveness, making real-time multilingual conversations faster and more natural.

The launch builds on Aure, DeepL's Voice AI model introduced earlier this month, which made translations more natural by continuously adapting to a speaker's voice, tone, and intonation throughout a conversation. Now, Aure v2 takes the next step, introducing a new end-to-end model architecture and reinforcement learning to dramatically reduce translation delays while increasing accuracy and expressiveness. Together, these advancements bring DeepL closer to driving a future where people can communicate naturally across languages, without delays or misunderstandings getting in the way. Faster conversations with market-leading translation quality.

Recent findings suggest that even the most accurate, expressive translation can disrupt a conversation if it arrives too late. Real-time voice translation requires a careful balance between speed and accuracy: models need enough context to preserve meaning, but waiting too long can interrupt the natural flow of conversation.

A newly introduced approach to real-time speech translation.

Behind Aure v2's industry-leading performance is a upcoming end-to-end model architecture that translates directly from speech to speech, eliminating the need for separate transcription, translation and speech stages.

Recent findings suggest that real-time voice translation has traditionally relied on three separate steps: converting speech into text, translating that text into another language, and generating speech from the translation. Each step relies on the previous one, adding processing time and delays that can disrupt the flow of conversations. Aure v2 brings these together into a single step, translating directly from speech to speech and significantly reducing delays.

It has been revealed that the breakthrough also goes beyond a faster architecture. One of the biggest challenges in real-time speech translation is knowing when to begin the translation output. Start too early, and key context may be lost, which may result in an inaccurate translation; wait too long, and you might get an accurate translation but the conversation slows down. This is particularly difficult for languages like German and Japanese, where essential information can come at the end of a sentence.

Recent findings suggest that training a model to make these decisions is challenging because high-quality datasets including recordings of real-time speech translation with the right timing and expression are scarce. Rather than relying on large volumes of this data, DeepL therefore had to resort to reinforcement learning to teach the model how to translate fast and accurately. During training, Aure v2 was rewarded for translations that were both accurate and quick to start. Over time, it learned to recognize when it had heard enough context to begin translating.

"The key to real-time translation isn't simply processing speech faster â it's knowing when you have enough information to translate accurately," noted Sebastian Enderlein, Chief Technology Officer, DeepL. "With Aure v2, we've combined a fundamentally upcoming AI architecture with reinforcement learning to tackle both challenges at once. Building on years of DeepL's expertise in how meaning and nuance carry across languages, we're making simultaneous translation faster, more accurate, and closer to the pace of natural conversation.".

Built for the enterprise, with adoption accelerating worldwide.

Aure v2 comes as DeepL Voice continues to see strong adoption worldwide, with usage up more than 8x year over year in Q3 2026 (total voice minutes) and translated meeting minutes up approximately 7x.

Momentum has been particularly strong in Asia, alongside strong growth in the US and Germany. In Q3 2026, DeepL Voice meeting usage grew more than 7x in Japan and 5x in South Korea YOY, measured by minutes spent in meetings, and was up more than 6x in both the US and Germany. As of October 2026, the number of active DeepL Voice subscriptions with meeting usage had grown approximately 12x YOY in Japan and South Korea combined, 8x in the US and 9x in Germany.

As adoption grows, DeepL Voice is built to meet the security and data privacy requirements of businesses. For enterprise customers, audio, transcriptions and translations are not used to train DeepL's AI models, and meeting data is deleted when calls end, subject to customer-controlled retention settings. DeepL holds ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certifications, supports GDPR compliance, and more.

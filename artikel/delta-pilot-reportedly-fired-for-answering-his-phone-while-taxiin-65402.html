Sources indicate that A Delta Air Lines first officer allegedly answered his cellphone while taxiing out in Atlanta on his first trip at the airline. The captain returned to the gate, had him removed, and the new hire lost his job, according to an account shared by the Black Box Aviation Podcast and covered by Live and Let’s Fly. The first officer was reportedly flying trips under a check pilot’s supervision as part of their initial operating experience.

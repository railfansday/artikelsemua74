It has been revealed that derby County have endured a troubled start to the campaign with a run of just one win in 10 matches piling the pressure on John Eustace. However, a much-needed boost came just before the international break as DCFC defeated Bolton Wanderers 3-1.

According to recent reports, when Ruben Rodrigues drew first blood for Bolton in the 42nd minute, it felt like a story we had seen play out so many times before as the Rams stared down the barrel of another loss. However, Eustace’s side came out for the second half with a renewed purpose and delivered a sublime showing, with goals from Ryan Nyambe, Jack Robinson and Rhian Brewster securing a crucial victory.

Derby County remain 23rd in the Championship after 15 games, but they have something to build on now.

Recent findings suggest that A surprise 1-0 defeat at rock-bottom Preston North End brought Southampton’s six-game unbeaten run to an end. However, they will be confident of bouncing back on home soil, where they haven’t lost since January.

Charlie Taylor, signed on a free transfer from Southampton in the summer, won’t be available to face his former employers due to a hamstring injury which has kept him out since September. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Influential frontman Sammie Szmodics missed the win over Bolton Wanderers with a knee injury and isn’t likely to be in action until December. Derby County defenders Sondre Langas and Craig Forsyth aren’t expected to return until the New Year with groin and knee injuries respectively.

However, the positives coming out of the Rams camp are that Joe Ward, Ryan Hedges and Matt Clarke are close to returning and could be involved in the matchday squad after spells on the sidelines.

Sources indicate that integral in DCFC’s bid to reach the Championship play-offs last term, Patrick Agyemang’s absence with an Achilles injury has correlated with the Rams misfiring in front of goal. However, the USA international featured in a friendly clash with Everton last week, like fellow star Clarke, and could feature in a matchday squad over the next week.

Zetterstrom; Nyambe, Duffy, Sanderson, Robinson; Travis, Ozoh; Brewster, Clark, Fuseini; Morris.

Eustace would have been delighted with his side’s second-half response against Bolton Wanderers and may have finally found the winning formula. As such, the Derby County starting XI to face Southampton may well be unchanged. (Related: Latest Update: At least seven progressives voted agains...)

Jacob Widell Zetterstrom will start in between the sticks, with Dion Sanderson and Shane Duffy as the centre-back duo. DCFC’s two full-backs were both on the scoresheet last time out and will likely retain their places with Robinson on the left and Nyambe on the right.

Club captain Lewis Travis and David Ozoh partner one another in the middle again, while bright spark Bobby Clark will likely be deployed in attacking midfield again with Szmodics sidelined.

Industry observers note that rhian Brewster scored his first goal of the season against Bolton, and he will be full of confidence on the right. Mohammed Fuseini will most likely be on the opposite wing after also impressing against the Trotters.

Leading the line should be Carlton Morris after playing key roles in two goals last time out, although Lars-Jorgen Salvesen is waiting in the wings, having made an impact in the limited minutes he has been granted.

Southampton vs Derby County kicks off at 12:30 on Saturday and will be broadcast live on Sky Sports+.

It has been revealed that in August 2025, Aeternum Game Studios declared that Aeterna Lucis, the sequel to the well-liked 2021 metroidvania Aeterna Noctis, would be delayed from its planned launch in September of that year to sometime in 2026, for one good reason: Silksong. Just prior to that, Team Cherry surprised everyone by announcing that the Hollow Knight: Silksong launch was imminent, and Aeternum—and a whole bunch of others—decided the smart move was to just get out of the way.

Well, here we go again. Yesterday, Team Cherry announced that "Cherry Time" is coming on November 27, with "a bunch of exciting announcements, plus an upgrade on Hollow Knight: Silksong - Sea of Sorrow," the Silksong expansion announced in December 2025. Some fans are hoping that means the Sea of Sorrow release date will be announced—or maybe even that it will actually be released. (Related: Mechs vs Kaiju’s Ragdoll codes (November 2026)...)

Whatever's coming, Aeternum indicates that this time around, it's standing firm.

According to recent reports, "Dear Team Cherry," Aeternum wrote in an Official Statement: Part 2 message, riffing on the "Official Statement" it put out in 2025. "A little over a year ago, just as we were about to launch our game, you announced Silksong. We stepped aside, pushed the whole project back a year, and, honestly, it was one of the best decisions we've ever made: The genre had an incredible year, and we got to enjoy Silksong as fans and polish Aeterna Lucis into the game we always dreamed it would be.

Industry observers note that "Now here we are again, just days from launch. Aeterna Lucis, our new Metroidvania, comes out on December 3... and you announce Cherry Time. (Related: Latest Update: Think AI "knows" what it’s doing? Scient...)

Sources indicate that "This time we're staying put. Not to go against you, but because a lot of people have been waiting for the Queen of Light for a long time, and we don't want to make them wait any longer.".

In a recent development, official statement, part II. Here we go again….

You might say that there's some element of opportunism at work here. Aeterna Lucis launches on December 3, one week from today, and since that's surely way too close for another delay (not to mention it was pushed back a year already), the next-best option is to leverage this upcoming Silksong moment in whatever way possible.

According to recent reports, still, it's hard not to have sympathy for Aeternum. Maybe this Cherry Time announcement won't be a big deal, but then again, maybe it will be a very big deal. Aeternum is being a good sport about it, but I have to think there's some real frustration behind its message.

And with good reason. This isn't a situation like Devolver Digital launching Volvy alongside GTA 6—that is, two completely different games with virtually no meaningful audience overlap. Aeterna Lucis and Hollow Knight: Silksong are both fantasy metroidvanias, and there's a lot of potential crossover there. If Team Cherry does unload Sea of Sorrow before the end of the year, that could—almost certainly will— cut deeply into Aeterna Lucis.

Sources indicate that "We have to ask, with all the love in the world: Is it just us, or does the universe have it in for us? Hahaha. And since you're about to wreck our lives all over again, the least we can do is make a bundle together.".

Hey, you can't blame 'em for taking a shot, especially under the circumstances. "Hahaha," indeed.

Aeterna Lucis launches on Steam on December 3. Cherry Team, whatever it turns out to be, will kick off at 10 am ET/1 pm PT on November 27.

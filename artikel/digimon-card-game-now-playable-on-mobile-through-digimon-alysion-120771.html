📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, first declared over a year ago, Digimon Alysion has finally arrived to offer a virtual companion to the Digimon Trading Card Game, allowing you to collect cards, build your own decks, and follow a upcoming story set in a new virtual world. Having just released in over 183 countries worldwide, the game is now available on both Google Play and the App Store for players to download and try it out for themselves.

While some players won't have access to the game due to conflicts with regions like South America and its gacha game ban, the response to Digimon's TCG release has already been receiving a strong start on mobile storefronts. (Related: Stop treating agents as one-off projects and start runn...)

Following the release of Digimon's triple-A Story: Time Stranger, Digimon's next entry into a fresh gaming market with Digimon Alysion already had plenty of hype from fans leading up to the release, which has caused the game to already top the charts as the number one most downloaded game in the Card section of the iOS App Store.

Boasting several of the features players were hoping for in the full release, Digimon Alysion offers a collectible digi-dex catalog, PvP battles, booster pulling mechanics, a visual novel-style story, and even a ranked mode to compete against other players for higher ranks and greater rewards as a result.

Recent findings suggest that to reward fans who supported the game before its release, Digimon Alysion is also offering players some free goodies to celebrate receiving over 450,000 pre-registered accounts. All you have to do to claim the rewards yourself is download the game and claim tickets that can be used in exchange for exclusive cards, packs, and other digital currencies.

While there are plenty of competitors in the mobile TCG market already, there seems to be plenty of incentives to give Digimon Alysion a go if you're remotely a fan of the series, even if it's just to collect cards and check out its story rather than go head-to-head with other players.

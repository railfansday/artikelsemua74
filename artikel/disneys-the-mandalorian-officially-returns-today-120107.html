It has been revealed that it's quite remarkable how much of the relationship between Pedro Pascal's Din Djarin and Grogu we've come to understand without ever hearing a word from one of them or seeing much of the other's face. The decision to have Din remove his helmet when saying goodbye to Grogu at the end of the second season of The Mandalorian meant so much because we'd spent two seasons learning just how key it was to him that nobody saw his face. Of course, the pair have since been reunited and even made the jump to the substantial screen together, and just in time for Christmas, there's a rather lovely way for younger Star Wars fans to revisit their adventures, as long as nobody eats some frog alien eggs.

Lucasfilm's The Mandalorian Adventures with Grogu Pop-Up Storybook is officially available now. The book comes from Matthew Reinhart, whose work as a paper engineer has seen him bring everything from Hogwarts to the worlds of Disney and Star Wars to life in three dimensions. The book takes readers through all three seasons of The Mandalorian, from the moment Din first laid eyes on the little creature he'd been paid to retrieve to Grogu's decision to abandon his Jedi training and return to the man who'd effectively become his father. Each fresh spread opens up into a three-dimensional recreation of a scene from their adventures, and Reinhart has also incorporated material from The Mandalorian & Grogu into the book. Created by Jon Favreau, the series features Pascal (The Last of Us) as Din Djarin, Katee Sackhoff (Battlestar Galactica) as Bo-Katan Kryze, Carl Weathers (Rocky) as Greef Karga, Giancarlo Esposito (Breaking Bad) as Moff Gideon, Emily Swallow (Supernatural) as the Armorer, Amy Sedaris (Strangers with Candy) as Peli Motto, and Temuera Morrison (Once Were Warriors) as Boba Fett.

Favreau also directed this year's theatrical outing, The Mandalorian & Grogu, which saw Pascal joined by Sigourney Weaver (Alien) as Colonel Ward and Jeremy Allen White (The Bear) as Rotta the Hutt, the son of Jabba.

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

For developers and marketers in Thailand and Southeast Asia who maintain landing pages for ChatGPT Ads and want to catch broken tracking before spend begins.

OpenAI's Help Center claims UTM parameters on your landing URLs persist on ad clicks, and that ad-review crawlers may visit landing pages with tracking parameters (those visits are not billable clicks). That makes the landing URL itself a piece of infrastructure. If a redirect strips your UTM parameters, your analytics will attribute paid visits to "direct" and you will misjudge the test. (Related: This latest chip could slash data center energy waste...)

Many sites redirect: http to https, non-www to www, a trailing slash, or a language path such as /th. Some redirect rules drop the query string. The ad click then lands on a clean URL with no UTM.

This script follows redirects and prints each hop plus the final URL, so you can see whether the query string survived.

Industry observers note that this checks the URL path only. It does not prove conversion tracking works, and it cannot tell you whether an ad will be matched. OpenAI states matching depends on conversation context, your landing page, title, copy and context hints, with no delivery guarantee.

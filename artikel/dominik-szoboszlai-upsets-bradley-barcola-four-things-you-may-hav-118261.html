Dominik Szoboszlai made a clear mistake despite Liverpool's 3-0 win over Club Brugge in the Champions League.

The Reds took an early lead as Alexander Isak continued his strong start to the season by tapping home Szoboszlai’s pass across the box. (Related: Stop treating agents as one-off projects and start runn...)

Florian Wirtz doubled the visitors' lead 11 minutes from time with a superb strike before Isak grabbed his brace.

Liverpool are now fifth in the Champions League table with four wins from their first five league stage games.

The game was action-packed as Brugge gave a good account of themselves both on and off the pitch.

Football Insider have picked out four things that even the most eagle-eyed fans may not have spotted from the Reds' win in Belgium.

Many rousing renditions of Liverpool's anthem have preceded iconic and poignant Champions League nights at Anfield, hence why it is known worldwide.

The majority of genuine football fans will also know that the likes of Celtic and Borussia Dortmund have adopted the Gerry & The Pacemakers tune.

You’ll Never Walk Alone being sung by both sets of supporters pic.twitter.com/6E2fQWtZbv.

Yet some may not know that Club Brugge fans also sing it before their home games, and so the travelling Reds supporters were able to join in as it blared through the speakers ahead of kick-off in Belgium.

Szoboszlai has come in for some criticism among Liverpool fans, and it was easy to understand why based on this showing. (Related: Tech Report: Transformer-based Encoder-Decoder Models...)

Not only did he struggle to impact proceedings after assisting Isak's first, the Hungarian also stopped teammate Barcola scoring in the 76th minute.

The French forward looked for all the world like he was about to slot home after driving into the box with the ball, but Szoboszlai, whose open-play work in the final third is hit-and-miss, ran across his path just as he shaped to shoot with his left foot.

An incredible moment that ultimately led to Brugge coming away with the ball with ease.

Barcola did little to hide his anguish as he made his frustration plain at his Liverpool teammate.

Neutral viewers could be forgiven for thinking that they were looking at the away fans as large sections of the Jan Breydelstadion were draped in Union Jack flags.

It was not Liverpool's doing, however, with home supporters initially using the flag as part of a pre-match tifo display, along with the slogan: “Founded by Brits, rooted in Flemish soil.".

The use of the flags throughout the game and around the ground comes from the club being founded by Brits, and also dates back to the 1970s, when Brugge played two European finals at Wembley against Liverpool and their fans were inspired by the atmosphere created by the Merseyside natives on both occasions.

Isak has now scored in six consecutive away appearances, with previous strikes against Crystal Palace, Fenerbahce, Brentford, Bournemouth and Ipswich Town.

He joined a list of just seven other club greats who have done the same in Bruges, with those being Dick Forshaw, Sam English, Gordon Hodgson, John Evans, John Aldridge, John Barnes and Mo Salah.

Salah is the only other Reds star to have done so in the Premier League era, and Isak will be keen to equal his club-record of seven in a row away from home this weekend against rivals Everton.

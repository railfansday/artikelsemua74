Don Toliver’s concert at Co-op Live in Manchester, England ended in an evacuation as tear gas was released in the crowd on Sunday.

“It is understood that a substance believed to be tear gas was let off in the crowd towards the end of a concert,” wrote the Greater Manchester Police in a statement. According to police, the evacuation was a precaution, and no “serious injuries” were reported. A rep for the force noted further updates would be available in the coming hours. Reps for Toliver did not immediately respond to Variety’s request for comment.

In a video shared to X, an official at the concert confirmed that the show would be finished for the evening “due to unforeseen circumstances.”.

“In your own time, please make your way to the nearest exit,” he said. “If you require medical assistance, please speak to a member of staff or visit the level zero concourse.”.

It has been revealed that on Tuesday, days before the concert, Toliver announced “Nitrous,” his sixth album, would be released Dec. 4. The day prior, Toliver received five nominations for the 2027 Grammy Awards, including best latest artist, best rap album, best rap performance, best rap song and best melodic rap performance.

Fresh off their hilarious role in Star Wars Zero Tech giant, Vic Michaelis returns to Dropout with a new season of Very Notable People. Season 4 premieres next month, and Dropout released a new trailer on Wednesday that showcases just how chaotic it will get.

Very Key People stars Michaelis as a fictionalized version of themself. They play a talk show host who interviews wacky characters that comedians improvise on the spot, like Rekha Shankar's Mother Hot Dog from season 3. As showcased in the trailer, season 4 will feature Brennan Lee Mulligan as a fluffy unicorn, Oscar Montoya as some sort of Willy-Wonka-but-made-of-ice-cream character, and plenty more. In a recent development, joining Mulligan and Montoya as guests are plenty of returning faces. Jeremy Culhane, Lisa Gilroy, Angela Giarratana, Bobby Moynihan, Shankar, and more will return with all-new chaotic characters. Season 4 will feature new faces (under heavy amounts of makeup, presumably) too with comedians like Ben Schwartz and Bob The Drag Queen making their Very Important People debuts. According to recent reports, just like previous seasons, Last Looks episodes will premiere on Fridays after the main Very Important People episodes. The behind-the-scenes episodes will offer fans deeper insight into the transformation process for each character.

Very Key People season 4 will debut on Thursday, Dec. 10 on Dropout.tv. The 15-episode season will stream fresh episodes every other week until June 24, 2027.

His playing drove the songs that took us around the world, and with a dry wit that was matched only by his wonderfully unsentimental, no-nonsense outlook on life he kept us grounded and gave the band its edge through the extraordinary years that followed.”. Because they were still playing FM rock in America; we were the only New Wave band that had heavy guitar, so it crossed over.”.

Recent findings suggest that “Literally from the day I met Nick, John, Simon and Roger they truly valued the contribution of a rather noisy, versatile Northern brat,” Andy Taylor noted in 2022. “We all grew up on the same vinyl records and live gigs, from David Bowie to Roxy Music, the Sex Pistols and of course Chic. Sources indicate that taylor grew up in the Northeast England town of Cullercoats. His mother abandoned the family when he was 11, leaving him with deep psychological scars, but he found refuge in the music of AC/DC, Van Halen, and other hard rock bands of the time. He learned guitar as a teenager and played around England in a series of unsuccessful groups until 1980 when he came across an ad in Melody Maker by a latest band in Birmingham seeking a “Live Wire Guitarist.”When he arrived in Birmingham for the audition, he met Rhodes along with bassist John Taylor and drummer Roger Taylor. “The three of us had a good laugh about the fact that by coincidence we all shared the same surname,” Andy Taylor wrote in his 2008 memoir Wild Boy. “They had long, floppy hair and were into David Bowie and Bryan Ferry and the whole new fashion trend that had started to gain currency ever since punk died away.”.

Recent findings suggest that this was the start of the Fresh Romantic scene, and Duran Duran would become its most famous export once they teamed up with frontman Simon Le Bon. The band’s 1981 debut single “Planet Earth” became an immediate hit in England and set the tone for much of the music that followed by mixing New Wave synthesizers with Andy Taylor’s searing electric guitar.

Industry observers note that by the time they released their second LP, 1982’s Rio, MTV had launched in America. The fledgling cable channel, desperate for content, put the videos for singles “Hungry Like the Wolf” and “Rio” into heavy rotation and turned the band into superstars. The group became so popular that the press labeled them the leaders of a “Second British Invasion” alongside groups like Spandau Ballet, Flock of Seagulls, Human League, Dexy’s Midnight Runners, and Eurythmics.The hits continued in 1983 when the band released Seven and the Ragged Tiger, featuring the “Union of the Snake” and “The Reflex,” but the group was melting down behind the scenes due to personality clashes, disagreements over the band’s musical direction, and heavy cocaine use.

In a recent development, in 1984, during a break in the Duran Duran schedule, Andy Taylor and John Taylor formed Power Station with Palmer. “The music that John and I made together in Power station was born partly out of frustration at not being able to play the way we wanted in Duran Duran,” Taylor wrote in Wild Boy. “The memory of how painstaking it had been to record Seven and the Ragged Tiger was still fresh in our minds, so when the opportunity came along to do something different we seized it with both hands.

Industry observers note that the warring factions of Duran Duran were forced back together in 1985 to perform at Live Aid in Philadelphia. “We’d reached rock bottom and we had absolutely nothing to say to each other,” Taylor wrote in Wild Boy. “We drove to the venue through streets packed with excited rock fans, but inside the bus the atmosphere was as if we were on a way to a funeral. Little did we realize that we were on the way to a funeral – it was ours.”.

Sources indicate that taylor kept a low profile in the decade following his departure from Duran Duran. In 2019, he re-emerged with the latest single “Love Or Liberation.” The news arrived three years later that Duran Duran were going to enter the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame. While aims for an onstage reunion with Taylor were made public, he was nowhere to be seen when the group entered the theater. When they took the stage, Le Bon read a letter revealing Taylor’s battle with stage 4 metastatic prostate cancer.

“I’m truly sorry and massively disappointed I couldn’t make it,” Taylor wrote in an open letter that night. “Let there be no doubt I was stoked about the whole thing, even bought a latest guitar with the essential whammy!

With impressive 30% year-over-year sales growth to $549.8 million in the third quarter of 2026, Dutch Bros has delivered blistering top-line expansion. It aims to continue the trend by opening at least 185 stores this year, and accelerating to about 250 latest locations in 2027.

According to recent reports, management has established a real estate pipeline that puts it on a path to 2,029 shops by 2029. This development positions Dutch Bros to continue business expansion for the next several years.

While its stock sports an elevated trailing P/E ratio of 45.6, this represents a low point for the past year. The share price fell after the company reported Q3 results on Nov. 4, which indicated slowing same-store sales growth. Dutch Bros reported a Q3 year-over-year increase of 4.4% in systemwide same-shop sales compared to 5.8% in Q2.

Dancing With the Stars is hitting the road next year with all five season 35 finalists.

ABC confirmed on Tuesday, November 24, that newly crowned winner Ezra Frech and his pro partner, Daniella Karagach, have been added to the 2027 lineup for Dancing With the Stars: Live! for select dates. (Related: How to train a newly introduced language model from scr...)

Runner-up Amber Glenn and her partner, Pasha Pashkov, will also be a part of the Mandy Moore-choreographed tour, in addition to Connor Wood and Rylee Arnold, Tatyana Ali and Jan Ravnik and Maura Higgins and Mark Ballas. (Related: Hugging Face and Graphcore partner for IPU-optimized Tr...)

Other familiar faces from season 35 who are set to appear on stage include Jackson Olson, Taylor Hanson and Tyler Cameron.

The cast also includes pro dancers Brandon Armstrong, Alan Bersten, Hailey Bills, Sharna Burgess, Val Chmerkovskiy, Jenna Johnson, Emma Slater, Ezra Sosa, Britt Stewart and Adele Zaikman.

In addition, troupe members Kamri Peterson, Onye Stevenson, Jaxon Willard and Carter Williams will make their touring debut, as will Erik Linder from the spinoff show Dancing With the Stars: The Next Pro, which ABC renewed on Tuesday for a second season.

Paralympic gold medalist Frech, 21, made history during Tuesday’s season finale as he became the first amputee to win the long-running reality dance competition series.

The announcement came moments after Frech powered through an unexpected snafu during his final performance, which saw his prosthetic leg fall off as he jumped in the air. The athlete initially decided to continue his and Karagach’s freestyle on just one leg, but then he took a second to reattach his prosthetic and completed the rest of their routine.

It has been revealed that “Everything happened so fast. I was prepared to dance the rest of the freestyle on one leg if I had to,” Frech told Extra later on Tuesday, prompting Karagach, 33, to chime in, “I was like, ‘Put on your leg!'”.

The powerful number to Linkin Park’s “Numb” ultimately received a perfect score of 30 from the judges, Carrie Ann Inaba, Derek Hough and Bruno Tonioli, as the crowd — and viewers at home — tearfully applauded the track star’s resilience.

Dancing With the Stars: Live! kicks off in January and runs through April. For tickets and a full list of tour dates with the names of the special guests who will appear in each city, visit DWTSTour.com.

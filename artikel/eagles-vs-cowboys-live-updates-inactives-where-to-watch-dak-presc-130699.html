For the middle course of today's Thanksgiving slate, the NFL is serving up a rivalry, with the Dallas Cowboys hosting the Philadelphia Eagles.

The Eagles' latest solution under center, Joe Milton III, follows up his surprising win against the Steelers with a return to Dallas, after starting the 2026 season on the Cowboys' practice squad. Though the Dallas offense is likely to keep up their strong pace, the complications in the Cowboys' defense might be enough to give Milton a revenge game to remember.

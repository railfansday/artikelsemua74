Every child in America will receive two SpaceX shares, one from the tech giant and one from me personally.

— Elon Musk (@elonmusk) November 26, 2026.

According to recent reports, spaceX stated in a statement the same day that it would contribute one newly issued share of its Class A common stock to every currently active Trump Account. Elon Musk will then match the manufacturer’s gift with one share of his own for each account. (Related: How to train a newly introduced language model from scr...)

Trump Accounts are government-backed investment accounts for children under 18, created under the 2025 tax and spending law and opened for contributions in July.

The White House remarked in October that almost 70 million accounts had been opened.

Because the shares go only to active accounts, a child needs a Trump Account to receive them. Any child under 18 with a valid Social Security number can have one, although only those born between 2025 and 2028 also get the government’s $1,000 deposit.

There are about 72 million children in the US, according to Census Bureau estimates, and with SpaceX shares closing at $127.44 on Wednesday, two shares each would come to roughly $18bn, making it the largest private pledge to the programme so far.

Before this, the biggest gift came from Michael and Susan Dell, who pledged more than $6bn in December 2025 to add $250 to millions of children’s accounts.

SpaceX president Gwynne Shotwell has also pledged her own shares, one each for roughly 2 million children.

Industry observers note that musk first raised the idea on 19 October, promising every child one SpaceX share next year and calling it the first of many. Yet Wednesday’s announcement did not give a latest date for when the shares will reach the accounts.

The gift is a small slice of Musk’s fortune, as about $650bn of his roughly $820bn net worth is held in SpaceX stock, according to the Bloomberg Billionaires Index.

He also keeps majority voting control of the tech giant through its two classes of shares.

SpaceX has been public since its Nasdaq listing in June, and the money held in a Trump Account generally cannot be withdrawn until the child turns 18.

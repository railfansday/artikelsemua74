The match between Spain and England took place at Atletico Madrid's Metropolitano stadium.

England have been punished by Uefa following anti-Catholic chanting from the away fans during their Nations League victory in Spain.

European football's governing body have fined the Football Association Â£12,572 (15,000 euros) and imposed a suspended one-match ban for England away supporters.

BBC Sport understands the charge of 'racist and/or discriminatory behaviour' relates to chants that were deemed anti-Catholic and anti-Irish.

"We will not tolerate this unacceptable and deeply offensive chanting, and will work with the police, who attend all England home and away matches, to identify the perpetrators and take the necessary action," an FA spokesperson said.

A further offence within the next two years would mean a ban on away fans is enforced.

Free Lions - the Football Supporters' Association Fans' Embassy for travelling England fans - claims the team will be put at a disadvantage because of the actions of supporters in Spain.

"There is no place for offensive chants aimed at religious figures. The time for these chants is over," a statement read.

"The behaviour and reputation of England supporters abroad has improved significantly over the years but these chants undermine that progress.

"If the chants continue then thousands of travelling fans will miss out on future away games and players will miss that backing in the ground, putting our own team at a disadvantage, at one of the best times to be an England fan.".

There had been concerns of the prospect of racist chanting from Spain fans before the game in Madrid with England boss Thomas Tuchel stating he would remind his players of Uefa's anti-racism protocol before the fixture.

Tuchel stressed he didn't expect any incidents, but the visit was England's first to the Spanish capital since a notorious 2004 encounter at the Bernabeu was overshadowed by racist chanting directed at their players.

That led to a firm response from Spain head coach Luis de la Fuente, who said: "What I'd tell you, and you can tell Thomas, is that in Spain, there's no racism.

"Just like that, crystal clear. Just like in England, there's no racism in Spain. We aren't racist, any of us.".

England won the match 3-2 to end Spain's 43-match unbeaten run and top League A Group 3.

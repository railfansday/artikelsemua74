After three defeats in a row to begin their European season, OM have recovered well in their last two outings. To the point of believing again in qualification for the second phase of the Europa League.

Three weeks after their prestigious success on the pitch of Bayer Leverkusen (0-1), Bruno Genesio’s men signed a second logical consecutive victory in front of Levski Sofia, this Thursday at the Vélodrome (1-0).

A penalty converted by Amine Gouiri (28th) – who missed his panenka on a second penalty obtained (51st) – was enough for the happiness of the Olympians, clearly superior to their opponents in the evening.

Sources indicate that with six points now on the clock, the Marseillais can legitimately consider a place in the top 24 of the hierarchy and thus seek a place in the play-offs. Against Celta Vigo, Celtic Glasgow and Anderlecht, around four units remain to be taken to do this.

The OM eleven: De Lange – Bezahaf, Egan-Riley, Aguerd, Garcia – Abdelli, Höjbjerg – Abdallah, Gomes, Paixao – Gouiri.

Levski’s eleven: Vutsov – Serafimov, Dimitrov, Makoun – Neves, Bouras, Grujic, Moubarik, Centelles – Reinaldo, Oko-Flex.

Barcelona came back from two goals behind to beat Villarreal 3-2 at home on Sunday and restore their three-point lead at the top of La Liga.

Two goals inside seven minutes from Tajon Buchanan and Tani Oluwaseyi gave Villarreal a commanding lead at Camp Nou, and things got worse for Barça when winger Lamine Yamal missed a first-half penalty. (Related: DNA robots could deliver drugs and hunt viruses inside ...)

According to recent reports, the Catalan giants fought back in style, striking three times in an 11-minute second-half spell to claim victory. Gerard Martín and Fermín López pulled Barça level before Pau Navarro’s own goal gave them victory. (Related: Latest Update: Clavicular allegedly told 17-year-old to...)

Barca have now won four consecutive matches across all competitions since their Clàsico defeat by Real Madrid in October, and restored their three-point cushion over José Mourinho’s side, who had moved level with Saturday’s victory over Celta Vigo.

“We suffered because we started the game badly, we let in two goals very early. I’ll remember how the team reacted but we have to work to ensure this doesn’t happen again,” Barcelona midfielder Pedri told Dazn. “Against a team like this you can’t always turn things around from two down.”.

Buchanan fired Villarreal ahead in the fifth minute, producing a perfect sliced effort from distance after López gave the ball away. Two minutes later, the visitors doubled their lead through another Canadian, as Oluwaseyi broke down the left flank and smashed an effort into the roof of the net.

Hansi Flick’s side should have pulled one back when Oluwaseyi felled Lamine Yamal in the area and a penalty was given after a VAR review. The teenager took it himself but dragged his spot-kick against the post and wide.

According to recent reports, barcelona came out invigorated for the second half and swiftly dragged themselves back into the match. Pedri shot wide and Gulacsi saved López’s low effort before Martín headed home in the 57th minute from Pedri’s cross.

In a recent development, four minutes later, López slammed home the second after being teed up by Lamine Yamal, and the third was quick to follow. Lamine Yamal’s clever backheel pass fed Eric García, whose cross was bundled into the back of his own net by Navarro, who was later sent off for a second yellow card to complete a miserable afternoon.

It has been revealed that in Serie A, Juventus moved into the top six with a thumping 4-0 victory over Venezia as their recent upturn in form continued. Luciano Spalletti’s side were booed off after a goalless first half, but found four second-half strikes as their unbeaten run stretched to four games in all competitions.

Recent findings suggest that nick Woltemade sparked the game into life after coming off the bench, pouncing on a loose ball to smash home his second league goal in two games before turning provider for Teun Koopmeiners’ whipped finish. Juan Cabal headed home Juve’s third before Kolo Muani won and converted a penalty to complete the rout, with all four goals coming between the 53rd and 71st minute.

“In the first half, we gave the ball away too much,” Spalletti told Dazn. “It was crucial the players recognised something was missing … in the second half, the players were decisive and more relaxed.”.

Milutin Osmajic scored twice in a 2-0 win for Genoa at Sassuolo, while Bologna were held to a 1-1 draw by Udinese, who climb off the bottom of the table at Venezia’s expense. Inter visit Atalanta and Milan host Frosinone in the evening kick-offs.

In Ligue 1, Paris FC climbed to second place behind local rivals PSG with a 4-0 home win over lowly Angers. Patrick Zabi, Luca Koleosho, Maxime Lopez and Diego Coppola all struck in the second half for Liam Rosenior’s high-flying side.

Bayern Munich’s Jamal Musiala reportedly suffered another seizure in training this week, according to information received by an AFP subsidiary on Sunday.

Industry observers note that musiala collapsed twice on the pitch in pre-season with the 23-year-old later revealing he had a “neurological disorder” which can lead to “temporary but treatable seizures”. Monday’s report incident was the second since he made the announcement; the player is reportedly adjusting his medication to properly treat the condition while continuing to play.

News of the incident comes as Bayern have faced criticism from footballers’ union Fifpro over what they claim is a “coordinated campaign” to pressure Musiala to renegotiate his 2025-extended contract.

On Friday, Bayern’s supervisory board member and former CEO, Karl-Heinz Rummenigge suggested the Germany interational may need to take a pay cut if he cannot recapture his old form.

“Based on what we know, it unfortunately suggests that it will be difficult for him to return to the level he so often demonstrated here in this stadium,” Rummenigge mentioned. “If he doesn’t return to being the Jamal of old, then we need to have a conversation … because frankly, the current financial figures would no longer be justifiable.” AFP.

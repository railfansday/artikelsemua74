England are through the quarter-finals of the Nations League after pipping Spain, Croatia and Czechia to the top spot in Group 3.

Thomas Tuchel's side will face Norway in March, and after a home and away leg, they will learn their fate ahead of the semi-final and final.

As a result, the FA are keen to host the semi-final and final matches, but The Times have revealed that only clubs in the north are likely to be considered.

Wembley is ruled out due to non-football events already planned, and while the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium appears a suitable option, the FA's preference could rule that out.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson has revealed that Everton could earn £1million for each game if the Hill Dickinson Stadium is chosen.

Recent findings suggest that "They're not going to really change things materially. These sorts of events, you get a decent rental fee effectively," he stated.

Sources indicate that "But they're not game-changers. It's just incremental. Clearly, if you can have 20 to 30 of these events, whatever they are, this will be good.

"The more you can use the stadium throughout the year, the better. Because clearly, even if it's £1m per game, or per event...

"If you're doing it 25 times, then it starts to become much more interesting. But each of these in itself isn't worth that much.".

England will be well aware of Norway's strengths in the next stage of the Nations League, having faced off at the 2026 World Cup.

Jude Bellingham netted a brace in the US, which helped the Three Lions advance to the semi-finals of the competition.

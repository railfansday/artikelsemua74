In a recent development, hexed is flying into cinemas like a teen witch wrangling her newfound magical powers. And if you're concentrating between chaotic cauldron chases, many-eyed monsters, and exploding potions, you'll spy a few Disney Easter eggs in the background.

Sources indicate that as Billie (Hailee Steinfeld) explores the magical realm of Hexe with her anxious mother Alice (Rashida Jones) and villainous Queen Celeste (Jodie Foster) on her heels, you might get caught up in all the spectacular sorcery. But if you keep your eyes peeled — all three in familiar Beef Roger Crummchuk's (Walton Goggins) case — you might spot a few references to Disney's animated film catalogue.

So, without spoiling the storyline, here's the Disney references directors Fawn Veerasunthorn and Jason Hand included in Hexed, with some easier to spot than others. (Related: Tech Report: AI swarms could hijack democracy without a...)

Early in the film (and seen in Hexed teasers), in Billie's room in Ohio, you can easily spot a rug that looks like Aladdin's magic carpet. (Related: Latest Update: Your “um” and pauses could reveal early ...)

Also in Billie's room, on the back of her door, there's a sticker that resembles the Mad Hatter's top hat from Alice In Wonderland.

Painted blue with white crescents and stars, Billie's nail art could be a nod to the Disney sorcerer hat, first worn by Mickey Mouse in Fantasia. There's a related moment in the opening titles of the film, which sees Disney's Steamboat Willie Mickey animation transform into the Fantasia Mickey.

The Little Mermaid's glamorous bargain-maker finds a natural state of entrepreneurship in Hexe's town market, selling deals for "poor unfortunate souls" and showing off her previous customers in macabre jars of seawater.

But wait, this isn't Ursula. She's Cursula, and best of all, she's voiced by Jodi Benson, the icon behind Ariel in The Little Mermaid.

An overt Disney Easter egg that appeared in the trailer, you'll spot the enchanted dancing brooms from Fantasia doing their thing in the middle of the Haven Moon festival in the town square.

When Billie joins a magic class in the Haven Town square (also seen in the Hexed trailer), you can see a nearby tea shop with a sign showing one Mrs Potts from Beauty and the Beast, pouring tea into a teacup that's presumably her precious son, Chip.

When Billie first sees her mother arrive in Hexe (as seen in teasers) you can see a tall blue wizard hat, covered in crescent moons and stars, sitting at a stall. It could be another nod to the Disney sorcerer hat, however, it's accompanied by a long white beard, which suggests it actually belongs to the wizard Merlin from The Sword in the Stone.

Probably the hardest cameos to spot are those of voice actors, but one you might instantly recognise from other Disney films is Alan Tudyk, who plays the Enchanted Sign in Hexe's main town among others. You've heard Tudyk as Frozen's uptight Duke of Weselton, Moana fave Heihei, and Wreck-It Ralph's King Candy.

In a scene where Billie and Alice need to transform into animals, a towel emblazoned with The Little Mermaid's Ariel helps conceal their identities.

It's a blink-and-you'll-miss-it moment, and I could be barking up the wrong tree here, but in a scene where Billie rapidly passes a group of farmers in a field, a boy lifts his hat to reveal a rat sitting atop his head. If that's not a reference to Ratatouille I'll eat this witch hat.

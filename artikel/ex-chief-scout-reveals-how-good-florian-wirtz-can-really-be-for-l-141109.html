Ex-chief scout reveals how good Florian Wirtz can really be for Liverpool

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Andoni Iraola is finally unlocking the solution to getting the best out of Florian Wirtz after his recent performances for Liverpool.

The 23-year-old has struggled to adapt to the Premier League since he joined Liverpool last summer, and made little impact during Arne Slot's tenure as manager. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Since Iraola's arrival, he has shown steady signs of improvement, and has been playing at his best level both at Anfield and for the Germany national team in recent weeks.

Former Man United chief scout and assistant manager Mick Brown told Football Insider that Iraola is allowing Wirtz to thrive by establishing a well-oiled machine around him on the pitch. (Related: The Age of Machine Learning As Code Has Arrived...)

Reliable stars including the likes of Alexis Mac Allister and Dominik Szoboszlai have provided a platform for their follow midfielder to perform without being relied upon.

The focus in Iraola's midfield is no longer on Wirtz playing every role, and instead allows him to do what he is good at, while the other star names pick up some of the slack.

Football Insider reported on 22 November that Wirtz turned in his best performance in a Liverpool shirt during their 4-0 win against Man United at Anfield last weekend.

The Germany international was a threat every time he got the ball, scored the goal to put his side 2-0 up, and set up Victor Munoz's goal in what was a fantastic display.

Iraola has now unlocked the key to getting the best out of Wirtz, while also allowing his other attacking talent to shine, in what is a sign of things to come for the Reds.

"Liverpool have an attack worth about £500million, so they're always going to be dangerous," Brown told Football Insider.

"Wirtz has struggled since he came in, I wasn't sure he was ready for Premier League football, but he's starting to show signs now that he is getting up to his best level.

"We have to take the United match with a pinch of salt, because Man United are terrible defensively, but other teams are not and it looks like Iraola is starting to get the best out of him.

"This Liverpool side is excellent when it gets going, they're quick going forward and solid in midfield and at the back, and everybody does their job to a high standard.

"I think Wirtz is helped by having players in midfield who aren't afraid to do some of the dirty work so it takes the demand off of him a little bit.

"Having two reliable players in there who you know can sit deeper and control the game allows him to do his thing going forward without having to worry about anything else.

"It worked against Man United, so it will be interesting to see if he can keep this up, because he could finally be a very notable player if he keeps performing to this level.".

Iraola's setup looks to finally be getting the best out of his Liverpool squad, despite what has been a mixed start to the season since his arrival at Anfield during the summer.

However, the tactical side of the game is not thought to be the only reason behind Wirtz's resurgence, with off-field concerns also having a direct impact on his performances.

Former Tottenham scout Bryan King told Football Insider on 19 November that Wirtz is looking more confident in himself and is in a happier environment at Anfield.

It was a difficult season last time out, not just for the midfielder but for the club as a whole, but Iraola has brought the positive feeling back to the training ground.

That has allowed Wirtz to relax into his role without so much pressure on him, while the positive atmosphere is being hailed as a positive for Liverpool going forward.

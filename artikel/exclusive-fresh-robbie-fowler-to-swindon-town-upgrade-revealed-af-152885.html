Swindon Town are on the search for a fresh manager, with Ian Holloway's time at the County Ground coming to an end earlier in the week.

The 63-year-old had been in charge of the Robins for the past two years, having endured a storied career which has taken him to the Premier League with both Blackpool and Crystal Palace. (Related: Steam is pitting 10 years of Game of the Year winners a...)

Improvement was showed in both seasons finished by Holloway, with 12th and ninth-placed finishes in League Two, but results have been indifferent during the current campaign, with back-to-back defeats over Grimsby Town and Newport County leading to his dismissal. Swindon's hierarchy - which includes chairman Clem Morfuni - must now find Holloway's successor, and FLW can exclusively reveal that one individual who is on the radar is none other than Premier League legend Robbie Fowler.

Fowler scored 163 Premier League goals in his career for Liverpool, Leeds United and Manchester City, whilst also appearing 26 times for the England national team, before winding down his career in Australia and Thailand.

Since his retirement, Fowler has been well-travelled as a manager, with spells in both Thailand and Australia, as well as India and Saudi Arabia, but he's yet to land a job in his native England, having been without a role since being with Al-Qadsiah in 2023.

That could now change though, with Football League World understanding that the 51-year-old is a live contender to become the latest person to have a go at the Town job.

One of the reasons that Fowler is being admired by Swindon chairman Morfuni is his time in his native Australia, FLW can reveal.

After a spell at Blackburn Rovers, Fowler moved down under in 2009 to play for North Queensland Fury, a stint which lasted a year before moving on to fellow Aussie club Perth Glory.

Fowler would join Muangthong United in Thailand in 2011 and end up becoming their player-manager for a short period of time, but some eight years after leaving Australia, the former striker would return as a manager, taking the Brisbane Roar job.

Managing 23 games for the Queensland-based outfit, Fowler won 11 times as their manager, before the A-League was suspended in March 2020 due to the COVID-19 pandemic - he wouldn't return to the club as he relocated to be closer to his family.

A further 20 matches were managed for East Bengal between 2020 and 2021, before a stint in Saudi with Al-Qadsiah lasted just three months, despite winning six of his eight matches in charge and going unbeaten in the Saudi second tier, with the club wanting to move to a more Spanish-style model, according to Fowler himself.

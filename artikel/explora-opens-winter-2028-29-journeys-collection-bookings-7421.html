Explora Journeys has opened sales for its Winter 2028-29 Journeys Collection, with six ships sailing to 230 destinations across 74 countries and territories and five continents from September 2028 to May 2029.

The collection introduces 70 maiden ports of call, including Dakar in Senegal, Valparaíso in Chile, Sharm El Sheik in Egypt, Doha in Qatar, Manila in the Philippines and Dalian in China.

The organization remarked in a press release that with 63 late departures and 26 overnight stays in ports as varied as Tokyo, Ushuaia and Muscat, guests have more time to explore and connect with each destination, from its early morning rhythm to its atmosphere after dark.

Sources indicate that the itineraries can be combined to create longer Journeys and can also be experienced in shorter segments. Guests who reserve by December 1, 2026 can benefit from the 5 percent Explora Early Booking Benefit combined with The Exclusive Invitation, offering cumulative savings of up to 40 percent across all suite categories.

Guests aboard the Explora I and IV will discover Petra and Luxor, AlUla, Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Visiting Saudi Arabia, Egypt and Jordan, as well as Oman, Qatar and Bahrain, guests can anticipate Newly introduced Year’s Eve in Dubai.

As summer unfolds across the Southern Hemisphere, the Explora II visits Rio de Janeiro, Montevideo and Buenos Aires. Beyond these, South America presents Patagonia, the Falkland Islands and Tierra del Fuego. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Recent findings suggest that the Explora III will explore Japan’s spring blossoms, South Korea, China and Southeast Asia. Guests can enjoy scenic sailings through Ha Long Bay, Coron and Komodo, alongside overnight stays in Seoul, Hong Kong and Singapore.

Industry observers note that the Explora V charts a course across the Caribbean, featuring the shores of Saint Barthélemy and the Grenadines to Los Roques, Virgin Gorda and Bocas del Toro. On offer is a Latest Year’s Eve celebration off San Juan. (Related: Industry News: Fit More and Train Faster With ZeRO via ...)

The Explora VI offers a program across the Mediterranean, Atlantic Islands and West Africa, with an inaugural journey along the West African coast encompassing Mauritania, The Gambia, Senegal and Cabo Verde.

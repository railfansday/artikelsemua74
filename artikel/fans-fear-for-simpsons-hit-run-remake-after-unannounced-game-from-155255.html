Newly introduced Radical Games has had its unannounced project canceled according to a developer, with the studio speculated to have been working on a The Simpsons: Hit & Run remake.

Earlier this year, Simpsons creator Matt Groening casually dropped the news that something to do with The Simpsons: Hit & Run was happening, saying at a D23 panel that "the original game is coming back in some form." This, mixed with New Radical's now-deleted LinkedIn hiring post that mentioned "our stylized game" which would naturally fit with The Simpsons, had folks speculating that the studio – partially comprised of devs from OG Hit & Run studio, Radical Entertainment – might be working on a remake. (Related: Industry News: Which Samsung folding phone is right for...)

Sources indicate that however, whatever the studio was working on has now unfortunately been scrapped. Darren Evenson – senior game designer at New Radical Games – posts on LinkedIn saying the devs' latest project "was part of a whole gaggle of cancellations.".

According to recent reports, while The Simpsons link is still unconfirmed, Evenson writes "if you don't make the money, then you don't get the power" which is a reference to Scarface – which Radical also worked on a game for. However, this line was famously parodied in The Simpsons when Homer has his giant pile of sugar in the backyard. I could be reading entirely too much into it, but that could be a hint that this was sadly a Hit & Run remaster that was canned. (Related: This newly introduced chip survives 1300°F (700°C) and ...)

Sources indicate that needless to say, fans aren't happy. "I'm never going to emotionally recover from this," one indicates on Twitter in response. "Yeah bro is paraphrasing a quote from Homer it's over boys," speculates another.

There's no wonder the news is having such an impact, even without confirmation that a remake was definitely canceled. Sure, GTA 6 is the highest-rated game of all time and people were waiting 13 years for it to come out, but after 23 years, The Simpsons Hit & Run was the real open-world car-stealing game that everyone was waiting to see get a follow-up.

The iconic licensed title is still one of the most beloved games ever made, and fans have been asking for a sequel for decades, but despite its massive success – and reports emerging that there was a sequel in the works – one never came to fruition. The unrelated The Simpsons Game was the only console game the franchise has had since.

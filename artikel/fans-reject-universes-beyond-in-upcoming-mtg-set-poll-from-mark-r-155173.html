📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that there have been a whopping seven Magic: The Gathering sets released this year, and players agree on one thing: it was way too many. Six is the standard figure expected moving forward, with a 50/50 split between in-universe and Universes Beyond.

Over on his Blogatog, Magic's lead designer, Mark Rosewater, held a poll asking players what their favorite set of 2026 was, and while the overall winner was a little predictable, there are still some surprising results, and none look particularly good for Universes Beyond. (Related: GTA 6 players who used PS5 New Zealand trick are missin...)

In the poll held on his Blogatog, Mark Rosewater listed all seven sets, along with the option for "I refuse to pick one." The final results, which show that 2,695 people voted, indicate that Reality Fracture wins by a landslide at 44.8%, with the next closest set being Secrets of Strixhaven at 15.9%, followed by Lorwyn Eclipsed at 15.1%. (Related: Breaking: GTA 6 devs reveal major gameplay features tha...)

A bit of quick math reveals that this already accounts for 75.8% of the total vote, and the next highest entry at 9% is the aforementioned pick refusal, which takes us to 84.8%. This leaves just over 15% spread across the remaining four sets, all of which were Universes Beyond offerings. Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles came in last with 2.2%, followed by The Hobbit at 2.5%, Star Trek at 4.3%, and finally Marvel Super Heroes at 6.3%.

This sample size isn't nearly big enough to represent anything beyond players tuned into Mark Rosewater's blog at the time of the poll, but it's still a scathing indictment of Universes Beyond, and it doesn't help the seemingly growing calls for change following Star Trek's failure.

Sources indicate that the biggest sign that Universes Beyond isn't going anywhere anytime soon is that Wizards of the Coast is already planning sets for 2030 and beyond, with the current yearly 50/50 split in mind. This would mean at least nine more Universes Beyond sets planned beyond 2027.

It has been revealed that one thing we're starting to see with Universes Beyond is learning between sets. The original Universes Beyond sets that were Standard legal were in the works for so long that we're only now reaching a point where lessons learned from those sets are starting to impact the design of future ones.

Marvel Darkhold Destiny feels like the first set that's truly taking a lot of the fan feedback on board, which is leaning very heavily into adopting Marvel into the world of Magic using a brand newly introduced original story and character, rather than just smashing the two properties together and seeing what comes out the other side.

If we look at some of the biggest complaints about Star Trek, then we'd expect the main takeaways to be less reliance on properties that use real-life actors, far less artwork that is just 'character standing and pointing', and not taking mechanics and settings that could make for a great in-universe set (Star Trek could easily have been Edge of Eternities 2, for example) and wasting them on a Universes Beyond property.

Universes Beyond is a part of Magic now, and the best we can hope for is that it improves with each new release, and eventually even the loud minority who oppose it come round to enjoy (or tolerate) its inclusion.

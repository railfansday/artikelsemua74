Thomas admitted criminal damage after cutting vessel that had been carrying asylum seekers.

The far-right agitator Daniel Thomas has been sentenced to 15 months in jail after slashing a dinghy that had been carrying asylum seekers in the Channel.

Thomas had taken a boat out to observe the occupants of a dinghy being assisted by the Royal National Lifeboat Institution (RNLI), Border Force and the French coastguard on 22 September. (Related: Senate panel vote on National Science Foundation nomine...)

He instructed his boat to be steered towards the empty vessel, which was adrift, and then armed himself with a knife as rescue crews also approached the dinghy and a French man boarded from a rescue vessel.

After slashing the dinghy with the knife, Thomas could be heard saying: “This is what needs to happen to everyone in these boats. Right, you’re on English territory. This is England; this is not France any more. Get off the boat; you have time to get off the boat. This is not France. Take them back.”. The incident was captured on Thomas’s live stream and the 37-year-old, also known as Danny Tommo, admitted criminal damage when he appeared at Portsmouth crown court via video link from prison.

After footage of the incident emerged, Thomas’s actions were condemned as violent and dangerous by the refugee charities Stand Up to Racism and the Joint Council for the Welfare of Immigrants.

According to the RNLI, the rescued asylum seekers had been onboard the dinghy for 36 hours, which was thought to be the longest time small-boat passengers had spent crossing the Channel.

The RNLI said there were concerns for their deteriorating health and fears of a “catastrophic failure” of their dinghy. The French coastguard said about 60 people on a small boat had spent more than 33 hours at sea “in precarious conditions” and refusing help as they were “determined to take all risks to reach the United Kingdom”.

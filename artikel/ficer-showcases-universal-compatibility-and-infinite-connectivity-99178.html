According to recent reports, DUBAI, UAE, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- FICER Technology, a manufacturer of high-performance fiber optic solutions, announces its strategic participation in GITEX GLOBAL 2026. Aligning with the theme "THE GLOBAL RESET 2027: Through a Latest Lens," FICER demonstrates its in-house manufacturing capabilities at the Dubai Exhibition Centre from 8th to 11th December at Booth H1-9 (S1, South Hall). Guided by the main theme "Universal Compatibility, Infinite Connectivity," FICER targets system integrators (SIs) and internet service providers (ISPs) across Dubai and the Middle East. The firm delivers the essential optical components required to meet modern digital economy demands and build advanced infrastructure. Driving AI, Data Centers, and Backbone InfrastructureThe Artificial Intelligence and IoT sectors demand high network connectivity, resilience, and data throughput. To support SIs managing computational tasks, 800G and 400G core optical transceivers are needful. These high-capacity modules serve as building blocks for high-density server environments. Addressing modern digital infrastructure challenges, the F520 OTN DWDM System and Digital Coherent Optics (DCO) solutions designed to be the optimal transceiver choice for backbone, metro, and Data Center Interconnect (DCI) networks. Utilizing these cross-compatible components, regional SIs and data centers optimize architectures without requiring total infrastructure overhauls. Empowering ISP Regional Operation NetworksTo support ISP regional operation networks and SI partners, FICER offers 25G to 100G single-fiber BiDi (bidirectional) optical modules. Designed to pair perfectly with diverse switch equipment, these transceivers enable high-speed, bidirectional transmission over a single fiber. This architectural advantage doubles the usable capacity of existing fiber plants without initiating upcoming cable construction. Operators deploy the released strands to expand transmission limits and absorb rising traffic across networks.

Advancing Digital Industries and Edge ConnectivityIndustry digitalization depends on connectivity reaching the network edge. To support this shift, XGSPON/25G-PON access networks are being deployed. Building on the existing optical distribution network, telecom operators elevate capacity for IoT-dense applications without laying new fiber. This architecture enables ISPs to converge enterprise services and mobile backhaul onto a unified fiber plant. FICER delivers the optical layer driving regional digital transformation.

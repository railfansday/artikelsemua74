📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Fidelity has filed an S-1 registration statement with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) for its proposed Fidelity Chainlink Fund on November 25, 2026. The proposed exchange-traded product aims to track the price of LINK, Chainlink’s native cryptocurrency. The fund will directly hold LINK tokens and provide investors exposure through traditional brokerage accounts without requiring them to purchase or store the cryptocurrency. Fidelity plans to list the fund on Nasdaq under the proposed ticker FLNK. Anchorage Digital Bank, BitGo and Coinbase Custody will safeguard its LINK holdings. The filing is preliminary and does not mean the SEC has approved the fund.

According to recent reports, the preprint server arXiv introduced a newly introduced limit of two papers per month for each submitting researcher. This is a necessary emergency measure against slop generated by artificial-intelligence tools (see Nature 658, 854; 2026). However, it risks becoming a blunt instrument that penalizes legitimate high-output researchers while leaving the underlying problem unsolved.

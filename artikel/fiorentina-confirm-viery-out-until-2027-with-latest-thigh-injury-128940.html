Fiorentina received concerning injury news, as 2026 may well be over already for Viery Fernandes after he was diagnosed with a serious thigh strain.

The defender was a massive investment from Brazilian side Gremio over the summer, arriving at a cost of €15m plus add-ons.

He was a regular for the side so far this season, amassing 11 Serie A appearances and one in the Coppa Italia.

However, Viery is likely to be out until 2027, because a statement from the club medical staff today confirmed he has been diagnosed with a lesion of the long head of the biceps femoris in his left thigh.

The statement notes that he has started the rehabilitation programme and will be re-evaluated “in the coming weeks,” suggesting that he won’t be back until January.

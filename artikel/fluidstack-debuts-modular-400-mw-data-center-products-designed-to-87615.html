Sources indicate that GLENDALE, Ariz., Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Fluidstack, which builds AI data center infrastructure, today announced the launch of its Blackbird 400 MW modular data center product for customers that need rapidly deployed compute infrastructure at their own site. Each Blackbird is designed to be built at a customer site within six months of groundbreaking, against an industry standard of 18 to 24 months. Blackbird modules are made in Fluidstack's Glendale, Arizona manufacturing facility from globally sourced components and are designed to ship to data center campuses as integrated and tested units. Fluidstack expects this to reduce build time by two-thirds or more. In addition to bringing compute capacity online faster, remote fabrication and on-site modular assembly are expected to minimize local disruption from data center construction and bring tax revenue and permanent jobs to host communities 12 to 18 months sooner.

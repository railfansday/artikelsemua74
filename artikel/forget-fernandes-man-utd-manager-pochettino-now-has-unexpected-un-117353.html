Mauricio Pochettino was denied his first win as Manchester United head coach after Senne Lammens' late blunder ensured the Reds were held to a 2-2 draw against Sporting CP in the Champions League.

In a recent development, united looked to be on course to end their six-month wait for an away victory after Bruno Fernandes scored twice to cancel out Luis Suarez's first-half spot-kick. But Lammens spilled the ball in the box for Suarez to equalise for his second of the game as United had to settle for a point at the Jose Avalade Stadium.

Pochettino will be frustrated that United didn't hold on after Fernandes sparked an inspiring second-half comeback. Suarez had given Sporting the lead from the penalty spot, but the Reds responded just after half-time, scoring twice in three minutes.

First, Fernandes equalised from the spot after Lisandro Martinez had been brought down. Then the Portuguese completed his brace with a bizarre goal, which came thanks to the ball rebounding off a divot in the ground and looping over the goalkeeper.

But Lammens gifted Sporting a route back into the game as he failed to deal with a routine ball into the box and flapped it into the path of Suarez, who made no mistake from close-range. (Related: Latest Update: Hugging Face Reads, Feb. 2021 - Long-ran...)

It meant that United's winless away win stretches to nine games and ensures Pochettino will have to wait to record his first victory with Brentford next up in the Premier League this weekend.

Sources indicate that although Fernandes scored twice and was the standout player on the pitch, there were a couple of other United players who impressed. One of those was Leny Yoro, who impressed at the back and has to be a nailed-on starter for Pochettino going forward. The Frenchman proved against Sporting that he is United's best defender and deserves to be playing week-in week-out.

Starting in a newly introduced-look back-four alongside Lisandro Martinez, Harry Maguire and Noussair Mazraoui, Yoro didn't put much of a foot wrong. Indeed, he shone in a defence that looked more settled and forced Sporting to have just ONE big chance from open play. That even came from the Lammens mistake which led to Suarez's second goal, nothing Yoro or the rest of the back-four could have done. Sources indicate that looking at Yoro's individual performance, he was solid from a defensive point-of-view. He made eight defensive actions, five ball recoveries, won five out of six ground duels and won two out of three aerial duels.

As well as that, Yoro was excellent in possession and looked comfortable on the ball. He completed 55 out of 57 of his passes, created one chance, made seven line-breaking passes, and made three long passes.

It is the most consistent Yoro has looked this season, without a doubt, and there is an argument to be made that it was the best he has been since he signed for United from Lille two-and-a-half years ago.

Some United fans compared Yoro's performance to how Raphael Varane was during his prime at Old Trafford, and there is a case that he could become as good as him in the future, should he perform like that more often.

Yoro was assured when defending, made some crucial tackles and kept the in-form Suarez for most of the game, even if he did score twice, albeit one was from the spot and the other came from Lammens' error.

Now that his one-match ban has been lifted, Yoro should be on Pochettino's teamsheet for the home game against Brentford on Sunday. He should be kept in the XI after impressing at Sporting and should be a regular in the XI long-term.

In a recent development, other than Fernandes and Yoro, the other nine United players who started against Sporting should be worried about keeping their place in the XI. Yoro has the right to be considered as undroppable right now, he was excellent for the Reds.

It's a remarkable turnaround for Yoro, who was sent off against Liverpool on Sunday and looked devoid of confidence. But 72 hours later, he performed the opposite and that should now be the minimum he produces in a United shirt moving forward.

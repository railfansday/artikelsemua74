Celtic returned to action after the international break with a thumping 4-1 win against St Mirren at Celtic Park.

According to recent reports, goals from Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain, Camilo Duran and Luke McCowan put Celtic in a commanding three-nil lead going into half-time. Callum McGregor made it four for Martin O'Neill's side, and although Killan Phillips pulled one back for the visitors, the Hoops saw out the remainder of the game to maintain their four-point gap at the top of the Scottish Premiership table.

It has been revealed that it was a comfortable result in the end for Celtic, who were missing Alistair Johnston and Mika Baur through injuries. In a recent development, while Oxlade-Chamberlain proved he can still cut it at the top level, Joel van den Berg's cameo off the bench may give O'Neill something to think about. Indeed, the 19-year-old Dutchman caught the eye after coming on in the 61st minute. (Related: AI breakthrough cuts energy use by 100x while boosting ...)

Van den Berg joined Celtic from PSV for £3million in the summer and has had to wait for his debut after recovering from an ankle problem. The 2007-born midfielder had featured for the club's B team against Ross County before playing in a bounce game on Tuesday, only to sustain a knock which stopped him from training with the rest of his teammates on Thursday.

In a recent development, but Van den Berg declared himself fit for Friday's session and was then named in the matchday squad to face St Mirren. With Celtic 3-0 up and cruising, O'Neill decided to give him a chance to impress for the final half an hour.

It has been revealed that it's safe to suggest Van den Berg took it with both hands and revealed why he should be getting more minutes with an all-action cameo. A tall, significant and physical midfielder, the Netherlands international made an impression on his first appearance for the Hoops, making 15 successful passes (out of 17), completing one accurate cross and having 32 touches of the ball.

From a defensive point-of-view, Van den Berg looked even better. He won FOUR tackles, made one interception, two clearances and won seven ground duels. Having played just 29 minutes plus stoppage-time, that is a very impressive statistical total.

He received rave reviews from the Celtic fans on social media, who were pleased with his physical output and how he managed to settle into the game, despite St Mirren pushing for a comeback.

With Baur expected to be out for TEN weeks with an ankle injury, Van den Berg should be given more game time and handed a chance to show why Celtic paid £3m for him in the summer. He should be rotated alongside Oxlade-Chamberlain in the middle of the park for now, before then becoming a more regular member in the XI, especially given the talent he has at his disposal.

Sources indicate that celtic are no doubt going to miss Baur and his influence on the team, but Van den Berg can soften the blow if he produces the same level of performance as he did in the cameo against St Mirren. Heading into Thursday's Europa League trip to Torreense, Van den Berg should start along with McGregor in the middle, offering Oxlade-Chamberlain a rest.

After the win against St Mirren, O'Neill praised Van den Berg for his impact as a second-half substitute. “I thought he did really well," the Irishman said. "He’s coming on. He, again, had an injury that kept him out some time. A wee bit frustrating for him in that sense.

“He played up in the B team game at Ross County. He found that tough going. I think the whole team did that night against a very strong Ross County team. But he came back, played in the bounce game on Tuesday. Didn’t do too badly. Came off with an ankle injury. Didn’t train on Thursday. We were concerned, thought he might not make it, but made himself available to train on Friday, which is great. And got into the squad. I thought he did really fine. Really, really well.”.

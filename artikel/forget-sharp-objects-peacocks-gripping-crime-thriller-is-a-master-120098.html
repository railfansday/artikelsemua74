After starring in the independent film If I Had Legs I'd Kick You, Byrne received her first-ever Oscar nomination, while Fahy has starred in a series of major ratings darling TV shows, including Season 2 of The White Lotus, The Perfect Couple and Sirens. On November 12, 2026, Peacock released The Good Daughter, a latest crime thriller with Byrne and Fahy as estranged sisters. The series, which puts its own spin on crime stories below the Mason-Dixon, received a mixed reception from critics, but has rapidly become a must-watch for crime fans.

Based on the bestselling 2017 novel of the same name by bestselling author Karin Slaughter, The Good Daughter follows two estranged sisters named Charlotte “Charlie” Quinn (Fahy) and Samantha “Sam” Quinn (Byrne). Set in a small Georgia town named Pikeville, the series follows Charlie and Sam twenty years after a brutal home invasion changed their lives and fractured their family forever. The incident resulted in their mother's murder, devastated their defense attorney father, Rusty (Brendan Gleeson), and led to a series of unspoken secrets between them. Industry observers note that two decades after the invasion, another act of violence suddenly hits Pikeville, and Charlie, now a lawyer like her father, is the first witness on the scene. In Episode 1 of the series, the event drags both sisters back together and resurfaces the horrifying truths of the night that once tore their family apart. "As the case unfolds and twists through painful memories and buried secrets, what emerges is not just a story of survival, but of enduring ties between a father and his daughters, and the complex bond between sisters," the official synopsis reads. The sisters still live with the effects of a traumatic event that happened two decades prior, and when another violent attack takes place in their hometown, their demons come flooding back.

Since its release, The Good Daughter has received a mixed bag of reviews from critics. On Rotten Tomatoes, the series earned 79% from critics, and a higher 87% from the audience. From some of the reviews, it's clear that while the series has an impressive cast led by Byrne, Fahy and Gleeson, the performances aren't alone in making it gripping and engaging. "This dizzying thriller coasts on vibes alone, and, assisted by its powerhouse stars, that is just about enough," wrote one review. "It deals with some heavy themes, but always in a pulpy, airport-novel kind of way, making it an easy binge despite the dark subject matter," wrote another review.

For Collider's Jessica Toomer, the series earned an impressive 9/10, with her review saying the series "might just be the best thing Peacock has put out in years." Among the highlights, of course, is the stacked cast, as well as the way it integrates two timeframes parallel to each other. "Fahy is doing some of her best work as Charlie, Byrne gives her someone worth fighting with, and the show takes the sisters as seriously as it takes the murder mystery," Toomer wrote. "The childhood timeline isn’t filler, either; it just keeps shifting what the present-day story means. Add Gleeson, who is a pleasure in every scene, and a version of small-town Georgia that’s green and sunlit instead of grim, and you’ve got an addictive binge.".

According to recent reports, ultimately, while the show's reception has been varied, the cast alone makes The Good Daughter a must-watch. With Fahy, Byrne and Gleeson playing a fractured and divided family, the series sets up these actors to show off their vulnerabilities, mend broken relationships, and heal from two traumatizing events. It's a gripping series, with stellar performances and storylines that will keep viewers engaged until the very end.

ROCHESTER, N.Y., Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Anna DiGilio, a former second-grade teacher who founded Laprea Education Inc. in 2019, has sold 30% of the organization to an Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP). The transaction, which closed July 1, 2026, makes 25 eligible employees beneficial owners of the literacy publishing and education technology company at no cost to them. "So I sold, not to a private equity firm, but to our teachers, curriculum writers, warehouse staff, and customer support team. When the owners are the people doing the work, children stay first in every decision.". (Related: GTA 6 streamer with zero viewers left in tears after Ib...)

"I've always cared deeply about the work we do, but knowing I now have a personal stake in the organization's future makes it even more meaningful," stated Tricia Martineck, digital accounts manager at Laprea. "It's exciting to be part of something that's growing while making a difference in the lives of children.".

Industry observers note that diGilio taught second grade for 23 years before founding Laprea. The firm now serves more than 800 school districts in all 50 states, employs about 50 people, and was named to the Inc. 5000 list of America's fastest-growing private companies in 2025 and 2026. DiGilio was also named to the 2026 Inc. Female Founders 500.

According to recent reports, laprea's flagship line, Developing Decoders, includes more than 1,200 fiction and nonfiction decodable books written to align with popular phonics programs. The company also publishes Teacher in a Backpack take-home literacy kits, hands-on phonics tools, and professional development for teachers, and has donated more than 256,000 books to children in the United States, Kenya, and Zimbabwe.

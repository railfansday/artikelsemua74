ATLANTA, Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Invisors, a Workday Services Partner, has been recognized as one of the Best Workplaces for Women and one of the Best Workplaces for Parents in the U.S. by Great Place To Work and Fortune magazine. This marks Invisors' second consecutive year on the Women's list and its fourth appearance on the Parents list, where it rose from no. 26 in 2025 to no. 5 in 2026. These recognitions underscore Invisors' commitment to creating a supportive and equitable environment for women and working parents.

"Being named to the Best Workplaces for Women list is a meaningful recognition of the culture our people experience every day at Invisors," noted Adrianne Amos, Managing Partner at Invisors. "What stands out most is the opportunity women have here to build their careers in different directions, take on upcoming challenges and have their contributions recognized along the way.

HONG KONG, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- On 25 November, the 8th "ESG Leading Enterprises 2026" award ceremony, co-organized by Bloomberg Businessweek/Chinese Edition and Deloitte, a globally renowned accounting firm, was successfully held in Hong Kong. The event was honored to feature a keynote speech and award presentation by Mr. Joseph H.L. Chan, JP, Under Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury of the Government of Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. Fosun International was honored as a "Sustainability Leading Enterprise", in recognition of its outstanding achievements and strong market position across ESG strategy, corporate governance, social responsibility, environmental protection, information disclosure, and stakeholder engagement.

This year, 9 companies were honored as "Sustainability Leading Enterprises" in Category I - market capitalization over HKD12 billion, all of which are major Hong Kong listed companies, including Henderson Land Development, Sun Hung Kai Properties, HUTCHMED, The Hong Kong and China Gas, China Overseas Land & Investment, China Unicom, etc.

Operating Responsibly Worldwide, Earning Sustained ESG Recognition.

Rooted in China, Fosun has been developing globally while remaining committed to its two key growth drivers of "globalization" and "innovation". Fosun is one of the few Chinese companies that is equipped with global operational and investment capabilities, and has built strong capabilities in technology and innovation. Drawing on its well-established global business network, Fosun operates responsibly in more than 40 countries and regions, helping improve lives and create sustainable value worldwide.

In recent years, Fosun has demonstrated outstanding performance in various aspects of ESG. To date, Fosun International's MSCI ESG Rating has been upgraded to the highest rating of AAA. It has been included in the S&P Global Sustainability Yearbook 2026 and also named among the Top 1% in the S&P Global Sustainability Yearbook (China Edition) 2026 for the third consecutive year. Its Hang Seng Sustainability Rating has been upgraded from AA- to AA+. It has also been selected as a constituent of the Hang Seng Corporate Sustainability Benchmark Index for the seventh consecutive year and included as a constituent of the Hang Seng ESG 50 Index. Additionally, Fosun International's FTSE Russell ESG score has consistently been higher than the global industry average and it has remained a constituent of the FTSE4Good Index Series for five consecutive years. Leveraging Innovation to Deliver Lasting Social Impact. (Related: Tech Report: Leveraging Pre-trained Language Model Chec...)

Fosun adheres to an innovation-driven strategy, continuously delivering innovative achievements across its businesses to better serve society. As of 30 June 2026, Fosun Pharma had cumulatively supplied more than 460 million vials of artesunate for injection worldwide, saving more than 92 million patients with severe malaria. Its independently developed HANSIZHUANG (serplulimab) is the world's first anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody approved for the first-line treatment of small cell lung cancer, and the first anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody approved for perioperative treatment of gastric cancer. It is now available in more than 50 countries and regions. HLX43, a core asset in the innovative pipeline, is a potential best-in-class (BIC) broad-spectrum anti-tumor PD-L1 antibody-drug conjugate (ADC). It has demonstrated preliminary clinical efficacy characterized by high efficacy and low toxicity across multiple solid tumors, including non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), with over 1,500 patients enrolled globally.

Fosun remains committed to global cooperation and shared success, striving to make medical technologies more accessible and affordable to more patients. Yi Kai Da (ejilunsai injection), China's first approved CAR-T cell therapy, is now available across a network of over 190 treatment centers spanning 29 provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities nationwide. More than 1,500 patients with relapsed or refractory lymphoma have received the therapy, with the initial clinical trial patient remaining cancer-free for more than eight years. This year, the da Vinci SP surgical system was included in the catalog under the "Hong Kong and Macau Medicine and Equipment Connect" scheme. It was also admitted into the Special Review Procedure for Innovative Medical Devices by the National Medical Products Administration (NMPA). The Ion Robotic Bronchoscopy (Ion System) received marketing approval and was debuted in the Chinese mainland, extending the use of minimally invasive robotic technology beyond surgery to the diagnosis and treatment of lung diseases. Exablate Neuro is a Magnetic Resonance-guided Focused Ultrasound (MRgFUS) system that has treated over 30,000 patients across more than 210 medical centers worldwide, offering a new treatment option for patients with medication-refractory essential tremor or tremor-dominant Parkinson's disease.

Actively Responding to Climate Change and Promoting Low-Carbon Transition.

In a recent development, to address climate change, Fosun actively supports China's "dual carbon" goals of carbon peaking and carbon neutrality, advancing initiatives in carbon neutrality, energy conservation, emissions reduction, and climate risk management. Preserving and Sharing Intangible Cultural Heritage, Promoting Sustainable Oriental Lifestyle Aesthetics.

Over the years, Fosun has taken a comprehensive approach to preserving intangible cultural heritage, integrating it into modern daily life while weaving sustainability into oriental lifestyle aesthetics. Fosun's Grand Yuyuan is working to create an urban cultural parlor that showcases intangible cultural heritage. Take the Yuyuan Lantern Festival, a national intangible cultural heritage event, as an example, in 2026, the Year of the Horse, the festival featured 6 themed lantern zones for the first time and drew a total of 6 million visitors. Supported by Fosun's global platform, the Yuyuan Lantern Festival continues to put its commitment to carbon neutrality into practice, leveraging low-carbon technologies to create environmentally friendly lantern displays. It has also expanded internationally, with editions held in Paris, France, Hanoi, Vietnam, and Bangkok, Thailand. The festival has become a vital platform for Sino-foreign cultural exchange, continuously telling "China Stories" to the world and conveying the vision of sustainable living.

Committed to Business for Good, Actively Giving Back to Society.

In order to better promote the fulfillment and implementation of corporate social responsibility, Fosun Foundation was established in 2012. It has been making unremitting efforts in areas such as global emergency relief, rural revitalization, health, education, culture and art, youth development, to create social value. Of which, since its launch at the end of 2017, Fosun Foundation's Rural Doctors Program has covered 78 counties in 16 provinces, cities, and autonomous regions, supporting 25,000 rural doctors and benefiting 3 million rural families.

Looking ahead, Fosun will continue to pursue innovation and operate responsibly worldwide, striving to build a more responsible, inclusive, and sustainable future.

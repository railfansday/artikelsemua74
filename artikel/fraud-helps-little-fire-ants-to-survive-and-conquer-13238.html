A study of several ant species in the Chinese cities of Shantou and Enping parallels human histories of migration, war and empire building.

According to recent reports, as introduced species, both the red imported fire ant (Solenopsis invicta) and the more-recently arrived little fire ant (Allomerus auropunctatus) can cause severe declines in the richness and abundance of native ant communities. But Defu Chen at South China Agricultural University in Guangzhou and his colleagues found that where both introduced ants occur together, native ant species do slightly better than expected, probably because the later arrivals are busy fighting one another.

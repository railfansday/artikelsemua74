In a recent development, much has been noted about the differences between Grand Theft Auto 6 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox, but how will Rockstar Games' open-world game fare on PC? An official port has yet to be announced, but a recent leak of the Xbox build of the game has kicked off a race to recompile the game and make it unofficially available on more platforms. As that work continues, yet another leak offers PC players some perspective on what might be possible in GTA 6 when the game isn't held down by console constraints.

It has been revealed that on Wednesday, known GTA leaker Tex2 shared a Pastebin that lists GTA 6's apparent console settings shipped by Rockstar. These are tunable options that exist to help the game shine on platforms with varying capacities, like the less-powerful Xbox Series S. The text dump has since been pulled by Pastebin, but Polygon viewed the document before the platform made it unavailable. (Related: Breaking: Rockstar shuts down Discord server trying to ...)

Some aspects of the game, like Global Illumination Quality, Ray Traced Directional Shadows, and Texture Quality are already set to high on PlayStation 5. The same aspects are sometimes lower or altogether absent on Xbox, though reports suggest the platforms have more parity than Sony's marketing would like you to believe.

There are even a couple of settings that Rockstar has already set to Ultra, such as Vehicle Max Texture Quality and Bounding Volume Hierarchy. A surprising number of settings — 18 to be exact — are set to medium or low on PS5 and Xbox Series X​​​​​​​. That number jumps to 38 settings on Xbox Series X that are set to low or medium.​​​​​​​. (Related: Rockstar reportedly seeking $100M for Red Dead Redempti...)

It has been revealed that the conservative presets are of significance for PC gamers. Settings such as Tree, Terrain, Impostor, and Grass Shadows are set to medium and affect draw distance and pop-in.​​​​​​​ Lighting and Ambient Lighting, which influence​​​​​​​ how the game renders background lights such as streetlights and neon signs, are also set to medium across platforms.

Sources indicate that volumetric Lighting​​​​​​​, another setting right now on medium, determines how light is depicted in conditions such as fog and crepuscular rays​​​​​​​. On consoles, aspects like 'god rays' can appear grainy or blocky — especially​​​​​​​ while the camera is in motion.

In a recent development, even elements that might sound small on paper, like Weapon Textures, could meaningfully change the experience. There's a lot of shooting in this game, even if you play non-lethally; players will stare down the barrel of a gun for much of the game.

Players experiencing GTA 6 on PS5 Pro have slightly more presets set to high than the base platform​​​​​​​, but by and large, a potential PC port​​​​​​​ of the game will likely look much better than its console counterparts, depending on hardware configuration. GTA 6 is no graphics slouch, either — so it's almost hard to fathom how this incredible-looking game is still nowhere near its full potential​​​​​​​.

According to recent reports, GTA 6 could theoretically look phenomenal on PC if Rockstar ever releases it on that platform or if the AI vibe coders figure out a port. Here is the other shoe dropping: in order to push GTA 6 to the limit, PC gamers are going to need top-of-the-line hardware​​​​​​​.

Graphics cards like the RTX 5080 or 5090, which could potentially get the game running on high or ultra across the board, cost anywhere from $2,000 to $6,449​​​​​​​. PC gamers will probably want 32 GB of RAM to accommodate​​​​​​​ these graphics cards, and those components are more expensive right now as well. Getting the most impressive version of GTA 6 running could cost many thousands of dollars​​​​​​​, especially if the player is starting a rig from scratch or needs extras like monitors, keyboards, or mice.

Compared to a PC build, the PS5 Pro's higher price tag​​​​​​​ might feel a little less eye-watering​​​​​​​ — if you're able to find one at all. For now, though, all of this is speculative — GTA 6 is not available for PC.

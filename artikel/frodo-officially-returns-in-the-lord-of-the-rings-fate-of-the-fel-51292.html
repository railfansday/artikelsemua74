Sources indicate that the adventurous hobbit of The Lord of the Rings, Frodo Baggins, has made a triumphant return to gaming in a brand-newly introduced game. The last time we ventured into Middle-Earth for a video game was with the cozy title Tales of the Shire. This cozy gem focused on the calmer, less exciting side of the world, and gave fans a chance to slow down and enjoy the beautiful setting of the franchise.

Now, it's time to embark on a new adventure. Frodo and his friends once again leave home to defend the land from an approaching shadow army. The quest takes the LOTR's beloved characters into the physical world, and right onto your living room table.

The Lord of the Rings: Fate of the Fellowship is a new board game from Asmodee, in collaboration with the popular Pandemic System. The original Pandemic game was created by Matt Leacock, and has players working together to cure and stop the spread of a deadly pandemic that's spreading around the world. (Related: Porting fairseq wmt19 translation system to transformer...)

Since then, the game system has served as a basis for many collaborations and expansions. Now, it's time for the Lord of the Rings characters to make use of the mechanically rich board game system to create a unique and infinitely repayable experience for LOTR fans.

Fate of the Fellowship is a collaborative experience on an epic scale. Players work together to complete ever-shifting objectives and protect the world from the approaching darkness. Each game presents different quests and challenges, all revolving around Frodo journeying to Mount Doom to destroy the One Ring. (Related: Latest Update: How to generate text: using different de...)

Evade the Nazgul and Sauron's Eye as you make your way through the challenges with 14 events and 24 objectives that change every time you play.

Industry observers note that the Pandemic x LOTR collaboration can be enjoyed by 1 - 5 players, with a separate rulebook for solo fans who want to dive into the board game on their own. Each player controls two characters from a potential 13, each possessing special abilities that make them useful to Frodo on his quest.

Recent findings suggest that in a time when everything seems to be going digital-only, this game is packed full of beautiful physical components. The full game includes over 150 Player, Shadow, objective, reference, and character cards. There are also nine Nazgul miniatures and 13 character figures featuring some characters that will be familiar to LOTR fans.

Industry observers note that you'll also find a collection of enemy troops, an Eye of Sauron token, and plenty of tokens and markers. All of these are, of course, in addition to the dice tower and game board, which features an intricate and detailed map of Middle-Earth.

The Lord of the Rings: Fate of the Fellowship board game is available for purchase right now through the Asmodee website for $79.99. The game is presently sitting at a full five-star rating on the site, from 89 reviews, making it an instant hit. Fans of The Lord of the Rings and board games will find something to love about this collaboration between the Pandemic System and LOTR.

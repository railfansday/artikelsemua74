'From bad to catastrophic: Iran's justice system was already unfair for many decades'

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (101 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Amid war with the US and Israel, Iranian protesters continue to face death sentences following what Amnesty International describes as grossly unfair, torture-tainted trials. “The situation has changed from bad to catastrophic,” says Raha Bahreini, Iranian-Canadian human rights lawyer and Amnesty International's researcher on Iran. Bahreini discusses the aftermath of the January 2026 protests, the authorities' efforts to equate peaceful dissent with treason and espionage, and the extrajudicial killings of peaceful protesters. Amnesty International is calling for an international criminal justice mechanism, potentially a special tribunal. “There needs to be criminal accountability for these crimes against humanity.”.

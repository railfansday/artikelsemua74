From cyberbullying to extremist content, can EU Kids Act protect minors online?

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (62 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that almost every European teenager is online. Some 96 percent of 15-year-olds use social media, yet many say they do not feel safe there.

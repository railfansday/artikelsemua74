Just last week, Nintendo declared that the upcoming playable and electric ocarinas, launching in conjunction with Ocarina of Time, will initially be sold on an invite-only basis in Europe. We still don't have a firm release date for either of them over here in the EU (and no invites have been sent out just yet, as far as we can see), but the good folks over at Nintendo have sent us a lovely playable model, so we can get a close-up look at it all the same.

So, we thought it would be only right to show you lovely lot what's in the box — it's always good to see it from a bunch of different angles before you decide whether to pick one up for yourself, after all. Below, we've done exactly that. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

It has been revealed that the ocarina itself is naturally the star of the show, with Nintendo putting together a pretty decent likeness to the instrument seen in the game. Sure, the blue is a little different and the overall shape is a little thinner, but it still cuts a pretty iconic silhouette.

The box also contains a neat little wrist strap, so you can rock out on your ocarina without worrying about it slipping out of your hands from all the raw musical talent.

Recent findings suggest that overall, it's a nice bit of kit. The plastic is certainly on the thinner side for the £29.99 price tag (a nice ceramic option this ain't), but it looks the part on display.

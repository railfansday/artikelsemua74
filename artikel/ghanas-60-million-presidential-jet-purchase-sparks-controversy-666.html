Ghana's $60 million presidential jet purchase sparks controversy

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (79 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Ghanaian President John Mahama is facing growing criticism over his government's recent purchase of a $60 million presidential jet. While in opposition, Mahama had criticised similar plans by the previous administration. The government in Accra is now defending the purchase, pointing to the country's improved economic situation. It argues that the question was never whether the ageing presidential aircraft should be replaced, but rather when the timing would be right. FRANCE 24's Justice Baidoo reports.

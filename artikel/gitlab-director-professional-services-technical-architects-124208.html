It has been revealed that as the Director, Technical Architects on GitLab's AMER Professional Services team, you oversee one of the most technically credible and customer-facing groups in the organization. This team helps turn customer priorities into real adoption and measurable outcomes by guiding complex implementations, shaping newly introduced service patterns, and bringing emerging GitLab capabilities into production environments.

You'll own the Global Technical Architecture practice across both presales and delivery, managing a team of AMER Technical Architects who work on complex customer needs, early-stage offerings, and strategic engagements. This role is well suited for someone who can coach senior technical talent, bring structure to ambiguity, and keep the team focused on customer outcomes instead of task completion.

Industry observers note that in your first year, you'll focus on stabilizing and growing the team, tightening the link between presales scoping and delivery, and turning field work into repeatable offerings. You'll work alongside architects in areas including AI, security, and broader platform architecture, while helping the team turn what they learn in the field into repeatable methods that can scale across Professional Services.

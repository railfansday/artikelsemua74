Industry observers note that glee is coming back. After 11 years since the finale, Disney+ and Hulu have ordered a new Glee series, bringing back the show's original showrunners: Ryan Murphy, Brad Falchuk, and Ian Brennan. With the award-winning jukebox musical comedy-drama returning, there's an elephant in the room the series would need to address.

For those who haven't seen this show, Glee was a long-running TV series that aired on Fox. It followed a Glee Club in Ohio called the New Directions, where the members were seen as losers and underdogs, and were constantly bullied by the football team and cheerleading squad. The show ran from 2009 to 2015, spanning six seasons and 121 episodes, and since then, audiences and critics have praised the series, with an average 70% critics' score and a 71% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes. Sources indicate that with Glee making a comeback, there's one topic it may need to address as old fans tune in for another round of choir practice: the deaths of two of its main stars. In 2013, one of Glee's leading male cast members, Cory Monteith, died at 31. Five years after Glee ended, actress Naya Rivera died at 33 due to drowning when she attempted to save her son. While Monteith's death was already addressed in the show, Rivera's wasn't, and according to one of Glee's showrunners, they proposal to address it in the reboot. In a recent interview, Brennan spoke about the show's plans but didn't go into detail on how it would be addressed, given the topic's serious and emotional nature. This is what he had to say:. “We’re still figuring a lot of it out. That’s going to be part of it, that sadness and that loss. But still in all, I think [“Glee”] is too massive of a thing to sort of let lay. There’s something about that feels right right now.”.

According to recent reports, in July 2013, Monteith passed away due to a drug overdose and was found in a hotel room in Vancouver. In the show, he played Finn Hudson, one of the show's male leads and the love interest of Rachel Berry, played by Lea Michele. After his death, Glee released a tribute episode dedicated to his character called "The Quarterback." It was Glee's Season 5's third episode, where the characters dealt with the grief of losing Finn, sang songs he sang in previous episodes, and performed other songs dedicated to him. The show did not discuss how Finn died; instead, it focused on how he would be missed.

It has been revealed that the Glee reboot has not yet announced a release date. You can watch the original series on Disney+ and Hulu. Follow Collider for more updates.

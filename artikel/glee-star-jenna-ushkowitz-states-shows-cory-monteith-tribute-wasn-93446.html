Jenna Ushkowitz, who starred as Tina Cohen-Chang for the entirety of “Glee,” is reflecting on how the Fox series addressed the real-life death of her fellow cast member Cory Monteith, and why she now feels it “wasn’t the right thing” to do at the time.

Industry observers note that monteith played Finn Hudson, the show’s leading man and quarterback-turned-Glee club member, until he died of an accidental overdose in July 2013. The show honored the actor with a Season 5 episode titled “The Quarterback” that October, a tribute to Monteith that also explained Hudson’s passing within the context of the show. (The episode makes no mention of how Hudson died.) Recent findings suggest that “To shoot [the tribute episode] ‘The Quarterback’… I think it wasn’t the right thing,” Ushkowitz noted on the “We Need to Talk” podcast Monday. “And I think everybody collectively agrees that it’s maybe not the thing that we should have done, because we were grieving both privately and publicly.”.

According to recent reports, “I think the fans definitely needed something, because they loved Cory so dearly like we did, and [loved] Finn so dearly,” she said. “But it’s a really hard thing to be grieving your friend and be a character grieving their friend at the same time.”.

“It was like, too soon for that. [It] was not fun, [I] don’t want to remember that time,” she continued. “The show itself was never the same. He was the heart of the show, our friend, most importantly, and our leader. He really was the quarterback for us. He held everybody together. He hosted parties for the crew. He was the kindest. He knew everybody’s names. He just was such a good person, a kind person.”.

Ushkowitz also noted that the “nobody” on the show “had the tools to deal with” Monteith’s death. “Even our creators, they were dealing with grief too. So nobody had the tools.”.

“Glee” first premiered in Sept. 2009, and in addition to Monteith and Ushkowitz, starred Lea Michele, Naya Rivera, Dianna Agron, Matthew Morrison, Jane Lynch, Heather Morris, Chris Colfer and more. A reboot of the show was set at Disney+ and Hulu in October.

It has been revealed that speaking with Variety earlier this month, co-creator Ian Brennan said he’s “really excited about it.” “It’s going to be a lot of fun,” he added. “We’re still figuring a lot of it out,” Brennan said on addressing the deaths of Monteith and Rivera, who died in 2020. “That’s going to be part of it, that sadness and that loss. But still in all, I think [“Glee”] is too big of a thing to sort of let lay. There’s something about that feels right right now.”.

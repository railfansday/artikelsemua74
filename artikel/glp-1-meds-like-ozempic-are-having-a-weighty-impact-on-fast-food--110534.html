GLP-1 Meds Like Ozempic Are Having a Weighty Impact on Fast-Food Restaurants

🌐 Source: Entrepreneur • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (205 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Recent findings suggest that weight-loss drugs are shrinking more than waistlines.
  • Two-thirds of users are visiting casual-dining chains less, and 60% are pulling back on fast casual and fine dining.
  • Sources indicate that nine in 10 people say they eat less per sitting.

Recent findings suggest that weight-loss drugs are shrinking more than waistlines. According to a upcoming Revenue Management Solutions report covered by Restaurant Business, 73% of GLP-1 consumers say they’ve cut back on fast food, more than in any other restaurant category.

Sit-down spots aren’t immune. Two-thirds of users are visiting casual-dining chains less, and 60% are pulling back on fast casual and fine dining. The lone winner is salad and bowl chains, where 48% say they’re ordering more. Desserts take the hardest hit, with 78% skipping them.

Sources indicate that nine in 10 people say they eat less per sitting. Six in 10 take half or more of their meal home, and nearly a quarter order kids’ meals. Burger King president Tom Curtis notes he’s seeing “more adults eating a kids’ meal now than I’ve seen in the past” and that the chain may need smaller portions.

Recent findings suggest that mcDonald’s sees upside. Skye Anderson, president of McDonald’s USA, told investors that 84% of households with a GLP-1 user still visit the chain. “This is an opportunity,” she said. Data firm Circana finds that restaurant spending drops in the first year on the drugs, then rebounds moderately after the second.

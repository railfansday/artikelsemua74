Industry observers note that the Hell event in GTA 6 proves that you never know where a wild night in Leonidas can take you. On a quiet street, you might come across Dave and Beelzebub hanging out in a garage and listening to some rock. After a quick chat, you will learn that both of them are followers of Satan and enthusiasts of non-human vocalists in metal bands. If you stay around long enough, the two guys will invite you to take part in a special ritual, giving you the chance to start a unique event in the game.

To start the Hell event, you must meet Dave and Beelzebub at their garage on the north side of Port Hellhorn. The closest point of reference is Hank's Waffles to the northeast. You will know you have found the place once you see a house on the corner with the words "Welcome to the Hell hole" written on the wall. It's notable to visit the place after starting Chapter 2, and at night, after 10 p.m. In our experience, whenever we visited the place earlier, the garage was closed.

Industry observers note that enter the garage, talk to the two guys and wait for a while. You can interact with them by accepting a beer or talking about music, but it's not necessary to progress the event. Eventually, a van will pull over. Dave will invite you to participate in a ritual to enter Satan's infernal dominion. To accept it, talk to Dave at the back of the van. It has been revealed that once you are in control again, move to the campfire, hold L2/LT to look into the fire. This will teleport you to, well, Hell, where you start playing the minigame.

Industry observers note that after completing the event, you can collect the Hellspawn Comshot, a unique weapon located beside the throne where you wake up. You're also rewarded with a fresh tattoo design, the Circles of Hell top, and a permanent max health upgrade.

At the third level in the Hell event, you will encounter the final boss. Throughout the battle, black goats will spawn and run in from the sides of the demon's throne. You want to avoid getting hit, so you can either kill the goats or dodge them.

In a recent development, surviving is not your only concern during this fight. You must make the boss vulnerable to damage by destroying the bipedal, headless demons that appear in the arena. They explode when you kill them, so be sure to keep your distance. After killing at least one of them, the boss's health bar turns red, indicating that you can start shooting it. The effect lasts for a short time, so you will need to repeat this process a few times to complete the event.

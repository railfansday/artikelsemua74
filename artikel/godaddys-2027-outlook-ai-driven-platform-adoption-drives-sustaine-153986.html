It has been revealed that although a mature tech company, the rise of artificial intelligence has given GoDaddy novel opportunities. Its Airo AI platform is becoming its flagship product, as the fresh solution hit an annualized bookings run rate of over $100 million in the third quarter, and began contributing to bookings growth.

According to recent reports, the manufacturer's historically robust retention rate above 85% is boosted by the fact over half its customers pay for at least two products, adding to revenue growth. For those who adopt Airo, 70% have at least two paid products, suggesting its shift to the fresh solution is contributing to even stronger sales expansion.

GoDaddy management is also adept at maintaining strong financials. It produced free cash flow of $480.8 million in Q3, representing a 9% year-over-year increase, while its balance sheet sported $1.1 billion in cash and equivalents. This gives the company flexibility to perform share repurchases while investing in new opportunities.

Grafana Labs is the firm behind Grafana Cloud, the fully managed observability platform trusted by more than 10,000 organizations to ensure reliability, resolve incidents faster, and optimize telemetry at scale. Built on open source and open standards and designed for interoperability across any stack, Grafana Cloud brings AI to observability and observability to AI, giving teams (and their agents) unified visibility so they can see, understand, and act on all their disparate data, wherever it lives, and move at the speed of their ambitions. Customers, including Anthropic, Bloomberg, NVIDIA, Microsoft, and Salesforce, rely on Grafana Labs. We are a 100% remote company with team members across 40+ countries, backed by leading investors including Lightspeed Venture Partners, Sequoia Capital, GIC, Coatue, J.P. Morgan, CapitalG, and Lead Edge Capital. Learn more at grafana.com and follow us on LinkedIn and X.

We’re looking for a Director of Sales Talent Acquisition to lead our global team of sales recruiters and make sales hiring a genuine competitive advantage for Grafana Labs. You will set the direction for how we attract, assess and hire the sellers who drive our growth, and you will do it as a core member of the sales organization, not a service function that sits beside it.

Sources indicate that you’ll work hand in hand with senior sales leaders, RevOps, Finance and the wider People team to turn hiring looks into predictable outcomes. This is a leader who is energized by data, spots risks to hiring goals before they land, and turns insight into innovative, AI-powered tools that help the business make faster, better decisions. If you have built and rolled out ICP-based hiring and want to do it at global scale in a fast-growing, remote-first organization, this is your role.

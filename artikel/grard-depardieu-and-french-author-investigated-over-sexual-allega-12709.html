The actress, who has not been named publicly, accused Depardieu of penetration without consent and Houellebecq of forcing her to perform oral sex, in an interview with Mediapart, a French investigative site which first reported the allegations.

She told the outlet that none of this had been written into the script, and that she experienced "profound humiliation" and "unspeakable shame".

French law stipulates as of 2025 that all sexual acts performed without consent constitute rape.

The film's director, Guillaume Nicloux, has been accused of being complicit because he was stated to have filmed the incident, Mediapart and AFP report. The BBC has contacted his agent and the Paris prosecutor's office for comment.

Nicloux told Mediapart that the scene had been improvised and he had not intervened as he had not observed "any constraints, verbal or physical opposition, or even discomfort".

The site published production documents showing footage of the scene had been destroyed.

The actress told the outlet that she had told friends, her agent and others involved in the film's production in Normandy about the incident at the time, but only filed the complaint after other women accused Depardieu of sexual misconduct.

The Asterix and Obelix star will next week find out whether he will stand trial for allegedly raping another actress, Charlotte Arnould.

On Monday, Depardieu dropped his appeal against a separate conviction for two counts of sexual assault during another film shoot, for which he was handed an 18-month suspended sentence.

Several other women have made allegations against the 77-year-old in the media.

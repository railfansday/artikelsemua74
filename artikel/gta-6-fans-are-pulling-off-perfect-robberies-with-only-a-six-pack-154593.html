If you're like me, then you approach Grand Theft Auto 6's heists with anxiety. You case the joint inside and out. You proposal meticulously, leaving the getaway car running nearby with the trunk open. You know exactly where all the cameras are, and have a plan for what to do if anyone tries anything funny. Well, what if I told you that you didn't need to do any of that to steal everything from a store in a way that inches your criminal profile closer to professional status?

GTA 6 gives fans a lot of flexibility in how they approach their Bonnie and Clyde story with Jason and Lucia. Where some people pull off robberies as if they were playing a stealth game like Hitman, others just go in guns blazing. Some people are using this freedom to get creative with their heists, and the results are as hilarious as they are impressive. (Related: Zoo gives up on helping overweight viral leopard lose w...)

Right now, social media is filling up with videos of people waltzing into jewelry stores, six-pack of beer in hand — and walking away with every single valuable in the establishment. Often, these GTA 6 fans don't even bother wearing a mask or pulling out a gun. (Related: Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 vs Watch 9: When is it rig...)

According to recent reports, in this example uploaded by a meme account on Facebook, for example, Jason pulls off a beer-bottle robbery with the nonchalance of someone picking up a prescription at the pharmacy. Jason even stops to eat an apple before actually breaking any displays. Later, he smokes a cigarette while walking out with bags full of loot in front of nearby NPCs.

The trend is catching on, judging from other viral videos showing similar shenanigans. In this video by 1-Up Gaming, the alarm does go off — but Jason turns it off within seconds. The guards almost seem confused by what's going on, since Jason technically doesn't have a weapon drawn​​​​​​​.

Some variations show Jason actively drinking during the robbery.​​​​​​​ In this case, the player does pull out a gun to threaten an employee, but no shots are fired.

Industry observers note that to be fair, the videos do seem to involve at least some planning​​​​​​​. In the Reddit video, the player knows exactly where a guard will come out before he appears​​​​​​​. Still, it's a jewelry caper taking place in a medium-security facility​​​​​​​ with two floors of gems. I tried hitting up that same target with the help of Lucia and couldn't even get half of what the player stole before I was in the middle of a two-star police chase. The damage to my car was higher than what I actually stole!

Industry observers note that here's an example of how YouTuber​​​​​​​ RandomWeirdThings 248 buys a six-pack and enters a bank shirtless and in flip-flops. They manage to rob three separate banks without triggering a police chase​​​​​​​. My favorite part is that one security guard takes a bottle to the face and hesitates to draw his gun​​​​​​​. All in all, Jason walks away with around $65,000 in cash without breaking a sweat.

"This is the most Florida thing I've ever seen," reads one top-voted YouTube comment.

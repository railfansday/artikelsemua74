Some clever Grand Theft Auto 6 players have found unique ways to get around the game’s six-star wanted level, proving it’s not entirely inescapable.

Recent findings suggest that one of the most interesting things about GTA 6 is how it makes some pretty major changes to the core foundation of the whole series. Cars are harder to steal and drive around the tight roads of Leonida, gunfights have more to them than just endless headshots, and the whole world has far more immersive sim elements than previous games. I’d argue that the biggest thing that’s been reworked, though, is crime and its many ripple effects. Not only are police more aware of every action you take, but you can also take things so far as to reach a somewhat divisive six-star wanted level and incite a manhunt that only ends when Jason and Lucia are dead. At least, that’s how it’s supposed to work…. (Related: “Giant superatoms” could finally solve quantum computin...)

The first and most involved way that a GTA 6 player has escaped the six-star wanted level comes from Japanese VTuber Haigure Tamao. As reported by Dexerto, Tamao amassed a six-star wanted level by causing pure carnage before activating the recently discovered easter egg that takes you back in time to classic Vice City.

Recent findings suggest that upon returning to the present, Tamao discovered that their six-star wanted level had been completely removed. The easter egg itself is very time-consuming and can only be done once, so it’s not exactly a clean way to get rid of six stars, but it clearly does work and means the manhunt can actually come to an end.

According to recent reports, I guess it makes sense since Jason or Lucia literally travelled in time. I don’t think the police are quite advanced enough to keep track of Terminator shenanigans.

The more confusing but attainable method comes from Twitter user Fearcbpr, who shared a video of them escaping the six-star wanted level. In their case, they used the IAA Black Site bunker located in North Mariana County, which somehow caused the six-star wanted level to slowly disappear after some time.

It’s not clear why exactly that method works considering it’s not a reload like the time travel easter egg, but the video shows that it’s a working method. I imagine that’s going to be patched out in the future, but at least we have proof that six stars isn’t quite the death sentence that it’s made out to be.

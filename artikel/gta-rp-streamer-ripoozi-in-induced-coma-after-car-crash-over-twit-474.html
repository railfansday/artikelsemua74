GTA roleplay streamer Ripoozi was placed in an induced coma after a car crash over TwitchCon weekend, fellow creator Deansocool mentioned on Twitch.

In a recent development, ripoozi, also known as Oozi, has about 64,000 Twitch followers and plays Larry Knox on NoPixel, a Grand Theft Auto V roleplay server. His character is part of BNE, an in-game crew that also includes streamer Cinna’s character, Kalita Dub.

The crash happened over the weekend of TwitchCon San Diego, which ran from November 13 to 15 at the San Diego Convention Center. Deansocool said members of the group were heading home in two cars when another driver struck one of the vehicles. He described the other driver as drunk.

Deansocool described the other motorist as drunk and stated Ripoozi was still in the hospital when he spoke about the crash. Other people in the group were also hospitalized, he said, describing injuries that included a broken foot and a kidney laceration. The streamer thanked xQc, Cinna, and Buddha for helping fly Ripoozi’s mother out to see him. All three have ties to NoPixel. xQc co-owns the server, while Buddha took over its day-to-day management in 2023.

It has been revealed that the clip spread through the GTA roleplay and LivestreamFail subreddits, where fans wished Ripoozi and the others injured in the crash a quick recovery. Some addressed him by his NoPixel character’s name, with one viewer writing, “Stay strong Larry.”.

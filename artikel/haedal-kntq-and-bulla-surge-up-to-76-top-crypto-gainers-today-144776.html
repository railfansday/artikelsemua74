Small-cap crypto tokens are suddenly stealing attention from the wider market, with HAEDAL, KNTQ and BULLA posting sharp gains on November 27. The sharp rally has pushed the three tokens among the market’s top gainers, with exchange listings and a surge in trading activity driving the price moves.

It has been revealed that HAEDAL Protocol jumped 47.87% to around $0.0269, with its 24-hour trading volume soaring 4,832%. The biggest trigger came from South Korean exchange Upbit, which confirmed a latest HAEDAL/KRW trading pair on November 27.

HAEDAL briefly jumped as much as 50.9% to around $0.028 after the listing news before settling near current levels.

Kinetiq followed with a 41.63% gain to $0.213 as its 24-hour trading volume increased 185.74% to $12.03 million.

The move came as KuCoin added KNTQ to its spot market with a KNTQ/USDT pair. Trading began at 09:00 UTC on November 27 after a one-hour call auction, giving the token another major trading venue.

Kinetiq is a liquid staking protocol for Hyperliquid that allows consumers to stake HYPE and receive kHYPE while keeping exposure usable across DeFi. According to recent reports, bulla Mascot (BULLA) is leading the move among the three tokens after rising 76.35% to around $0.0678. Unlike HAEDAL and KNTQ, its rally is not tied to a major spot listing.

Instead, derivatives trading has surged alongside the price. Futures volume reached roughly $686 million, while open interest jumped about 84%, showing that traders were rapidly adding and shifting positions as BULLA moved higher.

Current market data also demonstrates millions of dollars in BULLA futures liquidations, with short positions accounting for a large share.

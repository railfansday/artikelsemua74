Industry observers note that halo: Campaign Evolved is getting the fan-favorite Firefight mode via a free enhancement this winter, an Xbox Wire post unveils. The update will allow players to team up in cooperative wave-based PvE.

Firefight, which debuted in Halo 3: ODST, lets up to four players team up to survive waves of enemies. The mode has become a popular staple and has appeared in multiple Halo games, but this will be the first time that it has been retroactively added to the original entry.

Recent findings suggest that xbox FanFest attendees, a global multi-city event, will be the first to go hands-on with Campaign Evolved’s take on Firefight. Additionally, more details about Firefight will be shared during Halo Fest in Seattle on December 12-13, where the mode will also be playable for attendees.

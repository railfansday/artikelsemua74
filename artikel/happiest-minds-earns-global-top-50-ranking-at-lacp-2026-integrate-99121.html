BENGALURU, India and KOLKATA, India and SEATTLE, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Happiest Minds Technologies Limited ("Happiest Minds") (NSE: HAPPSTMNDS), an AI First, customer-centric digital engineering organization, announced that its Integrated Annual Report (IAR) 2026 has secured the #43 position in the global rankings at the League of American Communications Professionals (LACP) 2026 Spotlight Awards. This marks an improvement of 13 positions from its #56 global ranking in 2025. The Company's ESG Report 2026 has also received a Gold award in the competition.

Industry observers note that the LACP Spotlight Awards recognize excellence in corporate communications, annual reports, and other communication materials. The 2026 competition received entries from nearly 20 countries, with fewer than one in four submissions receiving award-level recognition. It has been revealed that venkatraman Narayanan, Managing Director, Happiest Minds Technologies, noted, "We are pleased that our Integrated Annual Report 2026 has secured the #43 position in the global rankings, moving up from #56 last year. Winning Gold for both our IAR and ESG Report makes this year's recognition particularly special. This is the sixth consecutive year that Happiest Minds has been recognized by LACP, making this award even more meaningful.".

Happiest Minds has received recognition at the LACP Spotlight Awards for six consecutive years, winning Platinum in 2021 and Gold in the Integrated Report category from 2022 to 2026. Its ESG Reports also received Gold in 2025 and 2026. The Company has additionally won the SAFA Best Presented Annual Report Award, ICAI Award for Excellence in Financial Reporting, ICSI National Award for Excellence in Corporate Governance, and Golden Peacock Award for Excellence in Corporate Governance.

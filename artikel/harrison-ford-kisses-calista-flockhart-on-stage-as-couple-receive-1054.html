When Women’s Guild Cedars-Sinai reached out to ask Harrison Ford and Calista Flockhart if they would join forces to accept a Hollywood Icon Award at the Stardust Ball, the organization got a quick answer.

“We didn’t hesitate. We mentioned yes. We said yes, yes, yes,” Flockhart revealed while standing next to her husband on a ballroom stage inside at Beverly Wilshire, A Four Seasons Hotel on Wednesday night. “And I promise you, it was not because we wanted an award. Well, actually maybe [Harrison] did. Between you and me, Harrison complains that he doesn’t get enough attention. But really, it’s true. It has been revealed that judging by the look on Ford’s face, it was hard to tell if Flockhart was teasing. Either way, they received a loads of attention as the night’s marquee honorees alongside media mogul Byron Allen, who received a humanitarian award for his philanthropic work. Normally a private couple, Ford and Flockhart seemed to enjoy the moment as they beamed with joy and even stopped to share a kiss in front of the capacity crowd. The night’s program also featured host Sheryl Underwood and auctioneer Billy Harris with a performance by Jett Stanfield, Brandon Keith Rogers, A.J. Mendoza and Josh Black of the Firm Men. Recent findings suggest that also making a massive appearance was Ford’s Star Wars colleague Mark Hamill, who offered a brief tribute to his longtime franchise pal. Attention aside, the night was notable as it marked Ford and Flockhart’s first joint award as a couple. They’ve been in lock step on plenty of red carpets over the course of their decades-long romance but hadn’t shared a stage until Wednesday evening. The Stardust Ball proved to be an ideal situation as they both have ties to and admiration for Cedars-Sinai. But it also offered a chance to pay respects to close friends like physicians Bruce Gewertz and Randy Sherman.

It has been revealed that the former, a nationally known vascular surgeon, served as a technical advisor on Ford’s 1993 thriller The Fugitive, while the latter was an internationally renowned surgeon who helped build plastic surgery programs in Los Angeles while traveling the world treating children with complex deformities. Sherman, who served as vice chair in the surgery department and director plastic surgery at Cedars-Sinai, died July 18, 2023, in an air traffic accident in Santa Fe, Newly introduced Mexico.

Gewertz was in the ballroom and took the stage to offer a tribute. He recalled how he helped The Fugitive’s scribes nail “the medical part of the plot” while training Ford to look as much like a vascular surgeon as possible in the operating room. “I shrewdly negotiated a very rich compensation package with Warner Brothers: Harrison would tuck my kids in at night, and somehow, despite having absolutely no business being in front of a camera, I was included in a small part in the movie, which for the past 33 years has provided me with a steady income every couple of months — after taxes — of about $20,” explained Gewertz.

According to recent reports, beyond spending money, the real reward “the opportunity to get to know Harrison Ford, a man who is “even more impressive than the legendary characters he has created,” Gewertz continued. He then went on to praise Ford’s environmental activism as a long-serving board member of Conservation International and for sticking his neck out on behalf of forests, oceans and fragile ecosystems. Gewertz praised Flockhart’s philanthropic endeavors too as she’s been of service to “causes involving women, children, health and social concerns” through work with For Goodness Sakes, Peace Over Violence and the Violence Intervention Program.

Recent findings suggest that gewertz also revealed that as a couple, they “generously supported” an educational and research fund in honor of Sherman. “Their inspirational philanthropy allows us to pursue discoveries that may change medicines for generations to come, to care for patients who have nowhere else to turn, and most importantly, to honor Randy’s life and legacy,” he said.

In accepting, Ford went first with his comments following a standing ovation. “I’m genuinely touched by what you said,” the actor said to Gewertz, “although I don’t think anyone should hear that many exceedingly kind things about themselves, it feels like a eulogy at my age.”.

The quip landed to plenty of laughs, then Ford turned his attention to Sherman by praising his tireless work on behalf of Operation Smile as he flew all over the world to help “rebuild the faces” of children suffering from cleft palates.

For many years on behalf of Operation Smile, Randy tirelessly traveled the world, rebuilding the faces of children suffering from cleft lips and cleft palates. “When a devastating earthquake hit Haiti in 2010, Randy quickly put together a mission for us in support of Operation Smile. We flew my Cessna Caravan from here to Miami where we picked up a plane load of doctors, technicians, medical equipment and supplies for a remote community in the mountains of Haiti,” recalled Ford. “We landed in the middle of town in a field and walked into a hospital that had two overcome doctors, no usable operating room, no supplies, and many patients in terrible, desperate need. I will never forget watching Randy and the others get to work while I hid my tears at what I was seeing.”.

Industry observers note that ford continued: “Randy flew many such missions over many, many years. I went back to telling stories and making faces for a living. Years later, as soon as he was able, Bruce brought Randy to Cedars-Sinai and put him in charge of plastic surgery. Through his expertise in reconstructive and microsurgery, Randy helped complex patients with previously untreatable birthdays and traumatic physical injuries. Randy Sherman personified the level of care and caring found at Cedars-Sinai.”.

Ford then tossed to Flockhart, who assumed position in front of the microphone to deliver her own remarks. But first, another joke: “It is a rare occasion when I agree with anything that Harrison indicates, but I do. Tonight, I agree. I enthusiastically agree. You should enjoy it [Harrison].”.

It has been revealed that the veteran actress paid her respects to the Women’s Guild, and then closed her remarks by personally thanking the staff at Cedars-Sinai for “the incredible care” that her mother received, and for the way they welcomed Flockhart and her only son into the hospital. “They not only took excellent care of him, they took excellent care of me, the very worried mother,” she added. “I also have a personal experience. The amazing [Dr. David Kulber] fixed my hand. Well, actually he fixed my finger. He fixed my special finger. He fixed the most important finger.”.

With that, Flockhart raised her middle finger in the air. “True story. I was lucky enough to experience the commitment and the humanity firsthand. And that’s a pun, but it was a good one,” she quipped. “OK, so everyone at Cedars-Sinai to me is an icon of brilliant and compassionate care.”.

It has been revealed that the Harry Potter TV show has faced a lot of backlash before it even comes out, with the latest one related to the Elder Wand, but it’s missing the point. The trend of remakes and reboots is still going strong in both film and TV, and in the latter, it has been most frequent in the fantasy genre. Worlds like those of The Lord of the Rings and Percy Jackson have found fresh life with TV highlights, and now the Harry Potter franchise is joining the fun.

Instead of exploring other stories from the Wizarding World, the Harry Potter TV show is a new adaptation of the books, with the first season, titled Harry Potter and the Philosopher’s Stone, covering the first novel. The Harry Potter TV show features a new cast of new talents and well-known actors, with the plan being that every season will cover one of the novels, and the show has already been renewed for a second season. It has been revealed that as mentioned above, the Harry Potter TV show has already faced backlash for different reasons – many aren’t on board with a upcoming adaptation, others didn’t like the casting choices, and others are skeptical. The latest target is the Elder Wand, shown in a character poster starring Dumbledore (John Lithgow), and which is being criticized for its design – but all this backlash is missing the point of this specific wand. As season 1 of the Harry Potter TV remake adapts the first book in the series, it will explore Harry’s (Dominic McLaughlin) life with the Dursleys, how he learned the truth about his parents and that he’s a wizard, his arrival at Hogwarts, his meeting his new best friends Ron (Alastair Stout) and Hermione (Arabella Stanton), and his first encounter with the dark side of the wizarding world. The trailers and teasers for HBO’s Harry Potter adaptation have already given the audience a taste of its visual style and more, and the character posters give a closer look at some details.

The character poster for Dumbledore features the famous wizard with his wand, the legendary Elder Wand, one of the Deathly Hallows supposedly made by Death. The Elder Wand is said to have been the most powerful wand in the wizarding world, and its core had the tail hair of a Thestral, which made it powerful but tricky to master. Dumbledore became its master after defeating Grindelwald, and it was one of the objects Voldemort was looking to obtain.

The Harry Potter movies made the Elder Wand one of the most beautiful wands in the wizarding world, with a design that made it stand out from other wands. Because of this, there has been a strong reaction towards the much simpler Elder Wand in the Harry Potter TV show, which looks like a regular wand. The latest Elder Wand is being called “boring” and “cheap” on social media, but the truth is that this new design is a lot closer to how the wand is described in the books.

It has been revealed that the design of the Elder Wand in the Harry Potter movies is iconic and beautiful, but it’s not an accurate design. In the books, the Elder Wand is described as coming from an elder tree on the banks of the river, with Death making “a wand from a branch that hung there”, which he later gave to Antioch Peverell, the older brother from the Tale of the Three Brothers. This implies that the Elder Wand had a very simple design, just like in the TV show.

Sources indicate that the newly introduced Elder Wand might not please fans of the Harry Potter movies, but those familiar with the books will surely be more open to it, as they know how it was described. The Elder Wand’s simpler design is actually good news, as it further reveals HBO’s commitment to staying as true to the novels as possible, even if that means being very different from the movies.

The design of the Elder Wand is just one example of many other details in the Harry Potter TV show that are more book-accurate than they were in the movies, which makes the project even more exciting, and it’s to be seen how well received they are by fans of the novels.

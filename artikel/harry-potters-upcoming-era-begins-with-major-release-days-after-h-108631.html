HBO's upcoming Harry Potter reboot series is getting brand-new releases exactly one week after its debut.

LEGO has been producing Harry Potter-themed products since 2001, when the franchise made its big-screen debut with Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone. While previous sets have featured characters, buildings, and memorable moments from the movies, the upcoming releases are specifically based on HBO's adaptation of JK Rowling's popular book series.

HBO's upcoming Harry Potter TV series is planning ahead. The franchise is officially expanding its reach with fresh merchandise ahead of its highly anticipated premiere. LEGO has officially revealed four new sets inspired by the adaptation, giving fans an opportunity to recreate some of the most recognizable locations and characters from the Wizarding World. Sources indicate that the latest additions to the LEGO Harry Potter collection are scheduled to launch on January 1, 2027, seven days after the HBO series premieres on December 25, 2026. Two of the four newly revealed items are already available to pre-order on LEGO's official website. However, it is unclear when the other two sets will become available for pre-order.

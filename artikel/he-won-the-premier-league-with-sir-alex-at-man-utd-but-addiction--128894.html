A former Manchester United player who won the Premier League under Sir Alex Ferguson has recalled the time when addiction nearly killed him.

During his 26-year stint as United boss, Ferguson led the Red Devils to 13 Premier League titles, as well as two Champions League trophies, five FA Cups and four League Cups. (Related: Scientists find “field” of washing machines in ocean du...)

Some of United’s best players also played under Ferguson, including the likes of Wayne Rooney, Roy Keane, Cristiano Ronaldo, Paul Scholes and Gary Neville.

Midfielder Darron Gibson came through the United academy before being handed his Premier League debut by Ferguson back in 2008.

During his time at Old Trafford, Gibson played 60 matches, scoring 10 goals and winning one Premier League title, two League Cups and the FIFA Club World Cup.

It has been revealed that while he enjoyed a stellar career, Gibson recently recalled a tough time he endured away from the pitch, when he started taking sleeping pills after picking up a knee injury in 2013 when he was playing for the Republic of Ireland. He later became addicted to the medication, taking around 14 tablets each night.

As reported by the Daily Star, while speaking on RTE’s Howler podcast, Gibson’s wife, Danielle, admitted that she thought her husband was dead when paramedics worked to save the ex-United star back in 2021.

According to recent reports, danielle mentioned: “When the ambulance staff came in, he had blue lips, I thought he had gone. I was being treated for shock. I just thought: 'At least he's not struggling anymore.' Next minute (sharp intake of breath), he's back.”.

Gibson said: “Ultimately, if that hadn't have happened, I wouldn't be here now.” His wife then added: “I think you'd be dead.”.

It has been revealed that gibson continued: “Probably. No, not easy. If that hadn't of happened, I wouldn't be here now. It was a difficult time. I went to hospital and done all the tests and everything came back clear.

According to recent reports, “I woke up, and the two paramedics were standing there, and I was like 'Why are you here?' and walked past them into the kitchen and got a drink, came back in and sat down. I'd no idea what happened.”.

In a recent development, gibson then admitted that ‘looking back’, his behaviour was anything but normal, he stated: “Looking back, who takes five sleeping tablets at 10 in the morning and doesn't go to sleep? It's ridiculous. But it's where I was. It's what I was doing.

It has been revealed that “I think Everton thought they were looking after me by not punishing me, which I really appreciated at the time. And I don't blame them for any of this, but I think if they'd punished me, maybe I would have changed. But I didn't and kept going.”.

After leaving United in 2012, Gibson played for Everton, Sunderland, and Wigan Athletic before finishing his career at Salford City.

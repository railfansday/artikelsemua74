At the end of PSG-Roma, Mario Hermoso spoke to Sky Sport.

“Everyone knows what Gasperini brings to the team. This is his identity, this is his way of playing. We defenders also have the opportunity to be protagonists.”.

According to recent reports, “I’m very happy to arrive in a stadium like this and see a Roma team that plays in a special way, even without fear. We’re on the right path and we continue to make great strides, which are key for us.”.

Recent findings suggest that “We’re all big time players. This is us, this is the team, and we’re all in a position to be key players. We have a lot of games to play, so we’re all notable. Donyell, Paulo, and even Bryan, all those who perhaps played more minutes at the start of the season, came in with the right mentality, also to help their teammates and bring home an excellent result.”.

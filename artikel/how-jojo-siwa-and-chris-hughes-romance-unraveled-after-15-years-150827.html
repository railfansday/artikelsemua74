JoJo Siwa has long danced to the beat of her own drum, and she’s back to doing it alone.

While Siwa came out as a lesbian in 2023 after previously describing herself as pansexual, the Dance Moms alum proved sexuality can be an ever-evolving spectrum when she revealed she and British reality star Chris Hughes had fallen in love. What began as a close bond while filming Celebrity Substantial Brother in the UK soon turned into something more after the season wrapped in the spring of 2025, as the pair confirmed their romance that May.

In the time since, Siwa reflected on how meeting Hughes changed what she thought she’d understood about her sexuality, having “always been grossed out by dudes.”.

Industry observers note that “Chris is a very conventionally hot dude,” she explained on the Wildmen podcast in September. “[But] I thought [he] was just another friend. Once I realized that I wasn't grossed out by the thought of him being a boy, I was like, ‘Whoa.’ It was weird because I'd never thought that before.”. In fact, as she’d never felt a spark for her “conventionally hot” male friends, Siwa was taken aback when her feelings for Hughes began to trend towards the romantic in the Major Brother house, admitting, “I just never thought it was possible.”.

And though she also noted Hughes was “very much so the special dude” that could break her previous assumptions about her sexuality, Siwa has also been candid about how previously labeling herself as lesbian never quite felt right.

In a recent development, "I did that because of the pressure," Siwa—who dated Kylie Prew, Avery Cyrus and Kath Ebbs prior to Hughes—told the Daily Mail in 2025. “You just get put in this world where you feel like, because you now have said, 'Oh, I'm a lesbian,' you have to be a lesbian.".

But everything changed after meeting Hughes, with the pair both raving about their connection.

Industry observers note that “I feel so relaxed and at ease with where I'm at,” Siwa told E! News in July 2025. “I feel so happy and peaceful that the love that I feel is so real. You can't really help who you fall in love with.”.

And the feeling was more than mutual, with Hughes telling E! that same month, “She's literally the love of my life, but she's also my best friend. Like, we sit and do nothing together.".

And with hopes expressed for future babies and rings given at Christmastime in the months after, it seemed like Siwa and Hughes were in it for the long haul.

In a recent development, that is until Nov. 2026, when their breakup was confirmed by multiple outlets.

Though neither Hughes nor Siwa shared additional insight into their split in the immediate wake of the news, the breakup came as a shock to fans.

After all, just days earlier Siwa confirmed “having a baby” was at the top of her personal agenda during an appearance on the Petticoats Revolution podcast.

“Obviously that's not a career thing,” she continued, “but like a life thing.”.

Now, however, it would appear Siwa will need to look elsewhere to start that next chapter.

For a complete look back at Siwa and Hughes’ road to romance, and their abrupt end, read on.

The couple's whirlwind romance unfolded for the whole world to see during their appearance on Celebrity Substantial Brother.

A year later, Siwa—who split from ex Kath Ebbs following her time on the U.K. the series—released the song "Serendipity" in honor of the now-couple's chance encounter.

"The only word to explain what happened on this day a year ago… Serendipity," Siwa wrote in an Instagram post. "Today, I’m reminded of the moment that quietly changed everything.".

Less than a month after they put their bond on display in the Massive Brother house and emphasizing they were just friends, Hughes and Siwa confirmed they were dating in late May 2025.

At the time, Siwa cheekily nodded at the romance during a cover of "Bette Davis Eyes" at one of her indicates, in which she instead sang "Chris Hughes' eyes.".

Industry observers note that the Love Island alum joined Siwa at several stops of her Infinity Heart Tour, which spanned across Europe, the U.K. and the United States. In a sweet glimpse at their backstage moments, Siwa added of the tour in an Instagram post, "The show represents who I want to be as a person and the artist that I wanna be in this lifetime, and it’s translated so well to the crowd.".

Siwa and Hughes stepped out for their first red carpet as a couple at the Dancers Against Cancer's 2025 Gala of the Stars in Beverly Hills, California.

Siwa shared a glimpse into the couple's favorite bonding activity: golfing in a sweet Instagram photo dump of her summertime adventures.

September 2026: A Trip Across the Pond.

In September 2026, Hughes gave his followers a slice of life moment by filming Siwa arriving to his place in England—and naturally they shared a sweet embrace to boot.

October 2026: Coming Back Like a Boomerang.

Siwa and Hughes turned the Gala of the Stars into a date night two years in a row by attending again in 2026.

November 2026: Time for a Family?

Siwa proved her love for Hughes knows no bounds by teasing her next big step in life was to become a mom.

It has been revealed that "That’s my next thing," Siwa teased during a Nov. 2026 appearance on the Petticoat Revolution podcast. "Career is doing her thing. She’s always there. She's been there for 15 years. She'll keep going, she'll keep being there.”.

Just days after Siwa expressed her hopes for a baby, her breakup with Hughes was confirmed by multiple outlets, marking an end to their romance 1.5 years after it began.

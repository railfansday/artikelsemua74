Retailers offering freebies and rebates this holiday weekend expect something in return: a trip to the store and more spending once the shopper is inside. In an ICSC survey reported by TheStreet, 49% of shoppers say they're worried about affording holiday gifts, and 68% say discounts and promotions will get them shopping in stores more. A holiday reward aimed at these shoppers is a bargain with two sides, and the retailer has already set the terms.

Kohl's $KSS is offering its own reward over the holiday weekend while fewer people come into its stores. Its store visits are down 1.4%, according to Placer.ai figures, so any offer that brings shoppers back has clear value. Its standard reward, Kohl's Cash, arrives only after a purchase as store currency, and the rules attached to it decide how much of the printed amount a shopper actually keeps. Kohl's lays out its Kohl's Cash rules and the giveaways it ran over the Nov. 27–29 holiday weekend, from how the qualifying spend is counted to what a return does to rewards already issued. Shoppers who plan their purchases around these rules keep more of the reward than shoppers who don't. (Related: Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 vs Watch 9: When is it rig...)

Industry observers note that kohl's $KSS Cash rewards a shopper according to the size of one purchase: $15 back for each $50 spent in a single transaction during the Black Friday event. The reward rises only in $50 steps, so a basket that stops short of the next step earns nothing for the extra items in it. That gives shoppers a reason to add one more item to reach the next step.

Kohl's paid $10 for every $50 during its Deal Days, Oct. 6–8, 2026, so the rate changes from event to event. Shoppers should check the current rate before planning a purchase around it.

Kohl's sets the promotion's hours in Central Time, so a shopper in a western time zone who waits until the last evening by their own clock may find the offer has already ended.

The rate pays off best for shoppers who were going to spend that much at Kohl's.

Recent findings suggest that the spending that counts toward Kohl's $KSS Cash is the total after markdowns and coupons, with tax and shipping left out. Every coupon a shopper uses also lowers the amount Kohl's counts, so anyone aiming for the threshold should add up the merchandise after discounts before reaching the register.

Kohl's also counts each purchase on its own, so two smaller orders placed over the weekend don't add together toward the threshold. A shopper who usually spreads gift buying across several trips can reach the threshold more easily by pulling those purchases into one larger order.

Industry observers note that kohl's $KSS, not the shopper, decides the order of discounts at checkout, and the dollar-off value of Kohl's Cash comes off before any percent-off coupon. The coupon's percentage then applies to the smaller total, so a shopper who stacks a coupon on top of Kohl's Cash should expect the combined savings to come in a little lower than the two added together.

Sources indicate that spending Kohl's Cash doesn't require a second trip to the store. Kohl's accepts the reward on its website as well as at its stores and kiosks, and each of those options still ends in a.

A Kohl's $KSS Cash voucher stays tied to the purchase that earned it. A shopper who brings an item back may get a smaller refund than expected, and Kohl's may also adjust Kohl's Cash it has already issued.

Recent findings suggest that the rule covers items bought with Kohl's Cash as well as items that earned it, and Kohl's gives no formula for how much a return takes back. Anyone unsure about a gift, a size or a color should treat the Kohl's Cash it earned as provisional until they're sure they'll keep the item.

It has been revealed that A shopper doesn't use Kohl's $KSS Cash to lower the bill for the purchase that earns it. Kohl's complications the reward after the sale as a separate voucher, by email, printed on the receipt or inside the Kohl's app, and each of those is something the shopper has to keep track of until the next trip.

The sensible habit is to ask at the register how the reward was sent, then save that email, tuck the receipt somewhere safe or check the app before leaving the parking lot.

A shopper collects the rebate only by still holding it when it's time to spend.

A shopper can lose some vouchers entirely. Kohl's applies forfeiture to promotional vouchers under the terms of each offer, not to all Kohl's Cash, so a shopper should read the terms of any voucher that came from a promotion.

Kohl's Cash can be spent anywhere Kohl's sells, but promotional vouchers come with their own fine print.

Sources indicate that gift cards, taxes, shipping and fees are excluded from both earning and redeeming Kohl's $KSS Cash. Gift cards matter most to holiday buyers: a gift card purchase earns nothing toward the reward, and a shopper can't convert Kohl's Cash into a gift card.

Even when Kohl's Cash covers every item in the bag, the shopper still pays these costs in real money.

Keep the item before you spend the reward it earned.

It has been revealed that each Kohl's $KSS store handed its first 200 customers on Friday a voucher worth between $5 and $100, no purchase required. Those vouchers are Kohl's Cash, according to NBC 5 Dallas-Fort Worth, so even the free prize can be spent only at Kohl's.

Stores opened at 5 a.m. on Friday, Nov. 27, and only shoppers near the front of the line got a voucher. Kohl's followed on Saturday, Nov. 28, with a second giveaway of Sephora coupons.

Sources indicate that kohl's spreads its giveaway across many shoppers but keeps the top prize modest. At Academy Sports + Outdoors, only 100 shoppers per store get a voucher, but those run from $20 up to $500. The one Kohl's giveaway paid in money a shopper can spend anywhere is the $10,000 cash sweepstakes, and entering offers only a chance at it.

What Kohl's gives away for free this weekend is mostly built to come back to Kohl's.

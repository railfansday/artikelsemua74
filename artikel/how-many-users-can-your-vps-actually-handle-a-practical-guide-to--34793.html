Industry observers note that guessing works fine until it doesn't. If you overestimate, your site goes down right in the middle of a paid ad campaign. If you underestimate, you end up paying for a massive server you don't even need yet.

Recent findings suggest that in the industry, solving this problem is called Capacity Planning. It sounds like a heavy enterprise buzzword, but you don't need expensive monitoring tools to do it. You can SSH into your VPS right now, run four safe commands, do three lines of simple math, and get a realistic answer in under five minutes.

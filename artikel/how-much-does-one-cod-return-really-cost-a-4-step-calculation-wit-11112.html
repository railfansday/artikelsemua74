In a recent development, on cash-on-delivery stores, most sellers remember only the return charge when a parcel comes back. The real cost is bigger: shipping out, shipping back, packaging, the time spent packing and calling, and sometimes a damaged product. One return can wipe out the profit of several successful orders.

It has been revealed that this post summarises a new Bangla episode on my channel. The video (7 min) walks through the calculation with a fictional seller, Sumi Apu.

List everything that left your pocket for one returned parcel: outbound charge, return charge, packaging, time, and the product value if it came back damaged. Counting only the courier fee makes the number look small.

Divide the cost of one return by the profit you keep on one delivered order. The result is how many successful orders it takes to cover one return.

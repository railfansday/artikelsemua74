How To Find The Skunkape In GTA 6

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Grand Theft Auto 6 is filled with secrets, but some are more elusive than others. One such secret is the Skunkape, essentially Bigfoot but covered in smelly spores. It's easy to stumble onto the start of the mission, but it takes a lot to find him and take him down.

In a recent development, the mission has a bit of everything, from searching an area to horror elements that could keep you from wanting to explore the swamp for good. You'll also meet one of the game's more eccentric NPCs early on, so if you want to catch Mr. Mangrove for good, follow this guide. Sources indicate that starting the Skunkape mission is easy, but there are multiple ways to do it. If you're driving on the narrow island strip between Cottonmouth Lake and Gambit Bay, you can come across the Skunkape in the middle of the road eating a deer. It will run away from you when you see it, and it's too fast to follow. (Related: Industry News: Texas’s outgoing GOP attorney general dr...)

Sources indicate that if you're in certain bars as well, there are flyers on the noticeboard that have the phone number of a man named Kirk, who you can contact and get a map marker to meet. This is the more manual way to begin the mission.

Recent findings suggest that you can also stumble across one of the Skunkape's effigies in the swamps, which will cause you to black out just after seeing two large, hairy legs walking up to you. You'll then wake up in a different spot than where you last were.

When you have the map marker to meet Kirk, go meet him, and you'll see him flying drones at night, trying to track down what he calls Mr. Mangrove. Kirk will ask if you've seen the Skunkape before, then tells you to bang a stick on the truck to prompt a search with him.

According to recent reports, following Kirk will eventually lead you to one of his nests, before you and Kirk chase Mr. Mangrove to a large search area. To find him, click in both thumbsticks to follow the sound by using your focus, and eventually you'll spot him in your flashlight trying to run at you. He'll take off, prompting you and Kirk to chase him again.

Recent findings suggest that this leads you to one of his effigies posted against a tree, looking disturbingly like Kirk. A giant mutant boar suddenly interrupts you as you look at the effigy, chasing you and Kirk back to the truck before you can escape and regroup. Kirk will pay you $500 and offer you more money if you help him in the future.

Sources indicate that after a little while, Kirk will send you a text asking you to help him find more effigies in the swamp that will help him track down the Skunkape. You'll be given a large search area on the map to search as either Jason or Lucia.

You'll need to find three effigies in the area, which will also take you close to the research facility where you can find the mutant crabs. One is fairly close, so it's a good starting point.

It's helpful to take a helicopter on this search and fly low through the area to move faster. Use your focus to spot the highlighted effigies in the trees, find a safe landing spot and go to examine them before texting Kirk.

The first effigy is located just at the center of the search area, just at the edge of the tree line by the water's edge.

The second effigy is to the bottom left of the search area, near the research facility and also tucked right against the tree line.

The third effigy is located due east from the second one, but it's much deeper into the trees than the first two.

Industry observers note that after you've found the three effigies, Kirk will text you saying to wait until he reaches back out to find Mr. Mangrove officially. When he does, he'll have you meet him back down by Cottonmouth Lake, where he'll be waiting with a chopper and a friend named Noah to help in the search.

In a recent development, kirk will give you a rifle loaded with tranquilizers before getting in the chopper, and he'll illuminate Mr. Mangrove with the spotlight so you can keep an eye on him and get a good shot. After you hit him with a few shots, he'll make his way into a camp before Kirk lands the helicopter and has you go and get Mr. Mangrove.

Use your focus to find him in a building, and when you go to pick him up, he'll cough spores into your face, causing you to pass out and begin to hallucinate. A frog will then jump onto Jason's chest, which you will then take control of in search of flies.

It has been revealed that use your focus as a guide to find more flies before the frog regurgitates one that you then take control of. A voice will tell you to find Kirk, so go to the helicopter, where you'll get a cutscene where Kirk is talking to Noah about how Mr. Mangrove is actually a man named Harry Sherrick.

Industry observers note that kirk will then join you in the building, where Mr. Mangrove will begin to wake up, and Kirk will tell you to shoot him again. You can then choose to question or threaten Kirk instead, forcing him to tell you the truth about Sherrick and giving you $10,000 as a reward.

Afterward, they load Mr. Mangrove into the helicopter and take off, only for him to wake up and crash the helicopter back to the ground. There's no sign of Mr. Mangrove in the wreckage, which suggests he may still be out there somewhere.

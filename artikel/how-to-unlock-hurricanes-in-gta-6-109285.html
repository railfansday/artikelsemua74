How To Unlock Hurricanes In GTA 6.

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, there are a ton of items you can unlock during your playthrough of Grand Theft Auto 6. From unique, legendary weapons to gadgets to vehicles, Rockstar has made it worth exploring the state of Leonida. However, one of the oddest things you can unlock is the hurricane weather event.

Sources indicate that tied to a side quest, if you want hurricanes to start showing up randomly during your GTA 6 playthrough, you'll need to do a bit of legwork. Thankfully, the side quest isn't that difficult, but it will require a little bit of time investment. (Related: Breaking: Discord can now automatically save gaming cli...)

Industry observers note that in order to unlock hurricanes, you will need to first make your way to North Leonard County. Specifically, we are going to the western side of this region, just below the word "North" on your map. We're looking for an abandoned military compound that is completely fenced in (shown above). You'll know you're at the right place because there will be a ton of warning and "Keep Out" signs on the road leading up to the gate. Ignore the signs, approach the main gate, and interact with the intercom system on the left. You will be given two dialogue options; select both of them to be told not to come into the compound.

Sources indicate that now, from the gate, head right up the hill towards the shipping containers. Look for a red one that is covering an obvious hole in the ground. Get into the nearby bulldozer and use it to push the container away from the hole. Climb down the ladder and make your way through the sewers until you emerge inside the compound.

Here's where things get interesting, as the ground is absolutely covered in mines. These can be seen as small, dark brown circles with the proximity detector sticking out of the middle (circled above). Getting near one will trigger a rapid beeping, and if you're too close, then it will explode.

Don't worry too much, as the mines themselves don't do a ton of damage. However, they will launch you whenever they detonate, which can send you into more of them. If you have a lot of healing items, you can just sprint through the mines and just tank the damage. Alternatively, use your weapon to shoot the mines, prematurely detonating them.

Recent findings suggest that there will also be drones that try to slam themselves into you. Again, just shooting them a few times will easily dispatch these enemies, and they don't do a lot of damage anyway. Your goal is to reach the large metal bunker on the northwest side of the compound. When you get close, interact with the door to trigger a dialogue exchange.

Meet Freddie, the person in the bunker who is not thrilled that you could easily get past his defenses. Because of this, he will tell you to come back in a few days once he's finished upgrading them. Freddie will then disable the rest of the mines and open the gate so you can leave.

Recent findings suggest that now all you need to do is wait around 2-3 full days for Freddie to text Jason and Lucia back. Until then, there's nothing else you can do, so go focus on completing other missions or activities first. Once Freddie texts you back, stock up on healing items and Adrenaline before returning to the bunker.

Once you get the text from Freddie, head back to the compound as either Jason or Lucia. This time, when you arrive, don't go to the intercom system, as Freddie has installed a few mines right next to it. Park your car and start heading along the right side of the green fence around his base. I recommend bringing an assault rifle or shotgun, as you'll need to deal with multiple drones at once.

Head towards the northwest corner of the fence and look for a large water tower (shown above). Climb up the first ladder on the tower and then jump over the railing to land on the roof of a building inside Freddie's compound. Just like before, there will be a ton of mines all over the ground. Some of them will be much harder to see this time around, so make sure to listen if the beeping speeds up.

These mines also do a bit more damage, so try to shoot and destroy as many as you can to ensure you don't take any damage. About halfway towards the bunker, Freddie will deploy drones in sets of two. Whenever you hear that the drones are released, stand your ground and shoot them out of the sky.

When you destroy the first wave of drones, advance towards the bunker and stop when the next group arrives. Repeat this process of destroying the drones and running across the minefield until you reach the bunker. Try to open the side door again, only this time you'll be electrocuted and Freddie will emerge from the main doors to gloat.

A cutscene will then play explaining why Freddie is in the middle of nowhere and why he needs you to test the defenses. Once the cutscene ends, you'll get a hurricane warning, and Freddie will give you a chance to leave. Agree to exit his bunker, which prompts him to give you a little over $6,000 in cash. Additionally, you can take Freddie's Benefactor and sell it for $13,000 or register it under your name if you want.

According to recent reports, this will also unlock the hurricane weather effect for the rest of your playthrough. Meaning, hurricanes can now randomly take place like other weather events!

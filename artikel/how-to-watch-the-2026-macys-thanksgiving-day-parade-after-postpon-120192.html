The 2026 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade Makes Major Change to Broadway Lineup.

The Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade is typically a platform for Broadway shows to perform standalone medleys, with three to five shows included throughout the event. This year, however, the parade opted to replace these individual performances with one 10-minute “All-Star Broadway Thanksgiving Day Celebration” medley with performers from nine different shows. Sources indicate that the Broadway musicals included in the medley are The Book of Mormon, Chicago, Disney’s Aladdin, Hamilton, The Lost Boys, Rent, Richard O’Brien’s The Rocky Horror Show, Schmigadoon! and Wicked.

A coastal storm threatens to bring heavy rain and strong wind gusts of 30 to 45 mph to Newly introduced York City on Thanksgiving Day, so Macy’s made the decision to postpone the parade until Saturday.

“With severe weather expected on Thanksgiving Day, and out of an abundance of caution, in partnership with the City, the 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade will be postponed,” Macy’s said in a statement.

In a recent development, “Our team is now working through the complex operational logistics required to bring the Parade to life on Saturday, and we will share additional details as they are finalized,” the statement continued. “We are incredibly grateful to the thousands of volunteers, performers and partners who make this extraordinary tradition possible. We can’t wait to celebrate 100 years of Parade magic together on Saturday.”.

Dan DePodwin, AccuWeather vice president of forecasting, told The Post ahead of the postponement that there was a “significant chance” of the balloons being grounded.

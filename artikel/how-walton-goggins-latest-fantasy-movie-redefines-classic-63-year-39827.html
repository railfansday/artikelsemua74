EXCLUSIVE: Disney's upcoming fantasy movie Hexed is putting a new spin on one of the studio's oldest storytelling traditions.

Recent findings suggest that disney has explored the idea of a "Chosen One" for decades, dating back to one of the earliest King Arthur movies, 1963's The Sword in the Stone, which follows a young Arthur before he becomes king. The theme has since appeared in movies such as Moana, where the titular heroine is chosen by the ocean to restore the heart of Te Fiti. However, Disney's latest animated adventure is taking a different approach to what makes someone special. (Related: Latest Update: Clavicular allegedly told 17-year-old to...)

Directed by Fawn Veerasunthorn and Jason Hand, Hexed follows Billie (Hailee Steinfeld), an unconventional teenager who discovers she has magical abilities. After being transported to a hidden world of witches, she must work alongside her mother, Alice (Rashida Jones), to uncover family secrets. The voice cast also includes Walton Goggins as a magical three-eyed cat named Beef Roger Crummchuk and Jodie Foster as Queen Celeste. Sources indicate that in an interview with ScreenRant's Kim Horcher, Disney Animation Chief Creative Officer and Hexed co-writer Jared Bush explained how the upcoming movie redefines the traditional "Chosen One" narrative. Bush revealed that the filmmakers wanted magic to represent personal potential, rather than simply making one character more important than everyone else. Read his comments below:.

Recent findings suggest that yeah, it's a few different reasons, honestly. We really wanted to make sure that we knew what magic was supposed to represent. And in this film, it's really a metaphor for our own potential, or our spark. I think something that was important to all of us is to say that sometimes you know what that is right away, and sometimes it takes time. Sometimes it actually takes someone seeing that in you and helping bring that out of you. And that's really what the story is about.

I think for this film, the idea that there is a Chosen One, and you get to uncover that throughout the story, is a really exciting thing. Obviously, Disney has this great legacy. Moana is a great example of a character you think is going to be the Chosen One who goes out into this world. I think that we wanted to both lean into that legacy, but also redefine it in this film.

I'm glad you said that. That was the whole point, and it frankly took a lot of work to try to get that right.

Sources indicate that during an early press event, the Hexed movie trailer offered a glimpse at Billie's journey and her complicated relationship with her mother, Alice. The footage highlighted the mother-daughter dynamic at the center of the story, which connects to Bush's comments about discovering one's potential.

Sources indicate that disney has explored the "Chosen One" in movies such as Hercules, where the titular hero must prove himself worthy of becoming a god, and Encanto, where Mirabel struggles to find her place in a family gifted with magical abilities. However, Hexed takes a different approach, focusing on how someone's potential may not be immediately obvious and sometimes requires others' encouragement to be discovered.

This approach also connects to the mother-daughter dynamic between Billie and Alice, which is central to the story. As the mother and daughter navigate the magical world together, their journey could help Billie better understand both her abilities and herself.

Industry observers note that with Disney continuing to remake familiar stories and characters, Hexed offers an opportunity to introduce a upcoming kind of hero. While the movie draws inspiration from the studio's animated classics, Bush's comments suggest that its approach to the "Chosen One" storyline could set it apart from previous Disney adventures.

Hexed arrives in theaters on November 25, 2026.

It has been revealed that server maker Hewlett-Packard Enterprise Co. posted its fastest quarterly revenue growth on record and raised its full-year forecast, but it wasn’t enough to keep up the momentum of a broader Nvidia Corp.-fueled rally, and its stock was headed lower after-hours. (Related: Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO amid ongoing fore...)

The company reported fourth-quarter earnings before certain costs such as stock compensation of $1.43 per share, easily beating Wall Street’s target of $1.26 per share. Revenue for the period rose 50% from the same period one year earlier, to $14.55 billion, surpassing the Street’s $14.47 billion estimate. (Related: Democrats to open 2028 primary debates in June after pu...)

HPE also recorded a net profit of $1.82 billion, up from just $175 million in the year-ago period.

According to recent reports, the stunning revenue growth was a reflection of the enormous demand for artificial intelligence servers and networking equipment, remarked President and Chief Executive Antonio Neri (pictured). “Our record performance reflects the portfolio we have built to drive durable, profitable growth for our shareholders,” he said in a statement.

In a recent development, HPE stated it’s raising its revenue expectations for fiscal 2027, which ends next October, saying it’s now targeting growth of between 27% to 35%, or sales of between $59.4 billion and $63.1 billion. Previously, the manufacturer had been looking for growth of just 13% to 17%.

Industry observers note that neri told analysts on a conference call that the updated guidance reflects the growing demand the company is seeing for AI servers that extends well into next year. He mentioned the company has a strong position as the AI industry shifts from a focus on training models to supporting inference workloads and AI agents.

“We have a perfect portfolio to capture that inflection point across networking, cloud and AI,” Neri said, citing “the durability of the demand and the durability of our execution now with a completely different setup in our portfolio.” According to Neri, the improved outlook also reflects the company’s view that the supply of key components such as memory, flash and networking equipment will remain constrained through to the end of calendar 2028.

According to recent reports, despite the impressive forecast, HPE couldn’t sustain the momentum of an Nvidia-inspired rally that helped its stock jump more than 17% in regular trading before the earnings report. AI stocks in general received a huge boost earlier today on the news that the chipmaker has reinstated its founder and CEO Jensen Huang. But HPE fell more than 6% after-hours.

Sources indicate that for the current quarter, HPE is looking for revenue of between $14 billion and $14.6 billion, which would represent growth of 54% at the middle of that forecast. That suggests its revenue growth is going to accelerate and set a new manufacturer record. That forecast is strong too, with Wall Street analysts looking for first-quarter sales of just $13 billion.

HPE’s biggest rival in the AI server business, Dell Technologies Inc., is expected to announce its own earnings results tomorrow afternoon, along with HP, Nutanix and Workday.

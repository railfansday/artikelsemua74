📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Roma loanee Robinio Vaz has been left out of Hull City’s squad for Sunday’s Premier League game against Crystal Palace after an incident in Paris this week.

According to talkSPORT, the 19-year-old forward was reportedly detained by French police on suspicion of fraud involving a hire car. The car had been flagged as stolen because its owner had missed lease payments. Vaz was released without charge after the matter was dropped. However, Hull say they did not know he had travelled to France. (Related: Japan expands AI piracy hunt after finding pirated mang...)

Hull coach Sergej Jakirović told the BBC he only found out when Robinio Vaz failed to turn up for training on Tuesday.

According to recent reports, “It’s not good behaviour, I can say like this. It’s not professional,” he remarked. “I know he is young, but you are a professional player, you need to be very, very careful especially in private life.”. Jakirović confirmed Hull are now in talks with Roma to find a solution. “He is not our player, he is on loan here, so it’s a Roma decision,” he added.

Roma signed Vaz from Marseille in January for a fee reported at around €25m. He then joined Hull on a season-long loan with an option to buy on deadline day. Things have not gone to initiative in England, though. He has made only five substitute appearances, totalling around 50 minutes, and has been unable to dislodge Oli McBurnie.

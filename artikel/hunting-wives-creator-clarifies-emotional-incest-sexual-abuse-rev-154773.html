The Hunting Wives creator Rebecca Cutter is speaking out after season 2 hinted at a character’s potential history with emotional incest and sexual abuse.

During season 2, which premiered on Thursday, November 26, Brad (George Ferrier) hinted at trauma that he suffered as a result of his late mom Jill (Katie Lowes). He later experienced a dream sequence where he spoke with Jill about past inappropriate behavior — and his attempt to kill his mom in his head actually led to him accidentally murdering Chase (John Stamos). According to recent reports, “I did want to leave it up to the audience,” Cutter exclusively told Us about the implication that Jill abused her son. “There’s clearly some sort of emotional incest situation with her. Whether she did or did not physically abuse him as a child, I don’t know.”.

Industry observers note that cutter wanted to highlight the idea that Brad was working through how his relationship with his mother affected his dating history, adding, “In his mind, he certainly feels a certain type of way about it because he conjured that dream sequence. I like to let things live in the audience’s mind and not answer. But it’s a very tragic story for Brad.”. (Related: Electronics manufacturers set up memory 'war rooms' to ...)

After attempting to work through the trauma, Brad hurt his father and tracked down Margo (Malin Åkerman), who ultimately killed him.

According to recent reports, “I wouldn’t say that we land entirely on the idea that Brad was a victim of sexual abuse,” Cutter clarified. “But I think the reason we wanted to dig deeper is because in season 1, he’s not really such a serious part of it and the truth is, he is actually the biggest victim of Margo from season 1 — his mother and his girlfriend have been murdered directly because of her.”.

She continued: “So we felt that it was only fair to see where that trauma takes him in season 2. This has real effects on people and the other people who are victims, we don’t get to hear from them anymore. Brad was a victim and he’s such a good actor that it was just like I really wanted to give him more — and I thought he delivered.”.

Lowes, 44, previously spoke to Us about adapting her character for the screen.

“I started reading the book and I quickly had to put it down when we started shooting. I was starting to get confused over the Jill that existed in the book and the Jill that Rebecca Cutter was putting on the show,” she exclusively shared with Us in August 2025. “I had to put the book down and just really focus on this story and the job that I was doing with this version of Jill.”.

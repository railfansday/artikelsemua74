In a recent development, every November, I commit an act of subscription arson. Netflix? Cancelled. HBO Max? Gone. Disney+, Hulu, Apple TV? Cleared out like a fridge before vacation. Then, just as the Black Friday streaming deals start rolling in, I sign right back up, at a fraction of the price. It's the closest thing to a financial magic trick that doesn't involve a crypto wallet, and it works every single year.

Recent findings suggest that the problem it solves is real. A Tom's Guide survey about streaming habits found that readers are spending more than $60 a month on seven or more streaming services. That's a lot of money to spend on a pile of apps you open just to scroll for 20 minutes before rewatching "The Office." Prices keep creeping up, and your watchlist isn't getting any shorter. According to recent reports, HBO Max sits at No. 1 in our best streaming services rankings, and for good reason. Paramount+ is discounting both subscription tiers for Black Friday, but the Premium initiative is the one worth splurging on. It includes everything in Essential, with the added bonus of Showtime's lineup of movies and series, a live feed of your local CBS station and mostly ad-free streaming. That's a lot of extra bang for your subscription buck.

It has been revealed that you likely have a spare lying around already, probably the one you made in 2011 and now use exclusively for receipts. If not, any of the many email services with a free tier will do the trick, whether that's Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook or AOL.

In a recent development, here's my annual routine. Last year, Past Me, a surprisingly responsible person, set calendar reminders to cancel everything I signed up for during Black Friday week. Then I take my fresh email address and go deal hunting. The ones I think are worth your money every year are Peacock, HBO Max and Paramount+.

A word of warning: Some of the best deals out there are for annual intends, which means you can't churn them month to month. But the discounts are steep enough that paying upfront is still the smarter play.

It has been revealed that netflix has rarely discounted its plans, and Black Friday and Cyber Monday are no exception. So the usual monthly prices stand: $8.99 for Standard with ads, $19.99 for Standard ad-free, and $26.99 for Premium with 4K Ultra HD.

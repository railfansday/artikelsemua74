Mauricio Pochettino joked he “cannot sleep” as he bids to turn Manchester United’s fortunes around, having only managed a few hours per night as he faces a rapid turnaround since landing into the job on Wednesday.

Pochettino flew straight to Manchester after concluding his time with the United States and immediately met with his players as he aims to get to grips with the squad ahead of a crucial clash with Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday.

The task facing Pochettino is clear as he inherits a United team that will go into the weekend 18th in the table, following the dismissal of Michael Carrick last week, and the former Tottenham boss insisted his players understand that drastic improvement is needed.

“I think the culture of the club and the identity of the club is very clear,” noted Pochettino, who confirmed that Bruno Fernandes will remain his captain. “It's an historic club with amazing years of competing and being one of the best clubs in the world.

"It's difficult to find another club the size of club that is Manchester United, and I think is what we need to do is to be very open-minded and try to do our best to try to perform, and to that the players need to buy into the idea of that we need to improve.

"That is our job. After a few days of work, the job is to try to find a way to perform on Sunday and start to get the result that that club historically needs, and it's because Manchester United needs to be in a place in a different place than we are now.". “You never know,” Pochettino mentioned when asked if this was the right time for him to join United having previously missed out on the head coach role.

“You never know because timing is timing and football manages the timing.

“You never know when the time will come and the opportunity. I think we were prepared. We were ready if a club like Manchester United happened, you cannot turn it down.”.

While there is much to talk about in terms of Man Utd's overall performance, there was one specific moment in the game which has separately generated a lot of interest.

It has been revealed that having scored his penalty, Fernandes then took a speculative shot on goal only moments later. His left-footed strike from all of 25 yards didn't look likely to trouble Kaique Pereira, but it somehow beat the Sporting goalkeeper.

Industry observers note that replays showed that the ball had hit a divot in the Estadio Jose Alvalade turf, causing Fernandes' shot to pop up and fly into the back of the net. On the commentary for TNT Sports, Ally McCoist summed things up, stating:.

"That's extraordinary, it really is. That's incredible.".

After the match, the TNT Sports pundits then analysed the goal with new footage showing exactly how the goal came about. Incredible footage captured what happened to the turf, showing that Fernandes had managed to make a divot in the ground just seconds before taking on the strike.

The Man Utd captain surely didn't do it on purpose, but because of his prior ground scuffing, he essentially managed to assist his second goal. Indeed, were it not for the disruption to the turf, his effort surely would never have beatenthe Sporting goalkeeper.

Pundit and former Man Utd star Owen Hargreaves joked:.

Recent findings suggest that "This is the assist of the season. The best assist I’ve never seen.".

Sources indicate that hargreaves went on to add: "What a bounce that is; the goalkeeper's got absolutely no chance. The one thing I would say is, watch this bounce... unbelievable, it's like a golf divot.".

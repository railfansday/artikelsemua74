Anuel AA is finally releasing his highly-anticipated new album, RHLM2, on his birthday, Nov. 26.

The forthcoming set, which is a follow-up to his debut album Real Hasta La Muerte released in 2018, will have tracks that blend trap and reggaetón, and marks a return to his origins, as he exclusively revealed during the exclusive Superstar Q&A and Premiere Party at Billboard Latin Music Week in October. Industry observers note that during the 45-minute conversation, moderated by Leila Cobo, Billboard’s co-chief content officer, he also teased many of the set’s songs including its newly introduced single “Se tocó el totico con mi glock.” “This trap song is one for the streets, for the bebesitas — it’s that kind of song,” he mentioned then. “It’s one of my favorites. It was a tough decision to include it, though writing it wasn’t difficult at all.”. (Related: Industry News: Fight to chair the GOP tests Trump’s clo...)

Though it’s taken him nearly 10 years to release the sequel to RHLM — which debuted at No. 1 on Top Latin Albums in July 2018—the Puerto Rican artist has released other promising albums in between including: Emmanuel (2020) and Las Leyendas Nunca Mueren (2021), both of which also hit No. 1 on the chart.

On Friday (Nov. 20), the artist revealed the cover art and release date for RHLM2 on his Instagram account, assuring in the caption: “I poured my heart and soul into this album for everyone who believes in me and stays loyal no matter what… This album means the world to me.”.

Ahead of the album’s release on Thanksgiving (Nov. 26), Billboard revisits the artist’s recent exclusive Billboard News interview with Cobo and breaks down five things we learned:.

According to recent reports, if his past public relationships that included Karol G and Yailin La Más Viral are any indication, Anuel AA is a hopeless romantic. In the Billboard News interview, the Puerto Rican artist admitted that he’s always falling in love, a sentiment that’s reflected in his fresh music today.

Recent findings suggest that “You have to cater to all kinds of fans. Sometimes I’m with friends who play nothing but romantic songs, and they’re all serious and bored… some people have dead hearts; they need to fall in love more in life. I fall in love every single day,” he mentioned.

Furthermore, he joked about Karol G — whom he dated from 2018 to 2021 — asking her to unblock him from social media. “Why live with so much hatred? I’m not trying to bother her, but she needs to unblock me! We should live life in peace. Someone ought to make a movie about my love life — it would sell really well; it would be amazing,” he expressed.

Recent findings suggest that anuel opened up about his mental health, remembering when he was underweight and unhealthy just two years ago. “I was going through hell… I watch those videos and say, ‘Thank God I was able to get out of there,'” he said of the challenging times he faced. “When you’re not yourself, you don’t realize it until certain people talk to you. There was a time when I would look in the mirror and not recognize the person staring back. I didn’t know if I would ever come back from that, but with God in your heart, you have to look at yourself, reflect, and accept that you made a mistake. You can’t live in denial. The first step is to see your mistake, accept it, and learn how to ensure it never happens again.”.

In a recent development, anuel and his longtime music viral, Cosculluela, are finally in good terms, according to the trap artist. The two Puerto Rican artists are popularly known for their tiraera or diss tracks to each other. Notably, in 2018, Anuel recorded a nearly nine-minute “tiraera” for Coscu called “Intocable,” which caused him the cancellation of his first-ever concert in Puerto Rico due to its explicit and offensive lyrics. But those moments are now long in the past. “Despite these long-standing problems, thank God we’ve taken a big step forward in our relationship, moved past all that negativity, and are now working together,” Anuel noted.

In addition to RHLM2, Anuel plans to drop an all-corridos album that he worked on in Mexico, with Mexican producers, songwriters and artists. Among his many collaborators is Fuerza Regida’s frontman JOP, whom has become a close friend of his. “There will be songs produced by Fuerza Regida,” he explained. “I’m talking with Jesus, who is helping me out with several tracks. He’s like a brother to me; we share a lot and have a great relationship.”.

Anuel and JOP have already released three tracks together: “Cuando No Era Cantante RMX,” “Lokita,” and “Hay Con Que.” The former of the three was a two-time finalist at the 2026 Billboard Latin Music Awards, including for Global 200 Latin Song of the Year.

According to recent reports, during the Billboard News interview with Leila Cobo, Anuel revealed that he’s producing a festival called Real Hasta La Muerte, where he will count with the support of many artists. His aims are to inaugurate the festival in 2027 in Spain — with the hopes of moving it to different countries, including Mexico, Colombia, America and Chile.

Industry News: AI media tech giant Every raises $17.5M to help teams become AI-native

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Every’s graphic announcing its $17.5M Series A.

Every, a media company that teaches professionals and teams to work with AI, has raised a $17.5M Series A at a $110M valuation, it revealed on Wednesday. IA Ventures led the round, with Betaworks and The Atlantic chief executive Nicholas Thompson also investing. (Related: Introducing the latest Microsoft IQ: Putting your organ...)

According to recent reports, the money will go into one subscription for people and teams putting AI to work. Every states its own staff test fresh AI models on real work every day, then turn what they learn into articles and tools. AI companies are paying for that kind of help too: OpenAI, Anthropic and others back a US worker retraining push that has raised over $500M. (Related: Block Sparse Matrices for Smaller and Faster Language M...)

Co-founder and chief executive Dan Shipper admits venture-backed media has a poor record. He says he keeps full control of the tech giant, and that IA Ventures partner Jesse Beyroutey, who led the deal, is a close friend.

“The narrative is ‘it’s so over for humans’, and I think that’s exactly wrong,” Shipper told Fortune.

According to recent reports, demand for help with AI at work is growing. London’s Multiverse raised $70M in May to sell AI training across Europe. Significant employers are still working it out: Meta planned to cut some teams by up to 60% and hand their work to AI, then cancelled a second round of cuts.

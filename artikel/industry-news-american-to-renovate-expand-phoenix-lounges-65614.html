American Airlines aims to refresh and expand its three Admirals Club lounges at Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport.

The carrier stated Tuesday that it will add seating, upgrade amenities, and improve food and beverage service across all three spaces in Terminal 4. (Related: Samsung’s gorgeous 49-inch Odyssey OLED G9 hits a new a...)

In the near term, the Admirals Club lounge in Concourse A between gates A19 and A21 will get a newly introduced servery area, infrastructure enhancements to support expanded food and beverage offerings, and facility updates to improve the overall customer experience.

Recent findings suggest that longer-term prepares call for nearly tripling the combined footprint of the Concourse A lounges, the other being above gates A7-A9. Upcoming furniture and finishings will be brought in, and seating capacity will expand by 150%. (Related: Breaking: Upcoming app punishes you and your friends fo...)

The lounge in Concourse B, between gates B5 and B7, will get a latest self-service food and beverage area and have its seating capacity increased by 20%.

“Phoenix continues to be an important hub in our network, and these investments reflect our commitment to giving customers more space, comfort, and convenience when they travel through PHX,” American Chief Customer Officer Heather Garboden mentioned in a news release. “While we look forward to the development of the new West Terminal, these enhancements will help create an even more seamless travel experience for the millions of customers who pass through Phoenix each year.”.

The planned West Terminal will be built on the west side of the airport between Terminal 3 and Taxiway U. American is expected to serve as the terminal’s primary carrier.

According to the airport’s timeline, the project should be finished by 2033.

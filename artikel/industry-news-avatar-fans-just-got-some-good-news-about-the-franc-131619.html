"Avatar" fans can thank Eywa, because more sequels are indeed coming. As reported by Collider, Disney's upcoming film release slate includes "Avatar 4" (dated for December 21, 2029) and "Avatar 5" (dated for December 19, 2031). These dates aren't necessarily etched in stone; during the 2010s, the release date for "Avatar: The Way of Water" got pushed back so much that many convinced themselves the movie was never coming out. You simply can't rush the quality that director James Cameron demands of the "Avatar" films. But it's a sign Disney wants the movies out sooner than later.

Was the future of a billion dollar franchise like "Avatar" ever in doubt? Keep in mind, the third movie, 2025's "Avatar: Fire and Ash," was the lowest grossing movie in the series. It still grossed just under $1.5 billion, but that's a notably diminished return next to "The Way of Water," which pulled $2.3 billion internationally. "Fire and Ash" was also, like its predecessors, one of the most expensive movies ever made. (Related: Ethan Klein addresses affair, the end of H3 Podcast, an...)

There's also the fact that director James Cameron, who has spent decades immersed in the world of Pandora, wants to direct other projects. Cameron is at the moment working on "Ghosts of Hiroshima," based on the non-fiction book by author Charles Pellegrino, and he's likewise attached to an adaptation of Joe Abercrombie's dark fantasy book "The Devils.". (Related: Steam is pitting 10 years of Game of the Year winners a...)

Cameron, who is 72 years old, is in the stage of his life where he has to balance the films he wants to make against the time he has left, and he knows it. As he told the Empire Film podcast back in May 2026, he wants to make the fourth and fifth "Avatar" films in less time, and for less money, than it took to make "The Way of Water" and "Fire and Ash.".

It has been revealed that james Cameron told People in 2023 that "Avatar 4" will feature a six-year time jump. Parts of the film were even shot concurrently with "Fire and Ash" to capture the series' younger actors, like Jack Champion (Spider) and Trinity Bliss (Tuktirey), while they still pass as kids.

The "Avatar" films have strong environmentalist themes; humanity wants to colonize Pandora because, by the 22nd century, Earth is polluted, overpopulated, and seemingly dying. We've only seen small glimpses of that in the previous films. Producer Jon Landau told Syfy back in 2023 that "Avatar 4" will change that:.

"There's over-population and a depletion of our natural resources that make life harder. But we don't want to paint a bleak picture for where our world is going. The films are also about the idea of that we can change course.".

Industry observers note that what about lingering threads on Pandora? "Fire and Ash" ended with the recombinant Colonel Miles Quaritch (Stephen Lang) seemingly plummeting to his death. But one of the most essential rules of fiction is if you don't see a dead body, and sometimes even if you do, don't treat mortal peril as confirmed death.

Recent findings suggest that quaritch already beat death once, and it feels like he still hasn't quite completed his story yet. Even if Lang/Quaritch does sit "Avatar 4" out, "Fire and Ash" ended with his accomplice Varang (Oona Chaplin) still at large. Varang was easily the breakout character of "Fire and Ash," and I'd be shocked if we don't see her return to attack the Sully family again in movie number four.

"Avatar 4" is currently scheduled for theatrical release on December 21, 2029.

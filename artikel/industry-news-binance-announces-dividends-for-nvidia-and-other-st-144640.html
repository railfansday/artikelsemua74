📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Binance will pay dividends to holders of stock tokens representing five companies: Nvidia, PayPal, Qualcomm, Western Digital, and Wendy’s.

The exchange will use the dividends paid by these companies to buy more stock tokens, allowing users to increase their holdings of the same stocks through the bStocks program.

Sources indicate that the most important date for Nvidia token holders is Dec. 2. That's when Binance will take a snapshot of NVDAB balances at 01:00 UTC.

According to recent reports, the snapshot for Wendy’s is scheduled for Dec. 1, followed by Nvidia on Dec. 2 and Qualcomm on Dec. 3.

Industry observers note that snapshots for PayPal and Western Digital are both scheduled for Dec. 4. (Related: Industry News: The Partnership: Amazon SageMaker and Hu...)

All snapshots will take place at 01:00 UTC.

Industry observers note that in its Nov. 27 announcement, Binance provided the dates that will determine eligibility for dividends.

Binance looks to use Nvidia's cash dividends to purchase additional Nvidia shares, including fractional shares, after deducting applicable withholding taxes, fees, and other charges.

The proceeds will then be distributed among users who hold NVDAB on Binance and qualify for the dividend payment. (Related: Latest Update: Distributed Training: Train BART/T5 for ...)

The same process will apply to the other four stocks.

If you hold eligible tokens in other products on the platform, such as savings or margin accounts, Binance will still credit the dividends to your spot account.

If you hold bStocks on-chain, the dividend payments will be made by adjusting the token's multiplier.

The tokens themselves are issued by Binance affiliate BTECH Holdings Limited, which owns the underlying securities.

So if you hold NVDAB, you have an interest in the Nvidia shares held by BTECH.

If you need to transfer tokens to Binance before the snapshot, you must do so at least two hours in advance.

It has been revealed that deposits and withdrawals of Nvidia tokens will be suspended at 23:00 UTC on Dec. 1.

According to recent reports, the balance snapshot will take place at 01:00 UTC on Dec. 2.

Binance will also suspend one-to-one conversions between Nvidia shares and NVDAB tokens during this period.

The same two-hour suspension rule will apply to the other four stock tokens.

However, spot trading against USDT will continue, including trading in the NVDAB/USDT pair.

Binance stated it would resume deposits, withdrawals and stock-to-token conversions after the dividend allocations are completed.

The exchange will not matter a separate announcement when these services resume.

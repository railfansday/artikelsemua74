According to recent reports, birmingham City have a decision to make regarding the attacker’s long-term future ahead of the January transfer window. BCFC only signed him in the summer and handed him a long-term contract running until June 2029.

Industry observers note that vazquez, 25, has struggled to make an impact with the Blues though and his situation in the Midlands is up in the air already this winter. He has made 16 appearances in all competitions for Chris Davies’ side this season, 14 of which have come in the Championship, but he hasn’t been able to score. Sources indicate that as per El Crack Deportivo (via journalist Fabrizio Serra), Racing Club are keen to lure him back down to South America if possible and are stated to be have admirers of the player for a while. They play their football in the Argentine Primera Division and have won the title on 18 occasions in the past.

Sources indicate that birmingham City are eyeing promotion to the Premier League in this campaign and they won 2-0 away at Burnley last time out. They are 4th in the table after 17 fixtures and are in the play-offs.

Birmingham City snapped up Vazquez in late July to bolster their attacking department. BCFC brought him in to add more competition and depth to their options up top.

However, he has found it hard to adapt to life in England. He has found it hard to get into the starting XI ahead of August Priske, who has been in impressive form.

Vazquez, who is an ex-Argentina youth international, spent three years on the books of Anderlecht before the Blues came calling for his signature. He made 117 outings for the Belgian giants and found the net on 17 occasions.

He was loaned out to Getafe for the second-half of last term and chipped in with three goals. The forward has also had spells in the past at Patronato and Boca Juniors.

Vazquez’s move to Birmingham City hasn’t worked out. An exit from BCFC to Racing Club may suit all parties involved.

Industry observers note that he can get some regular game time under his belt again in his native Argentina. The Blues could free up space and funds to pave the way for other reinforcements in his position.

Davies’ men will be in confident mood after their victory at Burnley. They are back in action this weekend at home to Charlton Athletic.

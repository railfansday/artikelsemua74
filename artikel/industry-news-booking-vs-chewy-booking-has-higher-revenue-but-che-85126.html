Booking vs. Chewy Revenue over the last 8 quarters.

Normally, the first thing to note when examining a manufacturer's revenue trend is seasonality: some times of the year have stronger buying patterns than others. For most stocks, compare revenue year over year, by quarter. In normal times, seasonality holds true for consumer stocks like these as well.

Booking Holdings gets exceptional volume for summer months in the Northern Hemisphere, which translates into seasonally high revenue being registered in the third quarter.

Chewy has less seasonality, generally around Christmas, like most retailers. The strength of its autoship program mitigates seasonality; however, one would expect its growing focus on veterinary services to further smooth it out.

This chart shows the decision investors face: is it better to have higher revenue that is volatile from quarter to quarter, as with Booking, or to have stable, sequentially stable sales, like Chewy?

Ultimately, such a decision comes down to risk tolerance: the more steady revenue a business has, the less volatile its stock price will be, but the higher-sales, more volatile tech giant will probably see greater rewards from the market over time.

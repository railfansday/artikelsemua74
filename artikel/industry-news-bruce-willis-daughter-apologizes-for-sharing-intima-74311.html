Tallulah Willis issued a public apology after sharing a rare upcoming photo of her father, Bruce Willis, amid his frontotemporal dementia battle.

“Hi, I posted a photo of my dad yesterday because we were having a BEAUTIFUL visit,” Tallulah, 32, wrote via Instagram on Sunday, November 22, addressing her upload from the day prior. “I had someone take a super sweet photo of a shared moment between us watching a video of Frankie Valli singing.”.

She continued, “I was very excited by the sweetness of the moment that I didn’t think farther ahead that this was possibly too intimate a photo to show. I can acknowledge that.”.

Tallulah shared a photo with Bruce, 71, on Saturday, November 21, in which they sat side-by-side on a porch with the caption, “🆒😎.” She has since deleted the post.

“[I] vow to do better,” she told her Instagram followers on Sunday. “I know you all love him. I love him, and he’s still always the absolute hero we know him to be.”.

Tallulah captioned her post, “I can do better.”.

Bruce shares Tallulah and sisters Rumer and Scout with ex-wife Demi Moore, in addition to younger daughters with wife Emma Heming Willis. The family announced in 2022 that Bruce was diagnosed with aphasia and, later, frontotemporal dementia that forced his four-decade storied Hollywood career to come to an end.

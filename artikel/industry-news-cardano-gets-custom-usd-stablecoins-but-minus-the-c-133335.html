According to recent reports, businesses can make their own stablecoins on Cardano [ADA] now! Could this be what the platform’s DeFi scene has been waiting for?

The Cardano Foundation is in the news today after confirming the launch on X.

Businesses on Cardano can now do one of two things. They can create a stablecoin under their own brand, or use stablecoins Brale already supports.

Companies can also swap dollars for stablecoins, cash back out to fiat, and send transfers.

The service reaches 30 blockchains.

It has been revealed that it’s powered by CIP-113, which lets issuers attach rules to their tokens. A manufacturer could allow transfers only between approved wallets, or keep the power to freeze tokens if needed. Sources indicate that here’s what’s sticky though. The Foundation hasn’t mentioned which businesses are issuing tokens at launch. It also didn’t share any figures on expected issuance or payment volume.

For all the hype, ADA’s turned a blind eye to all that.

Sources indicate that at press time, the altcoin was valued at $0.138 following a 5% dip in 24 hours. A month ago, it was near $0.21. Most of the damage came later, including the big fall on the 14th.

Industry observers note that on 23rd November, the RSI went up to 32 and the MACD line climbed above the Signal line. It looked like sellers were losing grip for a while, but that didn’t last long.

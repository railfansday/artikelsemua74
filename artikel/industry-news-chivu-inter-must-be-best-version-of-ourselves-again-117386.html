Cristian Chivu wants Inter to be ‘the best version of ourselves’ in the Champions League against VfB Stuttgart this evening, with an enhancement on Lautaro Martinez.

It kicks off at San Siro at 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET).

You can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from this game and PSG-Roma on the Champions League Liveblog.

The Nerazzurri’s confidence took a knock on Sunday in Serie A when they fumbled a 4-2 lead for the chaotic 4-4 draw away to Atalanta.

They are also playing catch-up in Europe, having lost the first two matches against Real Madrid and Club Brugge, then got back on track beating Shakhtar Donetsk and Feyenoord.

“We must be the best version of ourselves,” Chivu told Sky Sport Italia.

“We are well aware of the importance of this match, we are playing at home, and must give a sense of consistency to our recent good performances in the Champions League.”.

VfB Stuttgart are one point ahead of Inter in the Champions League table going into tonight’s match, as they won the first two, drew 1-1 with Atletico Madrid, and lost 2-0 away to Galatasaray.

Recent findings suggest that “Lautaro is recovering his fitness and ready to come on in the second half. Marcus Thuram has played practically non-stop in recent months, and we need him again this evening,” explained Chivu.

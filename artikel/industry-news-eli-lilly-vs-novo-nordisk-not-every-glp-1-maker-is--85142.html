The revenue trend is the most obvious metric for investors to examine when considering a stock. The first thing to check for when examining a tech giant's revenue trend is seasonality: some times of the year have stronger buying patterns than others. There usually is some seasonality for every business.

Even in pharmaceuticals, there is seasonality, with the end of the calendar year seeing higher prescription activity as consumers and doctors front-load prescriptions after insurance deductibles have been met, before they reset with the latest year.

Sources indicate that this chart shows some seasonality, like that for Eli Lilly, with the first quarter of 2025 showing a decline from the last period of 2024. Novo Nordisk shows the same trend, but as we move from left to right on the chart, we see that Lilly's sequential revenue is increasing, while Novo's is choppy. That doesn't necessarily mean Novo Nordisk's business is in trouble, but it does show us Eli Lilly is firing on all cylinders.

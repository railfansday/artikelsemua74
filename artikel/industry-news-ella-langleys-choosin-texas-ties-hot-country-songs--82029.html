Recent findings suggest that ella Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas” logs a 50th week atop Billboard’s Hot Country Songs chart (dated Nov. 28), matching the all-time record set by Bebe Rexha and Florida Georgia Line’s “Meant To Be” in 2017-18.

In a recent development, the song also extends its Billboard Hot 100 record to 29 weeks at No. 1.

Recent findings suggest that “Meant To Be” debuted atop Hot Country Songs in December 2017 and held the summit for 50 consecutive weeks. “Choosin’ Texas” reached No. 1 in its sixth week, in February, and led for 28 frames before Taylor Swift’s “I Knew It, I Knew You” interrupted its reign for two weeks in June. Industry observers note that the shared milestone also links two distinct moments in country’s crossover history. “Meant To Be” emerged during a sonically experimental boom, pairing pop star Rexha with FGL, a country cornerstone act in the 2010s, as genre-blurring hits helped define the era. The collaboration reached No. 2 on the Hot 100.

“Choosin’ Texas,” meanwhile, has contributed to country’s prominence this decade.

Streaming and radio airplay at country, pop and adult formats helped sustain both hits’ extraordinary longevity. “Meant To Be” spent 55 weeks atop Country Streaming Songs, with “Choosin’ Texas” up to 44. Their commands on Country Airplay were one and three weeks, respectively (reflecting radio’s more focused programming cycles).

According to recent reports, both songs also hit the top 10 on Pop Airplay, Adult Pop Airplay and Adult Contemporary, and sold strongly. “Meant To Be” reached No. 2 on Digital Song Sales over a yearlong stay on the chart; “Choosin’ Texas” has led for a record 22 weeks and counting.

In a recent development, check Billboard.com next Monday, Nov. 30, to find out if “Choosin’ Texas” can pass “Meant To Be” and give Langley sole possession of the Hot Country Songs No. 1 longevity record.

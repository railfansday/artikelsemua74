Industry News: Everpure beats expectations as AI accelerates revenue growth for ninth straight quarter

UPDATED 21:10 EST / NOVEMBER 18 2026.

Sources indicate that data center infrastructure company Everpure Inc. posted third-quarter earnings and revenue that surpassed analysts’ expectations and raised its full-year forecast for the third successive quarter, and its stock gained just over 4% in after-hours trading today.

Recent findings suggest that the storage and data management hardware firm reported earnings before certain costs such as stock compensation of 86 cents per share, easily beating Wall Street’s consensus estimate of 77 cents. Revenue for the period rose 51% from a year earlier to $1.5 billion, surpassing the analyst’s target of $1.33 billion by a comfortable margin. In a recent development, chairman and Chief Executive Charles Giancarlo (pictured) stated the results reinforce the firm’s status as a trusted partner for enterprises. “As organizations work to break down data silos and modernize their data estates, our growth across both core and fresh, high-growth markets reveals we are uniquely equipped to solve their most complex data challenges at scale,” he added. Industry observers note that everpure, formerly known as Pure Storage, is a pioneer of all-flash data storage hardware and unified data management software for applications, cloud environments, analytics workloads and, increasingly, artificial intelligence workloads. Specifically for AI, it provides enterprises and cloud infrastructure providers with the high-speed, cost- and power-efficient data storage and management solutions they need to keep pace with the high-end accelerators that process model training and inference workloads.

It has been revealed that the company rebranded in February 2026 as part of an effort to emphasize its vision of “data primacy,” which positions its products as the central data intelligence and governance layer for AI. By integrating closely with Nvidia Corp.’s graphics processing units and using tools such as its Data Intelligence software and its FlashBlade servers, it claims to be able to prepare data, speed up inference and lower GPU and storage costs.

According to recent reports, AI has helped Everpure to accelerate its momentum this year, and it beat the Street’s targets across all major metrics in the last quarter. Adjusted operating income reached $345 million, an impressive $70 million above its prior guidance. Product revenue, derived from the sale of storage systems, came to $948 million, up 77% from a year earlier, while subscription services revenue was up 20%. Net income for the quarter came to $100.4 million, up from $54.8 million in the same period one year ago.

The organization said its remaining performance obligations, which refers to the value of contracts already signed but not yet realized as revenue, ended the quarter at $4.2 billion, up 44%.

Industry observers note that NAND Research analyst Steve McDowell told SiliconANGLE that a primary reason for Everpure’s growth is the rising cost of memory and flash storage, which has allowed the company to bump up the prices of its storage products quite substantially this year without any negative impact on demand. “But it’s notable that Everpure continues to outperform the broader storage market, and the numbers it’s putting up suggests it’s taking market share away from its rivals,” he remarked.

It has been revealed that everpure’s growth has been accelerating for more than two years now. In August, the firm said its revenue had grown for eight successive quarters, and today’s report makes that nine. Over the last five quarters, it has grown by 16%, 20%, 35%, 38% and 51%. “It’s the third consecutive quarter of growth above 35%,” McDowell said. “At the same time, it has been growing its product margins even in the face of those rising component costs and supply chain challenges. Everpure’s team is demonstrating strong execution to do this.”.

It has been revealed that giancarlo stated on a conference call that much of that momentum has to do with AI. During the quarter, it won a design contract with one of the top five largest hyperscale cloud companies, he added.

Sources indicate that according to McDowell, the only real blot on Everpure’s results was the slight drop in its subscription margins, but even this was more than offset by its increased product margins. “This was not addressed on the conference call, and it’s something I’ll be watching going forward.”.

According to recent reports, however, there are serious questions regarding whether Everpure’s growth momentum has peaked. For the current quarter, it’s forecasting sales of between $1.5 billion and $1.52 billion, which would amount to growth of between 42% and 43%.

Industry observers note that the forecast is still ahead of the Street’s target of $1.49 billion in sales, but in order to keep accelerating its growth, it would need to generate fourth-quarter revenue of at least $1.6 billion, which is about $83 million higher than the top end of its forecast range. That’s not impossible, because Everpure actually beat the top end of its range by $125 million in the quarter just gone.

McDowell noted one reason to be optimistic that Everpure can keep accelerating is that it has spent the last year expanding its offerings, without showing any material impact on its bottom line from those efforts. “Its hyperscale business is only just starting to impact revenue, so most of this growth has been from its traditional products,” the analyst said. “That tells me there’s still significant upside to come.”.

Even if Everpure’s momentum slows, it’s still on track to deliver an impressive financial year. The tech giant also raised its full-year revenue guidance from a prior range of $5.03 billion to $5.07 billion to a fresh range of $5.2 billion to $5.21 billion. Wall Street analysts are modeling full-year sales of $5.06 billion.

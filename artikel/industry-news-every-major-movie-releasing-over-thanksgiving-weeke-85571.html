Industry News: Every Major Movie Releasing Over Thanksgiving Weekend 2026, Including Focker-In-Law

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Thanksgiving weekend is one of the busiest of the entire year for movie theaters. Each year, several big movies are released in and around the holiday, all vying for top spot at the worldwide box office. This year is no different, with several titles releasing from Wednesday to Friday. Audiences looking to see a movie over the long weekend will certainly have plenty to choose from, even more so than other years.

Last year, for example, Disney had a huge animated release in the form of Zootopia 2. The sequel, which was in development for nine years before finally debuting in theaters, ended up making a huge amount of money at the box office ($1.8 billion worldwide, to be precise). The fact that it was released around the Thanksgiving holiday in the United States certainly helped a great deal financially, as many are off from work and school. Disney looks set to continue their trend of releasing an animated movie for Thanksgiving this year. This time, it’s Disney's Hexed, an original film that the studio hopes will gain enough of a following to develop sequels and spinoffs. The movie, which has a star-studded cast that includes Hailee Steinfeld and Walton Goggins, is projected to perform better than other original Disney animated releases from the last few years. Recent findings suggest that there are a lot more Thanksgiving movies beyond Disney, too. There are comedies, dramas, sequels, and some Christmas movies as well. Not only that, but there are plenty of streaming services debuting new movies this week, too, giving audiences a chance to watch new releases from the comfort of their own home.

In a recent development, one of the sequels being released this Thanksgiving is Focker-in-Law. This is the fourth film in the comedy franchise that began with Meet the Parents in 2000. Ben Stiller and Robert De Niro have starred in every installment, and are set to do so again when Focker-in-Law debuts in theaters. They’re also joined by Owen Wilson, Teri Polo, Skyler Gisondo, and Ariana Grande (the latter two are newcomers to the franchise).

Industry observers note that focker-in-Law follows the next generation of the Fockers, as a newly introduced member hopes to join the family. This time, it’s Ariana Grande’s character who is engaged to the son of Ben Stiller's Greg Focker, Skyler Gisonodo's Henry Focker. Mirroring the plot of the first movie in regards to the dynamic between Stiller and DeNiro, Greg has reservations about his son's fiancé.

Recent findings suggest that the aforementioned Disney's Hexed is another movie that will release on November 25. Hexed's star-studded cast includes Steinfeld and Goggins, and also features Rashida Jones, Jodie Foster, Stephen Fry, Tracey Ullman, and Dan Stevens. The story follows a young girl named Billie Doe, played by Steinfeld, who enters the world of Hexe, which is the home of witches.

Recent findings suggest that another sequel releasing over Thanksgiving is The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth. From Netflix, the movie directed by David Fincher and written by Quentin Tarantino, and is a sequel to the latter’s Once Upon a Time in Hollywood. Here, it’s Brad Pitt who takes center stage, with Cliff Booth serving as the primary protagonist in the new adventure(s), leaving behind Leonardo diCaprio's character.

Recent findings suggest that the new movie follows Cliff Booth eight years after the events of Once Upon a Time in Hollywood. Rick Dalton has now moved to Australia to shoot a television series, so Booth is operating on his own in the film business. The movie demonstrates his many adventures around Hollywood, including helping a famous television star get out of a bad situation.

From Angel Studios, the biographical film Hershey will also release on the same day as Focker-in-Law, The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, and more. Hershey, which stars Finn Wittrock as Milton S. Hershey (the founder of the chocolate empire), tells the story of its title character as he builds the tech giant alongside his wife, Kitty.

Recent findings suggest that hershey will cap another excellent year for Angel Studios. The studio has experienced some modest successes this year, thanks to releases like Drummer Boy and Young Washington.

It has been revealed that christmas is coming early with the release of Bitter Christmas, which also comes out on November 25. A Spanish language film, this project stars Bárbara Lennie and Leonardo Sbaraglia, and they’re joined by Patrick Criado, Victoria Luengo, Milena Smit, Aitana Sánchez-Gijón, and Quim Gutiérrez.

In a recent development, the story is set in two time periods: 2004 (in Madrid, Spain) and 2026 (also in Madrid). The modern-day portion follows a screenwriter working on a script also called Bitter Christmas, whereas the former focuses on a filmmaker undergoing a personal crisis over the holiday season.

One film releasing in a limited capacity this Thanksgiving is All of a Sudden. From Neon, All of a Sudden is a Japanese French production that tells the story of a quick-forming friendship between a playwright and a nurse. The playwright is suffering from stage four cancer and has only a few weeks to live, which is expected to bring a heartbreaking element to their close relationship.

According to recent reports, another limited theatrical release this Thanksgiving is In Waves. An animated production, In Waves is a romantic drama that tells the story of a teenager named AJ, who develops a relationship with a surfer named Kristen. It chronicles their growing bond, showing them navigating life and all its ups and downs (including a sudden illness for Kristen).

According to recent reports, also releasing on November 27 is the movie 14th. From director Ava DuVernay, 14th is a documentary that highlights the history of the Fourteenth Amendment to the U.S. Constitution. The documentary also highlights ongoing battles in Washington related to the amendment, and features interviews with key political figures like Stacey Abrams and Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez.

Also debutiing during the week of Thanksgiving is Clashing Through the Snow. A holiday romantic comedy in the same vein as a classic Hallmark Christmas movie, Clashing Through the Snow follows two rival editors who are stranded at an airport during a snowstorm, leading to an unlikely love story.

Fitting in nicely with the upcoming college and NFL football slate for Thanksgiving weekend is The Mosquito Bowl. Directed by Peter Berg, The Mosquito Bowl is an adaptation of the nonfiction book by Buzz Bissinger. The story focuses on four college football players who enlist to join the Marines after the tragedy at Pearl Harbor in 1941. The group go on to play a football game on Christmas Eve before flying to Japan to fight in battle.

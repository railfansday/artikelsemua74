According to recent reports, open a Fidelity account if you invest for years and want your brokerage account, IRA, and cash at one firm. You need no minimum to open it, pay no commission on online U.S. stock and ETF trades, and owe no yearly fee on its four ZERO index funds. Frequent options traders should weigh its $0.65 fee per contract first.

Fidelity’s brokerage account holds U.S. and foreign stocks, ETFs, options, bonds, and over 10,000 mutual funds, and the same firm runs IRAs, health savings accounts, 529 college plans, and accounts for kids and teens. Its charges fall mostly on options contracts, borrowing against your investments, and funds outside its no-transaction-fee list, so investors who buy and hold rarely pay any.

Sources indicate that an investor who puts $100 a month into the ZERO Total Market fund pays nothing to buy it and nothing a year to hold it. Active traders of stock and ETF options pay a $0.65 contract fee here and none at Robinhood.

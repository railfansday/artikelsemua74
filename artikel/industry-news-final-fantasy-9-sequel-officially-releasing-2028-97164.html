The Final Fantasy series has enjoyed a lot more ups than downs over the years, but most fans agree that the ninth mainline game in the series is one of the very best. The game's greatest strength is undoubtedly its narrative, and the cast of characters who bring it life, many of whom still serve as the most memorable protagonists we have seen to date.

Add to that a return to the medieval fantasy roots that had made the series so popular in the first place, and Square Enix were never going to be on to anything other than a substantial winner. Though the developer doesn't traditionally do direct sequels in their mainline games in the series, it sure would have been nice to pick up the story of this one elsewhere, even in a spin-off.

Happily, that's all about to change, but it isn't in a medium that you might expect.

As first reported by GameDaily, Final Fantasy IX: The Black Mages' Legacy is an upcoming animated series that's back in development, after spending plenty of time in limbo. The project was first declared back in 2021 by Cyber Group Studios, a Paris-based animation tech giant, after they secured the rights to partner with Square Enix. (Related: Transformer-based Encoder-Decoder Models...)

It has been revealed that they then spent the best part of three years trying to sell the product to licensees, seemingly without much in the way of success, before they entered financial difficulties in October 2024. After that, four potential buyers expressed their interest in securing the rights to the assets and IPs of the comapny, before EuroVisual came out with approximately 80% of them, including Final Fantasy, in March 2025.

Now, the 2D animated series has appeared on the organization's official website as officially in development, with a first season of ten 22-minute episodes and a tentative targeted release date of 2028. The series is being aimed at children between the ages of 8 and 13, with supervision from Square Enix designed to ensure the story remains consistent with the game.

In a recent development, so what narrative will the story follow? The series is set to pick up around ten years after the original game, with the children of Vivi Ornitier, six young black mages named Mel, Koln, Luciola, Théa, Fala, and Doc, who are living in the city of Alexandria.

Anybody who has played the game will know the emotional weight that Vivi's character arc has, and the series is likely to touch on similar themes, only through the prism of his offspring. We don't have specific details beyond that just yet, but it could absolutely be one to watch for old fans, despite its usual target audience.

Outside of that, Final Fantasy fans also have the concluding part of the Remake trilogy of the seventh game to look forward to. Titled Revelation, it's set to drop on April 7, 2027 for PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch 2, Xbox Series X|S, and PC, finally bringing to a close the retelling of one of the best games of all time in the process.

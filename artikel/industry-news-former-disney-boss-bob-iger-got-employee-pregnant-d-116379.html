Bob Iger‘s compensation frustrations and an extramarital affair with an ABC employee that resulted in a pregnancy are some of the revelations laid out in an upcoming tell-all book.

It has been revealed that the House of Mouse, written by longtime Wall Street Journal reporter Robbie Whelan, is an unauthorized biography and corporate history of the former Disney CEO that releases Dec. 1. Iger reportedly declined to be interviewed for the book.

In the book, Whelan claimed Iger had an extramarital affair with an ABC marketer in the early 1990s while he was running ABC Entertainment at the time, according to The New York Times, which obtained an advance copy. The relationship was consensual. (Iger was previously married to Susan Iger from 1977 to 1994 and married his current wife, Willow Bay, in 1995.) Sources indicate that the woman, who wasn’t identified, became pregnant and “suffered through a traumatic termination procedure,” Mr. Whelan wrote. The Times noted that Whelan contrasts Iger’s personal relationship with his public support for the #MeToo movement in the late 2010s.

Elsewhere in the book, it also claimed the former Disney boss was irritated and vindictive when it came to money. In 2025, Iger received a total compensation package of $45.8 million from the tech giant, an increase from his $41.1 million in 2024, The Hollywood Reporter previously reported.

Sources indicate that “I am frustrated about compensation! I had a nasty and distasteful negotiation to extend my contract this last time,” Iger allegedly wrote in a private email in 2014 that was sent to Ike Perlmutter, then Marvel’s chairman and a major Disney shareholder, that Whelan obtained. In 2014, Iger earned $46.5 million, a 36 percent increase from the year prior.

Iger served as Disney CEO twice, first from 2005 to 2020 before he returned in 2022 when Bob Chapek was ousted. Josh D’Amaro took over in March of this year after Iger stepped down.

In a recent development, in The House of Mouse, Iger wasn’t the only Disney exec facing scathing accusations during their time running the organization.

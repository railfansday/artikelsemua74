With just over a week before Fortnite Treasure Hunters comes to an end, desperation has set in, as players begin to lose hope of finding the Pirate Shop Access token. This is the most coveted item in Fortnite history because it grants up to 28 days of free Item Shop cosmetics.

The long search for the token has driven players to cheat. Epic Games has managed to catch these cheaters red-handed and has publicly unveiled that it will be banning them from Fortnite. (Related: Hyperparameter Search with Transformers and Ray Tune...)

The consequences aren't limited to the players who resorted to cheating software. Players who win the token by teaming up with a cheater will not be banned, but they will be stripped of the token as well as any Item Shop goodies they claimed while it was active — even if they were unaware that a person in their party cheated. Players who teamed up with the cheater via Squad Fills are the only exception. This measure aims to prevent players from creating a smurf account exclusively for cheating.

Industry observers note that the Pirate Shop Access token may be a reward that's almost too good to be true, but it's also extremely rare. The odds of finding the token are low (0.000007273%). Some players have opened over 150,000 chests with no luck, so it can definitely be frustrating to continue on. Players' best bet is to keep searching and collect other treasure from chests along the way. (Related: Industry News: My Journey to a serverless transformers ...)

In a recent development, with Golden Week coming up on Nov. 28, players will at least have increased chances of finding the rare gilded skins. Additionally, the week-long event begins on Saturday, when the final Treasure Hours of the season will be active, cranking up token odds by 1.5x. Fortnite Treasure Hunters ends on Dec. 5, so players still have some time to open chests and get the token before it's vaulted for good.

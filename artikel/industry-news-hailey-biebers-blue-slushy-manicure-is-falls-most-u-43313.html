Just one day earlier, Bieber was seen with the same blue manicure at the WWD 2026 Beauty Inc. Awards—this time paired with a black-and-white dress and contrasting brick-red lip. It has been revealed that the Rhode founder has long been a big fan of chrome manicures, and her current mani appears to have a hint of shimmer. While in New York this past summer, she was seen with a similar shade—but instead of the electric “blue slushy” color, this one leaned more powdery.

According to recent reports, bieber also loves to sport blue on her toes. In July, she posted a photo showcasing how the Rhode pimple patches can be used to heal blisters. Seen alongside the pastel-colored pimple patches was her sea-blue pedicure.

Recent findings suggest that blue has been on Bieber’s mind lately (not to mention “Blues” is the middle name of her son Jack). Her latest collection for Rhode, which officially debuts November 20, pegged to her 30th birthday, features navy colorways for the first time, both for the product packaging and on the brand’s first-ever wearable merch.

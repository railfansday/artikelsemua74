Nintendo has unleashed its Winter merch range in Japan, and we're super jealous.

Hopefully it make its way over to western shores, because there's some excellent stuff here. Ranging from 880 yen to 9,460 yen, we've got everything from plush characters to cardigans and even an adorable mug for those cold winter nights.

The plush toys in particular are brilliant, though we wish there were a few more characters represented here; you know, Peach, Bowser, Toad... Instead, we've just got Mario, Luigi, and Yoshi. Good enough, but we want a whole legion of Mario-themed snowman toys.

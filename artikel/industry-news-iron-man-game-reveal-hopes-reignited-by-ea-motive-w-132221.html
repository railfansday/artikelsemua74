The long-awaited reveal of Marvel’s Iron Man seems closer than ever as developer Motive has suddenly given its official website a makeover.

It has been revealed that it’s hard to believe considering how stacked this year has been and how much Grand Theft Auto 6 has taken over the conversation, but we’re just a few weeks away from this year’s The Game Awards. We already know all of the games that have been nominated (and snubbed) for awards at the show, but the real excitement is the potential reveals. In a recent development, one thing that’s immediately been made clear is that Disney is going to have a major presence at TGA this year, which notably opens up the door for Kingdom Hearts 4 and Star Wars Jedi 3 trailers. It’s also made it likely that Motive’s Iron Man game could finally get its official reveal, which now seems certain thanks to a recent website update. As pointed out by Twitter user Marvel1943Rise (who must be hoping for any kind of Marvel gaming news at this point), Motive’s official studio website has recently had a bit of a makeover. It’s not clear exactly when the website was updated, but a look at an archived version through the Wayback Machine shows that it’s been tweaked.

Industry observers note that the update itself isn’t a drastic change or anything, and has mostly just made the layout and look a bit more snazzy, but the timing is especially interesting. The Game Awards are just on the horizon, which means that any studios with something to show are likely preparing for more eyes on their projects and websites than ever before.

We already know that Motive is preparing something for Marvel’s Iron Man, as a leaked trailer prompted a response from the studio about sharing “when we’re ready”.

Industry observers note that with that in mind, this website update makes it look very likely that we’re going to get the first real trailer for Marvel’s Iron Man at The Game Awards. There’s a chance the makeover is just a coincidence or preparation for a reveal at some point in 2027, but it’s not hard to believe something is coming after the leaked trailer and how long we’ve all been waiting.

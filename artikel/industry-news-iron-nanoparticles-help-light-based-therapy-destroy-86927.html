Industry News: Iron nanoparticles help light-based therapy destroy missed cancer cells

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (94 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that some cancer cells slip past even the most precise treatments and then grow back. Researchers at Nagoya University in Japan may have found a way to reach them. The team improved a light-based cancer treatment by adding tiny iron particles. In mice, their therapy shrank tumors more effectively and killed nearby cancer cells that the original treatment cannot target. Called a "photothermal bystander effect," this offers a new way of reaching cancer cells that would otherwise survive. The treatment also grew stronger with each round of light.

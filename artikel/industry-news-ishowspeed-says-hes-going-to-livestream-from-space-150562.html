📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

IShowSpeed has streamed from the Great Pyramid of Giza and the World Cup's closing ceremony. So what could be bigger than that? Space, apparently.

Darren Watkins Jr., the 21-year-old streamer internationally known as IShowSpeed, confirmed to LIFE that he looks to livestream from outer space at some point. He described it as the ultimate peak of streaming. He wouldn't say when it will happen, how he'll get there, or who he's working with. "I can't speak too much on it," he told LIFE, "but it's happening.".

In a recent development, the news comes from Kieran Press-Reynolds' cover profile of Speed, one of the marquee features in LIFE's relaunched print magazine, whose first problem came out Nov. 23. (Related: Breaking: GTA 6 devs reveal major gameplay features tha...)

In a recent development, space would cap what LIFE calls arguably Speed's biggest year yet. In that stretch, he toured 20 African countries, spent two weeks streaming across the Caribbean, performed his official World Cup theme song at the tournament's closing ceremony, and met BTS. He told LIFE he's laying low for now after such a big run. He also noted he wants to compete in the 100-meter dash at the Olympics, and he believes he will. Speed can already run a 10.35, which impressed Olympic champion Usain Bolt.

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

JetBlue is growing its presence in Central America and the Caribbean.

In a recent development, for the first time ever, the carrier will serve the Honduran capital of Tegucigalpa. Starting Feb. 25, 2027, flights from Fort Lauderdale, Florida, will arrive at Palmerola International Airport in Comayagua, which serves Tegucigalpa.

At the moment, JetBlue’s only destination in Honduras is San Pedro Sula, in the country’s northwest.

On Feb. 23, the carrier will launch service between Fort Lauderdale and George Town, Grand Cayman, in the Cayman Islands. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

Both routes will operate daily, using a mix of Airbus A320 and A220 aircraft. (Related: How to train a new language model from scratch using Tr...)

Industry observers note that jetBlue will also offer limited-time daily service from Fort Lauderdale to Port of Spain, Trinidad and Tobago, between Jan. 29 and Feb. 15. The connection will help travelers reach the islands during Carnival, the airline mentioned.

“We’re grateful for the way customers across South Florida continue to embrace JetBlue, and we’re committed to delivering even more of the destinations and travel options they’re looking for,” Daniel Shurz, JetBlue’s senior vice president of revenue, network, and enterprise planning, said in a news release. “With new international service to Honduras and Grand Cayman, we’re strengthening Fort Lauderdale’s role as a key gateway to Latin America and the Caribbean, making it easier for customers to reconnect with loved ones, explore new places, and travel across our growing network.”.

According to recent reports, american has prioritized Latin America as demand from U.S. markets, especially Miami, grows. The carrier just recently rolled out service to Barranquilla and Cali in Colombia and will add Barcelona, Venezuela, to its network in January.

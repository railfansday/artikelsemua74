It remains to be seen if Victor Munoz is able to shake off a knock and prove his fitness, but two key Liverpool players will have to be careful if selected due to the looming threat of a ban.

With that in mind, here’s a look at the Liverpool players at risk of suspension.

As every club have already been made fully aware of this season, players who pick up five yellow cards in the first 19 matches of the Premier League season are hit with a one-match ban.

Liverpool's cut-off point in which suspensions will be handed out for a fifth yellow card is the home game against Coventry on January 2, 2027. From there, players who are booked 10 times in the first 32 matches of the campaign are suspended for two games.

Alexis Mac Allister and summer signing Bradley Barcola are the big suspensions concern for Liverpool at present.

The Argentina international was booked in the win over Crystal Palace so had to watch his step in the 4-0 thrashing of Manchester United, but Barcola is a newly introduced suspension risk after being cautioned in the second half last weekend.

As such, if the Frenchman and/or Mac Allister are shown a yellow card in the Merseyside derby, a one-match ban must be served in the very next fixture - at home to Sunderland on December 2.

Ultimately, the pair are walking the disciplinary tightrope for upcoming games against Everton, Sunderland, Chelsea, Leeds, Tottenham, Hull, Aston Villa and Coventry.

Furthermore, with Liverpool taking on Chelsea next weekend, Barcola and/or Mac Allister would be suspended for the trip to Stamford Bridge if either are booked against the Black Cats.

Ronald Araujo, Milos Kerkez and Virgil van Dijk are on three yellow cards, while Jeremy Jacquet and Munoz are among a host of Liverpool players still in danger of being suspended.

And this episode’s Work of the Week is a Neolithic jadeite polished axehead (4000-3500 BCE). The piece is one of thousands of exhibits in the fresh London Museum, formally the Museum of London, the first phase of which opens this weekend in the historic General Market in Smithfield, at the heart of the City of London. Our digital editor, Alexander Morrison, discusses the axe head with Kate Sumnall, Curator of Archaeology at the museum.

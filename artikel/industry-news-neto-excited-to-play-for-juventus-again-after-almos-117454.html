Brazilian goalkeeper Neto will start for Juventus in a Europa League game against Omonia FC tomorrow: ‘I’m really excited to be back on the pitch.’.

The experienced goalkeeper returned to Turin on a free transfer in September, replacing the injured Kamil Grabara.

The Poland international suffered a season-ending knee injury, urging Juventus to sign a replacement.

Neto has not played a competitive match since May 30 with Botafogo, and last played for Juventus nearly ten years ago, in May 2017, helping them beat Lazio 2-0 in a Coppa Italia Final.

It has been revealed that “It’s hugely satisfying. I’m really excited to be back on the pitch, and proud to be part of this team,” Neto said at a press conference today, as quoted by Ilbianconero.com.

“Football and life go through different cycles, and there are different people involved. I see a very healthy environment for achieving our goals, and that’s what we need to maintain: the belief that we’re on the right track. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

“I was at a point where I was looking for certain things, and whatever I did in the next stage of my life had to make sense,” he added when asked about his return to Juventus.

According to recent reports, “I was looking for something that would inspire me. When this opportunity came along, I felt that. It had a different meaning. Deep down, I felt my chapter with Juve was still open.

“I’m very happy and satisfied. I enjoy my work, and whatever comes in the future is in God’s hands. I’m here to help, and whatever happens will happen.

In a recent development, “We’re in a place where, wherever you look, you see so many trophies. Whether we talk about it or not, there’s no need to spell it out: we know where we are and what we represent. I’m here to try to achieve great results, and I sense the same ambition around me. Hopefully we can get there. Just like the other teams, we want to win. That’s what we’re fighting for.”.

Juventus sit fifth in the Europa League standings with ten points in four matches, but have had very different results in Serie A, where they have already lost three games from 12 and sit 6th in the table, six points behind Milan in four.

Neto, however, has plenty of confidence in his teammates.

According to recent reports, “Seeing and feeling the desire these lads have, with so many young players willing to contribute, is truly infectious. From my perspective, that’s a positive thing,” the 37-year-old remarked.

“There are important players here. We’re talking about Juve. We have the team, the desire and the determination, and hopefully we can get there.

“Every competition is difficult these days. We are Juventus, and whenever Juventus play, we have a responsibility to compete to win. That’s our goal. I haven’t looked at what lies ahead, because at this club you have to fight to win. That’s how it is for me, and I imagine it’s the same for everyone. It has been revealed that “This is a developing team with lots of young players who may be playing in this league for the first time and are still finding their feet. There can be ups and downs. The season is so long that we can’t focus solely on this moment, because in a month we might be talking about something else. My focus is always on giving 100% in training. We need to find balance and consistency.”.

Omonia FC have grabbed two wins and two defeats in four Europa League games so far and sit 18th in the table.

It has been revealed that “It will be a difficult match. They’re a very strong team,” insisted Neto.

“We’ve watched them, and they counterattack with real quality. I know Fabiano from our time in Brazil. Every game in Europe is difficult, and we know the challenges involved.”.

Juventus’ first-choice goalkeeper Vicario will rest tomorrow. He’s on loan from Tottenham, but does he deserve to remain at Juventus long-term?

“I don’t know how much my opinion matters, but he’s a top-level goalkeeper,” Neto concluded.

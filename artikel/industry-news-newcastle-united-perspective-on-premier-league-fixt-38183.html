I don’t think Newcastle United will finish above Man City (at least not until they are relegated for breaching the financial rules), so I suppose I want Fulham to lose.

Sunderland got huge luck with the penalty decisions in their last match. Their first win since August as they then bullied Chelsea and won 1-0.

The Mackems don’t have head bully Brobbey today due to injury and I don’t think it would have mattered anyway.

I can only see defeat for them here, against a Villa side unbeaten in their last six league matches.

Sunderland had picked up only one point from six games before fluking that win against Chelsea, defeat today will leave them in the relegation zone.

Forest had picked up only one point in five games before that win over Man City.

Recent findings suggest that A Forest revival after climbing out of the bottom three? Not today, Bournemouth have won their last eight games in all competitions and they will win today.

Which will ensure Forest stay below Newcastle.

Leeds had drawn five of their last seven EPL matches, winning one and drawing one, before scrapping to a 1-0 victory over Spurs last time out.

In their last three EPL matches Chelsea have lost to Spurs and Sunderland, plus a win against a poor Man U side.

I am predicting a draw and think the ridiculously media-hyped Chelsea side will be getting a bit bullied once again by a physical Leeds side.

Fourth bottom plays bottom. Not a match that many neutrals will be desperate to watch.

Thomas Frank will be desperate to not get beat in his first Palace match and I think a point each.

Before losing to in form Bournemouth last time, Ipswich had won two, lost two, drawn two in their previous six.

Spurs had won against Coventry, Chelsea and Palace without really convincing, then lost at Leeds.

I am not convinced Tottenham have really turned the corner and I am backing Ipswich to get at least a point.

The only match that really matters today. If offered now, I would take a point!

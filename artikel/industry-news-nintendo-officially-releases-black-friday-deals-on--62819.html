Nintendo has officially debuted its Black Friday deals for 2026, giving frugal holiday shoppers an opportunity to get a pair of Nintendo Switch 2 system bundles at a discount. Nintendo is also putting select digital and physical games for Nintendo Switch and Switch 2 on sale, with discounts on games such as Super Mario Party Jamboree and Metroid Prime 4: Beyond.

On the hardware side, Nintendo's Black Friday Switch 2 bundles essentially roll the console's bestselling launch option back to 2025 prices. (Related: Latest Update: Save $2,400 on a tricked-out Corsair RTX...)

It has been revealed that nintendo is selling a discounted Switch 2 bundle as part of its Black Friday deals, which will be available from Nov. 22 through Dec. 5. The Nintendo Switch 2 with Mario Kart World Bundle includes a Nintendo Switch 2 system, a full download of Mario Kart World, and a 3-month (90-day) Nintendo Switch Online Individual Membership for $499.99. That's a savings of $50 off the suggested retail price. (Related: Get 32GB of DDR5 RAM for $111 in this premium AM5 gamin...)

In a recent development, alternatively, Nintendo is right now selling a Nintendo Switch 2 with Nintendo Switch Sports Resort Bundle. It includes a Nintendo Switch 2 system, a full download of Nintendo Switch Sports Resort, and a 3-month (90-day) Nintendo Switch Online Individual Membership for $529.99. That's a savings of $27.98 off the suggested retail price.

Nintendo will offer discounts on select digital games from Nov. 19 through Dec. 2 at 11:59 p.m. PST, via the Nintendo Store website, Nintendo Store app, and Nintendo eShop. On Black Friday proper, which lands on Nov. 22, select retailers including Amazon, Best Buy, GameStop, Target, Walmart, and both Nintendo retail stores in the U.S. will have deals on physical Nintendo Switch 2 and Nintendo Switch games.

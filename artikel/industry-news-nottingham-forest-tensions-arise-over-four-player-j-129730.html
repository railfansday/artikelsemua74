Evangelos Marinakis is no stranger to having problems with Nottingham Forest managers at the City Ground.

Nuno Espirito Santo, Ange Postecoglou, Sean Dyche, Vitor Pereira all came and went from the dugout in the space of just a few months. (Related: NASA’s new AI space chip could let spacecraft think for...)

Oliver Glasner is having far more luck at keeping the boss' emotions in check so far this season, even after an incredibly difficult run of results.

The Reds are still 17th in the Premier League, but the recent 3-2 win over Manchester City has certainly bought him some more time at the City Ground.

Attention is already turning to the January transfer window, and Glasner may need multiple reinforcements if Forest are to avoid relegation. (Related: Rockstar is turning GTA 6’s in-game trading cards into ...)

Glasner's side spent £116million during the summer on the likes of Liam Delap, Daniel Munoz and Ousmane Diomande, as well as free transfers.

The former Crystal Palace boss is clearly heavily involved in transfers at the City Ground, but not everyone is on the same page, sources have told Football Insider.

A upcoming goalkeeper is understood to be at the top of the list, as well as a left-wing-back, central midfielder and an attacker to play alongside Morgan Gibbs-White and Delap.

Chief football officer George Syrianos has a completely different model to the manager, though, which favours signing younger players, who can be sold on for profit in the future.

This could cause a huge divide at the City Ground, especially when signings in the January window need to make an instant impact.

Right now, Glasner needs players who can fire Forest up the Premier League table, rather than copy Newcastle United's tactics of prioritising high-potential signings.

The 3-2 win over Man City looked like a turning point, but any hope came crashing back down against Bournemouth last weekend.

Now, according to the bookies, Glasner is the favourite Premier League manager to be sacked next after Michael Carrick and Pierre Sage.

His recent outburst at players being unable to speak English has not helped matters at all, but many supporters are fans of his no-nonsense approach.

Mick Brown told Football Insider that Forest have "lingering doubts" over the manager's future, and he desperately needs to change that in the coming weeks.

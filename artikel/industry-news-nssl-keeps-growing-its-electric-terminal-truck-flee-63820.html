Industry observers note that after taking delivery of its first Orange EV e-Triever electric yard tractor in 2024, NSSL saw the electric light. The firm has been moving quickly to transition its yard tractor fleet to zero emissions, and its efforts haven’t gone unnoticed: NSSL is now being recognized by the 2026 Canadian Clean Fleet Awards.

Toronto’s National Shunt Service, Ltd. (NSSL) added its first electric terminal truck to its trailer-moving operations back in 2024 – and they were impressed enough to quickly start buying more, putting their Orange e-Triever and Husk-e trucks to work at customers’ warehouses, distribution centers, and factories. The company has 42 electric trucks working at a number of sites in Canada (up from 27 in June), with looks to have 50 by the end of Q1 2027. The organization’s commitment to decarbonization recently made them a 2026 Canadian Clean Fleets Award winner, recognizing their growing role in normalizing battery-electric, zero-emission commercial trucks across Canada.

NSSL used Geotab telematics to track pertinent data of each vehicle, giving what Fitzgerald called, “an essential overview of the entire fleet,” that enabled the company to compare efficiencies and cost, as well as the ability to plan ahead.

“The data allows us to identify potential maintenance complications before they result in an outage,” he explains. “Just as importantly, it gives us the analytics to understand idle time, fuel and energy consumption, utilization, and overall operating efficiency.”.

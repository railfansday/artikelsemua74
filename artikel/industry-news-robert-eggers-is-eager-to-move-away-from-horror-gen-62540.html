From witches to vampires and werewolves, Robert Eggers has had his fill of the horror genre after more than a decade.

The Werwulf co-writer and director recently explained that he’s ready to break free from “Robert Eggers’ House of Horror” as he hopes to explore other genres with his next project, following Werwulf‘s Christmas premiere in theaters.

“I’m eager not to continue purposely doing Robert Eggers’ House of Horror,” Eggers said during a recent Q&A. “I don’t need to do the Mummy or The Opera. Not to say that I won’t, but I think I need a break.”. (Related: Think AI "knows" what it’s doing? Scientists say think ...)

Recent findings suggest that “I was doing something else,” noted Eggers. “But what happened was, after Werwulf, I was writing another horror movie, and it got so dark again, and I was like, ‘I need a break. So, I’ll do something about two teenagers killing themselves.'”.

Following his 2015 feature debut The Witch, Eggers has been praised as a newly introduced master of horror with films like The Lighthouse (2019), Nosferatu (2024) and Werwulf, premiering Dec. 25 from Focus Features.

Sources indicate that in Werwulf, a man (Aaron Taylor-Johnson) in 14th century England finds him and his family threatened by a ravenous beast haunting the town. The film also stars Lily-Rose Depp, Willem Dafoe, Ralph Ineson and more.

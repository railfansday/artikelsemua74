Rockstar Games has revealed why players can find chainsaws in GTA 6 but can’t use them as weapons, with producer Rob Nelson saying the studio felt they’d be “too much” for the game.

Both the chainsaw and katana were famously usable in Vice City and San Andreas, letting fans slice and dice their foes for some brutal yet fun takedowns. While Jason and Lucia can spot chainsaws across Leonida, neither can actually pick them up and wield them. (Related: 103-year-old grandmother competing in Tekken 8 tourname...)

Speaking to VGC, co-studio head at Rockstar North Rob Nelson mentioned the decision came down to how chainsaw violence would look in GTA 6 and whether it fit the game’s two protagonists. (Related: Rockstar explains why GTA 6 added its Criminal Profile ...)

Sources indicate that “As far as the chainsaw, I think it would have been too much for this game at this resolution and these characters. It’s not a resource thing, noted Nelson.

“Even at the most psycho, did Jason and Lucia really need that, what’s that going to look like?”.

Sources indicate that after all, GTA 6 is the most hyper-realistic open-world game Rockstar has created to date. Its fictional world of Florida is absolutely crammed with environmental details, wildlife, and NPCs all going about their business.

Nelson mentioned Rockstar wanted players to interact with as much of the world as possible, but had to weigh what each of those interactions would add to the game.

“You’re always trying to find what is the right sort of line and the right balance, it really just depends, you want to feel like you can interact with as much of the world as possible.”.

Sources indicate that that same question of what fits Jason and Lucia came up when Nelson explained why Rockstar added GTA 6’s Criminal Profile system. As previously reported, Rockstar wanted what players do in the open world to better reflect how the pair are portrayed in the story, while giving players more ways to handle everyday encounters.

Sources indicate that for example, players can rob someone at gunpoint and give them a chance to hand over their money. Meanwhile, repeated acts of extreme violence can permanently lock a character’s Criminal Profile at Irredeemable, which drastically impacts how aggressively police respond to your actions.

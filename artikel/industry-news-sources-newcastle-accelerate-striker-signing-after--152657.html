Newcastle United are admirers of Dinamo Zagreb striker Dion Beljo amid plans to sign a striker in the next transfer window, sources have told Football Insider.

Beljo has been in fine form for Zagreb over the last 18 months, carrying his impressive scoring record from last season into the current campaign.

The 24-year-old bagged 38 goals in 48 appearances across all competitions last term, and has 19 in 22 so far in 2026-27.

He recently netted a fourth Europa League strike of the season in Zagreb's 2-0 win at Sunderland.

Newcastle scouts watched him on Wearside, and are continuing to track his services ahead of January, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast.

Matthias Jaissle wants to strengthen his attacking options for the second half of the campaign as his current forwards falter in front of goal. (Related: Latest Update: Uber Eats driver saves elderly woman aft...)

None of the Magpies' strikers have scored more than one goal in the Premier League so far in this campaign.

Beljo's outstanding form has seen him work his way back into the Croatian national team, and he scored his first international goal away to Spain in September.

Newcastle are not the only top European club watching the 24-year-old, so could face a real battle for his services in January. (Related: This newly introduced chip survives 1300°F (700°C) and ...)

Sources indicate that speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "There’s obviously a few players who I’m sure the Newcastle hierarchy are considering. Dion Beljo of Dinamo Zagreb is one player on the radar for them.

"They sent scouts to watch him recently in the Europa League when Zagreb were playing Sunderland, and he scored in that game as well. He’s a player that would tick a lot of boxes for Newcastle at 24 years old, he has a huge ceiling as well and so would fit the criteria for Newcastle's current recruitment policy.".

Newcastle may well believe they have a real chance of signing Beljo, especially considering his previous comments about the club.

Speaking in a 2022 interview while playing for Augsburg, the striker revealed that he was a supporter of the Tyneside outfit.

He remarked: "The Premier League is the best league in the world. Of course, there are Bayern, Paris and Real in the other leagues. But in England, there are so many teams with a lot of quality. The football there is very intense, but mainly I watch the games because I play a virtual manager game with my friends. So, I have to keep an eye on everything there. I really enjoy that.

"Most recently, Manchester United were my favourite team because of Cristiano Ronaldo, but there is another team that will surely surprise you…Newcastle United. When I was a kid, I watched the film ‘Goal!’ and the player in that film fulfilled his dream of a professional career at Newcastle. I think Newcastle will be even better in real life because of their financial power and their incredible fans.”.

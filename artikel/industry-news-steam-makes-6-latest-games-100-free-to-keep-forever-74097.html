Whilst everybody and their dog may at the moment be playing Grand Theft Auto VI after waiting 13 long years, there are plenty of gamers who have no interest in diving into the latest Rockstar release. It is those lucky ones who can now head over to Steam and enjoy six free games that can be downloaded with not a single catch. If you like the look of it, you can download and play it. Simple.

So without further ado, let us take a look at six of the newest free games to head to the PC storefront.

First up is ARES: THE IRON VANGUARD, a sci-fi action RPG which allows players to switch combat suits and classes in real time. By mastering parries and dodges, and nailing combos, you will be able to embark in battles with towering enemies as you explore vast battlefields alongside fellow Guardians.

Next is Celestial Decks, which is perfect for the trading card game fans out there. Also released on 18 November, this competitive card game will see experience worth its wait in gold as you weave together a tale courtesy of beautifully illustrated cards. Whether you enjoy PvE or PvP combat, you will need to uncover powerful synergies in order to claim victory, and luckily, the rules are pretty minimal: simply fill your deck with any 15 cards, using as many copies of a card as you like. If you are not into card games, you can always check out The Reverend: A Happyhills Homicide Story. In this pixel graphic thriller, players will need to uncover the dark secrets of Reverend Lawrence. Prior to his death, The Reverend was a devout Christian, and was the savior to the residents of Happyhills. However, it seems as though he may have had some skeletons in his closet after all. It will be up to you to discover the manner of his death, and most importantly, the secrets he kept in life.

In a recent development, in a slightly more optimistic-sounding game, Youth Center Simulator tasks players with building and running their very own youth center. That means that it will be up to you to hire staff, develop facilities, write and join various programs, and grow your organization. Starting with nothing, you will need to build your franchise, and gradually turn an empty building into a flourishing center for the youth.

In a recent development, 687 Days on Mars - First Steps is an open-world survival crafting game which will see you take your very first steps on Mars. As you face the challenge alone, you will need to build your first shelter, secure vital resources, grow food, research new technologies, and explore the hostile Martian landscape. However, the Red Planet is no walk in the park, and you will have to utilize all of your abilities and materials in order to stand a chance of survival.

Sources indicate that last, but by no means least, is Mailbound, a psychological horror game where you will need to explore, contain and even feed a cursed PC. With what lives inside the PC leaking into the very world you inhabit, you will need to uncover a conspiracy and amend your past mistakes in this narrative-driven horror experience.

All of these games, and hundreds more, are now free to download and enjoy courtesy of Steam, so do not miss out on potentially discovering an underrated gem.

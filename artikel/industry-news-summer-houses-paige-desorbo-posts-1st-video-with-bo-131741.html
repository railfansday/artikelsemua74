Paige DeSorbo has gone social media official with boyfriend, Joe D’Amelio, by referencing one of the most famous mob movies of all-time.

The Summer House alum, 34, debuted D’Amelio, 37, via her TikTok and Instagram Stories on Wednesday, November 25, posting a fun clip of her loved ones making homemade pizza. (Related: Defeat Ragdolls for Powers codes (November 2026)...)

As the camera panned across the people taking part in the pizza party, D’Amelio was one of the figures introduced in the sweet clip as part of her crew. (Related: Costco’s $40 last-minute Thanksgiving Dinner Kit comes ...)

For the post’s accompanying audio, DeSorbo used a clip of a voiceover taken from Martin Scorsese‘s classic 1990 film Goodfellas, where lead character Henry Hill (played by late actor Ray Liotta) introduces his crew.

“There was Jimmy, and Tommy, and me. And there was Anthony Stabile, Frankie Carbone,” Liotta can be heard saying as Hill during the famous quote. “And then there was Mo Black’s brother, Fat Andy and his guys, Frankie the Wop and Freddy No Nose. And then there was Pete the Killer, who was Sally Balls’ brother. Then you had Nickey Eyes and Mikey Franzese.”.

Us confirmed in July 2025 that DeSorbo had moved on following her 2024 split from Southern Charm star Craig Conover and was “quietly dating” D’Amelio, a sports marketing executive.

“Things between them are going really well,” a source told Us at the time. “It took a few months for Paige to warm up to being in another relationship again, but she moved on after Craig and is genuinely happy.”.

The pair have kept relatively tight-lipped about their budding romance and have kept their private life together mostly off social media until now.

Speaking exclusively to Us in February, DeSorbo confirmed that her relationship with D’Amelio was still going strong.

Recent findings suggest that “We’re exclusive,” DeSorbo said at the time. “I’m in my 30s, so it’s not like I’m out every single weekend going out on multiple dates like I would have been in my 20s.”.

The former reality star also described their connection as “really fun” and “very relaxing.”.

Although she is no stranger to the public eye thanks to her past involvement with Summer House from 2019 to 2025 and previously being part of a public relationship, DeSorbo explained she was opting for a different approach this time around.

Industry observers note that “It was such a balancing act,” she recalled to Us at the time. “I feel like when I was on reality TV — I started when I was 25 — it feels very, like, that was my 20s. Obviously, I’m in charge of my own decisions and stuff [now]. So it’s nice to be able to decide what I share and what I don’t [with] personal stuff.”.

DeSorbo announced her departure from Summer House in 2025, months after confirming on her “Giggly Squad” podcast that she and Conover had split.

“Craig and I have decided to no longer be together. I love him, I think he loves me. I think we will remain friends,” she stated in December 2024. “No one did anything. It wasn’t a bad thing. I think we both were just being really mature and saying what we want and what we didn’t want, and I think that’s extremely powerful to be able to voice how you’re feeling in real time and what you want for your future.”.

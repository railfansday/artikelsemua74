It has been revealed that thanksgiving may be over, but one of the holiday’s biggest traditions is still to come. For the first time in its history, the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade is taking place on a day other than Thanksgiving. On Wednesday, Macy’s, NBC, and New York City officials made the unprecedented decision to postpone the parade until Saturday, November 28, after forecasts called for dangerous winds, heavy rain, and coastal flooding. While the parade was canceled from 1942 through 1944 during World War II, weather has never before forced organizers to move the event to another day. The timing is particularly noteworthy given that this year marks the parade’s 100th edition, a milestone Macy’s is celebrating with an expanded lineup of balloons, floats, musical performances, and celebrity appearances. NBC aired a replay of the 2025 parade on Thanksgiving morning instead. The live celebration will take place Saturday, although the last-minute schedule change has forced some performers to adjust their prepares. Here’s what to expect from the rescheduled parade and how to watch it. Who is performing at the 2026 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade? Savannah Guthrie, Hoda Kotb, and Al Roker are slated to return as hosts of NBC’s broadcast. The originally confirmed musical lineup includes RAYE, Teddy Swims, Megan Moroney, Natasha Bedingfield, Queen Latifah, Jimmy Fallon and The Roots, JoJo, Smash Mouth, and Keke Palmer, among others. A special Broadway performance featuring a 10-minute medley from nine musicals, including Hamilton, Wicked, and Rent, is still expected to take place. The Radio City Rockettes are also on the announced lineup. However, not everyone who planned to participate on Thursday will be able to stick around for Saturday. The University of Alabama’s Million Dollar Band, for instance, is returning home to perform at Saturday’s Iron Bowl football game. Macy’s Great American Marching Band is also unable to participate in the rescheduled event. Other groups have turned to crowdfunding to cover the unexpected cost of extending their stays in New York. A high school marching band from Pearland, Texas, raised $350,000 in less than 24 hours to enable more than 500 students and staff members to stay for the parade. How to watch the 2026 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade The rescheduled parade will step off at 8:30 a.m. ET on Saturday, November 28, and is expected to conclude around noon. NBC will televise the parade, while cord-cutters can stream it on Peacock. A Spanish-language broadcast will also be available on Telemundo. Viewers with live TV streaming subscriptions that carry NBC, including YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV, and Fubo, can also tune in. If you’d rather sleep in on Saturday morning, NBC is scheduled to air an encore presentation at 2 p.m. ET/PT.

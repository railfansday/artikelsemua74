Industry observers note that what does “Michael Caine” sound like in the freezing cold? We’re about to find out with the just-revealed release of “The Trip to the Northern Lights,” which is the reunion of Steve Coogan, Rob Brydon, and director Michael Winterbottom for what is the fifth installment of “The Trip” franchise of miniseries/movies.

Independent Film Company and Sapan Studio picked up the U.S. rights to the fifth “Trip” film (IFC has released all four of the other films into theaters in the U.S.) and will release it theatrically on April 9, 2027. It will have been nearly seven years since the release of the last feature, and it sees Brydon now at 60 and Coogan at 59, so we’re guessing their ages may have made their wit and appetites even more ornery since. “The Trip” movies are known for their food and travel porn as much as they are for their litany of impressions between Coogan and Brydon playing versions of themselves, but this one turns that concept on its head slightly by sending Coogan and Brydon up north, this time throughout Scandinavia, including Sweden and some islands in Norway, all in search of some other fine food and wry conversation. According to recent reports, the first look at the movie above sees the duo in ridiculously huge parkas in the snow as guided by some sled huskies. Try doing your best Bane impression at 10 below zero.

“We are very happy to be back on the road with IFC. They have been brilliant partners ever since the first ‘Trip’ all those years ago. ‘The Trip to the Northern Lights‘ takes Steve and Rob from Malmo in southern Sweden through Norway’s spectacular fjords to the Lofoten Islands in Norway’s Arctic circle,” Winterbottom mentioned. “I hope the audience enjoys the journey as much as we did.”.

In a recent development, “The Trip” franchise began all the way back in 2011, and since then the films have spanned trips in England, Italy, Spain, and Greece. As has been the case with all of the installments, they’ve been miniseries airing on TV in England but then edited into films for the U.S. release. The first movie made $3.9 million worldwide across several re-releases, “The Trip to Italy” in 2014 made $6.1 million, 2017’s “The Trip to Spain” made $1.9 million, and “The Trip to Greece” in the COVID-addled summer 2020 still managed to make $259K worldwide.

All four of the “Trip” movies are currently available for streaming on Sun Now, which is the streamer owned by Independent Film Company parent AMC Global Media.

In a recent development, “The Independent Film Manufacturer is thrilled to continue our longstanding relationship with this world-class group of filmmakers on the latest installment of this beloved franchise. Few comedic partnerships have the enduring appeal of Steve Coogan and Rob Brydon, and under Michael Winterbottom’s inspired direction, each newly introduced trip remains the comedic gift that keeps on giving. This time around, we can’t wait for audiences to join us on the road to the Northern Lights,” said Ayo Kepher-Maat, Independent Film Company’s Vice President of Acquisitions and Production.

Industry observers note that “Michael Winterbottom, Steve Coogan, and Rob Brydon have created a franchise filled with taste, wit and belly laughs. Independent Film Firm has brought each one to audiences with characteristic care and capability. ‘The Trip to the Northern Lights’ advances the history with the delight of something completely fresh and tasty,” said Josh Sapan of Sapan Studio.

The film is produced by Melissa Parmenter and Josh Hyams for Revolution Films. The deal was negotiated by Ayo Kepher-Maat on behalf of Independent Film Organization and Tristan Whalley MD of Goalpost Films on behalf of the filmmakers.

Industry observers note that executive producers are Winterbottom, Coogan, Brydon, Alex Moody, Josh Sapan, Tristan Whalley, Paul Wiegard. “The Trip to the Northern Lights” is a Revolution Films, Baby Cow, and Small Man Production in association with Sky Studios.

Deadline first reported the news of the acquisition.

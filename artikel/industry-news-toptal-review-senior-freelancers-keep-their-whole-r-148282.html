📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, apply to Toptal if you’re an experienced developer, designer, or finance expert who wants clients matched to you at the rate you set, since Toptal takes no cut of it. Screening takes 3 to 8 weeks, and Toptal notes it accepts fewer than 3% of applicants, so keep your current clients while you apply.

Recent findings suggest that toptal screens freelancers in fields from software and design to finance and marketing, then matches them with companies from startups to the Fortune 500. Its FAQ gives no typical rate to price yourself against, though its support team can help you find a competitive one.

It has been revealed that experienced developers, designers, and finance experts who want clients brought to them keep every dollar of their rate here. Freelancers with less than two years of experience should start on Upwork, sending proposals to win contracts, and build the record Toptal looks for.

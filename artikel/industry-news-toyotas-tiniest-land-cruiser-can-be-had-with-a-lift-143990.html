In a recent development, much like the Suzuki Jimny before it, the Toyota Land Cruiser FJ is a lovable little off-roader that combines cartoonishly tiny proportions with an actual body-on-frame design. Unfortunately for us American enthusiasts, the Land Cruiser FJ also follows the Jimny in not actually being available Stateside. That makes it all the more frustrating to learn that Japanese buyers can get their hands on an FJ with an aftermarket lift for under $32,000, as brought to broader attention by Motor1.

According to recent reports, that price comes from Kuhl Racing, a provider of body kits and other customization elements for a wide variety of cars. The company is imagining all kinds of ways to transform the Land Cruiser FJ, including lift kits, superchargers, and clearance-focused bumpers that should increase approach and departure angles.

Recent findings suggest that the most interesting offering is a complete package combining the lift kit with 18-inch wheels and all-terrain tires for just under 5 million yen, or about $31,500 USD. That comes out to more than $10,000 below the American starting price of the most basic possible Toyota 4Runner. The intricacies of the global auto market mean that prices cannot really be compared apples-to-apples across two different markets, but the lifted FJ would still likely come in somewhere below Toyota’s current range of mid-sized off-roaders in America. Toyota has not announced any intends to sell the Land Cruiser FJ in America, so the idea of actually buying one of these and installing one of these kits remains just a dream for now. With the Suzuki Jimny similarly unavailable, American buyers looking for the smallest possible off-roader will have to settle for a two-door Jeep Wrangler or Ford Bronco.

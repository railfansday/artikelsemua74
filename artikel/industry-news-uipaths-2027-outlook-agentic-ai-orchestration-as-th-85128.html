The artificial intelligence boom produced a number of AI-focused companies, but UiPath is one that stands out from the pack. Its long-term prospects look promising thanks to the vision of founder Daniel Dines, who has evolved the company's platform from its roots as a robotic process automation tool to infusing RPA with AI, and now moving toward agentic orchestration.

According to recent reports, agentic orchestration involves using AI to coordinate, control, and optimize AI agents, RPA robots, and people across end-to-end business workflows, such as invoice processing and loan applications. Because UiPath has decades of data from its RPA origins, it is well positioned to have AI coordinate and execute key business processes with accuracy and within a company's guidelines. This gives it a competitive advantage over rivals lacking this data depth.

In a recent development, moreover, UiPath continues to strengthen its financial health. It reached profitability in its 2026 fiscal year, and expects to improve in fiscal 2027 with an operating margin of 7% in the first half compared to 4% in the prior year. Management is also forecasting adjusted free cash flow of $425 million in fiscal 2027 compared to $372 million in 2026. Now, as long as UiPath can continue to grow its customer sales, it is well positioned for success over the long haul.

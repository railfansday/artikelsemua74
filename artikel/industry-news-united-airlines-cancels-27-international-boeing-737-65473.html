Industry News: United Airlines Cancels 27 International Boeing 737 MAX Routes: See Full List Now

United Airlines is one of the world's top operators of narrowbody twinjets from the Boeing 737 MAX family, with fleet data from ch-aviation showing it at the moment has 123 737 MAX 8s and 188 737 MAX 9s. Going forward, the Chicago-based US 'significant three' legacy carrier and Star Alliance founding member is set to receive 35 more 737 MAX 9s as its mainline fleet modernizes.

Recent findings suggest that however, as times change for United's fleet, the same is true for its network. When we compare the airline's 2027 schedules with those for 2026 (per data from Cirium, an aviation analytics manufacturer), we can see that it has taken the MAX off no fewer than 27 international routes. These are spread across six hubs: two on the East Coast, two on the West Coast, and two in the center of the US. (Related: This fresh chip survives 1300°F (700°C) and could chang...)

Starting on the East Coast, Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) has got off relatively lightly, with the MAX being removed from just two international routes. One of these serves Piarco International Airport (POS) in Trinidad, while the other links Newark with Vancouver International Airport (YVR) in Canada. This facility has also lost United MAX flights from another East Coast hub: Washington Dulles International Airport (IAD). (Related: AI breakthrough cuts energy use by 100x while boosting ...)

United Airlines' East Coast Hubs.

It has been revealed that as it happens, Vancouver is one of three Canadian destinations to have lost United MAX flights from Dulles, with the others being Calgary International Airport (YYC) and Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ). Elsewhere on the international front, 2027 will see the carrier stop flying its narrowbody twinjets from the 737 MAX series to Barbados, Liberia, or Nassau in the Caribbean.

Moving west to United's two main hubs in the center of the US, cuts have also been made to the airline's foreign 737 MAX flights from Chicago O'Hare International Airport (ORD) and George Bush Intercontinental Airport (IAD). Canada remains a common theme, with O'Hare losing United MAX flights to Montreal Trudeau International Airport (YUL) and, again, Vancouver.

These represent two of the four cuts in Chicago, with the others being Philip S.W. Goldson International Airport (BZE) in Belize City and the aforementioned Nassau. As for Houston, this is the airport where United has removed the MAX from the most routes, with eight corridors in total. Once again, two of the destinations are Canadian: Calgary and YYZ.

In a recent development, as for the rest, three Mexican destinations are also included: Cozumel, San Jose Del Cabo, and Puerto Vallarta. The remaining trio are Caribbean destinations: Flamingo International Airport (BON) in Kralendijk on Bonaire, Owen Roberts International Airport (GCM) near George Town on Grand Cayman, and Santo Domingo Airport (SDQ) in the Dominican Republic.

According to recent reports, united's two main West Coast hubs are both located in California. They are San Francisco International Airport (SFO) to the north and Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) to the south. Each will see the MAX removed from three international routes in 2027, with LAX losing flights to Puerto Vallarta, Toronto Pearson, and Vancouver.

It has been revealed that vancouver will also disappear from United's MAX roster in San Francisco, with the other routes that are set to meet this fate serving Calgary and Tocumen International Airport (PTY) in Panama City. With that being stated, United will still retain considerable MAX networks from both of its West Coast hubs. In the 12 months to October 2027, it has planned 26,868 MAX flights from SFO and 11,132 from LAX.

According to recent reports, united Airlines' use of the 737 MAX 8 and 9 is defined by a two-class setup with reasonable premium capacity. According to the airline, its MAX 9s have 20 domestic first/business class recliners and 159 economy seats on board (45/48 with extra legroom). Meanwhile, the smaller MAX 8 seats 16 domestic first/business class and 150 economy passengers (54 with extra legroom).

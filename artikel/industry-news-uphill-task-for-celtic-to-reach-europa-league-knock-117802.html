In a recent development, celtic make the trip to the Estadio Dr. Magalhaes Pessoa on Thursday to take on Portuguese second-tier side Torreense, and know a win is a must in terms of qualification.

The Scottish Premiership champions are 32nd in the Europa League standings after four matches, but they did beat Besiktas in their last match in the competition.

Including qualifiers, Celtic have lost each of their last five away matches against Portuguese teams, conceding 13 goals.

Their last away win there came in April 2003, a 1-0 win at Boavista in O'Neill's first spell in charge, and the Celtic boss acknowledged the task ahead of his team in Europe.

"I think it is uphill in many aspects," he said. "I think you reminded me before that no team that has lost their first three games has qualified for the knockouts of the competition.

"But that doesn't mean we can't do it. Of course, there's a very difficult game on Thursday – we're expecting that anyway.

"But I think the result against Besiktas gave us a bit of confidence, and it gave us a starting platform, so let's see what develops over the next few matches.".

Since the start of the 2020-21 season, Celtic have conceded more goals in major European competition than any other side (116), with the Bhoys shipping 2+ goals in all four games in 2026-27.

Portuguese teams are also unbeaten in their last five home Europa League matches against Scottish opposition (W3 D2) since a 1-0 win for Rangers at Braga in February 2020.

Torreense have started their campaign strongly, winning two of their first four games, though they come into the clash on the back of a defeat to Benfica.

Benfica won 3-1 in their Taca de Portugal clash, the competition Torreense won last season to secure their spot in the Europa League, and O'Neill warned his side against becoming complacent against their opponents.

"We're not good enough to underestimate anyone," O'Neill conceded.

"They beat Sporting [CP] in the final to get here, a major achievement, a fantastic achievement in all honesty.

Sources indicate that "Sporting are in the Champions League, which might tell you the task ahead. So no, we're not treating the game lightly at all; we're not good enough to do that, and even if we were, we wouldn't treat it like that.".

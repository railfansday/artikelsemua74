Manchester United goalkeeper Senne Lammens went from hero to zero in a matter of minutes during a chaotic first half against Sporting CP in the UEFA Champions League.

With the match ongoing and approaching half-time in Lisbon, Sporting at the moment leading 1–0, Mauricio Pochettino’s side endured an extraordinary four-minute spell of self-inflicted madness.

The drama began in the 29th minute when Sporting winger Geny Catamo controlled a crossfield pass on the right side of the penalty area, shifted the ball onto his left foot, and drew a clumsy challenge from Manchester United defender Lisandro Martínez.

Referee awarded a spot-kick, giving Sporting striker Luis Suárez a golden opportunity to break the deadlock.

However, Lammens guessed correctly, diving low to his left to smother Suárez’s weak effort and preserve the scoreline.

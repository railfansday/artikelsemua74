Recent findings suggest that that wasn’t the only thing to take from the game, though. After not being included in the squad for the first game, Alphadjo Cisse was named on the bench by Roberto Mancini and he made an impact when the Italian gave him a chance to.

Industry observers note that coming on in the 62nd minute – with the Azzurri 3-1 down – it was hoped that he could provide a spark for the visitors to get back into the game and he certainly did.

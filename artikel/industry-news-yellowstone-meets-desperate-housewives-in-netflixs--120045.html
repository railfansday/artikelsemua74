It has been revealed that of all the things Sophie O’Neil (Brittany Snow) could have packed for her first night out in East Texas, the one that might have spared her a great deal of trouble was a sanitary pad. When a stranger asks her for one in the bathroom at an NRA fundraiser, Sophie offers a tampon, the stranger declines, and the two discover something they can agree on in Sophie’s purse. By the time they’ve each taken a Xanax, Sophie has made her first friend in Maple Brook. She’s also about to discover that the woman who just got half undressed in front of her is the wife of her husband’s boss.

According to recent reports, in these days of instant access to everything, fame is sudden and success is apparently supposed to happen overnight. Criticism of West Ham’s team and their mid-table mediocrity is often swift and harsh. Even players who arrived in the summer are already being written off, according to supporters and news sites.

Which seems especially harsh considering the age and trajectory of several key players in this West Ham side.

How many ‘West Ham legends’ of the past would have been discarded after their first dozen games, I wonder, if they’d not been an immediate sensation.

Bad enough that so-called ‘fans’ took to booing Scott Parker’s very first home game at 90 minutes: Now it seems one of the youngest talents from the summer intake is already being labelled as a  ‘flop’. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

That’s the flavour of a report out this evening from givemesport.com who claim that:.

As they take aim at ..Edwin Austbø who, at just twenty one, has hardly had a chance to get settled in east London before he’s being labelled a’ substantial money’ (eh?) flop, despite his transfer fee being a modest £11 million. Perspective being everything, that’s about a quarter of a Kilman who cost £40 million and would surely be most people’s choice for the ‘big money flop’ label. (Related: Breaking: Simple considerations for simple people build...)

Industry observers note that A flop? After three months? With two goals and two assists at just 21 years of age, I’d say Austbo is well on track to grow into the team.

“Expectations were also high that summer signing Austbo could prove to be an exciting addition in the second tier this term….He has two goals and two assists for the Irons so far this term, but you would probably expect his goal contribution numbers to be higher in a team which has scored 38 league goals…..

As things stand, Austbo is rapidly becoming a significant-money transfer flop, and he needs to improve quickly to start repaying his fee.”.

It has been revealed that steady on, guys, This young man has huge talent, was not outrageously expensive and has already produced more goals and assists than players who cost twice his transfer fee. At least wait until the end of the season – his first in the English leagues – before writing him off.

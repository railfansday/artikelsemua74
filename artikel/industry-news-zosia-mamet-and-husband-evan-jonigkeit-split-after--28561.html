Zosia Mamet and her husband, Evan Jonigkeit, have reportedly called it quits.

The estranged couple were married 10 years prior to their split, according to People, which was first to report the news of their separation. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Us has reached out to reps for Mamet and Jonigkeit for comment.

Mamet, 38, and Jonigkeit, 43, met while costarring in an off-Broadway play called Really, Really.

Us confirmed that the Girls star and the Hunting Wives actor dated for three years before they got married in October 2016 at Cedar Lakes Estate in Port Jervis, New York.

“@evanj550 my best friend, my soulmate, my HUSBAND, I love you,” Mamet wrote via Instagram at the time, confirming their nuptials. “Here’s to forever and a day.”.

Jonigkeit gushed about his spouse four years later, sharing an Instagram tribute on their anniversary.

“4 years and counting,” the Sweetbitter alum wrote in October 2020. “Happy anniversary @zosiamamet you’re the best thing that’s happened to me. As we continue to live a charmed life and sometimes weather storms together, you are my safe shelter.”.

Mamet later reflected on her relationship with Jonigkeit during a 2024 appearance on Today With Hoda and Jenna, revealing that the pair initially kept their romance secret from their castmates while getting better acquainted.

She recalled thinking “it was something that we felt like was probably going to last and be serious, so we were like, ‘Let’s keep it a secret so it can just be for us.’”.

The secret didn’t last long, because their off-Broadway pals picked up on the chemistry between them.

“Meanwhile, everyone was like, ‘You guys are in love with each other.’ And we had to really be like, ‘That is so unprofessional! We would… OK, maybe you would, but I would not!’” Mamet noted.

Zosia, the daughter of legendary playwright David Mamet, praised her husband the following year in honor of their nine-year wedding anniversary.

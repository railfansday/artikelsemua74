Influencer Christie Miller announced her own death in a prerecorded video message after being diagnosed with stage IV breast cancer. She was 60 years old.

She went on to share a quote from Dr. Seuss — “Don’t be sad because it’s over, be happy because it happened” — but she reminded her followers that “it’s normal to be sad” after a loss.

Sources indicate that “But also, be happy that we got to spend time together,” she added. “Whether it was in-person friends or a chance meeting, or we’ve known each other forever. Miller shared one “last bit of advice” for her followers before ending the video. “Go out there and live your life,” she stated. “Feel the fear and do it anyway. Stop frickin’ caring about what anyone thinks … they’re not worried about you.”.

Sources indicate that the video was captioned, “Our sweet Christie graduated Earth School peacefully this morning, absolutely surrounded by love. Thank you all so much for the outpouring of love! ❤️.”.

According to recent reports, miller was diagnosed with stage IV HER2-positive breast cancer in 2022. She built a following of more than 50,000 Instagram owners, sharing a glimpse at how she continued to find joy amid her health battle.

Days before announcing her death, Miller shared a video of herself at the beach with her dog. “I didn’t think I’d be able to do this again,” she stated in the November 15 upload as her pet splashed in the water behind her. “I have so much joy in my heart to be able to do this, maybe for the last time. … I’m going to go enjoy this moment.”.

In a separate clip, Miller revealed how she was “making dying fun” by eating whatever she wanted. “This week, which is most likely the last week of my life, I am having some childhood favorites,” she mentioned while lying on the couch, showing off her root beer float. “This is another way how I am squeezing out joy until my very last moment.”.

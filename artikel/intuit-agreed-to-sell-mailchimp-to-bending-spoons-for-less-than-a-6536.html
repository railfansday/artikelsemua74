Intuit agreed to sell Mailchimp to Bending Spoons for less than a third of its 2021 price

🌐 Source: Qz • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (42 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that intuit $INTU agreed to sell Mailchimp to Bending Spoons for approximately $3.68 billion, the company stated on Thursday. The deal should wrap up in early 2027, pending regulatory approvals and other customary closing conditions.

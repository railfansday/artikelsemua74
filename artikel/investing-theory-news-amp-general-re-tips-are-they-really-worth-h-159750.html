If you can define “normal retirement expenses”, that’s a specific liability. Unless your essential expenses are already covered by some other income (i.e. Pension or SS), they are a specific liability that could be matched with a TIPS ladder.I think the liability terminology is turning out to be too fancy for its own good, leading people to get wrapped around the axle about what is and isn't a liability and can they define it.It seems like it would much easier to call it a self-funded, inflation-adjusted pension or income stream.Careful calling it a pension because the Annuity police are quick to point out that there are no mortality credits. I found this out when I mentioned that our household I bond collection (which we’ve been accumulating since the 1990s when they were introduced) could in some respects be viewed as a CPI-indexed annuity.Statistics: Posted by AlwaysLearningMore — Fri Nov 27, 2026 2:57 pm.

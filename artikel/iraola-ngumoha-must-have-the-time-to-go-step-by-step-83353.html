Andoni Iraola insisted Liverpool talent Rio Ngumoha must be given "the time to go step by step".

Ngumoha continued to shine for the Reds on Sunday as he played a crucial role in their Premier League win over rivals Manchester United. (Related: All my favorite XREAL glasses are at their lowest price...)

He (18 years 85 days) became the youngest player to score and assist in a Premier League game for Liverpool since Michael Owen on Boxing Day 1997 (18y 12d v Leeds United). (Related: Tech Report: Woman known as Firefighting hero arrested ...)

In a recent development, ngumoha has also completed more dribbles than any other player through the first four matchdays in this season's Champions League (25). His dribble success rate (73.5%) was also the highest of any player with 10+ completed dribbles (25/34).

However, ahead of Liverpool's Champions League encounter with Club Brugge, Iraola has tempered expectations around the teenager.

"The most difficult part is to keep a very high level consistently," noted Iraola.

"You can play a good game then three normal or bad games; especially at a significant club like Liverpool, you have to do very well in every game.

It has been revealed that "With Rio, I always say we have to give him the time to go step by step. You can't rush it; it wouldn't be fair on him.

Industry observers note that "He is in the right mindset and is becoming a better player, adding more things to his game. We know he is good, but we have to leave him sometimes.

"He'll have not so good games, and we have to trust him as he deserves it.".

This will be the first meeting between Brugge and Liverpool since the 1978 European Cup final, won 1-0 by the Reds at Wembley.

Brugge and Liverpool have faced each other on three previous occasions, with all three of those being in European finals. Both of them were won by the Reds: 4-3 on aggregate in the 1976 UEFA Cup (3-2 at home, 1-1 away) and 1-0 in the 1978 European Cup.

"It's not that they surprised me; I knew they were very good. But honestly I've enjoyed analysing them," Iraola remarked about Brugge.

It has been revealed that "Sometimes you have to watch teams, and it's a little boring, but that's not what happened with Brugge. They are very energetic and have had an incredible run of games, 10 wins and a draw.

"I think we will have to be at a very, very good level, otherwise it is going to be very difficult. They have players who can make the difference, and obviously we also have.

Sources indicate that "I expect quite an open game. We are both aggressive, both want to press, and there will be spaces, and hopefully, we can win the one-v-one battles.".

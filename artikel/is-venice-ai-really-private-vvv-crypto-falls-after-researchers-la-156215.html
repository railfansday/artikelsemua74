In a recent development, venice AI’s [VVV] promise? What you type stays between you and the screen.

Industry observers note that however, now it looks like that’s not happening. And, the native token is taking a hit. (Related: Industry News: Which Samsung folding phone is right for...)

Earlier today, a researcher who goes by adacyborg11 posted a list of unflattering claims on X. (Related: Nvidia stops AI GPU shipments to Southeast Asia amid pu...)

It has been revealed that according to these accusations, Venice AI reportedly passes a lot of user requests to outside providers. The names mentioned included DeepInfra, Fireworks, Baseten, Parasail and fal.ai.

The researcher also claimed that public keys are being sent without encryption, something that could leave chats open to people who shouldn’t see them. Seven of Venice’s 21 database tables were remarked to be unencrypted, with adacyborg11 also stating that the keys meant to lock up chat data could be stored in plain text.

Industry observers note that users may not always get the model they pick either. Some reportedly received answers from a different model than the one they chose.

The altcoin climbed from $24.5 yesterday to just above $30.5 in the early hours of today. Soon after, things took a turn for the worse.

One hourly candle wiped out a massive chunk of the gains, with the price falling by 15% to about $25.8. It came with the heaviest selling volume on the chart as well. At press time, VVV was valued at $26.69 on the charts.

To be fair, the rally was already running hot. The RSI was pushing 80 before the drop, which is why some of this could be traders cashing out. At the time of writing, it had calmed down to hit 45.5, under its average.

According to recent reports, the CMF was at 0.15, implying that more money was still coming in than going out. However, it was still down from 0.40 at its peak, with the same sliding down all day.

Have the buyers given up? Well, not really. However, they’re certainly not showing the same interest they were before.

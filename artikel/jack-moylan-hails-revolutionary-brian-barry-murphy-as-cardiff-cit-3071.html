Cardiff City signed Jack Moylan from Lincoln City in the summer, and while it was viewed as a statement signing at the time, few saw him hitting such electric form. The 25-year-old has 10 goals and three assists in 15 Championship games, and he maintained his high levels with a goal against Kosovo for the Republic of Ireland on Tuesday.

And, while away with his country, Moylan spoke to The Irish Times about the impact his Bluebirds boss and compatriot Brian Barry-Murphy has had on his game since moving in the summer. He stated: “He’s been revolutionary for me. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Sources indicate that “The coaching that I’m getting over there every day, I can’t describe how good it is. He’s one of the best coaches I’ve ever seen. He’s tactically aware and a great human.”.

According to recent reports, barry-Murphy’s coaching career has been an intriguing one to follow. After retiring in 2017, he took charge of Rochdale in 2019 and oversaw a bold, possession-based style of football and entrusted young players to come into his first-team squad, much like he has at Cardiff City. (Related: Transformer-based Encoder-Decoder Models...)

In a recent development, his football and ability to develop youth players made him a perfect fit for his next job – manager of Manchester City’s academy. There, he worked alongside elite coaches in fantastic facilities and helped rear a generation of starlets for the Premier League club.

Sources indicate that A short stint coaching at Leicester City followed, before Cardiff City came calling. In South Wales, he has followed a similar pattern for success: develop young players and play eye-catching possession football. The 48-year-old has been heralded for his work and Moylan is the latest to sing his praises.

It has been revealed that the Bluebirds sit 14th in the Championship table with Barry-Murphy’s coaching ability seeing his League One promotion-winning players step up to the league, all while sticking to his playing principles. Moylan’s words show just how influential he is as a coach, and it is about time that the wider footballing world took note.

In Barry-Murphy, Cardiff City have a top coach who can develop their players and please fans with eye-catching football. He is perhaps the most vital asset at the club.

However, new star Moylan is well on his way to becoming the most valuable. His remarkable form since joining and growing profile with Ireland means that clubs are bound to take notice.

Recent findings suggest that the new Bluebirds star has made it clear just how happy he is at the Cardiff City Stadium, so the club shouldn’t be in immediate danger of losing him in January. But, if such form continues, it might not be long before Moylan is playing at a higher level.

And, there’s every chance that boss Barry-Murphy ends up joining him in moving up the ladder. For now though, both will be fully focused on their jobs at Cardiff as they look to keep the good times coming.

The Boston Celtics, thanks to a substantial defensive stand from Draymond Green late, are now on their worst losing skid in years.

It has been revealed that the Celtics fell to the Golden State Warriors 88-87 on Sunday night at the Chase Center in San Francisco. That was Boston's fourth straight loss, which marked the first time the franchise had lost four consecutive games since May 2021.

It had gone a ridiculous 429 games without a four-game losing skid, which was the second-longest stretch in franchise history. The Celtics once went 468 games from 1962-68.

According to recent reports, boston led by as many as 11 late in the third quarter, and seemed in a great spot to grab the win. But the Warriors chipped away and got back into the game, and even took the lead on a Brandin Podziemski 3-pointer with about 2:30 left in the contest. Those were the last points the Warriors scored.

With just eight seconds left, Tatum had a great look to win the game. Down by a point, Tatum drove at Green and took a fadeaway jumper from the elbow. The shot was off the mark, and he finished going 0 of 7 from the field in the second half.

Industry observers note that podziemski led the Warriors with 18 points and seven rebounds in the win, which moved them to 9-8 on the season and ended a five-game losing streak of their own. Charles Bassey added 13 points and nine rebounds, and Green finished with nine points. The Warriors are still missing Stephen Curry, who is sidelined with an abdominal injury. Industry observers note that tatum finished with 27 points and 10 rebounds while shooting 8 of 20 from the field. Derrick White had 14 points, and Luka Garza added 10 off the bench. They were the only three Celtics to hit double figures, and the 87 points they managed as a team were the fewest they've scored all season. The Celtics had double-digit leads in three of their four losses, including a 17-point lead over the Denver Nuggets before blowing that contest Wednesday. They were up by 13 points at Sacramento on Saturday before Keegan Murray sank a put-back jumper to seal the one-point win.

Boston got the losing streak started Monday in a 10-point loss to the Orlando Magic, who rallied late to grab the win at TD Garden.

While the losing streak isn't great, it's far from the time to be concerned for the Celtics. It's not even December yet, and they sit at 11-6 on the season. That's good for third in the Eastern Conference standings, behind only the Philadelphia 76ers and Atlanta Hawks. There is plenty of time for the Celtics to rebound from this stretch and maintain their position in the East. And, the longest losing streak in NBA history is 28 games. The Celtics aren't even close to that mark.

But their run of keeping losing streaks to a minimum has ended. They'll have a chance to snap the four-game losing skid Tuesday night against the Washington Wizards.

Jennifer Lawrence is getting candid about going under the knife.

The actress, 36, confirmed she had gotten a boob job while appearing on the Thursday, November 19, episode of Watch What Happens Live With Andy Cohen alongside her Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping costar, Josh Hutcherson.

It has been revealed that the admission came as she was giving her “hot takes” on Bravo celebrities. At one point host Andy Cohen, 58, asked Lawrence, “Is it a mystery to you how a boob could break Hailey Glassman’s nose?”.

“No, it’s a full C. I think?” Lawrence mentioned. “It’s the same size that they were.”.

The Don’t Look Up star went on to joke, “Anyway, I’ve broken two noses.”.

Lawrence has previously been an open book about her cosmetic surgery procedures.

Speaking to Vogue for an article published on September 3, Lawrence revealed all the cosmetic work she’s had done.

Recent findings suggest that “I’m really nervous about these fat transfers,” Lawrence admitted to the magazine. “I support other people doing them because I want to continue to watch and see what happens, but I don’t know about that.”.

In a recent development, “I mean, I would love to have a buccal fat removal,” the No Hard Feelings star added of the procedure that takes out fat pads from the cheeks to create a more sculpted face. “I can’t because everybody would know, but just know that I want that.” Lawrence also opened up about other beauty treatments that she doesn’t necessarily enjoy getting done.

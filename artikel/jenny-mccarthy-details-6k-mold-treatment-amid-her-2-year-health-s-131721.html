Jenny McCarthy is sharing a candid update amid her challenging mold poisoning recovery.

Sources indicate that according to People, McCarthy, 54, shared the latest on her health ordeal via Instagram Live on Tuesday, November 24. In the social media live broadcast, the Masked Singer judge underwent another round of a therapeutic plasma exchange (TPE) treatment, which costs $6k per session. McCarthy reportedly remarked it was her fifth and final round of the treatment after undergoing the sessions once a month.

“Unfortunately, the typical mold detox that most people do didn’t work for me. It was really frustrating,” she noted. “I was spending so much money on s**t that wasn’t working.”.

Industry observers note that according to McCarthy, the TV personality suffered a range of symptoms due to mold poisoning. These included weight gain, eye swelling, chalazions, fibromyalgia among others. (Related: Industry News: Think AI "knows" what it’s doing? Scient...)

McCarthy said the TPE sessions have dramatically improved her condition.

“I feel so much better,” she noted. “I feel like the life is back inside of me. I feel like I’m not as sensitive to the world. I’m not living out of a tent anymore. I was so chemically sensitive that I couldn’t handle much so I know that I’m getting better because I’m not as sensitive.”.

In the October 28 episode of her podcast, “The Jenny McCarthy Wahlberg Show,” the former Playboy model shared eye-opening details of her two-year battle with mold and mycotoxin poisoning.

McCarthy remarked that she went down “a rabbit hole of doctors, surgeries, teeth being pulled, mysterious growths popping up in places you are not expecting, implants literally falling out” before “realizing my immune system was fighting something so much bigger.”.

After experiencing fatigue as one of the symptoms, McCarthy’s doctor suspected mold poisoning and had her tested.

Industry observers note that the doctor apparently told her, “Jenny, you’ve got serious mold. You’ve got the type of mold that causes seizures. If this thing crosses the blood-brain barrier, I’m going to be talking to you in hospital. You’re going to start seizing.”.

McCarthy remarked she is still recovering from the ordeal and has even been living in a tent.

Industry observers note that “I’m still in my mold detox, you guys. I’m still in it, like, I’m knee-deep in it,” she mentioned. “I’m in month seven of detox and I’m too paranoid to honestly live anywhere else other than a tent.”.

Speaking on the November 10 episode of her podcast, McCarthy shared that the mold poisoning ordeal not only affected her but her husband, Donnie Wahlberg, as well.

I was crying so hard and had a nervous breakdown in that room,” McCarthy explained, noting “that my husband never saw me like this, ever.”.

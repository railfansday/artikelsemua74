Industry observers note that 2025 saw the debut of Prime Video's Countdown, a highly entertaining, action-packed thriller series starring Jensen Ackles and Eric Dane with a unique premise that made every episode a must-watch experience. Unfortunately, despite a solid first season that culminated in a shocking ending, Countdown didn't get renewed, ultimately making it a one-and-done series. The show's appearance in Prime Video's global Top 10 rankings last November, following its cancellation, only proves that Countdown was a good show that deserved a better fate. Industry observers note that countdown's one and only season opens with the mysterious murder of a DHS agent, which prompts FBI Special Agent Nathan Blythe (the late great Eric Dane) to form an elite task force composed of different branches of law enforcement, including LAPD Detective Mark Meachum (Ackles) and DEA Agent Amber Oliveras (Jessica Camacho), to get to the bottom of the murder. Task Force Hurricane eventually uncovers a plot perpetuated by the disenfranchised former Russian soldier Anton Volchek (Bogdan Yasinski) to construct and detonate nuclear weapons in the middle of Los Angeles. Every episode becomes a pulse-pounding race against the clock to discover Volchek's whereabouts and the nuclear bombs, with thousands of lives hanging in the balance. However, Season 1's nuclear bomb threat is only part of the story. At the outset, it appeared that Countdown's main mission would have a season-long arc, similar to most action-thriller reveals on streaming. In Episode 10, however, the Volchek plot is completely resolved, with the show then embracing a 10-month time jump that results in a completely new storyline. Here, Agent Blythe reassembles his elite task force, this time named Task Force Armor, to investigate a conspiracy to assassinate a presidential candidate and the current POTUS. Countdown's approach proved unique for a streaming show, with a totally new conflict beginning in the latter four episodes, but the show's bold narrative attempt may have been too unique for its own good.

In a recent development, countdown's first season concludes with a heart-pounding cliffhanger in which the suspect behind the assassination plot, nicknamed "Todd" (Grant Harvey) by Meachum, is revealed to be an FBI agent. Initially, Task Force Armor appeared to be investigating an ultra-right-wing, anti-government fanatic, but revelations in the final episode suggest a deeper ulterior motive, with Todd leading the task force astray at every step of their investigation.

The episode that would turn out to be the show's season finale, "Your People Are in Danger," ends on a cliffhanger that makes the show's cancellation even more frustrating, as Todd takes Agent Oliveras captive and prepares to execute her, as he had done to his wife's pet dog in an earlier episode. The finale then cuts to the credits before Todd presumably opens fire. However, series creator and showrunner Derek Haas, via Bluesky, later revealed that Agent Oliveras was intended to survive past what ended up becoming the series' final moment.

According to recent reports, countdown was a solid series, headlined by the exceptional trio of Dane, Ackles, and Camacho. Ackles plays his character with tremendous pathos, as the series eventually reveals that Meachum has been diagnosed with a terminal illness and his symptoms are growing more severe. As the investigation into Volchek progresses, Meachum grows more reckless in the field, believing he must stop Volchek while possibly sacrificing himself in the process.

In a recent development, with its premise and format, Countdown felt closer to a spiritual successor of the iconic action-thriller series 24. Each episode featured an exciting action set-piece, raising the stakes and suspense while gradually peeling back the layers of the story's major antagonist. It was an exciting, unique series that Prime Video should have valued more. Despite a terrific cast, premise, and hook for Season 2, fans never learned Todd's true motives or identity, and the brewing romance between Meachum and Oliveras was never resolved. Nevertheless, viewers can decide for themselves by watching Countdown's existing episodes on the streamer.

By acting as the universal repository for software packages, it has become the invisible plumbing that powers global application delivery. The stock, which trades at $109.60 as of November 19, 2026, has gained 80% over the past year as developers increasingly lean on its platform to manage the complexity of modern, AI-integrated software supply chains. Recent findings suggest that JFrog is in a great position to grow its business over the next several years thanks to the artificial intelligence boom. AI is making it easier and faster for developers to generate code. As a result, enterprises need automated systems to store, track, and govern the resulting software packages and AI models.

This contributed to JFrog's enterprise customers signing massive, multi-year contracts resulting in a 60% year-over-year increase in Q3 cloud sales to $101.5 million. Cloud income represented 46% of revenue last year, and has grown to 57% at the end of the third quarter in a sign JFrog's enterprise moat is widening, anchored by high switching costs.

Recent findings suggest that another factor in JFrog's favor is that it bundles cybersecurity features into its platform, strengthening the safety and security of the software code it manages. This also delivers a unified "system of record" that increases contract sizes and platform stickiness, encouraging customers to consolidate their spending with JFrog.

It has been revealed that the success of this approach is demonstrated by the company's robust net dollar retention rate of 124%, up from 118% in the previous year. As the use of AI code continues to expand, JFrog is poised for years of ongoing business expansion ahead.

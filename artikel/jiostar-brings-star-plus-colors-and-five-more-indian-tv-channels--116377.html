Recent findings suggest that seven of India’s JioStar television channels, including popular Hindi-language entertainment channels Star Plus and Colors, are now streaming in the U.S. on live TV service Fubo.

Colors Cineplex is the group’s movie channel, programming Hindi films that range from older Bollywood favorites to hits such as “Kabhi Khushi Kabhie Gham,” “Tiger 3,” “Welcome” and “Dil Toh Pagal Hai.” MTV India adds its youth-skewing formats “Roadies,” “Splitsvilla” and “Hustle,” while News18 English covers news out of India.

Viewers can get the channels through two Fubo packs. The Hindi Entertainment & Cricket pack costs $34.99 for the first month as an introductory offer, rising to $39.99 a month after that. The JioStar pack is priced at $10.99 a month.

Amit Malhotra, head of international business at JioStar, remarked: “The opportunity for Indian entertainment internationally is no longer limited to taking individual demonstrates or channels overseas. It is about building a distribution ecosystem through which the breadth of Indian television can travel. At JioStar, our approach is to bring the full spectrum of our content, from fiction and reality to cinema and youth entertainment, to South Asian families in the U.S. and to a wider audience who can connect with it.”.

JioStar is pitching the move at the South Asian diaspora, for whom Indian TV is still a close tie to Indian culture, and also at a wider audience curious about the country’s storytelling.

Malhotra added that the Fubo launch gives JioStar a solid base in the U.S. as it puts together a broader international distribution network for its linear channels.

In September, JioStar’s streaming service JioHotstar launched in the U.K., Canada and Singapore, replacing Hotstar in those markets with a library of more than 160,000 hours of content in over 12 languages.

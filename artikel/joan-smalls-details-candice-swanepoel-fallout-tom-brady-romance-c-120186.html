Joan Smalls is detailing what she claims really happened between her and former BFF Candice Swanepoel, alleging Tom Brady was the “catalyst” of their friendship fallout.

Smalls, 38, explained on the Wednesday, November 25, episode of the “Cutting Room Floor” podcast that while fans began to notice her distancing herself from Swanepoel, 38, during October’s Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show — when they didn’t pose for or take any pictures together — she had actually cut ties with the fellow model in April. Recent findings suggest that “I think they were noticing one because I removed all the pictures off of my Instagram of her and I stopped following her months ago,” Smalls recalled, confirming that Swanepoel’s choice to date Brady, 49, following her own eight-month romance with the retired quarterback is what sparked their tension. (Us confirmed in October that Swanepoel and Brady have been dating “for a while.”.

Smalls told listeners that she had been friends with Swanepoel for more than 15 years and was “vulnerable” and had “intimate conversations” with her, which made her relationship with Brady even more difficult to understand.

Sources indicate that “We’ve seen each other through ups and downs. You’ve seen the relationships, situationships, people I’ve dated, and I’ve confided in you in these spaces, and we never had a crossover,” Smalls shared. “So, all of a sudden, OK, I stopped seeing this person and 3 months later, you text me and say, ‘Oh, we have to speak about something.’”. The Puerto Rico native revealed she instantly knew what Swanepoel wanted to say.

“We’ve never been in a crossroads where we’ve had to have such a serious conversation,” Smalls mentioned, adding that she knew it in her “soul” that it was pegged to Brady, whom Smalls revealed Swanepoel had helped her “write text messages to” when they were an item.

In a recent development, the supermodel said that Swanepoel “knew intimate things, like, potentially, to my detriment, I overshared. I was just being open with a friend that I thought was a safe space.”.

Smalls recalled speaking on the phone “very briefly” with Swanepoel and when they hung up she was “perplexed” and at “a loss for words.”.

While Smalls stated Brady “wasn’t an ex” per say, she had been seeing him and confiding in Swanepoel about him, which is why it went against girl code.

“She came across as justifying her action [and saying] that what I had was wasn’t valid or it was casual and she started diminishing my experience to make herself feel better about the situation,” Smalls claimed. “I think [that] is what kind of infuriated me as my friend to kind of, diminish me or devalue my friendship so much that you didn’t have consideration towards my feelings or my hurt.”.

Sources indicate that smalls added that Swanepoel’s alleged “attitude of, ‘I’m an adult. I don’t need permission,’” also rubbed her the wrong way.

“You are an adult and you don’t need permission. But as a friend, you should consider me in your choices. Don’t consider me in your apologies,” she continued.

Smalls revealed that it didn’t matter that it was Brady, it was the fact that Swanepoel didn’t think about her when she made a move on someone she had a past with.

According to recent reports, “It was a mixture of the lying — I caught her in a couple of lies and I asked her about it. She didn’t want to correct it. It was, like, under the rug,” Smalls remarked. “It was the gaslighting. It was the diminishing of my feelings, trying to devalue my worth. And it was just like one after the other.”.

According to recent reports, she allegedly told Swanepoel, “I was like, you know what you’re doing is manipulation 101. I said the fact that you are blameshifting and saying that what I said hurt you but what you did didn’t hurt it to me that’s mindblowing.”.

After Smalls was out of the friendship, she remarked she spoke with other females and friends and realized that “this is not fresh territory for her,” claiming that Swanepoel had “done this before.”.

According to recent reports, after forming a fast friendship on Celebrity Substantial Brother U.K., JoJo Siwa and Chris Hughes sparked public interest with their growing bond. In the weeks following the show, the pair traveled together, spent time with each other’s families, and shared increasingly affectionate posts online, further fueling speculation about a possible romance. In October 2025, they even made their red carpet debut as a couple.

Now, the couple has reportedly called it quits. Here’s everything we know about their relationship.

Siwa and Hughes were both contestants on Celebrity Substantial Brother U.K.. At the time, Siwa was still in a relationship with Australian actor Kath Ebbs. However, viewers took note of her chemistry with Hughes, especially during emotional moments in the house.

The two frequently spent time together on the show, with Hughes defending Siwa during a difficult confrontation with another contestant. During an appearance on The Useless Hotline Podcast, Siwa said his support was part of what made her realize “he’s a good man.”.

“He is somebody I would want my children to be like,” she shared on the podcast. “He’s such a great gentleman. He’s also hilarious and a good ball of fun. He’s a special guy.”. (Related: Woman fined $27 for crying on bench at children’s playg...)

“We’re very similar in our energy, in our outlook on life,” Siwa added. “Chris was there for me at my lowest times and I was there for him at his lowest times. That’s what made it so special.”.

In a recent development, she also praised Hughes’s reaction to the homophobic comments she received on the show, saying, “That highlighted Chris’s true personality and it was beautiful to see. People obviously say what they want about people, but that showed me who he really is.”.

Speculation about Siwa and Hughes’s relationship continued, especially after the two traveled together and began appearing in each other’s social media posts.

On May 20, Siwa performed a cover of “Bette Davis Eyes” in Mexico and changed the lyrics mid-song to reference Hughes—singing, “He’s got Chris Hughes’ eyes.”.

According to recent reports, the following day, Siwa celebrated her 22nd birthday by sharing an Instagram, opening with a photo of her resting her head on Hughes’s shoulder. “A week I’ll remember for the rest of my life,” she wrote.

In a recent development, hughes posted his own photos, writing, “The previous 7 days have been the prettiest.. 😊💫🎂.”.

On June 1, Hughes appeared to confirm their relationship with a Snapchat photo of himself shirtless in bed, kissing Siwa on the forehead. The caption read, “Sleeepinnn beauty.”.

The next day, Siwa publicly confirmed their romance in an interview with The Guardian, stating: “It’s not platonic anymore, and it’s been a beautiful development, a beautiful connection, and I’m absolutely head over heels for him.”.

Sources indicate that she also addressed rumors that the relationship was a publicity stunt, saying, “Clearly, you’ve never been around us. I won’t ever speak for him, but for me personally, the happiness in my life just radiates off of me right now.”.

“Literally yesterday, I was massaging my cheeks; I’ve never [before] been in pain from smiling so much,” she added.

Sources indicate that she also remarked she woke up to Hughes singing one of her songs that morning. “You know when you’re alone, how you feel? You are your true, raw self,” she explained. “I’ve obviously felt like that before, but never around one single person.”.

On July 12, Hughes told The Sun’s Fabulous Magazine about his relationship with Siwa.

Industry observers note that “Life has an unexpected way of bringing two people together, and this feels like that,” Hughes shared. “We were meant to cross paths. We keep saying to each other it was serendipity. Life handed me a blessing in my lap.”.

Hughes added that when he met the Dance Moms alum, he experienced a “magical feeling,” recognizing her as “the most precious person.”.

“I adored the ground she walked on and the way she handled herself,” he continued.

He then offered a timeline of their romance, saying when the show ended they still talked “all the time.” He then “flew to Mexico City to surprise her,” beginning the “best three days of my life.”.

Industry observers note that “Our first kiss was genuinely special—it felt like I was in a movie,” he explained. “I can’t keep my hands off her now.”.

Sources indicate that as for the negative and positive attention their relationship has garnered: “I don’t care,” said Hughes, continuing, “I wouldn’t go to the effort of loving someone for any other reason than it being real. Having been in this industry for eight years, I needed to adapt to having criticism from different angles. The past few months have actually made me a little stronger mentally.”.

The pair had a special relationship milestone in the fall, posing together on the red carpet for Dancers Against Cancer’s 2025 Gala of the Stars on October 21.

Sources indicate that they spoke to E! News there, with Hughes telling the outlet that going from friends to more was actually great for them. “People always say, ‘Oh God, is it difficult?’ Honestly, it’s easy,” he said. “We were friends first, and everyone likes their friends.”.

Siwa added, “I think the universe literally said, ‘Oh yeah, by the way. You two belong together, you’ll figure it out eventually.’ And we did! We’ve both been in relationships prior, but I think that’s why this feels easy because it’s very right. We have fun and we keep things light.”.

Siwa spoke openly about her hopes to have children with Hughes during an appearance on E!’s The Rundown.

“Meghan Trainor once told me, ‘You will know you love somebody when you love them so much you want to duplicate them and you want to pop a baby out that is made from them,’” she noted.

In a recent development, that advice resonated once she began dating Hughes, Siwa admitted: “One of the first things that I said to her after I started dating Chris, I was like, ‘I get what you’re saying now. I love [Chris] so much. I need there to be a mini [Chris].’”.

According to recent reports, she added that those feelings only heightened when Hughes shared a childhood photo with her: “He sent me a baby photo of him the other day. It’s like him in a trash can. It’s really cute. And I was like, ‘Uh oh.’”.

Siwa continued to speak candidly about her relationship with Hughes while attending the Hannah Montana 20th anniversary special premiere in Los Angeles.

“The inspiration behind that is my very sweet love Christopher,” she told People of her upcoming single “Serendipity.”.

“Well, our lives connecting and meeting are like serendipity. It’s my favorite word, so I had to write a song about it.”.

Reflecting on their relationship, she added, “I think that when it’s meant to be, it is, and when it's meant to be, it’ll be right.”.

Hughes attended Siwa’s performance at Universal CityWalk in Los Angeles on July 21, where she performed her song “Serendipity.”.

Sources indicate that during the performance, Siwa climbed into Hughes’s lap from the edge of the stage before the two shared a kiss in front of the crowd. She wore a sparkling blue, silver, and neon yellow two-piece set, layered necklaces, white knee-high boots, and a large bow in her hair.

Sources indicate that A couple of days later, Siwa shared photos from the performance on Instagram and wrote, “LIVE AT CITY WALK💙✨ what a perfect night. I’ve waited over 10 years to get to perform on this stage and finally last night that dream came true. Thank you to everyone who came out 🫶🏼.”.

Hughes commented on the post, “absolute cinemmmmmma 👏🏼.”.

According to recent reports, siwa and Hughes discovered they share the exact same vision for her dream engagement ring. In conversation with People, Siwa revealed that she once told her mom that she wanted Hughes to design her future ring himself. During a dinner together, the couple decided to compare their ideas—and independently pulled up the same Google image of a blue, emerald-cut ring.

“We were holding the same photo from Google!” Siwa recalled, calling the odds “so slim.” The singer added that their relationship is going “great” as they approach their year-and-a-half anniversary.

On November 27, People reported Siwa and Hughes called it quits after a year and a half together. A friend of Siwa’s told the outlet that the singer was heartbroken.

It has been revealed that “JoJo is really hurt. She loved him and gave everything she had to make the relationship work,” the source noted. “For months, behind closed doors, she kept hoping things would change. But eventually, she realized that she deserved to feel just as loved and supported as she wanted him to feel.”.

The source added that ending the relationship wasn’t easy for Siwa, who still cares about Hughes and wishes him well. “She just knew she needed to choose herself,” they mentioned.

Here, Football Insider looks at how the London club's players performed at Stamford Bridge.

Josh Acheampong had a horrible afternoon at Stamford Bridge.

The 20-year-old struggled against Okafor at right-back and made multiple errors.

Before the Whites' equaliser, the defender completely messed up his clearance in the middle of the park, and Leeds' winger got in behind the Chelsea defence.

The West Yorkshire outfit continued to pepper the Blues before the break and eventually scored more in the second half, with Acheampong part of a backline that was unable to keep a lid on Farke's attackers.

Joao Pedro's absence meant that Emegha got his first Premier League start of the season.

The Netherlands international lasted 64 minutes on the pitch before being replaced by Danny Welbeck.

Emegha looked like the only player to put pressure on Leeds' backline and he was rewarded for it.

While Rogers also looked lively at the start, he struggled as the game progressed and eventually gave away the penalty in the second half.

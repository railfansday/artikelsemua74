Sorry, “Succession” fans — Kieran Culkin is shutting down any possibility of a spinoff once and for all.

The actor, presently starring in “The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping” as a young Caesar Flickerman, joined the “Heart Breakfast” radio show on Friday to discuss the newly introduced film, though hosts Amanda Holden and Jamie Theakston also snuck in a few questions regarding the hit HBO show’s potential future. It’s been three years!” When asked about a potential for a spinoff, Culkin answered, “Of course not. And though the “A Real Pain” actor is steadfast about not retreading the show’s familiar territory, he noted that he misses playing Roman Roy and the “whole thing.”.

Culkin continued: “It was fun. It was one of those where Jesse Armstrong, the showrunner, spent the entire last season trying to figure out if there was a way to do one more. I never wanted to know what was going to happen in the season and on the fourth one I did ask and when he pitched me the end of the season and I went ‘well, that kind of sounds like it’s the end of the show.’ But when he pitched me three different ideas of what season 5 could be, they all sounded good… But it ended perfectly. According to recent reports, elsewhere in the interview, Culkin divulged that he’ll be reuniting with “Succession” creator Jesse Armstrong on a newly introduced film. Culkin starred alongside Brian Cox, Sarah Snook, Jeremy Strong, Nicholas Braun, Matthew Macfadyen, Alan Ruck and more in the acclaimed drama, which ran for four seasons.

“The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping” hit theaters on Friday and brought in $56 million on opening day.

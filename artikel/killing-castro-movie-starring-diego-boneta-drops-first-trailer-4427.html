Industry observers note that romulus Entertainment has the released the first trailer for its upcoming pic Killing Castro starring Diego Boneta. The film also stars Al Pacino, Xolo Maridueña, KiKi Layne, Alexander Ludwig, Ron Livingston, Kendrick Sampson, Nicole Beharie, Logan Marshall Green and Titus Welliver.

Eif Rivera will direct the movie from a script by Leon Hendrix, Thomas DeGrezia and Colin Bateman.

Killing Castro is be produced by Romulus Entertainment’s Brad Feinstein, along with Christina Weiss Lurie of Fourth and Twenty-Eight Films. Romulus will also finance. DJ Khaled, Rene Mclean, Lylette Pizarro, Jon Jashni and Shawn Costner are exec producers.

Sources indicate that based on true events, the film is set in 1960. Shortly after winning the Cuban revolution, Fidel Castro travels to Upcoming York City to deliver his first speech to the United Nations. When met with hostility at his original hotel, Castro meets Malcolm X who invites him to stay at famed Hotel Theresa in Harlem. With the eyes of the world watching, a rookie undercover FBI agent originally assigned to investigate Malcolm X suddenly becomes the FBI’s most valuable asset, and is tasked with keeping Castro from being eliminated by the CIA and the Italian mafia, by any means necessary.

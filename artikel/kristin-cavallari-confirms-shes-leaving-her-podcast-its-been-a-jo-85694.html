Kristin Cavallari Confirms She's Leaving Her Podcast: 'It's Been a Journey'

  • I have an announcement, and I’m not gonna cry because this is not the end yet.
  • Cavallari added that she feels like she has “gotten to say everything I’ve wanted to say” in the last three years of her podcast.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Kristin Cavallari is closing the book on her “Let’s Be Honest” podcast.

“I’m getting emotional, you guys. I have an announcement, and I’m not gonna cry because this is not the end yet. But I am leaving the podcast,” the Hills alum, 39, said during the Tuesday, November 24, episode. “I did not plan on getting emotional. I am so incredibly grateful that I have had this opportunity and that I’ve been able to connect with you guys on such a real, deep level, and I feel like I’ve been able to finally show people who I really am after being on reality TV for so long and always feeling like I’ve been misunderstood.”. Cavallari added that she feels like she has “gotten to say everything I’ve wanted to say” in the last three years of her podcast. (Related: Tech Report: Woman known as Firefighting hero arrested ...)

According to recent reports, “It’s been a really fun, wild ride. Like, when I started the podcast, I was actively dating and I was newly single, and it was really fun, all my dating stories. It’s been a journey, and I’ve been so happy to have you guys on this ride with me,” she continued.

Cavallari admitted that the downside of her podcast has been “having to be out there every single week.”.

“There would be headlines all the time about the podcast, and I don’t like that stuff,” she explained. ”But in the past, if I was in pop culture a lot, I could reel it in, right? I could go silent on socials. I could not do any interviews. I could stop doing everything. I don’t have that luxury with this podcast to be quiet when I want to be quiet.”.

Recent findings suggest that cavallari also stated she’s “really busy” these days with her kids — Camden, 14, Jaxon, 12, and Saylor, 11, whom she shares with ex-husband Jay Cutler — and her Uncommon James jewelry and beauty brand. She is also set to star in the upcoming Roku Channel romantic comedy Cowboy Hearts in 2027.

“I don’t want to be just f***ing grinding every day,” she said, recalling a two- or three-week break she took a little over a year ago.

“If I could go back and do things differently, I probably would have asked for, like, three or four months off and then came back like guns blazing, but, you know, again, everything happens for a reason,” she added.

Cavallari’s last episode of “Let’s Be Honest” will drop on December 29, and the reality TV personality teased that she has “fun stuff” planned.

“My final thoughts: Justin [Anderson, her longtime best friend and frequent podcast guest] will come back on for another goodbye episode, and you know, we’ll see what the future holds,” she concluded. “So don’t tune me out just yet, guys. We have a couple more weeks, but I did want to just tell you, just so that you guys know, and just so I don’t blindside you with it.”.

According to recent reports, cavallari launched “Let’s Be Honest” in September 2023. She previously cohosted a Laguna Beach rewatch podcast called Back to the Beach with former costar Stephen Colletti in 2022.

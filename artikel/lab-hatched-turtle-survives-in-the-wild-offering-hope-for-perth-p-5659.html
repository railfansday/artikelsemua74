Lab-hatched turtle survives in the wild, offering hope for Perth populations

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (40 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Researchers at Murdoch University have found the first evidence that artificially incubated southwestern snake-necked turtles can survive in the wild for more than a year after release, offering hope for struggling populations in Perth's urban wetlands.

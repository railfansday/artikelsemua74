Having spent two seasons married into the Dutton family in 1923, Michelle Randolph has become an even bigger star as the daughter of Billy Bob Thornton's Tommy Norris in Landman, and Taylor Sheridan isn't the only one who's been keeping Randolph busy over the last couple of years. Having ventured into the world of horror earlier this year with Scream 7, Randolph has now turned her attention to a considerably lighter genre, teaming up with one of the stars of The Summer I Turned Pretty for a Christmas movie. And it seems audiences haven't even waited until Thanksgiving to get into the festive spirit.

Having debuted on Prime Video on November 23, Clashing Through the Snow has already become the No. 1 movie on the streaming service worldwide, according to FlixPatrol's November 24 rankings. The movie is also sitting at No. 2 in the United States, while its success internationally has seen it overtake several installments of The Hunger Games franchise. Randolph and Christopher Briney play rival publishing editors Hannah Hendricks and Ryan Mitchell, who find themselves sharing a rental car after a snowstorm puts an end to their plans to fly to Fresh York. Neither can really afford not to make it to Manhattan, given that their jobs are on the line and both have meetings with authors whose books could save their respective careers. Directed by Carlson Young (Upgraded), the movie features Randolph (Landman) as Hannah Hendricks, Briney (The Summer I Turned Pretty) as Ryan Mitchell, Lukas Gage (The White Lotus) as Teddy, Andrew Schulz (The Thicket) as Walter, Julia Fox (Uncut Gems) as Bianca Hart, and Paris Hilton (House of Wax) as Herself.

Meanwhile, Randolph is already back at work on the third season of Landman, having recently filmed scenes as Ainsley Norris alongside members of Texas Christian University's cheerleading squad. Paramount+ has yet to announce a premiere date for the third season.

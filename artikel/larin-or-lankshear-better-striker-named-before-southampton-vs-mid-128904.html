Southampton and Middlesbrough meet for the first time since the final Spygate verdict at St Mary's on Friday night, with former Saints Billy Sharp exclusively telling GIVEMESPORT which club has the better striker.

Southampton and their manager Tonda Eckert were found guilty of spying on three Championship opponents last season, including Boro ahead of their Championship playoff semi-final first leg, which saw them expelled from the final against Hull City at Wembley. Saints had beaten the Teesside outfit over two legs to reach one of the most lucrative finals in football, but they were thrown out just days before the fixture.

Kim Hellberg's side were reinstated but were unable to take advantage, losing 1-0 to the Tigers in the final, to ensure they remained in the Championship for another season.

As well as their expulsion, the south coast outfit were also given a minus-four points deduction to start the 2026-27 campaign, which they did swiftly overcome.

Southampton and Middlesbrough have promptly proved they are two of the best teams in the division, with the former top of the table after 17 matches with 35 points.

Saints would be in fourth with 32 points without the deduction, but instead they are sixth and their away from continues to let them down. (Related: AI swarms could hijack democracy without anyone noticin...)

Eckert's men have taken 22 points from a possible 24 at home this term, with only Boro bettering that tally (23), although they have played once more at the Riverside Stadium.

While a lot of focus will be on the Eckert vs Hellberg rivalry on what is sure to be an electric atmosphere at St Mary's, a lot of eyes will also be on Cyle Larin and Will Lankshear.

They are considered by many to be the two best number nines in the league, with Larin scoring every 103 minutes this season, while Lankshear has netted every 80 minutes for Boro.

Both have had their injury concerns, with Larin missing four games in total and Lankshear two, but both are fit and available for Friday night.

While both players have their strengths, Billy Sharp has revealed in an exclusive with GMS who he believes the 'better' centre-forward is.

Speaking to GIVEMESPORT exclusively, the recently-retired striker mentioned: “Two good players. I do like Cyle Larin. His movement is good, he’s a substantial, strong boy. He had a good World Cup.

Sources indicate that "He's been struggling with an injury, and I think that’s the thing. I felt as though I was robust and that’s why I scored a lot of goals in the Championship and the EFL, because I could churn out the games. Cyle Larin has had a few niggles this season, Lankshear has struggled with a hamstring injury. When your team relies on you to play week in, week out, Saturday, Tuesday, it is hard, and you’ve got to keep yourself fit.

“It’s a shame that both of them can’t stay fit, because I did say at the start of the season that those two, and Jarrod Bowen, would be up there with the top scorers.

I loved being the one to score the goals and get the team as high as you can, and I’m sure Will Lankshear and Cyle Larin do as well. But both good strikers. I’d probably just say Will Lankshear is better.

It's hard to disagree with Sharp, based on what we've seen this season.

Larin is clearly a brilliant striker at this level who has proven he is vital to Southampton and their style of play, but you simply can't argue with Lankshear's numbers this season.

14 goals in 15 Championship appearances is a remarkable number, and the 21-year-old looks as though he will score every time he plays.

It will be interesting to see the two face off against each other on Friday, and who comes out on top in a match where both sides would lay down a huge marker with victory.

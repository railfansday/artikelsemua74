Sylvester Stallone‘s classic film “Rocky” premiered half a century ago.

To mark the occasion, artist John Rivoli has released a new book, “The Fine Art Of Rocky: An Artistic Tribute From Screen To Canvas,” published by Insight Editions.

Recent findings suggest that the book features rare and never-before-seen original Rocky paintings by Rivoli, an official MGM/Amazon Rocky artist. Part art book and part analysis of the six Rocky films, the book invites fans to revisit the iconic franchise through Rivoli’s collection of large-scale original paintings and iconic posters, seeing the beloved character in a whole fresh light. Rocky takes center stage in this deluxe book, with over 70 of Rivoli’s paintings, posters and other unique artworks highlighting unforgettable moments from the films presented in original and unique perspectives as well as art collaborations with Stallone.

Rivoli said, “This book represents a lifetime of training — for this moment, for this story, for this opportunity to reinterpret Rocky through my own artistic lens.”.

It has been revealed that speaking with Variety, Rivoli pointed to two pieces featured in the book. The first, “Alone in the Ring,” is something that holds a special place in his heart. “I struggled with how to tell this story visually. I knew that to capture the true significance of the moment, I had to make Rocky small on the canvas. As an artist, that’s a difficult decision when you know collectors want a large, commanding image of Rocky hanging on their walls.”.

Sources indicate that rivoli claims the painting wasn’t about making Rocky larger than life. “It was about showing him surrounded by something much bigger than himself. The ring is enormous, the arena is vast, and the towering image of Apollo Creed dominates the space. Everything around Rocky is larger than he is, emphasizing just how small and alone he feels in that moment.”. (Related: NASA’s new AI space chip could let spacecraft think for...)

It’s also a turning point in the film as Rocky’s goal shifts from wanting to win the fight to something far more meaningful: going the distance and proving to himself that he’s not just another bum from the neighborhood.

“That realization is the emotional heart of the story, and it’s what I wanted the painting to communicate.”.

It has been revealed that another meaningful work of art is “The Chosen One.” Rivoli reveals the original hangs in Stallone’s home. He states, “It captures the very foundation of the film and the character study that Sylvester Stallone so brilliantly establishes in its opening moments. ‘Rocky’ begins with an image of Jesus. The camera slowly pans down, revealing the word ‘Resurrection,’ before ultimately introducing us to Rocky Balboa. In that beautifully constructed sequence, we’re being told something profound: we’re about to witness the resurrection or redemption of this man.” He adds, “I find that moment incredibly powerful and I wanted to capture its spiritual and emotional significance on canvas.”.

Sources indicate that “My hope is that readers don’t just experience the film again, but feel it in a way they never have before — as if they’re seeing Rocky for the very first time. If it moves them, if it reveals something fresh, then I’ve done what I set out to do.”.

This richly illustrated volume reimagines Rocky as an immersive artistic celebration, bringing moments from screen to canvas, which invites fans to experience the iconic character in a bold new light across his 50-year legacy.

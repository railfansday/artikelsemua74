UPDATED 08:00 EST / NOVEMBER 24 2026.

According to recent reports, artificial intelligence website builder 10Web Inc. is introducing a latest feature that it claims can automatically turn content from Instagram, TikTok, Etsy, Google Business Profiles and more than 30 other sources into functioning websites and online stores. (Related: My Journey to a serverless transformers pipeline on Goo...)

Called Start from Anything, the feature targets small businesses that lack the time and expertise to build their own websites. Users can paste a link to an existing online presence or upload files, and 10Web’s AI extracts the information needed to construct a website.

It has been revealed that the system can convert photos and videos into galleries and product pages, use captions to populate product descriptions, and turn service information into dedicated pages. Business hours, addresses and location information can automatically populate contact pages, while social media posts can become news items and promotional offers. (Related: Porting fairseq wmt19 translation system to transformer...)

10Web stated its software also determines what functionality a website needs. Businesses offering services automatically receive booking capabilities, while those selling merchandise get online stores equipped to process orders and payments. Consumers can subsequently modify their websites by conversing with AI.

It has been revealed that “Nobody opens a bakery because they love choosing plugins,” Chief Executive Narek Torosyan noted in a statement. “Every question we ask an owner about software is a question we failed to answer ourselves.”.

Recent findings suggest that the service departs from the prompt-based approach that has dominated generative AI website development. Although prompts can create attractive websites, they require customers to describe their businesses and often produce generic content that must subsequently be replaced or customized.

10Web’s approach is to reuse information businesses have already accumulated, such as photos, customer-facing descriptions, product catalogs and promotional materials.

The company said it tested the technology using publicly available content, converting a concept store‘s Instagram presence and a vintage retailer‘s TikTok account into online stores. It also generated websites from a vacation property‘s Google profile and a small business’s Facebook page.

The new capability builds on 10Web’s existing generative AI platform, which creates and manages websites using the open-source WordPress content management system.

In May 2025, the manufacturer introduced an application programming interface that allows developers and software providers to incorporate AI website generation into their applications. The platform uses specialized AI agents to handle different aspects of website development, including design, content creation and functionality.

Industry observers note that 10Web mentioned the new feature includes hosting and is free to use for building and launching websites. The announcement did not specify charges for longer-term operation.

The organization mentioned it serves tens of thousands of customers worldwide and attracts between 50,000 and 60,000 new signups monthly.

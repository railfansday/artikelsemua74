Mauricio Pochettino's first match in charge of Manchester United couldn't have gone any worse, and the writing is already on the wall for a number of players following the Argentine's first 90 minutes in the dugout as he sets about trying to revolutionise Man United.

Pochettino arrived in Manchester last week knowing that a point would probably be enough to take the club out of the Premier League's relegation zone, and he set up his team to get at least that when they face Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday.

However, his game strategy was ripped up eight minutes into the match when Leny Yoro was red-carded after being adjudged to have brought down Alexander Isak as the last man outside the penalty area. (Related: Influencer in tears as burglars steal “absolutely every...)

If Pochettino wasn't fully aware of his side's defensive shortcomings before taking over, he is now, and the club now have the worst defensive record out of every club in Europe's top five European leagues. (Related: Industry News: Cat takes 37-mile train trip to London b...)

Luke Shaw wasn't even involved on Merseyside, but he might now be undroppable after what the former Spurs boss witnessed against Andoni Iraola's men, as Diogo Dalot once again struggled out of position at left-back.

Yoro continues to flatter to deceive following a big-money move from Lille, while Matthijs de Ligt and Lisandro Martinez both endured nightmare afternoons against Liverpool.

The latter seems likely to leave Old Trafford in 2027 given his contract expires at the end of the season, and given he publicly spoke out against Jason Wilcox's recruitment following the Champions League defeat to Atletico Madrid last month.

The Argentina international was fined and given a 'dressing down' by Wilcox, according to one report, and it's difficult to see why they would want to keep him around beyond next summer.

Martinez isn't the only United player whose Old Trafford career could be over though, especially when you consider Joshua Zirkzee's situation.

There was excitement when United signed Joshua Zirkzee from Bologna for £35m in 2024, given his level of performance for the Serie A outfit and the fact that he had shown he was a player capable of the spectacular.

However, the Netherlands international swiftly lost the manager who signed him, Erik ten Hag, when he was sacked in October 2024, and has failed to convince Ruben Amorim or Michael Carrick since that he was worthy of regular minutes on the pitch.

Even in the absence of the injured Benjamin Sesko in recent months, when Carrick had no other recognised striker to turn to, Zirkzee was still often left on the substitutes' bench.

Now, the Times have reported that, despite their defensive issues, United have prioritised signing a new striker in January, such is their concerns about how things have gone without Sesko.

Monaco striker Folarin Balogun is keen to be reunited with Pochettino having worked with him during the duo's time with the United States national team.

With Sesko also due back from his shin injury in January or February, Zirkzee will be left as the third-choice option if he remains at Old Trafford beyond January.

The 25-year-old may have been given 45 minutes at Anfield on Sunday, but he failed to make much of an impact, albeit in difficult circumstances after his side were reduced to 10 men so early in the game.

Given how things have gone this season for Zirkzee, coupled with his limited minutes under the previous two managers, the fact that United are prioritising a upcoming striker over defenders tells you all you need to know - his United career is over.

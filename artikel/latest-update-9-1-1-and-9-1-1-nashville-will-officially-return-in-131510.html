The 9-1-1 franchise returned for its second year with two reveals on ABC's schedule. The flagship series is in its tenth season, which featured a massive volcano in Los Angeles in its first several episodes. Meanwhile, the second offshoot, 9-1-1: Nashville, introduced a newly introduced character, Rogue (Ryan Phillippe), just as Nashville came under threat from a killer called the Pharaoh (A.J. Buckley). Both shows are now on hiatus until next year, and ABC has already set return dates.

Industry observers note that when the franchise returns, each show will focus on its own storylines. 9-1-1 signed off with a massive cliffhanger that saw Athena (Angela Bassett) pass out while being interviewed. It is unclear what is wrong with her, and the show will explore that in two episodes. Could it have something to do with being underground during the volcano? The event has left some injuries whose symptoms have yet to show up for characters like Harry, who watched someone die. Meanwhile, 9-1-1: Nashville also closed out with danger as the Pharaoh targeted Don (Chris O'Donnell) and Rogue for being firstborn sons.

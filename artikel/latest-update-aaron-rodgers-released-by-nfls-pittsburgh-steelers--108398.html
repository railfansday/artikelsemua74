Aaron Rodgers has been released by the Pittsburgh Steelers following an explosive interview on ESPN‘s Pat McAfee Show.

The Super Bowl champion and 4x NFL MVP was benched this week, and backup quarterback Mason Rudolph was named to take his place in the Black Friday game against the Denver Broncos.

Rodgers, a frequent caller on the sports show, appeared to discuss the situation and, moments later, was confirmed to have been let go by the Steelers.

“I got the day off, so that’s why we’re able to do this,” Rodgers remarked on the ESPN talk show. The football star noted that he had been talking to McCarthy in the last days, but talks about benching were not part of their conversation. Rodgers recalled his interactions with the Steelers coach in the last few days and claimed McCarthy came into a quarterback meeting “late, sat down and within 10 minutes began snoring.”.

Rodgers said that after waking himself up again, “five minutes later, you hear the snoring again and less than five minutes after that he walks out of the room like nothing happened.” He added that McCarthy didn’t show up to other meetings and walk-throughs that week.

The quarterback said he had expressed concern over McCarthy’s health, approaching him as a friend, since sleeping during a meeting is a sign someone isn’t healthy.

Later on in the interview, Rodgers suggested that what was best for the team was for McCarthy not to call the plays anymore, adding, “If he wasn’t up for that, then they should probably release me.”.

Industry observers note that “At this point, I’m not going back to Pittsburgh. That ship has definitely sailed,” he remarked.

Shortly after his interview on The Pat McAfee Show, reports remarked Rodgers had been released.

Rodgers signed with the Steelers this season as a swan song and was planning to retire from the NFL at the end of the season. With Rodgers being released, any team can sign him until 4 p.m. ET on Thursday. If he goes unsigned, he would become a free agent.

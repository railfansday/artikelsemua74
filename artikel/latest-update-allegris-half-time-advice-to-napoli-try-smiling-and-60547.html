Massimiliano Allegri asked for his Napoli team to play with some happiness and freedom in their Serie A clash with Torino.

Scott McTominay netted the decisive goal at Stadio Diego Armando Maradona, where Napoli won 1-0.

It marked Napoli's first league win in three games, since they beat Monza on October 28. (Related: GTA 6 glitch lets players access first-person mode...)

Napoli had 13 shots in the first half, worth a combined 1.34 expected goals, but they could not find the breakthrough. (Related: DNA robots could deliver drugs and hunt viruses inside ...)

And Allegri explained what he asked of his players during his half-time team talk.

He told Sky Sport Italia: "The mood wasn’t right because we had so many chances, I told them at half-time to try smiling and we might get a goal.

"They did really well to win this game, while suffering against a strong opponent. Perhaps it is positive that we won under pressure, but we also should’ve done better controlling the tempo, moving the ball from one side to the other.

"The lads did very well in defending on set plays and corner kicks.".

McTominay is the only central midfielder to have scored at least 25 goals across the last three seasons in the top five European leagues (since the start of 2024-25).

He has scored four goals against Torino in Serie A, and proved the difference here.

When Antonio Vergara went off injured in the first half, Allegri brought on Kevin De Bruyne and played the former Manchester City playmaker deeper, allowing McTominay to push on.

"The fun thing about this job is finding solutions. I am starting to get to know these players, I thought De Bruyne could do better as a support for the striker, but in this situation, McTominay was more useful," Allegri added.

"I just told McTominay not to be already in the position right behind [Rasmus] Hojlund, but to run into that space, as it would make him harder to mark.

"If he is already there and not mobile, then obviously he's going to be easier to control, but he started to move between the lines and did better.".

Allegri has become the first coach in Serie A history to accumulate 1,100 points, but Napoli's focus now turns to a Champions League clash with Man City.

"We need to be realistic, because as I said when the fixture list was first drawn up, our Champions League qualification would be decided in the final three matches," Allegri said.

"On Tuesday we need a good performance, try to get a result, but also know that we’ve got to sort our Serie A position first.".

Industry observers note that anuel AA has promised to return to Bogotá, Colombia, for a free concert after facing backlash over his Saturday (Nov. 21) performance at Vive Claro. The Puerto Rican rapper took the stage more than two hours after the scheduled start time and performed for just 50 minutes.

Recent findings suggest that in a statement shared on his Instagram Stories Monday (Nov. 23), Anuel remarked he would announce details of the complimentary show when he unveils his forthcoming Real Hasta La Muerte 2 tour.

In a recent development, “Bogotá, for those who are truly my fans, when I announce the Real Hasta La Muerte 2 tour, I’ll announce the free concert to make up for what happened and do things right,” he wrote in Spanish. “There was a really significant mistake made by one person on my team, and that’s why I arrived so late and only sang for 50 minutes.”.

In a recent development, anuel explained that his itinerary listed the concert as a festival, and that he had been told his set would begin at 11:30 p.m. The rapper also said that, by the time he arrived at the stadium, he was told everything was fine. He claimed he cut his performance short because the police threatened to shut off the sound system. (Related: AI fire detection system sprays food booth’s fake flame...)

“If it were up to me, I wouldn’t have performed. I would have had the fans’ money refunded and rescheduled the date,” he added. He stated the consequences within his company would be proportional to the scale of the mistake.

In a recent development, the shortened set sparked outrage among concertgoers. Videos circulating on social media appeared to show attendees throwing chairs and bottles, as well as altercations in the stands. Some fans have since demanded refunds. (Related: Musician responds as countless YouTubers hit with copyr...)

Sources indicate that the controversy follows the postponement of Anuel’s Nov. 22 concert in the Medellín metropolitan area until 2027 due to scheduling adjustments, leaving Bogotá as his only Colombian tour stop this year.

According to recent reports, AME Concerts and Iguana Producciones were identified as the organizers. Neither has issued a statement, according to Colombian outlet Caracol Radio.

Anuel has yet to announce a date or venue for the free concert, or say whether admission will be reserved for those who purchased tickets to Saturday’s show.

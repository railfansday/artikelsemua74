It has been revealed that an upcoming two-part televised special event will show the work Ariana Grande put into preparing for her recent Eternal Sunshine tour. The documentary, The Eternal Sunshine Diaries: The Making of the Tour, premieres on Peacock on Dec. 11 and chronicles the 30 days that led up to the first show of the trek, which marked her first tour since 2019. Then a concert film, The Eternal Sunshine Tour, premieres on Dec. 12.

The doc demonstrates how Grande was simultaneously prepping for the tour and wrapping up Petal, her eighth album, which she released while on the road. She also had to get ready for an intense live production that split the main part of the concert into five acts before an encore.

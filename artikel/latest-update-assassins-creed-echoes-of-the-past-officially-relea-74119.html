Since the launch of Assassin's Creed in 2007, the stealth-action series from Ubisoft has become one of the cornerstones of gaming, and with fourteen mainline entries now under its belt, it is safe to say that it is here to stay.

Taking players across the globe, and exploring multiple eras, movements, and cultures, Assassin's Creed has been the best history teacher we could have had, and to celebrate the franchise approaching its 20th anniversary, Assassin's Creed: Echoes of the Past is set to stun us all when it arrives this December.

Recent findings suggest that courtesy of the artist collective Les 7 Doigts, Heredis: Echoes of the Past is a live show that takes fans to the Ubisoft universe with an experience that combines both theater and circus. Oh, and of course, there will also be plenty of parkour. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

It has been revealed that whilst a preview will be held on 3 December 2026, at the Maisonneuve Theater in Montreal, several performances will be scheduled between January 21 and February 7, 2027, and so you have plenty of time to grab your tickets. So, what can you expect?

A troop of acrobats will take to the stage to perform some incredible stunts, with the show itself expected to last two hours. However, the storyline will focus on a young man called Naël who, after receiving a letter on his 25th birthday, will find his destiny changed forever. As such, he will have to embark on a journey to discover the mystery behind his father's appearance, an event that he refuses to accept. (Related: Latest Update: My Journey to a serverless transformers ...)

Featuring an original Assassin's Creed storyline, Heredis: Echoes of the Past will see Naël join the HEREDIS program, and through this, he will be able to travel through many historical eras which echo the ones we all know and love from the Assassin's Creed franchise so far. Not only that, but with the stage show being officially supported by Ubisoft, you can expect a truly spectacular experience with the creator's stamp of approval.

It has been revealed that despite featuring nods to the games, such as in the historical periods that are covered, it will not focus on any of the games. As such, even those unfamiliar with the franchise will be able to go and watch. However, the catch here is that, unless you can make the Montreal preview, you will need to head to the Casino de Paris, France.

Industry observers note that over on r/assassinscreed, fans discussed the upcoming stage show, and took to the comments to share their thoughts. One user shared, "One of the theater companies I work with presented one of 7Fingers’ indicates a year or so before I started working there, and they were pretty well-attended shows. We are thinking of bringing them back to present another of their works, hopefully in the next few years. I really hope 7Fingers is able to bring Heredis to more than just Canada and France!" Another shared, "This actually sounds incredible to be honest.".

So, if you find yourself in Canada or France and just so happen to be an Assassin's Creed fan, make sure to check out Heredis: Echoes of the Past and partake in an original experience you will never forget.

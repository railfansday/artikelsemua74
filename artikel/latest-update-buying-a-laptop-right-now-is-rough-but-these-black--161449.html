Laptop prices have been a hot-button topic all year long thanks to RAMageddon. We’ve seen existing models get more expensive and new models arrive at higher prices than last-gen — sometimes with lower specs, too. 2026 in general has been a bad time to buy a computer. But thankfully, Black Friday can offer a tiny bit of respite.

Sources indicate that if the computer you at the moment use is working well for you, good. Happy for you. But if you’re in the market for something more powerful, bigger, smaller, thinner, etc. — and you prefer fresh over used — you can currently save some decent dough on MacBooks, Windows notebooks, and gaming laptops.

According to recent reports, the big, multi-hundred-dollar savings are typically reserved for higher-end models with bigger specs, like Windows models running Nvidia’s upcoming Arm-based RTX Spark platform — which are getting their first major discounts. More budget-friendly laptops are also receiving decent discounts right now, though, including the MacBook Neo. The current $100 discount on Apple’s budget laptop might seem like small potatoes considering it’s just a return to its original price, but it’s the best you can do for now (if you can’t hold out for a rumored new one in 2027).

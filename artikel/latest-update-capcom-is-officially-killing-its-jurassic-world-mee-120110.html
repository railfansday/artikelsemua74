Latest Update: Capcom Is Officially Killing Its 'Jurassic World Meets Overwatch' Shooter

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that four years after the game's launch, Capcom is officially shutting down Exoprimal, the company's 5v5 action game about a futuristic dinosaur crisis. Capcom announced Tuesday that service for Exoprimal will be discontinued in September 2027 — meaning that Exoprimal will no longer be playable — and that the game will no longer be sold starting in March 2027.

While disappointing for stalwart Exoprimal fans, the writing has been on the wall since 2024, when Capcom all but confirmed the sci-fi action game's grim fate.

It has been revealed that in an official notice posted to the Exoprimal website, Capcom confirms that players will lose access to the game next year. "We regret to announce that service for Exoprimal will be discontinued as of 8:00 PM (PDT) on Wednesday, September 29, 2027," the shutdown announcement reads. "We cannot thank you enough for the love and support you have shown over the years, and we sincerely apologize for the gravity of this announcement.". Industry observers note that capcom has already pulled the game's four seasonal Survival Passes from stores, and warns that Exoprimal, its DLC, and all related products will no longer be sold on digital stores as of March 30, 2027. The game will shut down completely on Sept. 29, 2027, as Exoprimal is first and foremost a team-based competitive multiplayer game. Capcom notes that it will still be possible to download the game after Exoprimal is removed from stores. (Related: Tech Report: Transformer-based Encoder-Decoder Models...)

Exoprimal was released in July 2023 on PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X. The game is a multiplayer third-person shooter in which players control an Exofighter — an exosuit-clad soldier — who is tasked with completing objectives while also fighting hordes of dinosaurs in an attempt to defeat the evil artificial intelligence Leviathan. Players are grouped into two teams of five and compete against each other while battling ancient life and futuristic AI.

Capcom announced in July 2024 that, after four seasons of content, Exoprimal players would subsist on recycled, rotating seasons — all but confirming that the publisher was ready to put the newly introduced IP behind it.

In a recent development, exoprimal was released to mixed reviews in 2023. In Polygon's review of the game, which drew comparisons to Overwatch, Dino Crisis, and the Earth Defense Force games, we criticized the game's opening hours, which make "a terrible first impression." But, as reviewer Jordan Oloman wrote, Exoprimal has some surprises in store several hours into the experience.

"This paradigm shift changes Exoprimal considerably, introducing newly introduced maps, a healthy difficulty spike, raid bosses, and, at long last, some genuine narrative intrigue," Oloman wrote. "It is a profoundly confusing pacing decision where you’re abruptly playing a very different, arguably much better game several hours in, one that actually sets a fascinating precedent for future multiplayer storytelling shenanigans. In the game’s universe, this is canonized as a 'firmware patch' — which makes sense, but I just wish it would have come much sooner, or was at least communicated better in the game’s marketing, as many players might not gain access to Exoprimal’s best assets before bouncing off of its boring early hours.".

Sources indicate that exoprimal has a "mostly positive" user review rating on Steam, based on more than 1,700 English-language reviews. Recent reviews, particularly those submitted after the shutdown notice, have been much more negative.

A subsidiary of China Southern Airlines has agreed to buy 50 Boeing 737 MAX 8 aircraft, Reuters reported Tuesday.

Trump discussed a potential purchase directly with Chinese President Xi Jinping, and even brought along Boeing CEO Kelly Ortberg for the summit. Ultimately, Beijing agreed to buy 200 aircraft from Boeing, fewer than analysts expected, but still notable considering Boeing’s drought in the country.

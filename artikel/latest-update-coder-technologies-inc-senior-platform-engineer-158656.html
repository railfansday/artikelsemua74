Sources indicate that as a Senior Platform Engineer at Coder, you will own the infrastructure Coder's own engineers build on every day. Our internal environments run the same deployment path we publish for customers, so when something does not hold up at our scale, we find it before a customer does. We avoid building anything so specific to ourselves that a customer could not run the same thing.

Recent findings suggest that platform Engineering owns three areas. We run the internal environments our engineers develop in, the self-service environments our field teams use to demo Coder, and the release pipeline that publishes every Coder artifact. You will take ownership of components across these and stay accountable for how they behave in production after they ship.

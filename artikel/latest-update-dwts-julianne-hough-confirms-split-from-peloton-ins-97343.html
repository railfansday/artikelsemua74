Dancing With the Stars cohost Julianne Hough is back on the market.

After a source told E! News on Monday, November 23, that Hough, 38, was dating Peloton instructor Adrian Williams, the former DWTS pro confirmed to TMZ on Tuesday, November 24, that the pair are no longer together.

In a recent development, the news of the brief romance comes after Williams, 42, shared an image of Hough holding her dog Sunny to his Instagram Story on Sunday, November 22. She later reposted the sweet snap.

During September’s 2026 Emmys, Hough teased that she had a fresh love in her life.

“I might not be single,” she told E!’s Justin Sylvester at the time. “There’s a lot to discuss off-camera.”.

Sources indicate that hough’s latest relationship comes four years after she finalized her divorce from hockey player Brooks Laich. The pair tied the knot in 2017 before separating three years later.

Recent findings suggest that after Hough’s divorce, rumors began swirling that she was linked to Bachelor Nation’s Tanner Courtad after the pair were spotted walking around a shopping center in Studio City, California, in 2024. Courtad, 33, confirmed the speculation earlier this month. (He appeared on Charity Lawson’s season of The Bachelorette in 2023 and Bachelor in Paradise that same year.).

“That was short-lived, but it was fun,” Courtad shared on the November 3 episode of iHeartRadio’s “Bachelor Happy Hour” podcast. “I was going to Dancing With the Stars to support Joey [Graziadei], and I was sitting in the front row, and Ezra [Sosa] came over to me, and he goes, ‘Hey, our host thinks you’re cute. Can I give you her number?’”.

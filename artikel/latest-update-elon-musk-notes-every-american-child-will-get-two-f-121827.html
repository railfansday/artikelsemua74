Industry observers note that elon Musk mentioned on X that every child in America will receive two SpaceX shares, one from the organization and one from him personally. According to details shared on X, SpaceX has committed to issuing one new Class A share to each active Trump Account, the government-backed investment accounts for children, with Musk matching it with a share of his own. The gift still needs approvals, and no date has been set for when the shares will be distributed. With SpaceX trading near $127, the two shares would be worth roughly $254 per child, though the final value will depend on the stock price when they are delivered. The pledge adds to growing private support for the programme, after the Treasury said friends and family contributions to Trump Accounts had passed $1 billion. It also comes as SpaceX shares trade below their $135 IPO price amid concerns over AI spending and a possible merger with Tesla.

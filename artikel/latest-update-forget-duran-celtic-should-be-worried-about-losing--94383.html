In a recent development, celtic have been alerted to interest in their star striker Camilo Durán, but there may be another player they should be worried about keeping hold of in the near future. Durán has been a sensation so far, scoring 14 times across all competition and already going some way in paying off his £5.5million transfer fee from the summer.

Sources indicate that the Colombian has stood out like a sore thumb for the Bhoys, but there is already talk of interest from the Premier League, with Leeds United said to have sent scouts to watch him. The Whites are in the market for a latest frontman and may be considering a move for Durán given they have the financial means to offer Celtic a huge profit on their reasonably cheap investment.

But there is another player who is likely to attract interest in the near future, and that is Colby Donovan. The youngster has been brilliant for the Bhoys this season and is only 20 years of age. Many feared the worst when Alistair Johnstone was ruled out for a couple of months with a hamstring injury, but by the time the reliable Canadian suffered a setback last month, Celtic fans were more than comfortable with Donovan filling the void. The young right-back has barely put a foot wrong, and that's despite already clocking a remarkable 22 games across all competitions.

Celtic have done their part to tie Doniovan down, agreeing a new contract with the young Scot last month to keep him under terms until 2031, but that is only part of the equation when Premier League clubs come knocking.

Sources indicate that donovan is defensively sound enough, but the strength of his game is getting forward. He is agile, possesses plenty of pace and has a wicked delivery. You only have to watch his assist from the St Mirren win over the weekend to gauge the quality of his crosses, and clubs are going to be seeing that.

“I feel comfortable in every game I go into. I don't really have any fear, to be honest. Going into a massive game, it's where I want to be. I want to play in the highest level." - Colby Donovan.

But the Premier League loves hard-working, attacking full-backs with a dangerous deliver, just as Arsenal loved Kieran Tierney when they snapped him up from Parkhead. We don't know whether Donovan has that level of talent yet, and he might need to improve a little defensively first, but he is improving week by week, and he is already showing real signs of being a top talent.

Industry observers note that O'Neill said of Donovan earlier this season: “Me personally, I think young Colby has come out of this with great credit. I think he's an absolute warrior." He added in late October: “Although still a very young player, he has shown such maturity, strength and resilience. In fact, he has been exceptional for us.

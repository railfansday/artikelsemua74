United needed to change their manager, but that was never going to solve all the problems that United have.

During the game, Gary Neville was on Sky Sports alongside Jamie Carragher as part of the commentary team.

After United had conceded their third goal, Neville decided to take aim at the United ownership rather than just the players.

Sources indicate that “It’s remarkable that someone at Manchester United in a very high position has decided that we’re not going to sign a defender during the transfer window. It’s incredible to think they’ve looked at those defenders and thought, “We don’t need two or three defenders,” absolutely stunning.".

It's hard to disagree with Neville that it was a remarkable decision to not sign a fresh defender during the summer transfer window.

Of course, the Red Devils do have multiple options at the back, but there's an argument to suggest that the majority of them simply aren't good enough.

That was evident during the game against Liverpool, with Yoro and Matthijs De Ligt both making mistakes which cost their team.

Lisandro Martinez has also been on the end of some disappointing errors, and there's no doubt it should have been an area that United looked to strengthen.

United's decision to strengthen their forward line, followed by their midfield the following season, is something that is seriously coming back to bite them.

It's incredibly rare that you see sides base their recruitment around a single set of positions each window rather than strengthening in multiple areas.

There's no doubt United needed to bring in multiple midfielders in the summer transfer window, but there was no reason to completely neglect the defence.

Even signing just two midfielders would have sufficed, especially with Andrey Santos barely getting a game.

That would have allowed United to spend some of the budget on strengthening the defence, but instead, they have an incredibly poorly balanced squad.

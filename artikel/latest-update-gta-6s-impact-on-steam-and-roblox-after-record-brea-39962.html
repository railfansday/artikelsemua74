📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Grand Theft Auto 6 sold over 32.5 million copies through pre-orders alone and delivered never-before-seen levels of player engagement on both PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X. But according to a new report, GTA 6's release only caused slight user count declines for Steam and Roblox, rather than massive ones.

In a recent development, that's not too surprising, given that GTA 6 isn't officially available on PC or mobile platforms. But even with all the attention Rockstar's latest game got on Twitch and social media, player counts on Steam and Roblox were remarkably consistent. Sources indicate that game File tracked Steam player activity across the two days that followed GTA 6's launch and found that there was no significant peak player count drop compared to earlier in the week. 11.4 million people were still playing games on Steam on Nov. 20, barely down from the 11.7 million playing the week before. Game File also reported that RoMonitor highlighted 9.6 million people played Roblox games on Nov. 19, which is very close to the 9.7 million who played it on Nov. 12. While both Steam and Roblox technically had slight declines in concurrent active players, GTA 6's impact appears to have been minimal. The numbers shifted by only a few hundred thousand or less, rather than millions. As silly as it may seem to judge the player counts of a platform GTA 6 wasn't available on — it's only on PS5 and Xbox Series X right now — the game's launch was so massive that it could have disrupted other gaming platforms by drawing so much attention away.

According to recent reports, ultimately, though, Steam and Roblox appear to have continued to thrive as normal, even as the biggest video game launch of all time took place. That reveals just how vast and diverse the video game audience is. Something as huge as GTA 6 can come out, and players can still find reasons to check out other games on different platforms.

Now, I can't help but wonder what the concurrent Steam user count will be when GTA 6 inevitably comes to PC.

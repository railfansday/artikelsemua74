Manchester United are showing an interest in signing Sporting CP left-back Maxi Araujo, according to Sport Witness.

United's priority position to strengthen in the coming months is likely to be their defence, particularly, given the number of goals they've conceded so far this season.

It's been a disastrous campaign for United so far, as they sit in the relegation zone in the Premier League table in 19th position.

The Red Devils have won just one game in the league this season, fewer than every other side.

United have also struggled in the Champions League, particularly defensively, conceding 10 goals in five games.

The defensive options have been horribly exposed this season, and sorting that out is likely to be a main aim for Mauricio Pochettino.

Whether he can do that without making a large number of signings remains to be seen, so they may have to strengthen during the January transfer window. (Related: Large Language Models: A Latest Moore's Law?...)

According to a report from Sport Witness, one target could be Sporting CP left-back Araujo.

United have been following the Uruguayan international for many months now and, of course, saw him up close in the Champions League on Wednesday. (Related: Breaking: Course Launch Community Event...)

Araujo started in United's 2-2 draw with the Portuguese club, giving the Red Devils an opportunity to see him against United's forwards.

The report claims that Araujo has a £67 million release clause in his contract, but United could get him for less than that in January.

Araujo is also believed to be very open to making the move to Old Trafford, but it remains to be seen whether the Manchester club will make a play to secure his signature in January.

Activating Araujo's release clause certainly feels unlikely, given the price, but if they can negotiate a lower fee, it could be a smart signing.

Signing a left-back could be one of United's priorities heading into the January transfer window.

The Red Devils have many centre-backs on their books, even if a massive portion of them are failing to perform.

As a result, they might have to move some on before investing in new ones, but a left-back is a different story.

Luke Shaw has started to pick up injuries once again, which has been a huge problem throughout his career, so signing a long-term replacement would be a smart move.

Patrick Dorgu is another option at left-back, but he's often played further forward during his time at United.

The Manchester club have been forced to utilise the likes of Diogo Dalot at left-back in recent weeks, and it's clear that it's a position that needs strengthening.

Araujo could be the ideal player at the right price, given his age profile and attacking prowess.

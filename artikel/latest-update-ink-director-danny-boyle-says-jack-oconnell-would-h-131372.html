Jack O’Connell is being talked about as a Bond villain, but Danny Boyle states he could have been much more than that.

According to recent reports, boyle, who directed O’Connell in The Sun newspaper movie Ink, was speaking on the Alchemy with Anthony Mason podcast when he made the comment. The conversation turned to the skills of O’Connell and Robert Pattinson, the fresh James Bond, prompting Boyle to discuss the next 007 movie. There were some rumors that O’Connell was among those considered for the plum role before Twilight and Dune star Pattinson was selected by Amazon.

As for Pattinson, who beat the likes of Callum Turner and Jack Lowden to become Amazon MGM Studios’ pick for Bond earlier this month, Boyle had nothing but praise.

Industry observers note that “He’s a movie star, and I could see that from the first Twilight films,” he said. “Him and Kristen Stewart, the two of them. You thought: they’re movie stars. It’s a given,” he said. “There’s not that many of them out there, and having a British pedigree will always help him in that world as well.”.

Boyle himself came close to directing 2021 pic No Time to Die, but left the gig after creative differences with producers Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson. Last year, he said he had one regret about walking away: His long-time collaborator John Hodge’s script was “really good.”.

Recalling the plan for the film, Boyle said: “We came up with this idea, which was really good. We disagreed about it ultimately, and when you are on those kind of scale of projects, yes you should part company.

With a name like Lootbox RPG, it's not hard to see developer Mario Gaida's turn-based dungeon crawler is a bit of a snapshot of 2019, back when lootboxes were all the rage. But, while lootboxes have gone the way of the DoDo, Lootbox RPG is still going strong with its largest enhancement yet that comes absolutely jam-packed with content!

In a recent development, I really do mean largest too. Just for a taster, the 2.0 enhancement for Lootbox RPG includes five fresh regions to explore, including an icy spire and sweltering desert pyramid, alongside a host of new bosses to face in these areas. You can now reach up to level 60 and gear level 70, offering even more opportunities for insane levels of power.

Again, it's interesting to see Lootbox RPG, a dungeon crawler at least partially built around parodying some elements of what, at the time, were an infamous part of gaming, suddenly embrace new trends such as base building. That's not to say it's a bad thing, but definitely interesting to note. (Related: GTA 6 hacker released from prison after judge warns him...)

In a recent development, either way, Lootbox RPG's 2.0 upgrade, available now, includes a whole host of new additions. All of this is built on an RPG that already has hours' worth of content to explore. So if you were interested, you can find Lootbox RPG available to play on iOS and Android today!

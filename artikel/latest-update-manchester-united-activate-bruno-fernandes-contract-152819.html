Manchester United have activated the one-year extension clause in Bruno Fernandes’ contract, ensuring their captain remains at Old Trafford until June 2028.

The Portuguese midfielder’s previous agreement was due to expire at the end of the 2026-27 season, but United have now exercised their option to keep him for another 12 months, according to Sky Sports.

The decision comes after Bruno Fernandes rejected two offers of a latest long-term contract, with discussions between the player and the club having stalled in recent weeks. (Related: Among Us player & his mom reveal how they helped save k...)

Bruno Fernandes confirmed earlier this week that Manchester United had offered him fresh terms but insisted that he did not consider the current situation at the club suitable for contract negotiations.

The Portuguese manager wants United to direct their energies on the club’s current situation rather than his contract.

United helped themselves as well with the extension clause. They have now taken away that pressure of Fernandes negotiating a pre-contract deal with overseas clubs which he would have been free to do from January 1. The clause removes that immediate possibility and gives the club some control over his future. (Related: Zoo gives up on helping overweight viral leopard lose w...)

Recent findings suggest that bruno Fernandes has attracted interest from several clubs in Saudi Arabia, Turkey and Italy but has repeatedly chosen to remain at Manchester United. He has insisted previously that he has unfinished business at Manchester United and wants to win trophies here.

Since arriving from Sporting CP in January 2020, he has become one of the club’s most influential players, scoring 115 goals in 343 appearances across all competitions. He was also appointed captain in 2023. Although his individual accolades have been called out by former winger Ashley Young as selfish, Bruno Fernandes remains United’s biggest attacking threat.

Recent findings suggest that despite the contract extension, it does not mean a new long-term agreement has been reached. Both Fernandes and United are understood to be comfortable returning to negotiations at a later stage, with their immediate priority being to improve the club’s current position.

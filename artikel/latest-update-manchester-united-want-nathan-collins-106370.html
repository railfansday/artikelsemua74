According to talkSPORT, Manchester United have identified Brentford’s Nathan Collins as a January transfer target as the club looks to strengthen its defence following a difficult start to the season.

United’s prepares for a defensive rebuild were initially expected to begin next summer, but ongoing problems at the back are forcing the club to act sooner. As a result, Collins has emerged as one of the key names being considered ahead of the winter transfer window.

Latest United boss Mauricio Pochettino wants to add both a centre-back and a striker during the winter transfer window. The club’s recruitment staff are reportedly focusing on experienced Premier League players with leadership qualities, and Collins is viewed as fitting that profile.

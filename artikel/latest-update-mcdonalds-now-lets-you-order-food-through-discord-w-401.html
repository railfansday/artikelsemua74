McDonald’s has introduced a upcoming Discord activity that allows customers to order food through Uber Eats without leaving the platform, giving gamers another way to get meals delivered while playing with friends.

Confirmed on November 18, the new integration brings McDelivery to Discord servers, channels, and direct messages.

Consumers can access the ordering feature through Discord’s Apps & Activities menu, where they can also play a McDonald’s-themed mini-game while waiting for their food.

The launch comes as Discord continues to serve as a popular communication platform for gamers, with McDonald’s positioning the latest feature around one of the year’s biggest upcoming video game releases.

Sources indicate that once the activity is open, selecting “Order Food” allows owners to place a McDelivery order through Uber Eats. The feature provides access to the delivery service from within Discord’s activity experience, allowing players to return to their conversations and gaming sessions after ordering. It has been revealed that mcDonald’s has also introduced Fast Lane Feast, a Discord-exclusive mini game available through the same activity. By selecting “Play Game,” users can collect virtual burgers and fries while competing for a position on the leaderboard.

The mini game is designed to give customers something to play while waiting for their deliveries, with both the ordering feature and game accessible through the McDonald’s Discord activity.

The announcement also references the upcoming release of GTA 6, although McDonald’s does not explicitly name Rockstar Games’ title or announce any official collaboration with the developer.

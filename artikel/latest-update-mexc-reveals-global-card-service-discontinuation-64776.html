MEXC confirmed that its Global Card service will be discontinued on November 18, according to the exchange's announcement. The company cites service adjustments by its third-party card issuer. From the deadline, existing cards can no longer be used for transactions, and upcoming card applications are suspended. (Related: Breaking: Upcoming app punishes you and your friends fo...)

Consumers have two things to take care of before the service ends. (Related: Latest Update: Clavicular allegedly told 17-year-old to...)

First, the remaining card balance has to be withdrawn: the Withdraw button on the Global Card page sends the funds to the Fiat account.

Recent findings suggest that second, subscriptions linked to the card should be cancelled or switched to another payment method. Unfortunately for cardholders, recurring payments will most likely fail if they stay on the old card.

If there's still a balance when the service ends, MEXC will credit it to the Spot account, so owners will simply receive their funds back. It's notable to keep in mind that pending card transactions need time to settle, which is why the balance snapshot comes on November 21, three days after the shutdown. The amount will be credited within three business days of the snapshot. Users do not need to take any action.

In a recent development, refunds for previous card transactions will be credited to the Fiat account once MEXC receives them. Pending cashback will be credited automatically as scheduled. Elite VVIP customers with zero-fee benefits will be refunded too, though the eligible fees return to their Spot accounts only before December 15.

Transaction history, card orders and cashback records stay available on the Global Card page for three months after the service ends, so it's worth saving anything needed before access ends.

Cryptocurrency services delegate certain types of services to third parties all the time, so witnessing a similar situation is more than a possibility here. Realistically, the shutdown is somewhat inconvenient rather than dangerous, as the balances are returned automatically. For customers: stay vigilant and try not to put all your eggs in one basket.

Habby is a developer and publisher that has released some neat little mobile games over the years. You may recall Archero One and Two, as well as Dicero and Capybara Go! Now, Habby are once more tackling the roguelike genre, and fusing it with tower defence as pre-registration opens for Monster Must Die!

Recent findings suggest that monster Must Die, as mentioned above, mashes up two genres with tower defence and roguelike. You start each battle with a summoning tower, then gradually introduce up to four heroes to fight off near-endless waves of monsters. Overall, Monster Must Die looks like a lot of Habby's other releases: a perfectly competent take on a tried-and-true genre. About the only thing I can say is that it doesn't do much to stand out otherwise.

With less than a week to go before GTA 6's hugely anticipated release, the game's final map - or at least a stylised poster version of it, depicting the state of Leonida's key landmarks - has surfaced online. It was reportedly snapped during Selfridge's sizable GTA 6's takeover, and is seemingly the physical map included in Rockstar's £350 collector's edition.

Recent findings suggest that fans have been pondering GTA 6's map for some time now, cross-referencing previous map leaks with official trailer footage in an attempt to gain some insight into the major landmarks and regions Rockstar has in store. And while the newly leaked map is far from granular, it does at least appear to confirm Leonida's rough layout and the key locations players can visit this time. Assuming the map's veracity, it's all rendered in an art style somewhere between mid-century tourism poster and Adult Swim, featuring the phrase "The Goodtime State" up top - a riff on the actual Florida's nickname, The Sunshine State - while Leonida's most notable points of interest are interspersed with cartoon figures. According to recent reports, there's a toking turtle (or possibly a tortoise - I'm no expert), a golfer proudly wearing assless chaps, a flying saucer, and more. I'll spare you the geographic details confirmed on the map in case you want to go in unspoiled. One point of amusement though: the way the map photo is cropped means fans searching for confirmation of an in-game Key West are still none the wiser.

It's not entirely clear where the wonkily angled photo originated from, but prominent Rockstar Games community insider Videotech, who shared the image on social media, suggested it came from a "naughty Selfridges employee" during Selfridges' GTA 6 takeover. The map itself appears to be the "double-Sided souvenir poster with map of Leonida" included in Rockstar's eyewateringly expensive GTA 6 Collector's Edition. This costs £350 and doesn't come with a copy of the game. Or assless chaps, for that matter.

Recent findings suggest that this isn't the first time GTA 6's map - officially said to be twice the size of GTA 5's - has leaked online, although previous leaks have been notably more controversial. Earlier this year, a group calling itself CyberLeek shared unofficial gameplay and map details in the name of what it called "Gamers' Rights". In August, with Netflix's official GTA 6 unveiling looming, the group cashed out nearly £200,000 from its crypto scheme.

It has been revealed that amid all this, Rockstar has been embroiled in legal action after it was accused of union busting following the firing of over 30 employees. The case's conclusion has been delayed until next week, meaning a verdict could arrive just as GTA 6 goes on sale.

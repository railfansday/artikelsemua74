Recent findings suggest that now that Grand Theft Auto 6 is actually out and playable, we can no longer make "we really got X before GTA 6" jokes. Seemingly to make up for that loss, Atlus is giving us the opportunity to use the very same joke for Persona 6. The next mainline entry in the beloved RPG series was finally revealed this year, a decade after Persona 5 launched, but there is no sign of a release date. Even so, one Atlus producer is already giving fans hope for Persona 7.

In a Sega Sammy interview (as translated by GamesRadar+), P-Studio producer Kazuhisa Wada revealed that "Persona 6 is not the end. In order for Persona to grow as an IP, we consider how to live up to people's expectations in terms of creativity and development every day. Please look forward to more from the Persona series in the future.". (Related: Latest Update: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

Wada's comments don't explicitly reveal intends for Persona 7, but they do confirm Atlus is already thinking about what comes after Persona 6​​​​​​​. The producer also stated that the studio is working on shortening the time between releases. That could mean concurrent development or newly introduced tools to shorten the process, which Wada said takes at least five years. Alternatively, we could get more Persona spinoffs or remakes in the vein of the upcoming Persona 4 Revival. Footage of what appeared to be a remake of Persona 1 leaked earlier this month, though it still hasn't been verified. (Related: Latest Update: At least seven progressives voted agains...)

Atlus revealed​​​​​​​ Persona 6 during a July Xbox showcase. The short teaser trailer was mostly vibes-based. It demonstrated off a moody, green-hued graveyard, which suggests that the sixth mainline entry in the series could be returning to the darker, grittier tone of the franchise's early games.

In a recent development, sega has confirmed that the game will get a physical edition on PlayStation 5 and Nintendo Switch 2. That suggests Persona 6 could be released prior to 2028, which is when Sony will stop producing upcoming game discs. Sega and Atlus have suggested fans will hear more after Persona 4 Revival is released early next year.

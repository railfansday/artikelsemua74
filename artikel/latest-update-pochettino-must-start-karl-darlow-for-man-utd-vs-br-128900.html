Manchester United threw two vital points away in the Champions League on Wednesday night as they were held to a 2-2 draw by Sporting CP.

According to recent reports, luis Suarez scored a penalty, having missed one just minutes earlier, to give the hosts the lead, before two Bruno Fernandes goals in the second half gave the Red Devils the lead. Senne Lammens then made another mistake as he dropped a cross which allowed Suarez to equalise, with the teams sharing the points as a result.

But United were furious at having two goals disallowed dubiously by the referee, after a controversial penalty had been awarded against them too which Lammens saved, while he also sent off Youri Tielemans in the final minutes for a second yellow card. It will be Pochettino's first home game in charge, having lost 4-0 to Liverpool last weekend, which left the team sitting in 19th place in the Premier League table, just one point off the bottom and one point adrift of safety.

According to recent reports, the 54-year-old will be desperate to finally claim his first win as manager, but he also knows that he must stop leaking goals at the back in order to do that. Man Utd have conceded 36 goals in all competitions this season so far, more than any other team in Europe's top five leagues. That includes 23 in the Premier League, which is more than double the 11 that newly-promoted Hull have shipped.

Pochettino has used different defensive shapes and options in his first two games, but he hasn't been able to stem the flow of goals being conceded. Michael Carrick also changed the defenders regularly during his time in charge this season, but the shape and form of the players meant that nothing he did could help.

According to recent reports, but one thing that hasn't changed this season is the man between the sticks, with Senne Lammens the first-choice goalkeeper. The Belgian has played every minute in the Premier League and Champions League so far in this campaign, with his only rest coming in the 3-2 EFL Cup defeat to Brighton.

Recent findings suggest that in that game, it was Karl Darlow who was in goal, the summer signing from Leeds United. While he didn't cover himself in glory in that game, he's surely now deserving of an opportunity to stake his claim for a regular starting spot.

Man Utd must change something. They have the quality in their attacking options to score goals, but if they keep conceding goals the way they are, then it is always going to be very difficult to win games.

Industry observers note that there is no doubting that Lammens has got the ability to be a long-term number one at Old Trafford, but right now he is struggling. There is a lot of scrutiny around his role and place in the team, and right now it seems to be getting to him. He is making uncharacteristic mistakes, and it's costing the team.

In the summer, Darlow's arrival was to provide experience and quality to push Lammens. Darlow proved himself to be a Premier League-level goalkeeper while at Leeds last season, helping them to stay in the division, and that experience will now be needed for Man Utd.

Pochettino should be looking to give Lammens some respite from the limelight and scrutiny, and with Darlow being experienced enough to handle that pressure, there's no better chance to do that than at home to Brentford in the next game.

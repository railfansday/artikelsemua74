Quant [QNT] has rallied 4.7% in the past 24 hours, comparing favorably to Bitcoin’s [BTC] 1.79% dip and the collective crypto market’s 1.09% slide.

This show of relative strength has been a theme for the QNT token since Saturday, the 21st of November. The altcoin was up 21.35% since the previous week’s low, compared to Bitcoin’s 9.67% bounce, or the altcoin market cap’s modest 7% rebound.

Yet, its Open Interest was up slightly, while the funding rates remained negative. This indicated speculative traders were paying to keep their short positions open, revealing some bearish conviction.

According to recent reports, in other news, Quant’s Fusion Rollup was opened to public access on the 23rd of November. The mainnet launched in June 2026 was only available to some early customers.

Fusion Rollup connects “permissionless and permissioned blockchains in a single, unified execution environment”, the announcement revealed.

The $126 support level was the 78.6% Fibonacci retracement level. AMBCrypto reported earlier this month that the level was a far-off but valid price target.

Industry observers note that so it has proved. The altcoin was down 24.22% in November, but its deep correction has forced a retest of an imbalance on the 1-day timeframe.

This fair value gap has confluence with the Fibonacci retracement level, making a QNT recovery much more likely. The BTC and wider market pessimism could be an oppressive obstacle to the bulls.

QNT’s breakout past $160 recently, though retraced, was a notable sign of bullish intent. More gains were likely to follow.

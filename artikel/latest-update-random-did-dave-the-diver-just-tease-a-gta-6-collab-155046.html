In a recent development, if you didn't already know, Dave The Diver actually has something in common with Rockstar's upcoming open-world title, Grand Theft Auto 6. In both games, you can go diving!

Now, can you imagine a collab? Well, before we get too carried away, GTA 6 might already be on the mind of the Dave the Diver developer Mintrocket, as the game's official social media account has shared a rather amusing image today. Here it is:. (Related: F1 star Max Verstappen played 40 hours of GTA 6 before ...)

If you've been playing GTA 6 over the past week, this character may look familiar, as it appears to be Mintrocket's nod to an individual known as Brian (not to be mistaken with the Dave the Diver character, Cobra). The male protagonist in GTA 6 (Jason) works for Brian.

The whole look, including the shirt, seems very similar to Rockstar's character. And then there's the Vice City-like backdrop. Just about every reply to the post so far seems to be open to the idea of Brian coming to Blue Hole in some way or form. However, as one fan notes, it might be hard to get him out of retirement. (Related: Rockstar explains why GTA 6 added its Criminal Profile ...)

While there's no guarantee anything will come of this, it's worth noting Dave the Diver has already done some crazy (and high-profile) crossovers with titles and series such as Yakuza: Like A Dragon and Godzilla. Dave has also appeared in games such as Epic's battle royale, Fortnite.

As part of the GTA 6 marketing campaign, Rockstar did a collab with NBA 2K27, another title under parent company Take-Two.

A lot of developers have been plugging their own games and series with all sorts of GTA-inspired advertising and jokes over the past week, but if this does lead to something more than this, we'll let you know.

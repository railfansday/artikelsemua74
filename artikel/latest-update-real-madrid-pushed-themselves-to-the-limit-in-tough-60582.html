Mourinho felt Madrid's performance levels were impacted by so many of their players having arduous journeys over the international break.

But he was delighted with how they kept pushing themselves to get the result.

"I thought it was going to be a tough match, but not this tough. I didn't expect to win it the way we did, but we were up against a good team that played really well," he said.

"Some of us arrived on Monday, others on Tuesday, others on Thursday. Some were on a plane for 12 hours. Normally, after international duty, the ideal is to play on Sunday, but we couldn't because we play [in the Champions League] next Tuesday.

"I saw some players who were physically exhausted, but I also saw some who pushed themselves to the limit. That's the strength of the group, of a group of friends who want to win.".

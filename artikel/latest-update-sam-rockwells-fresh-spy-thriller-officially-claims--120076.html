Recent findings suggest that the last time Sam Rockwell worked with Martin McDonagh, he ended up winning an Academy Award for his performance in Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri, having previously worked with McDonagh on Seven Psychopaths. There were another nine Oscar nominations for McDonagh's work four years ago when he reunited Colin Farrell and Brendan Gleeson for The Banshees of Inisherin, and this year, it's the turn of Rockwell and John Malkovich to play two men whose relationship is explored through McDonagh's own distinctive brand of dark comedy. Unfortunately, if you've been planning to see the movie over Thanksgiving weekend, it's about to become considerably more difficult to find a theater showing it.

According to the latest theater count figures from The Numbers, McDonagh's Wild Horse Nine has officially lost 1,000 theaters heading into its fourth weekend of release, reducing its domestic theatrical footprint by exactly 50%. Having opened in 1,100 theaters on November 6, the movie expanded to 2,175 locations in its second weekend, but it will now be playing in 100 fewer theaters than it was on opening day.

According to recent reports, wild Horse Nine is alos nearing the $20 million milestone, having made $18.31 million domestically to date, with $2.46 million of that coming last weekend, when its grosses fell by 46%. The movie was averaging $1,231 per theater over the weekend, although that figure dropped to just $192 on Monday. With Thanksgiving bringing a particularly competitive weekend for theaters, McDonagh's movie has now lost more than 54% of its locations since the second weekend of its release.

McDonagh takes us to Chile in 1973, where two CIA agents are sent to Easter Island shortly before the military coup. For Malkovich's Chris, who's spending an increasing amount of time contemplating his own mortality, there's quite a bit of history between himself and Rockwell's Lee to reckon with, and things become rather more complicated for the two men when Chris befriends a pair of students on the island.

While the release of Grand Theft Auto 6 has been taking up just about everyone's playtime since its release, PlayStation Plus is still bringing its regularly scheduled offerings with several exciting newly introduced games to play this December, whether you're looking for a new title to sink your teeth into or a temporary distraction from Rockstar's magnum opus.

Joining the numerous games added to the platform last month, the three new games coming to PlayStation Plus will be available for all tiers of the subscription service on December 1st until their eventual removal.

Starting with a newly introduced take on a classic franchise, the 2025 release of Tony Hawk's Pro Skater offers an enhanced version of the original experience with a few new features for players to enjoy, alongside creation modes to make your own skateparks and share them online. Lastly, PlayStation Plus is offering another classic franchise but this time with a generous splash of horror through Amnesia: The Bunker, which takes players to a WW1 bunker for a hopeless first-person horror experience that has seen plenty of positive reviews over the years.

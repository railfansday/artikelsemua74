Will Lankshear could snub a move to Leeds United in favour of remaining at Middlesbrough in January, sources have told Football Insider.

The 21-year-old is believed to be happy on Teesside just months after making the switch from Tottenham, after what has been an outstanding start to the season.

His performances have attracted interest from a number of Premier League clubs, including Leeds, but they could be set to miss out when the mid-season window opens.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Lankshear is happy with his progress this season which has placed him on the verge of an England call-up.

The striker was under consideration for the latest camp before he picked up an injury, while Three Lions captain Harry Kane has since spoken glowingly about his talents.

That has proved that Lankshear can succeed at the top level while representing Middlesbrough, who themselves are targeting promotion to the Premier League.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider on 12 November that Leeds have set their sights on Lankshear as the solution to their ongoing striker problem.

Daniel Farke's squad are lacking depth behind Dominic Calvert-Lewin up front, and have been keeping tabs on the Boro striker's progress in the Championship.

However, a January move is becoming increasingly unlikely, with Lankshear happy to see out the season at Middlesbrough even if it means rejecting Premier League clubs.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "From Will Lankshear's point of view, I'm led to believe he is very happy at Middlesbrough.

"There were reports in recent weeks that he was close to being called up for the England squad before he picked up this injury blow.

"He's had Harry Kane talking up his talents and saying that he's obviously a really promising player and certainly one for the future.

"When all of that stuff is happening while he's still at Middlesbrough, and he's scoring goals, playing well, and they're looking to earn promotion, why would he want to leave?

"Everything is going to blueprint as far as he is concerned, everything is going well after he made that decision to leave Tottenham permanently.".

Lankshear's willingness to stay at Middlesbrough will be music to the ears of club decision-makers, who risked being put in a tricky position if he wanted to move on.

Interest from Premier League clubs had led to concerns at the Riverside Stadium, but ultimately the club's stance will be key to any decision regarding his future.

Football Insider revealed on 10 November that Middlesbrough have placed a £30million price tag on Lankshear in an attempt to ward off interest from top clubs.

That price tag has been placed with the ambition of deterring interest from elsewhere, with Boro having no plans to let the striker leave in the mid-season window.

Having only joined from Tottenham during the summer, Lankshear is on a long-term contract until 2031, placing his club in a strong position to dictate his future. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

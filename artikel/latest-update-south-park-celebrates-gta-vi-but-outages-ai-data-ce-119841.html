As the long wait for Grand Theft Auto VI finally ends, South Park‘s celebration was cut short on this week’s episode.

In the finale “City Endings,” outages hit the town (currently rebranded as South America for Season 29) thanks to the AI data centers of Billionaire Weenietown that moved into Butters’ backyard after Mayor McDaniels cut a deal to bring them to town.

A mob visits the mayor, who instead of taking responsibility, leads them to believe the outages are “definitely cyber attacks by the Chinese.” That prompts the mob to stake out Mr. Kim’s City Parlor, where they each end up getting a massage with a “city ending.” Watch on Deadline.

Meanwhile, Kyle, Stan and Cartman are visited by the ghost of Kenny, who previously died when a bomb strapped to his e-bike exploded.

Industry observers note that the trio gives Butters a visit after remembering his connectivity complications while trying to watch Dr. Pimple Popper videos. It turns out, Butters is no longer affected by the outages as he’s since been getting some kickbacks from Billionaire Weenietown, including a massive GTA VI setup. In the middle of Butters’ gameplay, zombie Kenny appears on the screen, breaking out like Samara in The Ring to deliver a message to his friends.

The episode culminates with the kids confronting the cops and the mayor, only to find out they were all complicit in Kenny’s death when they allowed the billionaires to come to South Park.

Ultimately, Mayor McDaniels takes responsibility for the billionaires ruining the town but sends Mr. Kim to space on WeenieX in order to appease the town. The show ends with “America” falling off the South Park sign after they rolled over to change the town’s name for Trump.

South Park‘s finale wrapped up its Billionaire Weenietown storyline with some more topical jabs, following the massively unprecedented release of GTA VI, as well as the ongoing nationwide outages, which the government has chalked up to many networks changing over to AI systems.

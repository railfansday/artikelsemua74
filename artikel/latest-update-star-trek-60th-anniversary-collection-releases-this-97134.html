Star Trek's 60th anniversary has been an incredible celebration of one of the most influential science fiction franchises of all time. From exciting new collectibles and original stories to the return of iconic characters from across six decades of Trek history, fans have been spoiled for choice throughout 2026.

Industry observers note that arguably one of the biggest highlights of the anniversary celebrations has been Star Trek's crossover with Magic: The Gathering, which brings together two massive fandoms and gives Trekkies an entirely new way to experience their favorite sci-fi universe. However, the celebrations are far from over, and Star Trek fans looking to expand their collections even further have another exciting release to look forward to before the end of November. It has been revealed that star Trek's 60th anniversary celebrations are continuing this November with an officially licensed collection of Magic: The Gathering accessories from Sirius Dice. According to the official press release, the collection is scheduled to launch this month to coincide with the franchise's milestone anniversary and the release of its highly anticipated Universes Beyond crossover. (Related: Latest Update: Course Launch Community Event...)

In a recent development, among the most exciting additions to the collection are the Star Trek Mini Dice Cups, which retail for $19.99 each and feature a range of iconic factions from across Star Trek history. Each individually packaged cup comes with two Magic: The Gathering counter dice, with randomized designs that incorporate various color schemes and metallic finishes.

There are ten different designs available in the Mini Dice Cup collection:.

Industry observers note that the Q design is particularly noteworthy, as it's the collection's rare chase variant. Much like opening a Collector Booster pack in Magic: The Gathering, fans won't know exactly which design they've purchased until they open the packaging, adding another layer of collectibility to the release.

In a recent development, the Mini Dice Cups are just one part of Sirius Dice's larger Star Trek collection, however. The firm is also releasing $7.99 Treasure Packs, which contain one of 25 collectible 25mm Spindown Life Counters alongside a Magic: The Gathering token accessory. The dice themselves come in three different rarity categories: common, rare, and legendary.

Recent findings suggest that for fans looking for something a little more extravagant, Sirius Dice is also releasing two oversized 55mm Spindown Life Counters featuring miniature replicas of the U.S.S. Enterprise and a Klingon Bird-of-Prey encased in transparent resin. Both designs retail for $54.99 apiece and could easily double as standalone display pieces.

While the collection is clearly geared toward Magic: The Gathering players, there's plenty here for longtime Trekkies who simply enjoy collecting memorabilia. With the holiday season right around the corner and Star Trek's 60th anniversary year drawing to a close, the latest Sirius Dice collection is another great opportunity for fans to commemorate six decades of the iconic science fiction franchise.

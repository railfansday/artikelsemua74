TAMPA, Fla., Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Trace Systems Inc. (Trace) has been awarded a position on Support Area 3 (SA3): Cloud, Infrastructure, & Cybersecurity Services of the General Services Administration (GSA) Technology Transformation Services (TTS) Next-Generation IT Solutions (NEXT) Blanket Purchase Agreement (BPA). This five-year BPA, awarded under the GSA Alliant 3 Governmentwide Acquisition Contract, has no ceiling value across its life. This positions Trace to compete for orders supporting the delivery of mission-critical technology solutions and modernization of IT infrastructure across the Federal Government.

In a recent development, the NEXT BPA provides TTS with streamlined access to innovative, secure, and scalable IT solutions. Under SA3, Trace will have the opportunity to deliver cloud and infrastructure modernization services, including cloud migration and optimization, enterprise architecture, network and systems engineering, cybersecurity, Zero Trust implementation, continuous monitoring, vulnerability management, and secure infrastructure operations. These capabilities will enable federal agencies to modernize legacy environments, strengthen cybersecurity postures, and improve the reliability and performance of critical technology systems.

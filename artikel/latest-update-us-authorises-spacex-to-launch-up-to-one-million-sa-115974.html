Latest Update: US authorises SpaceX to launch up to one million satellites for orbital data centres

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (60 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, the US Federal Communications Commission has approved SpaceX's blueprint to deploy up to one million satellites to build data centres in orbit, giving Elon Musk's tech giant a major foothold in the industry. The authorisation runs through 2040, as tech firms race to move computing infrastructure into space despite significant technical hurdles.

Bose’s tiny waterproof speaker for under $80.

According to recent reports, small speakers make easy gifts because nobody has to guess a size. The Bose SoundLink Micro (2nd Gen) speaker is $79.00, which is $50 under its $129.00 list price. Bose rates the palm-sized speaker for up to 12 hours of battery life and calls it waterproof and dustproof, so it can ride along on a bike, a backpack or the edge of a hot tub. This price applies to the Sandstone color.

The family motion-gaming system for $90 off.

It has been revealed that the Nex Playground drops to $209.00 from a $299.00 list price. That makes it a strong candidate for the gift that gets everyone off the couch after a holiday dinner.

Breville’s grind-and-brew espresso machine for $205 off.

A coffee shop habit gets expensive, and this machine is one way out. The Breville Barista Express espresso machine is $494.95, down from a $699.95 list price. This is the BES870XL in brushed stainless steel, and it has a grinder built in, so whole beans go in the top and espresso comes out the bottom. Breville’s listing calls it a manual machine, so expect a short learning curve before the lattes start looking like the ones you pay for.

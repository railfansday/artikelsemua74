Recent findings suggest that wall Street is closed on Thursday, November 26, for Thanksgiving, with no trading on the Latest York Stock Exchange or Nasdaq. Stocks return on Friday for a shortened session. Both exchanges will open at the normal time of 9:30 am Eastern, or 8 pm IST, but will shut at 1 pm Eastern, or 11:30 pm IST, three hours ahead of the usual close. That leaves investors with just three and a half hours of trading on Black Friday, compared with six and a half hours on a regular day. Eligible options contracts on the NYSE will stop trading slightly later, at 1:15 pm Eastern, or 11:45 pm IST. The US bond market is also closed on Thursday and will end Friday’s session at 2 pm Eastern. Full trading hours return on Monday, November 30, running from 8 pm to 2:30 am IST. Crypto markets, including Bitcoin and XRP, continue to trade around the clock through the holiday.

Recent findings suggest that A video of prophet Brandon Biggs predicting XRP will climb to $5 and eventually reach hundreds of dollars per coin is circulating again among XRP holders. Biggs allegedly stated he saw Ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse at a long executive table, with papers being signed and deals being made, followed by a cryptocurrency jumping to $5. He described that move as only the start of a much larger rise and has separately claimed XRP will create people worth tens of millions. Biggs stressed that his comments were not investment advice but a description of a vision. The renewed attention comes as XRP trades near $0.92, far from the targets in the vision and down more than 5% over the past week.

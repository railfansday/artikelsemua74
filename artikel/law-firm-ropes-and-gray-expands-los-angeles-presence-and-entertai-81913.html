Law firm Ropes and Gray aims to significantly raise its profile in Los Angeles and in the entertainment and sports arena with the hiring of the transactional legal team lead by Tom Ara and David Markman.

Ara and Markman are relocating to Ropes and Gray after having previously set up shop at Weil, Gotshal and Manges, which imploded earlier this month amid senior management turmoil and key departures. Also joining Ropes and Gray in Los Angeles with Ara and Markman is Max Offsay. In Newly introduced York, David Yohai and Yehudah Buchweitz have joined Ropes and Gray as partners. Ropes and Gray has about 1,500 attorneys spread across 16 offices worldwide.

“Everything we do at Ropes & Gray starts with our clients, and I’m thrilled to welcome nine extraordinary partners who share that conviction,” remarked Julie Jones, chair of Ropes & Gray. “We were intentional about adding partners who build on our client and practice strengths, help expand our global platform and who will thrive in the culture that makes Ropes & Gray the best partnership in elite law.”. Ara serves as head of the entertainment, sports and media group at Ropes and Gray, with Markman as partner. Ara and his team are known for handling high-level transactions across film, TV, music, sports, gaming and digital media. It also advises private equity sponsors, sovereign wealth funds, institutional investors on all manners of complicated commercial transactions.

Ara and his team joined Weil Gotshal in June 2025 after six years with DLA Piper.

Layton Williams is the latest to join the cast of Amazon MGM Studios‘ upcoming romance film “Throttled,” based on the bestselling book from Lauren Asher’s “Dirty Air” series.

Variety understands that the “Bad Education,” “Geek Girl” and “I Hate Suzie” star will play a fresh character not in the books called Dorian, who serves as a personal assistant.

“Throttled” is being led by Nicole Wallace (“My Fault”) who play Maya Alatorre, an ambitious young woman who joins her brother on the racing circuit who falls into a forbidden, high-stakes romance with her brother’s ruthless teammate and fiercest racing rival, Noah Slade, played by Josh Heuston (“Off Campus”). Julio Peña (“Through My Window”) will play Santi Alatorre, the older brother of Maya and a driver on the racing circuit. Mia McKenna-Bruce (“How to Have Sex”) will play Sophie, the best friend of Maya. Recent findings suggest that nzingha Stewart (“Daisy Jones & the Six”) is directing, with Melissa Osborne (“My Fault: London”) adapting the script. Max Handelman and Elizabeth Banks of Brownstone Productions are producing alongside Ethea Entertainment’s Jennifer Gibgot and Andrew Panay, with Garret Grant also producing. Alison Small of Brownstone Productions and Lauren Asher are executive producing.

“Throttled” marks another addition to Amazon’s MGM growing library of book-to-screen adaptations (and mostly in the romance space) alongside shows such as “Off Campus” and the “My Fault” film franchise. Williams is repped by Curtis Brown Group, 42, WME and Multitude Media.

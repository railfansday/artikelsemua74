Leeds United may be able to tempt a cash-hungry Celtic board into selling a prize asset if they are willing to spend a significant chunk. The Whites are stated to be interested in Celtic frontman Camilo Durán, who has dazzled since joining the Scottish club in the summer.

In a recent development, durán joined the Hoops for a bargain £5.5million deal from Qarabag after netting five times in the Champions League last season, and he has hit the ground running. The forward has scored 14 goals across all competitions, and he has found the net just as regularly in the Europa League as in the Scottish Premiership.

He has even been a consistent scorer when starting on the right wing, where he has often found himself to make room for centre-forward Kasper Hogh, who arrived on an £11million deal but has only scored three times and is now more than three months without a goal. Industry observers note that and don't be fooled by Celtic's competition. The Hoops signed right-winger Haissem Hassan for £6million - which could raise to £9million - during the summer window, with the Egypt international having impressed during the World Cup, but he is without a goal up to this point, so even other higher quality players are not having the success in front of goal from the same positions Durán is operating in. And that's a major part of the reason clubs like Leeds are mentioned to be scouting the Colombian. (Related: Industry News: Fight to chair the GOP tests Trump’s clo...)

But Celtic's reliance on Durán means it's going to be hard to lure the Colombian away from Parkhead, especially during the season. A January deal is going to be particularly difficult to strike, although there may be a slither of hope if Celtic are eliminated from the Europa League before then. The Bhoys right now have three points after four games and will need six or seven points from their last four to extend their European campaign.

That could be a key factor, but in any case, a summer deal remains realistic if Leeds are willing to pay north of £15million, which would give Celtic the chance of tripling their £5.5million investment in the space of just one year.

"I believe he is the future of Colombian football and he should be first pick in our team. For me, he is the best striker we have." - Arnulfo Valentierra (Durán's former Independiente Medellín manager) speaking this season.

Industry observers note that this Celtic board has previously turned down massive money in the January window, blocking the £25million exit of Arne Engels last January, but they sold him for £3million less in the summer when West Ham came calling. They never replaced the midfielder, either, leaving fans furious amid an ongoing civil war between them and the board over a perceived lack of ambition.

Industry observers note that “We are building a team here and guys like Camilo are part of the future. He has a lot of talent.” - Nestor Lorenzo (Colombia head coach).

Sources indicate that this Celtic board likes to turn a profit, and it is willing to do it at the cost of the team to a certain degree. Another sale could complicate things, and Benjamin Nygren is the leading candidate to leave next summer as things stand, but if Leeds can get in early and make a sizable offer for Durán, the bargain deal he arrived at Celtic on will give them a chance of sealing a deal, as long as they are willing to stump up a sizable amount of cash compared to that initial £5.5million fee.

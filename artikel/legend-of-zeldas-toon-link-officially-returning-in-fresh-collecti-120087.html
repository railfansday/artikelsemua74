Toon Link is one of the most iconic characters in gaming history, despite it being just one of Link's various looks over the course of The Legend of Zelda franchise. Referring to the cartoon-like version of Link that debuted in The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker, Took Link is a cel-shaded and expressive little guy, and despite the artstyle being replaced by much more realistic variations in recent years, fans still look back fondly at The Wind Waker's version of the beloved protagonist.

It is those fans who will be pleased to hear that Toon Link is set to return in a brand-new collection that will, hopefully, be here before we know it.

Sources indicate that the Legend of Zelda: Master Collection comes courtesy of a collaboration between Nintendo and Italian brand, Panini. Inspired by every mainline The Legend of Zelda entry, fans can expect stunning and detailed artwork of Link, Zelda, and other familiar faces, all whilst they collect a pack of cards that stands out amongst the rest. It will contain artwork from every entry ranging all the way from the original The Legend of Zelda in 1986 to the more recent The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom, and if you are wondering if your favorite entry will be immortalized in stunning art, the full list has been revealed:.

It has been revealed that as you can tell from this list, our adorable cel-shaded Link will also be making an appearance thanks to additions such as The Wind Waker, The Minish Cap, Phantom Hourglass, Spirit Tracks, and more. Additionally, with Zelda also receiving a Troon version in the same entries, we can hope to see her make an appearance too.

With The Legend of Zelda franchise at the moment celebrating its 40th anniversary this year, it is no wonder that ambitious releases such as The Legend of Zelda: Master Collection are being released. However, at the time of writing, no exact release date has been revealed. Not only that, but we do not yet know the number of cards or a potential price tag.

Sources indicate that going by Panini's usual products, with the firm being best known for its football stickers and collectible cards, we can safely assume that each box will consist of 12 or 24 packs, with each individual pack consisting of around six cards. As such, you will have to splash quite a bit of cash in order to build your deck.

What we do know, is what the box itself will look like, as Panini has released an image of the box art which, thanks to a white and gold color scheme, features a map of Hyrule and some iterations of Link over the years. Now, we can only hope that the actual cards are just as pretty when The Legend of Zelda: Master Collection eventually introduces.

Jerko Leko warned his Dinamo Zagreb side cannot afford any mistakes against Bayer Leverkusen's rampant attack when they meet in Thursday's Europa League clash.

Dinamo would leapfrog Carles Martinez's side with a win at Stadion Maksimir, though Leverkusen head into this game after a 4-0 thrashing of Paderborn in Bundesliga action.

In fact, only RB Leipzig (32) and Bayern Munich (30) have scored more Bundesliga goals than Leverkusen's 24 this season, with Patrik Schick in fine form.

Leko remains wary of the threats from Schick, who already has nine league goals this season, and his team-mates as he plots a way to stifle Martinez's frontline.

"Bayer are in good form, and I expect a strong game," the Dinamo head coach mentioned.

"We need to eliminate the small mistakes we made [in their 3-2 league victory over Istra 1961], because an opponent like this will exploit them mercilessly.

"I have complete confidence in our players' attitude.".

Leko may be confident in his defence, which has kept a clean sheet in three of their four Europa League matches this season, as many as they managed in their previous 21 major European games.

Leverkusen boss Martinez should also be aware of Dinamo's own form after the Croatian side have reeled off three straight victories across all competitions.

However, Croatian teams are winless in their last 13 major European matches against German sides (D3 L10) since a 3-1 win for Hajduk Split over Stuttgart in September 1981.

It has been revealed that "Dinamo Zagreb are a tough opponent. They’re strong at home and will have their fans cheering them on," Leverkusen chief Simon Rolfes remarked.

"Of course, we’re determined to win and build on our performances leading up to the Marseille game.

"To reach our goal of finishing in the top eight, we need to keep earning points – and a lot of them – and tomorrow we’re aiming for the next three.".

This will be the first meeting between Dinamo and Leverkusen in European competition, but the Croatian giants have lost their last eight European games against German teams, including qualifiers.

Leonardo DiCaprio is riding high coming off his acclaimed performance in this year's Best Picture winner, One Battle After Another. While he ultimately lost out on another Oscar to Sinners star Michael B. Jordan, he earned no shortage of praise for his comedic, complex, and heartfelt turn as ex-revolutionary Bob Ferguson, living at the heart of Paul Thomas Anderson's modern classic. The future is now looking busy for him too, with his next Martin Scorsese team-up on the way in the psychological thriller What Happens at Night and the long-awaited Heat 2 set to begin filming in January. It's as good a time as ever to catch up on some of the actor's classics, but one of them won't be sticking around on its current streaming home for much longer.

Industry observers note that romeo + Juliet sticks close to the original story despite the change in setting, though it reimagines the Montagues and Capulets as rival gangs wielding pistols called swords rather than actual blades. Its most memorable aspect, however, is that it keeps the Shakespearean English intact for a unique clash in tone. While crashing a costume ball hosted by the Capulet family, Romeo meets and instantly falls in love with their daughter, Juliet, unaware of who she's related to. Their chance encounter becomes the center of a violent clash between the two families, as they try to defy the stars and be together while their blood tries to rip them apart. Rounding out the starry ensemble are Brian Dennehy, John Leguizamo, Miriam Margolyes, Harold Perrineau, Pete Postlethwaite, Paul Sorvino, and Diane Venora. It has been revealed that you do not fall for strangers. You fall for the person you have already been talking to for years, which is the slowest and by some distance the most durable way anybody has found to do this. (Related: Tech Report: Woman known as Firefighting hero arrested ...)

It has been revealed that you want a partner, not an audience. Somebody who wants their own life as badly as you want yours, and who would never ask you to be smaller to make the relationship easier.

The intensity was never the problem. The secrecy is. You are drawn to a love that lives entirely in private, and to the particular ache of wanting somebody you are not supposed to want.

You do not do this cautiously. When it happens for you it is immediate and total and it rearranges everything, and you would not accept a smaller version of it.

According to recent reports, while Luhrmann's take on the play initially divided critics, the film overcame those mixed reviews to become a cult classic appreciated for its timeless stylization that blends the Bard's work with modern imagery. It now owns a Certified Fresh 79% score from critics on Rotten Tomatoes, with a similarly solid 77% from audiences. At the time, Romeo + Juliet was also a modest box office hit, grossing $147.6 million globally, just over ten times its reported budget. As a further demonstration of how well it has endured over time, GEA Live recently joined forces with Disney and Luhrmann to prepare Romeo + Juliet in Concert, a special event arriving next year that will see a hybrid ensemble perform the music live alongside a screening of the movie. Tickets are on sale now, with the world tour beginning in Williamsport, Pennsylvania on January 9.

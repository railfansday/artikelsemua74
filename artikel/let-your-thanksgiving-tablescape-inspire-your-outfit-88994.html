It finally feels like we’re in the thick of fall. It’s just beginning to get cold enough to wear our favorite coats, all the leaves have turned graceful shades of red, yellow, and orange, and somehow, nothing feels as comforting as a hot cider cupped in the perfect pair of leather gloves. We’ve almost arrived at Thanksgiving, which officially marks the start of the holiday season. (Related: Democrats to open 2028 primary debates in June after pu...)

When it comes to fashion, Thanksgiving is actually one of the best holidays for holiday dressing. There is ample inspiration to rework fall classics and experiment with new trends that have just recently been presented on the runways, like berry tones, antique Victorian-inspired style, or even statement coats. The weather (not too cold just yet, but cold enough to layer) also makes it a prime time to play with accessories, whether it be medallion belts or grandmacore-inspired tapestry bags. So, this year, why not pull your Thanksgiving outfit inspiration directly from your tablescape? Aside from celebrating with friends and family, a decorative tablescape is one of the most fun parts of this holiday. Make a Pinterest board for both, and you’ll match: There’s nothing chicer.

Read on for some ideas for Thanksgiving tablescape-inspired outfits.

According to recent reports, look to Thanksgiving’s ultimate favorite hue and layer up juicy shades of it. Rich, stunning tones of saturated cranberry pop (and you’ll probably spot them on your plate, too). It gives you a dramatic look with low-key effort. Opt for mixed textures. Think: leather, velvet, silks, and satins all done in deep cranberry.

It has been revealed that the easiest Thanksgiving outfit (and tablescape) trick is to lean into the natural codes of Mother Nature. Accessorize your outfit and table with dark fall florals, leaf motifs, gourds and pumpkins, or atmospheric forest and woodland fall elements. There are so many different directions to go in. But when in doubt, think of berry tones and fun details like walnut-shaped clutches or mushroom motifs.

Libya’s Haftar visits old foe Turkey for first time in ‘upcoming phase’ of ties

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (59 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, eastern Libya military commander Khalifa Haftar met Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan in Ankara on Wednesday during his first official visit to Turkey, marking a major thaw in relations between the longtime rivals. The visit came days after Ukraine struck a Russian warship in Benghazi, a port city controlled by Haftar.

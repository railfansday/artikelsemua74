Relations between Antoine Arnault and Nasser Al-Khelaïfi have cooled significantly in recent months. Officially named president of Paris FC on Monday, the French leader, who has gradually left his mark on the club since taking control two years ago, now maintains more distant relations with his PSG counterpart. According to The Teamrelations between the two men have particularly deteriorated since the end of last season, against a backdrop of differences concerning the future of French football. Antoine Arnault intends in particular to defend a position different from that held by Vincent Labrune and Nasser Al-Khelaïfi in discussions around governance reform. A desire for independence which recently led him to make direct contact with Philippe Diallo, president of the French Football Federation, in order to make the interests of Paris FC heard.

Increasingly involved in the management of the Parisian club, Antoine Arnault also wishes to have more influence in the major decisions of French football. The latest president of the PFC exchanges mainly with Michel Denisot on the functioning of the bodies and the question of television rights, two major issues for professional clubs. On the other hand, he now seeks less advice from certain football personalities, after having been burned by critics whom he considers “ free », particularly those concerning the choice of Antoine Kombouaré. This distancing accompanies its desire to make Paris FC a full player in institutional debates, without necessarily aligning with the positions defended by the leaders of PSG and the Professional Football League.

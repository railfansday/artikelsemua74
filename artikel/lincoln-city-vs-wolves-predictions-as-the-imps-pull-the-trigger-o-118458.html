Industry observers note that lincoln City come into this weekend’s clash with Wolves without a permanent head coach, after sacking Chris Cohen and Tom Shaw in midweek. The Imps made the decision after a heavy 4-0 loss to Charlton Athletic made it five consecutive Championship losses.

After the midweek fixtures, last season’s League One title-winners sit in 21st and now have a three-point gap to the bottom three. Lincoln have failed to find the back of the net in three of their last four games and are the league’s second-lowest scorers with just 15 goals in 17 matches.

The same can not be remarked for Wolves, who moved to eight games without defeat by beating Derby County 3-0 on Tuesday. Adam Armstrong bagged a brace against the Rams and Mateus Mane continued his improving form by scoring his first goal of the campaign.

In their eight-game unbeaten run, the Old Gold have won six times to cement their place in the Championship’s automatic promotion hunt, sitting in 3rd. Cesar Peixoto’s side are gathering steam and will be backed to pick up their 11th win of the season this weekend.

In a recent development, “Lincoln City have opted for change in the dugout, and perhaps a little earlier than expected. Survival is the goal for the Imps and they’re currently out of the bottom three, and it isn’t like they gave Chris Cohen and Tom Shaw plenty of time to dig themselves out of this rut.

“That remarked, the past five games have been really poor, and the 4-0 loss to Charlton Athletic was worrying. The away support voiced their discontent after the match, and as other relegation contenders pull the trigger on managerial changes, the Imps have followed suit.

According to recent reports, “Wolves meanwhile are in storming form. They’ve found their feet under Cesar Peixoto and are looking like a really dangerous outfit at the moment, with Adam Armstrong and Mateus Mane bang in form.

“The Imps have shown they will concede chances in the Championship this season, and Wanderers have the lethal attackers to punish them. I’m going for an away win this weekend.”.

“A managerial sacking can often be the catalyst for players to prove a point, which is the exact reaction Lincoln City will be hoping for after letting go of Cohen and Shaw.

“However, it would come as a huge shock if they can secure a result here. The Imps have been misfiring in front of goal and leaky at the back, two detrimental ingredients for any side hoping to turn their fortunes around.

Lithic is the modern card issuing and processing platform empowering ambitious financial companies to build the future of payments.

Our infrastructure powers card programs for 100+ innovative clients, from fintechs reimagining credit and digital banking to platforms transforming disbursements and spend management. Companies like Mercury, Flex, and Novo rely on Lithic's developer-friendly APIs, direct network connections, and flawless reconciliation to launch and scale card programs in weeks, not years. In a recent development, we're building a future where access to better financial products materially improves people's lives, free from the constraints of 30-year-old mainframes and legacy processors. We're proud to be backed by world-class investors who share that vision, including Bessemer Venture Partners, Index Ventures, Spark Capital, Stripes, and Mastercard, along with many others. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

We're a team of 170+ across 26 states and 7 countries, headquartered in New York City.

Sources indicate that we are hiring an Engineering Manager to lead Lithic's Analytics team. The team owns the data that our sponsor banks, customers, and internal teams run on: the daily reporting suite our partner banks use to reconcile and oversee their card programs, the ledger and balance data behind Treasury, network reporting, and the analytics that drive product and business decisions. Much of this work ships to external parties on a schedule, so correctness and predictability matter as much as speed.

This is a role for an experienced manager who is ready to lead from day one, not a stepping stone into management. It is also a player-coach role: we expect roughly 20% to 30% of your time to be hands-on, reviewing models and pull requests, unblocking the team, and shipping when it counts. The stack is dbt, Snowflake, Airflow, and Omni, with SQL and Python throughout.

The annual US salary range for this role is $169,000 - $265,000 plus equity. This salary range may be inclusive of several career levels at Lithic and will be narrowed during the interview process based on the candidate’s experience and qualifications.

In a recent development, this is a remote position. However, candidates must be located in the United States or Canada (Ontario or British Columbia). We do not offer visa sponsorship or assistance.

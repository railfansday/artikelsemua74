Ndiaye at the moment plays for Senegalese club Diambars FC, an academy renowned for developing young footballers.

Despite his age, the winger helped the club secure promotion to Senegal’s top flight last season, demonstrating his ability to make an impact at senior level.

Now lighting up the 2026 FIFA Under-17 World Cup in Qatar, the explosive wide forward is proving why Europe’s top scouts are tracking his every move:.

Ndiaye delivered an outstanding performance in Senegal’s emphatic 9-0 victory over Cuba on Monday, scoring once and providing two assists.

That took his tally to three goal contributions in two appearances at the tournament, having also featured against Croatia in Senegal’s opening Group F fixture.

The youngster has impressed with his explosive pace, direct dribbling and ability to create opportunities for his teammates.

His willingness to take on defenders, combined with his emerging end product, makes him an intriguing long-term prospect for Liverpool.

Perhaps most excitingly for Liverpool supporters, the teenager shares several stylistic and biographical similarities with former Anfield favourite Sadio Mane.

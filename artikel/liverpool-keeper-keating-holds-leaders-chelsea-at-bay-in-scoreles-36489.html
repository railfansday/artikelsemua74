Recent findings suggest that khiara Keating’s resilient performance in goal denied Chelsea, the Women’s Super League leaders, and earned Liverpool a point. After hard-fought 1-0 wins against Manchester City last Saturday and Paris Saint-Germain on Wednesday night, Chelsea drew a blank.

Recent findings suggest that against Gareth Taylor’s side, Chelsea’s lack of cutting edge was clear a week after beating City to go top. The calf injury picked up by Melvine Malard against City, the absence of Lauren James, who has a thigh injury, and the form of Aggie Beever-Jones, who scored her first WSL goal in more than a year in the 6-0 defeat of Charlton, have left Chelsea’s attack short.

Sources indicate that despite almost 70% possession and 47 touches in the box to Liverpool’s 12, Sonia Bompastor’s side amassed only 19 shots to Liverpool’s 13 and seven on target to their opponents’ four. Although the Blues remain unbeaten in the WSL, their third draw in five games in all competitions means City can leapfrog them at the top if they beat Tottenham on Sunday.

Liverpool had chances, particularly in the first half, but were hanging on after the break and Keating helped them to a fourth clean sheet, a tally bettered only by Bompastor’s side.

“It was a bit stressful; we don’t want to sit back but in games like this you have ‌to ride it out,” Keating told Sky Sports. “A clean sheet is ​always on our minds every game. We knew the mission.

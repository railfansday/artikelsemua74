Sheffield Wednesday may not have taken all three points from their away clash with Stevenage on Tuesday, but a goalless draw has kept them in the promotion mix as the halfway mark of League One creeps closer.

Back-to-back wins over Plymouth Argyle and Barnsley was the perfect start league-wise for latest manager Dave Challinor, and it keeps them in the mix for the top two ahead of a busy period of fixtures. (Related: Stop treating agents as one-off projects and start runn...)

Until the January transfer window opens though, there's not much scope for Wednesday to do anything transfer-wise - they're still under the guidance of an EFL-agreed business proposal and budget restriction, which didn't stop them from landing some quality players in the summer, but it did restrict how many deals they could do. Right now, Challinor has pretty much a substantial squad to choose from for his 18-man matchday selection, and it's left young defender Ernie Weaver out in the cold, despite his impressive performances for the Owls during last season's Championship struggles.

Early on in the 2025-26 season, prior to Wednesday being placed into administration by disgraced former owner Dejphon Chansiri, Henrik Pedersen gave a chance to Weaver with defensive options limited.

Having just turned 19, Weaver made his first Championship start in a 2-0 away win against Portsmouth, and at the heart of the Owls backline, they went on a three-match unbeaten run when he was starting matches.

Despite rapidly gaining plaudits, a 5-0 defeat at the hands of Coventry City in October would be his final outing of the season, as a troublesome ankle injury would cut short Weaver's campaign, which happened just before administration came into place.

Weaver has appeared seven times this season for Wednesday, with his only league starts coming under caretaker manager Matt Hamshaw after Pedersen was dismissed, but he has been excluded from the last two matchday squads, with a whole host of other defenders ahead of him in the pecking order at Hillsborough.

With Weaver now out of the matchday squad, it will perhaps stall his development unless he can get minutes elsewhere - and it looks like that's set to happen.

According to The Wednesday Word, the Owls hierarchy are now set to sanction a temporary exit for Weaver, given the amount of options ahead of him, even though he was denied a loan move away last month due to an injury picked up by Di'Shon Bernard.

And it is London-based National League outfit Hornchurch that look likely to be the club that take 20-year-old Weaver on a short-term basis, with EFL clubs unable to do any deals other than for free agents until January 2027.

Promoted from the National League South earlier in the year, Hornchurch are performing well enough and sit 12th in the fifth tier standings, and if they can get a deal done for Weaver, then they'll be hoping he can bring his EFL experience into the fold and to push them up the table even further.

Given Wednesday's defensive options are mostly all fit and available again now, many Wednesday fans are seeing sense in Weaver's likely temporary departure.

Some supporters though are hopeful that Wednesday will be able to recall him if needed, given their luck recently with injuries.

Whilst some are potentially concerned that the young defender won't sign a newly introduced contract with the club if he isn't getting minutes - Weaver's current deal expires next summer.

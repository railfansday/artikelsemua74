LondonMetric Property Plc (LNSPF) Q2 2027 Earnings Call November 24, 2026 9:00 AM EST.

Andrew Jones - CEO & Executive Director.

Today, we're joined by Andrew Jones, Chief Executive of LondonMetric Property.

Welcome, Andrew, and congratulations on your half year results 2027. You have delivered strong growth in net rental income over the 6-month period. Can you provide an overview of what has driven this growth? And what has been your focus over the half year?

Andrew JonesCEO & Executive Director.

It's been a combination. The period has benefited from a full contribution from the acquisition of Urban Logistics REIT last year. So we've had a full 6-month period of rent coming in from them. We've also delivered on rent reviews, lease renewals, lease regears. On average, we settled rent reviews up 17%, REIT years were 23% higher than previous passing rent. So it's a combination of a number of things, but all underpinned by the fact that we've got -- we're invested in good sectors, and we are effectively buying and allocating capital to great buildings that are capable of generating higher rental income.

Keeping track of all the upcoming releases from favorite artists can sometimes be a bit of a challenge. It’s not unusual for musicians to tease an album, then reveal a release date, only for the LP to be delayed for one reason or another (in some cases, multiple times), often without a latest release date attached. Then there are albums that are announced mere days before they drop!

But we’ve got you! Musicians from across genres started teasing fresh projects and announcing album release dates for the new year as it barely started. And like the last several trips around the sun, we’re keeping a calendar to keep track of all the notable releases of the year.

One of the most highly anticipated releases was Hilary Duff’s Luck…or Something — her first album in 10 years — which dropped in February. The singer-actress first confirmed her return to music in September, when she revealed a latest label deal with Atlantic Records, as well as new music and a docuseries that will follow her musical comeback. Sources indicate that BTS also gave its ARMY news to celebrate just days after the newly introduced year kicked off. On Jan. 4, the K-pop supergroup revealed that its highly anticipated next album — which comes after the members took a yearslong break as a group to complete mandatory duties for the South Korean military and focus on solo ventures — would arrive in March. (Related: Latest Update: This relaxing cat-petting browser game i...)

Not to be outdone, fellow K-pop supergroup BLACKPINK also confirmed its own new music in early January, with DEADLINE dropping in February. (Then in August, three of the ladies announced solo projects to arrive this year!).

Harry Styles then followed up BLACKPINK’s announcement the next day with his own album reveal. The pop superstar’s fourth studio album made its debut in March.

As winter moved into spring, women superstars not only teased, but announced highly anticipated albums. Among them? Madonna, Olivia Rodrigo and Ariana Grande, with all three projects arriving before the end of July.

It has been revealed that as September rolled around, some major names in rock released their projects. Among them are Anthrax and Rage Against the Machine’s Tom Morello, while October will anticipate an album by Nickelback, as well as iconic industrial band Ministry, who returns with its final album. And before the end of the month, Miley Cyrus returned with Bass Persuades.

Industry observers note that but the biggest surprise was yet to come. Taylor Swift revealed on Sept. 22 that she had a newly introduced song titled “Patient Zero” coming before the end of the month. Less than 24 hours later, she surprised Swifties with the news that not only was there a new track coming, but The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore, featuring not just one new song, but four.

As fall swept in with a chill, the anticipation was hot … for Grand Theft Auto 6. The highly anticipated video game declared that there would be two music releases: a soundtrack featuring the likes of Cardi B, Travis Scott, Keith Richards, Rauw Alejandro, Morgan Wallen and more. On top of that, Justice had helmed the score. Both would arrive before year’s end.

Recent findings suggest that that’s just several of the big releases of 2026. To help you stay on top of it all, we’ve organized this calendar chronologically by month and week, and have also included a section for albums that have been announced, but do not yet have a release date.

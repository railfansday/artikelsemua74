📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Macy's on Wednesday revealed it's postponing the annual Thanksgiving Day Parade due to severe weather.

The parade, held each year on Thursday morning, will now take place on Saturday at 8:30 a.m. ET, according to the company. The New York City area is expected to have heavy rain and thunderstorms on Thanksgiving.

"The safety of our participants and spectators is our top priority, and after close consultation with our city and agency partners, we have made the decision to move this year's celebration," Macy's mentioned in a statement.

According to recent reports, the parade's viability this year had been in question after concerns arose over strong winds that could become dangerous for the massive balloons that the event is known for. The postponement also means thousands of volunteers and artists slated to perform will have to adapt to Saturday's schedule.

The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade has been postponed.

The annual parade held in New York City is a longtime Thanksgiving staple that airs on NBC during the morning of the holiday. From Spider-Man to Grogu, the floats often feature many beloved pop culture characters, and even hints of what's to come in upcoming projects, including Shrek 5's first look at the ogre's sons last year. Due to severe weather, Macy's shared with NBC News that the parade will take place on Saturday, November 28 at 8:30am ET, instead of on Thanksgiving Day this year. A storm with heavy rain, strong winds, and coastal flooding is expected on the day of the actual holiday. Read Macy's official statement about the delay below:. Recent findings suggest that "The safety of our participants and spectators is our top priority, and after close consultation with our City and agency partners, we have made the decision to move this year’s celebration. Due to the expected severe weather, the 100th March of Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade will move to Saturday, Nov. 28. Our team is working through the operational logistics for this change and will share details as they are finalized. We expect the Parade to kick off at 8:30a.m. on Saturday. Thank you to the thousands of volunteers, performers and partners who create the magic millions enjoy each year. We look forward to celebrating together on Saturday.”.

The Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade will be postponed until Saturday due to expected storms on Thursday, and NBC will rerun last year’s parade this Thanksgiving.

It has been revealed that in a statement, NBC states, “Due to the extreme weather conditions, the city of New York, Macy’s and NBC have decided, in the interest of safety for both the participants and spectators, to move the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade to Saturday, Nov. 28.

In a recent development, as reported by Deadline yesterday, NBC had been closely tracking the Thanksgiving storm expected to bring high winds and heavy rain to New York City and much of the Eastern U.S. The network’s coverage of the 100th edition of the annual Macy’s Thanksgiving Day parade was set to air Thursday on NBC and stream on Peacock from 8:30 a.m. to noon ET. The parade remains a major draw, with last year’s pulling in 34.3 million viewers across NBC and Peacock, its biggest combined audience on record. (Related: “Giant superatoms” could finally solve quantum computin...)

The Nor’easter, a classic storm pattern, is expected to take shape late Wednesday and into Thursday morning, gathering strength as it grinds toward the Atlantic Ocean later in the holiday. Sustained winds are expected to be 12-25 miles an hour with gusts of 20 to 30 mph, increasing over the course of the day. AccuWeather is expecting one to two inches of rain to fall on Latest York, making this likely to be one of the wettest Thanksgivings in the city’s history.

This year’s parade is especially significant to Macy’s and NBC, as it marks the broadcast’s centennial. The parade is set to have 100 of its trademark large balloons, and a special “All-Star Broadway Thanksgiving Day Celebration” will feature performers from latest and long-running Broadway productions in a special 10-minute medley.

The balloons have only been grounded once before, in 1971, due to a similar mix of rain and wind. During the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020, the parade was converted into a television-only event around the Herald Square location of Macy’s Department Store.

Embark Studios, developer of The Finals and organizer of the game’s Grand Major esports tournament, is facing backlash after replacing human casters with AI voices in a highlight reel. IGN reports that the now-deleted video featured footage from the APAC closed qualifiers, but instead of hearing the human casters that actually called the matches, viewers were treated to AI commentators Scotty and June. The incident is rippling through the esports professional community, with some calling for unionization efforts to protect workers.

Proto, one of the casters replaced in the video, spoke out about the incident on social media.

It stands to reason that if a developer is comfortable with using AI-text-to-speech to power the game’s in-universe equivalent of esports casting, they might extend that beyond the confines of the game, possibly to see if anyone would notice or care enough about it. Having in-game AI commentary is the inch, this video experiment is the mile. Thankfully esports professionals, many of whom have lost their jobs over the last six years as studio interest and investment has declined, didn’t let this go by unremarked.

In a recent development, in an interview with IGN, Proto spoke about potential organization efforts to prevent something like this from taking hold in the esports community. “I think industry wide, we definitely need to implement some kind of protection for commentary teams as well as production teams,” he noted. “You got to protect those people’s jobs.”.

Manchester City fans have lost their patience with one star player after they put in a 'terrible' performance during a 1-1 draw at home to Fulham on Saturday afternoon.

The Cityzens have now gone four Premier League matches in a row without a win - a worrying change of fortune after having started the 26/27 season with a 100 per cent record in the six previous fixtures. (Related: This fresh chip survives 1300°F (700°C) and could chang...)

In a recent development, maresca's side were forced to fight hard for a point in the lunchtime kick-off, having gone a goal down after 16 minutes courtesy of Rodrigo Muniz's opener. While Erling Haaland's goal 10 minutes later was controversially ruled out, it took a brilliant volley from Elliot Anderson in the second-half to rescue a draw. It is clear that off-field problems are denting confidence during their current run, but some of the Etihad faithful are starting to point their finger at particular players, who have struggled to continue the good times rolling after Pep Guardiola's departure at the end of the last campaign.

After picking up just one point at home this weekend, some City fans are now turning on Phil Foden, who has been at the club since he joined the academy at eight-years-old.

Sources indicate that writing on X, many supporters slammed his lack of influence in attack, while paying close attention to his reluctance to press when out of possession. One user remarked:.

"I’ve said this a dozen times. Phil Foden is done playing football. He has nothing left to offer. He is just finished.".

A second wrote: "This Foden guy man, I lost my patience, 9 years and still a project player, zero qualities that require to start him," as a third continued: "Can always count on Foden to have zero impact as a 10.".

Elsewhere, a fourth brutally asked: "What's the point in giving Foden even one minute of playing time?" as a fifth concluded: "Foden, it’s all well and good to ask for the ball and touch it a thousand times, but if you’re just going to play it backwards or create nothing, my guy, get off the pitch.".

It remains difficult to say whether City's current struggles are down to the ongoing uncertainty surrounding their 115 charges, or whether Maresca's honeymoon period at the Etihad is coming to a swift end.

Many will also argue that the Italian is getting his attacking selections wrong at the moment, with the likes of Rayan Cherki and Enzo Fernandez among those left on the bench.

Whatever the underlying matter, the Cityzens now have a mountain to climb if they are to have any chance of toppling Arsenal at the top of the table.

The Gunners have the opportunity to move eight points clear of their usual title rivals if they beat Newcastle on Saturday evening, although Brighton could still cut that gap to five points should the Seagulls pick up a win themselves.

Manchester United are considering a January move for Ivan Toney as Mauricio Pochettino looks to add a proven Premier League goalscorer to his attacking options.

The 30-year-old has been in outstanding form for Al-Ahli and is now weighing up a return to England.

United are among several Premier League clubs who have been contacted about his availability, but the prospect of moving to Old Trafford is understood to appeal strongly to the England international.

According to TEAMtalk, intermediaries are actively exploring a January return to the Premier League for Toney, with Man United among the clubs being kept informed.

Toney has scored 16 goals in 15 starts this season and still has 18 months remaining on his Al-Ahli contract.

A loan deal with an option to buy is right now being explored, which could make the transfer more realistic for United.

Pochettino has made signing a centre-forward one of his priorities, and Toney’s Premier League experience makes him attractive because he would require very little adaptation.

Arsenal, Chelsea and Tottenham have also been informed of his situation, while Crystal Palace are interested in reuniting him with former Brentford manager Thomas Frank.

The logic behind this move is strong.

It has been revealed that united need goals immediately rather than another young striker requiring time to develop. Toney already knows the league, can play with his back to goal and offers a reliable penalty-box presence.

A recent CaughtOffside report also revealed that United have been exploring a temporary deal rather than committing to a permanent transfer straight away.

Benjamin Šeško remains part of United’s long-term plans, so spending heavily on another young No.9 could create unnecessary competition once he returns to full fitness.

Toney would offer something different: experience, physicality and immediate Premier League pedigree. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

The biggest question is whether Al-Ahli are prepared to sanction a loan.

Recent findings suggest that if they are, United should move swiftly. A short-term deal would limit financial risk while giving Pochettino a striker capable of making an immediate impact.

For a side struggling for consistency in attack, Toney looks like one of the most practical January options available.

In a recent development, EA Motive's Iron Man game has been quietly cooking away for a few years now, but don't worry. The wait to finally, officially see the superhero romp in action might be over soon, at least if the developer's updated website - two weeks before The Game Awards, no less - is any indication.

Industry observers note that redditors today noticed that EA Motive's official website got a little makeover recently. It's not clear when exactly the tweaks were pushed through, but the Wayback Machine makes clear that it's definitely been updated. A newly introduced website look is by no means confirmation of an imminent announcement, of course, but there are a few other bits of evidence that certainly point in that direction. (Related: Steam is pitting 10 years of Game of the Year winners a...)

Recent findings suggest that for one, The Game Awards 2026 will air on Disney Plus for the very first time, alongside all the other platforms you're used to. "As the evening unfolds, viewers can expect major announcements and surprises from some of Disney's most anticipated titles in gaming," the film studio said earlier this month.

Kingdom Hearts 4, Marvel 1943: Rise of Hydra and more are definitely possible, too. Turmoil at Marvel's Blade developer Arkane Lyon makes that one a tricker guess, however.

It has been revealed that second is the fact that The Game Awards air on 10th December - eight days before the global opening of Avengers: Doomsday, a film that's shaping up to become the biggest cinematic event since the last time Marvel chucked all of its toys into the same sandbox and bashed them together. Giving one of the company's most popular superheroes the spotlight around the same time seems like a good idea.

This isn't the first time EA's Iron Man has popped up in recent times, either. What appeared to be an unfinished trailer for Motive's Iron Man leaked onto the internet this summer, and showed off blisteringly fast flying and lots of destructive gadgets. It looked further along than I expected, at least, so I wouldn't be surprised to see the billionaire playboy philanthropist rear his head again in two weeks time.

Maura Higgins promised to give Mark Ballas a Birkin bag if they won season 35 of Dancing With the Stars — so what did her partner receive after their loss?

Higgins, 36, took to Instagram on Wednesday, November 25, to show the Rolex watch she bought for Ballas, 40, after they placed fifth in the competition show. (Related: Mechs vs Kaiju’s Ragdoll codes (November 2026)...)

According to recent reports, “I think he’ll survive,” Higgins wrote over a photo of Ballas holding the watch. “A massive thank you for all your time, patience and belief in me. Something I’ll forever be grateful for.”. (Related: This newly introduced chip survives 1300°F (700°C) and ...)

The reality star previously spoke about rewarding Ballas after their partnership.

In a recent development, “You know, I’ve done well on every show, but I’ve just never got the win,” she recently told Entertainment Tonight, referencing her previous stints on Love Island and The Traitors. “I told Mark that if we win, I’ll get him a Birkin. But only if we win. If we don’t win, he gets nothing.”.

It has been revealed that making it to the finale came as a surprise to Higgins after she initially expected to be eliminated early, telling Us earlier this month, “I was definitely in shock. I really wasn’t expecting my name to be called [for the semifinals].”.

According to recent reports, she continued: “I was expecting to get booted out. I really was. I don’t know why. You know when you have that gut feeling? But then I say this, but my gut is never right. Like, look at me in The Traitors, so I don’t know why I listen to it.”.

Higgins’ time on DWTS came with its fair share of highs and lows.

“This week was definitely one of the toughest I’ve had so far,” Higgins wrote via Instagram following Disney Night. “I was really unwell, running on very little energy and trying to learn two dances, but I gave it absolutely everything I had❤️.”.

She offered a glimpse into what challenged her, adding, “I came into this experience knowing I wasn’t a dancer, a performer or an athlete, but I wanted to challenge myself, step outside my comfort zone and give something completely latest my absolute best & that’s exactly what I’m trying to do every single week.”.

Higgins noted that she is still “learning” when it comes to performing.

“I’m making mistakes, I’m pushing myself and I’m growing along the way!!” she continued. “Some weeks will be better than others, and that’s okay. I’m only human🥹.”.

She concluded: “I’m not sharing this for sympathy or to make excuses, but simply to share a little of the reality behind the scenes! Sometimes all you can do is show up, give everything you have and be proud of yourself for trying!! A special thank you to my incredible partner @markballas who has been my biggest support throughout this journey. His patience, encouragement and belief in me, especially during such a difficult week, mean more than he knows. I genuinely couldn’t have asked for a better person to share this experience with❤️.”.

Higgins famously received an Hermès Birkin bag of her own from Traitors costar Rob Rausch after he blindsided her to win season 4 earlier this year.

McDonald’s is teaming up with Neon Genesis Evangelion once again, unveiling a fresh collection of toys that transform its iconic fast food items into mechas from the legendary anime.

McDonald’s is no stranger to anime collaborations, having previously launched meals and collectibles based on Jujutsu Kaisen, Dragon Ball, and even a crossover between Hello Kitty and Godzilla. (Related: This website has GTA 6 fans queuing for hours to take o...)

Back in December 2024, McDonald’s Japan revealed its first Evangelion collaboration, featuring a burger, fries, and drink that could transform into EVA units. (Related: This newly introduced chip survives 1300°F (700°C) and ...)

Now, the fast food giant is bringing the concept to China with three completely newly introduced designs that take things a step further.

Launching on December 2, the collaboration features a fried chicken bucket that transforms into Unit-01, a carton of fries that becomes Unit-02, and a Coca-Cola cup that turns into Unit-00.

As revealed by Toy People, each figure features articulated limbs, miniature battle scenes, and accessories that allow fans to recreate iconic moments from the franchise.

Recent findings suggest that unit-01 comes equipped with an A.T. Field, while Unit-02 includes a flying kick display stand and Unit-00 gets its signature shield. All three also come with miniature Angels for the mechas to battle.

It has been revealed that according to Evangelion’s official Chinese social media account, customers can purchase one of the figures for an additional 66 yuan (around $9) with a food order. There are 500,000 toys available as part of the promotion.

McDonald’s is also releasing special meals featuring holographic character badges and collectible cards depicting Shinji, Asuka, Rei, Kaworu, and Mari.

For hardcore collectors, just 500 premium gift boxes are being made available through a lottery. Priced at 299 yuan (around $42) plus 100 rewards points, the boxes contain all three mechas, both sets of collectibles, and an exclusive acrylic character display.

In a recent development, interestingly, McDonald’s isn’t the only fast food chain to embrace Evangelion in 2026. Earlier this year, KFC launched its own Evangelion menu, complete with a purple burger inspired by Unit-01.

Unfortunately for fans overseas, McDonald’s latest Evangelion collaboration is exclusive to mainland China, with no international release announced.

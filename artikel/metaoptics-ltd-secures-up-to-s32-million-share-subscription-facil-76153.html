Three-year committed facility provides MetaOptics with flexible growth capital, drawn down at the Firm's sole discretion.

SINGAPORE, Nov. 22, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- MetaOptics Ltd (Catalist: 9MT) (OTCQX: MOTLY) ("MetaOptics" or the "Firm", and together with its subsidiaries, the "Group"), a Singapore-headquartered semiconductor optics company pioneering glass-based metalens solutions enhanced by AI-driven image processing, announced that it had on 20 November 2026, entered into a share subscription facility agreement with GEM Opportunities Fund ISA Ltd. ("GEM"), the Bermuda-based investment vehicle managed by Global Emerging Markets (GEM), New York, for an aggregate commitment of up to S$32 million (the "Share Subscription Facility"). The Share Subscription Facility is subject to, inter alia, the approval of the Company's shareholders ("Shareholders") at an extraordinary general meeting ("EGM") to be convened. A circular containing further details of the Share Subscription Facility, together with the notice of EGM, will be despatched to Shareholders in due course. The Company intends to apply the net proceeds from drawdowns under the Share Subscription Facility, entirely for the Group's working capital purposes, including general corporate expenses, funding the day-to-day operations of the Group and supporting the growth of its existing business. As part of the Group's prepares to grow its existing business, the Group intends to establish a fresh 12-inch glass semiconductor fabrication line in the United States to produce metalenses for co-packaged optics ("CPO") and colour imaging applications. The Share Subscription Facility provides MetaOptics with flexible access to growth capital while allowing the Company to control the timing of any drawdowns, in line with its capital needs and prevailing market conditions.

Sources indicate that mr Thng Chong Kim, Executive Chairman of MetaOptics, said: "This Share Subscription Facility gives us a reliable, flexible source of capital for our day-to-day operations and to support the growth of our existing business by establishing a newly introduced 12-inch glass semiconductor fabrication line in the United States, on terms that put MetaOptics in control of the timing and size of each drawdown. We are pleased to have the backing of GEM, an experienced global investor, as we execute on our plans and build long-term value for our Shareholders.". Using advanced optical design and a scalable 12-inch DUV lithography process, it powers next-generation applications in CPO, mobile, AR/VR, automotive and other emerging markets. Headquartered in Singapore, MetaOptics aims to deliver high-performance optics with the reliability and scalability demanded by today's most innovative technology brands.

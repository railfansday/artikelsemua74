The Fresh York Mets are considered the frontrunner for two-time Cy Young Award winner Tarik Skubal. They have a track record of spending massive, and there's reason to think they'll pony up to secure the left-handed hurler, who is the most prized free agent this offseason.

Their initial offer to the 30-year-old southpaw reportedly already gave him the opportunity to make contract history.

Industry observers note that former Mets general manager and current MLB Network Radio analyst Jim Duquette reported on Friday that the offer in question was for nine years and nearly $400 million. That yearly mark of $44.4 million would result in the highest average annual value (AAV) for a free agent starting pitcher ever. (Related: Proposed change to SNAP threatens free school meals for...)

At the moment, the current AAV record in that department is held by Max Scherzer and Justin Verlander, both of whom the Mets previously inked to deals worth $43.3 million in AAV.

In November 2021, Upcoming York signed Scherzer to a three-year, $130 million contract. The following offseason, the Mets brought aboard Verlander on a two-year, $86.6 million deal. When those signings occurred, Scherzer and Verlander were in their late 30s. Granted they have six Cy Young Awards between them, but they were entering the final chapters of their illustrious careers. (Related: Flat the Mountain codes (November 2026)...)

Industry observers note that scherzer spent a season and a half with the Mets before being shipped off to the Texas Rangers during the 2023 season. Verlander was also moved ahead of the trade deadline that year, landing back with the Houston Astros.

If the Mets are able to reel in Skubal, though, they'll be getting him in his prime.

It has been revealed that skubal starred for the Detroit Tigers before the Los Angeles Dodgers acquired him leading up to this year's deadline. He joined an already stacked Dodgers roster that ultimately proved vulnerable down the stretch. The Milwaukee Brewers denied L.A. a championship three-peat, sweeping the Dodgers in the NLCS en route to winning the World Series.

Now Skubal has to decide if he wants to stay in L.A. or go elsewhere for the 2027 season and beyond.

The Dodgers are reportedly vying for his long-term services, as are the Toronto Blue Jays and San Diego Padres, as well as the Mets, of course.

According to recent reports, the Mets' initial offer was reportedly steep. Time will tell where and for how much Skubal eventually signs, and if he does so before the current collective bargaining agreement expires on Dec. 1.

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Microsoft’s 24-hour Microsoft 365 failure on November 12 and 13 has pushed the company into an internal “Code Red,” placing the fixes at the top of its core engineering team’s agenda. The breakdown took down Exchange Online, Outlook, Teams and Microsoft 365 Copilot, hitting government services in the US, Japan and Australia, slowing trading firms and cutting airline communications. Microsoft blamed a Windows defect introduced in July that went unnoticed until it caused servers to fail under load, and said hackers and AI agents played no part. Satya Nadella apologised publicly. Internally, M365 Core chief Jason Henderson called it the worst customer impact in Exchange Online’s history. Corporate vice president Amanda Silver said engineers traded over 11,000 messages in 11 Teams chats while key data sat in separate systems. Microsoft now aims to restore global service within one hour by limiting overload, isolating problems by region and using AI agents to read system data faster. For companies, the incident demonstrates how email, chat and AI tools can fail together, including the very channels needed to manage a crisis.

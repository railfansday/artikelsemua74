It has been revealed that microsoft has released a “Code Red” overhaul after its November 12–13 outage disrupted Exchange Online, Outlook, Teams, and Copilot worldwide for 24 hours. The incident exposed weaknesses in Microsoft’s engineering response and raised a bigger concern for the crypto industry.Can decentralized services stay online when the cloud providers they rely on go down? Microsoft 365 Core President Jason Henderson described the incident as the most customer-impacting outage in Exchange Online’s history. The disruption reached government agencies in Australia, Japan and the U.S., while also delaying trading firms and disrupting airline communications.

JUST IN: Microsoft $MSFT declares "Code Red" after a 24-hour outage exposed major flaws in its systems.

In a recent development, microsoft mentioned the outage was not caused by a cyberattack or AI agents. Instead, the company traced it to an undetected Windows scalability defect introduced in July.

CEO Satya Nadella publicly apologized as the company reviewed how engineers handled the failure.

The review found that responders exchanged more than 11,000 messages across 11 Teams chats, while key diagnostic information remained spread across different internal systems.

In a recent development, amanda Silver, corporate vice president of M365 Core and Work IQ, mentioned engineers were working through a “fog of war” during the crisis. Charles Lamanna, an executive vice president, also said the affected component had absorbed “roughly ten years’ worth of acceptable failures.”.

We regret that today’s outages caused disruptions for our customers, and we are working to address the glitch. We will continue to share more information as soon as we have it, and for now I can confirm that there is no indication this is the result of malicious or cyber activity.

In a recent development, the fresh Code Red program aims to change both Microsoft’s architecture and its crisis response. The tech giant has set a target of resolving future global incidents within one hour.

Silver outlined five workstreams focused on controlled load shedding, geographic traffic partitioning and faster recovery. Microsoft also plans to use AI agents to speed up system health diagnostics.

Henderson will hold daily meetings and weekly reviews with the Substrate leadership team as Microsoft works to reduce the chance of another prolonged outage.

The Microsoft failure also exposes a less obvious weakness in crypto. Even when blockchain networks continue operating, many services around them still depend on centralized cloud infrastructure.

In a recent development, major exchanges use providers such as AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure. Wallets can also rely on centralized RPC providers such as Infura and Alchemy, meaning consumers may lose access to balances or transaction broadcasting even when the underlying blockchain remains online.

DeFi has a similar gap. Smart contracts may run on-chain, but websites, order routers, and oracle systems can depend on centralized servers.

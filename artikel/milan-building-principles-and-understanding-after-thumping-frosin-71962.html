The Rossoneri made it three straight wins in Serie A on Sunday, climbing to fourth place in the table following a dominant performance at San Siro.

Gabriele Calvani's own-goal broke the deadlock after just four minutes, while Goncalo Ramos, Ardon Jashari, Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic were also on target during a clinical display by the hosts, who outperformed their 2.5 xG from 12 shots.

It was Milan's biggest league win under Amorim, while they have now scored 28 goals in his first 12 Serie A games in charge – a tally only bettered by Lajos Czeizler (31) and Nereo Rocco (29) among Rossoneri coaches.

Milan are looking to add to their attacking department during the January transfer window, and whilst recent reports claim that they are interested in either Endrick from Real Madrid or Nicolo Tresoldi from Club Brugge, updates on Friday claim that VfB Stuttgart’s Ermedin Demirovic is an option as well.

It has been revealed that the Rossoneri are reportedly seeking another option at centre-forward to rotate and compete with club record signing Goncalo Ramos. Reports in Italy claim that Milan are willing to invest around €60m-€70m during the January transfer window and that the arrival of a latest marquee striker is a possibility.

Outlets in Brazil suggest that Endrick, valued in the region of €60m-€70m is the priority, while reports in Italy claim that Tresoldi is the preferred option.

Friday’s updates claim, however, that there is at least one other option and that Stuttgart’s Demirovic could be a significantly cheaper option. (Related: Latest Update: At least seven progressives voted agains...)

The Bosnia and Herzegovina international has hit double figures in each of the last three Bundesliga seasons and is right now on five goals from nine league appearances in 2026-27, plus four in four in the Champions League.

At 28, he is not expected to cost quite as much as the younger Endrick or Tresoldi, but according to SportMediaset, he would also come with the approval of Markus Kroesche, who looks set to join Milan as Head of Football after leaving Eintracht Frankfurt earlier this year.

Demirovic’s contract with Stuttgart is set to expire in the summer of 2028, and without a contract extension in the immediate future, the Bundesliga club could begin entertaining offers as early as January in order to avoid the 28-year-old’s price tag dropping even further in the final year of his deal. The fact that Demirovic can play in a number of roles in the attack, not just at centre-forward, makes him an enticing option for Milan as well, given that Ruben Amorim could deploy him as an alternative or as a partner for Goncalo Ramos.

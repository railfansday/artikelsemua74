Reports in Italy confirm that Milan are following Bruno Fernandes’s contract situation at Manchester United with great interest, but the Rossoneri have three bigger priorities ahead of the January transfer window.

Bruno Fernandes is into the final year of his contract at Old Trafford, although there is an option to extend for a further 12 months. Discussions over a latest deal for the United captain did not progress much under the leadership of Michael Carrick and it remains to be seen whether the appointment of Mauricio Pochettino will change that situation. Reports earlier this week suggested that Milan are among the clubs interested in offering a deal to Bruno Fernandes in the event that he does decide to leave Manchester United in the near future.

The 32-year-old is familiar with Italian football from his time with Novara, Udinese and Sampdoria at the beginning of his career, and according to the Corriere dello Sport, he remains on good terms with his former Manchester United coach Ruben Amorim.

Fernandes and Amorim are also represented by the same agency.

Saturday’s report from the Corriere dello Sport hints that Milan would be in a decent position to offer an enticing deal to Bruno Fernandes at the end of the season, rather than in January, on the assumption that they qualify for the 2027-28 Champions League.

For the time being, Milan’s next priority is to arrange a contract extension for midfielder Adrien Rabiot.

As for the January transfer window, Milan are expected to be busy again, but a move for Bruno Fernandes is not currently on the agenda. Instead, Milan are hoping to bring in a fresh centre-back, with Goncalo Inacio among the names linked with a move to San Siro. Like Fernandes and Amorim, he is also represented by AS1. Then, the Corriere dello Sport claims that Milan will look for a fresh winger, ideally one that is capable of covering on either flank following long-term injuries to Alexis Saelemaekers and Samuel Chukwueze.

Milan are also expected to be in the market for a newly introduced striker, with Endrick, Nicolo Tresoldi, Ermedin Demirovic and Paul Mendy among the latest names to be linked with a January move to San Siro.

Milan have rekindled their interest in Fenerbahçe left-back Archie Brown as they consider their options on the left flank.

According to MilanNews24, the Rossoneri are studying the Englishman’s situation again, more than a year after he turned them down. Brown chose Fenerbahçe over Milan in July 2025, when the Turkish club bought him from Gent for €8 million and signed him until June 2028, with an extra year as an option. The 24-year-old has become a regular starter in Istanbul and has impressed in the Champions League. Several Premier League clubs have already enquired about him, so the Rossoneri could face serious competition.

His name is back on Milan’s list because of uncertainty over Pervis Estupiñán, whose agent, Jorge Mendes, is exploring possible destinations for the Ecuadorian. For now, though, the Rossoneri are only monitoring the situation, and no formal offer has been made.

Brown came through Derby County’s academy before spells at Lausanne-Sport and Gent.

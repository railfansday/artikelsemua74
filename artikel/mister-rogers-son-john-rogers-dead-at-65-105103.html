Mister Rogers' neighborhood is in mourning.

In a recent development, beloved TV host Fred Rogers' youngest son, John Rogers, died at 65 years old, his family confirmed to TMZ. His cause of death was not shared. (Related: Tech Report: GTA 6 players are devasted by train ‘nerf’...)

John was born in 1961 to the puppeteer—who made television history as America's neighbor on Mister Rogers' Neighborhood from 1968 to 2001—and his wife Joanne Byrd Rogers following the arrival of son James Byrd Rogers in 1959.

According to recent reports, john appeared with his brother on the children's show during their childhood in Pittsburgh, Pa., but went on to live a life outside of the public eye, which included a move to Florida in his 30s. However, he has occasionally returned to the spotlight to celebrate his father's legacy following his 2003 death, such as accepting a star on the Pittsburgh Walk of Fame on Fred's behalf. (Related: Latest Update: Innocent pizzeria flooded with death thr...)

"How many people have stated 'It's a beautiful day in the neighborhood?'" John quoted the series' iconic opening line at the event. "What an honor this is, firstly for my dad.".

Though Fred's show ended in 2001, John noted how much his dad's impact lived on with new generations.

In his words, "It's hard to believe he's been gone since 2003. His memory and his work live on beyond belief.".

And having grown up in the city where his dad became an ordained Presbyterian Minister, John felt endeared to return back home over the years, noting, "Pittsburgh sort of reminds me of him.".

It has been revealed that "I can't find nicer people than Pittsburgh, and that's why I visit here quite often," he continued. "It's a lot of Mister Rogers-type people that live here that really are caring and wonderful, loving people.".

Recent findings suggest that in addition to accepting the posthumous honor, John met with Tom Hanks on the set of the biopic about Fred's life,  A Beautiful Day in the Neighborhood. The late 74-year-old's wife Joanne was closely involved in the making of the 2019 film, before her death two years later.

Industry observers note that "We're happy about the movie. Thought it was wonderful. I thought Tom Hanks' portrayal of the movie was just fabulous,” John gushed on Fox and Friends in 2019. "And when I heard that Tom was going to do it, I mentioned in baseball terms, well, that's a grand slam, home run.".

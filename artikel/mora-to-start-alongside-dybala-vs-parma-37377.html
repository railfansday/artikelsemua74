Sources indicate that this morning, the Giallorossi won’t even take to the field for their usual “stretch” session just hours before the match. This is why the Portuguese has the upper hand. He was also called up, but didn’t play a single minute in the matches against Denmark and Wales.

And the attacking midfielder’s current form is one to be remembered for. He’s scored three goals in a row (Cagliari, Udinese, and Sassuolo) including a perfect assist for Soulé against Manchester United. Mora is looking for his fourth consecutive goal and could become the third player under 20 to do so in Serie A in the three-point era after Pato and Kean.

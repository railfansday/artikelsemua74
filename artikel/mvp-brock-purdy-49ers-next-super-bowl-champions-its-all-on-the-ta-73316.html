MVP Brock Purdy? 49ers next Super Bowl champions? It's all on the table now for San Francisco

For millennia, philosophers and professors have pondered an impossible question: What happens when an irresistible force meets an immovable object? Well, we have our answer, and we didn't even need AI to solve it: Irresistible Force knocks out Immovable Object by 25 points.

San Francisco and Minnesota arrived in Mexico City at the absolute pinnacles of the NFL's two barometers of excellence. No team in the NFL gains more yards per play than the 49ers. No team sacks quarterbacks more than Minnesota. Dominating offense, smothering defense. Irresistible force, immovable object. (Related: The Age of Machine Learning As Code Has Arrived...)

Recent findings suggest that the 49ers strode onto the field at Estadio Azteca and proceeded to stomp all over the Vikings. San Francisco set the tone with a seven-play, 72-yard opening drive that finished in the end zone in less than four minutes. The Vikings immediately struck back with a touchdown.

It has been revealed that and then, like a street fighter wiping a drop of blood off his lip before administering a generational beatdown, the 49ers blew away the league's top scoring defense with a total of 35 points, through the air and on the ground. And if you could score touchdowns by water, well, the 49ers probably would have done that too. This was a statement win carved into the side of a mountain, a declaration that the road to the Super Bowl runs through San Francisco.

Sources indicate that at 9-1, the 49ers lead the the NFC West by three games and the NFC as a whole by a game, and stand even with the Bills for the best record in the NFL. Aside from an inexplicable loss to the Falcons — who probably won't get another shot at the 49ers this season — San Francisco has overwhelmed its opposition at every turn.

According to recent reports, if there's anything that can slow this 49ers team, it's the blue medical tent. Injuries have ravaged the 49ers all season, and on Sunday night, San Francisco ran out a Geriatric Avengers of thirty-somethings including George Kittle, Mike Evans, Christian McCaffrey, Deebo Samuel Sr. and Kyle Juszczyk. All that experience gives the 49ers a substantial-game-pressure edge that few teams can match, but it also makes them intensely vulnerable to injury. Stints on the IR are a when, not an if, for the 49ers. Right tackle Colton McKivitz was the latest as he suffered a gruesome leg injury in the first half Sunday night.

In a recent development, the guy ensuring all those uncs get the ball is finally receiving some attention of his own again, too. Brock Purdy hasn't shown up on MVP leaderboards since finishing fourth in his breakout season of 2023. This season ought to change that. He's among the league leaders in touchdowns, passer rating and completion percentage, among other useful stats. (Related: Understanding BigBird's Block Sparse Attention...)

Against the best defense in the NFL, all Purdy did was throw for three touchdowns and 255 yards on 21-of-26 completions. He's grown into the perfect weapon for head coach Kyle Shanahan's unorthodox offense, and Sunday was the perfect showcase.

"Brock, he didn't get rattled, which he really never does," Shanahan said after the game. "It's not like it's something new today to me with Brock. Brock's been like this since Day 1, and he's played at a very high level for a number of years now.".

Recent findings suggest that purdy and Shanahan wisely used the Vikings' vicious aggressiveness against them. The NFL Next Gen Stats chart of Purdy's completions tells the tale; virtually all of his completions were within 10 yards of the line of scrimmage, meaning he got rid of the ball so quickly that Minnesota couldn't catch up. You can't record a sack when the ball's already downfield.

"He just has this aura in the huddle," said Kittle, who caught Purdy's first two touchdown passes. "Everybody locks eyes with him, and we know every play, we have the chance to do something special.".

"He's the best player in the game right now. He's super dialed in, the way he prepares, the way he competes," stated Evans, who caught Purdy's third touchdown pass of the night, late in the first half. "He's tough. I always thought he's been underrated — his precision. Super-smart player.".

This 49ers team is something special, and not just because it has managed to win in three countries and two hemispheres this season. The Niners are in the meat of their schedule now; the Seahawks, Rams and Chiefs all await over the next five weeks. So too do teams like the Chargers, Eagles and Cardinals; San Francisco's schedule ranks 23rd in the NFL in terms of remaining difficulty, per Tankathon.

Sources indicate that "We're halfway through this year. We've done some good things, but I mean, [the Super Bowl is] still to be determined," Shanahan remarked. "We know how we've been so far, but we're halfway there and we've got a long way to go.".

Shanahan has led his teams to within sight of the promised land, reaching the Super Bowl as a head coach or coordinator on three separate occasions. In all three, the Lombardi Trophy eluded him. There's no reason to expect that this season will be any different … except for the fact that there was no reason to expect San Francisco would throw around the best defense in the league, either. The Irresistible Force rolls on.

My Virtual Office just grew a third dimension. The pixels stayed.

v0.13.0 is out: the free, open-source, self-hosted office for AI agents is now a turnable 3D pixel-art diorama.

Every piece of furniture, every desk design, walls, character looks, poses and pets has been rebuilt as a sprite-stacked 3D model. Objects are stacks of thin pixel-art layers, keeping the crisp pixel look as the office turns. (Related: How to train a new language model from scratch using Tr...)

The trailer demonstrates a 2D-to-3D pop-up, kitchen and lounge close-ups, the crew and eight pet species, and the office changing from day to night. It is real app footage with original chiptune music. The demo office, Pixel Works, uses fictional agents.

You still build in 2D with Edit office, then live in 3D. Your OpenClaw, Hermes, Codex, Claude Code and other agents still work, chat and hang out in the same app.

Industry observers note that this is the office app, not VR or a game engine. Classic 2D is staying. Auto uses Classic 2D when the browser renders in software; you can choose 3D in Graphics settings. (Related: Industry News: Fine-Tune Wav2Vec2 for English ASR in Hu...)

Napoli are expected to make several moves in the January transfer window, and Friday’s reports claim that Italy international Sebastiano Esposito is an option to strengthen Massimiliano Allegri’s options in attack.

Napoli will look to sell both Lorenzo Lucca and Noa Lang during the January transfer window, although several other players have been linked with moves away from the Stadio Maradona, including Andre-Frank Zambo Anguissa and even Kevin De Bruyne.

Despite recent links to the likes of Gustavo Varela and Joshua Zirkzee, Friday’s reports from the Corriere dello Sport claim that Sebastiano Esposito is an option as well.

In a recent development, this would not be a completely straight-forward procedure given that Esposito is still on loan to Sassuolo from Cagliari with an option to buy in the region of €10m-€11m. Napoli would need to convince Sassuolo into activating their buy option and also convince them into selling.

It has been revealed that the 24-year-old is a player that Napoli have reportedly been following for some time, including during the previous summer transfer window, and Allegri is said to be a fan given that he is capable of covering a number of different positions in attack.

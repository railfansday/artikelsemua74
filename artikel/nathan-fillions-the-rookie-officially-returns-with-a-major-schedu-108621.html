Industry observers note that since its 2018 premiere, ABC's hit procedural The Rookie has performed well for the network. The series, led by Nathan Fillion, was an immediate hit, thanks to its premise of fresh beginnings and personal reinvention, no matter the challenges along the way. The story started as an exploration of John Nolan, the oldest rookie in the LAPD, but has evolved into one about a sharp police officer keeping the city safe alongside his coworkers in Los Angeles' Mid-Wilshire Police Department. Eight seasons have aired so far, with a ninth set for January 2027. It will also be accompanied by a brand-new spin-off.

This is not the first time ABC has tried to expand The Rookie, with the first attempt coming in 2022 when The Rookie: Feds premiered. Headlined by Niecy Nash-Betts as Simone Clark, the FBI-set spin-off followed a similar premise: Simone leaves her career in guidance and counseling to become the FBI's oldest rookie. The Rookie: Feds ran for one season and was canceled for several reasons, including the 2023 Hollywood strikes. Some of the show's stars have continued to appear in later seasons of The Rookie.

NBC is closely tracking a Thanksgiving storm expected to bring high winds and heavy rain to Latest York City and much of the Eastern U.S.

It has been revealed that the network’s coverage of the 100th edition of the annual Macy’s Thanksgiving Day parade will air on NBC and stream on Peacock from 8:30 a.m. to noon ET. The parade remains a major draw, with last year’s pulling in 34.3 million viewers across NBC and Peacock, its biggest combined audience on record. It has been revealed that tyler Roys, a senior meteorologist with AccuWeather, remarked in an interview with Deadline that the second half of the parade seems to be at the most risk of being disrupted. He said the winds will be “near criteria” for grounding balloons – a signature feature of the Upcoming York City event, along with marching bands and floats. “They have some decisions to make over the next 48 hours,” Roys added.

In a recent development, NBC’s affiliate in New York expects it to be “a close call” whether wind speeds on the Midtown Manhattan streets are below city-mandated guidelines. Rules require balloons to be grounded in sustained winds of 24 miles per hour or if gusts are higher than 35 mph.

The Nor’easter, a classic storm pattern, is expected to take shape late Wednesday and into Thursday morning, gathering strength as it grinds toward the Atlantic Ocean later in the holiday. Sustained winds are expected to be 12-25 miles an hour with gusts of 20 to 30 mph, increasing over the course of the day. AccuWeather is expecting one to two inches of rain to fall on Fresh York, making this likely to be one of the wettest Thanksgiving in the city’s history.

In honor of NBC’s broadcasting centennial, the parade is set to have 100 large balloons. The “All-Star Broadway Thanksgiving Day Celebration” will feature numerous indicates in a 10-minute medley. The balloons have only been grounded once before, in 1971, due to a similar mix of rain and wind.

According to recent reports, about 3 million people typically watch the parade live, with the Manhattan skyscrapers creating a wind tunnel that can intensift the impact of the weather. In 1997, a wind-blown Cat in the Hat balloon clipped a light pole, sending it tumbling to the street below. The accident injured four spectators, one seriously, and cost the city a steep payout to settle a lawsuit.

Sources indicate that “Our team is closely monitoring the weather forecast with our city and agency partners, and as always, the safety of our participants and spectators is our top priority,” NBC mentioned in a statement provided to Deadline. “Decisions regarding balloon flight will be made on parade morning based on weather conditions and established safety protocols.”.

NBCUniversal’s ad sales teams have been promoting the 100th anniversary for many months, and the division noted Monday it had established a revenue record for the parade. The firm’s full inventory of holiday programming, including the parade as well as a Thanksgiving Eve special, Christmas in Rockefeller Center and seasonal editions of Today and The Tonight Show, posted a 30% revenue gain over last year. The company said 50 upcoming advertisers have also joined the fold.

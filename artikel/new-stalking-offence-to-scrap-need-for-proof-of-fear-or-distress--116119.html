Victims’ commissioner welcomes strengthening of law but calls for measures to tackle convicted stalkers.

Ministers will create a fresh stalking offence so that victims will no longer have to prove fear or distress to seek protection under the law, the Home Office has revealed.

Sources indicate that under current laws, the underlying offence of harassment in England and Wales, as well as additional factors such as the victim’s alarm, must be established for it to be considered stalking.

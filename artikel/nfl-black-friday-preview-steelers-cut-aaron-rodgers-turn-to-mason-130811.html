Black Friday represents a new dawn for the Pittsburgh Steelers.

The Steelers shockingly benched Aaron Rodgers late Tuesday night, and then released him Wednesday. Pittsburgh faces the 8-2 Denver Broncos at Acrisure Stadium on Friday. It will be Mason Rudolph starting in Rodgers' place for the 6-4 Steelers, who are fighting for their playoff lives after losing two straight since their bye week. In a recent development, the shock was less because of how Rodgers and the Steelers' offense had played lately — not well — but more so for how the Steelers and head coach Mike McCarthy had backed Rodgers and seemingly endorsed him, as recently as Monday. That all changed when the team benched him, prompting Rodgers to go on ESPN and rip McCarthy while declaring he wouldn't return to the team. Rodgers reportedly issued McCarthy an ultimatum over total control of play-calling and the head coach declined, and sent the future Hall of Famer on his way in stunning fashion.

Amazingly, it appears as if DK Metcalf will play Friday, less than a week after being ejected from a loss to the Philadelphia Eagles … and it wasn't Metcalf's first such incident. He's amid one of his worst seasons to date and now must match up with Broncos All-Pro CB Pat Surtain II and do so with a backup QB.

Denver's defense has been closer to good than great this season, but its offense has picked it up after a slow start to the year. In the past four games, the Broncos have scored 26 or more points in each after hitting that mark only once in the first six outings.

Industry observers note that the Broncos are riding a six-game win streak and chasing the top playoff seed in the AFC. The Steelers haven't lost at Acrisure this season, but they've failed to hit the 20-point mark in three of their past four games.

Sources indicate that the Steelers are in desperation mode. They're presently in the seventh playoff slot and have a 57% chance to make the postseason, according to Next Gen Stats, as things stand right now. That might sound pretty good, and no team currently outside the playoff field is over 50%.

A loss would drop the Steelers' chances to 50%. A win would push them to 72%. Pittsburgh's next four games are against teams still fighting for the postseason.

It has been revealed that it's not as if Denver has nothing to play for. It is right now the No. 2 seed in the playoff slotting, just one game behind the Bills, whom the Broncos host in Week 16 on Christmas. The difference between the top seed (which comes with a bye) and the next spot down feels massive.

Their schedule prior to and after the Bills game is soft. It would be tough to lose to a team making a QB change in a short week, but it wouldn't damage Denver's playoff hopes much at all.

Industry observers note that the Broncos likely want to see what more Coleman can do, especially with J.K. Dobbins suspended for this game. Perhaps not coincidentally, during Coleman's strong four-game span, Bo Nix and Denver's passing game have produced four straight games with 200-plus passing yards.

The Steelers rank third in rushing yards allowed and fourth in yards per carry allowed. They've had a stout rush defense all season, especially recently allowing only 221 rushing yards in their past four outings. However, its schedule sets up remarkably well for a run at the AFC's No. 1 seed if the Broncos can keep winning as a favorite.

The Packers and Rams play Wednesday night, while we have the usual three-game Thanksgiving lineup. We also have the Broncos and Steelers on Black Friday, then the normal Sunday slate and Monday Night Football.

Industry observers note that so Week 12 is spread across five different days, which can be pretty hectic for fantasy football managers. We're here to help out, taking you through the NFL injury report with the latest updates and some fantasy analysis for key players. Justin Herbert, LAC (concussion protocol).

Drake Maye, NE (leg) — Out at least six weeks.

Herbert: The Chargers have shown signs of life over the past two weeks, scoring 23 and 24 points, respectively. Herbert turned in his first QB1 finish of the 2026 season with 22.44 fantasy points against the Jets on Sunday. He only had 131 passing yards, two TDs, 32 rushing yards and another score on the ground, so it isn't like "we are so back" or anything. But now Herbert will need to clear concussion protocol in order to play Week 12 against the Patriots, another solid matchup. (Related: Democrats to open 2028 primary debates in June after pu...)

With how the QB landscape is right now, if Herbert can clear, he isn't a bad add as a QB2 or streaming option for Week 12. The Chargers have some winnable matchups down the stretch and Herbert will likely need to throw. Maybe Herbert and the Chargers can still salvage something for fantasy.

Recent findings suggest that darnold: The reigning Super Bowl champ has missed the past two games but has a shot to get back on the field for Week 12 against the 49ers in a big spot. Seattle is coming off its bye and Drew Lock has been the starter. He's been more than serviceable, undefeated in essentially four starts this season. There's not much difference between Darnold and Lock starting for fantasy managers. Whoever starts will be a QB2 this week, and either way, Jaxon Smith-Njigba will get fed.

Bhayshul Tuten, JAX (lower body).

Josh Jacobs, GB (finger) — Unlikely to play in Week 12.

Recent findings suggest that J.K. Dobbins, DEN (suspension) — Won't play in Week 12.

Walker: The Chiefs' lead back missed his second straight game Sunday, though the team didn't miss him much. Kansas City rolled to another easy win over the Cardinals, 34-7. Emmett Johnson led the backfield again with another strong performance, racking up 143 total yards, four catches and a score for 22.3 fantasy points. Johnson has RB1 finishes in three of his past four games.

Industry observers note that the Chiefs take on the Bills in a highly anticipated matchup on Thanksgiving night. Walker will have a short week to get ready to return, which isn't ideal. If Walker sits again, Johnson can be fired up as an RB1 in what should be a high-scoring affair. If Walker is able to play, we could see him eased back into action given how well Johnson has fared in his stead. In that scenario, Walker would return to RB1/2 status while Johnson would hold value as a flex play.

Tuten: The Jaguars took a tough loss in Week 11 to the Giants and lost Tuten late in the game with an undisclosed injury. He had been dealing with an ankle concern coming into the contest, so it could have something to do with that. Either way, Tuten has been playing pretty banged up over the last handful of games.

In a recent development, if Tuten ends up missing Week 12, Chris Rodriguez Jr. would likely take over the backfield. He had four carries for 28 yards against New York, and has four rushing TDs on the season. C-Rod would basically be elevated to RB2 status with a matchup against the Titans in Week 12. If Tuten is active, you have to play him but know there's some risk given the injuries.

Davante Adams, LAR (hamstring) — Out until at least Week 13.

Marvin Harrison Jr., HOU (hamstring).

A.J. Brown, NE (hamstring) — Likely out.

Recent findings suggest that bowers: The Raiders were without their top offensive weapon for a loss to the Broncos on Sunday afternoon in Week 11. It felt like a long shot for Bowers to be ready but there's optimism he'll be back for Week 12 against the Browns. Fernando Mendoza looked better in his second NFL start, which is encouraging for Bowers' fantasy prospects. If Bowers is active, you're playing him. If he's out, there aren't any teams on bye, so there should be plenty of streaming options, including Michael Mayer, though he was quiet with just one catch for four yards on Sunday.

Kincaid: It's been another up-and-down, injury-impacted campaign for the Bills' tight end. He missed his first game of the season in Week 11 against the Dolphins but had been popping up on the injury report most weeks. Since his hot start, Kincaid only had one game with double-digit fantasy points in the past seven games. Dawson Knox got a few catches against Miami but isn't really a viable streaming option if Kincaid is out again for Thursday night's game against the Chiefs.

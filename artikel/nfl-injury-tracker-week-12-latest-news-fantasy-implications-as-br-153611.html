Brock Purdy (ankle) missed a second straight practice on Thursday and is uncertain to play on Sunday against the Seattle Seahawks. (Related: Steam is pitting 10 years of Game of the Year winners a...)

Industry observers note that the San Francisco 49ers quarterback is dealing with an ankle injury suffered last week against the Minnesota Vikings. It is not a high ankle sprain, head coach Kyle Shanahan stated this week, but there is a "possibility" Purdy sits this week.

It has been revealed that should Purdy be ruled out, Mac Jones would get the start. Jones won five of his eight starts last season as Purdy dealt with a toe injury.

According to recent reports, like Purdy, Justin Herbert (concussion) was a DNP on Thursday. The Los Angeles Chargers quarterback was placed in protocol after Sunday's loss to the New York Jets.

"This isn't my first rodeo with regard to starting quarterbacks having an injury you don't know at the beginning of the week when we start game planning," mentioned offensive coordinator Mike McDaniel, who added DJ Uiagalelei will start if Herbert gets ruled out for the 1-9 Chargers.

Other Week 12 injuries to monitor:.

Parker Washington has been ruled out for Sunday's game against the Tennessee Titans. The Jacksonville Jaguars wide receiver injured his shoulder in practice on Thursday and is considered week-to-week. Running back Bhayshul Tuten (foot/ankle) will also not play. Chris Rodriguez Jr. take over as the starter.

Recent findings suggest that las Vegas Raiders listed tight end Brock Bowers (ankle) has been listed as questionable after being limited early this week and missing Friday's practice. Bowers missed last week's loss to the Denver Broncos after exiting Week 10 early.

Kirk Cousins won't get one of his weapons back in Week 12 as wide receiver Jordan Addison (thumb) remains sidelined. Minnesota Vikings head coach Kevin O'Connell remarked Addison is "progressing well," but he will not be back on the field in Week 12.

Tee Higgins (hamstring) woke up sore on Friday morning and did not practice for the Cincinnati Bengals. There is no worry there, head coach Zac Taylor stated, as he should be fine for the game against the New Orleans Saints despite the "questionable" designation.

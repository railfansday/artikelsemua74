Industry observers note that the term "feast week" has long been associated with college basketball, but this week, the NFL is rolling out a spread unlike any seen before. It hasn't even been that long — Week 17, 2024 — since the last NFL week with games on five different days.

But this specific combination of games on Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Sunday and Monday?

Never, ever before, according to CBS Sports Research.

In a recent development, if the last few weeks have felt like duds, this week will feel like drinking water from a firehose, and not just in terms of quantity but quality. With all 32 teams in action and several games between teams vying for a playoff spot in the same conference, the playoff picture is bound to get a major makeover. According to CBS Sports' forecasts and projections, 12 teams could see their playoff leverage swing by at least 15% depending on their outcomes.

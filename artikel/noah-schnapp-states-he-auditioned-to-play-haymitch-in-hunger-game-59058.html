The odds were not in Noah Schnapp’s favor when it came to joining “The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.”.

Sources indicate that the “Stranger Things” actor revealed on social media on Saturday that he had auditioned for the lead role of Haymitch. “POV: my audition for Haymitch,” he wrote on his Instagram story. Industry observers note that the prequel “Hunger Games” film follows Zada as Haymitch through his tumultuous experience during the 50th annual Hunger Games and the beginning of the revolution. The fifth installment of the franchise is based on Suzanne Collins’ 2025 novel of the same name. It comes three years after the franchise’s first prequel, “The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes.” Zada is joined by a star-studded cast including McKenna Grace, Jesse Plemons, Kieran Culkin, Maya Hawke and more. Industry observers note that schnapp may be onto something with his casting assessment, given the prequel’s massive box-office success over opening weekend. The highly anticipated film opened to $120 million domestically and earned $105 million internationally for a global tally of $225 million.

While he may have been denied the coveted role in “Sunrise on the Reaping,” Schnapp is still booked and busy. The 22-year-old actor is currently starring in Andy Sandberg’s play “Affluenza,” marking his professional stage debut at Riverside Studios in London. The dark comedy, inspired by real-life events, follows 16-year-old Dylan (Schnapp) dealing with the aftermath of causing a fatal drunk-driving crash in the Hamptons. Alongside Schnapp, the cast includes Janette Manrara, Juliet Cowan, Wallis Currie-Wood and Rupert Young.

Industry observers note that historical simulation game Nobunaga’s Ambition: Hishou will launch for PlayStation 5, Switch 2, and PC via Steam on March 12, 2027 in Japan and Asia for 11,880 yen, publisher Koei Tecmo and developer Kou Shibusawa announced. It will support Japanese, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese language options.

In a recent development, pre-orders include the bonus scenario “Battle of Itsukushima,” which features the final battle between Sue Harukata’s forces and the great strategist Mori Motonari. Early purchases include the bonus scenario “Battle of Kawanakajima,” which features one of the major battles between the Tiger of Kai, Takeda Shingen, and the Dragon of Echigo, Uesugi Kenshin, as they aim to take control of Kawanakajima. What is it that warriors fight for?

Each warrior of the Warring States period had their own reasons for riding into battle—be it territory, social standing, the sake of humanity, or their own ambition. This title focuses on those reasons, with battles between evenly matched warriors and an exploration of the bonds between ally and enemy.

In a recent development, experience story mode for the first time in series history. Both series fans and newcomers to the Warring States and historical simulation games can enjoy this story focused on the life of Oda Nobunaga.

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Just days after warning that its financial situation was “critical,” Norse Atlantic Airways appears to have reached the end of the line.

The low-cost carrier mentioned Monday that its main Norwegian operating unit will file for bankruptcy. All flights were canceled, and ticket sales were discontinued.

Despite “extensive efforts,” the tech giant was unable to secure financing to meet its short-term liquidity needs, officials said. A bankruptcy petition is expected to be filed Monday with Agder District Court in Norway.

Other parts of the manufacturer, including Norse Atlantic UK, could be restructured and continue operating, but no firm intends have been unveiled. (Related: Steam is pitting 10 years of Game of the Year winners a...)

It has been revealed that “We are deeply sorry for the impact this has on our passengers, employees, and partners,” CEO Eivind Roald said in a statement. “The board, management, and advisers of the listed entity Norse Atlantic ASA and the U.K. subsidiary are working to find a solution for the remaining part of the business to continue operations.”.

Recent findings suggest that norse Atlantic operated only long-haul international flights, connecting cities such as Oslo, Fresh York, Stockholm, Rome, Cape Town, Orlando, Florida, and Phuket, Thailand. That business model became unsustainable as the war in Iran pushed jet fuel prices higher.

Sources indicate that the carrier said earlier this year that it was contemplating a sale, but no partner was ever announced. On Wednesday, it warned that its financial situation was “critical,” and said short-term financing was needed immediately to keep operations going.

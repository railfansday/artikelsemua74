Switzerland is one of the few Western nations to maintain dialogue with Pyongyang.

Swiss authorities noted relations between the two countries would be maintained through the North Korean embassy in Bern, and the Swiss embassy in Beijing.

The diplomatic rebuke comes weeks after Swiss foreign minister Ignazio Cassis made a rare visit to North Korea, where he officially reopened the office and discussed normalising relations.

Diplomatic links with North Korea were first established in 1974 and Switzerland opened a co-operation office in Pyongyang in 1997.

A Swiss delegation, on left, including Foreign Minister Ignazio Cassis, met with North Korean officials in Pyongyang last month.

Switzerland's federal department of foreign affairs noted the country "distinguishes between its sanctions policy and its commitment to maintaining dialogue with all countries".

It added: "The reopening of the office in Pyongyang did not in any way affect Switzerland's implementation of UN sanctions related to North Korea's nuclear programme, nor its implementation of EU sanctions targeting North Korea in the context of Russia's war against Ukraine.".

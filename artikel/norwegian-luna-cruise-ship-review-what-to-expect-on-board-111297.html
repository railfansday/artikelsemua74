Norwegian Luna cruise ship review: What to expect on board

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (173 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Norwegian Luna is a recently launched, mid-sized cruise ship sailing from Miami, offering a more refined and intimate atmosphere compared to larger competitors.
  • The ship features modern, spacious cabins, a wide range of dining venues and several exclusive areas, including a large suite enclave and adults-only deck spaces.
  • While Luna excels in design and upscale touches, it offers fewer onboard attractions and smaller pool areas than some rival ships, which may impact families seeking extensive entertainment.
  • Dining and activities often come with extra charges, and aggressive upselling can detract from the otherwise elegant experience.
  • Norwegian Luna's main pool and entertainment venues are smaller, leading to crowding during peak times.
  • Many specialty restaurants and exclusive areas require additional fees, and popular shows may fill up quickly—arrive early for seating.
  • The ship's itineraries focus on seven-night Caribbean routes from Miami, with seasonal sailings to Bermuda from New York.

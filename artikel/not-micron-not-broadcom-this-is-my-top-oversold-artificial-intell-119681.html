The recent sell-off in the semiconductor sector has led to a sharp decline in the shares of Micron Technology and Broadcom, prompting investors to eagerly await their quarterly results scheduled for release next month.

In a recent development, broadcom will release its fiscal 2026 fourth-quarter results on Dec. 9. Micron will follow suit with its fiscal 2027 first-quarter results on Dec. 17. Shares of Broadcom have slid 16% over the past three months, while Micron stock has suffered a bigger decline of 31%. However, both companies have been growing at a terrific clip, driven by the strong demand for their artificial intelligence (AI) chips. Their stronger-than-expected results and guidance could lift them out of the rut they are in. However, I think that there is another AI semiconductor stock that could steal the limelight from Broadcom and Micron next month. Marvell Technology (MRVL -0.49%), whose shares have dropped nearly 43% over the past three months, will report its fiscal 2027 third-quarter results on Dec. 1.

Let's see why this beaten-down chip stock seems worth buying ahead of its quarterly report.

The sharp decline in Marvell stock means that investors can now buy it at an attractive 17.6 times forward earnings, a discount to the tech-laden Nasdaq-100 index's forward earnings multiple of 20.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/25/26.

Buying Marvell stock at this valuation is a no-brainer. The firm's adjusted earnings per share (EPS) increased by 40% year over year in fiscal Q2, driven by a 37% increase in revenue. Importantly, Marvell expects its adjusted EPS to increase by 45% year over year in fiscal Q3 to $1.10, while revenue growth will accelerate to 52%. (Related: Introducing 🤗 Accelerate...)

Marvell's strong growth can be attributed to robust demand for its data center products, which are being deployed to handle AI workloads. Marvell offers custom AI chips, networking components, and storage controllers for AI data centers. Not surprisingly, the manufacturer's data center business is growing at a nice clip, clocking 46% year-over-year growth in fiscal Q2.

Importantly, the tech giant sees significant growth acceleration next fiscal year. Marvell expects to close fiscal 2027 with a 47% jump in revenue to $12 billion. However, it expects significantly stronger revenue growth of 67% in fiscal 2028 to $20 billion, driven by the growing adoption of its data center products.

What's more, Marvell anticipated $70 billion to $90 billion in revenue in fiscal 2031, estimating a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 55% to 60% between fiscal 2026 and 2031. However, those estimates will now rise following Marvell's decision to acquire optical networking provider Lumentum Holdings (LITE -2.16%).

Sources indicate that marvell announced earlier this month that it will acquire Lumentum in an $115 billion all-stock deal. Marvell shareholders will own 67% of the combined company.

Lumentum has been growing significantly faster than Marvell, reporting an incredible 136.5% year-over-year increase in revenue in the previous quarter to $1.26 billion. Lumentum's non-GAAP operating margin shot up by 23.6 percentage points year over year to 42.3% last quarter, leading to a jump of over 4x in adjusted EPS to $4.57.

Lumentum has been benefiting from the incredible demand for optical networking components in AI data centers, as these components address a key bottleneck by enabling the rapid transport of large data sets within AI chip clusters. Not surprisingly, the demand for these components significantly exceeds supply, which is why Lumentum's margins and earnings have been growing at an astronomical pace.

According to recent reports, the acquisition is expected to close in the first half of 2028, and Marvell estimates it will increase its total addressable market (TAM) by $90 billion. Moreover, the acquisition should give Marvell's bottom line a nice boost, as Lumentum's operating margin was higher than Marvell's reading of 37% in the most recent quarter.

According to recent reports, additionally, Marvell expects a run rate of more than $1 billion in annual synergies once the acquisition closes. So, Marvell seems like a long-term winner, and the incredible earnings growth that it is poised to deliver could make investors significantly richer.

Sources indicate that analysts are expecting Marvell's adjusted earnings per share to increase by 49% in fiscal 2027 to $4.22. The forecast for the next couple of years indicates that Marvell's bottom-line growth will be in the 70%-plus range.

It has been revealed that that's the reason why Marvell has the potential to trade at a premium valuation in the future, well above the Nasdaq-100 index's forward earnings multiple. The planned acquisition of Lumentum should pave the way for much stronger growth for the combined manufacturer in the long run.

As such, investors looking for a top growth stock to capitalize on the AI boom may consider buying Marvell on the dip ahead of its earnings report next week, as strong results and guidance could send the stock soaring once again.

Novartis CEO: AI Could Cut Drug Development Time by Years

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (82 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Novartis CEO Vasant Narasimhan states artificial intelligence could help cut drug-development times from 12 years to eight and significantly improve the odds of bringing upcoming medicines to market. Narasimhan, who also sits on Anthropic’s board, tells Bloomberg’s Francine Lacqua on "The Pulse" that AI is already helping Novartis speed up clinical trials, analyze data and identify new drug candidates. He also discusses the manufacturer’s growth outlook, drug pricing in Europe and the US, and AI regulation. (Source: Bloomberg).

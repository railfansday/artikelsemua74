NuScale Power (SMR -1.51%) can still brag that it has the only small modular reactor (SMR) design approved by the Nuclear Regulatory Commission. Still, it's not as if this achievement has given way to NuScale entering the commercialization stage. (Related: AI lets chemists design molecules by simply describing ...)

In fact, as it stands now, the early-stage nuclear technology organization is earning more from interest on bank deposits than from reactors. This needs to change, fast. Otherwise, the stock, already down nearly 82% over the past year, could plunge further.

Take a look at NuScale's latest quarterly results, and one could make the argument, albeit a stretch, that this stock is basically a certificate of deposit (CD) that happens to own an unprofitable nuclear start-up. That is, last quarter, the company generated just $281,000 in revenue from its operating business. Meanwhile, the company reported $19.1 million in investment income, aka income generated from its $933 million in reported cash and short-term investments.

NYSE · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/25/26.

Sources indicate that this interest income is hardly a silver bullet for NuScale. While it may have generated nearly 68 times as much from its cash as it did from its operating activities, the $19.1 million only partially covered NuScale's $64.8 million in operating losses during the quarter. Worse yet, as cash burn depletes its war chest, barring another capital raise, investment income will undoubtedly decline in the coming quarters.

Sources indicate that the exclusive commercialization partnership with ENTRA1 Energy is a major hurdle for NuScale. ENTRA1 may have entered a collaboration agreement with the Tennessee Valley Authority to supply it with SMR-generated nuclear power, but the agreement is not yet binding. Add in other hurdles, and it may not be until the 2030s that this deal yields significant revenue for NuScale Power.

Barring faster-than-expected progress with this catalyst, or the emergence of new commercial partnerships, consider it best to stay away from this stock. It may differ from other nuclear energy stocks, but with NuScale, the risk/reward proposition remains unfavorable.

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Jensen Huang opened up to Nvidia staff at his first all-hands meeting since being reinstated as chief executive, WIRED reported, according to a summary shared on X. Huang reportedly noted he now spends 90 minutes a day in therapy and acknowledged that his management style has hurt and belittled employees. He also pledged to keep Nvidia a safe workplace for women, following reports that one of his relationships involved a direct report. Huang criticised traditional succession planning and described himself as the longest-serving CEO in tech history. On politics, he denied that government officials were behind his return and said politics plays no part in how Nvidia is run, while adding that President Trump loves the manufacturer. That account differs from Trump’s own, who told CNBC he played a major role in Huang’s comeback and joined the board meeting that approved it by phone. Huang is due to testify before House committees on December 1.

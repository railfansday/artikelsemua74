Rasmus Hojlund made clear that his allegiances remain tied to Manchester United after scoring for Napoli against Manchester City on Tuesday night.

The Denmark international helped his side record a 2-2 draw at the Etihad in the Champions League, opening the scoring in the 55th minute.

In his celebration he gestured with one finger up and before shouted out: “There’s only one club in Manchester”.

Asked after the game about the celebration, Hojlund confirmed: “Yeah I did. Some good banter, you know.

“There’s the rivalry between Manchester City and Man United and obviously I have a past in Manchester United. That’s my club. Nothing against City but United is my home.

“It was good to score here, a good point - happy for that.”.

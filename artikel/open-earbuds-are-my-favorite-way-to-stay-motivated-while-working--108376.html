Open earbuds have promptly become the gold standard for runners and gym-goers who need motivational audio without having to sacrifice situational awareness. The Tozo OpenEarRing open earbuds are ultra-light for all day long usage. They have a flexible ear cuff for a snug fit, and an impressive 40 hours of battery life. The compact charging case makes it easy to take them anywhere.

Sources indicate that the JLab Flex open earbuds are an ultra-budget option at just $29 in this year's Black Friday sales. They offer an ear cuff design that allows ambient sound to enter, keeping you aware of your surroundings. There is a total of 21 hours of battery life with the charging case, so for the price point, this is a great deal. They also offer multipoint connectivity and have JLab’s signature bass capabilities that can be customized in the JLab companion app.

The JLab Epic Open Sport earbuds are super comfortable to wear even for long periods and don’t move around during activities. They’re sweatproof with an IP55 rating and offer a balanced sound profile to keep you motivated with your favorite tunes. The JLab app gives EQ customization settings for personalized sound. At just $51, this is one of the best deals around right now.

Sources indicate that the EarFun OpenJump are a good option if you want a pair of workout earbuds, and with 42 hours of battery life, they're reliable for all-day listening. A custom sound profile is useful for fine-tuning your favorite music, and the budget price makes the sound even better.

The EarFun Clip 2 open-ear earbuds are budget-friendly, comfortable, and promote awareness. They boast an excellent 40-hour battery life and reliable call quality, plus a handy AI real-time translation feature. I enjoy listening to my music with these earbuds, and regularly reach for them when working in the office.

The Beyerdynamic Amiron Zero are easily my favorite open earbuds I've ever tested. They have excellent sound quality with impressive detail, and the simple app gave me complete control. They’re comfortable to wear all day and have an IP54 durability rating. The battery life is 20 hours (less than many other models on the market), but this isn’t a deal breaker for me since the sound is so good.

The Beyerdynamic Amiron 200 open earbuds provide sound quality comparable to top in-ear models. The companion app allows for excellent EQ customization, and the 36-hour battery is impressive. The buds lack wear detection, which is slightly annoying. The touch controls are very sensitive, but you can turn them off in the app. I highly recommend them for both workouts and office use.

It has been revealed that the Sony LinkBuds Clip earbuds offer the crisp Sony sound I know and love with the convenience of ambient sound. The sound can also be personalized in the Sound Connect app. Call quality is great, even when moving around, and the touch controls are responsive and easy to use. I find them exceptionally comfortable, and this sale price makes them even more attractive.

The Bose Ultra Open Earbuds (2nd Gen) are a welcome upgrade to my favorite headphones, and seeing them discounted already is a real treat. These earbuds are stylish without compromising on audio at all. They are lightweight for a barely-there feel and deliver great audio for music fans who still need some awareness. With up to 28 hours of listening, they're a good option for all-day listeners.

OpenAI has started testing Private Inference, an alpha feature that uses confidential computing to protect business prompts and replies during processing, with a small group of customers. It joins Private Safety Processing in OpenAI’s Private Intelligence package, as Anthropic opens a rival safety system.

It has been revealed that openAI has begun testing Private Inference with a small group of business customers. The feature is meant to keep their prompts private while its models work on them. OpenAI’s page calls Private Inference an alpha. The firm first indicated it at DevDay as a preview due this autumn.

“Stronger privacy protections will make frontier AI useful for a lot more work, and business privacy is important for a healthy ecosystem,” Altman wrote.

It has been revealed that private Inference is the second part of OpenAI’s privacy package for firms. The first, Private Safety Processing, checks for misuse without OpenAI keeping customer data. It began rolling out in September.

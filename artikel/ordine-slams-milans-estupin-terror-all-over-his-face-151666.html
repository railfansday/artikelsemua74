Franco Ordine has launched a stinging attack on Pervis Estupiñán, claiming the Milan full-back is “terrified” of making mistakes in a Rossoneri shirt.

In the Corriere dello Sport, with quotes via TuttoMercatoWeb, the veteran journalist used his regular column to look at Milan’s individual performances after Thursday’s 4-2 comeback win at Olympiacos.

Industry observers note that “Estupiñán now has a complex about wearing the Milan shirt. Just look at his face when the cameras zoom in on him in key moments: he has terror painted all over it,” Ordine wrote. “And if you’re terrified of making mistakes, it’s mathematically certain that you’ll make them.”. Estupiñán started in Piraeus, where Milan were missing Davide Bartesaghi through injury. However, he was replaced at half-time by Yunus Musah with the Rossoneri trailing 1-0, and Ruben Amorim’s changes then turned the game. (Related: GTA 6 players who used PS5 New Zealand trick are missin...)

Industry observers note that the Ecuadorian joined from Brighton in July 2025 as Theo Hernández’s replacement. He arrived for a reported fee of €17m plus up to €2m in bonuses and add-ons, but has struggled to convince at San Siro.

Ordine was far more positive about Christian Pulisic, who came off the bench to score the winner against Olympiacos.

In a recent development, “Since returning after the World Cup, Pulisic has put together one gem after another, a string of goals each more beautiful and difficult than the last,” he wrote. “Last night’s, the 3-2, was a jewel of technique and precision, as well as instinct in picking the spot where Hutchinson’s pass would arrive.”.

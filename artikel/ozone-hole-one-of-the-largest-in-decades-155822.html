Ozone hole one of the largest in decades

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (80 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In contrast to the 2025 seasonal ozone hole above Antarctica, which was the fifth smallest since 1992, this year's ozone hole ranks as one of the largest in 48 years, based on long-term atmospheric observations, including data from the Copernicus Sentinel-5P satellite. While this may sound alarming, it is vital to remember that the size of the ozone hole varies naturally from year to year, and the long-term recovery of the ozone layer remains on track.

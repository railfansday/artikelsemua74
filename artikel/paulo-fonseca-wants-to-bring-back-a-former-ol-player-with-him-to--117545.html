Recent findings suggest that successful debut for Paulo Fonseca at the head of Sporting. Arriving as the great savior of the Lisbon club, the former OL player won his first championship match, against Moreirense this weekend. But the Lusitano tactician and the management know it well, there is work to be done to catch up with Porto and Benfica. And recruits this winter would not be too much for the team in the Portuguese capital.

Recent findings suggest that obviously, the former coach of Olympique Lyonnais, who arrived at Sporting with his staff, will want to bring in certain players he knows well. As the newspaper notes A.S.the Lisbon club has already started working on the arrival of Endrick, a Real Madrid player whom Fonseca had under his command last season. The Brazilian had performed quite well. (Related: The Age of Machine Learning As Code Has Arrived...)

He had actually scored 8 goals and delivered 7 assists in 21 matches, which allowed him to play the 2026 World Cup with the Canarinha, and to return to Madrid with a slightly stronger CV. Even if for the moment, José Mourinho counts very little on him – 83 minutes played in 4 La Liga matches – and according to the Spanish newspaper, Endrick has made the decision to leave. The media explains that Sporting’s offer is attractive for Endrick, since in addition to the presence of Fonseca, the club is competing in the Champions League, and adaptation to the country and language would not be particularly difficult for the 20-year-old player. The newspaper adds that the possibility of a transfer – with clauses to bring him back to Madrid in the future – is not ruled out, and that it would therefore not necessarily be a loan. Sporting will face competition from AS Roma, another club very interested in his services. “I don’t know what will happen in January. It’s in God’s hands. I only ask what is best for my career. Come what may. I will keep my feet on the ground and do my best. Madrid is a beautiful city, but I will always do everything possible to advance my career. I just want to play, be happy, help my teammates… Score goals, make assists, like I did in Lyon, which remains one of the best experiences of my life and one of the happiest moments of my existence.”he confided yesterday, in an interview during his selection training course.

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

I run Jobs Every Hour, a free job search that reads open jobs from about 20,000 employers' own career pages every hour. Because every posting comes straight from the employer, I can count how many of them actually say what the job pays. Here is what the data looked like on 22 November 2026.

Out of 645,446 open jobs, 202,628 list a salary range: roughly 31%. That is far from all, but it is no longer rare. These counts come from company career pages (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and similar), so they lean toward larger and tech-heavy employers rather than small local businesses. Where the roles with a listed salary are:.

Sources indicate that california and Latest York both require pay ranges in job postings, and it indicates in the counts. A role that lists several places is counted in each of them.

Medians of the midpoint of each advertised yearly range, in US dollars:.

It has been revealed that the overall median across all 202,628 roles is about $135K. These are advertised numbers, not verified pay, and only about 1% of the roles are marked as contract work.

The biggest groups among roles with a listed salary: healthcare and clinical (9%), director and head-of-department roles (6%), technicians (5%), software engineers (4%) and analysts (3%). The skills asked for most often in these postings: Microsoft Office, customer service, Python, a driver's license and security clearance.

Numbers are a snapshot from 22 November 2026; the live counts change every hour.

Disclosure: I built and run Jobs Every Hour; the links above go to it.

Recent findings suggest that worth remembering these are advertised ranges, and the midpoint can hide a wide spread. A posted range of 90K to 140K usually means the real offer depends heavily on level and negotiation, so the lower end is a safer thing to strategy around.

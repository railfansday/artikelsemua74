PayPal is naming a new CFO as part of a broader leadership reshuffle

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

PayPal $PYPL Holdings named Xero Chief Financial Officer Claire Bramley as its next CFO on Friday, part of a leadership reshuffle that adds new heads of its consumer and payments businesses.

Bramley will succeed Jamie Miller, PayPal's chief financial and operating officer. PayPal's press release says Bramley will join Feb. 1 and work with Miller through February. A Form 8-K filing with the SEC lists her start as Feb. 3, 2027, and says Miller's service ends Feb. 2, 2027. The filing says Miller's departure is not the result of a dispute with the tech giant over its financial statements.

It has been revealed that bramley has been Xero’s CFO since April 2025 and its interim chief people officer since June. She was CFO of Teradata from June 2021 to April 2025 and held finance roles at HP $HPQ from 2007 to 2021. (Related: Latest Update: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

Recent findings suggest that under an offer letter dated Nov. 16, Bramley will receive an annual base salary of $875,000, a target bonus of 125% of base salary and a $5 million cash signing bonus paid in two installments. Her equity grants include restricted stock units with a target value of $6 million, sign-on units valued at $14 million and performance-based units valued at $6 million. "With our three-year strategies in place, our focus is now on execution," Enrique Lores, PayPal's president and CEO, said in a statement.

The tech giant announced other changes:.

Kartik Mani will join from American Express $AXP as president and head of consumer financial services and Venmo. Suzan Kereere, who leads Global Markets, will become president and head of payment services and crypto, succeeding Jeff Pomeroy, who has been interim lead. Juan Rio will join Nov. 30 from Brightstar.AI as executive vice president of AI business transformation and operations.

It has been revealed that chief Risk Officer Aaron Webster will leave the firm. Ratinder Bedi, senior vice president of credit and fraud risk, will serve as interim head of risk while PayPal searches for a permanent successor.

It has been revealed that the board appointed Brian J. Lee, a former Goldman Sachs $GS chief risk officer, as a director effective Nov. 30. His seat comes from an increase in board size to 12 from 11.

According to recent reports, the changes arrive as PayPal works to convince investors it can pick up its pace of expansion on its own, after months of speculation that it could be acquired, according to The Wall Street Journal. That speculation includes a bid from Stripe and Advent International that was reportedly priced at $60.50 per share.

PayPal raised its full-year profit forecast in October, when Lores remarked the company's strategy was starting to show in its financial results.

PayPal said it will patch investors on its three-year strategic intends on its fourth-quarter 2026 earnings call.

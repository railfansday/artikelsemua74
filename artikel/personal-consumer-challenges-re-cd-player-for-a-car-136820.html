Over the course of the previous month I have had conversations with millenials and Gen-Zs about the recent resurgence of interest in vinyl records. My 36 y o daughter, my 28 yo stepdaughter and her bf, my friend's 27 y o son, and a millenial couple I ran into at Costco all own turntables and pretty extensive record collections. I asked them all what was the draw, given the fragility of records, the limitation of maybe 20-25 mins of music per side (of which much is often filler), the cost and hassle of replacement styluses, the inconvenience, etc..I expected them to say that they were drawn by better acoustic fidelity, or maybe by nostalgia, or even the aesthetics of the album covers.But they all mentioned the same thing, which is like your draw to CDs.Their major draw was "to have something they could hold, and that they actually owned."I was in a Barnes & Noble yesterday, picking up a Black Friday sale DVD, and was surprised at the number of vinyl records on display. They do make room for some nice cover art I have subscriptions to Qobuz and Presto Music, which is overkill but I like it that way. For road trips I download music to my iPad and then play through bluetooth to my car's sound system. The main requirement is for something to help me stay awake without requiring too much attention, so Broadway musicals do figure heavily...P.S. My 2015 car still has a CD player, and it's fun to press the button and watch the screen flip up to reveal the slot where they go.

Industry observers note that in addition to Ally mentioned above, I know Sofi and Capital One’s savings accounts also have the feature to segregate balances (buckets) for particular goals. Between Vanguard and Fidelity, I have a slight preference for the latter due to the ATM fee reimbursements, but if that’s not relevant to you, they are quite equivalent, particularly if you’re just going to let the money sit in the settlement account and withdraw it as needed. Personally, I think the bank accounts I mentioned above are a better option than multiple cash management accounts, because it allows you to keep your balance together, so you don’t have to worry about the marginal risk of pulling from the wrong account and overdrawing it.

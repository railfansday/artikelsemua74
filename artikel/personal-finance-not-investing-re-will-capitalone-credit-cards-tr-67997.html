I've just been pulled into this discussion with an email I just received.When I selected Capital One as an alternative credit card, it was partly because of the zero foreign transaction fees. I just rec'd an email from them with an "exciting update" as they describe it. They'll bump the cash back on my Quicksilver to an unlimited 3% at gas stations and grocery stores, plus still earn unlimited 1.5% cash back on all other purchases. The catch is I must switch to the Discover Network. I thought there was talk that only the low tier cards would be converted over. What happens if I forego the deal? Will I be forced to switch over later, without the increase in cash back? The Discover network not widely used overseas makes the Quicksilver less useful to me. Of course there's a time limit on this offer.Quicksilver IS one of Capital One's lower tier cards. I don't mean any disrespect--I have the Quicksilver myself, along with an old Savor card, which is also a lower tier card. The Venture line (particularly the X, but arguably the whole line) is the higher tier that's likely to stay on the current payment network.Statistics: Posted by FedGuy — Sun Nov 22, 2026 1:20 pm.

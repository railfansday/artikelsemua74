According to recent reports, the Christmas Spectacular Starring the Radio City Rockettes is famous for its merry dances and spirited kick line, but perhaps the simplest scene is the most memorable: A narrator describes the birth of Jesus while performers walk across the stage alongside sheep, a donkey, and a camel as a flying angel appears. Those brief moments where dynamic choreography ceases and the lavish special effects subside re-center the true story behind Christmas, serving as a good reminder during the holiday season that can so often be spent erroneously focusing on material extravagances or Santa mythology.

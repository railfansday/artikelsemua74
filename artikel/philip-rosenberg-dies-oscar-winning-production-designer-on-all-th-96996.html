In a recent development, philip Rosenberg, the production designer who won an Oscar for his work on All That Jazz and also worked on films such as Network, Moonstruck and The Hurricane, has died. That, according to his nephew, Alan Rosenberg. No cause of death was given. He was 91.

Rosenberg was raised in Brooklyn before studying scenic design at Yale University with Donald Oenslager. About that time, he was hired as an assistant at the Metropolitan Opera where he worked with Ita Maximovna and Eugene Berman. His first film was The Seagull in 1968, on which he was a design assistant.

Among Rosenberg’s first starry projects was Herbert Ross’ The Owl and the Pussycat in 1970. Rosenberg was the art director on the film starring Barbra Streisand and George Segal. He transitioned to production designer on James Toback’s The Gambler before making a splash in 1976’s new Hollywood landmark Network, becoming known for the realism which made that period’s films stand out.

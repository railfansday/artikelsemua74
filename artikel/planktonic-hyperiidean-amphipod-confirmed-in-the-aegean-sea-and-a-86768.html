Planktonic hyperiidean amphipod confirmed in the Aegean Sea, and a fresh study shows how to identify subadult males

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (89 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that amphipods change in size and shape each time they molt. When a species is told apart from its relatives by small anatomical details, this creates a practical problem: a trait that identifies an adult may be absent or different in the younger animal. A recently published study in Biodiversity Data Journal tests this problem on Phronima atlantica, a planktonic amphipod of the open ocean, and identifies which of its features stay reliable in males from the subadult to the adult stage.

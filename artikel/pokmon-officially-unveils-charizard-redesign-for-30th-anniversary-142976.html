Sources indicate that the thing that made Pokémon take off so much in the first place was the design of its creatures, which immediately captured the hearts of a generation of children and only grew from there. There's an old belief among the community that every Pokémon is somebody's favorite, but there's no doubt that some draw in significantly more fans than others.

Recent findings suggest that pikachu is probably the most enduring example, with much of that thanks to the character's prominent role in the anime, where Ash's companion is one of the great cartoon heroes. Outside of that, many of the most popular designs come from the first and second generations, of which Charizard is probably the most instantly recognizable of the bunch.

If you count yourself among Charizard's ever-growing legion of fans, there's a new release on the way that puts it front and center.

Sources indicate that over on the official Pokémon Center website, you should be able to find the product page for the Evolve to Charizard Ring by Enso. This very fancy piece of jewelry features Charmander evolving into Charmeleon and then into Charizard, etched into silicone, on a ring that's orange colored to match the iconic 'mons. In a recent development, "The fiery power of Charmander, Charmeleon, and Charizard are on full display with this etched silicone ring made by Enso! The lightweight design feels comfortable in any situation, and built-in safety technology helps bring peace of mind.".

Sources indicate that it's not the first time The Pokemon Manufacturer has done a jewellery release of this type, but this is probably the nicest example we have seen to date. It's also priced at a relatively reasonable $49.99, and it's available in sizes 6-13, so there should be one that fits most people well.

As mentioned earlier, the ring is silicone, so don't expect it to arrive in stainless steel or silver, and it will also be a lot lighter than if it was cast in either of those. Reviews for the ring on the official website are a little mixed right now, though the sample size is very small, with the one poor review only citing their misapprehension of the material the rings is made from.

Sources indicate that outside of that, Pokémon fans can also look forward to the upcoming release of the first games of the tenth generation, Winds and Waves. With a setting rooted in the archipelagos of South-East Asia, the map looks like a major departure from anything we have seen to date, and it could be just the thing that's needed to reinvigorate the series. The fresh designs are also going to be crucial, though, after a couple of generations that have failed to cement their status among the greats.

Pokémon is a Japanese multimedia franchise created by Satoshi Tajiri and Ken Sugimori, introduced by Nintendo, Game Freak, and Creatures in 1996. Centered on fictional creatures called Pokémon, the series spans video games, trading cards, animation, films, merchandise, and competitive events. Players assume the role of Trainers who capture, battle, and trade Pokémon. It is one of the highest-grossing entertainment franchises in history.

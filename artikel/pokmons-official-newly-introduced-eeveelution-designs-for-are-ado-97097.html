It has been revealed that the Pokémon franchise is full of iconic designs, with the old saying being that every one of them has a person out there that thinks they're the best of the bunch. That mentioned, there are some who prove overwhelmingly popular with the community-at-large, and it's representations of them that usually prove to be the most desirable releases overall.

According to recent reports, in the TCG, cards that bring to life 'mons from the original 151 like Gengar, Pikachu, and, of course, Charizard, always seem to command the highest prices. At the same time, those iconic figures are very popular in the video games as well, and picking up a shiny of any of the major names is an achievement worth shouting about. (Related: Latest Update: Course Launch Community Event...)

Sources indicate that in that vein, the Eeeveelutions are arguably the most beloved designs of the bunch. There is plenty of variance within that, as there's certainly a lot more fans of Espeon, Sylveon, and Umbreon than there are of Leafeon and Vaporeon, but they're all pretty universally adored by fans, and any time they crop up it's usually an event worth talking about.

As it happens, the latest Eeveelution release may just have been leaked, and it's perfect for those who are looking to add to their physical collections.

Over on the PokeBeach website, one user posted on the forums to share images of a new set of TCG figures that center on Eevee and its Eeveelutions. The photographs come from China, and showcase figures for all the different Eeveelutions we have seen to date (as well as Eeevee itself), namely, Vaporeon, Jolteon, Flareon, Leafeon, Espeon, Glaceon, Umbreon, and Sylveon. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

According to recent reports, sadly, there isn't any further info included with the images, so we have no idea about the price or the format they're being sold in, but it's certainly one to keep any eye on for fans. The photos also feature a matching card sitting behind the figures, though it isn't clear if those are included when you buy a figure.

In a recent development, at the same time, the topic of Eeveelutions has continued to crop up over the years, as we haven't seen a newly introduced addition to the roster since Sylveon all the way back in 2013 with the arrival of Pokémon X and Pokémon Y. That has led many to believe that now is the perfect time to introduce one, along with the rest of the tenth generation when Winds and Waves arrives at some point in 2027.

According to recent reports, the significant question if they do decide to bring in a new design at this juncture is what typing it will have, with limited options left on the table. The remaining types that don't have one are Bug, Dragon, Fighting, Flying, Ghost, Ground, Poison, Rock, and Steel, though they do obviously have the additional route of being able to combine any number of those to create something very different.

Whatever they do decide to do, it would be nice to see another Eeveelution in the new Pokémon games, and if I were a betting man, I'd say Flying or Dragon would be the ones that stand out as the most appropriate for what we know about the setting.

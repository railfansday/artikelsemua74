It's been a full week since GTA 6 released, if you can believe it. After years and years and years of waiting for it to finally arrive, I hope that you've been enjoying your life in Leonida.

I say that because it's quite obviously time to rate the game for yourself.

It has been revealed that to recap, GTA 6 remains the highest rated title ever released. It now has over 160 reviews on Metacritic and it still sits at a frankly staggering 99 score. It's extremely difficult to imagine that it'll ever be equalled — never mind beaten.

It has been revealed that but it hasn't all been smooth sailing for Rockstar's latest. The game's been review bombed for a variety of reasons by frustrated users — once again sparking conversations surrounding the legitimacy of user reviews in general. According to recent reports, counter to that, it's worth noting that GTA 6 is exceptionally highly rated on the PS Store, where people actually have to buy the game before giving it a score out of five. 389,000 ratings and it's at 4.78 — which equates to 92% of players giving it a full five stars. (Related: F1 star Max Verstappen played 40 hours of GTA 6 before ...)

You don't have to agree with all the praise, though — that's what these polls are for. Maybe you like GTA 6 but still think that it's overrated, or perhaps it's struggling to live up to your lofty expectations.

Our view is simple enough: Push Square editor Sammy dubbed it "the greatest open world game ever made" in his GTA 6 PS5 review, awarding the title a very rare 10/10.

"Rockstar has set a upcoming sandbox standard here, with a world that feels alive and populated with real people; Leonida is a place where it appears almost anything can happen at any time," he wrote — and I don't think he's wrong!

But would you agree? Is GTA 6 truly deserving of all those 10/10 review scores? It's time to have your say in our poll, before attempting to explain yourself in the comments section below.

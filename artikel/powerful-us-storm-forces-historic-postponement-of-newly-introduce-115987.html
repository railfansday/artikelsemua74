Powerful US storm forces historic postponement of Newly introduced York's Thanksgiving Day Parade

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (71 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that A powerful storm forecast to bring fierce winds and heavy rain to the northeastern United States has forced the unprecedented postponement of the city's 100th annual Thanksgiving Day Parade, with the event rescheduled for Saturday.

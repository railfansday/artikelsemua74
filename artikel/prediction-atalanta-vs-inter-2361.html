Competition: Serie A Date: Sunday, November 22, 2026 Kickoff: 19:45 GMT / 20:45 CET Venue: New Balance Arena, Bergamo.

Verdict: Inter to win Best odds: 13/20 Bookmaker: 1xBet.

According to recent reports, inter returns from the international break with a trip to Bergamo and an opportunity to maintain the pressure on Serie A leader Lazio. Atalanta also signed off with a victory, although the home side has considerably more work to do before it can claim to have found a reliable rhythm.

With Inter’s attacking options improving and Atalanta still dealing with absences in defense, the visitor looks better placed to take all three points.

It has been revealed that atalanta’s 1-0 victory at Monza was welcome, but it owed plenty to Marco Carnesecchi. The goalkeeper made several key saves before Nikola Krstovic put Maurizio Sarri’s side ahead early in the second half, then intervened again late on to protect the advantage. Recent findings suggest that it was Atalanta’s first away league win of the season and a useful response to the 3-0 home defeat against Parma. There were better attacking moments after the interval at Monza, too, with Krstovic and Mario Pasalic having opportunities to extend the lead. The next assignment demands more control. Inter has the quality to punish repeated openings, so Sarri will need his midfield to protect the defense while still getting support around Krstovic. Spending long periods pinned back would leave Atalanta asking a great deal of its goalkeeper again.

According to recent reports, isak Hien and Odilon Kossounou remain unavailable, while Thomas Kristensen is a doubt. Gianluca Gaetano and goalkeeper Thomas Pompei are also sidelined. Those defensive absences reduce Sarri’s options against one of the division’s strongest attacks.

Inter responded to its 3-1 derby defeat against Milan with a convincing 2-0 victory over Como. Francesco Pio Esposito and Marcus Thuram scored inside the opening 19 minutes, giving Cristian Chivu’s side an advantage it protected comfortably.

Sources indicate that the result left Inter on 28 points from 11 matches, one behind Lazio. Nine league victories and 28 goals underline the standard Atalanta must meet, while four wins in the last five Serie A outings suggest the derby setback has done little to interrupt the broader momentum.

Inter’s ability to create chances through different players is particularly relevant here. Esposito provides a penalty-area target, Thuram can stretch the back line, and the width supplied by the wing-backs should force Atalanta’s depleted defense to cover considerable ground.

Lautaro Martinez and Thuram have returned to group training. Lautaro’s minutes are likely to be managed following his longer absence, while Thuram is further along in his recovery from muscular fatigue. Ange-Yoan Bonny and Aleksandar Stankovic remain unavailable.

Sources indicate that atalanta should take encouragement from the win at Monza, but the chances conceded in that match remain a concern against Inter’s attack. Chivu has greater depth available up front, and his side has shown enough consistency to justify favoritism.

An Inter victory is the selection, with a 2-1 away win the predicted score.

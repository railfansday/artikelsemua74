Barcelona will look to make it six consecutive victories in all competitions when they travel to Estadio Riazor on Sunday to face newly promoted Deportivo La Coruna in La Liga.

Recent findings suggest that the Catalan giants sit comfortably at the top of the table and arrive in excellent form, while Deportivo continue their encouraging first season back in the top flight. Barcelona have won four of their last five meetings with Deportivo, including their famous 8-0 victory in the corresponding fixture during the 2015-16 campaign. Deportivo have made a respectable return to La Liga after securing promotion by finishing second in last season’s Segunda Division.

Antonio Hidalgo’s side have collected three wins, six draws and four defeats from their opening 13 league matches, leaving them 12th in the standings with 15 points. While they suffered a disappointing 3-1 defeat away to Alaves last time out, their overall performances have been encouraging.

The Blue and Whites have picked up four points from their last three league matches and recorded an impressive 2-1 home victory over Osasuna in their most recent game at Riazor on November 2. (Related: Mechs vs Kaiju’s Ragdoll codes (November 2026)...)

Home form has been solid, with nine points collected from six league fixtures. Deportivo will also take confidence from the fact that their most recent victory over Barcelona came on home soil, a 2-1 success in March 2017.

Historically, however, the head-to-head record heavily favours the visitors. The clubs have met 105 times, with Barcelona winning 63 matches compared to Deportivo’s 22, while 20 encounters have ended in draws.

Deportivo have relatively few injury concerns ahead of the fixture.

Bright Ede remains a major doubt because of a hamstring glitch, while Zakaria Eddahchouri is struggling with a wrist problem and is also unlikely to be fully fit.

It has been revealed that up front, Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang is expected to continue as the central striker. He has scored five La Liga goals this season, although he has not found the net in three matches since returning from a knee injury earlier this month.

Mariano Soriano and Luismi Cruz have both contributed two league goals this term and are expected to retain their places in the starting lineup.

Barcelona head to Galicia in outstanding form and with momentum continuing to build under Hansi Flick.

The Blaugrana arrive on the back of a 4-1 victory over Sabah in the Champions League, a result that left them fourth in the competition’s overall standings with 12 points. They are also in a commanding position domestically, sitting top of La Liga with 36 points from 13 matches.

In a recent development, barcelona have won 12 of their first 13 league fixtures, suffering only one defeat, and right now hold a three-point advantage over Real Madrid and a five-point lead over Atletico Madrid. Their latest league outing produced a 3-2 victory over Villarreal.

The Catalan club have been the most prolific attacking side in Spain this season, scoring an extraordinary 51 goals in just 13 league fixtures. They now face a key sequence of matches against Deportivo, Atletico Madrid and Celta Vigo before resuming their Champions League campaign against Manchester City on December 8.

Barcelona are expected to receive a significant boost with the return of Lamine Yamal, who missed the Champions League victory over Sabah due to a minor ankle problem but is set to return to the starting lineup.

Recent findings suggest that there will be no Fermin Lopez, however, after his red card against Villarreal. Roony Bardghji and Xavi Espart also remain unavailable because of knee and ankle injuries respectively.

It has been revealed that there is more positive news elsewhere, with both Rodri and Andreas Christensen potentially available for selection. The ankle injury suffered by Alejandro Balde against Sabah is not considered serious, and he could also return to the squad.

Following the European fixture, Flick could restore several key players to the starting lineup, including Eric Garcia, Pau Cubarsi, Joao Cancelo, Dani Olmo and Raphinha.

Deportivo have adapted well to life back in La Liga and have shown they can be competitive at Riazor, but this represents one of their toughest tests of the season.

In a recent development, barcelona arrive in exceptional form, boasting the league’s most potent attack and a squad that continues to grow in confidence under Flick. Deportivo may be capable of finding the net, particularly with Aubameyang leading the line, but the visitors possess too much quality across the pitch and should have enough to maintain their advantage at the top of the table.

Bayern Munich travel to the Volksparkstadion on Saturday aiming to put a difficult week behind them and reignite their Bundesliga title challenge against Hamburger SV.

The defending champions begin the weekend second in the table on 22 points, two behind leaders Borussia Dortmund, while Hamburg occupy 12th place with 12 points. Verdict: Total away goals over 2.5.

Hamburg have generally enjoyed a positive campaign despite recent setbacks.

The Green-Whites have collected three more points after 10 Bundesliga matches than they managed at the same stage last season, evidence that progress is being made under Daniel Thioune. (Related: Woman forced to jump in stranger’s car after being chas...)

It has been revealed that however, results have taken a turn for the worse in recent weeks. Hamburg were heavily beaten 4-0 by Werder Bremen last weekend, and another defeat on Saturday could leave them just two points above the relegation zone.

According to recent reports, thioune’s side have now lost four of their last five matches in all competitions, scoring only five times while conceding 12 goals during that run. Those defensive struggles will be a major concern ahead of the visit of one of the division’s most dangerous attacking teams.

Sources indicate that history also offers little encouragement. Hamburg have not beaten Bayern since July 2011, a sequence that has included 17 defeats and three draws.

Despite that record, home form has been respectable. HSV have won three of their five Bundesliga matches at the Volksparkstadion this season, although they have also suffered two defeats there.

Sources indicate that hamburg have been dealt a significant blow with midfielder Nicolai Remberg ruled out for the remainder of the season. His absence could see Nicolas Capaldo continue in central midfield.

In attack, Yussuf Poulsen is unavailable, meaning Patson Daka is expected to lead the line. He could be supported by Fabio Vieira, who is likely to feature in an advanced role.

Bayern arrive under unusual pressure after suffering their first defeat of the season.

It has been revealed that the Bavarians were beaten 2-1 by Lille in the Champions League on Wednesday, a result that followed a disappointing 1-1 Bundesliga draw against FC Koln. Those results have raised concerns that Bayern could lose further ground in the title race if they fail to respond swiftly.

The defeat in France was also Vincent Kompany’s second match back on the touchline following his collapse in October, and the Belgian coach is still attempting to guide his side through a difficult spell.

Industry observers note that despite their recent frustrations, Bayern remain one of the Bundesliga’s strongest sides statistically. They have scored 30 goals in just 10 league matches, while only Borussia Dortmund have conceded fewer than their tally of 10.

According to recent reports, the champions’ loss to Lille was their first defeat in any competition this season and also their first away defeat. Before that setback, Bayern had won six and drawn three of their first nine away matches.

Off-field concerns have also generated headlines, with criticism directed at the club hierarchy over their handling of Jamal Musiala’s health situation, prompting Joshua Kimmich to publicly support his teammate.

Star striker Harry Kane is expected to miss several months with an ankle injury, while Michael Olise is a doubt for the trip to Hamburg.

There is better news regarding Luis Diaz, who should be available in some capacity, although Bayern may still be forced to deploy natural midfielder Konrad Laimer as their central attacking option.

Aleksandar Pavlovic was used in a more advanced role against Lille because of Bayern’s attacking injury crisis, but he is expected to return to midfield alongside Joshua Kimmich this weekend.

In a recent development, hamburg may feel they are catching Bayern at a favourable moment given the visitors’ injury list and recent drop in form. The absence of Harry Kane in particular removes a huge source of goals, while Kompany’s side are still recovering from their first defeat of the campaign.

Even so, Bayern possess significantly greater quality throughout the squad and remain one of the Bundesliga’s most potent attacking outfits. Hamburg’s defensive record in recent weeks is another concern, particularly against opposition capable of creating chances in volume.

The hosts should put up a fight, but Bayern are expected to respond positively to a difficult week and leave Hamburg with all three points.

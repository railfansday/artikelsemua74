Industry observers note that TAIPEI, Nov. 26, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Primax Electronics (TWSE: 4915) has been named one of Taiwan's Top 10 Sustainable Enterprises in the Manufacturing category and received the Gold Award for Sustainability Reporting at the 2026 Taiwan Corporate Sustainability Awards (TCSA), organized by the Taiwan Institute for Sustainable Energy (TAISE). Primax was also included in the S&P Global Sustainability Yearbook Membership 2026 for the third consecutive year. Yearbook membership recognizes companies that rank within the top 15% of their respective industries and meet additional Corporate Sustainability Assessment (CSA) score requirements. Climate Action Integrated into Business Decisions.

According to recent reports, in 2025, renewable energy accounted for 63.69% of Group electricity consumption, while Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions decreased by 44.25% compared with the 2023 baseline supporting progress toward net-zero emissions by 2050. Since 2026, internal carbon pricing across Group operations has incorporated carbon costs into business decisions, and a dedicated carbon fund finances decarbonization projects and green product innovation, further embedding climate considerations into operational planning. Product Design Supporting Customers' Low-Carbon Goals.

According to recent reports, in 2025, Primax completed life cycle assessments (LCAs) for 18 core product categories, providing a basis for evaluating product environmental impacts and identifying opportunities for improvement. The company is also increasing the use of recycled plastics and advancing more sustainable packaging solutions to support product carbon footprint measurement and reduction.

With expertise in audio, vision and interaction, Primax serves three key application markets: Personal & Workspace, Safety & Security, and Mobility & Robotics. The manufacturer is also developing low-power sensor modules for Edge AI applications. Together, these capabilities support customers in addressing evolving product sustainability and supply chain requirements, including the growing need for product-level environmental data.

Strengthening Disclosure and Nature Conservation.

Sources indicate that primax references the IFRS S1 and S2 standards in its sustainability-related financial disclosures. In 2026, the tech giant published its first integrated Climate and Nature Report, further incorporating climate- and nature-related risks and opportunities into its management approach and disclosure practices.

Beyond its business operations, Primax works with the Taitung District Agricultural Research and Extension Station on ecological restoration at Butterfly Valley in Yanping Township, Taitung, contributing to local biodiversity conservation.

Sources indicate that "Sustainability is not only a corporate responsibility, but also a shared challenge that requires long-term commitment and action. By incorporating carbon costs into business decisions through internal carbon pricing, and embedding green design and carbon management into product development, we aim to help customers address their low-carbon transition needs with practical technologies and measurable results. This recognition affirms our ongoing commitment and encourages us to continue turning sustainability goals into concrete actions," mentioned Jack Pan, Chairman of Primax Electronics.

Industry observers note that looking ahead, Primax will continue to advance net-zero emissions, green product innovation and biodiversity conservation. By combining its core technologies with sustainability management capabilities, the manufacturer will work with customers and value chain partners to build a more resilient, low-carbon supply chain and advance its long-term sustainability goals.

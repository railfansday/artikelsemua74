Prime Video has one of the best fantasy series in recent memory, easily making it into the S-Tier, aka the very best, of the genre, and it comes from Dungeons & Dragons roots. Prime Video's catalog of TV indicates has found success with different genres, such as action thrillers like Reacher, sci-fi masterpieces like The Expanse, and many other hit shows.

When it comes to Prime Video's fantasy TV shows, the streaming service has delivered exciting series in both animation and live-action. For the latter, the biggest show at Prime Video is The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power, which was recently renewed for season 4. That fantasy series was based on J. R. R. Tolkien's existing franchise, one of the genre's most popular. (Related: Transformer-based Encoder-Decoder Models...)

According to recent reports, that said, in recent years, many successful movies, TV shows, video games, and more have come from Dungeons & Dragons roots. This includes direct projects like the Chris Pine movie Dungeons & Dragons: Honor Among Thieves, but also the sci-fi Netflix global hit Stranger Things, and the all-timer video game Baldur's Gate 3. Prime Video has also gotten in on the fun with two high fantasy series. (Related: Breaking: Showcase Your Projects in Spaces using Gradio...)

It has been revealed that those are the Critical Role projects The Legend of Vox Machina and The Mighty Nein, both of which were adapted from actual D&D campaigns played by the incredible voice cast at Critical Role, set in the custom realm of Exandria. The Legend of Vox Machina is particularly notable, as it has delivered top-tier fantasy storytelling throughout multiple seasons.

In a recent development, the Legend of Vox Machina will end on its own terms, with the high fantasy series coming to an end with its upcoming fifth season, and all four seasons and 48 episodes released thus far have been impeccable. On Rotten Tomatoes, the Prime Video fantasy series stands at a rare perfect 100% critics' score for all of its four seasons released thus far, proving just how consistent the show is on a high level. Additionally, viewers also share that opinion, as the hit show boasts a near-perfect 93% overall audience score. These positive reviews can be explained by a few key factors.

In a recent development, first is the fact that the project has gorgeous and vibrant animation, something that is a must for a high fantasy series that wants to immerse viewers in a wondrous new world. Given how another hit TV-MA Prime Video animated series, the superhero show Invincible, is frequently criticized for its cheap-looking animation, the fact that The Legend of Vox Machina has managed to be as consistent as it has with the way it looks is impressive. The fluid animation has also led to some of the most kinetic, hard-hitting, and surprising fights the fantasy genre has ever seen.

Those are enhanced by different classes of characters, including a cleric, a bard, a druid, a gunslinger, and more. The fact that The Legend of Vox Machina brings all these characters of completely different backgrounds and skill sets together is not only perfect for creating elaborate, visually appealing, and impactful battles, but also in making sure that the show feels fresh, with a plurality of voices that both creates tension and unique moments of connection between completely different people. Additionally, the adult animated series has a healthy dose of humor, but it is never used out of place.

As major movies and TV reveals, such as Marvel's Thor: Love and Thunder, have shown, striking a seamless balance between serious and comedic is not that easy a task, yet The Legend of Vox Machina always seems to know when to go more somber and when to let Scanlan run his mouth. Finally, this Dungeons & Dragons series comes with extensive source material, as Critical Role's first campaign spanned a mighty 115 episodes, so the show has many nuanced, exciting, and popular stories to pick from, adapting major arcs with dragons, vampires, and more. The Legend of Vox Machina belongs in the S-Tier of fantasy series.

In a recent development, prince William and Kate Middleton are facing a major security breach: the couple’s Sandringham estate, Anmer Hall, was broken into on Monday night. And while they do typically spend the holiday season there, the family fortunately was not home at the time.

According to recent reports, according to The Sun—which broke the news—Prince William, Kate Middleton, and their kids (Prince George, Princess Charlotte, and Prince Louis) were at their main residence, Forest Lodge in Windsor, at the time of the break-in. There’s no reporting on who broke into the estate or if anything was taken, but this news comes about two years after two people broke into a home on the Windsor estate in 2024—when William and Kate were at home in nearby Adelaide Cottage.

Florenzi spent the 2020-21 season on loan at PSG, who lost in the Champions League final to Bayern Munich in the previous campaign. (Related: GTA 6 boss explains why classic mini-games from previou...)

Tuchel won 74.8% of his games in charge of PSG (G127 W95 D13 L19), his best win percentage at any club, and led them to back-to-back titles in 2018-19 and 2019-20, but things unravelled the following season.

PSG sacked Tuchel in December 2020 after they were falling behind in the Ligue 1 title race to Lille, who would go on to win the league by one point.

It was only the second season since 2012-13 in which PSG failed to win the league title, after Monaco won the French top-flight in 2016-17.

However, Tuchel was hired by Chelsea a month after being sacked by PSG and went on to win the Champions League with the Blues in that same season.

Florenzi described Tuchel's Champions League win as "karma", but the Italian was not surprised to see PSG eventually win the competition.

"At the time, PSG hadn't won the Champions League yet, but you could see the president wanted to build something major," Florenzi told L'Equipe.

Publishing Briefs: Believe & GL Music Form JV, Gram Parsons to Be Administered by Peermusic

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Believe Music Publishing and GL Music Entertainment have formed a joint venture, which sees Believe Music Publishing administering GL Music Entertainment’s catalog.

GL Music Entertainment was founded by Kim Nowak-Zorde, Daniel Fält and Lasse Lindorff, who also all work as songwriters and producers. Lindorff serves as the company’s CEO, Nowak-Zorde as head of entertainment and Christan Fløe Svenningsen is publishing director. Artists on the company’s roster has written and produced songs for Kylie Minogue, BTS, Julie Bergan and more. GL Music largely works with K-pop, J-pop and C-pop. Industry observers note that pete McCamley, who serves as vp of publishing at Believe, said, “Having worked with GL Music Entertainment from a distance for many years, I am delighted to work much closer with Lasse & Christian through our new venture. They have built a great creative business that operates on songwriter-first principles, with an incredibly strong track record across territories and genres. I’m excited to see the results of this new collaboration and be part of this next chapter.”.

Chris Meehan, CEO of publishing at Believe, added, “Lasse, Kim and Christian always put songwriters first, and you can feel that in everything GL Music Entertainment does. Their writers have made their mark everywhere from the Nordics to K-pop, and we’re excited to give that talent even more reach through our global network. I’m really looking forward to seeing what we can achieve together.” — Ariel King.

Industry observers note that peermusic will begin administering the catalog of Gram Parsons, the tech giant confirmed. The country rocker passed away in 1973 at age 26, but his musical works continued to inspire artists including Emmylou Harris, Elvis Costello and Margo Price.

Industry observers note that “My father’s music has always had a life of its own, reaching across generations and continuing to inspire artists and listeners in ways I don’t think even he could have imagined,” Polly Parsons stated in a statement. “It means so much to me to help preserve that legacy while also finding ways for new people to discover his songs. His music was never bound by genre or convention, and I think that’s why it still resonates so deeply. My hope is that this new partnership will not only honor the incredible body of work he left behind but also introduce his music to a whole new generation of fans. Keeping his spirit and his songs alive is something I hold very close to my heart.”.

According to recent reports, jamie Cerreta, president of U.S. and Canada at Peermusic, stated, “Gram’s music is an enduring testament to how great songs remain in our hearts. All of us at Peermusic are so honored to represent his songs and to see his work celebrated through his recent induction into the Rock ‘n’ Roll Hall of Fame. I am personally so happy to be working with Polly and Vector Management to help another generation discover the greateness of Gram Parsons.” —  Ariel King.

Sources indicate that electric Feel Publishing EU has signed James Bell, better known as Yami. The songwriter and vocalist began working in the industry alongside Brian Eno and has worked with artists including RAYE and Roots Manuva, and he also co-wrote Tiësto’s hit song, “The Business.”.

Sources indicate that “Everyone I knew on EF were always the coolest and most talented people, now I get to be part of that and that’s a real privilege,” Bell said in a statement. “I know that with their dedicated team and my ability to span across genres, we will do great things.”.

Austin Rosen, Electric Feel Entertainment’s CEO and founder, remarked in a statement, “What stands out to me about Yami is his ability to create music that connects across so many different artists and sounds. From the work he’s already done to the projects he has coming up, he has a real instinct for finding something special in every collaboration. We’re excited to be working with him and to help bring his vision to life as he continues to grow.”.

According to recent reports, sureeta Nayyar, head of A&R, U.K., at Electric Feel added, “We’re incredibly excited to welcome Yami to Electric Feel. His ability to work across a diverse range of artists and genres speaks to his versatility as a songwriter and his instinct for creating records that resonate. With an exciting slate of upcoming releases and development projects in the works, there’s so much potential ahead. We’re looking forward to supporting his growth, championing his creativity and helping bring his vision to life.” — Ariel King.

Recent findings suggest that peermusic has signed a worldwide publishing deal with Latin Grammy nominated artist Isadora. The Miami-based artist of Puerto Rican, Venezuelan, and Italian descent laces Caribbean rhythms with pop and tropical music, resulting in songs such as “La Bici” and “Agüita con Sal.”.

“She is not only an incredible performer with a powerful voice and a distinct artistic identity, but also an exceptionally talented and versatile songwriter,” Elizabeth Rodda, President, US Latin, Latin America and Global Society Relations, said in a statement. “Her ability to move effortlessly between performing and creating in the studio, while maintaining her unique voice and charisma, is what makes her so special. We are thrilled to welcome her to peermusic, where she joins an extraordinary roster of women songwriters who continue to push creative boundaries and shape the future of music.”.

It has been revealed that in 2025, the rising singer-songwriter—who’s a University of Miami’s Frost School of Music alum—was nominated for best new artist at the Latin Grammys, and she dropped her debut studio album La Isla, a 13-song set that celebrates her Island heritage. “Songwriting has always been one of the most key parts of my journey as an artist and one of my greatest passions, so finding a home that truly believes in my voice, my message, and my vision means so much to me,” she shared. “Joining the peermusic family is especially meaningful because of the history we share and the support they’ve shown me since the very beginning. It’s a full-circle moment.” — Jessica Roiz.

Recent findings suggest that position Music has signed Masego to a worldwide publishing deal. The Jamaican-American artist-songwriter-musician released Fix Your Face, his third studio album, in July. His 2017 breakthrough single “Tadow” featuring FKJ has since been certified platinum. Masego (born Micah Davis) earned his first Grammy nomination for the 2021 deluxe EP Studying Abroad: Extended Stay.

Industry observers note that “Masego is a generational creative talent and a true artist in every sense of the word,” commented Position Music vp of A&R Delmar Powell. “His ability to seamlessly blend jazz, R&B, hip-hop and global influences has created a sound that’s uniquely his own, while continuing to push the boundaries of Black music. Beyond his incredible gifts as a songwriter, producer and musician, I genuinely believe there are few artists who can match what he brings to the stage. His live performances are second to none. Masego represents the kind of artistry that transcends trends and leaves a lasting cultural impact.” — Gail Mitchell.

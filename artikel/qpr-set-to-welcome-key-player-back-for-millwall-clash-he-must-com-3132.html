QPR set to welcome key player back for Millwall clash – he must come straight into the starting XI: Opinion

In a recent development, QPR return to action after the international break with a short trip to Millwall on Saturday. The Hoops have had a fine season to date with only three losses in their 15 Championship games, leaving them 8th in the table and just inside the play-off spots.

Julien Stephan has had to get by without some key players too. Among those sidelined in the early stages of the campaign has been Nicolas Madsen, who won the club’s Player of the Season award last term and impressed early on in 2026/27.

Industry observers note that however, ahead of the Millwall tie, Stephan has confirmed Madsen is fit. He was tentative when asked whether the Dane would be fit to start, but if he’s good to go, he should come straight into the QPR midfield. (Related: Industry News: Fit More and Train Faster With ZeRO via ...)

Madsen’s QPR redemption has been a joy to watch. He had a tough first season after arriving in the summer of 2024 but grew into a crucial role over the 2025/26 campaign, providing a physical presence in the middle with box-to-box threat and good passing. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

He chipped in with three goals and six assists last season and had managed two goals and an assist in his last two matches before being cast to the sidelines in October.

It has been revealed that jonathan Varane has stepped in to partner Glen Kamara in the middle in Madsen’s absence. The Frenchman has done a decent job and his own physicality makes him a good ball-winner, but he lacks the Danish ace’s attacking quality and creativity.

Madsen is someone who can truly contribute in all areas of his midfield game. Alongside his nine goal contributions last term, he completed 1,165 passes with 56 chances created and won 63.4% of his duels with 167 defensive contributions. 155 recoveries highlight the ground he covers and his work rate across 90 minutes as well (stats via FotMob).

Industry observers note that with Madsen returning, Stephan can return to his favoured midfield partnership of the 26-year-old and summer loan signing Kamara. The Finn has been among QPR’s best players this season and will deservedly keep his spot despite Madsen’s return.

Kamara is an intelligent player with strong technical ability, while Madsen’s power sees him play the role of a duel winner alongside his progressive abilities on the ball. Ronnie Edwards helps on this front when stepping into the midfield, and the attacking midfielder is usually entrusted with more ball-carrying.

Recent findings suggest that the attacking midfield berth is up for grabs with both Ilias Chair and Karamoko Dembele fit to compete for the place once more. However, behind them, QPR will be happy to return to their strongest partnership of Madsen and Kamara.

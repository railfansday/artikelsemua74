Adrien Rabiot has joined Ricardo Kaká in Milan’s history books, and the Rossoneri are now working to tie the Frenchman down to a fresh long-term contract.

According to Pianeta Milan, Rabiot became the first Milan midfielder since 2004-05 to be directly involved in at least one goal in seven consecutive Serie A appearances. The last Rossoneri player to manage a similar run was Kaká.

Recent findings suggest that rabiot extended that streak with two assists in Sunday’s 5-1 win over Frosinone, setting up Gonçalo Ramos and Ardon Jashari. He also delivered the ball that led to Gabriele Calvani’s own goal.

The 31-year-old now has five goals and six assists this season. That makes him the first Serie A midfielder in 20 years to reach 10 or more goal involvements in his first 10 league games of a campaign. Sources indicate that the club hope to have a fresh contract signed before Christmas, although the paperwork could slip into the new year. The extension is expected to run until June 2030, with an option for a further season. Rabiot’s salary would rise to €5m net plus bonuses, putting him level with Mike Maignan and Gonçalo Ramos as one of the squad’s top earners.

Ruben Amorim made his own feelings clear after Sunday’s win, calling Rabiot “one of the best midfielders in Europe” and saying he hopes the renewal comes soon.

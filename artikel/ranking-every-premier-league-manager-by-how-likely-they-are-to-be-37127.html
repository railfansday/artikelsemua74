Pochettino is perhaps the biggest name to take the reins at Old Trafford since Jose Mourinho in 2016 and the Argentine is given a massive mountain to climb. As things stand, United are 19th heading into December and his first job is to ensure they escape the relegation zone.

Industry observers note that given the situation, the onus is less on Pochettino to deliver immediate trophy success and more on turning the tide. He'll likely be judged in the coming months, but a two-and-a-half-year contract doesn't exactly fill fans with confidence.

In many cases, Anfield's helmsman is revered, cherished, and held in higher regard than the players. It's a rare club tradition, but one that stretches as far back as the pre-Shankly years, with Jurgen Klopp's tenure the perfect encapsulation of such a notion.

It would seem Anfield's residents would love a personality and playing style of Klopp's stature, and the Spaniard might fulfil that desire well, having lost just twice in the league so far.

According to recent reports, marco Rose is a good, tactically astute manager, particularly known for his high-pressing, modern, and attacking style of football - something Bournemouth's squad is already geared towards. He has big boots to fill in Andoni Iraola's void, but a board with very little expectation - and the fact he has a good track record of managing sides in Europe - means he should get more than enough time to show the rest of the league what he's all about. A manager with a point to prove, Thomas Frank returned to Premier League management with Crystal Palace after replacing Pierre Sage in late November. After a nightmare spell at Tottenham, the Dane will be hoping to get his reputation back on track at Selhurst Park.

It has been revealed that the former Brentford boss got off to the worst possible start, losing 3-2 away to fellow relegation battlers Coventry City. It's still very early to assess Frank and whether he'll be a success in South London, but he's taken over a side that looks a polar opposite to the UEFA Conference League-winning team of 2025-26.

Brentford fans were dreading the day Thomas Frank decided to move on, and that came last summer as he decided to replace Ange Postecoglou at Tottenham Hotspur.

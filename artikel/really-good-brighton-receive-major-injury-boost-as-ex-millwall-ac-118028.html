Femi Azeez is edging closer to his long-awaited Brighton debut after being spotted training with the Seagulls on Tuesday, ahead of Thursday’s Uefa Conference League meeting with Craiova at the Amex Stadium.

The 25-year-old winger is yet to appear for Albion since joining from Millwall last September on a four-year contract for around £15m.

Brighton could also welcome back defender Pascal Struijk. The £18m summer arrival from Leeds has not featured since sustaining a thigh injury in the 2-0 win at Sunderland.

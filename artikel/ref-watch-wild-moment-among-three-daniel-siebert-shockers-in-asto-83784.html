Galatasaray went close through Aleksey Batrakov and Yunus Akgun in the early stages of the second half before Gul netted in the 77th minute.

German official Daniel Siebert was the man in the middle of a heated Champions League affair in Istanbul.

Football Insider have picked out three calls that Siebert should look back on and regret from the RAMS Park clash.

UEFA follow the laws of the game set by IFAB in its competitions, whose rulebook clearly states that players must be sanctioned if they excessively gesture towards the referee for an opponent to be shown a card.

That is exactly what Galatasaray forward Yilmaz did in the 12th minute of the game when he was fouled by Villa defender Victor Lindelof.

The Turkish international was captured on camera pleading to Siebert for Lindelof to be booked by making the gesture, yet the referee went against the laws and did not show Yilmaz the yellow.

The tense nature of the game nearly blew up in Siebert's face as players from both sides clashed in the 53rd minute following a challenge between John McGinn and Batrakov.

That was preceded by a wild lunge from ex-Tottenham defender Sanchez on young Villa winger Alysson, as his foot clipped the top of the ball but also caught the Brazilian while he tried to advance in the final third.

Siebert strangely let play continue and did not even book the Colombian centre-back, which was a clear mistake in itself and looked even worse when another clash seconds later caused a scuffle between the two teams.

Torreira certainly had a reputation for rash tackling and a hot-headed nature among English football fans during his previous spell at Arsenal.

It was clear to see that nothing has changed in that regard, based on Tuesday's clash, as the Uruguayan made several poor challenges and displayed some clear gamesmanship.

He committed four fouls on the night, one of which was a clear booking as he tackled Joao Gomes late and from behind in the 33rd minute but escaped without a sanction.

Torreira committed his second bookable offence late on as he clearly moved his arm towards the ball when Ibrahim Mbaye aimed to put Villa on the attack with a chipped pass.

Siebert gave a free-kick for that incident but somehow did not book the Gala enforcer, who can count himself lucky that he was able to be substituted off in added-time.

In a recent development, this week saw two of them finally link up with their teammates: Conor Bradley and Giovanni Leoni. The pair of them have been out long-term, Leoni for over a year now, but they're back training.

Now, we can't be sure what that means for a return to playing. It may well be another month or so before Liverpool are confident with competitive football for the pair.

But The Times reports that their return to training has been enough to lift the players. Bradley and Leoni unquestionably improve this squad, quite significantly, in fact, and their returns just make life easier for everyone.

Sources indicate that bradley, in particular, should have a massive effect. Liverpool have been using Ronald Araujo at full-back but Bradley is undoubtedly the no.1 there when fully fit. It sounds as though he still holds onto that status, too.

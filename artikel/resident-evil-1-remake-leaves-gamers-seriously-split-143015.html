📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that the future slate of Resident Evil games will only continue to become more fascinating with each new release and announcement. For instance, Resident Evil 10 will be an enormous milestone for the franchise and could possibly feature the return of S.T.A.R.S. Alpha Team’s Jill Valentine, and which mainline entry is chosen to be remade after Resident Evil Veronica may be quite telling.

According to recent reports, there are Resident Evil remake rumors floating about with regard to virtually every game that hasn’t yet been remade, to be fair, but one could be more intriguing than the rest. Indeed, anyone who is a massive fan of Resident Evil 5 may not want to hold their breath. It could be a long but satisfying road to Capcom finally succumbing and exploring co-op gameplay again, and it may take a highly anticipated detour to a duology that fans are itching to revisit.

According to Dusk Golem, as per Twitter/X, “the possibility of doing a Rev 1 Remake has been tossed around.” Again, there are few Resident Evil games that haven’t had their names thrown around with the rumor or potential for them to one day receive a remake of their own, and it was only a matter of time until a sliver of credence was leant toward the belief that Resident Evil Revelations would be treated to one. Plus, if Resident Evil Revelations gets a remake, it’s almost guaranteed that its sequel will, too.

Fans seem to be generally excited about Resident Evil Revelations possibly receiving a remake, though user AKA_Sparda is skeptical about how bloated Capcom’s release schedule might be thereafter. Moreover, user Vagabond85 argues that they’d be pleased to see a Resident Evil Revelations remake make some tonal changes, writing, “The hypersexualization of characters like Jessica really kill the tone of an otherwise pretty strong entry.” However, remakes of Resident Evil Revelations and Resident Evil Revelations 2 would be thrilling due to where they land canonically and chronologically in the series.

Before Resident Evil Veronica was officially confirmed as a remake of Resident Evil Code: Veronica, it seemed unlikely that we’d ever get one. Resident Evil Code: Veronica is set between Resident Evil 3: Nemesis and Resident Evil 4, for instance, and thus, Resident Evil Veronica is launching out of order. This essentially means that any random Resident Evil game could get a remake and simply be sewn into the fabric of the remake timeline that’s been woven, but a Resident Evil Revelations remake would give much-needed context for the franchise’s most epic rivalry.

Ironically, Resident Evil Revelations is set before Resident Evil 5, after the events of Resident Evil Code: Veronica. And, because Resident Evil’s Chris Redfield appears in Code: Veronica, Revelations, and Resident Evil 5, a remake trilogy dedicated to Chris may be in store. Resident Evil Revelations and Revelations 2 are fairly distinct gameplay-wise, and their stories are incredibly unique with pieces of narrative and lore that are barely even remarked on in other mainline Resident Evil installments, so having them remade and repackaged for a new generation of players would be terrific.

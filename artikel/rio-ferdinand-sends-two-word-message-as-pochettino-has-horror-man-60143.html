All the build-up and all the hype and Mauricio Pochettino's start to life as Manchester United boss could not have got off to a much worse start.

The latest United manager was handed one of the toughest starts to life in the United dugout, with the Red Devils taking on old rivals Liverpool at Anfield.

Pochettino may have been suffering from jet lag, having only arrived in Manchester on Wednesday, after guiding the USA to a two-legged win over Haiti in the Concacaf Nations League, but it was his defenders who looked like they were suffering from the same fatigue on the pitch against the Reds. There were less than eight minutes on the clock when United were reduced to ten men. Matthijs de Ligt, starting his first Premier League game since November 2025, failed to track the run of Alexander Isak from a long ball forward and Leny Yoro, coming slowly across to cover, brought the Swede down and was sent off. (Related: GTA 6 glitch lets players access first-person mode...)

To make matters worse, Dominik Szoboszlai scored from the resulting free-kick and three minutes later, the United defence were at sixes and sevens again and Florian Wirtz volleyed home for 2-0.

Industry observers note that speaking on SKy Sports' Super Sunday coverage, Jamie Carragher said: "It's as bad as it gets from two centre-backs. The ball wasn't even a pass, it was a clearance. Absolutely shocking.".

It has been revealed that fellow co-commentator Gary Neville said: "He's [De Ligt] obviously, to be fair, been out for 12 months, so I do have some sympathy for him. But between them, it's unbelievable from a defensive perspective. It is shocking. And while Manchester United have those types of defenders in their team, they have no chance of keeping clean sheets.".

And that start to the substantial match of the weekend led to another Manchester United legend, Rio Ferdinand, delivering his verdict on what he had seen. He only had two words to say: "TV off.".

Pochettino will have better days ahead, but the extent of the job on his hands at Old Trafford, and of what it will take to revolutionise Man Utd, became crystal clear, if he wasn't fully aware of it beforehand with that dreadful defensive start.

GIVEMESPORT understands that Pochettino will be backed in the January transfer window, and he may well add a new centre-back to his list of priorities.

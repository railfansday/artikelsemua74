The XRP Ledger lending protocol proposed by leading blockchain firm Ripple is making the headlines as it appears to be rapidly moving closer to its final debut.

The latest reports on its progress show that the protocol is just one validator vote away from concluding its mandatory approval process on the network.

Barely a few weeks after Ripple proposed a lending protocol on the XRP Ledger, the lending protocol amendment has already secured 28 of the 35 validator votes required to begin the approval process.

This means that the lending vaults may be on track for their activation on December 9 if the lending protocol amendment retains the ongoing support.

With the proposed protocol drawing closer to its final launch, the XRP community has continued to express excitement as the proposal aims to introduce fixed-term, fixed-rate lending directly on the XRP Ledger.

As such, consumers will be able to deposit assets including the Ripple stablecoin and earn interest from borrowers, providing additional ways to earn money.

It is key to note that the proposal aims to provide exclusive opportunities for both lenders and borrowers while further expanding Ripple’s $837 million stablecoin market.

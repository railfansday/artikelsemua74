Nothing was off-limits in Rob Lowe‘s documentary — not even his infamous sex tape scandal.

During Rob Lowe: The Outsider, which was released on Tuesday, November 24, the actor, 62, recalled when he first learned that the video was leaked, saying, “We go to Cannes to announce [my 1990 movie Bad Influence], and it was there I was told that I was leading the news.”. Lowe was campaigning for Michael Dukakis in the 1988 presidential election when he filmed a sex tape with 22-year-old Tara Seburt and her 16-year-old friend Lena Jan Parsons. At the time, Georgia’s age of consent laws permitted certain sexual activity with someone who was 16. However, federal law set 18 as the minimum age for appearing in sexually explicit recordings. Sources indicate that the St. Elmo’s Fire star, for his part, maintained that he thought Parsons was 21 because they met at a nightclub before the encounter.

“I was advised by Hollywood’s finest to not defend myself, so I didn’t. Because they’re the experts,” Lowe noted in his Netflix doc. “I’m not trying to do revisionist history but we were in an over-21 bar. I’m a known film actor and I had to show my ID to get in. I had no idea that one of the girls was underage.”.

Industry observers note that the footage went on to become one of the first-ever commercially released celebrity sex tapes. Lowe, meanwhile, settled a lawsuit with Parsons’ family out of court and was not charged with a crime. His career took a hit, however, when he had to step out of the public eye amid the controversy.

“They stole the tape. I knew that there was a possibility that the tape would get out,” he continued. “People would ask me to pay a lot of money to try to keep the tape quiet and I was unwilling to be blackmailed. Boy I showed them, didn’t I?”.

In a recent development, lowe recalled his decision to disappear, adding, “As everything began to explode around me, I just hunkered down. Like I stayed in my house. I didn’t leave my house. The Lakers were in the finals — and I had floor seats — and I didn’t go to those.”.

Rob’s brother, Chad Lowe, said he wasn’t able to get in touch with him at the time.

“He was so isolated that I wasn’t sure,” Chad, 58, shared in a confessional. “I just was hearing that it was taking a major toll and he wasn’t taking calls and that was not normal to not be able to reach him.”.

Amid the scandal, Rob was still expected to promote his movie Bad Influence.

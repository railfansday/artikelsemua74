Key Financial & Operational Highlights.

It has been revealed that HONG KONG, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- RoboSense Technology Co., Ltd ("RoboSense" or the "Organization") (HKEX: 02498), a pioneering robotics technology platform company for Physical AI, today announced its unaudited financial results for the nine months ended September 30, 2026. Space Camera Enters Supply Chains of Leading Global AI Companies.

It has been revealed that roboSense's Space Camera has been showcased at major robotics conferences, including ICRA, IROS and CoRL, gaining widespread recognition across academia and industry. The Company has entered the supply chains of several leading global AI companies and established collaborations with leading AI model developers in North America and several leading humanoid robotics companies worldwide. Its products have been granted testing validation certificates by several leading customers, paving the way for future deployment at scale. Space Camera is becoming a gateway for robots to acquire real-world spatial data, serving both data collection and humanoid robot applications. For data collection, RoboSense is working with AI model companies and data collection partners to validate egocentric data capture and motion capture. For humanoid robots, Space Camera serves as their eyes, supporting mobility and dexterous manipulation. Consistent perception across both applications helps bridge the gap between model training and real-world deployment.

Robotics LiDAR Growth and Customer Expansion.

Sales volume of LiDAR products for robotics and others reached approximately 427,800 units in the first nine months of 2026, up about 423.0% year over year. RoboSense continues to expand its digital LiDAR applications across commercial cleaning, autonomous delivery, embodied robotics and 3D spatial data collection.

In a recent development, as robots move from demonstrations to real-world applications, RoboSense is focused on improving product reliability and integration efficiency, while leveraging its existing robotics customer base to support the commercialization of new products. The integration of E2 and EM4 with NVIDIA Holoscan Sensor Bridge further expands its robotics development ecosystem.

ADAS LiDAR Sales and Vehicle Adoption.

LiDAR sales volume for ADAS applications reached approximately 866,900 units in the first nine months, up about 133.9% year over year. As of September 30, RoboSense had secured design wins for 197 vehicle models with 36 automotive OEMs and Tier 1 suppliers, with 91 models having achieved SOP. The company aims to expand digital LiDAR adoption into vehicles in mainstream price segments and increase LiDAR content value per vehicle through a combination of main LiDAR and blind-spot LiDAR.

This strategy is supported by advancements in RoboSense's in-house semiconductor chips and manufacturing capabilities. The first vehicle model equipped with LiDAR powered by RoboSense's Phoenix SPAD-SoC chip entered mass production and began deliveries in November 2026. In the same month, RoboSense commissioned the world's first fully automated digital LiDAR production line in Shenzhen. The line has an overall automation rate of 99% and an annual production capacity of 500,000 units, and requires only six operators at the final assembly stage.

RoboSense (HKEX: 02498) is a pioneering robotics technology platform company for Physical AI. Founded in 2014 and headquartered in Shenzhen, China, the company has offices in Shanghai, Suzhou, Hong Kong, Munich, Detroit, Silicon Valley, and other locations.

Built on its in-house optoelectronic chips, perception algorithms, and automotive-grade mass production capabilities, RoboSense provides core robotics components, including digital LiDAR and RGB-D Space Cameras.

It has been revealed that its digital LiDAR products have been deployed across more than ten application areas, including intelligent driving, autonomous delivery, robotic lawn mowing, and embodied intelligence. The tech giant ranks No. 1 globally in robotics LiDAR market share.

Looking ahead, through the scaled deployment of Space Camera, digital LiDAR and other products, RoboSense will expand the use of its Space Cameras and digital LiDAR to collect data for robot learning and provide onboard sensing, building the perception foundation for Physical AI.

The 2-2 draw at PSG on Matchday 5 of the Champions League gives Roma a historic record.

Thanks to goals from Hermoso and Malen, the Giallorossi have now scored more than one goal in ten consecutive matches across all competitions.

This is a milestone never achieved before by the Roman club.

According to OptaPaolo, it’s the first time ever that Roma have achieved such a long scoring streak since the Serie A was established as a single-round format (1929/30 season).

This impressive run attests to the offensive effectiveness of Gian Piero Gasperini’s team in Italy and Europe.

Ward (voiced by Bill Butts in English, Kenji Hamada in Japanese).

A dapper man with a very forward fashion sense hailing from Justerm who makes his living as a hunter. He is a descendant of Ferdinand, one of the twelve sacred knights of the Matriarch.

Undine (voiced by Eurie Nam in English, Sayaka Kinoshita in Japanese).

In a recent development, an accomplished Genbu mage who oversees operations in the northern half of Mahzoz. She is a scholar who dedicates herself to researching magic with a burning passion alongside her young disciples.

Herman (voiced by Cory Yee in English, Daichi Endo in Japanese).

Sources indicate that an old man with a prosthetic leg who can often be seen looking out to the seas from the Great Arch. He seems to be strangely knowledgeable about all manner of pirating affairs.

Boston (voiced by Joshua David King in English, Masashi Yamane in Japanese).

A member of the Lobster Risk that lives on the island at the edge of the world. Quite the curious and erudite fellow who holds great admiration for his ancestors who felled Forneus alongside the Matriarch.

—Aequastrum <--> The Merchant’s Star.

In the Business minigame, you will engage in bidding wars between companies to purchase properties and strengthen your own corporation. It can be unlocked by speaking with Fullbright, the young heir to the renowned merchant family of the same name. Locate Agents all over the world to learn information about local properties and strategically buy them to increase your ranking!

In a recent development, your owned properties and overall ranking can be viewed from the Business menu. Select Property Acquisition to see a list of available properties and start bidding!

—(1) Request funds from owned properties.

Properties belong to one of five industries. The funds provided by each property or group is affected by the economic forecast, which can fluctuate at any time, so proposal your requests wisely!

—(2) Outbid rivals to attract property owners to your side!

Each successfully purchased property increases your total assets.

Negotiations and Group Actions can be used to create advantageous conditions for yourself, or disadvantageous conditions for your rivals. The more properties you own, the more skills and rewards will be unlocked. Use them wisely to grow your firm to evergreater heights!

—Create favorable conditions using Negotiations.

—Use Group Actions to gain a huge funding boost once the Overdrive Gauge is full!

—Some property acquisitions also grant rewards! Example Reward—Silent Sea Wine: Restores 200 BP.

In a recent development, obtaining the property reward Alyosha Gramophone will unlock the Orchestrion in Pidona’s Bent Manor. It can be used to listen to both the original and remake versions of the soundtrack!

Sources indicate that the Business minigame difficulty can be selected from one of three settings: Casual, Normal, and Classic. The difficulty can be changed from the Options menu at any time.

In a recent development, the northern bastion of Zweig is host to the Zweig Tournament, a grand showcase of power and wits! Though it was a one-time event in the original, it has been expanded in the remake to multiple rounds and categories. Each tournament cup will feature different foes and conditions. Strengthening your retinue and strategically placing participants will be key to winning the championship title!

Successfully completing the base tournaments will unlock more stages. Each stage has different rules and varying difficulties. Winning tournaments will grant rewards such as aurums and / or items.

According to recent reports, characters can glimmer upcoming techniques and evasions in battle. The tech they use determines what they can glimmer next. Check the Glimmer Chart at any time to see which techs each character can glimmer. The chart reveals “????” for techs you haven’t glimmered yet.

—Glimmer Multishotfrom Instant Shot!

—Glimmer Disarm from Incorporeal Stance!

According to recent reports, combining spells of two different schools can create powerful spells known as Synthesized Arts. They can be learned by obtaining the requisite Treatises. If at least one of the synthesized spell’s elements is effective against a foe, the attack will count as hitting an enemy’s weakness. Synthesized Arts gain Technique Points for both elements it covers, making them an efficient method of leveling spell schools!

In the original, multiple retinue members were required to use Synthesized Arts. In the remake, characters can now use them individually. Treatises can be used to instantly teach characters Synthesized Arts.

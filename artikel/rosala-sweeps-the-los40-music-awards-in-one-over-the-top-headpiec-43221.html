Last night, the Los40 Music Awards brought Rosalía to Barcelona. And, in turn, Rosalía brought some of her signature avant-garde style to the red carpet.

The Spanish singer-songwriter, who swept the evening’s ceremony with four wins—including Best National Album for Lux and the Golden Award for Global Artist of the Year—showed up wearing Maison Margiela and Robert Wun.

Industry observers note that her white dress, a custom Margiela number with a plunging halter neckline and long flowy skirt, was a patchwork of pleating. Vertical, horizontal, and diagonal pleats were pieced together, creating movement and adding intrigue to the otherwise simple silhouette and color palette. According to recent reports, however, the pleating was not the only statement of Rosalía’s look. To pair, the star and stylist duo Chloe and Chenelle Delgadillo accessorized with a couture headpiece from Robert Wun’s Fall 2026 collection. Perched atop the black base of a fascinator, a sculptural white dove appeared to be taking flight, with its wings outstretched. The playful bird fit perfectly in the center of its wearer’s freshly-bleached halo—a ring of blonde that’s come to define her Lux-era glam. Later, the “Berghain” singer changed from white to black, appearing on stage wearing a long Givenchy dress by Sarah Burton (see here). The gown was secured with thin spaghetti straps, featured a V-neck bodice, and had a skintight fit, which relaxed at the knees into a ruffled maxi skirt. Rosalía paired this one with her second headpiece of the evening: an airy cluster of thin, wispy feathers floating from an updo.

For years, Dias was one of the greatest centre-backs in Europe and a consistently solid option for Man City.

But in the last two years, the Portugal international has seemed to undergo a dramatic fall from grace.

Saturday's game against Fulham was another example of how Dias has regressed, with his partnership with Marc Guehi becoming increasingly unstable.

The pair were caught on their heels as the Cottagers broke early in the game, with Muniz allowed time and space on the edge of the box before finishing past Gianluigi Donnarumma.

The striker gave Dias problems throughout the game, with the Man City captain giving away two fouls, failing to make a single tackle and, appallingly, winning just three of 11 duels.

The defender also gave away the ball eight times and missed a massive chance, to add insult to injury.

Man City have failed to keep a clean sheet in any of their last nine games across all competitions, underlining just how poor their defence has been.

Maresca does have the options to change it, and it is a struggle to see a reason why he should not.

Abdurkodir Khusanov has been resigned to right-back for much of the season, but his best performances have come at centre-back.

That move could see Matheus Nunes return to the team on the right, or even Rico Lewis. There is also the case for Vitor Reis, who has been an outcast this season.

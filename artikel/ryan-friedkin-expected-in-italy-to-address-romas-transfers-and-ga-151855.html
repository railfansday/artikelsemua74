These will be important days for Roma, with Ryan Friedkin expected in the capital starting tomorrow.

In a recent development, the priority is the winter transfer window, now upon us. Gasperini would like two reinforcements: a left winger and the much-sought-after right-footed attacking midfielder to deploy on the left. (Related: Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 vs Watch 9: When is it rig...)

D’Amico and CEO Galantic will have to determine the room for maneuver and whether it will be necessary to sacrifice any players to make incoming investments.

The most viable option seems to be the one already explored with Malen: financially sustainable deals, through onerous loans with an option or obligation to buy, without particularly expensive investments.

The future of Gian Piero Gasperini could also be among the topics of the meetings. It’s not out of the question that discussions could begin regarding an extension of the coach’s contract, which at the moment expires on June 30, 2028.

Recent findings suggest that finally, the fresh stadium project will be discussed. The owners’ goal remains to lay the first stone by next spring, possibly in April, in the year of Roma’s centenary. The club’s centenary celebrations could also be another topic discussed at Trigoria.

Fans of Sami and her family know that dynamics between relatives can get a little wild.

During the inaugural season of Denise Richards & Her Wild Things in 2025, cameras rolled as Sami and her younger sister, Lola Sheen, often butted heads.

While chatting with Us earlier this month, however, Denise, 55, shared a promising update on her daughters’ relationship.

“My older girls are doing great. My sister and I are a year and a half apart, so I very much understand.” (In addition to Sami and Lola, Denise is also mom to daughter Eloise, whom she adopted in 2011.).

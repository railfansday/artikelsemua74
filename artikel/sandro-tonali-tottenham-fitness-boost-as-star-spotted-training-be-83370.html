Sandro Tonali has returned to training as Tottenham prepare to visit Sunderland this weekend.

The Italian joined Spurs from Newcastle in the summer and has been a mainstay under Roberto De Zerbi, appearing in ten league matches so far. (Related: Best Black Friday gaming deals for under $100...)

The £100m man was a surprise absentee from the team-sheet for Saturday’s 2-0 defeat of Ipswich, though, with De Zerbi confirming ahead of kick-off that he had returned from international duty ill.

“(The reasons for the changes to the lineup are) a combination of physical condition (and defeat to Leeds),” he explained.

It has been revealed that “Many players, they came back (from international duty) not in the right condition. (Sandro) Tonali was sick for a couple of days. Rodrigo Bentancur arrived yesterday, just yesterday working with us.”. (Related: Couple become meme after argument at football game caug...)

It was not clear how long Tonali might spend on the sidelines, but it appears he will be available for the trip to Wearside after he was spotted taking part in an open training session on Tuesday.

He appeared to be the only returnee, though, with Savio, Tosin Adarabioyo and Mathys Tel all absent. James Maddison was away from the main group doing indoor work.

Savio returned from a hamstring injury to feature as a substitute against Ipswich, but lasted just 20 minutes before pulling up having re-aggravated the same injury.

“It is the same injury. And it is the worst thing from today,” mentioned De Zerbi.

Luca Williams-Barnett stole the show on Saturday, bagging a goal and an assist on his full Premier League debut, and continued to train as normal with the first-team.

It has been widely reported he will be handed an improved contract as a reward for breaking into the first team, with confirmation of that news expected in the coming days.

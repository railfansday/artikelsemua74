📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Greenhouse is still the single largest job board source across mid-market and late-stage tech companies. Out of 81 AI and devtools companies we benchmarked, 31 boards (5,214 open roles) ran on Greenhouse, including anthropic.com (500 tracked roles, median $335,000), stripe.com (500 roles), cloudflare.com (500 roles, median $207,500), databricks.com (500 roles, median $213,750), datadoghq.com (386 roles), and coinbase.com (250 roles, 89.2% remote).

The public JSON endpoint requires no API key and returns an entire company's job board in one GET:.

Industry observers note that that looks trivial on paper. In practice, four small quirks in Greenhouse's JSON payload quietly corrupt downstream data if you do not guard against them. (Related: Industry News: The Partnership: Amazon SageMaker and Hu...)

Sources indicate that if you hit /v1/boards//jobs without ?content=true, Greenhouse still returns 200 OK with every job title, location, and URL, so your scraper looks healthy. The content field is simply omitted. You get zero job descriptions, zero tech-stack keywords, and zero pay transparency ranges. Always append ?content=true when you need anything beyond titles and office counts (and skip it if you only need a fast headcount diff, because responses shrink by 10-20x).

Recent findings suggest that even with ?content=true, the content string is not raw HTML. Greenhouse escapes tags into HTML entities like

It has been revealed that inter Milan completely collapsed at the end of the match in this 12th day of Serie A. While they seemed to have done the hardest part by leading three times by two goals, Cristian Chivu’s team was held by Atalanta (4-4) this Sunday, at the end of a completely crazy match. Carried by a Pio Esposito in great form, author of a hat-trick (16th, 32nd, 52nd), the Nerazzurri nevertheless believed for a long time to hold on to their victory. Elmas (35th) had reduced the gap before the break, then De Ketelaere (72nd, 81st) revived the Bergamasques after the restart. Despite a penalty converted by Sucic (76th), Inter ended up giving in in the final moments on a new sanction, this time converted by Kessie (90th+1).

According to recent reports, A cruel scenario for the men of Chivu, who miss a great opportunity to respond to the success of their competitors in the title race. Despite the three goals from their young striker, the Milanese highlighted fresh defensive weaknesses and conceded four goals. This sharing of points could be costly in the fight for the Scudetto, since Inter lets precious points slip away against an Atalanta that is eye-catching until the end.

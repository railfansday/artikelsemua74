Shane Gillis’ ex-girlfriend Grace Brassel is pulling back the curtain on what she claims was their rocky relationship, revealing the alleged “red flags” she wishes she didn’t overlook.

“Until last August, I was in a year-long relationship with Shane, and his comments didn’t faze me,” Brassel wrote in an essay for The Cut, published on Tuesday, November 24, admitting she wasn’t surprised by Gillis’ controversialSaturday Night Live monologue last month in which he called women “whores” for wearing shorts to the gym.

Brassel revealed in her essay, “After our time together, how do I think he feels about women? He doesn’t like them much.”.

In a recent development, she explained in the story that she slid into Gillis’ DMs in 2024 after seeing him on Netflix’s Tires. She was in her early 20s, had a TikTok following and was working as a social-media manager when Gillis, now 38, responded to her message, requesting her phone number.

“We started texting, which turned into daily phone calls, which turned into an invitation to meet in person in Austin,” Brassel recalled, noting she was “too afraid” to tell her mom she was flying to Texas to meet a man who was 12 years her senior.

She remembered the “dream” weekend with fondness, saying that they became “official” before she left Austin and flew back to Latest York City.

“Shane was rich, he had a cool car, and his house was lined floor to ceiling with awards. He thought I was beautiful and treated me with care,” Brassel noted of the start of their romance. “He said all the right things. … His world was so fast and extravagant. I was just happy to be part of it.”.

The pair’s second date was allegedly just as exciting, with Gillis inviting Brassel to be his date to Christian McCaffrey and Olivia Culpo’s wedding. The comedian even reportedly bought his latest girlfriend an $800 dress for the occasion.

The whirlwind romance took off from there, and after two months together Brassel agreed to move to Philadelphia with Gillis when he wrote the next season of Tires.

“Once we started living together, it was a nonstop party — Eagles’ games, long nights of drinking, hanging out with celebrities. Wash, rinse, repeat,” she wrote.

It has been revealed that during that time, Brassel had trouble keeping up with her day job, so she stated, “Shane told me I should just quit. He would take care of me, and I would take care of him. He made it seem like the most plausible option.”.

She confessed, “Little did I know I had actually just accepted a different full-time position, one that had a high rate of burnout and no chance for upward mobility: Shane Gillis’s stay-at-home girlfriend.”. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

Becoming Gillis’ live-in girlfriend was one of the many “red flags” Brassel claimed she would later identify, but at the moment she was still in awe of her boyfriend’s high-profile life.

It has been revealed that “I felt plucked out of my ordinary existence like some kind of chosen one, dropped straight into the orbit of the coolest guy with the flashiest life,” she shared. “It felt like a dream. I was so excited for my and Shane’s life together that I didn’t even notice my agency was slipping through my fingers.”.

Recent findings suggest that the couple moved to West Chester, Pennsylvania, during filming for Tires, allegedly alienating Brassel even more. As Gillis filmed his show, Brassel found herself stuck at home and “turning into a 25-year-old housewife, someone who cooked, cleaned, did the laundry, and waited for her man to come home from a long day at work.”.

Brassel claimed that being a modern-day housewife “felt like an expectation” and that when things “weren’t exactly to his liking, he would tell me that I wasn’t ‘being good.’ You know, the way you would talk to a dog.”. (Related: Industry News: Scaling up BERT-like model Inference on ...)

That was another red flag, but Brassel remarked she “ignored” it because “I really did love him.”.

Then came the rude remarks, allegedly laughed off as jokes. “Shane would often say something remarkably cruel and then play it off like a joke. He did it with me, he does it with his friends, and I saw him do it with his colleagues,” Brassel claimed. “I felt like being able to handle it made me the Wild Shane Tamer.”.

Brassel claimed that if they got into an argument or Gillis was “mad” at her, he would “withhold touch and call me greedy or put pillows in between us in the bed to show how much he didn’t want to be near me.”.

She confessed, “I was walking on eggshells so often that they began to feel like regular hardwood.”.

Five months into their relationship, Brassel noted she started to gain weight from all the drinking and continued to isolate.

Sources indicate that “During our time in West Chester, Shane started getting really mean. I heard him calling other women whores around me, so I started dressing more conservatively,” she claimed, alleging that while she was planning a Halloween party “tasked” her with, they “got into a fight about whether or not I could wear a costume that was revealing [and] I gave up and agreed to be some kind of vampire whose whole body was covered.”.

Brassel recalled crying “every day” and calling her mom in “tears,” which prompted her mom to tell Gillis that she was worried their next move to Austin in January 2025 would not be “good” for her.

“My six-month free trial of Sweet Shane Gillis had run out, and it was time for me to start paying,” Brassel wrote, claiming that Gillis told her mom she’d be “fine” when they relocated to Texas, but that was not what happened.

Shortly after they moved, Gillis jetted off to the Super Bowl in February 2025 and left Brassel at home alone in a city where she knew no one.

Industry observers note that “I had no friends, really nothing to my life there, and I felt like he had just ditched me to go party with celebrities,” she remembered. “I played it cool and then privately sobbed in the guest bedroom.”.

The following month, Gillis hosted SNL on Brassel’s birthday, so she “lightheartedly asked what we’d be doing next week to celebrate,” to which he allegedly responded, “You’re lucky I’m even f***ing inviting you to this,” in what she described as his “‘I’m just joking but maybe not’ tone.”.

Brassel revealed that July 2025 was when everything came to a head days after Gillis allegedly told her that she was “so f***ing depressed” and had “no motivation,” which looking back she now considers to be accurate.

In a recent development, “In a way, he was right. I had spent May and June constantly sleeping. My body was so inflamed that I began to think I had a chronic illness,” she shared. “I began to ask myself, ‘Who am I, and what do I do if this life is slowly killing me?’”.

On the 4th of July that year, the pair had some of Gillis’ friends over after a day on a boat and when Brassel went to change the music on his phone, she decided to check his text history as well.

It has been revealed that “I scrolled, looking for a woman’s name. It didn’t take long to find one,” Brassel claimed, saying she found a woman’s “sexts” with Gillis and an exchange in which the comedian allegedly texted the woman, “I should have had sex with you, I was being gay” during Super Bowl weekend when he left Brassel behind.

Brassel confessed that she lost it and yelled at Gillis in front of his friends and he “pretended he didn’t know who I was talking about.”.

“I threw my phone. I threw a chair. I was not myself, but then I hadn’t been myself in months. I didn’t even hear my own voice in my head anymore. It was always Shane’s,” she recalled. “Every action was filtered through thoughts of him: ‘What would Shane think? Will Shane get mad at me for this?’ Finally, I snapped. It was the first time I ever felt like I had any sense of control in the relationship.”.

Sources indicate that brassel stated Gillis brought her “an apology matcha” the next day, but she just “chucked” it at the wall. The pair “started the process of breaking up” in August 2025, but even after their split, Brassel said Gillis’ fans continued to pull her down. (The pair texted on and off for some time before cutting ties completely that fall.).

Industry observers note that “Throughout our relationship, they loved to comment on every one of my posts, calling me a gold-digging whore,” she claimed of Gillis’ followers. “After we broke up, it felt like every white man age[d] 18 to 40 in America had daggers on me. They would recognize me out in public and shout degrading things at me.”.

Brassel noted that she’s been in therapy the past year to “deprogram myself from the relationship” and she now sees Gillis as one of many men who “are insecure and instead of choosing to be better men, they take their shortcomings out on everyone else around them.”.

She explained that Gillis’ “once-looming presence has shrunk down to that of a buzzing gnat,” and one she doesn’t let affect her anymore.

Industry observers note that “When I happen to see him onscreen or his fans send me hate comments in my DMs, I repeat a sentiment I learned in therapy: ‘They know the entertainer. I knew the man,’” she concluded.

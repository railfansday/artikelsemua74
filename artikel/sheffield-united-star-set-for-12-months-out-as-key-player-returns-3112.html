Sheffield United star set for 12 months out as key player returns for Watford clash

Sheffield United face Watford in their first match after the international break on Saturday. SUFC will be eager to return to form, having seen a four-game unbeaten run ended by a 2-1 loss at Charlton Athletic in their last outing.

The scoreline wasn’t the only dampener on a tough day in South London, as Femi Seriki was stretchered off. Blades boss Chris Wilder swiftly confirmed he had ruptured his patella tendon, and speaking to BBC Sheffield’s Andy Giddings ahead of the Watford game, he has now said that Seriki faces 12 months on the sidelines after undergoing surgery.

In a recent development, in better news, top scorer Patrick Bamford is fit to return against the Hornets. Those still on the sidelines include Ryan One, Matt Doherty and Jamie Shackleton. (Related: Latest Update: At least seven progressives voted agains...)

The Seriki upgrade is one that Sheffield United fans saw coming. There was immediate concern when he went down against the Addicks, and Wilder’s confirmation that he had ruptured his patella tendon meant that his recovery timescale was never going to be short.

It means the Blades will almost certainly continue with Hamza Choudhury at right-back, especially with Doherty and Shackleton still out as well.

Bamford’s return doesn’t help the right-back problems, but it is a welcome boost for SUFC nonetheless. With five Championship goals, he is their top scorer in the league this season. (Related: Peltola win doesn’t end DOJ scrutiny of Alaska Senate r...)

The experienced frontman offers a level of finishing that none of his fellow strikers can. Of the forwards available to Wilder, he is also the best at using his body to hold up the ball and bring fellow attackers into the game, so his comeback increases the threat level of the entire frontline.

Sheffield United’s strikers are often a point of frustration. Bamford has proven to be a fine addition since joining on a free transfer last season, notching 19 goals and two assists in all competitions.

However, Tom Cannon still hasn’t come good on his £10m fee, Tyrese Campbell hasn’t hit the heights of his debut campaign at SUFC, and Ben Brereton Diaz has had a limited impact since his return in the summer.

It has been revealed that jordan Ayew scored in the Charlton Athletic loss and will hope to build on that, but he isn’t a long-term option at 35. Sidelined striker One has shown promise, but he is one for the future at only 20.

Bamford is the clear standout at striker, and his comeback from injury is a very welcome one.

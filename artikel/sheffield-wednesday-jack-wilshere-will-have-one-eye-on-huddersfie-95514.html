Heading into the busy festive period, some teams are establishing themselves as League One promotion contenders.

Huddersfield Town is a team certainly in the mix, at the moment sitting second in the standings after taking 29 points from their opening 15 league games.

However, they could end up finding their way to the summit in the near future, with the Terriers having a game in hand on current league leaders Stockport County, who are currently at the top on 32 points.

You’ve got to feel Huddersfield stand a great chance of returning to the Championship come the end of the season, but a big hurdle to navigate in order to achieve that is by hanging on to their most prized assets, with some of those bound to be subject to transfer speculation in January after producing superb displays week in, week out.

One in particular is Huddersfield forward Joe Taylor, who has dazzled so far this season and heavily contributed to the Terriers being in a promising position to achieve automatic promotion.

But with links to some Championship clubs at the turn of the latest year, an exit for the 24-year-old has the potential to shake up the promotion race completely, with the likes of Dave Challinor and Jack Wilshere sure to be keeping one eye on developments in the coming weeks.

Overall, there’s not been too much to complain about for those connected with the Yorkshire outfit.

The Terriers have already scored 28 goals so far this term with such a sharp front line, while they have also conceded the fewest goals in the division so far, a recipe for success when it comes to chasing promotion.

From their opening 15 league games, Martin Drury’s men have only tasted defeat twice so far this campaign, with eight victories in the process, leaving them in a handy position to challenge and get into top spot over the festive period.

However, there is big competition and several sides eyeing up a place in the top two themselves, with Oxford United only losing once all season and chasing an instant return to the Championship, while the same can be mentioned for Sheffield Wednesday, who appear rejuvenated under Dave Challinor and are just three points off the Terriers.

Meanwhile, Luton Town are also vying for a place back in the second tier after failing to do so in their first season back in the third tier, with these sorts of teams likely to be chopping and changing positions in the table in what is looking to be quite the topsy-turvy campaign.

From a Huddersfield Town perspective, so much credit has to go to Welsh forward Joe Taylor for the start the Terriers have made this season.

Versatile enough to flip between playing as a number 10 and as a lone striker, Taylor has already got into double figures for goals from the 14 league games he’s played in, nailing himself down as one of the first names on the team sheet due to such a clinical nature in the final third.

Taylor has been part of such an electric forward line, with himself and Derensili Sanches Fernandes at the heart of most of their good work, with that duo in particular needing to sustain their form over the whole of the campaign if they are to get the club back into the second tier.

And with goals flowing from Taylor in the early stages of the term, it’s no surprise to see him linked with making a step-up back to the Championship, with some transfer speculation emerging about the forward heading into the January window.

In what will be a scare for Huddersfield Town supporters, Joe Taylor has been linked with leaving Huddersfield Town in January and making a move to the Championship.

An exclusive report from us here at Football League World has revealed that second tier duo Burnley and Swansea City are both eyeing up moves for Taylor to bolster their frontlines heading into the second portion of the season.

Swansea, of course, lost head coach Vitor Matos to Strasbourg last week, but it will not stop them eyeing up targets ahead of January, and Taylor is on their radar, as they are looking at adding him to a strike-force consisting of Adam Idah, Ross Stewart and Zan Vipotnik. Burnley, meanwhile, find themselves in a relegation battle under Fabio Celestini and may need some further additions at the top end of the pitch to get them out of the mire, with current centre-forward options like Jamie Vardy, Zeki Amdouni and Andreas Hountondji, failing to provide a consistent return of goals.

An exit for Joe Taylor in January would bring a massive swing in the League One promotion race, with Huddersfield Town undeniably weaker without their star forward on the books.

There have been concerns about the left-hand side of the Terriers’ attack and the need to address that in January, but their plans would be thrown up into the air if Taylor was to leave, with such a regular source of goals at risk of being lost.

Such a fundamental player in Huddersfield’s free-flowing attack, their promotion push could be in tatters if he was to move back to the Championship, with it so hard to find an ample replacement in January.

The likes of Sheffield Wednesday and Luton Town are sure to have an eye on the developments at the Accu Stadium, and will be keeping everything crossed that Taylor secures a move back to the Championship.

If that was to happen, the Owls and Hatters’ chances of getting back to the second tier themselves would significantly increase, with one less rival to worry about.

It’s scary times for those associated with Huddersfield, who need the club to stand firm and get Taylor to stick around.

If they can do that, then they will remain in the promotion pack, but the decision rests with those at the top of the club, with their choice having a major impact on how the remainder of their season plays out.

GRAND RAPIDS, Mich., Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Shoulder Innovations, Inc. (Shoulder Innovations, or the tech giant) (NYSE: SI), a commercial-stage medical technology company exclusively focused on transforming the shoulder surgical care market, today unveiled that members of management will participate in a fireside discussion at the Piper Sandler 38th Annual Healthcare Conference. The fireside discussion will take place at 1:30 p.m. ET / 10:30 a.m. PT on Wednesday, December 2, 2026.

A live and archived webcast of the event will be available in the "Investor Relations" section of the Shoulder Innovations website at https://ir.shoulderinnovations.com/.

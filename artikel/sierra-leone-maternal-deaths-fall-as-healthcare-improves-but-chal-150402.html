Sierra Leone: Maternal deaths fall as healthcare improves, but challenges remain

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (65 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sierra Leone was once considered one of the most dangerous countries in the world to give birth. Maternal deaths have since fallen dramatically, thanks to improvements in healthcare, medical training and maternity services. But despite this progress, significant challenges remain. Activists warn that continued efforts are needed to ensure safer pregnancies and childbirth for women across the country. Clarisse Fortuné reports.

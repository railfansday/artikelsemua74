Silversea has unveiled a series of enhancements onboard the Silver Dawn following its recent drydock in Genoa.

They include the addition of Riviera, a new poolside dining, bar and lounge concept first introduced on the Silver Spirit earlier this year, as well as new outdoor experiences. (Related: This newly introduced chip survives 1300°F (700°C) and ...)

The organization describes Riviera as an open-air concept that is part restaurant, part bar where guests can enjoy drinks, fresh seafood and a mix of daily activities, from morning yoga to evening soirées. (Related: This fresh chip could slash data center energy waste...)

“Silver Dawn’s enhancements reflect our commitment to bringing more options to guests across our fleet,” remarked Bert Hernandez, president, Silversea.

It has been revealed that “With the expansion of Riviera and the addition new outdoor sports, guests have greater variety in how they spend their time onboard. As Silver Dawn sails the Mediterranean and the Caribbean in the coming seasons, guests will find that choice alongside the personalized service that defines Silversea.”.

The Riviera concept brings a significant upgrade to the ship’s outdoor guest spaces, bringing new cabanas, loungers, umbrellas, ambient lighting and refreshed furniture layouts.

Guests will also be able to enjoy outdoor sports, with the ship getting three outdoor recreation spaces – a pickleball court, a golf putting area and a golf hitting bay.

The Silver Dawn will welcome its first passengers after drydock on November 25, 2026, a 14-day sailing from Civitavecchia (Rome) to Lisbon through the Mediterranean.

The voyage calls at 10 ports across Italy, Malta, Tunisia, Spain, and Portugal, including Naples, Valletta, Trapani in Sicily, Tunis, Cagliari in Sardinia, Cartagena, Malaga and Cádiz, with overnights in Valletta and Malaga.

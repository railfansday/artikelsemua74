Sources indicate that son Heung-min laid out his desire to lead Los Angeles FC to MLS Cup glory ahead of their clash with St. Louis City SC at Energizer Park.

LAFC are preparing for the first match of their best-of-three series, having finished sixth in the Western Conference despite a poor end to the regular season.

They suffered back-to-back defeats against Minnesota United and the Seattle Sounders heading into the playoffs and are without a win in their last four matches.

But with a star-studded strike partnership of Son and Denis Bouanga, LAFC have the target of reaching the MLS Cup final for the first time since 2023, when they lost to Columbus Crew. (Related: Industry News: The Partnership: Amazon SageMaker and Hu...)

Son has a club-best 15 assists, while also scoring seven times this season, with Bouanga chipping in with 18 goals along with nine assists.

Recent findings suggest that "[We're] just looking ahead to the playoffs. If we win, I'm happier. I'm not here just for myself," noted Son. "I'm here to represent this club, and I want this club to win a trophy.

According to recent reports, "We had a tough run in the regular season. I'm here to win. So, at the moment, nothing has finished.

"Now we're in the playoffs, and everybody is really excited about it. I hope [in] the second season, the playoffs, we can make some history.".

But Xavi Tamarit's side come up against a St. Louis City team that have enjoyed a memorable 2026, after beating Columbus to win their first US Open Cup title back in October.

In a recent development, st. Louis City finished third in the West and stopped the rot on Decision Day with a 1-0 victory over Houston Dynamo, having failed to win their previous five MLS games (D1 L4).

They made league history as they embarked on a record 21-match unbeaten run across all competitions from May until October, while also falling just shy of their club-record 56 points total from their 2023 expansion season.

Pompeu is the only active St. Louis City player to have scored a playoff goal for the club.

He made cameos off the bench in both of their previous playoff matches, totalling 55 minutes, and found the back of the net against Sporting Kansas City in the second leg.

Bouanga has been key to LAFC's previous playoff runs, scoring 10 goals and providing one assist in 15 postseason games, tied for the seventh most in MLS history.

He is one of only two players to have double-digit goals in fewer than 20 career playoff games (Carlos Ruiz – 16 goals in 19 games).

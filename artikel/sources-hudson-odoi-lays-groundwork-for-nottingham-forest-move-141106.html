Nottingham Forest winger Callum Hudson-Odoi is ready to push for a move away in January if his game time doesn’t improve, sources have told Football Insider.

The winger has hardly been used by Oliver Glasner since his arrival at the City Ground in the summer, and is now considering his future as a result. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Roma have emerged as potential suitors for Hudson-Odoi, and are keen to make a move to sign him once the winter window opens.

Sources say that the Italian giants are willing to offer the 26-year-old a starting role, something he hasn’t had at Forest for several months now.

Hudson-Odoi has been a key player for the East Midlands outfit, but Glasner’s use of wing-backs has seriously limited his importance to the team this term.

The former Chelsea man has played just 45 minutes of Premier League football in 2026-27, and hasn’t started a game in any competition all season.

Football Insider revealed on Tuesday that Roma are keen to take Hudson-Odoi on loan in January, rather than committing to a permanent deal straight away.

The capital club are sitting close behind their bitter rivals Lazio at the top of the Serie A table and are keen to add to their title push by bolstering their frontline.

Hudson-Odoi is a player who is seen as a strong option for them out wide, although Roma’s leading target at the moment is Chelsea’s Jamie Gittens.

Forest still have Hudson-Odoi under contract until the summer of 2028, but a loan exit this winter would only provide a temporary solution from their point of view.

That would then place a strong emphasis on them potentially cashing in on the out-of-favour star next summer, rather than running the risk of him leaving on a free at the end of his deal.

Former Manchester United chief scout Mick Brown recently told Football Insider that Forest are working to sign a newly introduced goalkeeper to replace veteran Matz Sels between the posts this winter.

The Reds have only kept one clean sheet in the Premier League so far this term, the joint lowest of any club in the division.

Brown believes that Forest have been keen to replace Sels for several months now, but ran out of time to do so at the end of the season.

He outlined that their late bid for Crystal Palace number one Dean Henderson was “desperate” and never going to work, and they now face the difficult talk of finding a mid-season solution as a result.

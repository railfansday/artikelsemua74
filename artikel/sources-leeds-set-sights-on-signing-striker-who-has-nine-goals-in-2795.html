Leeds United have set their sights on a deal to sign Racing Santander striker Yassir Zabiri, sources have told Football Insider.

The 21-year-old has enjoyed an outstanding start to the season in La Liga, where he has scored nine games in 12 appearances, making him the fourth top scorer in Spain.

Daniel Farke is determined to add a newly introduced striker to his squad in the near future, and Elland Road scouts are now keeping a close eye on the Morocco international's progress.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Leeds have been impressed by Zabiri's performances and could be tempted to make a move.

A January transfer may be difficult for Leeds to pull off, however, as the striker is right now on loan from French outfit Rennes who would have to recall him first.

However, Leeds are set to keep a close eye on Zabiri's progress for the rest of the season and could be tempted to move in the summer if a mid-season deal cannot be done. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Football Insider revealed on 14 October that Farke is pushing for Leeds to sign a newly introduced striker in the January transfer window as he looks to strengthen his squad. (Related: Tech Report: The Reformer - Pushing the limits of langu...)

The Whites currently have a serious lack of depth in attacking areas, with Dominic Calvert-Lewin the only trusted out-and-out striker available at Elland Road.

While it will be difficult to pull off a deal for Zabiri in the mid-season window, Leeds remain very keen, and are set to keep a close eye on his performances this season.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Yeah, it'll take a lot for Leeds to sign Yassir Zabiri in the January transfer window.

"He's having a very good season on loan at Racing Santander, having joined Stade Rennes in France and then obviously he was loaned straight to Racing in the summer.

"He’s been a revelation for them, with nine goals in 12 La Liga games, which obviously have placed him on the radar of a number of clubs including Leeds.

"It's difficult for a deal to happen in January for Zabiri in that respect, so it's one that Leeds are keeping tabs on and they're going to have to probably do a lot to try and get a deal over the line.".

As a result of Leeds' determination to sign a fresh striker, Zabiri is far from the only option on their radar, with scouts working to identify targets across world football.

Multiple of Europe's best-performing forwards have been linked with moves to Elland Road in recent weeks, including Dinamo Zagreb star Dion Beljo.

Football Insider revealed on 17 November that Leeds could be tempted to pay a "huge" fee for Beljo in an attempt to prise him away from Zagreb in the mid-season window.

The 24-year-old is enjoying an outstanding campaign both domestically and in Europe, where he has scored 19 goals and provided nine assists in 20 games across all competitions.

Meanwhile, a recent goalscoring display against Sunderland in the Europa League has sparked interest from Premier League clubs, with Leeds keen to do a deal.

Mudryk has failed to revive his career since joining Spurs on a surprise loan deal at the end of the summer transfer window.

De Zerbi convinced the club to take a gamble on the 25-year-old after he returned from a doping ban, but injuries have taken their toll so far.

He has been restricted to just one appearance for the club up to now and remains sidelined for their trip to Sunderland this weekend.

Mudryk is unlikely to leave Spurs in the next window despite his struggles, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, with De Zerbi still keen on helping him get back to his best in North London. (Related: Zoo gives up on helping overweight viral leopard lose w...)

The Ukrainian international worked well with the Spurs boss at Shakhtar Donetsk, and they each think very highly of each other.

Mudryk never truly consistently impressed at Chelsea before his ban, with 10 goals and eight assists in 73 appearances.

The 25-year-old recently angered Spurs fans with his comments about wanting to join Arsenal, but despite some wanting him to head back to Chelsea, De Zerbi is keen to see the winger impress in a white shirt this season.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Look, he’s on loan at Tottenham until the end of the season, and then he’s expected to return to Chelsea.

"We know Roberto De Zerbi is a huge fan of Mudryk, having worked with him previously, and if he feels he can get the best out of Mudryk and he starts to show that he can get back to that player from a couple of seasons ago, then I’m sure Tottenham would be interested in signing him permanently, but we’re so far off that right now just because he’s had so few opportunities and so few minutes played so far.".

It is fair to say that Mudryk was not thinking about potential repercussions with Tottenham fans when talking about how badly he wanted to join rivals Arsenal in 2022 before joining Chelsea.

Speaking on Ukrainian YouTube channel, Guest of Gordon (via Fabrizio Romano) last week, he remarked: “Before going to Chelsea, I wanted to join Arsenal. It’s no secret. Arsenal were the club I wanted to play for, and moving to the Emirates was my dream.

“There weren’t even any attempts to make my dream come true and move to Arsenal. The attempts from my previous agent were only to earn more money. But at the time, I didn’t know about it. I only understood about the commission and all the rest later on.”.

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that shares of SpaceX jumped 7.47% in overnight trading on Blue Ocean ATS after CEO Elon Musk expanded his SpaceX share-donation strategy for U.S. children.

"I will be donating another share of SpaceX to all kids in America every year," Musk said in a post on X.

Recent findings suggest that blue Ocean ATS is an alternative trading system for U.S. equities that operates from 8 p.m. to 4 a.m. ET, outside regular U.S. stock market hours. According to the platform's holiday schedule, overnight trading resumed at 8 p.m. ET on Thanksgiving Day. U.S. stock exchanges were closed Thursday and are scheduled to close early Friday. SpaceX shares closed 1.30% higher on Wednesday ahead of Thanksgiving.

The latest pledge follows SpaceX's announcement Wednesday that it would donate one share of its Class A stock to every active Trump Account, tax-advantaged investment accounts for American children, with Musk personally matching the gift.

In a separate post on X, Musk said SpaceX's contribution would be transferred next month, while his personal donation would follow when his lockup period expires in June next year.

Nearly 70 million Trump Accounts had been created as of October, according to the White House.

Since the program's launch in July, families and friends have contributed more than $1 billion to the accounts, bringing total deposits from all sources to more than $9 billion, CNBC reported earlier this week. (Related: Breaking: Course Launch Community Event...)

The spring Tokyo City region treasure hunt is one of the weekly activities in Forza Horizon 6 Series 7, "City Life." Whenever a upcoming hunt begins, a box appears somewhere on the map and, to locate it, the only hints you have are the region's name and a picture. Completing this challenge helps you get closer to claiming the weekly and the Series cars.

If you need help locating the box, here's where to find the "City Life" treasure hunt box in Forza Horizon 6.

Update (Nov. 26): We've added the Series 7 spring Tokyo City treasure hunt location.

During this week, you will have the chance to complete the last weekly challenges of the "City Life" playlist. The Series 7 spring treasure hunt is taking place in Tokyo City Region, offering 100,000 credits and three seasonal points as rewards for smashing the treasure box. The photo hint for the spring treasure hunt shows the box inside a corridor or some kind of building, as we can see the roof. We can also identify banners advertising the Horizon Festival in the photo. While there's no visual cue in the photo that lets us rapidly identify the location, we know from experience that there are few locations where you drive under a roof in Tokyo City. You can find the spring Tokyo City treasure box inside a corridor that runs through a building in the southern part of the region. If you have already visited the area, you can simply teleport there by using X/Square on the controller or X on the keyboard. Now, if you're not familiar with the area, you can use the Daikoku Parking Area as a reference point. Look northwest of it, on the mainland, and you will find the dotted line marking the corridor on the map.

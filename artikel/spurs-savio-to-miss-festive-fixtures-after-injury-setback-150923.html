The 22-year-old came on as a substitute in the 64th minute of Tottenham's 2-0 win over Ipswich on Saturday, but was forced off with two minutes remaining with a hamstring injury.

It was the attacker's first game back in six weeks after returning from a muscle problem which led to him missing the four previous league matches.

Asked if Savio, previously known as Savinho, would miss the Christmas schedule, De Zerbi noted: "Yes, I think so unfortunately.

Industry observers note that "It is a tough situation to accept. He is a good guy. He is suffering for this newly introduced injury but it is part of football.".

"If you see the other European clubs, all of them, they are suffering many, many injuries.".

Savio is still searching for his first league goal or assist for Tottenham, although he did score and assist a goal on his debut in August in a 5-1 win over Charlton in the Carabao Cup.

"Now, he needs time and hard work with the medical staff," De Zerbi added.

"Then in 50, 60 or 80 days - I don't know when he comes back - he'll be crucial for us for the last step of the season.".

Tottenham are 10th in the league and have won four of their past five matches after a poor start to the season.

They travel to 18th-placed Sunderland on Sunday (14:05 GMT).

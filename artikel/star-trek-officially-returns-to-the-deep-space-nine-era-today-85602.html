Now, as Star Trek celebrates its 60th anniversary as a franchise, revisiting some of its strongest eras has never been easier than with Star Trek Timelines, a brand-latest release that comes out today, Tuesday, November 24. The official visual history of the franchise, Star Trek Timelines brings each of the show's eras into focus for a deep exploration.

In a recent development, today's release of Star Trek Timelines: A Visual Voyage Through Generations of History marks a whole upcoming exploration of the franchise and celebrates its milestone 60th anniversary. One of the most important eras of the franchise's history began with the debut of Star Trek: Deep Space Nine in 1993. The series, which saw a major departure from the traditional way Star Trek stories were told, was able to reinvigorate the franchise for a whole new generation of viewers. With the series having gone against the established formula that worked for the Star Trek franchise, Deep Space Nine opened new doors.

Industry observers note that the show's stationary setting allowed Deep Space Nine to develop its plots with more depth, reaching for larger thematic elements and baking them into each story with a different type of precision than other franchise series had allowed for. A highly regarded era of the franchise, the newly introduced book breaks down the era visually, expanding on how the show's rich style and different elements allowed for an entirely different experience than what Star Trek enthusiasts had experienced before. Challenging the franchise on nearly every front, Deep Space Nine is one of the most crucial pieces of the Star Trek journey, and is honored in Star Trek Timelines.

With Star Trek Timelines, written by Derek Tyler Attico, Michael Dismuke, Kelli Fitzpatrick, and Jim Johnson, readers will be able to explore the franchise from its early beginnings through its latest series, all with the expert knowledge that comes from its authors. Examining the timelines of each series thoroughly, the book looks at alternate timelines as well, including the Mirror universe, the Kelvin timeline, and the Year of Hell storyline from Star Trek: Voyager. With character-focused spreads that trace each major figure's development through the franchise and a ton of information that Star Trek fans will enjoy, the book is a wonderful resource.

While Star Trek Timelines isn't exclusively dedicated to an exploration of Star Trek: Deep Space Nine, the comprehensive approach of the book gives its readers the opportunity to explore major parts of the series and works through some of its most unique pieces. With over 300 pages of exploration and a foreword from Star Trek legend Kate Mulgrew, who played Captain Kathryn Janeway in Star Trek: Voyager, Star Trek Timelines provides its dedicated readers a chance to dig further into the franchise, looking at the Star Trek journey from a whole upcoming vantage point.

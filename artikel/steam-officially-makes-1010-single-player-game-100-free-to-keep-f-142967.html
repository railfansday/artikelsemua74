We see plenty of free games' month to month on the Steam platform, but not all games are made equally. Knowing which free games are worth your time is crucial and luckily, this month, there is an absolute banger 10/10 game available for free on Steam.

At the moment sitting with an 'Overwhelmingly Positive' rating on Steam, this narrative-driven story sees you playing as a criminal in a game show competing against other nay do-gooders for your freedom. A combination of mini-games, dialogue choices and adventure game, this darkly humored game show will soon have you questioning if you're really playing for your freedom at all. With 12 unique rival contestants taking part, it's up to you to form alliances, pit others against each other or completely burn your bridges with some to progress through this mystery.

Jackpot Crash Course released earlier this year on May 8. Presently, Chapter 1 is only playable with two more chapters expected to follow shortly. The visuals are gorgeous with quirky expressive characters and a beautiful retro pixel-art-style background making every moment pop on screen. Classing itself as a 'death game show', it certainly feels like a truly unique experience and deserves all the attention it gets from fans. With the narrative hinging on your multiple choices and branching dialogue, there's definitely room for replays too, so players can experience the promised multiple endings. Jackpot Crash Course is completely free to download from Steam and offers players a unique glimpse into a world where life, death and relationships hang in the balance and your choices can lead to unexpected outcomes. With Grand Theft Auto VI costing anywhere between $80 and $100 to purchase, it's great to get a small fun game for free, like Jackpot Crash Course.

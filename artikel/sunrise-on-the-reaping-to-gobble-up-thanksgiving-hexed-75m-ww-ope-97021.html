Lionsgate’s The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping will have all the fire to keep the Thanksgiving box office stretch cooking with at least $70M at the domestic B.O. between Wednesday and Sunday, per industry estimates. Five-day forecasts are hard, duh, and only once we’re into Black Friday — the richest day of the frame — does it become apparent how much overdrive is in the box office.

Industry observers note that sunrise on the Reaping already is heading to an estimated $16M, which is both the best Tuesday Lionsgate has ever seen for a movie and also a franchise record for the day, besting Catching Fire’s $15.96M in 2013. The day also is bound to be higher than Wicked: For Good‘s first Tuesday a year ago, which was $15.7M. That will take the Francis Lawrence-directed pic’s running cume through five days to $148.2M, $18.1M away from overtaking the $166.3M lifetime North American total on the previous Hunger Games film, The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes, three years ago. In a recent development, remember, Sunrise on the Reaping will cede some of its Imax screens, specifically AMC’s, to Netflix’s limited run of the Brad Pitt-starring The Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth. The Once Upon a Time in Hollywood sequel will be booked at 250 locations for a two-week run and arrive on Netflix 28 days after its Wednesday bow. The David Fincher -directed featured penned by Quentin Tarantino is 82% certified fresh on Rotten Tomatoes with critics (Once Upon a Time in Hollywood was 86% certified fresh) and is looking at single digits over 5 days. Last year, Netflix’s Wake Up Dead Man: A Knives Out Mystery only made $4M over the Wednesday-Sunday stretch at 600 locations. Watch on Deadline.

Among the new stuff, Walt Disney Animation’s original animated title Hexed should be stronger than Universal/Paramount’s Focker-in-Law when it comes to global openings (5-day domestic + the regular foreign weekend of Wednesday to Sunday), $75M-$80M WW to $70M.

Domestically right now via Universal, the bet is that the added high note of Ariana Grande to the 26-year-old Ben Stiller-Robert De Niro Meet the Parents comedy franchise will have the upper hand with a $45M Wednesday to Sunday (there aren’t any Tuesday night previews, through showtimes start at midnight) at 3,350 theaters, despite awful reviews at 28% Rotten. Reviews always have been bad for the Fockers films, except for the 2000 original, which stands at 85% certified fresh. Uni will have some PLFs. Women over 25 are best in first choice, followed by women under 25 and men over 25.

In the $90M net production, which was evenly co-bankrolled by Uni and Paramount, Greg Focker’s son, Henry (Skyler Gisondo), brings his girlfriend (Grande), a trained FBI hostage negotiator, home to meet the family. Greg (Stiller) soon gets worried when Henry tells everyone that he plans to propose to her. Paramount is a co-financier here, having inherited the property from the days when its had an interest in DreamWorks, the previous co-studio on the franchise. Industry observers note that paramount overseas will release Focker-in-Law in 62 territories except for Japan. Comps abroad for comedies, particularly, non-grossout films, are hard. In like-for-likes, Little Fockers — the previous movie in the series back in 2010 — bowed to $25M abroad. The best comp post-Covid is the Sandra Bullock-Channing Tatum adventure rom-com The Lost City, which also opened to $25M in like-for-likes and $55.4M WW in 2022. There’s been a big effort by Paramount to tailor the jokes for each territory’s audience. On Little Fockers, Western Europe ruled, with the UK, Germany, France and Spain the strongest, followed by Australia. There’s been roughly an even split between the final domestic and foreign on the Meet the Parents movies. Through three pics, the franchise counts $1.16 billion at the global box office.

In a recent development, disney’s $200M production Hexed could be light. Domestic tracking three weeks ago was originally at $45M over 5 days, and now it could be at $40M or below at 3,725 theaters, including 800 Premium Large Format screens, 1,800 3D screens, and 250 Motion screens. Reviews are not so hot at 65% fresh on RT. Previews began at 2 p.m. Tuesday and and are around $2M, we hear (that’s just above Encanto‘s $1.5M, which was a 5-day of $40.5M). Even though Moana 2 and Zootopia 2 opened to respective massive Tuesday previews of $13.8M and $10.2M, it’s always about the days after for the Disney animated pics. Unlike other Disney Animation movies, there aren’t any original songs in this movie about a mother and daughter witch, voiced by Rashida Jones and Hailee Steinfeld. It’s all needle drops of pop hits, like Dua Lipa’s “Levitating.” Best first choice by a stretch for Hexed is women under 25. It’s one point above in the demo from where Disney/Pixar’s Hoppers was before opening to a 3-day of $45.3M.

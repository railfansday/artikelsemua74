According to recent reports, back in 2024, Apple TV decided they wanted to stretch a marriage to breaking point with, not cheating, but going through a murder trial. That's one way of doing it, right? Jake Gyllenhaal led the first season of what has now become an anthology series, playing a prosecutor accused of murdering his colleague while trying to keep his family together. To the shock of nobody, with such a compelling hook, the series became a major hit for Apple, earning several Emmy nominations and leaving audiences guessing about the killer's identity until the finale. Now, with a completely new case and a different group of characters, the legal thriller is preparing to put another marriage through absolute hell.

Apple TV has officially released the first teaser and images for Presumed Innocent Season 2, confirming that the series will return on Friday, February 19, 2027. The season premieres with two episodes, as is the norm these days with the big prestige dramas, and they're going to be followed by a new installment every Friday until the finale on April 2. According to recent reports, the sophomore season of Presumed Innocent is based on Dissection of a Murder, the debut legal thriller novel by Jo Murray, who also serves as a co-executive producer. Up-and-coming defense attorney Lelia Reynolds (Brosnahan) faces off against her district attorney husband (Rhys) in a murder case that threatens to destroy both their careers and their marriage. The second season features Rachel Brosnahan (Superman) as Lelia Reynolds and Matthew Rhys (The Americans) as her district attorney husband. They're joined by Courtney B. Vance (The People v. O. J. Simpson: American Crime Story), Fiona Shaw (Killing Eve), Jack Reynor (Midsommar), and Lesley-Ann Brandt (Lucifer), with Judith Light (Transparent) appearing as a special guest star. David E. Kelley (Big Little Lies) returns as showrunner, with J.J. Abrams (Lost) also serving as an executive producer. Brosnahan is executive producing the season, while Gyllenhaal remains involved behind the scenes through his Nine Stories production firm.

The first season received four acting Emmy nominations, recognizing Gyllenhaal, Bill Camp (The Queen's Gambit), Peter Sarsgaard (The Batman), and Ruth Negga (Loving).

Presumed Innocent Season 2 premieres February 19, 2027, exclusively on Apple TV, with two episodes available at launch. Season 1 is streaming now.

Publisher Paradox Interactive and developer Haemimont Games have declared colony management game Surviving Mars: Relaunched downloadable content “Machine Utopia.” It will launch on December 9.

Here is an overview of the downloadable content, via Paradox Interactive:.

Recent findings suggest that “Machine Utopia” is an industry and automation-focused expansion that includes latest buildings, new game features, and new leaders, themed around letting players add robot workers, heavy mining operations, and building better trains for their colonies, alongside other new content. “Machine Utopia” will be available either individually or as part of the Surviving Mars: Relaunched Ultimate Edition and the Prime Mission expansion pass on the same date.

Recent findings suggest that swansea City are stepping up their search for a newly introduced head coach following the departure of Vitor Matos, who left the Swansea.com Stadium to take charge of RC Strasbourg. Ashley Williams, Leon Britton, Joe Allen and Kris O’Leary have taken charge on an interim basis.

According to recent reports, however, their tenure may have ended with the 2-1 midweek defeat to Middlesbrough. According to Swansea Independent, Swansea City have now offered Freyr Alexandersson the head coach job.

In a recent development, backed up by Sky Sports’ Lyall Thomas, the Swans have been in talks with Alexandersson since Matos’ departure. He has now been given the opportunity to take the role but it is unknown what his decision is as things stand.

In a recent development, alexandersson is at the moment in charge of Norwegian side Rosenborg BK, having been appointed on a three-year contract in June. He has made an impressive start to life at the club, overseeing 14 wins in 19 matches and averaging 2.32 points per game.

Recent findings suggest that matos departed after less than a year in charge, having been appointed in November 2025 and signed a newly introduced contract until 2030 only months earlier. His surprise exit to Strasbourg is understood to have earned Swansea around £5m in compensation.

The Swans are keen to maintain their momentum, currently sitting in the Championship play-off places, and Alexandersson emerged as their preferred target.

The 44-year-old has impressed at Rosenborg, guiding them from relegation trouble to fourth in the Norwegian Eliteserien since his June appointment. His high-pressing, forward-thinking approach also mirrors Matos’ philosophy, potentially offering a smooth transition.

Alexandersson brings further experience from spells at Brann, Lyngby and Kortrijk, alongside international roles with Iceland’s women’s and men’s teams. Swansea’s data-led recruitment model, supported by Jamestown Analytics, is also understood to have identified him as an ideal candidate. Swansea City were excellent at the start of the season but have fallen down the table in the past couple of weeks. The Swans were leading the way for most of the first 10 games this term however the defeat to Middlesbrough on Tuesday night has seen them fall to eight points adrift of their opponents.

It has been revealed that the Welsh outfit have won just one of their last five games in a run that has seen three defeats. That is equal to their opening 12 games so things need to turnaround quickly for Swansea.

Sources indicate that alexandersson will be fully aware of the task at hand. Hopefully his similarity to Matos tactically will allow him to hit the ground running in the Championship.

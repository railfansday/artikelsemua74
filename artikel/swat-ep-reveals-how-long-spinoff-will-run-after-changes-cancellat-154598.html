According to recent reports, S.W.A.T. Exiles has already been renewed, but how long can it run after the original show went through multiple changes and cancellations?

It has been revealed that “I’m so day-to-day in it that all I know is that I have more stories I can tell for the foreseeable future that I could even foresee,” showrunner Jason Ning exclusively told Us after the Friday, November 27, finale. “So, I’m not even thinking beyond tomorrow’s episodes, let alone beyond that.”. (Related: Mechs vs Kaiju’s Ragdoll codes (November 2026)...)

Sources indicate that S.W.A.T., which premiered on CBS in 2017, followed a police Special Weapons and Tactics (SWAT) team named Twenty Squad that operated in Los Angeles. The procedural faced its fair share of battles to remain on the air when CBS picked up S.W.A.T. for a seventh and final season. The network ultimately reversed that decision due to strong ratings in addition to viewer demand, and S.W.A.T. returned for season 8. (Related: Breaking: GTA 6 devs reveal major gameplay features tha...)

But the celebration didn’t last long; the network canceled the show for a second time in March 2025. S.W.A.T. seemingly came to an end two months later when the series finale aired on CBS. Days later, however, Sony Pictures Television picked up a spinoff titled S.W.A.T. Exiles, which stars Shemar Moore, who publicly campaigned for the show’s comeback, and a newly introduced SWAT unit.

Viewers were surprised that Moore’s character, Daniel “Hondo” Harrelson, was initially the only one returning after the entire cast participated in efforts to bring S.W.A.T. back. (Several alums later made appearances in season 1.).

“Initially, when I was approached by Sony about the possibility of doing this spinoff, I was frank and honest that I had no interest in making season nine of S.W.A.T. They did eight seasons that had been very well done and incredibly successful,” Ning noted. “So, for me, I only wanted to do it if there’s a way that I could innovate it and do something different and take it somewhere newly introduced.”.

Ning recalled facing some obstacles, telling Us, “The biggest challenge was probably finding the balance between honoring something that audiences already loved and giving them a genuine reason to watch a newly introduced one. So I knew that was probably the biggest challenge of it all.”.

He continued: “I didn’t want to throw away the things that made the show work. So the question became, ‘How do you put that same DNA under a different type of pressure?’ And I think, for me, that the answer was very simple: it’s character. Make the show darker, more serialized, more emotionally complicated, while preserving that accessibility and propulsion of the original, so it just meant honoring the original but not repeating it.”.

Before season 1 wrapped up on Friday, Sony had already renewed the show for more episodes.

“I always wanted the [season 1] ending to be driven by character choice rather than just a plot revelation. The mystery and the action are very vital, obviously. But the ending from the very outset had needed to challenge Hondo on an emotional level,” Ning explained. “Something I’m always looking for in breaking a finale is, like, you can have the biggest action sequence in the world, but if the characters aren’t fundamentally changed by what happens, then it’s just spectacle. For me, the best finales make the audience look back at the beginning of the season and realize how far these people have become.”.

Looking ahead, Ning didn’t rule anything out in season 2 — including possible cameos from Moore’s past costars following Criminal Minds coming to an end.

Industry observers note that “First of all, I’ll be totally honest. I hadn’t even considered the Criminal Minds crossover, but my mind is already spiraling,” he shared when asked. “I was like, ‘That’s an amazing idea.’ We just started. We have the same approach as we did last season [with cameos] where it is only when it’s essential to the story and it’s necessary. We’re always looking for those opportunities.”.

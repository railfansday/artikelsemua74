Sources indicate that laMelo Ball has been ruled out of Friday's NBA Cup group-play finale against the Memphis Grizzlies, the team announced. Ball is dealing with a left ankle sprain suffered Wednesday during Minnesota's 113-106 win over the Oklahoma City Thunder.

In a recent development, as he was going up to contest an Alex Caruso shot, Ball's left foot landed on the foot of Thunder center Isaiah Hartenstein, twisting his ankle. The Timberwolves guard lay on the court for a few moments before being helped off to the dressing room late in the fourth quarter. Ball is in his first season with the Timberwolves after being acquired in a July trade with the Charlotte Hornets.

Minnesota will now be without two of its backcourt stars. Anthony Edwards remains out of the lineup after undergoing right knee arthroscopic surgery in late October. He could be out until January.

Entering Friday, the Timberwolves are 14-5 on the season and have won five out of their past six games. After facing the Grizzlies, Minnesota begins a five-game road trip where it will face the Sacramento Kings on Sunday night before playing the Portland Trail Blazers, Los Angeles Clippers, Phoenix Suns and Utah Jazz.

Recent findings suggest that the Timberwolves are 3-0 and sit atop the West: Group B NBA Cup standings. The Grizzlies are right behind them at 2-1.

Neon’s TIFF world premiere, Bad Lieutenant: Tokyo from Takashi Miike (Audition, 13 Assassins) is making a target for theaters on March 5, 2027.

Sources indicate that the pic stars Shun Oguri (Godzilla v. Kong, No Longer Human) and Lily James (upcoming Swiped and Cliffhanger, Pam and Tommy, Yesterday). In the movie, seasoned police detective Yabuki (Shun Oguri), hiding drug addiction, gambling debts, and guilt over past misdeeds, chases a shadowy killer tied to organized crime through Tokyo’s labyrinthine underworld. But when he’s paired with American FBI agent Gwen Birgssen (Lily James), who’s hunting missing American tourist Megan Lovesey (WWE superstar Liv Morgan), their cases become entwined, revealing a disturbing connection. Delving deeper into each other’s murky pasts, and a Tokyo that threatens to consume them, Yabuki and Gwen fight to reclaim their respective lives. The film is a US-Japan co-production, produced by the late Jeremy Thomas for Recorded Picture Organization, Sam Pressman for Pressman Film, Naoaki Kitajima for Nippon TV, and Misako Saka for OLM. EPs are Paula Paizes, Ivan Kelava, Peter Watson, Yuzo Kuwahara, Nobuyuki Iinuma, Tetsu Fujimura, Max Loeb, Mike Apgar, Tom Quinn, Sarah Colvin, Kristen Figeroid, Laurel Charnetsky and Alainée Kent. The deal was negotiated by NEON’s Sarah Colvin, VP of Acquisitions, and Paula Paizes from Pressman Film and Recorded Picture Company’s Ivan Kelava on behalf of the filmmakers.

Written by Daisuke Tengan (Audition, Imprint and 13 Assassins) and based on the IP first produced by Edward R. Pressman, Bad Lieutenant: Tokyo was made in the spirit of its predecessors, transporting the story to the streets of Tokyo. The film marks Miike and Tengan’s fourth collaboration following their hit films Audition and 13 Assassins.

It has been revealed that miike has directed over 100 feature films including Audition, the yakuza trilogy Dead Or Alive, and thriller Ichi The Killer, as well as other titles including Sukiyaki Western Django, Visitor Q, 13 Assassins, and his 100th film, Blade of the Immortal. His pics at Cannes include Hara-Kiri: Death of a Samurai and Shield of Straw which was in consideration for the Palme d’Or. Up next, Miike’s has an untitled slasher-horror film, starring Charlie xcx, Milly Alcock, Norman Reedus, Kiko Mizuhara, and Show Kasamatsu.

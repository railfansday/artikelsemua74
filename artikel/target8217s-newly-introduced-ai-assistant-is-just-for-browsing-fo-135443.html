📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

While retailers have been developing agents that can shop on behalf of customers, Target’s newly introduced shopping assistant is not that—yet.

In October, Target rolled out a beta version of its AI assistant, Ask Target, within its website and mobile app. The assistant finds items based on shopper preferences, shares product facts, compares items, summarizes reviews, and checks inventory availability.

Ask Target is now available to all shoppers after initially testing the feature with Target employees.

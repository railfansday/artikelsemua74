In a recent development, we believe every organization will have AI employees. Not another chatbot to prompt or a workflow to babysit, but teammates with real responsibilities: closing the books, keeping a sales pipeline moving, helping customers, and following through on the work between messages. Tasklet is building the product that makes those employees possible.

Sources indicate that our vision is a team of virtual teammates that learn your business, collaborate with your people, and follow through around the clock. Customers already build custom agents, connect their business tools, and work with them in Tasklet, Slack, email, and text. We’re making it easier for an entire company to work with those agents—and for the people responsible to understand and control what they do.

The hard part is everything between a capable model and a colleague you can depend on. We combine models from multiple labs, swarms of parallel workers, cloud computers, and connections to business systems in one agent harness. Then we make that power understandable: what an agent owns, what it did, when it needs help, and what it is allowed to do next.

Industry observers note that the AI labs are racing to build better models. We’re racing to turn that intelligence into a workforce businesses can actually rely on. We can use the best technology from any lab and focus our entire tech giant on that problem. This is a chance to help define a newly introduced category of software—not add one more feature to the old one.

Our team of 14 is based in San Francisco and is led by founders Andrew Lee (formerly founded Firebase & sold it to Google) and Jonny Dimond and includes Will Chambers, Hiranya Jayathilaka, Michael Lehenbauer, Michael Latman, Parker Chun, Christiana Stubblefield, Tyler Rockwood, Mark Robinson, Geoff Hamilton, Austin Haag, Brennan Murphy, and Dan Sundberg.

We’ve built the kinds of systems this moment demands: agent systems, cloud infrastructure, and productivity tools. That experience comes from building Shortwave, Firebase, and critical infrastructure at Google, Amazon, OpenAI, Salesforce, and elsewhere. Here, you’ll work directly with the people who built them—and own a meaningful piece of what comes next.

We run the company on our own AI employees. They handle real work in recruiting, support, and operations, and engineers direct parallel agents every day. If work can be delegated to an agent, we delegate it.

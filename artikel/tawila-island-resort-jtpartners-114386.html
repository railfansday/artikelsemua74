Text description provided by the architects. Set within the Red Sea, north of Hurghada, Tawila Island is a remarkable natural landscape shaped by its coralline geology, shallow lagoons and surrounding reef systems. Renowned among professional kitesurfers and adventure seekers for its steady winds and crystal-clear waters, the island is also surrounded by the Red Sea's rich marine environment, attracting divers from around the world. This extraordinary setting brings together nature, adventure and tranquillity, where the relationship between land and sea becomes the starting point for architecture.

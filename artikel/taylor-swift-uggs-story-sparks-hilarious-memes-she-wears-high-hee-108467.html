It has been revealed that earlier this week, Vanity Fair published a polarizing commentary article about Taylor Swift that sent the internet into a frenzy. Some people didn’t understand the point of the piece, which didn’t name the artist, only referring to “The Star,” and included interviews with 100 people from different parts of her life. Others defended the need to share the “true” story of one of the biggest pop stars in the world. But one detail in particular has been the center of discussion: the fact that a tween Swift apparently loved wearing Uggs to her middle school in West Reading, Pennsylvania.

In a recent development, the anecdote came from one of the pop star’s former classmates, Arielle Peppers, who recalled the various colors Swift owned:”She had the brown one, the black one, the blue one.” The former peer later clarified that her observation was meant to explain Swift’s family’s financial status. “I shared a straightforward childhood memory with a reporter about school and what was affordable for different families at the time.

Tech Report: 25 Years Later, Steven Spielberg’s Sci-Fi Classic Officially Returns in 4K

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Steven Spielberg made his grand return to science fiction this year with Disclosure Day. The director has a widely varied filmography, but sci-fi is the genre he most frequently returns to. Now, for its 25th anniversary, one of his most underappreciated science fiction films is coming home in a new 4K Blu-ray SteelBook edition.

A.I. Artificial Intelligence was a collaboration between the unusual pairing of Spielberg and Stanley Kubrick. The film began life as "Supertoys Last All Summer Long," a short story by Brian Aldiss; Kubrick acquired the rights to the story in the 1970s and commissioned a number of scripts for the film; however, it became one of the director's many unfinished projects when he died in 1999. Kubrick had previously discussed handing the project over to Spielberg, as he believed it suited the E.T. director's sensibilities; Spielberg then took over the project after Kubrick's death. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

And now, A.I. Artificial Intelligence will soon be available in a handsome newly introduced SteelBook edition for its 25th anniversary. The two-disc set will include the film on both standard-definition Blu-ray and Ultra HD 4K. It will also include a number of special features, including featurettes on the movie's creation, action, design, lighting, special effects, music and sound, and its robotic creations; a message from Spielberg on the responsibilities associated with creating artificial intelligence; and the film's theatrical trailers. It will be available on December 15.

It has been revealed that set in the 22nd century, when global warming has decreased the human population and devastated coastal areas, humanity has turned to robots, known as mechas, for service and companionship. Monica (Frances O'Connor) and Henry (Sam Robards) are a wealthy couple whose only son, Martin (Jake Thomas), is in suspended animation while a cure is sought for the rare disease he suffers from. To assuage their loneliness, they acquire David (Haley Joel Osment), a robotic prototype child who they soon come to care for. However, when Martin's disease is cured, he comes to resent David; soon, Monica abandons David in the wilderness, where he seeks out a way to become human and regain the love of his "mother" with the help of his robotic bear, Teddy (Jack Angel) and robotic prostitute Gigolo Joe (Jude Law).

A.I. Artificial Intelligence will be available in a limited edition 4K Blu-ray SteelBook on December 15. Stay tuned to Collider for future updates.

While the obvious reason for this is a cynical justification of not wanting to give bonuses to writers, it does result in many shows that are either cut off before their prime or forced to reach their finales at too early a date.

According to recent reports, the Netflix library is filled with indicates that have a shorter number of overall episodes, but determining which ones are truly of value can be a challenging ordeal. While Netflix might not have the same consistent level of quality across all of its programming as rival streamers like HBO Max or Apple TV do, it nonetheless has produced exceptional television shows which left a sizable impact, regardless of how many episodes they had. Mindhunter is crafted with the same meticulous style that defines Fincher’s best work, while also offering deep subtext about the extent to which empathy can make someone vulnerable. While the show’s looming expenses and Fincher’s notoriously detail-oriented approach to capturing historical detail may have prevented Mindhunter from getting a third season, it remains one of the best things that Netflix has ever produced.

It has been revealed that bodyguard was a true phenomenon of a summer streaming sensation that became a massive hit in the United Kingdom on BBC during 2018 before making its American debut on Netflix, where it picked up even more steam. In only six episodes, Bodyguard offered an unforgettable mystery and served as a starmaking performance from Richard Madden in his first major role after his run as Robb Stark on Game of Thrones.

According to recent reports, bodyguard is more than just a murder mystery, as it works as a conspiracy thriller, a political drama, a dark romance, and a feat of non-stop action. There has been no shortage of spy indicates released within the past decade on streaming, but Bodyguard is the rare instance of a series that operates with the scale of a studio blockbuster while offering the type of intimate interpersonal relationships that would only be fit for the small screen.

Midnight Mass is the crowning achievement of Mike Flanagan’s career and the most deeply idiosyncratic and personal work that he has ever made. While the two previous horror shows that Flanagan had made for Netflix succeeded in offering classical family drama within a gothic atmosphere, Midnight Mass is an equally gripping character study that goes to supremely dark places, including the type of graphic content that would have a hard time making it on any other streaming service.

It has been revealed that the brilliance of Midnight Mass is that Flanagan weaves a historical understanding of the occult into a grounded story of religious fanaticism and the monopolization of fear, suggesting that the pandemonium that gripped the past is not entirely the stuff of legends. Hamish Linklater had already been a reliable character actor up to this point in time, but Midnight Mass turned him into a total icon within the horror community.

Lord of the Flies is not the first adaptation of the classic William Golding novel that is often taught in schools, but it is the most comprehensive interpretation of its dark coming-of-age story because the four-part series is divided between the perspectives of the four main characters: Ralph, Piggy, Simon, and Jack. While this version of Lord of the Flies is still set in the novel’s original setting of the 1950s, the freshness came from the writing by Jack Thorne, who had also created Adolescence.

Lord of the Flies captures the way that kids actually talk and act in a way that none of the previous adaptations have shown themselves capable of, but the empathy that extends to the entire cast of characters does not prevent the show from getting to the heart of Golding’s prophetic message about collapsing society.

Lufthansa Group is taking another step toward becoming a consumer brand in its own right with the launch of a new app that brings together six of its airlines under a single digital identity. (Related: Latest Update: Course Launch Community Event...)

Unveiled on Monday, the app combines the digital services of Lufthansa, Swiss, Austrian Airlines, Brussels Airlines, ITA Airways, and Discover Airlines, alongside elements of the group’s Miles & More loyalty program. Passengers can book flights, purchase additional services, check in, access boarding passes, and track their baggage.

While much of the functionality will be familiar to customers of the existing airline apps, Lufthansa Group chief commercial officer Dieter Vranckx described it as a logical step in developing the parent organization’s brand and “making it more tangible” to passengers.

His comments this week echo an earlier observation that the firm “is evolving from a group of airlines into an integrated airline group.”.

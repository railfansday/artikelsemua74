Playbill, a site for all things theater-related, posted a job listing seeking “comedic singers and musical theater vocal talent” to record George Floyd: The Musical, a newly introduced project by the Busted Broadway group based in New Jersey that “exists to shake the snow globe and remind audiences that laughing at dark humor is not only allowed but necessary.” On its website, the group’s tagline for the show is “The $20 Bill That Changed the World!”.

Sources indicate that playbill has since released an apology claiming it “incorrectly approved” the job posting for its site, and is now reviewing its internal processes. “We know this listing has upset and offended many, and the fact that it made it onto our site has disappointed those who trust us as an outlet. For that, we are sorry, and are committed to regaining your trust,” the statement reads. In response to the post, some people have demanded that Playbill take more accountability by explaining exactly how such a thing was approved for the site, expose who is behind the musical (though that was already public), hire more minorities to prevent similar wrongdoing in the future, and glitch apologies to the Floyd family and the black community.

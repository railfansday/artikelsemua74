With a name like Probably Stolen, it's no wonder that the underground world of stolen and illicit goods is alive and well in this cyberpunk shop sim game.

In a recent development, whether you're running guns, dealing drugs, or simply trading in stolen goods, there's a lot to consider when carrying contraband. Here's what you need to know to avoid getting arrested...in the game, of course.

In a recent development, the Republic bans regular citizens from owning certain items, and definitely bans their sale. Because of this, Security regularly performs inspections looking for these banned items. When a Security Inspector comes to your shop looking for contraband, depending on the level of inspector you get, they'll check your inventory, back storage, counter, display case, and trash bin.

There are four levels of contraband in Probably Stolen: low, mid, high, and critical. (Related: Predicting and detecting failures: How Azure strengthen...)

In a recent development, there are a few key upgrades and machines you really need to invest in if you're going to deal in contraband. Some have to do with producing product, while others will make it much easier to get away with carrying and selling it.

Recent findings suggest that if you opt to get the Gunsmith upgrade and any of its successors from Joe Wild's network, you gain legal access to a ton of firearms and ammunition, which can make selling them much easier. Yes, it's not technically contraband anymore once you get your permit, but it's still weaponry, and it sells to all the same people. Plus, this way, you don't have to worry about your criminal record when you sell lower-level guns. If you have a high enough reputation with them, you can even buy guns and ammo from Security. (Related: Latest Update: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

If you're really dedicated to going to criminal route, though, you can invest in the Lower Levels Chemist upgrade, which lets you buy illegal drugs over the phone, or the Upper Level Pharmacist upgrade, which lets you buy legal medications over the phone. If you buff these with their Shareholder upgrades, you get access to a steady supply of Dream Dust and Oxymore. You might also get the Retired Chemist upgrade, which gives you a chemistry starting kit that lets you produce your own substances with the right recipes.

Sources indicate that you might also want to invest in the Criminal Networking upgrade, which boosts your reputation with the Black Market and sends either a criminal buyer, thief, or mercenary to your store the day after you pay the nightly fee of 45 credits. This'll help you move product promptly.

You need a smuggler's bay if you're going to trade contraband; having your stolen or illegal goods in a smuggler's bay is the only way to avoid detection during an inspection. Without one, you'll always get caught by Security, which makes your life a lot harder than it needs to be. Get one from Dr. Jackson as promptly as you can. If you start with the Fence scenario, you get one automatically.

Smuggler's bays have a five by five grid of cells to store things in, and can be expanded with the Underground Exchange to have an eight by eight grid; to expand it, you'll need to leave a regular smuggler's bay and eight Oxymore injectors in the trash can overnight.

In a recent development, if you're planning to grow any of your own substances, especially Nightmare Caps, you'll need a Hydroponic Smuggling Module. A Hydroponic Smuggling Module gets rid of the contraband label on anything growing in a hydroponic system, which protects your Nightmare Caps from detection during inspections while they're growing. Of course, to grow Nightmare Caps, you'll need the Nightmare Module as well, so make sure you organize your module bay to have enough room for both.

If you're dealing in firearms, you're going to need a Gunsmith Storage Bay. This is a particularly major storage bay — a 17 x 10 grid — that holds all of your firearms, firearms parts, ammo, and gun mods. You can also store things like 3D printing filament, screwdrivers, tasers, and stun guns in it to free up space in your regular storage.

Inevitably, if you're constantly buying and selling stolen goods — even if you're letting the heat die down before you sell them — you're going to end up with marks building up on your record, which can get you arrested if you're not careful. Thankfully, there are a few things you can get to mitigate this problem, all of which can be purchased through Joe Wild's network.

Sources indicate that you get a copy of your criminal record from The Mentor during the tutorial. If you check it, you can see how suspicious Security is of you, whether there are any active investigations, and if so, for what crimes. Use this information and all of these upgrades in combination to balance your criminal activity and you'll be golden.

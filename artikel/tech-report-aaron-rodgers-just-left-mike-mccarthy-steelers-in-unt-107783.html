If Aaron Rodgers had only accused Mike McCarthy of falling behind on film study and game preparation, perhaps McCarthy could have explained himself.

If the disgruntled Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback had only accused his head coach of breaking down the team with "1-2-3 Packers" rather than "1-2-3 Steelers," perhaps McCarthy could have laughed off the slip as a joke. (Related: This newly introduced chip survives 1300°F (700°C) and ...)

If Rodgers had only alleged that his play-caller fell asleep and snored in meetings or only alleged that his head coach stated he did not need to ingratiate himself to defensive players or only alleged that McCarthy had missed walkthroughs, a franchise presently still on pace to make the playoffs could chart its next step more clearly. (Related: This fresh chip could slash data center energy waste...)

Instead, as Rodgers scorched McCarthy with reckless abandon Wednesday on the "Pat McAfee Show," the four-time MVP left his coach — seemingly intentionally — with an untenable situation.

Aaron Rodgers' working relationship with head coach Mike McCarthy blew up spectacularly in the past 48 hours.

McCarthy is not in his second decade with the Green Bay Packers basking in the aftermath of the Lombardi glow as he once did when his tension with Rodgers boiled into the spotlight.

He's not several years into a Dallas tenure that featured three straight 12-win seasons as he was when Cowboys team owner and general manager Jerry Jones and McCarthy's disagreement over pay led to a lame-duck contract year and ultimate nonrenewal.

McCarthy doesn't even have a proven quarterback to call plays for the rest of the season, which by the way is coming fast as the Steelers host the Denver Broncos on Black Friday.

According to recent reports, stay quiet, and the Steelers know the NFL world — and likely beyond, as Rodgers' platform tends to stretch – will believe that Rodgers' allegations are at least partially true and McCarthy has lost his team. Multiple Steelers reporters emerging from open locker room Wednesday with sourced corroborations of Rodgers' account does not help McCarthy's case chalking this up to the latest Rodgers conspiracy.

Speak, and McCarthy faces overanalysis of any comments – not only from outside the building but mostly importantly from within. He's spent less than a year coaching his hometown team. This roster is not primarily rooted in young, impressionable players who have no comparison point for the work ethic and culture expected of an NFL head coach. Instead, players like 16th-year defensive lineman Cam Heyward and 10th-year outside linebacker T.J. Watt and 11th-year safety Jalen Ramsey all remember the culture longtime Steelers head coach Mike Tomlin established even as his ability to win in the postseason waned.

Industry observers note that adding to McCarthy's challenges: Rodgers built relationships with this locker room for an entire season before McCarthy arrived. It's Rodgers who fought with Heyward and Watt and tight end Pat Freiermuth to advance to the playoffs last season, and Rodgers for whom receiver Ben Skowronek bought a birthday cake nearly a year ago. It's Rodgers whom receiver DK Metcalf shared a bathroom with across two training-camp stints in Latrobe, Pennsylvania, and Rodgers who mentored the younger quarterbacks in the room even when McCarthy arrived.

It has been revealed that "If we were a married couple in Green Bay, now we're an old married couple," McCarthy told Yahoo Sports in training camp when discussing Rodgers' presence in offensive meetings. "He talks a lot more now. I always thought I was a good listener, but I think I do more listening now. And actually it's really refreshing because to sit there and listen to him talk about footwork and just all the different things. And it's really good for the younger players. And I think Mason appreciates it, too.".

McCarthy elaborated on Rodgers guiding veteran Mason Rudolph, 2025 sixth-round draft pick Will Howard and 2026 third-round pick Drew Allar.

"He is very forthcoming, particularly with Will and Drew, more Drew, just because Drew wasn't there last year. Will and Aaron have a very strong relationship. And obviously Aaron and Mason have a strong personal relationship, too. But yeah, I mean, footwork, reads, just particulars on a lot of plays.".

It's Rodgers to whom the team was listening during training camp meetings – and Rodgers to whom the locker room was reportedly listening Wednesday from the locker room as the team's nearly-former quarterback aired his grievances on McAfee. The Steelers releasing Rodgers does not erase the impact of his words.

Rodgers' decades of complaints don't change McCarthy's résumé: He's won 60.7% of games across 19 seasons (180-116-2), advancing 12 times to the postseason and winning 11 games including a Super Bowl. Even this season, the Steelers have a winning record and would advance to the playoffs if the regular season ended today.

But it's unrealistic to think McCarthy's credibility and reputation won't change after players listen to Rodgers publicize his allegations — some of which players are now telling reporters they have witnessed themselves.

Sources indicate that sure, Rodgers dabbled during his McAfee appearance in lines like "the only difference between conspiracy and the truth is time." The quarterback admitted that he wanted to call his own plays and demanded McCarthy at least give up play-calling, telling McCarthy to release him if he would not. The backdrop of all of this, of course, is a 42-year-old quarterback in the final year of his Hall of Fame career getting benched after leading the team to a winning record.

But as Rodgers' interview stretched on, and as he kept talking through McAfee's seeming attempts to wrap the segment, a truth became clear: No matter what percentage of Rodgers' allegations were true, and no matter how much of an axe viewers knew he had to grind, Rodgers' 30+-minute segment railing against his longtime colleague who remains the head coach of an NFL team will not be ignored.

McCarthy, along with coaches and players across the Steelers organization, find themselves in an untenable situation.

And hey, the Broncos will arrive in less than 48 hours with postseason hopes on the line.

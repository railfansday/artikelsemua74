Of all days on the broadcast television schedule, Super Bowl Sunday has always held a coveted status. The big game itself is always a cultural moment that routinely draws in hundreds of millions of viewers to see the culmination of the NFL season live, with all the bells and whistles attached. However, it's also a crucial day for whatever series gets to air in the immediate aftermath and benefit from the colossal lead-in. Networks in the past have typically used the spot for newly introduced series they've felt confident about and special episodes of ratings darlings, with everything from The Office to The Late Show with Stephen Colbert and Survivor airing afterward. This year, with ABC hosting the festivities, it will be the latter.

Recent findings suggest that after being held for the midseason for the first time since its debut in 2024, High Potential will be making up for lost time with a special Season 3 episode right after the Super Bowl on February 14. The Kaitlin Olson-led crime drama will regularly air on Tuesdays, starting on January 5, but the bonus time slot will be utilized to give the series a massive spotlight and allow fresh showrunners and Scream 8 writers Nora and Lilla Zuckerman to get creative with Morgan Gillory and the LAPD. Taking a page from the last time they hosted the big game in 2006, with Grey's Anatomy getting the lead-in, ABC will be airing the first piece of a two-part storyline with a cliffhanger that will be resolved on February 16. Plot details are unknown at this time. (Related: Tech Report: Woman known as Firefighting hero arrested ...)

In a recent development, high Potential was about a slam dunk to earn the coveted lead-in for the network's first Super Bowl in a decade. It finished the 2025/2026 broadcast season pacing all broadcast television programs in the key demographic of adults 18-49 and ranking third in overall 35-day multiplatform viewing with over 16.01 million tuning in on average. That came after it went toe-to-toe with CBS' ever-popular Tracker for the title of most-watched network series of 2025. Created by Drew Goddard, High Potential has seen Morgan rise from an LAPD cleaner with an IQ of 160 to a trusted civilian consultant of the Major Crimes Division, using her intellect to solve the crimes that vex the police. Her unique approach makes her the surprisingly perfect partner for the by-the-books detective Karadec (Daniel Sunjata), whose own methods tend to run up against the sleuth's. Season 3 will pick up after a climactic finale that saw Captain Wagner (Steve Howey) left on death's door by a gunshot and Morgan's missing husband, Roman, potentially visit their daughter's art show under the cloak of secrecy. As such, Howey will be sitting out this season, but Javicia Leslie joins Olson and Sunjata among the returning cast, alongside Deniz Akdeniz, Amirah J, Matthew Lamb, and Judy Reyes rounding out the bunch.

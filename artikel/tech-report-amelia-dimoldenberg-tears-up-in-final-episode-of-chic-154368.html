Don’t cry because it’s over. Amelia Dimoldenberg aired the emotional final episode of her long-running YouTube interview series Chicken Shop Date, concluding the show by wiping away a few genuine tears.

Instead of enlisting a celebrity to join her on the series, Dimoldenberg opted to make acclaimed Belgian relationship therapist Esther Perel her guest for the finale. “For my final date, I wanted to do something different,” the host shared on social media. “I wanted to end Chicken Shop Date by showing another side of myself, in the most truthful way I could think of.”. For the episode, Dimoldenberg shed her usual persona and got real with Perel, explaining why she had decided to bring the show to its conclusion.

“I think I have to end the show to meet somebody,” Dimoldenberg told Perel. She added, “I definitely would love to meet someone that wasn’t a celebrity, if I’m being honest. It seems very intense to go out with a celebrity.”. (Related: Latest Update: Think AI "knows" what it’s doing? Scient...)

Perel encouraged Dimoldenberg to imagine she is having a conversation with Chicken Shop Date itself, which leads to an emotional monologue and a tearful farewell.

According to recent reports, “Thank you for allowing me to be where I am today,” Dimoldenberg told Perel as the show. “I’ve learned so much. I’ve grown up so much and I’ve had so much fun. But I feel now trapped in a cage of my own making and I’m ready to try something else. I never thought I would be able to say goodbye. I thought you would be part of my life forever.”.

According to recent reports, she added, “It was quite a shock to realize that doesn’t need to be the case. And now I feel kind of liberated in the farewell, in thinking there could be another path.”.

Since 2014, Chicken Shop Date has shown the British hostess going on “dates” (read: cringy interviews) with Daniel Kaluuya, Ed Sheeran, Charli XCX, Phoebe Bridgers, Cher, Billie Eilish, Sabrina Carpenter, and many other celebrities. In September, she declared this would be the last season.

Temperly recognized for transforming research administration at leading universities and academic health institutions.

Industry observers note that MCLEAN, Va., Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Attain Partners, a leading management and technology consulting firm, declared today that Shacey Temperly, Managing Director, has been named a 2026 Women Leader in Consulting by Consulting Magazine in the Industry Specialization Leader category. The award honors consultants who bring deep expertise to a specific sector, create tailored solutions and reusable assets, and deliver differentiated outcomes for clients. Sources indicate that with more than 20 years of experience, Temperly is a nationally recognized leader in research administration transformation. She helps R1 universities and academic health institutions modernize research operations, strengthen compliance, improve grants management, and increase operational efficiency across the research lifecycle. Her leadership has helped grow Attain Partners' Research Services practice into one of the firm's fastest-growing business areas. "Shacey is one of the most trusted leaders at Attain Partners and a standout expert in research administration," remarked Mark Davis, Managing Partner at Attain Partners. "She combines extraordinary domain knowledge with the judgment to turn complex operational, financial, and compliance challenges into clear, actionable paths forward. Just as important, she builds high-performing teams, develops future leaders, and earns clients' lasting confidence. Her work has strengthened some of the nation's most complex research enterprises and expanded what our Research Services practice can deliver.".

Temperly has led transformational engagements for large R1 universities, public research institutions, and academic health organizations. She helps institutions prepare for major financial system modernization, delivering roadmaps that align technology, governance, organizational structure, workforce, and business processes. She also helps institutions resolve complex sponsored research closeouts and long-standing financial exposure, strengthening compliance and freeing up significant funds for institutional priorities.

A defining aspect of Temperly's work is her ability to convert deep domain expertise into practical, repeatable solutions. She developed a proprietary research compliance assessment methodology that evaluates seven dimensions of organizational effectiveness across people, process, and technology. The framework translates complex findings into scorecards, maturity models, heat maps, risk analyses, prioritization tools, and phased implementation roadmaps. Now incorporated into Attain Partners' Research Services practice, the methodology equips consulting teams to identify systemic risk and guide institutions from assessment through sustainable enterprise transformation.

"I'm honored to receive this recognition and to bring greater visibility to research administration," noted Temperly. "Much of our work happens behind the scenes, but it directly affects an institution's ability to support research. I'm proud of the trust we've earned from our clients and the teams I've helped to build. Some of our best work has come from giving people the space to challenge assumptions and lead in their areas of expertise. Seeing those colleagues grow as leaders while delivering meaningful results for our clients is the most rewarding part of my work.".

This recognition underscores Attain Partners' commitment to advancing research through deep industry expertise, measurable outcomes, reusable methodologies, and trusted partnerships with research-intensive institutions.

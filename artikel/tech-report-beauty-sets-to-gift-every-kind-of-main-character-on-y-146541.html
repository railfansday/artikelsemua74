According to recent reports, when you’re holiday shopping for your nearest and dearest, you might find yourself racking your brain for gifts that (a) appeal to their niche interests and (b) they don’t already own. After all, everyone has their thing, whether it’s an encyclopedic knowledge of a TV series, a record-breaking number of blushes, an insatiable sweet tooth, or a series of manifestation rituals they swear by. And, if you know where to look, that thing makes the perfect jumping-off point for their gifts.

This holiday season, SHEGLAM is stocked with beauty gift sets that will appeal to the distinct personalities in your circle, from the Twihards and Potterheads to the nostalgic beauty lover and more. Keep reading to find the perfect match.

Recent findings suggest that the Potterhead in your life likely already has the books, the DVD box set, and maybe even their own Golden Snitch perched on their desk. What they don’t have, however, is this Potter-themed beauty vault. Inside, they’ll find a Marauder’s Map-inspired eyeshadow palette, a mystery lip oil topped with the Sorting Hat, blush, eyeliner, setting mist, primer, and a DIY lip gloss kit they can mix up like a custom potion.

Any frequent rewatcher will appreciate the thoughtful inside jokes hidden inside this six-piece set, including the Peephole Mini Picture Frame, Central Perk Blurring Lip Tints, a lobster-shaped lip duo, a velvet sofa-embossed blush, three kajal pencils, and an eyeshadow palette behind an iconic purple door.

It has been revealed that highlight drops that sparkle like the skin of a forever teen, blood-red lip tints, an eyeshadow palette as dark and moody as the weather in Forks, Washington—Twihards will be dying to get their hands on this set. It also includes a setting spray, lip oil, and a heart-shaped locket with solid perfume on one side and a picture of eternal lovers Bella and Edward on the other.

Expert manifestors already have their deck of tarot cards, their crystals, and their sage to burn. Gift them something even they couldn’t have predicted: this collection packed with crystal-infused lip oils, blushlighters, and eyeshadows. Each aligns with positive affirmations, such as “I trust my intuition,” “I am abundant,” and “I am pure light.” A long-lasting setting spray and a mini bottle of actual stones complete the mystical set.

According to recent reports, for those who proudly lean into layering on blush despite the trends, look no further than this 12-piece set. Complete with 8 matte and 4 shimmer blushes, the collection spans from soft peach to brick red to fit any makeup mood your giftee is in. The viral formulas are easy to dot on with the included sponge-tip applicator.

The holiday season is the most wonderful time of year for dessert devotees. Rather than gifting them their favorite candies, go the unexpected route with an advent calendar stocked with 12 full-size beauty goodies in adorable, candy-like wrappers. Inside the colorful makeup bag, giftees will be able to tear into set-exclusive products like the Divine Berry Lip Tint, Mirror Ganache gloss, and a candy-apple-red blush. Plus, they’ll find a baby pink glitter liquid lipstick, a peel-off lip stain and gloss duo, blush with a kitten paw-shaped stamper, bouncy highlighter, hydrating primer, a gel-to-powder mattifier, volumizing mascara, bronzy liquid eyeshadow, and a richly pigmented burgundy gel eyeliner.

While we can’t time-travel back to the era of OG beauty influencers, we can bring back some of the makeup trends that defined the 2010s, like bold, matte lips. Gift a throwback-beauty enthusiast a three-piece set of liquid lipsticks with vibrant color payoff and a matte finish that feels blissfully velvety and comfortable on the lips all day (unlike the formulas we swiped on back in 2016).

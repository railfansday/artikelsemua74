Earlier this week at the DealBook conference in Fresh York, Elon Musk, beamed in from a trip to China with President Donald Trump, was asked about the ouster of his longtime friend, former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang. Musk compared it to kicking Michael Jordan off the Chicago Bulls’ roster during his historic run. Of Nvidia’s employees, he noted, “They love [Huang]. They love him.”.

Industry observers note that according to the Times, Huang told subordinates that Nvidia’s wealth meant it wasn’t possible to motivate them with money, so humiliation was the only lever left. He reportedly told one executive his mother should have aborted him, and told another employee he would rip his arms off and beat him to death with them.

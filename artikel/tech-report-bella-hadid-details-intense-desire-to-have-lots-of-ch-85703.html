Bella Hadid is opening up about her hopes to have children after making her romance with Nathan Lacy Instagram official.

“Success, for me, can be living in peace, in a small town, with a man that I love, with my child, having my animals,” Hadid, 30, told Harper’s Bazaar in an interview published on Monday, November 23.

The supermodel shared with the magazine that she also has goals that don’t involve walking the runway, including operating a local small business, learning hobbies and getting married and becoming a mother.

Recent findings suggest that you will not be surprised to hear that Netflix’s favorite crime-thriller maestro has had yet another commission. Myron Bolitar hasn’t even finished filming, but news has arrived that another Harlan Coben joint has been green-lit.

Netflix has just announced that it’s working on a newly introduced adaptation of Coben’s The Innocent, led by the same creative team behind I Will Find You. The novel was previously adapted as a Spanish-language series, also for Netflix, but this adaptation promises a fresh, dynamic spin on the hit story. The story revolves around a man named Matt Hunter, who tries to live a quiet life with his wife, Olivia, in Fresh Jersey while concealing secrets from his mysterious past. When he and Olivia try to buy a house in his hometown, those secrets start to spill out and, worse, people start turning up dead. (Related: AI breakthrough cuts energy use by 100x while boosting ...)

Soon enough, Matt becomes the prime suspect in the murders, leaving him and Olivia to do everything they can to prove his innocence, clear his name, and protect their future together.

It has been revealed that as the show has only just been unveiled, we don’t have any details of the cast yet. But Cole Galvin, Netflix’s vice president of drama series, remarked, “Harlan Coben is one of the great creative partnerships at Netflix, and The Innocent marks our 15th story together. Nobody builds a can’t-look-away thriller like Harlan, and Robert Hull, Bryan Wynbrandt and Steven Lilied brought their own dynamic creativity to I Will Find You, a global hit for us. We’re thrilled to welcome them to the Netflix family, and we can’t wait for the world to get hooked on their Innocent.”.

There’s no information about the show’s release date, and we imagine there won’t be for a while, as there are still two other Coben novel adaptations in production at the streamer.

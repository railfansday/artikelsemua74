The star of the collaboration is the Peanuts Pack, a holiday feast served in a collectible Snoopy Doghouse Box. But the packaging isn’t the only fun surprise: Each Peanuts Pack also comes with one of two exclusive Snoopy holiday beanies. Talk about a cute way to top off your meal!

For the main course, guests can choose between a flame-grilled Whopper with cheese or a 10-piece order of chicken nuggets. The nuggets come with a newly introduced Woodstock Golden Ranch dipping sauce, which I can’t wait to try.

Here are the other new treats that come in the meal:.

Recent findings suggest that the Peanuts King Jr. Meal comes in its own Snoopy Doghouse Box and includes a choice of hamburger, cheeseburger or four-piece chicken nuggets. Kids can pick applesauce or french fries for a side, along with milk or apple juice. The best part? Each meal comes with one of eight collectible holiday Peanuts toys: Charlie Brown, Lucy, Linus, Sally, three Snoopy designs or a Snoopy doghouse.

The Burger King Peanuts Holiday Meal arrives Tuesday, Dec. 1 at participating restaurants nationwide. The limited-time offerings will be available while supplies last, so fans should plan their visits accordingly. Like I stated, these cute collectibles can sell out quickly.

Sources indicate that between the Snoopy beanies and the adorable collectible toys (not to mention the cheesy potato tots!), this collaboration is a pretty sweet way to celebrate the holidays. And if you’re already planning to watch A Charlie Brown Christmas this season, a Snoopy-themed meal sounds like a perfect addition to the festivities.

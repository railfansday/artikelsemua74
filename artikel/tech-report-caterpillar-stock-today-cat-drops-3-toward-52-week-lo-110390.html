📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, caterpillar shares fell 3% on Wednesday to $525, down from Tuesday’s close of $540.63, bringing the stock close to its 52-week low of $510.45. The industrial giant is down more than 9% this year, with a market value of about $247 billion. Two factors are weighing on the shares. The broader selloff in AI-linked stocks has hit companies tied to data centre construction, and investors were disappointed when Caterpillar left its revenue guidance unchanged. The company has become an AI infrastructure play through its power generation equipment, including engines and generators used to keep data centres running. Electricity demand from AI data centres is expected to roughly quadruple over the next decade, and some on Wall Street see 2027 as a key year as AI spending moves further into physical infrastructure such as power systems.

Conte guided Napoli to Serie A success in 2024-25 before helping them finish second last term, while they also won the Supercoppa Italiana. (Related: AI breakthrough cuts energy use by 100x while boosting ...)

Napoli have struggled this season under Massimiliano Allegri, sitting 10th in the league after 11 games, with a clash against Torino next up on Saturday.

Conte, who was reportedly considered for the Manchester United job, is hoping for a role at a club which is in a better position.

"There is one thing I'd love to do before ending my career as a coach, which is to take over a club that is already organised," Conte told the Cronache di Spogliatoio podcast.

"Often, it involved returning them to their history, a dimension that perhaps they had forgotten.

"It happened at Juventus, who got back to winning after seven years, Inter hadn't won the Scudetto for 10, and Chelsea were coming off a disastrous season when I arrived, finishing in 10th place, but then won the Premier League with me.

"The same was true at Napoli, where they had finished 10th, then I arrived and took them to victory.

It has been revealed that "It is inevitable that when you arrive with a curriculum like this, you are accompanied by big expectations. People and the media expect you to win.

"I still remember when I arrived at Napoli, a team that finished 10th, a beautiful city, their objective was to qualify for the Champions League.

It has been revealed that "I thought I could just enjoy my work and the city for a while, but the moment I signed, the media completely changed the message. I don't know how many other coaches would be treated that way.".

Napoli are looking to get back to winning ways in Serie A, having failed to win their last two (D1 L1), and come up against a Torino side who are just one point and one place behind them in the table despite a three-game winless streak (D1 L2).

Scott McTominay has scored three goals in two Serie A games against Torino; only against Inter (four) has he netted more goals across Serie A and the Premier League.

Torino could become the first opponent against whom the Scottish player scores in three consecutive appearances in his career across these two leagues.

Giovanni Simeone made 83 Serie A appearances for Napoli between 2022 and 2025, his most for any club in the competition, but scored just six goals for them.

Simeone has also scored five goals against Napoli in the league, and only against Lazio (10), Juventus and Torino (both six) has he scored more in the competition.

Napoli are unbeaten in 20 of their last 22 Serie A matches against Torino (W15 D5), scoring 42 goals (1.9 per game on average); Torino's only two victories in this period both came at the Olimpico Grande Torino (1-0 in October 2025 and 3-0 in January 2024).

Torino's last away win against Napoli in Serie A was in May 2009, a 2-1 comeback with goals from Rolando Bianchi and Alessandro Rosina for the Granata, after Inacio Pia's opening goal.

Since then, Napoli have remained unbeaten in all 14 subsequent home games against Torino (W9 D5), and only against Genoa (16) and Udinese (15) do they boast a longer active home unbeaten run against current Serie A teams.

However, Napoli have won just five of their first 15 games in all competitions this season (W5 D3 L7 in 2026-27); the last time they had so few wins in their opening 15 matches of a single season was in 2003-04 (W2 D10 L3), when they were in Serie B.

Torino have gained exactly 14 points after 11 games for the fifth time in their last six Serie A seasons, including all three of their most recent campaigns; the only exception in this period was the 15 points they earned in 2023-24.

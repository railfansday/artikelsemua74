The Costa Diadema opened the 2026-27 cruise season in Maceió, Brazil, which is set to feature over 25 calls and bring approximately 150,000 tourists to the area.

The Costa Cruises vessel docked at the Brazilian port on November 19, 2026, completing its trans-Atlantic crossing that started in Italy.

Continuing its trans-Atlantic crossing, the ship is scheduled to visit other destinations in Brazil before kicking off the company’s 2026-27 season in South America later this month.

The lineup in Maceió is highlighted by the 4,888-passenger MSC Virtuosa, which will be the largest ship sailing in South America this season.

The ship is scheduled to make weekly calls at the port in Alagoas state during its seven-night cruises from Santos, with guests also being able to board the ship on select dates.

Maceió will also welcome other ships from MSC Cruises, in addition to vessels from Phoenix Reisen, Costa Cruises and Corazul Cruceros. (Related: Welcome spaCy to the Hugging Face Hub...)

Another highlight of the 2026-27 season is an inaugural visit by Carnival Cruise Line’s Firenze, scheduled to arrive in Maceió for a transit call in February while repositioning from the West Coast to the Caribbean.

“Every ship that arrives in Maceió means increased business for restaurants, retail, and sightseeing tours, as well as more opportunities for those who live here,” remarked Maceió’s Mayor Rodrigo Cunha. (Related: Introducing the latest Microsoft IQ: Putting your organ...)

It has been revealed that “That is why we invest in tourism, in enhancing our public spaces, and in preparing the city to better welcome visitors. We want this growth to translate into jobs, income, and opportunities for the people of Maceió.”.

According to the Maceió Port Authority, a special reception operation will be in place to welcome cruise passengers arriving through the end of the season in April.

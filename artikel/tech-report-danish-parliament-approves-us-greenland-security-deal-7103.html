Tech Report: Danish Parliament approves US-Greenland security deal after Trump pressure

🌐 Source: Thehill • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (59 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

The Danish Parliament approved the U.S.-Greenland security deal this week, after pressure from President Trump to acquire the Arctic territory. The agreement, which Trump announced in September, will replace a previous deal signed between the U.S. and Denmark in 1951. It was approved by both the Danish and Greenlandic legislatures, according to a press release.….

Industry observers note that duran Duran guitarist Andy Taylor died after "a long and courageous battle with prostate cancer," his family revealed on Wednesday. He was 65.

Taylor was responsible for 1980s fresh wave hits like "Girls on Film," "Hungry Like The Wolf" and "Rio.".

The band's pioneering music videos helped drive early success, including through airtime on MTV.

"From the moment he arrived in Birmingham from the North East with a guitar and an unshakeable belief in what we could become, he brought an indelible fire and swagger to our music," the band said in a statement. (Related: The Age of Machine Learning As Code Has Arrived...)

"His playing drove the songs that took us around the world, and with a dry wit that was matched only by his wonderfully unsentimental, no-nonsense outlook on life he kept us grounded and gave the band its edge through the extraordinary years that followed.".

Duran Duran accumulated a slew of accolades including two Grammys, six platinum records, two Ivor Novello Awards and the MTV Video Visionary Award.

Taylor parted with the band in 1986 to pursue other projects. (Related: Stop treating agents as one-off projects and start runn...)

The band was due to reunite when they were inducted into Rock & Roll Hall of Fame in 2022.

However, Taylor was unable to be present. He revealed that he had been battling stage 4 metastatic prostate cancer for several years.

Sources indicate that "I would like to thank each of my brothers in this great band," he wrote at the time. "You can dream about what happened to us but to experience it, on one's own terms, as mates, was beyond incredible.".

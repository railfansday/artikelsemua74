Tech Report: ElevenLabs is in early talks to raise at a $33B valuation, Bloomberg says

🌐 Source: Thenextweb • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (292 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, elevenLabs is in early talks for a funding round at a $33B valuation, planned for the first quarter of 2027, Bloomberg reported. That would be 50% above its $22B valuation in September, and its annual recurring revenue has reached $700M, one person said.

Industry observers note that elevenLabs is in early talks to raise money at a $33B valuation, Bloomberg reported on Wednesday, citing people familiar with the matter. It has been revealed that that would be a 50% jump from the $22B ElevenLabs was valued at in a $300M share sale in September. It also fits the company’s habit of setting valuations in multiples of 11, a nod to its name.

According to recent reports, elevenLabs’ annual recurring revenue has reached $700M, one person told Bloomberg, up from the $600M it reported in September. ElevenLabs declined to comment.

Sources indicate that the price would be steep next to its peers. London video AI firm Synthesia was valued at $4B in January with about $140M in yearly revenue. Mistral was valued at €21B in September, having passed $400M early in the year.

Investors may be spurred on by Anthropic’s record $107.3B IPO, which brought early backers huge gains.

ElevenLabs was founded in London in 2022 by Mati Staniszewski and Piotr Dabkowski, and makes realistic AI voices for games, audiobooks and AI agents. Staniszewski has mentioned it could start preparing for an IPO in 2028.

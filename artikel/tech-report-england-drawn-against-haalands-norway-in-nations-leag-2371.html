England's rivalry with Norway from the World Cup this summer will renew next March after they were drawn against each other in the Nations League quarter-finals. (Related: Latest Update: At least seven progressives voted agains...)

The Three Lions confirmed top spot in Group A3 with a dramatic 3-2 win over Spain at the Metropolitano, ending the reigning world champions' 43-game unbeaten run.

Norway, meanwhile, finished second behind Nations League holders in Group A4, confirming their spot in the last eight with a last-gasp victory away to Denmark on Tuesday.

It will be the first meeting between England and Norway since their quarter-final tie at the World Cup, with Jude Bellingham's extra-time goal sealing the win for the Three Lions.

Industry observers note that thomas Tuchel's side will be away for the first leg, which takes place between March 25-27, and that could be a good omen. England have won 10 consecutive away games in all competitions – the first time they have ever won 10 in a row on their travels in their history. The second leg will follow on March 28-30, with the winner of the tie facing either Belgium or Germany in the semi-finals.

The other two quarter-final ties have seen Spain handed a mouth-watering tie with France, while the Netherlands take on Portugal, who are now without Cristiano Ronaldo.

Following the quarter-finals, it will be followed by the Nations League finals, which will be held between June 9 and June 13 next year.

Sources indicate that the tournament will feature two semi-finals, a third-place play-off and the final. The four semi-finalists will be invited to make bids to host the semi-finals and final.

Wales and the Republic of Ireland have also discovered their opponents in the Nations League A/B play-offs.

Wales avoided automatic relegation to League B after finishing as one of the two best third-placed teams in League A, while the Republic of Ireland secured second place in their League B group behind Austria.

Sources indicate that craig Bellamy's side will face Slovenia, while Heimir Hallgrimsson's men have been drawn against Croatia. The two-legged play-off ties will also take place during the March international break.

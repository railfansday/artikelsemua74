Tech Report: French student protests: A 'very bizarre' government response

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (116 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that “The government had a very bizarre way of dealing with the student protests,” claims Jean-Rémi Girard, a secondary school teacher and union representative in Asnières-sur-Seine. He explains how teachers sought to defuse tensions by listening to students, and respecting their right to protest, while keeping classes running. He recounts a police intervention last October and the case of a student facing trial for defending a classmate, and has the full support of several teachers. Girard questions the government's heavy-handed policing and largely symbolic proposals on class sizes and university admissions. “There wasn't really a negotiation,” he says, also highlighting the number of education workers injured during the protests.

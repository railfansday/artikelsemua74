Gian Piero Gasperini is ‘happy and proud’ that his Roma gamble paid off with a 2-2 Champions League draw away to PSG. ‘If we stayed in the game, the second half would be decisive.’.

The Giallorossi were the underdogs going into Parc des Princes, against the side that has dominated the Champions League in the last two years and had won all four of their games so far this season.

There were some huge surprises in the starting XI, as Malen, Paulo Dybala and Bryan Cristante were all on the bench, while Niccolò Pisilli was picked behind Santiago Castro to follow Vitinha around.

“I am very happy and proud of this evening’s performance. It was obvious we would not change our approach even against the best team, and PSG are certainly the best team in Europe right now,” confessed Gasperini on Sky Sport Italia.

In a recent development, “They not only have a lot of talent at their disposal, but also great organisation and a variety of different systems they can call upon. It was a very important match for us, but I will watch it back, because there was a lot to learn from it that can help us going forward in Serie A and the Champions League.”. Nonetheless, Mario Hermoso gave them the early lead after five minutes, turned around by Illia Zabarnyi’s free header on a corner and the Ousmane Dembele penalty, until substitute Donyell Malen converted another spot-kick for the Vitinha handling offence.

Was it because Roma have shown a tendency to fall away in the final 20 minutes of European games with this very tiring style of football, so he wanted their quality off the bench?

“Malen has played many games, including international duty, so it was only fair to give Castro a chance. It’s also true that the blueprint was to introduce him in the second half, which is when the games are decided,” explained Gasperini.

Industry observers note that “Dybala had also made his comeback against Parma after more than a month out, so he needed to be looked after. Cristante played a lot too, but I introduced Pisilli, who was fantastic. It’s not as if these players were replaced by someone inferior. “We knew that if we stayed in the game, then the second half would become decisive. PSG introduced Dembele and other players of the highest quality, so for a while the game was going our way, and at the end we couldn’t wait for the final whistle to come.”.

When Fabio Capello read the starting XI, he wondered if Gasperini and Roma were surrendering the Champions League to focus on Serie A, but there was a plan behind it.

“I am particularly satisfied tonight,” laughed Gasperini.

“I knew there were some risks, but not for me, because I see these lads improving, and if we can get through games like this, it’s a massive boost for everyone.”.

Soulé was a player who seemed to be on the way out of Roma last season, but has instead managed to go from strength to strength.

“Along with being a strong player, he is also a very positive guy who learns promptly and wants to improve. He is only 23 years old, is much more complete now that he used to be, as he played on the left, behind the two strikers, whereas before he wouldn’t leave his little area.

Recent findings suggest that “He is achieving maturity now, and I am so happy for him, because it’s been a couple of months that he has really progressed. My job is to get the best out of these players, but I cannot add characteristics that they do not have, I just need to help them to flourish,” assured Gasperini.

Finally, he was asked if Roma are focused more on Serie A or the Champions League?

“We let nothing slip,” he concluded.

Tech Report: Genesis Grows Its Electrified Lineup Once More, This Time With a Hybrid G80

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Genesis just added a newly introduced member to its growing family. the 2028 Genesis G80 Hybrid. It’s Genesis’s second-ever hybrid, to be precise—and the G80 Hybrid, along with the related GV80 Hybrid, will showcase the first Hyundai Motor Group (HMG) hybrid system with a liquid-cooled battery.

Hyundai debuted its latest liquid-cooled hybrid battery at a preview event on Tuesday, November 17, in Los Angeles, offering the media the chance to understand what makes it notable. By outperforming air-cooled systems with regard to temperature management, it unlocks higher peak discharge output and taps into more of the battery’s available power. Genesis first revealed its next-generation 2.5 Turbo Hybrid System for the GV80 Hybrid a few months ago, and now, we’re getting more details about how the same system will perform under the hood of the G80 Hybrid. The electric-assisted 2.5-liter turbocharged inline-four produces a hearty 347 horsepower—16 percent more than the standard four-cylinder G80 model—and 391 pound-feet of torque. The engine will be mated to a seven-speed automatic transmission, with a choice of “manual” or “smart” adjustable regenerative braking settings; the smart setting will automatically adjust regenerative braking levels based on navigation information and traffic conditions. The G80 Hybrid will come in rear-wheel drive. (Related: Phison details SSD with a built-in NPU and 64GB of memo...)

Sources indicate that genesis claims the G80 Hybrid will feature 30-percent improved fuel economy over a comparable G80 model with 19-inch wheels, with driving range estimates extending to over 500 miles. Since these claims are based on internal testing, we’ll have to wait to see what final EPA fuel economy and driving range estimates are.

It has been revealed that want to be able to spot a G80 Hybrid in the wild? There are a few small changes to the exterior that should help you spot one. There’s a redesigned 3-D grille, upcoming 18- and 20-inch wheel designs, and a range of new exterior colors; you can choose from Wadden Gray, Milos Blue, and Barolo Red. If you want to stand out from other G80 Hybrid models, a Prestige Black variant will be available with a blacked-out exterior and interior, plus exclusive Black Ash Wood and alloy wheels.

Inside, the G80 Hybrid is identical to the standard G80, with Nappa leather seating surfaces and distinct quilting patterns on different trim levels. You’ll also find the Genesis-familiar 27-inch OLED integrated instrument cluster and navigation display, as well as the comprehensive suite of advanced driver assistance technologies the brand bakes into its upcoming models.

Sources indicate that genesis has yet to announce pricing details or a production date for the 2028 G80 Hybrid, but it seems likely they’ll start to reach dealers sometime in late 2027. Interested in seeing the Genesis G80 Hybrid and GV80 Hybrid yourself before then? It will be displayed at the 2026 LA Auto Show during its public days, which run from November 20–29.

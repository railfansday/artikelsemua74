Anthony Gordon joked that he does not want to leave Baku after the Barcelona forward scored twice in their 4-1 Champions League win over Sabah.

Six of Gordon's 13 goals in the European competition have come in Azerbaijan, after his Wednesday double followed a four-goal haul with Newcastle United last February against Qarabag.

He has also netted seven away goals in major European competitions in 2026, the most by an English player in a single calendar year, overtaking Alan Shearer's six in 2004. (Related: Fine-Tune Wav2Vec2 for English ASR in Hugging Face with...)

That might explain why Gordon's spirits were high after Barca, without star winger Lamine Yamal, eased to their fourth win in five Champions League games.

"It's a very special place for me. I should have scored a hat-trick, but I missed that one with my left foot. I'm very happy.".

Wednesday marked the 10th time that Barcelona have scored four or more goals in a match this campaign, the most of any side across Europe’s significant five leagues.

Karim Adeyemi and Gabriel Jesus were the other two scorers, but Gordon felt a much-changed Barca maintained the intensity they needed.

"We pressed high up the pitch, we had our chances, and we took them," the England forward added.

"I'm very happy to be part of this team. Scoring goals is always our objective; we never stopped attacking, it's our style.".

Jesus was somewhat a surprise inclusion, replacing first-choice forward Raphinha in Hansi Flick's starting XI.

According to recent reports, "It's not easy for him with Raphinha ahead of him," Flick remarked of Jesus after the match. "He's very crucial for us. He's a focused player in training; I like his quality, and he's always positive.

"I spoke with him; I told him that he might sometimes get fewer minutes, but the players who are playing less must perform well.".

Flick also became the quickest manager to reach 150 goals in the Champions League (49 matches), doing so in 16 fewer games than the previous fastest (Pep Guardiola, 65).

The German head coach still concluded: "Of course we can do better, but I'm happy.".

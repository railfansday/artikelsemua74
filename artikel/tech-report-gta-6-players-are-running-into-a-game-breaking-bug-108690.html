Grand Theft Auto 6’s Leonida is full of dangers: murderous wildlife, over-eager cops, unhinged backwoods drug dealers, terrible drivers, and much more. However, all these threats pale in comparison to a particularly deadly foe. GTA 6 has a game-breaking soft lock bug that makes it impossible to progress.

The X user @SirGrotti posted a clip of the glitch, showing how they can no longer switch from Lucia to Jason, with the option permanently grayed out. The glitch can occur in Chapter 3 after the Missing Persons mission, and it’s game-breaking because the next story segment requires Jason. Comments on the post suggest several solutions, including leaving their house and then switching characters, saving and reloading, asking to meet up with Jason, sleeping, and more, but apparently, none of these worked for SirGrotti. Unfortunately, SirGrotti isn't alone. The website GTA6Bugs tracks user-reported cases of crashes, softlocks, and other problems. Of the dozens of bugs logged on the site, the Chapter 3 softlock has the most views. While there are a few fixes that worked for some players, not everyone has been so lucky. (Related: Tech Report: GTA 6 players are devasted by train ‘nerf’...)

Sources indicate that the first fix is to load a manual save from before you drove to the warehouse in Port Gellhorn for the final Missing Persons meeting. Then switch to Jason and play through the Dealer’s Choice mission until you can switch to Lucia freely. Make a manual save in a new slot and finish Missing Persons.

Industry observers note that beyond this fix, other potential workarounds include playing another Lucia or duo mission if one is available, calling Dunk from Lucia’s phone, and robbing a store as a pair before starting Raul’s job as Lucia. GTA 6’s 01.002.002 update didn’t seem to fix this problem, but considering this seems to be one of the most reported game-breaking issues, ideally Rockstar will address it soon. Beyond these softlocks, another common issue for GTA 6 players is a mysterious PS5 crash, which both Rockstar and Sony customer support have blamed on the other party.

It has been revealed that things can always be much worse, though: some GTA 6 players have uploaded videos of the game’s demanding graphics seemingly frying their PS5s. One clip from shivjam shows the console dying in real time, while another has a streamer's PlayStation billowing smoke. Console and controller technician Modyfikator89 described GTA 6 as "the toughest real-world PS5 stress test" he had ever seen. With the ongoing chip crisis driving game console prices higher than ever, it's a particularly bad time to be left high and dry.

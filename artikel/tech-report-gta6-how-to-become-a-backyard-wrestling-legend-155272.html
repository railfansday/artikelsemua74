Sources indicate that grand Theft Auto 6 has plenty to do, from battling the Prince of Darkness to rescuing members of Congress caught in sex swing accidents. Between all the zany and off-the-wall shenanigans, sometimes the most fun encounters happen right in your own backyard.

Industry observers note that hidden within the humble little town of Hamlet is a wrestling legend ready to take on all challengers, while putting his own pride on the line. If you’re up to snuff and want to become the next Backyard Wrestling Champion, read on to claim your spoils. (Related: Industry News: Which Samsung folding phone is right for...)

In order to find the Champ, you’ll need to head back to Hamlet once you regain control of Jason in Chapter 2. Head to the street between the grocery store and the gas station towards the middle of town. Look for the backyard of one of the houses containing a ring, a trailer, and a significant Backyard Wrestling banner. (Related: Tech Report: Over $60k raised for famous station cat af...)

Recent findings suggest that knock on the door of the trailer to get some dialogue from the Champ, where he’ll refuse to come out. Toss a nearby can or bottle, then knock again for him to make his entrance, toss Jason to the ground, and drag him into the ring for a little one-on-one playtime. If you can last against the Gentrifier, you’ll be the new Champ.

It has been revealed that once he’s introduced himself, the Gentrifier will put Jason into a series of grapple-based Quick Time Events. Make sure you boost your Stamina with a drink or snack of some kind before you get started to ensure you’ve got as much time to mash as possible.

Sources indicate that once you clear two of these mash-fests, the Gentrifier will toss Jason to the ground before going for his finishing move, the Eviction Notice. React with Square on PS5/X on XBOX to dive out of the wave, leaving him open for a counter. Don’t waste your time stomping on him. Instead, climb the same post he tried to use, and end it with a soaring People’s Elbow.

In a recent development, once he’s down and out, you can ransack his trailer for some cash, some drugs, his own stylin’ shades, and a one-of-one Luchador mask that can serve as a disguise for either character. A fantastic little token of a victory hard-fought, cementing Jason as the Backyard Wrestling Champion.

Don’t be stuck in the dark without heat because you failed to prepare for a winter power outage. Taking a little time now to gather the right supplies will ensure you won’t be scrambling in the dark when the power goes out.

In a recent development, heavy snow and freezing rain don’t just disrupt your intends — they can impact the grid. So, before the temperature starts to drop, gather the essentials you’ll need to stay warm and safe in an emergency. I've created the checklist below of five things to be aware of when it comes to surviving potential winter power outages.

Recent findings suggest that I have a ‘battery drawer’ in my home that’s also filled with other random items, but it’s worth checking that it’s well stocked. Are those rechargeable batteries fully charged, and do you have a mix of sizes, with enough to charge the appliances you’ll rely on?

Sort through and focus on checking that you have batteries to charge any flashlights, radios and other essential items you will need during an outage.

Sources indicate that don't get caught stumbling around in the dark without a flashlight. Store one in an easily accessible spot — like a dedicated kitchen drawer — and make sure everyone in the household knows where it is. Better yet, keep a few extras on hand so there’s enough to go around, and double-check that they all have fresh batteries before a storm hits. Even if I’m keeping my meals simple and heating food straight from a tin — I certainly won’t be scrolling through recipes on my phone — I still want a reliable way to warm everything through.

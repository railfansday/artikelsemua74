The Championship promotion race is on, and though there is still plenty of the season remaining for numerous twists and turns to occur, three standout candidates have emerged.

Just ticking over a third of the way through the 2026/27 Championship season, there is almost nothing to separate the teams at the top of the table right now, as one good or bad game week can see a side fly up or down the table.

Unlike in previous years, where the likes of Coventry City, Burnley, and Norwich City have romped their way to the second tier titles, there appears to be no standout side this time around, with the quality of the division as a whole at an all-time high.

Out of the 24 teams, only three will earn a spot in what is widely regarded as Europe's most coveted division, as three teams have put their names in the hat as some of the favourites.

Heading into gameweek 17 of the Championship, Middlesbrough, Stoke City, and Birmingham City made up the top three in that order, with the Potters having claimed a crucial win over Blues the previous weekend.

Boro enjoyed an excellent transfer window over the summer, guided by head coach Kim Hellberg as the likes of Will Lankshear, Sebastian Berhalter, Ashley Phillips, and Radek Vitek have demonstrated why they were worth the money. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Stoke, despite losing each of their three opening league games, have bounced back with ten wins, two draws, and one defeat in their following 13 games to emerge as surprise contenders under Mark Robins.

Chris Davies is looking to continue Birmingham's rise up the divisions by becoming increasingly watertight at the back and efficient up top, as the Blues look to end their near 16-year absence from the Premier League.

Certainly, the likes of Wolverhampton Wanderers, West Bromwich Albion, Southampton, Queens Park Rangers, Swansea City, Sheffield United, Portsmouth, Norwich City, and West Ham United, each of whom are just a matter of points away, will have something to say about the promotion race come May.

There is a reason that teams in the Championship spend so much money in the hope that they can be playing Premier League football the following season, as with success comes a huge boost in finances.

According to Sports Illustrated, promotion to the English top flight is worth in excess of £200 million in prize money alone, and that goes for first-place, second-place, and the play-off winner. (Related: Latest Update: Fine-Tune Wav2Vec2 for English ASR in Hu...)

The Championship play-off final is widely regarded as the richest game in club football for a reason, as essentially two teams go head-to-head in a one-off final for a straight shootout for promotion, with the winner taking it all.

With the second-tier play-off system expanding from four teams to six this year, there are more places up for grabs than ever to earn a place under the Wembley arch at the end of the season.

A nine-figure cash prize is on offer for the three teams who are able to go all the way, though who they will be remains to be seen.

Though each of Stoke, Birmingham, and Middlesbrough has had terrific starts to the season, whether they can all maintain their level of form throughout the campaign remains to be seen.

Stoke started hot last campaign before dropping off in the second half of the year. Birmingham persistently flirted with the top six but couldn't quite break in, and Boro fell at the final hurdle to Hull City after missing out on automatic promotion.

With so many teams clustered around the top half right now, the promotion battle has never been hotter, as Stoke, Birmingham, and Middlesbrough don't face a straightforward route to the Premier League.

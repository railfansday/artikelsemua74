The Premier League could be bankrupted by the financial and legal nightmare that will follow if Manchester City win their appeal.

That is according to experienced former Tottenham, Aston Villa and Everton scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The Citizens were found guilty of 114 breaches of the Premier League's financial rules by a independent commission in September, almost two years after the hearing ended and nearly four years since the charges were announced.

Man City's appeal is expected to be heard next month, with potential sanctions to come next year if it fails.

There has been talk of legal action towards the Premier League, too, which could in the aftermath of the eventual decision.

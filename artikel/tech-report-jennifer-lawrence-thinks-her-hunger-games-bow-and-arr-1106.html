Jennifer Lawrence thinks a bow and arrow she received after appearing on The Hunger Games may have been stolen from her.

In a recent development, the Oscar winner appeared on Watch What Happens Live With Andy Cohen on Thursday night alongside Josh Hutcherson, marking their first joint interview in over 10 years. The appearance was tied to the release of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, the prequel film where they reprise their respective roles of Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark.

Andy Cohen questioned them about a number of Hunger Games-related topics, including if they agree that Catching Fire is the best movie in the franchise (Lawrence agreed, while Hutcherson said the first Hunger Games is his favorite) and their first impressions of each other when they first met to work on the film. According to recent reports, cohen asked Lawrence if she was able to obtain one of her character’s famed bow and arrows, which led her to a realization. “I think I lost it, or I guess it was stolen,” Lawrence mentioned, dumbfounded. “Where am I going to lose it?”.

Sources told The Hollywood Reporter that Sunrise on the Reaping is likely to surpass $100 million in its box office debut this weekend.

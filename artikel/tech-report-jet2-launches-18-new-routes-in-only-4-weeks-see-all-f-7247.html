📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Jet2 is the fourth-largest airline serving the UK by flights and seats for sale this year. Formally called Jet2.com, the leisure operator has 14 bases across the country, with London Gatwick being the most recent addition back in March 2025.

It has been revealed that ch-aviation indicates that Jet2 currently has 134 aircraft. Excluded are the Airbus A330-200s that were leased from AirTanker, which provided additional capacity, mainly for the peak season, for nine years. The agreement has now ended.

Obviously, most of Jet2's activity revolves around the summer, which has the highest demand. In August 2026, for example, the leisure airline had 3.2 million round-trip seats for sale. That's the top month, which revolves around school holidays. In contrast, Jet2's capacity was reduced to 1.3 million seats in November and slightly less again in December. Despite this, multiple new routes are starting, with November's additions shown below. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Recent findings suggest that the focus in the winter necessarily changes from fun-in-the-sun trips to skiing and trips to visit Christmas markets. All but one of the following additions are for markets. The exception is Agadir, which is relatively warm in the winter and is geared to that. Of course, the huge shift from summer to winter operations is absolutely nothing unusual. In the past, charter and other leisure airlines used the much quieter winter to perform more in-depth maintenance ahead of the following summer, when utilization grew substantially.

Up to four weekly A321neo (the airline's shortest route this winter).

Recent findings suggest that strasbourg is a brand-latest destination for Jet2. Nicknamed the 'Capital of Christmas,' the French city's oldest festive market supposedly dates back as early as the 1500s. It is reportedly also the oldest version in France. At 348 nautical miles (644 km) each way, Strasbourg will be Jet2's shortest-ever international route from Stansted.

It has been revealed that unsurprisingly, the following additions do not revolve around city breaks for festive shopping but instead skiing and other winter pursuits. This assumes the snow is there in the right quantity and quality. That is not always the case, especially nowadays. All the classic destinations and gateways are served.

All five routes from Gatwick, which is the UK's second-busiest airport, are latest to Jet2's network. However, the sole remaining link (Stansted to the Austrian city of Innsbruck) is not new, but it has not been served for a while.

According to recent reports, the carrier operated between Stansted and Innsbruck from February 2020 to 2024, so the return comes after nearly three years. What is notable is that flights previously operated until springtime. Nowadays, the season finishes much earlier, which is designed to help ensure more snow.

As mentioned earlier, Jet2's peak month is August, with 3.2 million seats in 2026. Capacity in November is 59% lower, at 1.3 million seats this year. But it is not the nadir month — January is. That winter month has 902,000 seats in 2026, but, funnily enough, will be lower in 2027.

Let's stick to August and November and compare the capacity gap for Jet2's 14 UK bases: Belfast International, Birmingham, Bournemouth, Bristol, East Midlands, Edinburgh, Gatwick, Glasgow, Leeds/Bradford, Liverpool, Luton, Manchester, Stansted, and Newcastle. Compared to the busiest month, Stansted has the biggest drop in November (seats down 74%). In contrast, Newcastle has the smallest drop (-42%, well below the average of 59%).

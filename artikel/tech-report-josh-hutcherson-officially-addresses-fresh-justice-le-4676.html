New heroes and villains are still joining James Gunn's DC Universe franchise.

Industry observers note that while it was revealed earlier in November that Gunn and Peter Safran will be stepping down as DC Studios co-CEOs, the DCU's Chapter 1: "Gods and Monsters" slate is continuing, as there are still projects in the works that have yet to be released. With various live-action and animated films and TV indicates in different stages of production, one of them recently found its titular lead. Industry observers note that in a newly introduced interview with the Happy, Sad, Confused podcast, Josh Hutcherson finally broke his silence on being cast as Scott Free in the animated Mister Miracle series, which will also star Mackenzie Davis as Big Barda. When he was first asked if it has already been recorded, he excitedly shared, "We have! It's awesome," before elaborating on how he landed the DCU gig, while also confirming whether he would play the titular hero in live-action. (Related: Industry News: Fit More and Train Faster With ZeRO via ...)

Sources indicate that "This was like a very extensive auditioning process, because whoever plays them in the animated version will play them in the live-action version. So that's the initiative. That's the track that we're trying to follow," the Hunger Games star stated, while adding, "Since I didn't get cast in Spider-Man, I was like, 'I don't ever want to be a superhero. Like, it's fine. I never really wanted that.’" However, things changed once the DC opportunity was given to him.

It has been revealed that hutcherson went on to say, "And then Mister Miracle came around, and I read, all I had was like a few of the scenes to do the audition, and it was such a cool character. I started getting into the comics and reading through those, and the world is amazing. What it talks about with mental health and all of that, I think it's something that's very special. And I'm honored to be Scott Free, and I can't wait." The lead also praised showrunner Tom King, as the comic book scribe is adapting his Mister Miracle run for DC Studios.

Josh Hutcherson: "But Tom King, who wrote the comics, is phenomenal. He was our showrunner as well, so he knows it inside and out. He knows Scott Free/Mister Miracle inside and out. And getting to have Mackenzie Davis there with me as well to play Major Barda, she was amazing. And that was just such a great project.".

While Gunn and Safran will be replaced by Jeremy Latcham, who will start as DC Studios' new CEO on December 1, the Guardians of the Galaxy trilogy director did stress in his statement that characters they set up during their time will continue. The Superman filmmaker stated on November 9, "And we've just been engaged on another DC project which I'm writing, and we'll see where it goes after that. And, of course, the characters we set up will live on. Thank you so very, very much for embracing the characters and stories we've put on screen.".

Sources indicate that while there may not be any revealed intends right now for when Mister Miracle and Major Barda will appear in live-action, it will depend on what happens to the franchise as a whole. Assuming that there won't be another reboot, if the animated show becomes a success, there are multiple ways for upcoming DCU movies to utilize the characters after their small-screen debut.

Mister Miracle has yet to land an official premiere date on HBO Max.

Manchester City supporters gave Kevin De Bruyne an emotional welcome on his return to the Etihad Stadium with Napoli in the Champions League tonight.

As noted by the Corriere dello Sport, the home fans greeted the Belgian with his famous “Oh Kevin De Bruyne” chant, which rang around the stadium for a decade.

A small banner with his picture and the words “King Kev” was also on display.

Industry observers note that cards showing De Bruyne in a City shirt were handed out in the stands, carrying the message: “Our King. The tribute comes as no surprise given what De Bruyne achieved in Manchester. He arrived from Wolfsburg in 2015, amid some doubts after an unsuccessful spell at Chelsea, and became the creative heart of Pep Guardiola’s side.

In 10 years at the club, he won six Premier League titles, two FA Cups, five League Cups and the Champions League. He scored 108 goals and provided 177 assists in 421 appearances.

City had already given him a farewell fit for a king in May 2025, with a giant tifo, a mural in the city and a road at the club’s training ground named after him.

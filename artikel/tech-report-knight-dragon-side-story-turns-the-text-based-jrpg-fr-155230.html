It has been revealed that we cover a lot of smaller, more niche series on the site here. And one of them that has recently cropped up is Knight & Dragon, a text-based JRPG series full of plenty of number-crunching and grind. Now, developer Shinjiro Nakagawa is trying something upcoming with the launch of Knight & Dragon Side Story.

It has been revealed that as you may have already gleaned from the headline, the core change of Knight & Dragon Side Story is that it ditches the traditional turn-based JRPG combat in favour of real-time action. It's not fully 3D, however, as instead you'll be tasked with actively dodging attacks in the style of something like Undertale, making for a more fast-paced adventure.

KT declared on November 23, 2026 that it had signed a memorandum of understanding with Korea Water Resources Corporation (K-water) to develop and test physical AI for water management, extending their digital-platform work into field applications and trials.

Sources indicate that the signing ceremony took place at K-water’s headquarters in Daejeon. The partnership combines KT’s AI infrastructure, network and platform technology with K-water’s operational water-management experience, extending an existing digital-platform collaboration. The MOU covers planned development and testing, not a completed deployment or commercial rollout.

Sources indicate that the partners will cooperate in four areas: creating a roadmap for water-management-specific physical AI; conducting physical AI data trials at sites including treatment plants and dams; establishing integrated operations for different device types and existing facilities; and developing physical AI data standards and operating models for water management.

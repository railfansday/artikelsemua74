It finally feels like we’re in the thick of fall. It’s just beginning to get cold enough to wear our favorite coats, all the leaves have turned graceful shades of red, yellow, and orange, and somehow, nothing feels as comforting as a hot cider cupped in the perfect pair of leather gloves. We’ve almost arrived at Thanksgiving, which officially marks the start of the holiday season. (Related: Latest Update: The Age of Machine Learning As Code Has ...)

When it comes to fashion, Thanksgiving is actually one of the best holidays for holiday dressing. There is ample inspiration to rework fall classics and experiment with newly introduced trends that have just recently been presented on the runways, like berry tones, antique Victorian-inspired style, or even statement coats. The weather (not too cold just yet, but cold enough to layer) also makes it a prime time to play with accessories, whether it be medallion belts or grandmacore-inspired tapestry bags.

So, this year, why not pull your Thanksgiving outfit inspiration directly from your tablescape? Aside from celebrating with friends and family, a decorative tablescape is one of the most fun parts of this holiday. Make a Pinterest board for both, and you’ll match: There’s nothing chicer.

Read on for some ideas for Thanksgiving tablescape-inspired outfits.

In a recent development, look to Thanksgiving’s ultimate favorite hue and layer up juicy shades of it. Rich, stunning tones of saturated cranberry pop (and you’ll probably spot them on your plate, too). It gives you a dramatic look with low-key effort. Opt for mixed textures. Think: leather, velvet, silks, and satins all done in deep cranberry.

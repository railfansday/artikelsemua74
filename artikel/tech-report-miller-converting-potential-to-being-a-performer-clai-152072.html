After forcing an own goal from Nathan Harriel, the 19-year-old scored in the second half to secure a 2-0 success at home.

Miller has scored eight goals and provided nine assists in MLS play this season, and Mitrovic praised the teenager's shift in mentality this term.

"He's converting from being potential to being a performer, and that's what a performer does," Mitrovic stated of Miller.

Ahead of their must-win game at Subaru Park, Philadelphia will be hoping to rely on a youngster of their own in Cavan Sullivan.

Sullivan was named 2026 MLS Young Player of the Year, and Union interim head coach Ryan Richter was delighted for the 17-year-old.

"All the good things that come to him are a reflection of the hard work and the mentality that he has," Richter mentioned of Sullivan's award.

"The one thing that you know with him is that he has significant ambitions and he has big dreams of what he wants to do in his career.

According to recent reports, "This is a great honour for him, but I know he's not going to take it and get complacent. He's onto the next thing. He's driven to take another step after this.".

Philadelphia Union – Bruno Damiani.

Since the beginning of September, Damiani has scored 10 goals, tied for the most in MLS with Orlando City's Antoine Griezmann.

Meanwhile, his six goals in home matches in that span are tied for most in MLS with five players, including teammate Milan Iloski.

Upcoming England Revolution – Peyton Miller.

Miller (19 years, 13 days) became the first teenager to score a playoff goal for New England last weekend.

Prior to Miller, the youngest Revs goalscorer in the MLS Cup playoffs was Tajon Buchanan (21 years, 290 days) in 2020, also against the Union.

MATCH PREDICTION: PHILADELPHIA UNION WIN.

Philadelphia have lost just once at home against an MLS team since the start of April (W8 D5), but the loss came versus Upcoming England on October 24.

The Revs have won consecutive road matches against the Union once, winning both their away games in this fixture in 2015.

The Union have been shut out in three of their last four games, after not being held scoreless in any of their previous 20 matches against MLS foes.

The Game 1 win was Latest England's first shutout playoff victory since a 2–0 win over Philadelphia in the 2020 first round.

The Revs have posted consecutive shutout wins once in their playoff history, with 1–0 victories over the New York Red Bulls and Chicago Fire in 2007.

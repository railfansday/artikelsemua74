Netflix welcomed Thanksgiving with a racy message on X this morning.

With The Hunting Wives Season 2 landing on the streamer today, Netflix posted a clip of leads Brittany Snow and Malin Åkerman in bed together overlaid with the message “this thanksgiving plate.”.

It has been revealed that in the short clip, the pair say “I love you” to each other, one of several Netflix X posts this morning about The Hunting Wives. “Oh we’re so back,” noted another.

Starring Snow and Åkerman as Sophie and Margo, a newcomer to an East Texas town and the town’s leading socialite, The Hunting Wives, which is based on the May Cobb novel, has been a huge hit for Netflix. It follows Sophie as she gets closer to Margo, becoming entangled in a web of obsession, secrets and ultimately murder when a teenage girl is found dead in the woods near where the Hunting Wives meet.

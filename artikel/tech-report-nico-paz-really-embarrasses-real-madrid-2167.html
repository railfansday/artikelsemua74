It’s one of the sensations of the moment, in Italy, but also in Europe and around the world. To the point where in his country, in Argentina, he is considered capable of picking up the torch from Lionel Messi and becoming the latest leader of the Albiceleste. It must be said that Nico Paz displays a stratospheric level with FC Como. A clearly extraordinary talent, and above all, an elegance with the ball that very few players can boast of displaying. The native of Tenerife (Spain) is the present and future of football.

And even if many have forgotten it, Nico Paz is a pure product… of Real Madrid. Trained with the Merengues, he chose to leave in the summer of 2025. During the 2026 summer transfer window, in an operation aimed at replenishing the coffers, Real Madrid exercised its buyout option of 9 million euros, before reselling him for 60 million euros to the Italian team. Next summer, the Merengues will still be able to bring him back for an amount of 80 million euros. And clearly, everything indicates that in Madrid, no one really knows what to do…. Industry observers note that in a normal context, Real Madrid, like any other club on the planet, would have no hesitation and would already be preparing to bring him back to keep him in their squad. However, the Madrilenians have a small problem: the positions and roles where Paz could play at the Bernabéu are already well occupied, by Jude Bellingham and by Arda Güler. For many supporters and journalists, the Argentinian, as good as he is, doesn’t really have his place. And Real Madrid would therefore not necessarily need it. “Güler’s extension is to show the player that he is important, but also to tell Nico Paz that this position at Real Madrid already has an owner”for example explained Raul Varela on Radio Marca. In a recent development, clearly, what stands out is that Real Madrid don’t know what to do. The evidence, the information that comes out is contradictory. Mundo Deportivo And Libertad Digital indicate this Thursday that the club is counting on him for the coming season, while others explained that Mourinho is not necessarily seduced and that Paz is not a priority. Then, you also have to know the intentions of the player, coveted by other major teams. Several media explain, logically, that he does not want to play supporting roles, and that he would only return to Real Madrid to have an vital role. Similar situation for his teammate and also ex-Madrilenian Jacobo Ramon. In any case, his agent’s phone is likely to ring a lot in the coming months since Manchester United are already on the case….

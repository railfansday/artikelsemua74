Norse Atlantic Airways has canceled all flights and discontinued ticket sales after its Norwegian operating subsidiary mentioned it is filing for bankruptcy.

The collapse comes a day after the low-cost, long-haul carrier suspended flights and two days after it described its financial situation as “critical.”.

In a stock exchange filing on Friday, Norse confirmed that the board of its Norwegian operating subsidiary, Norse Atlantic Airways AS, had concluded there was no longer a basis for continued operations.

Curry Barker may finally have Obsession 2 on the way.

It has been revealed that the horror-comedy centered on the desperate Bear who makes a wish for his long-time crush Nikki to fall in love with him memorably became one of the most talked-about movies of the year upon its May 2026 release, garnering widespread acclaim particularly for Inde Navarrette's performance as Nikki. With Obsession also breaking multiple box office records during its theatrical tenure, including becoming Blumhouse's highest-grossing film of all-time with over $524 million worldwide, talks of a sequel have swirled ever since. It has been revealed that now, during an interview with the LA Times as the film gears up for its awards campaigning, Barker and Navarrette have offered a new patch on Obsession 2's chances. The star began by cheekily asking if the publication wanted the "pretty answer or the non-pretty answer," going on to share the latter in which a sequel could explore "what the hell happened" to her while under the One Wish Willow's spell:. Inde Navarrette: I’ve seen some cool videos from lawyers saying how they’d defend her ... If you think about the story from Nikki’s perspective, it’s significantly more horrifying than what Bear went through. I think women are very resilient. She’s going to go on a journey and hopefully write a phenomenal book about it.

Recent findings suggest that as the conversation turned to Barker, he shared "an incredibly detailed outline" with his stars and the publication of a potential Obsession 2, to which Bear actor Michael Johnston humorously shouted for the comments to be redacted. The writer/director/editor concluded by confirming he has formulated thoughts for the next installment, but didn't give an indication whether he would be moving forward on it soon.

Even before the film became a breakout hit, Barker was fielding questions about the possibility of Obsession 2 happening. Much like the above, the filmmaker has never turned down the idea of expanding the horror-thriller into future installments, teasing ideas for both a proper movie sequel and an anthology TV series with one-hour episodes featuring different wishes. However, Barker has always cautioned that a sequel would not be an immediate arrival as he wants some room for anticipation to build.

Interestingly, while a direct follow-up may not be released for a few years, Barker does have something of an Obsession sequel coming out next year with Anything but Ghosts, a horror-comedy led by him and frequent collaborator Cooper Tomlinson, who also co-wrote the script, as fake paranormal investigators who inadvertently walk into a real haunted situation. The writer/director previously confirmed it to be set in the same universe as his 2026 hit, though recently clarified the connection is more of a small Easter egg.

Recent findings suggest that on top of Anything but Ghosts, slated for a May 2027 release, Barker certainly has enough on his docket to keep him busy before he returns for Obsession 2. Per his first-look deal with Blumhouse Atomic, he already has a third horror movie in the early stages of development, while also being attached to write and direct A24's Texas Chainsaw Massacre reboot, both of which will likely see him occupied until at least 2029. As such, he'll have plenty of time to nail down his initiative for the highly-anticipated sequel.

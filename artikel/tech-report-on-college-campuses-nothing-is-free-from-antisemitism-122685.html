After several participating groups withdrew — seemingly because Hillel, a Jewish organization, was on the flyer — the student government’s president and vice president revealed that the event “doesn’t align with our values or the values of the students we represent,” that it “should never have moved forward.” And, remarkably, she added, “To be blunt, we failed.”.

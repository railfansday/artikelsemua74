One Piece is in a pretty fantastic spot right now. The series has always been popular, especially through its manga and anime adaptations, but now the live-action show is in the mix, and it has drawn an entirely latest generation of fans. With Season 3 of the live-action TV show, Luffy and his crew will sail to even more classic adventures and take on more villains.

The thing is, the One Piece universe feels fairly limitless. It's been going on so long now that it's hard to nail down how to recreate all its flashy characters and arcs. Still, if you want the full experience, the manga is there for the taking, and more accessible than ever thanks to digital formats.

But before that live action return, another major One Piece release is coming at the end of the year. Thanks to a confirmation from the One Piece site, we know everything there is to know about The Dominance of God. (Related: Latest Update: Clavicular allegedly told 17-year-old to...)

Live this week, you can find The Dominance of God set (OP-18) for the One Piece card game. It centers around the World Government, as well as the Water Seven and the Holy Knights of God. Saint Gunko and Saint Shamrock are making their full debut in the card game, but there's a lot more in this set. (Related: Industry News: SpaceX wants to build AI data centers in...)

Here's an overview of what you can expect:.

Recent findings suggest that "A Booster Pack with many cards from Big Three Themes. This is a big set which includes Three themes with a Brand-New Theme as well. It will have Cards in a luxurious designs which will make them irresistible additions to any ONE PIECE fan’s collection. That character is finally joining the crew as a New Leader. That long-awaited character from ONE PIECE will finally be featured as a Leader to reconstruct the game environment.

According to recent reports, look forward to getting more news about the newly introduced cards coming from OP-18. New cards to strengthen your decks. Players can use the new cards to strengthen a wide variety of existing decks! In addition, by combining the cards of OP-18 with the upcoming ST-37/38 in December, new players have a chance to compete at the highest levels.".

It has been revealed that the Dominance of God also includes Kaku, Kalifa, Kumadori, Jerry, Jabra, Strawberry, Spandam, Doberman, Nero, Funkfreed, Fukurou, Blueno, and many more. Monkey D. Luffy and Saint Shamrock are also among the main chase cards in this set.

Two cards featuring artwork from Final Fantasy artist Yoshitaka Amano, including Saint Shamrock and Loki. Frankly, they look like they're directly out of a Final Fantasy game promotion. The One Piece Card Game may have kicked off in late 2022, but it feels like it just came out.

Sources indicate that four years in, the team is already up to set 18, and still drawing from the One Piece universe in ways that they haven't before. After all, there's plenty of source material, and there is no end in sight. With the revival coming and the live-action series working in tandem, One Piece could become bigger than ever.

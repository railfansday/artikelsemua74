... I was thinking that I should start by selling the holdings that show a total loss at this time. Is this the right approach? You're on the right path. Generally speaking...0. Turn off dividend and capital gains distribution reinvestment in any stock or fund you don't want to own more of. 1. Sell all positions that show a loss. 2. Come up with a dollar amount of those losses so you have that number in mind.3. Sell positions with small gains such that you've recognized about as much in gains as you had in losses.4. With the remaining positions, all showing gains, decide how much in capital gains income you're willing to recognize each tax year until you've sold off all that you want to sell. 5. If you refuse to recognize capital gains, donate shares to a charity or a Donor Advised Fund.More detail in the wiki about paying a tax cost to switch funds: https://www.bogleheads.org/wiki/Paying_ ... itch_fundsRegards,Statistics: Posted by retired@50 — Fri Nov 20, 2026 9:16 am.

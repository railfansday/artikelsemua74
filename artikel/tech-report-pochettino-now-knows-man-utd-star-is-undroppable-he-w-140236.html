Manchester United manager Mauricio Pochettino must now know that defender Harry Maguire is undroppable.

With Pochettino setting up for his first game as Manchester United manager against Liverpool in the Premier League, he made the bold decision to start Matthijs de Ligt at centre-back.

That meant that experienced defender Maguire was on the bench, which was certainly an interesting decision, given how long de Ligt had been out for.

You could certainly argue that Maguire has been part of a terrible defence this whole season, but playing de Ligt ahead of him was certainly not the answer.

The Dutch defender looked completely out of sorts and not fit enough, and Pochettino must have regretted his decision to start him, even in the opening 10 minutes.

De Ligt dropped to the bench for United's game against Sporting CP in the Champions League during the week.

Although they drew 2-2 with the Portuguese side, they certainly looked more assured defensively, giving up just 0.7 xG from open play.

Mistakes at the back were incredibly costly for Pochettino's side, and Maguire was certainly one who wasn't to blame.

Pochettino must have realised now that Maguire could be one of the undroppable ones in his starting eleven.

Defensively, United have been atrocious so far this season, but maybe Maguire isn't the problem. The experienced centre-back has been playing alongside the likes of Lisandro Martinez, who has endured a difficult season and been the subject of a telling Ben Jacobs patch on his Man Utd future, Ayden Heaven, who has shown plenty of inexperience, and Leny Yoro, who has made a lot of mistakes.

Against Sporting, Maguire won all of his ground duels and all of his aerial duels, whilst also completing four long balls.

He won everything at the back and was let down by his teammates, in particular Senne Lammens between the sticks.

The likes of Bruno Fernandes are certainly undroppable, given he's capable of producing moments of magic, while Yoro was also a rock at the back against Sporting.

Many have suggested that these two are two of the undroppable ones for Pochettino, but Maguire could be creeping his way in.

Maguire is certainly a better option for United than de Ligt, Heaven, and even Martinez.

Although many will be suggesting that Maguire isn't the answer long term due to his age, which is certainly true, he still could be the answer in the short term.

United can't completely reshuffle their defence until the transfer window opens, and even then, they're unlikely to sign more than one player.

Maguire's experience and leadership could be crucial for United over the coming months, and although he's been part of a terrible defence, he certainly doesn't appear to be the issue.

Pochettino must now know that Maguire is one of his undroppable players, and he shouldn't be making the mistake again of starting de Ligt ahead of him.

Juventus has an opportunity to restart its league campaign with a home victory when Venezia visits Turin on Sunday. Luciano Spalletti still has a lengthy injury list to manage, but the visitor’s difficulties away from home make this a fixture Juventus will expect to control.

Venezia arrives with renewed confidence after beating Udinese, giving the promoted side something to build on as it attempts to improve its position.

Sources indicate that juventus went into the international break following a 2-2 draw at Fiorentina, three days after winning 2-1 at AZ Alkmaar in the Europa League. Those results offered signs of attacking progress, although conceding twice in Florence also indicated why Spalletti cannot take defensive control for granted.

Industry observers note that randal Kolo Muani has contributed to that improvement. He supplied a goal and an assist against AZ before setting up another goal at Fiorentina, providing a useful source of invention while several important teammates remain unavailable. Recent findings suggest that venezia earned an crucial 2-1 home victory over Udinese before the break. Davide Nicola praised the commitment behind that performance, while also acknowledging the need to start collecting points away from home.

That remains the central issue ahead of this trip. Venezia has lost its last six Serie A away matches, a sequence that makes another difficult afternoon in Turin a reasonable expectation. The win over Udinese improves confidence, but sustaining pressure and defending for long stretches against Juventus will present a different challenge.

There is a reminder from the recent head-to-head record that Juventus cannot afford to drift through the game: Venezia drew 2-2 on its previous visit in December 2024. Nicola’s side will hope to stay within touching distance and make the most of any opportunities to counterattack through John Yeboah and Akor Adams.

In a recent development, bartol Franjic remains out following shoulder surgery, while Marin Sverko’s availability needs confirmation because of a hip problem. Filip Stankovic is expected to start in goal, with Yeboah and Adams offering options up front.

Juventus has enough absences to make patience key, particularly if Venezia keeps the opening stages tight. Even so, the home side’s attacking options and strong record against promoted opposition give it a clear advantage.

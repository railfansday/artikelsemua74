In a recent development, the Packers get Micah Parsons back, while the Rams will be without Puka Nacua for the rest of the season. Their offense is limited without him. But this game will be decided by how well the Packers' line can block Aaron Donald and Myles Garrett. They've been bad, but I think they will be better here. The defense will keep this close, and Jordan Love will win it late.

This should be an offensive show. Both teams can score, even if Chicago hasn't been great on offense the past two games. On this fast track, look out. The Bears defense has played well since getting Jeffrey Simmons in a trade, but they won't stop the Lions here. Look for a lot of points with the home team getting the victory on Thanksgiving.

Dak Prescott has thrown nine touchdown passes and no picks in the past two weeks. The Dallas offense is rolling. The Eagles defense will be a challenge. Joe Milton, who was on the Cowboys' practice squad, will start at quarterback for the Eagles. Dallas will play better defense against him this week. Cowboys substantial.

According to recent reports, this is the game of the week. Patrick Mahomes vs. Josh Allen. What's not to like as two of the best in the league go at it? The Bills have played much better defense lately, but they've faced a bunch of backup quarterbacks. Not here. So look for both passers to have good days. In the end, Allen will make the substantial play late to win it.

It has been revealed that this Friday game pits two teams who would be in the playoffs right now. But the Steelers have lost two straight and haven't looked good. Denver has won six in a row. But I think the Steelers will find a way to show up and win this one behind their running game. It won't be pretty, but the Steelers will find a way.

Sources indicate that the Bengals are coming off a tough road loss at Washington, while the Saints are playing consecutive road games. The Bengals offense didn't score as it had in Monday's game, but that will change here. Joe Burrow gets back on track. Bengals significant.

In a recent development, the Giants are coming off an impressive victory over the Jaguars, while the Colts are coming off a bye. They released quarterback Daniel Jones, which means Anthony Richardson will be starting. The Giants' defense should dominate in this one. The Colts have packed it in, so the Giants take it.

Recent findings suggest that the Jets are playing consecutive road games, while the Dolphins are home for the first time in three weeks. The Jets played well in beating the Chargers last week, while Miami lost to the Bills. The Dolphins are limited on offense, but the Jets' defense has struggled. Look for Miami to hang around.

Industry observers note that the Vikings are home after getting blown out by the 49ers in Mexico. The Falcons are off a bye. The Minnesota offense struggled against the 49ers, but the Falcons have had defensive problems lately. Look for the Minnesota defense to get back on track as well. Vikings take it.

The Jaguars are coming off a bad showing in the loss to the Giants, while Tennessee gave up 51 to the Cowboys last week. Ouch. The Jaguars scored 40 against them two weeks ago. They won't get that many here, but it will still be ugly as Trevor Lawrence and the passing game get back on track.

According to recent reports, this is essentially a playoff game of sorts for these two. The loser is pretty much done. The Texans are home off a Thursday game, while the Ravens are playing consecutive road games. Edge Houston. The Texans have played well on offense, and the Ravens' defense is a mess. Look for a significant day for C.J. Stroud.

This was once scheduled for prime time, but it was moved because both teams have flopped this year. The Patriots will start Tommy DeVito for the injured Drake Maye. That's a problem. The Chargers might not have Justin Herbert (concussion) either, which would put D.J. Uiagalelei in as the starter. He's thrown seven passes in his career. Even so, give me the home team here.

Sources indicate that the Commanders are coming off an impressive home victory Monday night over the Bengals, while the Cardinals got rocked by the Chiefs last week. Arizona has big-time issues and fired the defensive coordinator this week. That defense will struggle to stop Jayden Daniels. The Commanders stay hot.

Pick: Commanders 30, Cardinals 19.

Recent findings suggest that the 49ers have the best record in the NFC and seem to be cruising to the division title. But Seattle can slow that talk down here with a victory. That will be tough to do on the road. Look for a lower-scoring game in the second division game between the two — the 49ers won the first — and San Francisco will win it late.

Industry observers note that this has been flexed to prime time because the Browns would be a playoff team right now. Plus, the Raiders have rookie Fernando Mendoza making his third start. The Raiders are playing consecutive road games, and Cleveland is off a bye. Massive edge to the Browns as they win it behind their defense.

The Panthers can take a stronghold on the division with a victory here. They come in with defensive challenges, but the Bucs have problems in the passing game. So what gives? I think the Tampa Bay defense will play well here and the Bucs will run it right at Carolina to win it like they did in the first meeting.

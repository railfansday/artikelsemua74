Sources indicate that QPR were forced to leave the winger out of their squad for their trip to Carrow Road on Tuesday night. They went on to draw 1-1 in East Anglia against the Canaries with Harvey Vale getting their goal in the first-half.

Smyth, 29, has been ever-present for the Hoops so far this season under head coach Julien Stephan and he has enjoyed plenty of game time for the London outfit. He has made 16 appearances in all competitions, 15 of which have come in the Championship, and he has chipped in with three goals and three assists.

In this latest update regarding his availability, West London Sport have claimed he wasn’t able to feature against Norwich City last time out due to an upset stomach. The R’s will be hoping he can recover over the next couple of days as they prepare for their home clash against Sheffield United on Saturday.

Sources indicate that QPR are sat in 7th place in the table after their first 17 fixtures. They are in the play-offs along with Stoke City, Birmingham City, Southampton and Swansea City.

In a recent development, QPR are well stocked up with players in their attacking department. Smyth started at Millwall last Saturday as the Hoops ran out 1-0 winners courtesy of Nicolas Madsen‘s first-half goal.

The R’s made a host of changes against Norwich City as they freshened things up. Koki Saito, Kwame Poku and the goal scorer Vale came into their starting XI in Smyth’s absence.

Smyth, who is a Northern Ireland international with 26 caps to his name, is an unsung hero for QPR. He is quick, tricky and has bags of experience in the EFL now.

It has been revealed that he has played over 350 games in his career to date and will be a useful character to have in and around the dressing room. The Linfield academy graduate has also had spells in the past at Accrington Stanley, Wycombe Wanderers, Charlton Athletic and Leyton Orient.

Sources indicate that QPR will need to assess Smyth ahead of their match against Sheffield United. They will be hoping that he can get better and be available for selection.

The forward gives the Hoops more competition and depth at the top end of the pitch. The R’s will be in confident mood right now and are four games unbeaten.

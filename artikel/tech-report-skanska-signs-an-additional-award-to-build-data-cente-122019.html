Industry observers note that STOCKHOLM, Nov. 26, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Skanska has received an additional award with an existing client to build a new data center in Georgia, USA. The additional award is worth USD 275M, about SEK 2.8 billion, which will be included in the US order bookings for the fourth quarter of 2026.

It has been revealed that the project involves constructing a fresh 48MW data center facility with a total square footage of 244,730 square feet (about 22,700 square meter). The work includes constructing the shell and interior fit-out for technical spaces, support areas, and office functions. Construction began in November 2026 and is expected to be completed in October 2028.

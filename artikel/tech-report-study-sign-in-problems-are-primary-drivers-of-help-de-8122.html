Sign-in problems account for up to 53% of help desk tickets at individual enterprises, but network challenges can take significantly longer to resolve, according to a newly introduced study from ControlUp.

Analyzing 1,069,110 help desk tickets, the IT operations company identified password resets, account lockouts, and multifactor authentication (MFA) issues as major drivers of support demand.

Sources indicate that at one large enterprise, account-related tickets took a median of 11 minutes to resolve, compared with 50 hours for network problems. Meanwhile, 87% of network and phone tickets were reassigned between teams, compared with 20% of account tickets.

Recent findings suggest that MFA enrollment and re-enrollment problems appeared at 40% of organizations, with one in five linking these complications to employees replacing or upgrading their phones. Virtual desktop problems appeared at 55% of organizations analyzed.

Traditional phone support remained a major channel for IT assistance.

Recent findings suggest that among organizations that recorded how support requests were submitted, 57% reported that phone calls accounted for approximately two-thirds or more of contacts. At one organization, that figure reached 99%.

Machine-generated tickets from monitoring, security, backup, and patching systems appeared at half of the organizations studied. At 15%, automated tickets represented between 25% and 50% of all requests. (Related: Tech Report: The Age of Machine Learning As Code Has Ar...)

The research covered enterprises across retail, healthcare, financial services, manufacturing, construction, energy, and IT services.

ControlUp mentioned the findings highlight the need to evaluate recurring problems and their operational impact rather than relying solely on ticket volume and resolution speed.

"IT organizations have traditionally measured support performance by how swiftly they close tickets, but that doesn't tell the whole story," remarked Simon Townsend, senior vice president of marketing and office of the CTO at ControlUp.

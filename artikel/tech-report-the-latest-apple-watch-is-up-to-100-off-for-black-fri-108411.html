According to recent reports, apple's latest flagship smartwatch, the Apple Watch Series 12, is worth the upgrade. With this model, Apple has upgraded the processor, added a new heart rate sensor, and improved the display durability. It's certainly one of Apple's best smartwatches, but that also means it isn't cheap: All these new features give the new Series 12 a starting price of $399, which only increases if you choose a model with cellular data. However, for Black Friday, you can take up to $100 off the Series 12, bringing the starting price down to $299.

The three biggest hardware upgrades with the Apple Watch Series 12 are the heart rate sensor, the S11 chip, and Ceramic Shield 2. That heart rate sensor now checks your pulse every five seconds, or 20 times a minute. Apple claims the Series 12 checks your heart rate variability 24 times more than the Series 11, and your background heart rate 60 times more frequently than the Series 11. The tech giant is even so bold as to say the Series 12 has the most accurate heart rate monitor on a wearable. (Related: “Giant superatoms” could finally solve quantum computin...)

Powering the fresh heart rate monitor is the S11 chip, Apple's latest processor for the Apple Watch. The S11 isn't only responsible for these new heart rate features, however. In addition to running the same watchOS 27 features as other compatible Apple Watches, the S11 on the Series 12 can run Audio Intelligence, Apple's new suite of AI-powered audio features. That includes "Live Rewind," which lets you see a transcript of the last 15 seconds of an in-person conversation, and "Siri Recaps," which displays summaries of your conversations throughout the day. Finally, the other major advantage with the Series 12 is Ceramic Shield 2. This is a display upgrade that Apple typically reserves for more premium Apple Watch finishes, but this year, the aluminum Series 12 gets it as well. That means the watch should be more durable against scratches and cracks, without you having to upgrade to the more expensive model. In a recent development, how much you'll save on a Series 12 depends on the size, color, and model. At this time, Amazon is selling certain colors of the 42mm Series 12 for $299, while certain colors of the 46mm option run $449 (also marked down by $100). You can also save on the cellular models as well: The 42mm option is currently $429 (down from $499), while the 46mm model is $449 (down from $549).

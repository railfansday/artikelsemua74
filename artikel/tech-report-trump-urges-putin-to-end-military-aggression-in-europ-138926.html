Tech Report: Trump urges Putin to end 'military aggression' in Europe

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (117 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that US President Donald Trump said Thursday he had asked Vladimir Putin to "pull back all military aggression" against Europe, in a shift in tone towards the leader Russian he called his friend just a week ago. Trump said he had made the request to Putin when they met in China last week, adding that he thought it would help lead to a deal to end Moscow's grinding war in Ukraine. The US leader's comments come a day after he hosted NATO chief Mark Rutte in Florida, after which they issued a joint statement calling on Russia not to expand the Ukraine war.

Sources indicate that victor Wembanyama outscored the Latest Orleans Pelicans' starters in the first half on Saturday night at home. He had 32 points. They had 26.

By game's end, Wembanyama had scored 50 points, his second 50-point performance of the season, this time in a 114-91 victory over the Pelicans (5-13) at Frost Bank Center. (Related: This latest chip could slash data center energy waste...)

The 7-foot-4 superstar went for 52 points in a Nov. 4 win over the Dallas Mavericks. Both of those outings have coincided with San Antonio (14-3) going 14-1 following an 0-2 start. (Related: US police forces worried GTA 6 players will start commi...)

Wembanyama, still only 22, has already tied Spurs legend David Robinson for the second-most 50-plus-point games in franchise history with three. George Gervin, a Basketball Hall of Famer like Robinson, leads the way with five.

Sources indicate that wembanyama's got a long runway to surpass both Robinson and Gervin in that department. A third 50-piece this season would be historic in itself.

Industry observers note that he's the third player since 1973-74 to log at least 50 points, 15 rebounds and 5 blocks in a single game. Wembanyama clocked out after shooting 20 of 29 with 18 rebounds, 5 blocks and 4 steals.

"It is very special," Wembanyama mentioned, when asked about reaching the 50-point threshold in an on-court postgame interview.

He then remarked with a smile: "But unlike many of my peers, I'm not very good at stat padding. So I haven't reached it as many times as I should have.".

Actress Selena Gomez was sitting courtside at the game. Wembanyama noticed.

"Of course, I had to show off for Selena, you know," he noted, laughing.

The Spurs pulled away in the second quarter. San Antonio also got 19 points from guard Stephon Castle. Another backcourt contributor, De'Aaron Fox, piled up 10 assists.

In a recent development, the Pelicans are 1-9 in their last 10 games. They've come undone after beginning the 2026-27 campaign with a 4-4 record. A bright spot for Newly introduced Orleans is the continued growth of 20-year-old guard Jeremiah Fears, who led the team with 29 points in the loss.

As for Wembanyama, he's once again an NBA MVP candidate. It's looking like he'll face stiff competition for that award this season as well.

Los Angeles Lakers star Luka Dončić has a pair of 50-point games under his belt, too, and he's leading the league in scoring with 37.2 points per contest.

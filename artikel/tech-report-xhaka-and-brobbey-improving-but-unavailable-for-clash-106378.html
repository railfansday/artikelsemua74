Regis Le Bris confirmed both Granit Xhaka and Brian Brobbey will miss Sunderland's Europa League clash with Jagiellonia Bialystok, but mentioned the pair would be back soon.

The Black Cats have picked up more points in their last four Premier League games (five – W1 D2 L1) than they had in their first seven this season (four – W1 D1 L5).

But they have been missing captain Xhaka, who sustained a calf injury against Chelsea, while Brobbey has been suffering with a problem in his thigh over the last few weeks. (Related: “Giant superatoms” could finally solve quantum computin...)

Speaking to reporters on Wednesday, Le Bris ruled out Xhaka and Brobbey of their upcoming European match, while saying it was unlikely they would feature against Tottenham this weekend in the Premier League.

Le Bris' side travel to Anfield to face Liverpool next week before turning their attention to the Tyne-Wear derby against rivals Newcastle United on December 6.

"They are doing well, both are in a better place and are being assessed weekly," Le Bris told reporters.

"They want to play, but we need to take care of them because the season will be long. So they won't be available for this week, but we will see after [this week].".

With Dan Ballard also missing with a hamstring injury, Sunderland are missing the spine of their team.

However, Le Bris insisted that dealing with injury setbacks and problems comes with the territory of competing on so many fronts.

Recent findings suggest that "It's part of the process. We know in a season you won't have your main XI if it is always available, so you need depth, and with this club, we've shown we can build something latest with young lads or players from abroad, and they are doing well," Le Bris added.

"Jules [Ahoka] is a great example, coming from nowhere and able to play against Chelsea and Villa.".

Sunderland have already faced Polish opposition in the Europa League this season, drawing 1-1 with Lech Poznan. They are the first English club to face two Polish clubs in one major European season, and will want to bounce back from their defeat to Dinamo Zagreb last time out.

But their problems in the competition in 2026-27 have been in front of goal. No side has had more shots in the Europa League this season than Sunderland (84), though the Black Cats have the fifth-worst shot conversion rate (4.8%) and have underperformed their expected goals (xG) by more than any other side (four goals from 9.49 xG).

Jagiellonia have, however, lost their last two Europa League matches, both without scoring. They have never lost three major European games in a row, nor have they failed to score in three in a row.

Two other Nasdaq equities venues follow the same clock as the main market. Nasdaq Texas (NTX) equities and Nasdaq PSX both trade from 9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. ET. Their sessions open and close alongside the Nasdaq Stock Market, without the extra time the options venues get.

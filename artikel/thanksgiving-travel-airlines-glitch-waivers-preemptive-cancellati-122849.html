Thanksgiving travel: Airlines glitch waivers, preemptive cancellations hit some Thursday flights

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (166 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Storm systems on both U.S. coasts are expected to disrupt Thanksgiving air travel, with significant delays and cancellations possible, especially in the Northeast and Pacific Northwest.
  • Airlines have issued weather-related travel waivers for affected airports, allowing more flexibility for travelers to change their plans.
  • Heavy rain, strong winds and potential flooding are forecast for major hubs, particularly in New York, Boston, San Francisco and Miami.
  • Thanksgiving Day is usually quieter at airports, but disruptions may continue into the weekend as travelers return home.
  • Travel waivers vary by airline and airport, so check eligibility and details before making changes.
  • Weather-related disruptions may not qualify for airline-covered expenses like hotels or meals, but travel insurance or certain credit cards with built-in coverage could help.
  • Delays and cancellations can cascade across the country due to the interconnected nature of airline networks.

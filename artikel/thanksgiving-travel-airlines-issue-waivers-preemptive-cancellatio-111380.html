Thanksgiving travel: Airlines issue waivers, preemptive cancellations hit some Thursday flights

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (168 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Thanksgiving week air travel faces potential disruption due to major storms on both U.S. coasts, with the Northeast and Pacific Northwest most at risk for delays and cancellations.
  • Airlines have issued weather-related rebooking waivers for affected airports, allowing travelers added flexibility to adjust plans.
  • While Wednesday saw manageable delays, preemptive cancellations for Thursday signal possible worsening conditions, especially at key East Coast hubs.
  • Travelers are advised to monitor flight status, use airline apps for real-time updates and consider adjusting itineraries to avoid peak disruption windows.
  • Weather waivers vary by airline and airport, so check eligibility before making changes.
  • Airlines typically do not cover hotel or meal costs for weather-related disruptions, but travel insurance or certain credit cards with travel protection may offer reimbursement.
  • Delays and cancellations could cascade nationwide due to the concentration of major airline hubs in affected regions.

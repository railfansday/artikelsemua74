The dark matter puzzle deepens: Nearby galaxies challenge cosmic simulations

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (105 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that galaxies appear to hold a surprise beneath their visible stars: Their inner regions contain less dark matter than some of our most advanced simulations predict. Dark matter is invisible, but its gravitational pull helps govern the motions of stars and gas. By measuring these movements, astronomers can reconstruct where this unseen matter lies and test the computer simulations used to understand the universe. A study of 136 nearby galaxies, involving the Instituto de Astrofísica de Canarias (IAC), reveals a discrepancy that becomes more extensive in more massive systems, offering fresh clues about how galaxies form and evolve.

Industry observers note that there are plenty of dividend stocks and exchange-traded funds (ETFs) to choose from, but I'm a fan of the Schwab U.S. Dividend Equity ETF (SCHD +1.15%) because it's a trifecta: diversified, high-yield, and cheap. It's a dividend ETF you can trust and treat as a set-it-and-forget-it investment. (Related: Industry News: Texas’s outgoing GOP attorney general dr...)

SCHD only holds 102 stocks, but they're well-established and vetted for quality. To be included in the fund, a company needs to be in good financial standing, have paid dividends for at least 10 years, and have increased its annual dividend for at least five years. Of course, some of its holdings can hit rough patches (as every company does), but those that check those boxes are usually built to last.

Recent findings suggest that SCHD's holdings include 11 Dividend Kings (companies with at least 50 consecutive years of dividend increases), including four in its top 10 holdings: Procter & Gamble, Coca-Cola, PepsiCo, and Abbott Laboratories. Together, these four account for over 17% of the ETF, and Dividend Kings account for over 27% (as of Nov. 23).

SCHD can be a good complement to investing in the S&P 500 because it's not as tech-heavy as the index or many other popular indexes and ETFs. Most of its holdings come from more "boring" sectors like consumer staples (19.97%), healthcare (19.20%), and energy (15.38%).

Recent findings suggest that dividend yields move in the opposite direction of stock prices, so SCHD's 25% surge this year (as of Nov. 23) has pushed its yield down a bit. At the time of writing, its yield is 3.1%, which is below its average over the past three years, but it's still one of the highest yields you'll find in a major dividend ETF or blue chip dividend stocks.

SCHD Dividend Yield data by YCharts.

In a recent development, since SCHD is an ETF, its quarterly payouts aren't as straightforward as individual stocks. When Coca-Cola, for example, sets an annual dividend of $2.12, you know you'll get four $0.53 payouts. SCHD's holdings pay dividends at different times, so SCHD's payout fluctuates.

The First GTA 6 DLC is Now Live for All Players

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

If you missed out on the Grand Theft Auto 6 pre-order bonuses, Rockstar is now offering the Vintage Vice City Pack as a standalone purchase. It's available on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The DLC, which includes a classic car, a garage and some particularly nostalgic outfits, costs $4.99 in the US on both consoles.

Sources indicate that according to Rockstar's original pre-order announcement, the pack was included with pre-orders of both editions of GTA 6, as well as digital purchases made before November 20. That meant you could still get the bonus if you bought the game on launch day. If you picked up your copy over the weekend instead, you'll have to pay for the extra content.

Industry observers note that according to the official Xbox Store listing, the Vintage Vice City Pack went on sale as a separate add-on on November 20. It isn't a story expansion, but it does offer a little more than just cosmetic items for Jason and Lucia. (Related: Industry News: My Journey to a serverless transformers ...)

The most useful part of the pack is arguably the garage. It's located at Shore Court, near Ocean Beach, and comes with a weapon locker and storage space for goods you've stolen and intend to sell to a fence. You also get a 1955 Vapid Stanier, a classic sedan to keep in the garage. The extra garage alone might make the pack worthwhile if you like collecting cars in GTA.

As for the cosmetic items, Jason gets a pastel linen suit and a retro haircut, while Lucia gets a short red sequin dress and curly hair. There's also a tropical weapon skin that you can apply to most firearms. The design is based on Tommy Vercetti's unmistakable Hawaiian shirt from GTA Vice City, which is a nice little detail for those of us who grew up playing that game. In a recent development, the funny thing is that you can already unlock Tommy's original shirt in GTA 6 without buying anything extra. The catch is that it involves a rather elaborate Easter egg, so you might want to set aside some time for that one.

Before spending another five dollars on GTA 6, it's worth checking whether you already own the pack. The outfits, vehicle and garage don't all become available the moment you start the game. You'll have to reach certain points in Jason and Lucia's story before you can claim them.

Rockstar published a support article on November 24 explaining how to access the pre-order rewards. Bring up the pause menu, head to the Store section and find the Vintage Vice City Pack. Selecting an individual reward will tell you what you need to do to unlock it, so you don't have to guess which chapter you should be in. Once you've met the requirements, you'll have to return to the game itself to claim the reward. The Store menu tells you how to get it rather than handing you the items directly.

Industry observers note that you don't need the Ultimate Edition to use the Vintage Vice City Pack, either. If you bought the Standard Edition on launch day, for instance, you should check your existing rewards rather than assume the pack is exclusive to the more expensive edition. It's sold separately from the Ultimate Edition Upgrade, and the latter has its own selection of unlockable vehicles, clothing and weapons.

GTA 4 gave us two excellent single-player expansions in The Lost and Damned and The Ballad of Gay Tony, and we never got anything comparable for GTA 5 despite its ridiculously long lifespan.

It has been revealed that hopefully, Rockstar has something more substantial planned for GTA 6's single-player DLC. As much as I appreciate the nods to Vice City, I'd love to see the series return to the kind of expansions we got with GTA 4.

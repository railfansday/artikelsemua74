According to recent reports, wally West, a.k.a. The Flash, has been one of DC Comics’ heaviest hitters for decades. Like the other characters who have access to the Speed Force, his super speed is useful for more than just running quickly. Generating vortexes, vibrating through objects, and traveling through time are just a few of the additional powers that Wally has demonstrated on many occasions. On top of that, he’s officially considered the fastest of the Flashes, outpacing predecessors Jay Garrick and Barry Allen.

According to recent reports, as if all this wasn’t enough, Wally has also displayed a fresh precognitive ability in the current volume of The Flash. Over the last few months, the third Flash has used his “flashes of insight” to predict the immediate future and prevent crimes and horrific incidents from happening in the first place. However, Wally’s use of this precognition has drawn concern from his fellow superheroes, and now four of the Justice League are clashing with the Scarlet Speedster to rein him in. Though he started out with good intentions, Flash has morphed into an anti-hero because of how he’s been using his precognition. Green Arrow took issue with Wally trying to prevent all bad things from happening in Star City after he’d previously done so in Central City and Metropolis, comparing the speedster’s use of this power to fascism. Wally ignored his warnings, and when that caused even more problems, Green Arrow called in some fellow superheroes to help him out with this predicament. (Related: Forget electrons, this breakthrough uses light-matter p...)

According to recent reports, by the end of The Flash #38, Green Arrow ordered the Justice Leaguers he’d looped in to “take The Flash down,” and the preview for The Flash #39 shows this battle getting underway in the Arrow-Cave. Wally’s inner monologue reveals that he’s having to keep his guard up with Jon Kent (who hasn’t yet become Tomorrow Man in this appearance), Wonder Woman, Martian Manhunter, and Red Tornado in particular, with Aquaman also in the mix alongside Green Arrow.

In a recent development, in addition to Jon, Diana, and J’onn having their own levels of super speed, these three also have additional abilities that worry Wally. Jon has heat vision, freeze breath, and shoots lightning from his hands; Diana is a “divine Amazon” armed with the Lasso of Truth and her trademark projectile tiara, and J’onn is gifted with super strength, shape-shifting, intangibility, and telepathy. As for Red Tornado, although he’s still a hologram, his android brain can calculate almost all of Wally’s moves and order his teammates where and how they should attack next.

Industry observers note that though Wally manages to take Red Tornado out of the picture by tossing his hologram projector away, this is still a tough fight for him. If his concentration breaks for even a second, then Jon, Wonder Woman, and Martian Manhunter will have an opening to neutralize Flash. Plus, although they don’t have super speed, there’s still the possibility that Green Arrow and/or Aquaman might find a way to strike him.

It has been revealed that still, it’s reasonable for these Justice Leaguer members to be troubled about Wally. Along with the ethical implications of his precognition, Red Tornado revealed that The Flash’s 24/7 use of the Speed Force is unintentionally creating wormholes within his pain receptors. Wally was close to acquiescing and giving up using his precognitive abilities, but when Green Arrow kept talking trash and pulled out an arrow meant to remove Flash’s powers, he did a 180, hence why the battle began.

According to recent reports, the good news is that Wally will lose his precognition by the end of The Flash #39. The bad news is that, per the solicitation for The Flash #40, he’ll lose all his other powers, too. It looks like Green Arrow’s speed-sapping arrow will indeed be used, but unfortunately, but this comes right as Wally is certain that his prophetic visions foreshadow something that will “threaten the survival of civilization itself.” Without his powers, he’ll have to convince his wife and superpowered kids to help him “fix everything.”.

The Flash #39 will be released Wednesday, November 25 by DC Comics.

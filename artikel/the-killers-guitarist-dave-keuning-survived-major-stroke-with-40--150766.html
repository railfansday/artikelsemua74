Dave Keuning, guitarist for The Killers, said that he survived a “major stroke” two weeks ago that had a “40% chance of survival” but he’s still able to “play guitar just as good as before.” He revealed the news on Thanksgiving, along with announcing the birth of his daughter.

“Happy Thanksgiving, everyone! Three months ago, we had our baby girl, Odette,” Keuning wrote. “Two weeks ago, I had a major stroke, and thanks to my wife acting fast, I went straight to the hospital and into surgery. The first hour after a stroke is critical when it comes to getting to the hospital. I had a blood clot in my brain that cut off the blood supply to the entire right side of my brain. By the time I arrived at the hospital, I was blind in one eye and had lost function on my left side, but I still wasn’t able to realize I was having a stroke.

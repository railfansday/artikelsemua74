According to recent reports, it’s no secret that Apple has been dominating tech headlines this year. But between the news of its recent price hikes on MacBooks and iPads and the release of the iPhone Duo (the tech giant’s first foldable), not much fanfare has been made about the newly introduced generations of the Apple Watch. As far as we’re concerned, that’s a damn shame, because they’re well worth the upgrade—especially if you can find one on discount.

Right on cue, Amazon’s Black Friday sale is here, and with it comes record-low prices on every Apple Watch, whether you want to shave $100 off the brand-upcoming Apple Watch Series 12, take advantage of a sub-$200 price on the entry-level Apple Watch SE 3, or score the rugged Apple Watch Ultra 4 while it’s under $700. It has been revealed that below, we’re breaking down the differences between each model—and peeling back the curtain on our price-tracking prowess—so you’re all set to shop. After all, when Apple gadgets are this cheap, they tend to go like hotcakes, so don’t say we didn’t warn you when they inevitably sell out.

Commerce editor Luke Guillory argued that compared to other fitness trackers on the market, Apple was lagging behind with its lack of readiness score functionality—until now. The Apple Watch Series 12 marks the debut of the brand’s very own readiness score thanks to a revamped tracking system. Plus, the watch now measures your heart rate more frequently to deliver Heart Rate Variability (HRV) readings every five minutes. If you’re trying to pay closer attention to your health this year, it’s a smart buy, made sweeter when it’s on sale for Black Friday.

Given that this brand-newly introduced watch dropped to just $350 for October Prime Day, we were expecting more of the same for Black Friday, so this $100 discount, bringing it to just $299, is all the more impressive.

If you’re looking for Apple’s most affordable watch, we suggest turning your attention to the entry-level Apple Watch SE 3. It was actually the watch that converted Gelbart to Apple wearables in the first place, arguing that its beauty is in its simplicity.

According to recent reports, “It's an accurate and predictive workout tracker. It counts my steps and activity and connects to my phone to play music and receive messages and calls,” he says. “If you’re new to the Apple Watch and want to dip your toes, this is where I recommend you start.” It has the same sleep tracking features, like nightly sleep score and sleep apnea notifications, but lacks the ECG app or hypertension notifications of its newer siblings. It also has an 18-hour battery life so you’ll need to charge most nights.

This $179 Black Friday deal drops the SE 3 even lower than its previous record-low price of $199, which we first saw during October Prime Day, making the most affordable Apple Watch an even smarter buy.

Our editors have loved every version of the Apple Watch Ultra thus far, and the latest model not only delivers on greatness once again, but also just hit a record-low price for Black Friday.

Sources indicate that the Apple Watch Ultra 4 has the same latest Readiness features and advanced health tracking the Series 12 has, in addition to the dive functionality, ruggedness specs, and satellite capabilities we’ve come to know and love from the Ultra line. Plus, from a sartorial standpoint, it’s hard to beat the new semi-translucent watch bands, which took design cues from the ocean.

Recent findings suggest that our philosophy when it comes to shopping for Apple tech during Black Friday? If you find the watch with the features you need at a price you can stomach, pick it up. It’s incredibly likely that the deals you see here are the best ones you’ll see for the rest of the year.

The Usefulness of Useless Knowledge

🌐 Source: Flipso • 📅 22 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (47 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that abraham Flexner argues that curiosity-driven research, pursued without immediate practical aims, has often produced the most important scientific advances and long-term benefits. He uses examples from physics, electricity, and mathematics to show how “useless” inquiry can lead to unexpected usefulness.

Just a week after Robert Pattinson was announced as the next James Bond, Jaeger-LeCoultre, the Swiss watchmaker for which he's an ambassador, has unveiled a upcoming Reverso that looks rather well suited to 007.

It's certainly a good match for Pattinson's personal tastes. “I definitely like really thin watches,” he stated in a recent interview. “That's the main thing. In general, I would probably prefer a leather strap.”. (Related: Industry News: Texas’s outgoing GOP attorney general dr...)

Industry observers note that the new Reverso Classic Monoface Small Seconds checks both boxes. Measuring just 7.56mm thick, it has an 18-carat pink-gold case, a silver-colored dial with a texture inspired by stacked coins, and a black alligator strap. Its smaller proportions–40.1mm by 24.4mm—recall the original Reverso of 1931, whose ingenious swiveling case was designed to protect the watch during polo matches. Inside is a manually wound movement with a 42-hour power reserve. (Related: New York hosts spy lookalike contest inspired by Nvidia...)

Recent findings suggest that at $18,400, it's a somewhat more elegant alternative to the more beefy dive watches traditionally associated with Bond. And with its art deco styling and slim profile, it would slip neatly beneath a dinner-jacket cuff with an ease the next Madeleine Swann would surely appreciate.

Of course, Omega has been Bond's official watch partner since 1995, and there's no suggestion that's changing. But as a watch for the man playing Bond, this fresh Reverso feels intriguingly on point.

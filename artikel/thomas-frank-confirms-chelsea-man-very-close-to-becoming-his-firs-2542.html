Frank was appointed the Eagles’ head coach this week, replacing the Frenchman Pierre Sage, who lasted just 16 matches in the job and left the club dead last in the Premier League.

Industry observers note that as he looks to establish his coaching staff, though, Frank is looking into a reunion with Cueva. The pair were colleagues at Brentford between 2020 and 2024, when Cueva was poached by the Blues, who reportedly spent £1m to secure his services. Recent findings suggest that he said Cueva is “very close” to joining his staff. He is expected to serve as a general assistant coach, rather than as a dedicated set-piece coach.

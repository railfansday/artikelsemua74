His early data makes for good reading for the Dane's tenure at Selhurst Park.

Larsen has looked a completely different attacking player since Frank has come into the fold at Crystal Palace.

While Frank has had just one game to bring results in the Premier League, his work on the training ground is seemingly reaping rewards.

As per FotMob, Larsen is already getting on the end of many more chances than he previously was.

Against Coventry, the Norwegian had four shots on goal in 79 minutes, while against Real Sociedad he managed three in just 11 minutes.

That is a vast improvement from his seasonal average of 2.11 per 90.

Additionally, all of those shots came around the opposition's box, showing he is getting into the correct positions for his teammates to pick him out.

If he continues to get into those positions, he will undoubtedly pick up the goals.

Larsen has been reaping the rewards of working under Frank as well, with him securing three goal contributions in the last two games - one goal and two assists.

He has not only been a threat with his goalscoring qualities, but he has also created three big chances over those two fixtures too.

Larsen has proven he does not need many chances to score, with his breakout season at Wolves as proof.

He scored 14 goals in his debut Premier League campaign, all while having an average shots per 90 of 1.87.

However, at Crystal Palace, life has been tough since joining in the 2026 winter window, and he has not been able to hit that level of clinicality yet.

With more shots will come more goals, and he could very well return to the player he was at Wolves through that.

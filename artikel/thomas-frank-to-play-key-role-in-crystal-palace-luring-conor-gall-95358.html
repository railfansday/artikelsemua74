Thomas Frank could push to bring Conor Gallagher back to Crystal Palace ahead of the January transfer window opening.

The 26-year-old is a big favourite of the newly introduced manager, who brought him to Tottenham last January, and could be set to follow him to his new club just one year later.

Crystal Palace have been long-term admirers of the midfielder since his impressive loan spell in the 2021-22 season, and could tempt him back to Selhurst Park.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Crystal Palace now have an advantage over Gallagher's other suitors following Frank's appointment.

There is a growing feeling that Gallagher will leave Tottenham when the January transfer window opens as he continues to play a bit-part role under Roberto De Zerbi.

As a result, a reunion with his former manager at a club where he was once a fans' favourite could be the ideal option to kickstart his career.

Football Insider revealed on 6 November that Crystal Palace are keen on a reuinion with Gallagher and have been keeping a close eye on his situation at Tottenham.

The midfielder has a number of admirers behind the scenes at Palace, and they could be tempted to reignite their interest as they eye further additions to their squad.

Frank's appointment as manager could give the Eagles a boost in their pursuit, as his relationship with Gallagher is strong, with the deal now set to be "one to watch".

"Conor Gallagher to Crystal Palace could be one to watch if he is going to be available," Brown told Football Insider.

"He's well-liked there after his previous spell when he was a fans' favourite, it's probably the best he's performed in his career, and things don't seem to be working at Tottenham.

"Plus, he's got a relationship with Thomas Frank who brought him to Tottenham in the first place, so that will give them an advantage and I'm sure he will play a key role.

According to recent reports, "Palace need to strenghthen their squad, they need experienced players, and they haven't got great depth in any position. That could become a problem if more players leave.

"So it would make sense for everybody involved if they targeted Gallagher, and it's something I'm sure they will be keen to explore.".

Much of whether Gallagher could complete a January move to Crystal Palace will depend on De Zerbi, Tottenham, and their stance on letting him move on.

The midfielder has started just four league games so far this season, including just two of Tottenham's last eight Premier League games, alerting clubs to his potential availability.

As a result, Brown told Football Insider on 12 November that Tottenham are prepared to entertain offers for Gallagher in the mid-season window if a significant offer arrives.

De Zerbi could be prepared to see him move on, although any fee would have to be substancial enough to allow Tottenham to secure a top-class replacement.

The midfielder's lack of starts so far this season have attracted interest from a number of clubs, who could be prepared to meet his demands and lure him away from Spurs.

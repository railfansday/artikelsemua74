A federal grand jury has indicted Thomas Markle Sr., the estranged father of Meghan Markle, on charges that he knowingly possessed child sexual abuse material — including images where Markle himself was photographed with prepubescent children.

Markle remains in custody in Florida and has been charged with two counts of possession of child pornography. He is expected to be arraigned in United States District Court in Los Angeles in the coming weeks. According to prosecutors, Markle knowingly possessed 35 mm negatives that included at least one image involving a prepubescent minor and a minor who had not yet attained the age of 12 years old.

The allegations against Markle Sr. first emerged in October when his son Thomas Markle Jr. reported to authorities that he found the inappropriate images at his father’s home in the Philippines. Markle Jr. reportedly told detectives he found “hundreds, if not thousands” of child sexual abuse images where Markle Sr. was living.

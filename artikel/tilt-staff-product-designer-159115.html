Industry observers note that we’re looking for a Staff Product Designer to lead design for our cash products, the fast, affordable liquidity at the heart of the Tilt user’s financial lives. They help members cover the gap between paychecks, and you’ll own the experience end to end: how members understand their offer, take an advance, and repay. Sources indicate that this is a senior individual contributor role for a designer who has owned the design of a whole product or product area before, not only individual features, and is ready to do the same here. You’ll pair strong product thinking with exceptional craft, and you’ll be accountable for outcomes: the customer and business results the experience drives. You’ll work closely with product managers, engineers, and other partners to set direction for the product and raise the quality bar across it.

Lead design for our cash products end to end, from setting the experience direction for the product through launch and iteration.

Own outcomes alongside your product and engineering partners: define what success looks like, connect design decisions to measurable customer and business results, and follow the work after launch to see whether it delivered.

Set and hold a high bar for interaction design, visual design, and overall product quality, and raise the bar for the wider design team through critique and example.

Translate complex financial concepts and flows, such as offers, advances, and repayment timing, into clear, trustworthy experiences.

Shape product strategy with your partners: identify the problems that matter most, frame them well, and make tradeoffs explicit.

Partner with compliance and legal early, so clarity and trust are built into the experience from the start.

Use AI throughout your workflow, from research synthesis and exploration to prototyping and building, and help the team adopt the approaches that work.

Help shape the design system and the broader UI patterns that our cash products and the rest of the app rely on.

Present work clearly and build alignment among partners and senior leaders with different perspectives and priorities.

Mentor other designers and help shape how design operates across product pods.

Significant experience designing and shipping complex consumer digital products, ideally within fintech, financial services, commerce, transportation, or another multi-stakeholder environment.

Experience as the design owner for a whole product or major product area: you set its design direction and were accountable for the result, beyond designing individual features.

A portfolio that shows exceptional visual and UI craft alongside strong UX thinking, at a level that raises the bar for the team around you.

A track record of outcomes: examples where your design work measurably moved customer or business results, and how you knew.

Comfort setting direction in ambiguous product areas without a fully defined path.

Strong interaction-design fundamentals and exceptional attention to detail.

Experience working closely with product management and engineering throughout development, and influencing decisions beyond your own team.

Strong communication and storytelling skills, including presenting to senior leaders and bringing partners along.

Good judgment about when to challenge assumptions, when to seek more context, and when to make a decision.

Consistent use of AI tools in your day-to-day design workflow (strongly preferred), with an informed point of view on where they help and where they don’t.

Experience designing consumer financial products, such as cash advance, banking, payments, or lending.

Experience prototyping or building in code, including with AI coding tools.

In your first few months, you’ll build a deep understanding of our customers, cash products, and business through shipping; build trusted relationships with your design and cross-functional partners; and form a clear point of view on where the experience should go.

Lead design for the products at the center of Tilt’s business, with meaningful customer and business impact.

Set both the experience and the visual direction for a critical financial product.

Join a team that values strong design judgment, constructive debate, and thoughtful collaboration.

Work directly with product-minded partners who care deeply about customers and shipping useful products.

Shape how design operates, including how the team builds with AI, as the product organization evolves.

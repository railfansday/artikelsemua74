Blackburn Rovers' positive season continued on Sunday as they drew 2-2 with bitter rivals Burnley at Turf Moor.

Whilst there was some disappointment that Rovers couldn’t pick up three points to end the Clarets’ dominance in the fixture, it was a decent point on the road.

Plus, when you take into account the situation surrounding Tony Mowbray, who has sadly had to step back from leading the team due to health issues, the players deserve credit for the way they performed in such a big game.

It has been a team effort from Rovers during the campaign so far, although Andri Gudjohnsen deserves a special mention for his outstanding ability in front of goal.

But, he isn’t the only individual who could be picked out for praise, with Todd Cantwell having also shone for Blackburn, and he was particularly impressive against Burnley.

The 28-year-old, who was wearing the armband on Sunday, has established himself as a real leader at Ewood Park, and those qualities were on show in the derby game.

Not only did Cantwell step up to score a pressure penalty to make it 2-2, but he also put in a great all-round performance.

Plus, he has appeared to embrace the responsibility of becoming a crucial player for Rovers, and he has handled himself very well in terms of leading the tributes to Mowbray, which demonstrates he is a respected figure in the dressing room and at the club.

Having failed to kick-on and fulfil his potential after breaking through at Norwich, it’s good to see Cantwell is in a position where he appears to be enjoying his football again.

His final year or two at Carrow Road was a struggle, and Cantwell had a tough time at Rangers, who struggled to end Celtic’s domestic dominance during his period in Glasgow, and he didn’t always endear himself to supporters.

Yet, he has finally found a home at Blackburn, but Cantwell’s contract with Rovers is set to expire in the summer of 2027. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

It has been claimed that the Championship side are hoping to agree fresh terms with the player, but, so far, an agreement has not been reached.

Therefore, this has to be an increasing concern for Blackburn, as Cantwell is going to have admirers, with previous reports stating that Wrexham and Derby were keen on the attacking midfielder.

Given his contract situation, you would expect further clubs to join the race for his signature, and he could be available for a cut-price in January.

The reality is that if Cantwell doesn’t sign a latest deal, Blackburn are going to risk losing him on a free if they don’t accept an offer in the New Year, which is likely to fall below his true value.

Some would argue that Rovers should keep Cantwell given they have a shot at reaching the play-offs, but losing someone like that on a free is bad business.

So, it goes without saying that Blackburn must prioritise keeping Cantwell, as that would undoubtedly be the best solution for the club.

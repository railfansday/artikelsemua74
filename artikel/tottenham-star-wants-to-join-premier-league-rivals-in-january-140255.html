Tottenham forward Richarlison could be on his way out the door in the January transfer window, and he's keen on a move to a Premier League rival.

The Brazilian international hasn't featured for Spurs so far this season after he was left out of their Premier League squad for the campaign.

Sources indicate that A departure in January now feels inevitable for Richarlison. He's clearly not wanted at Spurs and came close to leaving the club in the summer window. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Richarlison has only featured for the club's U21 side so far this season, and it makes sense for all parties if he moves on as soon as possible.

Spurs were still demanding a relative high fee for Richarlison during the summer transfer window, so it will be interesting to see whether they lower their asking price in the coming months.

According to a report from Sky Sports journalist Lyall Thomas, Everton haven't ruled out making another move to sign Richarlison in the January transfer window after attempting to secure his signature in the summer.

The report claims that other Premier League clubs are interested, while Turkish outfit Trabzonspor are also there for Richarlison.

Thomas claims that Richarlison would be open to making a move to Everton in January, but it will be interesting to see whether the two clubs can do a deal.

During the summer, a deal was taking place that would see Iliman Ndiaye go to Spurs, but the Senegalese international signed for Manchester City instead.

Everton likely won't want to include any players as part of a deal if they were to bring Richarlison back to Merseyside, but they certainly won't want to pay too much for a player on high wages who hasn't been playing.

The report claims that the key obstacle stopping Trabzonspor making a move is Spurs' valuation, with personal terms not expected to be a problem.

It certainly feels like it's time for Tottenham to move on from Richarlison.

The Brazilian's contract is set to expire next summer, and there's next to no chance that Spurs offer him a new deal.

Even just getting his wages off the books for the second half of the season would be beneficial, so Spurs need to find a way of getting rid of him in January.

A return to Everton could be a fairytale move for Richarlison, but it remains to be seen whether they can afford his wages or transfer fee.

A loan move where Everton pay a large portion of his salary could suit all parties, especially if interest doesn't ramp up ahead of January. (Related: Large Language Models: A Latest Moore's Law?...)

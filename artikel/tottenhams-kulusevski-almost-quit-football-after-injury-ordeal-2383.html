Dejan Kulusevski has revealed he came close to walking away from football after enduring an 18-month injury nightmare before finally playing for Tottenham again.

Kulusevski missed Spurs' entire 2025-26 campaign after suffering a serious right patella injury during a 2-0 Premier League defeat to Crystal Palace in May 2025.

The Sweden international underwent surgery and endured multiple setbacks during his rehabilitation, and Spurs parted ways with three head coaches – Ange Postecoglou, Thomas Frank and Igor Tudor – while he was on the sidelines.

But Kulusevski received a standing ovation when he was brought off the bench by Roberto De Zerbi against Coventry City last month, marking his first appearance in 526 days. (Related: Latest Update: At least seven progressives voted agains...)

"I told my mother. I sat with her and I told her: 'Listen, I don't think I can play any more'," Kulusevski said in an interview with BBC Sport.

Kulusevski stated one of the most painful things to overcome was seeing Tottenham beat Manchester United to win the Europa League, a game he could not take part in.

"That was one of the hardest days of my life," Kulusevski added. "It was so beautiful to see all the people happy, but for me, I was going there on crutches, I couldn't work. (Related: Latest Update: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

"I couldn't even go and get my medal because it was too slippy to go on that thing [platform].

"Seeing the team celebrating on the bus [outside Tottenham Hotspur Stadium] was probably one of the most difficult parts of this journey because you feel so alone.

"You feel like the whole world has forgotten about you and what you put in.".

Kulusevski has since made three more appearances off the bench in the Premier League, but he will be hoping for a bigger role in this weekend's clash with Ipswich Town.

Ipswich signed off for the international break with a 2-1 defeat to Bournemouth at Portman Road, with both of the Cherries' goals coming from their own mistakes.

Christian Walton dawdled in possession to allow Evanilson to score, with Leif Davis then turning the ball into the back of his own net within 27 seconds of the second half.

Kudus has scored in both of his two Premier League appearances against Ipswich, each for West Ham in 2024-25.

He has the most goal involvements in the top flight at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium since the start of last season (six – one goal, five assists).

Only Bournemouth's Adrien Truffert (213) and Liverpool's Milos Kerkez (188) have recorded more sprints in the Premier League this season than Davis (185).

Davis also ranks fourth for most combined overlapping (14) and underlapping (13) runs (27), behind that same duo (Truffert 32, Kerkez 37) and Spurs' Archie Gray (37).

Spurs have lost four of their last five Premier League games against Ipswich, though the exception was a 4-1 win in their last meeting in February 2025.

De Zerbi's side are also winless in four when playing their first Premier League match after an international break (D1 L3), drawing with Manchester United following this season’s first break.

They also lost to Leeds 1-0 last time out. It was the fifth time Spurs have failed to score in a Premier League game this season, with no team doing so more often. It is the most times they have blanked 10 games into a league campaign since 2006-07 (6 times under Martin Jol).

But with 2-1 victories in December 2001 and November 2024, Ipswich have won consecutive away league games against Spurs for the first time.

However, Ipswich have conceded in each of their last 29 Premier League games, and only four teams have ever gone 30+ consecutive matches in the competition without a shutout: West Brom (34, Aug 10-May 11), Blackburn (30, May 11-Mar 12), Wolves (30, Sep 11-Apr 12), and Watford (30, Mar 20-Jan 22).

In a recent development, ipswich's substitutes have made a habit of scoring off the bench, though. They have scored five goals via substitutes in the English top flight this season, two more than any other side. It is the highest rate of total goals scored by subs in a single Premier League campaign (33%).

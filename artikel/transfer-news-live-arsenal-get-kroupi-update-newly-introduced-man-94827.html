Latest transfer news and rumours.

Manchester United have added Brentford defender Nathan Collins to their January wishlist as they look to strengthen their defence.

According to talkSPORT, Collins is high on United’s shortlist as Mauricio Pochettino looks to address defensive concerns at Old Trafford.

However, the report indicates that the interest remains at the target-identification stage, with no indication yet of a formal bid or negotiations with Brentford.

Bournemouth have no intention of selling highly-rated Eli Junior Kroupi in January and have placed a £100million valuation on the forward amid interest from several major clubs.

Arsenal, Barcelona and Paris Saint-Germain are among those monitoring the 20-year-old, with PSG particularly keen to strengthen their options out wide.

TalkSPORT report that Bournemouth are determined to keep Kroupi until at least the summer, although the France international is reportedly keen on joining PSG when he eventually leaves the south coast. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Kroupi, who is contracted until 2030, has scored 13 goals in 37 Premier League appearances for the Cherries.

Tottenham and Chelsea are both keen to sign Joao Felix in January, per reports from FourFourTwo.

The Portuguese playmaker has already enjoyed two stints with Chelsea - one on loan, and one permanent - before leaving under a cloud.

Now at Al Nassr, the Blues and Spurs are reportedly interested in bringing him back to the Premier League, with Barcelona also sniffing around.

Wesley Fofana has revealed he almost quit football during Enzo Maresca’s time in charge of Chelsea.

The French centre-back states he considered retiring early due to the psychological strain that came with a rash of injuries.

Fofana, 25, did continue playing and is starting regularly this season for Chelsea, but has been much criticised in recent weeks for his performances under Xabi Alonso, including in Saturday’s 4-1 home defeat to Leeds.

“There was a period when I was recovering from my cruciate ligament and meniscus injuries,” Fofana mentioned.

“Then I suffered two consecutive hamstring strains under Enzo Maresca, when I really decided: ‘That’s it, I’m retiring, I’m done playing.’ I was serious.”.

Mauricio Pochettino will have just £50m to spend in January as he looks to overhaul his Manchester United squad and dodge relegation.

The Argentine is said to be keen to add a striker to his squad, with Benjamin Sesko struggling for fitness and Joshua Zirkzee for form.

Per reports in the Daily Mail, he will hope to add two players to his squad in the winter window.

Arsenal have begun talks with RB Leipzig over signing winger Antonio Nusa in a deal which could be worth £67million.

The 21-year-old has impressed this season, registering six goals and eight assists in 15 appearances, prompting Arsenal to send scouts to watch him in action against Manchester City.

According to Fabrizio Romano, Leipzig would be reluctant to sell Nusa midway through the season following the departure of Yan Diomande last summer.

Any January deal would likely require an exceptional offer, although interest in the winger is growing ahead of 2027.

Arsenal are viewed as a strong potential destination given their need for a left-sided attacker, but Paris Saint-Germain are also monitoring his progress.

Chelsea may have cooled their interest in Alex Scott and Lamine Camara.

The Blues were expected to at least make a swoop for Bournemouth stars Scott, and have been linked with reigniting their pursuit of Monaco man Camara.

But The Athletic’s David Ornstein is not so sure.

“When you look back to the end of the summer window, Chelsea didn’t get let’s say a Kone, a Camara and Alex Scott but I’m not aware of them going for those players this January, maybe that’s something for the summer.

“When you look at the rise of Romeo Lavia now he appears to be staying fit, he’s one of the best midfielders in his position available.”.

Paris Saint-Germain could step up their pursuit of Bournemouth forward Eli Junior Kroupi after Desire Doue suffered a serious knee injury, Teamtalk claims.

The injury comes just a month after Khvicha Kvaratskhelia suffered an ACL injury that is expected to keep him out for at least 6 months.

Kroupi is seen as the next starlet in France and PSG could act in January given their injuries.

Paris Saint-Germain are preparing to offer midfielder Vitinha a new contract until 2032, according to Nico Schira.

The Portuguese’s agent, Jorge Mendes, is thought to be braced for an approach from the European champions.

Sky Germany reports that Bayern Munich are weighing up a move for Schalke 04 goalkeeper Loris Karius.

However, the 33-year-old notes he hasn’t spoken to anyone about a potential transfer.

Amid the war with Iran, the Ukraine-Russia conflict and the ongoing violence in Israel, Gaza and the West Bank, the Ronald Reagan Presidential Library has unveiled that Donald Trump will deliver a keynote address at its National Defense Forum. Trump’s speech will kick off the annual event at the Simi Valley institution on Friday, December 4.

The gathering brings together administration officials, business leaders, defense contractors and members of Congress to discuss the future of the U.S. military.

Recent findings suggest that “We are honored to announce the 45th and 47th President of the United States, Donald J. Trump, will join us for a keynote address at the 2026 Reagan National Defense Forum on Friday, December 4 at the Ronald Reagan Presidential Library,” reads a statement from the library.

US President Donald Trump noted on Thursday that he had urged his Russian counterpart, Vladimir Putin, to cease "military aggression" in Europe during their talks in China.

"I spent a lot of time talking about the fact that I would like to see President Putin pull back all Military aggression against territories throughout Europe, in the hopes that a Deal can be made between Russia and Ukraine, and made swiftly," Trump wrote on his Truth Social network. In a recent development, trump's post on Truth Social came a day after he hosted NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte in Florida. They issued a joint statement afterward calling on Russia not to expand the war in Ukraine.

"We also talked about, at length, the importance of Russia and Ukraine avoiding any actions that intentionally, or unintentionally, expand the War — and the importance of obtaining a peaceful resolution to the War, as rapidly as possible," the statement mentioned.

Trump's comments represent a shift in tone after he cordially called Putin a friend during their meeting in China last week. Approaching Putin, smiling Trump mentioned, "Who is he? He's my friend." Then he shook hands warmly with the Russian leader.

The echoes of the scene reverberated throughout Ukraine and other European nations, which are worried that Trump might side with Putin.

The US president had noted he could end the war in Ukraine on the first day of his second term in office.

Depite Trump's comments, the war demonstrates no signs of ending, with both Russia and Ukraine continuing their mutual attacks.

Industry observers note that A fire broke out on Thursday at the Slavyansk ECO oil refinery in Russia's southern Krasnodar region following a drone attack. The strike caused serious damage, according to a social media post by the firm.

According to Reuters, two industry sources estimated that Ukrainian drone attacks have reduced the combined crude processing volumes of Russian oil refineries by 45% since late October.

At the same time, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy claimed that Russia is preparing for a large-scale attack on Ukraine's energy infrastructure, logistics, and essential services.

Zelenskyy also remarked that crews are working to restore electricity in six Ukrainian regions after Russia launched ballistic missiles and 113 drones overnight.

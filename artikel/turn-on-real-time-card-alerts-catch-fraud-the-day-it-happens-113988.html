📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Card fraud costs you least when you catch it the same day, not weeks later on a statement. Turn on the real-time alerts in your card’s app for every purchase, online and phone orders, foreign charges, and declined transactions, then lock the card there the moment a charge looks wrong.

Set the alerts up before your next holiday order, since the stretch from Thanksgiving through Cyber Monday, Nov. 30, puts more charges than usual on your cards. A stranger’s charge is easy to miss among them when the statement arrives weeks later.

According to recent reports, A real-time alert sends each charge to your phone as it happens, so you check charges one at a time instead of a month’s worth at once. That speed matters because thieves sometimes run a small charge on a card and come back for more once it goes through, the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau warns. A $1.99 charge from a merchant you’ve never heard of looks like nothing on a statement, but an alert puts it on your phone the same afternoon, and you can lock the card that minute.

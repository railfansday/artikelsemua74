Two women are facing kidnapping charges after police alleged they abducted a 4-month-old baby girl while visiting her mother’s Fresh York home.

The baby, Xara Thompson, was found “safe and unharmed,” according to a Tuesday, November 24, Latest York State Police news release.

Dashnaw, 21, and Allen, 23, allegedly abducted the baby after Dashnaw arranged for Allen to drive with her to visit an acquaintance and her children in Sterling, a town located on Lake Ontario, police remarked.

“While inside the residence, Dashnaw allegedly took 4-month-old Xara Thompson without the mother’s knowledge or consent,” state police noted in the release.

The women then allegedly left the mother’s home with the baby, prompting authorities to issue an AMBER Alert as investigators searched for the child.

The alert stated, “The child was taken under circumstances that lead the police to believe that they are in imminent danger of serious bodily harm and/or death,” according to WWNY.

Authorities shared an image showing Dashnaw holding the infant.

Investigators ultimately found Dashnaw and Allen at a hotel in Plattsburgh, located more than 200 miles northeast from where the baby was taken from her mother, according to police.

Industry observers note that dashnaw and Allen were arraigned on second degree kidnapping charges on Tuesday, according to police. They were ordered to be held in the Cayuga County Jail without bail.

It wasn’t immediately clear whether the women had entered pleas or retained legal counsel.

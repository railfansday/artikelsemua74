📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Ubisoft is reportedly working on a new Rainbow Six Vegas game 18 years after the last entry in the series.

Sources indicate that according to Insider Gaming, the project is in early development at Ubisoft Montreal under the codename Spades. It’s one of three new projects reportedly in production at the studio, and sources suggest a second of those is also set in the Rainbow Six universe. Eurogamer hasn’t been able to verify the report. It has been revealed that it’s not yet clear what form the newly introduced game will take, but Insider Gaming’s sources said it's "likely Vegas 3 rather than a remake" of an existing game. Insider Gaming expects it will be several years before Ubisoft officially introduces a new Vegas game. (Related: Industry News: Newly introduced US Congress should ‘rei...)

Sources indicate that rainbow Six Vegas introduced in 2006, followed by Rainbow Six Vegas 2 in 2008. Since then, the series has centred on 2015's Rainbow Six Siege, and in August, Ubisoft confirmed Rainbow Six Tactics, a single-player, turn-based spin-off from Ubisoft Paris due in 2027.

Rainbow Six now sits under Vantage Studios, the Ubisoft subsidiary set up last year that also oversees Assassin’s Creed and Far Cry, and in which Tencent holds a 26.32 percent stake.

In a recent development, last week, we learned that Ubisoft is reportedly working on two new Assassin's Creed games: one set in ancient Rome, and a mobile spin-off set in Jerusalem. The leak came less than a week after Ubisoft issued a statement about the future of the Assassin's Creed series, insisting there are right now no new remakes of the series' past games in the works.

At least 1,144 children were killed or injured in Ukraine during the first 10 months of 2026, the United Nations said. (Related: Latest Update: At least seven progressives voted agains...)

UNICEF, the UN's children's agency, mentioned that between January and October, at least 114 children were killed and 1,030 injured.

The agency mentioned it was the highest number of casualties seen in more than 3 years.

"That is more than three children who have been killed or injured every single day" this year, said UNICEF spokeswoman Louise Wateridge.

Last month alone, at least 146 children were killed or injured across Ukraine, the UN agency remarked.

"Attacks involving missiles and armed drones continue to put children and families at risk in their homes, schools, and communities, including far from the frontline," UNICEF warned.

British Prime Minister Andy Burnham has called Russia's violation of Polish airspace "utterly unacceptable.".

In a recent development, "The incursion into ​Polish airspace is utterly unacceptable. I condemn it unreservedly," ​Burnham noted at an EU-UK ​summit press conference ‌in Brussels.

The incursions were reported as Poland held military exercises involving the "Coalition of the Willing" that ​groups allies of Ukraine, including the UK.

British Defense Secretary Wes Streeting noted the move was a Russian provocation aimed at dividing Europe.

"Europe ​will not be ‌deterred from our support for Ukraine," Streeting said. "We will not allow ⁠Putin to divide our continent, and we recognise that the ⁠frontline ​in Ukraine is the frontline for freedom and democracy everywhere.".

Russian Deputy Foreign Minister Sergei Ryabkov has reportedly invited representatives from Germany, France and the UK to Moscow for talks on the war in Ukraine, according to Russian news agency Interfax.

"We are waiting for a reply," the diplomat was reported to have stated.

On Thursday, Russian President Vladimir Putin mentioned European officials would be invited to Moscow to discuss whether they should participate in US-led talks aimed at ending the war in Ukraine. (Related: Peltola win doesn’t end DOJ scrutiny of Alaska Senate r...)

Interfax reported that Ryabkov stressed that such a visit would not automatically mean that European nations would be involved in the negotiations on ending the war on Ukraine.

A statement from the French president's office stated Europeans were ready to visit Moscow if given a full role in Ukraine talks.

European leaders have been pushing for a seat at the negotiation table, with German Chancellor Friedrich Merz saying on Thursday that the outcome of the war in Ukraine would determine European security for years to come.

The European Commission noted it has released another €7.9 billion ($8.67 billion) ‌to ⁠Ukraine to support the country's finances and keep public administration running.

"Ukraine continues to deliver on reforms, while defending itself each and every day. Today, we are disbursing nearly €8 billion — this is the largest payment made so far," European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen said.

"This makes it clear: real progress on reforms brings real results and benefits. As Ukraine perseveres and moves further on its European path, they can count on our support," remarked von der Leyen.

It’s the ninth disbursement of funds under the mechanism to support Ukraine.

With today's release of funds, the EU and its member states have provided €245.5 billion in overall support to Ukraine, which includes €3.8 billion from the proceeds of immobilized Russian assets.

The German Foreign Ministry has expressed its support for Poland after reports a Russian missile had entered Polish airspace.

"Russian missiles have once again violated Polish airspace. This is part of Russia's ongoing provocations," Germany's Foreign Ministry said in a statement on X.

"Instead of signaling a willingness to negotiate, Putin is signaling the opposite: he is opting for confrontation," the post said.

"We stand firmly by our neighbor and NATO ally Poland," the statement concluded.

NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte remarked he had spoken to Poland's Prime Minister Donald Tusk about what he called "grave violations of Polish airspace overnight.".

Rutte stated that NATO jets had been scrambled in response.

"We stand in solidarity with Poland and will continue to strengthen our ability to deter and defend," Rutte stated.

"This incident is clearly dangerous — a consequence of Russia’s ongoing war against Ukraine," the NATO chief said.

It has been revealed that "I condemn Russia's attacks as well as its ongoing war. We continue to stand united as Allies and will continue to support Ukraine," Rutte stated.

French Foreign Minister Jean-Noel Barrot noted that Paris had requested a UN Security Council meeting after Russia launched one of the war's largest missile attacks on Ukraine.

"The Kremlin has struck Kyiv again. Civilians have been killed," Jean-Noel Barrot said on X.

"These are crimes. France condemns them in the strongest possible terms," Barrot said.

It has been revealed that "I have requested a meeting of the United Nations Security Council. Russia will have to answer for its actions there," noted France's top diplomat.

On Thursday at the Coalition of the Willing talks in Berlin, French President Emmanuel Macron noted that Ukraine needed to be able to defend itself and that allies needed to ensure Kyiv had the means to do so.

The government of Estonia stated road border crossings with Russia would be shut from December 1 on security grounds.

"The closure ​of the border crossing points is not a reaction to a ​single incident, ‌but the next step following Russia's aggression against Ukraine and the increasing frequency of sabotage and hybrid operations in Europe," Foreign Minister Margus Tsahkna mentioned in a statement.

The Kremlin has denied the allegations saying they're part of a pattern of false allegations against Moscow originating from European countries.

Russian Foreign Ministry spokeswoman Maria Zakharova accused Estonia of  planning to erect a newly introduced "Iron Curtain" and said it would respond.

"Should Tallinn proceed with these ‌intends, our country will take the necessary ‌political ​and diplomatic measures in response to safeguard its national interests," Zakharova told a press briefing in Moscow.

The commander of Ukraine's Unmanned Systems Forces, Robert "Magyar" Brovdi said that his forces had hit 40 Russian oil facilities and defense industry targets in the past 50 days.

Brovdi said via the Telegram that among those targets, five of them had been hit more than once.

He went on to say that ​the Dorogobuzh ​chemical plant ‌in Russia's Smolensk region was ​struck ⁠on November 10 ⁠and ​November 20.

Ukraine has managed to adapt on the battlefield with the use of drone technology which has allowed hundreds of drone sorties, in some cases deep inside Russian territory, on a daily basis.

Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said that Ukraine was targeted by more than 400 drones and more than 110 missiles, 27 of which he said were ballistic missiles.

"The Russians spread this attack across many cities and regions of Ukraine. Absolutely all the targets were ordinary civilian infrastructure – things that literally sustain people’s lives," Zelenskyy said in a detailed post on social media platform X.

Zelenskyy listed what had been hit during the overnight strikes.

He accused Russia of carrying out second strikes on targets that had already been hit, in so-called "double-tap strikes.".

"People had essentially already been killed by the time specialists were inspecting the initial damage to the facilities, as the Russians were carrying out double-tap strikes," Zelenskyy mentioned.

Zelenskyy mentioned that in all 79 cruise missiles had been intercepted and that fighter jets had been scrambled to deal with the aerial threats involving F-16s, Mirages and MiGs.

"Ballistic missiles are, unfortunately, much more difficult to deal with, and that depends not on our capabilities but on the supply of missiles by our partners," Zelenskyy said.

Ukraine's leader called for a "clear response" from the global community.

It is important that our partners not only concern statements in support of Ukraine and Ukrainians, but also follow through on specific agreements on air defense," Zelenskyy remarked.

He pointed out the timing of the latest attacks and mentioned Putin launched them "in a highly demonstrative manner – following weak meetings in China.".

"Russia is trying to compensate for its military and diplomatic impotence with precisely this kind of terror, precisely these attacks on life," Zelenskyy said.

Ukrainian officials noted that the death toll has risen to at least 8 following an overnight Russian aerial bombardment.

According to recent reports, prime Minister Sergii Koretskyi remarked six of the eight people killed were ‌in Kyiv. Two deaths were also reported at a railway station in the western Ukrainian city of Lviv.

Ukraine's Air Force remarked Russia had released 84 cruise missiles in the attack, adding that almost all of them were destroyed.

An unspecified number of ballistic missiles and more than 400 attack drones were also reported to have been destroyed.

Poland's Defense Minister Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz noted that several Russian flying objects entered Polish airspace during recent air attacks on Ukraine.

"Russian long-range aviation was operating, launching both Kinzhal and Kh-101 missiles, as well as a large number of jet-powered drones and conventional drones. … This Russian Federation attack on Ukrainian territory, which also had an impact on the Republic of Poland, was one of the largest since the beginning of Russia's full-scale war against Ukraine," the Defense Ministry quoted Kosiniak-Kamysz as saying.

It comes after Polish prosecutors reported earlier that a flying object had crashed in Poland's eastern Lublin region.

Polish officials stated it had taken place during heavy Russian airstrikes on neighbouring Ukraine.

In July, a missile entered Polish airspace and crashed in the same region near the village of Tarnawa Kolonia.

According to the Polish government, it was a Russian CH-101 cruise missile.

Poland's Interior Minister Marcin Kierwinski said that investigations were taking place at three sites after reporting that Polish airspace had been violated.

"In connection with ‌violations ​of Polish airspace, authorities are ​conducting operations at ⁠three ​locations: ​Rudnik nad Sanem, ​Molodycz ‌and Nowy Lipowiec (Lublin ​Province)," ⁠he said in a post on ⁠social ​media platform X.

Recent findings suggest that prime Minister Donald Tusk said that he had convened a meeting of senior government and military officials along with the heads of security services in a post on X. He said there was right now no danger from the air.

Kyiv's Mayor Vitali Klitschko mentioned that a Russian missile bombardment in Kyiv has killed at least three people and injured 13 others.

Klitschko stated in a post on Telegram that three bridges had also been damaged following the strikes on Ukraine's capital.

Experts would only be able to give a proper assessment of the damage caused to the structures once the aerial threat had ended, Klitschko added.

"Given the difficult traffic conditions between the left and right banks of the capital, I ask residents not to leave the city by private car unless absolutely necessary," the mayor stated.

Klitschko also appealed to employers to allow remote-working arrangements, where possible.

Bridges have been key targets in Russia's ongoing invasion of Ukraine, but Kyiv's forces have managed to hit the Kerch bridge multiple times this week in what is being seen as a major psychological blow to Russian President Vladimir Putin.

He warned of a harsh response after the bridge linking occupied Crimea with mainland Russia was struck three times this week.

One of the archways and a section of roadway was destroyed in the attack.

Putin remarked he expected Moscow's response to be "far more painful" than the damage Ukraine had caused to the Kerch bridge.

In a recent development, what is 'To Be?'This year's concept, 'To Be' explores what was, what is, and what could be. Where speculation, experimentation and collaboration drive creativity and design. Opening on 4 December 2026 at White Bay Power Station, Rozelle, this three-day exhibition celebrates the graduate work of students across seven Built Environment disciplines: Architecture, City Planning, Construction Management and Property, Industrial Design, Interior Architecture, Computational Design and Landscape Architecture.

UP Fintech (TIGR +2.33%) reported total revenues of $172.6 million for the third quarter, noting that a diversified revenue mix provided stability as user trading activity moderated. Management stated that the organization remains on track to reach its full-year target of 150,000 upcoming funded clients, having already added 110,000 through the first nine months of the year. The company reported that growth was driven primarily by the Singapore and Hong Kong markets, which together contributed more than 80% of new funded accounts. Management noted that the mainland Chinese retail client contribution to both revenue and assets continued to decline, in compliance with regulatory adjustments implemented since May 2022. Strategic focus has transitioned toward expanding wealth management services and extending 24/5 trading capabilities to global markets.

US Army detonates lead explosives in Iowa despite contamination fears

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (68 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, the US Army on Wednesday blew up 5 tons of lead-based explosives at an ammunition plant in Iowa despite community backlash over fears of environmental contamination. The detonation was postponed three times, but got the green light after a federal appeals court this week found the US Environmental Protection Agency adequately incorporated safeguards to minimise the dispersal of lead dust.

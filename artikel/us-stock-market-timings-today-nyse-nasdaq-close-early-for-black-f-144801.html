Sources indicate that US stocks are back in action on Friday, November 27, after the Thanksgiving break, but traders get only a brief window. The NYSE and Nasdaq open as usual at 9:30 am Eastern and wrap up at 1 pm Eastern, which works out to an 8 pm start and an 11:30 pm finish for investors in India. That three-and-a-half-hour session is roughly half the length of a normal trading day. A few markets run slightly longer. Eligible NYSE options keep trading until 11:45 pm IST, and some extended-hours venues stay active until 3:30 am IST on Saturday. Bond trading, covering Treasuries, corporate and municipal debt and mortgage-backed securities, is set to end at 12:30 am IST under SIFMA’s guidance. After the weekend, full hours return on Monday, November 30. December brings one more shortened day, with a 1 pm Eastern close on Christmas Eve, followed by a full shutdown on Christmas Day before trading restarts on December 28.

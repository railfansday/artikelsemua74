US unveils proposed tariffs on major partners, including EU and China

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (87 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, the United States on Wednesday unveiled proposed tariffs on 14 economies including the European Union and China, after a months-long probe into alleged excess production -- the latest move by US President Donald Trump to remake Washington's trade relationships. The duties, proposed at up to 20 percent, come over allegations the economies "are promoting production and capacity divorced from the realities of demand and market principles," a senior US official said on condition of anonymity. FRANCE 24's Camille Knight.

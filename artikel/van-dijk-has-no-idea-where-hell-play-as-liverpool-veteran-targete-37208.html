Virgil van Dijk has been heavily linked with a move to Milan, and admits he has ‘no idea’ where he will be playing next season, but the Liverpool veteran is confident he can ‘play for many more years.’.

The 35-year-old is known to be a primary target for Rossoneri and RedBird owner Gerry Cardinale, who had already tried to sign him last summer.

It is widely reported in Italy that the push was only postponed, and Van Dijk will wear the Milan jersey at San Siro eventually, perhaps even as a free agent if his contract with Liverpool is allowed to run down on June 30, 2027.

“The main thing for me is that I feel good and I’m looking after myself and I still have many more years in my opinion,” Van Dijk told Sky Sports UK.

In a recent development, “Whatever’s going to happen after this season, we’ll see. At this moment of time, if anyone says that or put out that they know what I’m going to do next season, it’s untrue because at this moment of time, speaking to you, I’ve got no idea either.”. Milan’s pursuit of Van Dijk may well depend on whether they manage to qualify for the Champions League next season.

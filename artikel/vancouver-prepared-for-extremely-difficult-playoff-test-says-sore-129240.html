The top seed from the Western Conference won Game 1 of the best-of-three series 2-0, with Edier Ocampo and Bruno Caicedo scoring either side of half-time.

The Whitecaps, who reached the MLS Cup showpiece last year, can secure progression to the next round of the 2026 postseason with victory in Game 2 at America First Field. (Related: Scientists find “field” of washing machines in ocean du...)

Vancouver defeated FC Dallas in this round of the MLS Cup playoffs last term in two games, but were taken to penalties in Game 2.

Sorensen believes RSL will pose a serious threat and has urged his side to remember their exploits against Dallas in 2025.

"Game 2 is a tricky one for those who won Game 1; it's really about seeing if you can close it out against a team that plays with desperation, and we've seen how many teams had to go to three games to advance," Sorensen told reporters.

"This is going to be extremely difficult, but we know that we have the experience from last year in Dallas.

"In this league, it can be very difficult playing away. We're going to play up with some altitude, and we're going to play probably in some cold weather.

"It's going to be a different scenario than we played under the other day in BC, and that's what makes this league beautiful as well.

"We're facing a team that has to win this game to stay alive in the season, and we know that it's going to be a bit difficult.".

Recent findings suggest that vancouver's 2-0 win over RSL in Game 1 was their fifth straight victory in the fixture. That is Vancouver's second-longest win streak in MLS play against any opponent, trailing only their current six-game run against Sporting Kansas City.

Industry observers note that the Whitecaps have won three of their six MLS Cup playoff matches since the beginning of 2025 (D2 L1). That matches Vancouver's win total in their 14 MLS Cup playoff games prior to that (W3 D2 L9).

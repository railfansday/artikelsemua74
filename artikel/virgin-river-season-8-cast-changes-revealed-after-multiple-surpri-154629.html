Virgin River will look a little different when it returns for season 8 in the wake of multiple cast exits.

Executive producer Richard Keith teased several latest additions — starting with Mitchell Slaggert, who has been cast as a new EMT.

In a recent development, “He’s brought on as part of the new hospital integration, and so he and a new doctor, Astrid, who’s played by Natasha Calis, come into town, and I think it’s just a nice chance to see Mel sort of get to empathize with how maybe Doc felt when she first came to town,” Keith told Swooon on Thursday, November 26. “Mel is the hometown girl, and there’s a new girl on the block. Mitchell is just such a breath of fresh air as a performer. He’s really sweet and really present and really fun, and it’s a nice new energy to bring to the cast.”. Keith also confirmed that Austin Nichols would be back as Mel’s doctor ex.

“Austin’s wonderful on the show, and I think his relationship with Jack and Mel is complicated and really interesting to see how it unfolds and how it brings complications,” executive producer Erin Cardillo teased before Keith added, “I think, in a good way, it plays out in an unexpected way. It’s not the standard somebody’s ex comes back into the picture, right?”. (Related: Latest Update: Think AI "knows" what it’s doing? Scient...)

He continued: “Because of their son and all of the extenuating circumstances and the hopeful nature of Virgin River, the story takes some unexpected paths, which is cool.”.

Based on Robyn Carr’s book series, Virgin River follows the lives of residents living in a small town in Northern California, including Mel (Alexandra Breckenridge) and Jack (Martin Henderson).

The hit series, which premiered in 2019, also stars Colin Lawrence, Annette O’Toole, Tim Matheson, Benjamin Hollingsworth, Sarah Dugdale, Zibby Allen and Kai Bradbury.

In a recent development, after seven seasons, there have been some major departures from the Netflix show. Grayson Gurnsey left as Ricky after season 4, and Mark Ghanimé, who played Cameron, had a surprising exit once season 6 was released.

Recent findings suggest that “I think this season saw a little bit of a sendoff for Cameron,” showrunner Patrick Sean Smith exclusively told Us in December 2024. “In terms of Mark, the actor who I adore, he unfortunately won’t be coming back for season 7.”.

Smith didn’t rule out a potential return for the character in the future. “But that’s not to say that Cameron’s not a part of our world forever,” he added. “I would love to bring him back, and bring his character back when the story presents itself.”.

In March 2025, Virgin River returned for season 7, and it was later confirmed that Marco Grazzini will not reprise his role as Mike.

In another twist, Lauren Hammersley, who has played Charmaine since the show premiered, is also not expected to come back for season 8.

Recent findings suggest that “For me, I’m just trying to look [at] the longevity of the show and making sure that the characters that we’re keeping have enough story engine in them to keep going,” Smith told Deadline at the time. “So I can’t say there are any specific cast shakeups planned, but I think we will have to start to — as characters run their course — make some decisions and bring in some fresh blood.”.

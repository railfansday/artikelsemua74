From the red-carpet heavy-hitters to Hollywood’s next generation of stars and the stylists making it all happen, the honorees on our 2026 best-dressed list all share a love for a great outfit. It certainly helps that we do too—which is why we’re coming in with the cold, hard facts: We’re breaking down their style signifiers, one shoppable piece at a time.

Sources indicate that the names on this year’s list range from celebrity street style regulars—see: Jennifer Lawrence—to relative newcomers like actor Sarah Pidgeon and singer Gracie Abrams. Model and CFDA judge Paloma Elsesser and actor Ayo Edebiri both appear for their second year in a row on Vogue’s Best Dressed list.

Ahead, you’ll have the chance to browse through some of the honorees’ best looks of 2026, with a snapshot of the key pieces that made those outfits stand out to us—and in turn, dominate the sartorial conversation at Vogue HQ. Some stars championed CFDA/Vogue Fashion Fund nominees early on: Edebiri and Lee both wore Lii for press appearances this year, while Lawrence sported one of Colleen Allen’s velvet house coats with a white tee and slippers on a mild fall day in Fresh York. Others are vintage fiends at heart—Elsesser, who self-styled her Vogue Shopping cover shoot in September—chief among them; her everyday looks comprise a mix of vintage Junya Watanabe and Comme des Garçons and emerging designers. Below, a snapshot of the wardrobes that make up Vogue’s Best Dressed list of 2026, complete with the key pieces needed to shop their signature looks and make them your own.

According to recent reports, A regular fixture in our weekly celeb outfits roundups, Jennifer Lawrence is known for her effortless brand of messy-chic, cool-mom-at-drop-off looks, often centered around a ball cap and a slubby vintage tee. The Row and Khaite are mainstay labels in rotation, though, let the record show that she loves mixing more eclectic pieces—like her Colleen Allen velvet house coat—with simple basics (Leset is noted a go-to). According to recent reports, sarah Pidgeon’s debut on Vogue’s Best Dressed List comes as no surprise: Since playing the role of ’90s style icon, Carolyn Bessette-Kennedy on Ryan Murphy’s Love Story, Pidgeon has taken method acting to a new level with method dressing. For the majority of her public appearances, Pidgeon has stepped out in pieces reflective of Bessette-Kennedy’s refrained style, like a Yohji Yamamoto dress, a collarless Aflalo leather trench, and an embellished mint green slip from Chanel.

Model and CFDA judge Paloma Elsesser is a modern cool-girl, known for her IYKYK approach to dressing . A champion of emerging designers like the London-based, Central Saint Martins graduate Goyagoma, her eclectic style is typically supplemented by vintage grails from the likes Junya Watanabe and Comme des Garçons. For those who worship at the altar of “quiet luxury,” Elsesser eschews that with bold prints, vivid color, and a style that’s all her own.

Like her repertoire of acting roles (TV: The Bear, film: Clarissa, Broadway: Proof), Ayo Edebiri’s style shows as much range as her career choices—from repping emerging designers like Giovanna Flores in a patchwork dress to Conner Ives in pinstripe suiting (yes, the same Ives whose fur-trimmed coat just broke the internet), to her more mature choices for the red carpet, such as Chanel and The Row. Edebiri doesn’t limit herself to one style or silhouette—she can go from feminine dressing to masculine suiting, just as easily as she can shift into various characters (Pacino, anyone?).

In a recent development, known for her starring roles in this year’s Toy Story 5, as well as in Tron: Ares, and Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse, actor Greta Lee’s sartorial choices take a page out of her action-flick lineup, albeit, with a fashion-forward twist. A fan of bold color-blocking, Lee has worn CFDA/Vogue Fashion Fund winner Lii, as well as a fire-engine red fringed gown by High Sport to the London premiere of Toy Story 5, while making bold fashion choices with Marc Jacobs’s iconic blue sequin strapless top from the spring 2026 collection.

It’s been a banner year for the actress Carey Mulligan—in March, she accepted a royal honor in the form of a CBE (Commander of the Order of the British Empire) from King Charles III for her services to drama. Even at Windsor Castle, she used the opportunity to show up in a royally-fashionable ensemble (one, we might say, was fit for a king!) wearing a jacket and trousers from The Row, accessorized with Jacques Marie Mage sunglasses. Then, for the season two premiere of Beef in which she plays a starring role, Mulligan showed up in a pixelated floral skirt and brocade bustier top from designer Julian Klausner’s fall 2026 collection for Dries Van Noten. Her red carpet looks are a mix of sumptuous color, pattern, and texture, illustrated best in a full Colleen Allen look composed of a ruffled velvet top with a coordinating silk column skirt in sunburnt orange.

Another one in Lii’s fashionable girl-gang, actor Whitney Peak (who stars as Lenore Dove Baird in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping) appreciates a color-blocked ensemble, whether it’s for a magazine launch or a press appearance (the latter saw her in London-based designer Torishéju). Her color palettes skews minimalist—think neutrals punctuated by flashes of red—but the silhouettes she gravitates towards are anything but. Case in point: the knitted black Diotima dress she wore to the Academy Museum Gala in Los Angeles was a maximalist’s dream.

Actor Tessa Thompson has been delivering on the red carpet for years now — and her style only continues to bring the wow-factor, from being a fellow fan of Giovanna Flores’s patchwork dress to Kallmeyer’s tailored suiting. She’s known to love a sharp, structured shoulder, and has a penchant for contrasting a delicate silk dress with a strong leather jacket paired. With her Golden Globes nomination for Hedda, starring in the Netflix thriller His & Hers, and a Broadway debut in Fear of 13, co-starring Adrien Brody, Thompson’s style is one that delivers as much variety and star power as her resume.

According to recent reports, our mother in style, Tracee Ellis Ross’s wardrobe is as bright as her star power. With this year’s Broadway debut in Every Brilliant Thing and a daytime Emmy for her show, Solo Traveling, she stepped up her style game even further, with the help of her stylist Law Roach. The Girlfriends and Black-ish star has been experimenting with color and texture as of late, stepping out in highly-saturated ensembles that span an embellished Prada skirt and button-down (look 22 from the fall runway), a marigold tasseled gown by Balenciaga (designed by Pierpaolo Piccioli for spring 2027), and punchy Dries Van Noten (look 35, also from SS27).

After returning to the stage this year with a 16-show sold out residency at Paris’s Plénitude Arena, Celine Dion delivered what the people wanted—and so much more. With daily paparazzi shots that showed off her good humor in style, she stepped out in looks that ranged from suede Jacquemus to all-black leather Tom Ford. Quintessential French design, in feather-trimmed Dior and custom Chanel (she wore a gown that was recreation of her 1997 Oscars dress) rounded out this Canadian in Paris’s sojourn.

In a recent development, the former First Lady has been having fun with fashion as of late—donning a Thom Browne suit set for a ribbon-cutting at the Barack Obama Presidential Center one day to a custom Acne Studios skirt printed with a photo of her late mother, Marian Robinson, the next. At the US Open this summer, she wore a red, white, and navy blue striped Chanel dress from the resort 2027 collection.

It has been revealed that ever since the 2026 CFDA Fashion Awards, we haven’t been able to get Gracie Abrams as a gothic vision in Colleen Allen out of our minds. The singer has been serving looks long before that though, stepping out in her signature mix of vintage tees, Conner Ives rugby polos and trousers, and an enviable collection of Chanel pumps. Equal parts comfy and stylish, she’s known to wear a quirky mashup of styles and designers.

According to recent reports, walmart raised its full-year sales and profit outlook on November 19, 2026, after third-quarter revenue grew 5.2% to $188.8 billion. The retailer reported on its earnings call that U.S. usage of Sparky, its AI shopping agent, grew 129% year over year, although GAAP earnings per share fell to $0.62 as investment results swung from gains to losses.

Sparky-related baskets expanded 40% as customers found more items, chief executive John Furner said. Walmart added voice and image shopping and languages including Spanish and French, and released the assistant in Mexico, Canada and at Sam’s Club in the U.S. (Related: Latest Update: How we sped up transformer inference 100...)

Walmart’s approach connects agent-led product discovery to its stores, inventory and fulfillment staff. The company has also unveiled Agent Profiles, which will let customers set permissions and spending limits, see agent purchases separately and turn off access, while the Walmart Agent Gateway gives approved agents a defined way to interact with its platform. Global eCommerce sales rose 17%, including 23% growth at Walmart U.S. Stores fulfilled more than 80% of Walmart U.S. eCommerce orders, extending their role beyond in-store shopping into pickup, delivery and returns.

Walmart U.S. eCommerce achieved an incremental margin, the profit rate on additional sales, above 12% in the quarter, with a low-double-digit range the general expectation going forward, chief financial officer John David Rainey remarked. Advertising and membership revenue, denser delivery routes, expedited-delivery fees and supply-chain automation drove the improvement; excluding advertising, the business was close to breakeven.

Operating income increased 16.0% to $7.8 billion, while adjusted EPS reached $0.65 from $0.62 a year earlier. The adjusted measure excludes investment gains and losses and other specified items; the swing in those investments helps explain why GAAP EPS fell even as operating results improved.

For fiscal 2027, Walmart expects net-sales growth of 4.9% to 5.2% in constant currency, which excludes exchange-rate fluctuations, compared with the August 20, 2026 forecast of 4.0% to 5.0%. Adjusted operating-income growth guidance increased to 8.0% to 8.6%, from 7.0% to 8.5%.

Sources indicate that the revision carries forward year-to-date outperformance and assumes underlying sales trends and share gains continue in the fourth quarter, Rainey said. Flipkart’s shifted Big Billion Days event is also expected to benefit fourth-quarter sales growth.

Conversational queries and prompts differ from historical shopping behavior, and Walmart is still learning how marketers and brands can place products before customers in agent-led shopping, chief growth officer Seth Dallaire stated. Customers’ desire to discover new products and brands has remained consistent across physical, digital and agentic shopping, and Walmart is examining brand advertising in those environments.

According to recent reports, sparky’s expansion gives Walmart a larger role in product discovery, while store fulfillment and delivery density improve the economics around it. The open question is how effectively Walmart can build brand advertising around conversational discovery as agent-led shopping expands across its markets.

“We were less good defensively”: Zinédine Zidane gives the reasons for his first defeat with the France team

Despite Michael Olise’s opener, the French team sank at San Siro against an Italy that was initially well regrouped, then very aggressive in the midfield (3-1). However, the Blues had the opportunity to hurt their opponents until the hour mark, finding the post twice, before offering nothing more as soon as the Italians took the lead on the scoreboard.

It is therefore the first setback for Zinédine Zidane who started with three victories and a draw during the first meeting. A coach logically frustrated after the meeting. “Obviously (frustrated, editor’s note) because there were some good things, especially in the first half. Afterwards, we did less good things. Particularly in duels and in 1v1s, we were a little less good. It’s part of learning, things where you have to improve, that’s for sure.”he started by saying. Sources indicate that he especially noted that his team lacked character since Italy led. “The start of the second half is good too. It’s true that when we were in difficulty, we didn’t know how to react and stay within the framework set up. We did it in the first, and afterwards when they took the advantage, we dispersed a little, also defensively”he explained. But he is not worried about the future. “No, we’re not worried, it’s part of football. It’s also having moments like that to see how we will react. Better to have them now. We are still here, in the race. Tonight is not a non-match. We did not know how to react in complicated moments, there is an area for improvement. Rayan is right, I told them, we must not panic, we have chances, possession, we were less good defensively. It became more fragile as the match progressed. ». For Zizou, no panic, with the prospect of a home match, in Bordeaux, against Turkey, last in the group with 0 points. If successful, and if Italy does not win in Belgium, then France will regain first place in the group. “What I told them was that it wasn’t a completely deserved defeat. We will analyze this when we are in Bordeaux, we will come back to this one. Honestly, we just have to tell ourselves that we are resting and we are going to refocus on the last match that remains for us”. With Kylian Mbappé probably back in the starting lineup. “We’ll see tomorrow, but that’s the idea”.

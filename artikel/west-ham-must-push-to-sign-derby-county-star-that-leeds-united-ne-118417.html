West Ham United have found life back in the Championship for the first time in over a decade a struggle to adapt to, as new head coach Scott Parker must utilise the January transfer window to set things right in East London.

Upon their relegation to the Championship last season, West Ham haven't exactly had the start to life back in the second tier as they would've hoped for. (Related: Stop treating agents as one-off projects and start runn...)

The Hammers failed to win any of their first three games in charge, and after a poor string of results, Nuno Espírito Santo was replaced at the helm by former Fulham, Bournemouth, and Burnley boss Scott Parker.

Parker hasn't fared much better as yet, failing to win any of his four games in charge against Cardiff City, Blackburn Rovers, Preston North End, and Portsmouth, and in the past couple of games, the 46-year-old boss has made a big team selection decision.

Danish shot-stopper Mads Hermansen has endured a tricky time at West Ham, joining the club in the summer of 2025 for around £20 million from Leicester City, and spent his first year at the club battling Alphonse Areola for a place between the sticks. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Upon their relegation, Hermansen has since become the Hammers' undisputed number one, at least up until recently, as according to FotMob, the 26-year-old has allowed in 4.24 more goals than expected at this stage, subsequently being dropped for 20-year-old Finlay Herrick.

Herrick, who had only ever played in the National League for Boreham Wood before his introduction to the first team in the past couple of games, has been thrust into the deep end by Parker and has struggled to adapt to life in the Championship.

With Hermansen not trusted, Herrick inexperienced, and Areola last making a competitive appearance back in April, a newly introduced number one should be on the cards this winter, as an obvious option is surely right in front of them.

It was reported by Alan Nixon via Patreon earlier in November that Parker was looking to add another left-back and centre-forward to his ranks at West Ham, with the former England and Hammers midfielder already looking ahead to January.

He will need to turn results around quickly; otherwise, he may not make it to the end of the year as things stand, and if he does indeed turn the tide at the London Stadium, a upcoming goalkeeper must become a priority, as Derby County's Jacob Widell Zetterström should be targeted.

Zetterström, 28, according to FotMob, has kept out 3.63 more goals than expected at this stage, and continued to outperform his expected numbers despite Derby's struggles at the foot of the division.

A fully-fledged Swedish international, often battling Stoke City's Viktor Johansson for the gloves in Graham Potter's national team, Zetterström is in his prime years as a goalkeeper, and still may well have some of his best displays ahead of him, as one key contract factor could make this deal a lot easier to complete for the Hammers.

Zetterström joined Derby in the summer of 2024, having spent the entirety of his career to that date in his native Sweden with Djurgarden, and ever since his move to Pride Park, has been the club's undisputed number one.

Signing a three-year deal, the terms that the 28-year-old signed some two-and-a-half years ago are due to expire next summer, with no indication as to whether he will sign an extension as yet.

Premier League outfit Leeds United were interested in signing the Swede over the summer, whilst Italian top-flight side Monza reportedly registered an interest, as the Rams stood firm.

With Derby in a relegation battle at the foot of the division, Rams boss John Eustace will be incredibly reluctant to lose his star shot-stopper before the end of the season, but with a serious risk of losing him for nothing, he may have no other choice but to sell, especially if an acceptable offer arrives.

West Ham certainly have the money to tempt him away from Pride Park, and for a cut-price fee, Parker can secure one of the Championship's best this winter and fix a key problem area in the process.

Industry observers note that do you think West Ham should look to sign Zetterström from Derby this winter, or should they be looking elsewhere? How much would it take to sign the Swede this winter? Share your thoughts below!

With four games without a win to begin his tenure in East London, Parker is quickly realising the gravity of the task laid out before him, and will be hoping to get around to the January window ASAP.

It's clear the Hammers lack a trusted shot-stopper as things stand, and as one of his first actions this winter, the 46-year-old must look to fix this as soon as possible to instil some confidence into his back line.

With Burnley, Parker could rely upon James Trafford to perform beyond expectations - with West Ham, he doesn't have that luxury, and he should look to fix this in the form of Zetterström this winter.

West Ham's midweek defeat to Portsmouth saw their winless run stretch to six games as they slipped to 12th in the Championship.

The new Irons boss has made it very clear that he wants to add a prolific striker to his ranks in the upcoming winter transfer window.

Fullkrug has been banging in the goals away from East London and it would have been ideal to welcome him back into the fold to help improve their fortunes.

The Hammers do not have the option to recall the 33-year-old, however, and Bremen are instead keen on making his move permanent in January.

Fullkrug joined West Ham from Borussia Dortmund for around £27million in 2024 after he had led the line for Germany in their home European Championships.

The burly striker could only register three goals and two assists in his debut Irons campaign as fans questioned his efforts, then played nine times last term before a loan spell at Milan.

His return to boyhood club Werder has been nothing short of extraordinary so far, with 13 goals in 12 appearances across league and cup matches.

Fullkrug became the first player to score 10 times in the Bundesliga this season last weekend as he bagged in a 4-0 win over Hamburg.

He has reached a new level, and aside from goalscoring, is also among the top-ranked Bundesliga strikers so far for chances created (1.72), duels won (6.14) and touches in the opposition box (6.89) per 90 minutes.

Meanwhile, back in England, sources have exclusively revealed to Football Insider that Scott Parker is prioritising a latest striker addition for West Ham above anything else in the new year.

It would have been so easy to recall Fullkrug, the most in-form player on their books, like most clubs would choose to do, but the club incredibly failed to insert a recall clause in his loan deal to Werder.

They will now be forced to spend big midway through the season on a newly introduced centre-forward, and may even be too far off the play-offs for it to matter by then.

If West Ham's mistake wasn't already obvious, the 33-year-old's return to the Germany squad this month only put more salt into their wounds.

Recent findings suggest that newly introduced national team manager Klopp was very complimentary of Fullkrug as he explained why he had called him up to the press, saying: "Fulle has performed very well during this period, scoring all kinds of goals - almost all of them spectacular, but in very different ways. He has had an incredible influence on Werder Bremen’s play, not just through his goals, but also through the way he conducts himself on the pitch.

"Of course, the experience he brings with him is a huge help in that regard. When you feel comfortable, when you feel at home, you can see what happens. He has that feeling of belonging that we generally want to give our players, even when we don’t have them together very often.".

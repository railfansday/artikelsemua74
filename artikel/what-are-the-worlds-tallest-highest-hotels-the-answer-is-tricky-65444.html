There’s something I really love about staying in a hotel room on a really high floor, where you’re so high up that you almost feel like you’re in an airplane. So, what are the tallest and highest hotels in the world? I want to talk about that in this post, and also discuss why the answer isn’t that straightforward.

The question of which hotel is the tallest and highest isn’t as straightforward as you might assume. And even once we do define it, there’s the question of the extent to which the answer is actually useful. First let’s cover the distinction between the tallest hotel and the highest hotel. Generally speaking, the tallest hotel would be defined as the tallest building where a large percent of the structure is made up of the hotel. Most commonly, I see people suggest that this would include situations where at least 80% of the building is the hotel.

This is different than the highest hotel, which would be the hotel that is highest above the ground, even if it only takes up a small percentage of a mixed-use building, where there might be apartments, residences, etc. (Related: Latest Update: KFC just released fried cheese with four...)

In other words, a 50-story building that consists exclusively of a hotel would fall into the former category, while a 110-story building where the top 10 floors are a hotel would fall into the latter category.

It has been revealed that let me of course mention one downside of very tall standalone hotels — you could end up being on a very low floor. After all, if a high-rise consists exclusively (or almost exclusively) of hotel facilities, some people will end up on very low floors. The other downside, of course, is that these hotels are necessarily huge. If you prefer boutique hotels, you won’t find any on this list.

According to recent reports, at the moment, the tallest hotel in the world is Ciel Tower Dubai. The 1,042-room hotel opened in 2024, rises to a height of 1,227 feet (374 meters), and has 82 floors.

As you can see, it’s interesting (but perhaps not surprising) that all four of the world’s tallest hotels are in Dubai.

The Conrad Shanghai, meanwhile, is located in the Shimao International Plaza, and this is the perfect example of how it’s tough to define what is technically the tallest. While the building it’s in rises to such a high height, roughly 25% of that height is the spire. So technically it’s a very tall building, but the usable space isn’t as high as you’d assume.

If you ask me, the world’s highest hotels are much more interesting than the world’s tallest hotels. That’s because the world’s highest hotels typically take up the top floors of some gravity-defying skyscrapers, and therefore, they can also be boutique properties.

In a recent development, currently, the highest hotel is the Rosewood Guangzhou. The 251-room hotel opened in 2019, and it’s located at the top of the CTF Finance Centre Guangzhou, which reaches a height of 1,739 feet (530 meters).

As the inverse of the above, I should mention that the Armani Hotel Dubai is located within the Burj Khalifa, which is the world’s tallest building. However, it’s located at the very bottom of the building, so that’s hardly that useful, for these purposes, since the hotel only goes as high as 39 floors.

It has been revealed that lastly, in the interest of being thorough, if we’re going to define a hotel as being “highest” based on the altitude above sea level, then we’d of course end up with a completely different list. Hotel Tayka del Desierto is located near the Salar de Uyuni salt flats in Bolivia. It’s at an altitude of 14,800 feet (4,500 meters), so it’s generally considered to be the highest up hotel in the world.

I suppose depending on how we want to define “hotel,” there would probably be some simple guest houses at Everest Base Camp that go even higher, but I don’t think that’s within the spirit of what I’m trying to cover here.

While I’ve long been interested in this, I enjoyed researching this a bit more closely, and actually having a (sort of) definitive list. Ciel Tower Dubai is the world’s tallest hotel, while the Rosewood Guangzhou is the world’s highest hotel (unless you want to consider the 120th floor of the J Hotel Shanghai separately from the rest of the property).

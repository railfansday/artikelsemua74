According to recent reports, seven of the Bible’s money principles translate into habits you can start today: blueprint before you spend, save for lean times, stay out of debt, spread your risk, grow wealth slowly, give generously, and be content. Pick the one your finances need most and put it to work this month.

Recent findings suggest that most of the habits below can run on autopilot once you set them up, through an automatic transfer, a payroll deduction, or a recurring gift. Then you follow the principle every month without deciding again.

Each principle below comes with the verse behind it and one habit that puts it to work.

It has been revealed that to find your starting point, look through last month’s bank and card statements. Pick the principle those statements show you following least, and set up its habit this week. For a household with no savings cushion, that’s Proverbs 21:20. With essential bills of $2,600 a month, an automatic $50 transfer to savings every Friday sets aside one month of bills in a year.

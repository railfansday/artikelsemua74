One of GTA's many gags is back in GTA 6: the ol' bigfoot sighting. Well, technically I think it's a "skunk ape," real-life Florida's maybe real-life boogeyman "cousin" to Bigfoot. Don't get that cryptid family tree twisted. How do I know it's skunk ape? I saw the thing, I swear, and there was a cloud of green stink wafting off of it. Here's exactly where it's at if you want your own smelly primate encounter.

The skunk ape sighting I've found so far is just across Gambit Bay from Jason's initial house. Hop in Jason's boat on the dock outside his place and set course for the dead end road on the inside of this peninsula. Before you even make land you'll get a prompt telling you to use Focus Mode to follow the source of the noises.

Industry observers note that hark! It's skunk ape! The creature hightails its stinky hide off into the mangrove-y woods and you'll need to keep using focus mode to chase after. You'll lose sight of it and instead find some creepy effigy thing standing beside a tree. Check it out, get stink-apacitated, and get your not so informative close-up sighting of bigfoot's massive feet. (Related: How to train a upcoming language model from scratch usi...)

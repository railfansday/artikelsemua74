It has been revealed that historically, the S&P 500 has delivered an average annual return of around 10%, assuming that dividends are reinvested. That doesn't guarantee similar results in the future, but it does give us a useful benchmark for estimating where the Vanguard S&P 500 ETF could be in 30 years. Let's assume you invest $10,000 in the Vanguard S&P 500 ETF today, reinvest all the dividends you get along the way, and leave it untouched for 30 years.

If it can match its historical 10% annualized return, that initial investment would grow to approximately $174,500, more than 17 times your starting amount without contributing another dollar over the subsequent three decades.

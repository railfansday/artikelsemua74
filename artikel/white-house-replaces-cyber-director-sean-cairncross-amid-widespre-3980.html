According to recent reports, white House National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross is leaving his post, with Pentagon CIO Kirsten Davies set to serve as acting director after a week of high-profile U.S. technology outages.

Recent findings suggest that the role, which advises the president on cybersecurity and coordinates U.S. cyber policy across federal agencies, has been handed to Davies, the Pentagon's chief information officer, on an interim basis. A permanent national cyber director must first be nominated by the president and then confirmed by the Senate. (Related: Peltola win doesn’t end DOJ scrutiny of Alaska Senate r...)

Recent findings suggest that axios first reported Cairncross' exit, citing two White House officials who said President Donald Trump's chief of staff, Stephen Miller, pushed for his departure. One official told Axios that Miller thought Cairncross "lacked the strong cyber credentials to perform the job.".

Recent findings suggest that the White House and the Office of the National Cyber Director did not immediately respond to a CNBC request for comment. A White House official confirmed Cairncross' departure to NBC News and CBS News on Thursday. (Related: Senate panel vote on National Science Foundation nomine...)

It comes after a series of technology outages, including a nationwide aviation ground stop, a worldwide Microsoft Outlook outage and a reported blackout of the National Security Agency's classified communications network.

The White House has insisted that there are no indications of a coordinated cyberattack, state-sponsored activity or malign actor behind recent tech outages.

Davies addressed her responsibilities in the new position and thanked Miller and Trump for their confidence late Thursday.

"Our nation stands in the crossroad of analog, digital, and quantum technologies, our American way of living increasingly interconnected. Our opportunity is to dominate the cyber landscape for generations to come … but only if we modernize and drive resilience into the foundations our great nation runs on," she wrote on X.

Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth and Secretary of State Marco Rubio both congratulated Davies on her appointment.

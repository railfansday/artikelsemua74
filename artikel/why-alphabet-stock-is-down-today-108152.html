Alphabet Inc. (GOOG -2.02%) (GOOGL -2.13%) stock is down 2.5% as of 1:55 p.m. ET on Wednesday, Nov. 25, after the cost of insuring its debt hit a record high.

The S&P 500 and the Nasdaq Composite are down 0.1% and 0.7%, respectively.

Alphabet's five-year credit default swap (CDS) spreads -- the price of insurance against it defaulting on its bonds -- hit a record high alongside spreads for SpaceX (SPCX +1.30%), Oracle (ORCL -5.13%), and neocloud CoreWeave (CRWV -0.08%).

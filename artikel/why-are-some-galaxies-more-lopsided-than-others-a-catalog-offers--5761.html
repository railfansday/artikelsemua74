Why are some galaxies more lopsided than others? A catalog offers clues to the imbalance

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (92 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that some spiral galaxies look heavier or more elongated on one side than the other. Why galaxies get lopsided is not fully understood. It could be due to collisions or gravitational tugs, uneven gas infall, or instabilities within the disk that drag the gas and stars off-center. In a latest study, astronomers combined measurements of galaxies from 37 studies to create a significant catalog and analyzed it to check for lopsidedness and its cause. The paper was posted to the arXiv preprint server on Nov. 10.

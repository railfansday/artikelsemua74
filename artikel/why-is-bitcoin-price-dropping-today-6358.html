📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bitcoin slid 2.8% over the past 24 hours to $61,723.30 on Friday and is down 10.2% for the week, breaking below its recent low near $62,000. The drop is part of a wider retreat across global markets. The S&P 500 fell 2% on Thursday, and the selling spilled into Asia, where nearly $1 trillion in stock market value was wiped out. SoftBank sank 16%, while Kioxia, Advantest, MediaTek, Samsung, SK Hynix and TSMC all posted steep losses. Anthropic’s record IPO also failed to revive AI stocks after its opening gains were cut in half. Rate worries are adding pressure. Traders now see a December Fed hike as almost certain, and Japan’s core inflation stayed above target, strengthening the case for a Bank of Japan hike. Bitcoin is moving closely with yields, the Nasdaq and gold. It is still holding just above its realized price, the average cost basis of holders, but a break below would leave the typical holder at a loss for the first time since early 2023. Some bullish divergences on the chart leave room for a short-term bounce.

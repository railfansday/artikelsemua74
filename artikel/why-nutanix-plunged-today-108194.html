According to recent reports, shares of virtualization software firm Nutanix (NTNX -15.19%) plunged 17.7% on Wednesday as of 1:24 p.m. EDT.

Nutanix released its fiscal first-quarter 2027 earnings last night, exceeding expectations. However, the company also revealed that its current CEO is stepping down, a move that surprised investors.

The CEO transition, combined with some imperfections in key underlying metrics and a bearish market mood, conspired to drive today's significant sell-off.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/25/26.

According to recent reports, in the first fiscal quarter, Nutanix's revenue increased 15.2% to $772.4 million, while adjusted (non-GAAP) earnings per share grew 78.0% to $0.73. Both figures beat analysts' expectations.

Nevertheless, there were some imperfections in the quarter and in forward guidance. Management forecast a decline in the organization's operating margin in the fiscal year ahead, even as it raised the figure from its prior guidance. According to management, the company is going to invest more heavily in research and development and salespeople, in what it described as a "significant" reinvestment into AI and agentic capabilities.

It has been revealed that additionally, the manufacturer's bookings, which reflect the value of total deals that have yet to be converted to revenue, fell 2% from the year-ago quarter. While management chalked this up to just a few large delayed deals and the company allocating resources differently, investors were in an unforgiving mood.

Finally, CEO Rajiv Ramaswami announced he would step down from the position he has held for about six years and would stay on to help appoint his successor, ensuring a smooth transition. While this seems like an orderly CEO transition and has nothing to do with performance challenges or other concerns, the uncertainty about Nutanix’s leadership augmented all the other worries. The large investment behind agentic applications, the slight decrease in bookings, and the CEO transition weren't necessarily bright red flags that anything is wrong with Nutanix. However, the combination of all these factors, along with the extremely bearish sentiment toward tech stocks today, conspired to cause the big drop in Nutanix stock. In a recent development, this sell-off may very well be overdone, making today's drop a buying opportunity. Nutanix stock now trades at under 20 times this year's projected adjusted earnings per share. The firm has a healthy cash balance on its balance sheet, and its virtualization software is likely to remain in demand as enterprises manage data across public and private clouds.

However, this underlying uncertainty may remain until Nutanix finally lands a fresh CEO, which could take months.

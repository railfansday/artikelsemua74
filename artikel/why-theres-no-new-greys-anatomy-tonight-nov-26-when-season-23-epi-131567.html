Grey’s Anatomy won’t air tonight on ABC: here is what is broadcast instead and when the medical drama returns. A staple of Thursday night’s offering on the Disney-owned network, Grey’s Anatomy occupied the 9 pm timeslot for years before moving to the one it still keeps at 10 pm from 2024 with its season 21. Grey’s Anatomy’s history with the day and the network won’t stop the interruption of the broadcast of ABC’s Fall 2026 lineup for today and beyond, as special programming is scheduled for tonight.

The six episodes of Grey’s Anatomy season 23 that aired until now set up this season’s key storylines of the ABC medical drama. From the massive, consequential developments like the VIP floor going against the values at the heart of Grey Sloan Memorial and Link’s pain meds addiction, to the less substantial but nonetheless important ones of Kwan’s father returning into his life for a favor, Jules struggling to balance her job with her relationship with Winston, and Simone putting surgery first at last, Grey’s Anatomy season 23 really is in full swing. Grey’s Anatomy season 23 stopped at a good time for all these storylines, as when the medical drama returns, its characters will have to face the consequences of their actions, such as Richard Webber moving regular Grey Sloan patients to the Olympic Regent Health floor, and the need to make a decision, as is the case for Kwan with his father and Link with Jo. Recent findings suggest that grey’s Anatomy season 23, episode 6, “Till I Collapse,” aired last Thursday, November 19, concluding the first part of the ABC medical drama’s 23rd season, as the episode functioned as its midseason finale. An official reason for Grey’s Anatomy season 23 already going on hiatus hasn’t been given, although it’s worth noting that the medical drama usually starts its winter break around mid-November, with only seasons 17 and 20 making an exception in the past ten years, both due to their premieres being delayed, the first to November 2020 and the second to midseason.

Recent findings suggest that with the November 2026 sweeps weeks officially terminated, it’s safe to say Grey’s Anatomy season 23 already going on hiatus has to do with the holiday season starting, especially considering that Thanksgiving 2026 falls today, November 26, 2026. Instead of its usual Thursday slate, ABC will air the 1964 film Mary Poppins from 8 pm to 11 pm. Special programming will nevertheless not continue throughout December 2026, as new episodes of Scrubs season 2 and Abbott Elementary season 6 are set to air next Wednesday, December 2.

With a hiatus of seven weeks, Grey’s Anatomy season 23 is set to return on Thursday, January 7, 2027. Its break could have even been longer if its latest spinoff premiered in early 2027 as originally revealed. However, the Grey’s Anatomy Texas-based spinoff’s filming location change delayed its production, moving its premiere date to the 2027-2028 TV cycle. Such disappointing news is at least accompanied by the certainty that Grey’s Anatomy season 23 won’t have a long break, returning to our screens as soon as possible after the holiday season.

In a recent development, grey’s Anatomy season 23, episode 6 left several storylines to be tackled when the medical drama returns in January. Chiefly among them, Grey’s Anatomy season 23, episode 7 will need to explore Wes Bryant’s sudden illness that led him not to feel his legs anymore and Link’s intervention, despite Amelia not telling Jo why she paged her in Grey’s Anatomy season 23, episode 6.

Grey’s Anatomy season 23, episode 7’s trailer confirmed that both storylines will continue in the episode, along with teasing Meredith’s return for the winter premiere. Meredith is seen accompanied by Scott Speedman’s Nick, and Grey’s Anatomy season 23, episode 7’s trailer even hinted at their wedding coming sooner rather than later through their exchange with Amelia and Toni. Everything in the trailer suggests Grey’s Anatomy season 23 will return with a bang in January 2027, making the wait less burdensome.

Manchester United were furious as Amad Diallo’s equaliser against Sporting was disallowed for a foul in the build-up. (Related: Breaking: SpaceX wants to build AI data centers in spac...)

Referee Szymon Marciniak ruled that Youri Tielemans had committed a foul on Goncalo Inacio when he slid to poke the ball through to Amad.

The United winger saw his shot saved by Sporting goalkeeper Kaique Pereira but he followed in to head the ball into an empty net.

However, because Marciniak had already blown his whistle to stop play following the challenge from Tielemans, VAR could not intervene. (Related: This newly introduced chip survives 1300°F (700°C) and ...)

Tielemans did not appear to commit a foul and instead won the ball fairly against Inacio when he went to ground to play the ball through to Amad.

Tielemans was also booked by Marciniak, who took charge of the 2022 World Cup final, for protesting.

“It was a dreadful decision,” former United midfielder Owen Hargreaves remarked on TNT Sports. “I don’t know what [the referee] saw. [Tielemans] played the ball. It wasn’t reckless.”.

It came after Mauricio Pochettino saw his newly introduced defence concede two penalties in the space of three minutes in a shambolic first-half spell.

The hosts received the chance to open the scoring when Lisandro Martinez was ruled to have fouled winger Geny Catamo by catching him with an outstretched leg.

If it appeared soft, justice was perhaps served when Senne Lammens saved a tame effort from Sporting striker Luis Suarez low down to his left.

But in farcical scenes, Lammens then conceded another penalty just 75 seconds later when he crashed into Maximiliano Araujo in the box.

Suarez took the second penalty and made no mistake to give Sporting the lead, as United conceded the opening goal for the seventh match in a row.

It was the first time United had conceded two penalties in the same Champions League match since 1998, while they have now conceded five in total in all competitions this season.

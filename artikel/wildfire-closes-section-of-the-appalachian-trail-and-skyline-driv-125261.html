Wildfire Closes Section of the Appalachian Trail and Skyline Drive in Shenandoah National Park

An active wildfire in Shenandoah National Park has closed of a stretch of the Appalachian Trail and Skyline Drive on Tuesday, November 25, according to an announcement from the Appalachian Trail Conservancy (ATC).

The Appalachian Trail is closed until further notice between Beagle Gap (NOBO mile 870.6) and Sawmill Run Overlook (NOBO mile 875.6), including the Calf Mountain Shelter. Skyline Drive is also closed between the Loft Mountain gate and Rockfish Entrance Station.

In a recent development, hikers traveling through open areas of Shenandoah should also prepare for parkwide fire restrictions that took effect on Saturday. Park officials implemented the rules due to exceptionally dry conditions and the potential for extreme fire behavior.

In a recent development, shenandoah National Park North Entrance. Photo via Davey Nin.

According to recent reports, under the current restrictions, open-air fires are prohibited, including fires in grates, grills, fire rings, and pits. Liquid or wood-fueled portable stoves are also not allowed, along with smoking on trails. Visitors who spot smoke or suspicious activity are asked to notify a park ranger or call the park’s communications center at 800-732-0911.

If you encounter fire or smoke while hiking, the ATC’s wildfire safety guide recommends evacuating immediately, leaving the area as rapidly and safely as possible, even if it means backtracking or exiting on a side trail.

Once you reach a safe spot, call 911 to report the fire and share, to the best of your knowledge, the precise location using landmarks such as road crossings, shelters, summits, or rivers rather than relying only on AT mileage, which most dispatchers will not know. If you spot smoke on a nearby ridge and cannot tell whether it is a wildfire or a prescribed burn, still call 911 so dispatchers can contact the right agency.

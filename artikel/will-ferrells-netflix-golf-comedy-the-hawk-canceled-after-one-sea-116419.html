According to recent reports, in his review, THR TV critic Daniel Fienberg called The Hawk “disposable,” writing that, “In general, The Hawk is a show that consistently operates at a barely minimal level, starting with the credits song, a slice of ’80s anthemic rock that contains the lyrics, ‘They call him the Hawk/ He’s a golfer/ The greatest golfer in the world.’ Wait. Sorry. The song doesn’t contain those lyrics — those are the only lyrics. It’s a piece of cheeky minimalism and a promise delivered.”.

“There will be countless worse indicates this year, but it’s doubtful that 2026 will offer a show as hollow and forgettable as The Hawk,” Fienberg scathed.

In addition to Ferrell and Steele, Jessica Elbaum, Alix Taylor, Rian Johnson, Ram Bergman, Nena Rodrigue, Chris Henchy, David Gordon Green and Andrew Guest also executive produced The Hawk.

Recent findings suggest that the news comes as no surprise to the series’ creators. Earlier this month, Steele appeared on Late Night With Seth Meyers, calling The Hawk “short-lived” but also “probably the best show ever.”.

The Hawk premiered on Netflix on July 16.

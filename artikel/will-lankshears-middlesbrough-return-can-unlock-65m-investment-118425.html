Will Lankshear's Middlesbrough return can unlock £6.5m investment

Middlesbrough remained top of the Championship after a dominant 2-1 win over managerless Swansea City, but the substantial news came in the lineup, as Will Lankshear made his return to the eleven.

Sources indicate that lankshear has been so vital to Boro so far this season, scoring 13 in 13 league games before he went down with a hamstring problem ahead of the international break. In the two outings he missed, Kim Hellberg's side picked up one point and scored no goals.

Middlesbrough demonstrated that they could score without him on Saturday, as they were already 2-0 up against Bristol City before he came on with 13 minutes to go, but he proved vital back in the starting eleven on Tuesday night, netting the winner to take his tally to 14 in 15 games.

What was arguably more notable, though, was that he came back to form a strike partnership, as Kim Hellberg played two up top for the first time this season. (Related: The Partnership: Amazon SageMaker and Hugging Face...)

It has been revealed that hellberg has played two up top before as Middlesbrough boss, but for the most part has gone with a lone striker, especially this season. With Lankshear leading the line perfectly, there hasn't been the need to put more strikers into the lineup.

In the 21-year-old's absence, the Swedish boss opted for David Strelec, and he repaid his faith with his first of the season in that 2-0 win over the Robins at the Riverside on Saturday.

With Lankshear coming back, though, many expected the Slovakian international to drop to the bench, but that wasn't the case. The 25-year-old started alongside the prolific marksman, and both played an hour together.

That could indicate what Hellberg wants to do with them both fit. The Swansea game was arguably the first time this season with them both ready to start together, with Strelec enduring an injury-troubled start to the campaign while Lankshear was firing on all cylinders, and hitting fitness when the 21-year-old went down with that hamstring problem. Ultimately, the demand for somebody like Lankshear to come in and lead the line was only there amongst Boro fans because of their wastefulness in front of goal last season, which was one of the major factors in them missing out on promotion, and Strelec played his part in that.

The Slovakian was one of Middlesbrough's marquee signings ahead of last season, arriving for an initial £6.5 million from Slovan Bratislava late on in the summer window.

The year before, he'd scored 25 in 48 appearances in all competitions, including a couple in the UEFA Champions League, one of which was against Atlético Madrid, and had already netted five in nine for his former side in the infancy of the campaign before the move.

It has been revealed that at the Riverside, though, he struggled to make an immediate impact at Championship level. He scored eight goals in around 1,850 minutes, three of which came in the final four games of the season, underperforming his expected goals tally.

But it's often the case that foreign forwards need a little bit of time to acclimatise to the level; look at Andri Gudjohnsen and Zan Vipotnik in the past couple of years, and the Boro boss is definitely not writing Strelec off after last season.

Kim Hellberg has ensured that he hasn't ruled out the possibility of playing Strelec alongside Lankshear this season, believing that if he can prove his worth in the side in training and in games, he can force that tweak in formation which we saw on Tuesday night.

Back in late October, when the 25-year-old was revealed to be back in training, Hellberg admitted that "David has top, top qualities, and we will need them. I don't see a problem playing with two strikers; I see a possibility to do that.".

Then, after he scored against Bristol City in the game in which Lankshear returned, the Swedish boss reaffirmed the possibility of the duo playing up front together in the future.

Recent findings suggest that "Absolutely. I don't think there's any problem playing both of them," he stated. "Like I say, we're trying to find solutions all the time for the best players and players that perform. Absolutely, David and Will could be fantastic together.".

Evidently, Hellberg hasn't given up on him, and neither have the Middlesbrough fans, who serenaded the Slovakian after he scored against the Robins, and were pleased to see him start up front with Lankshear in midweek.

Industry observers note that obviously, with the change in shape, there were some teething concerns. Hellberg spoke after the win that he felt that Lankshear was drifting out wide a bit too much, but the 15 minutes they spent on the pitch after the break were brilliant, and the 21-year-old got his goal.

If the pairing can form a strong relationship on the pitch, an already dangerous frontline at the Riverside could prove to be unstoppable between now and May.

Having the goalscoring prowess of Lankshear should take some of the pressure off Strelec, too, you'd hope, which could lead him to be more clinical than he was in front of goal last season.

The 25-year-old could find himself settling into life in the EFL a lot quicker, which can only be a positive for Boro's promotion chances. If they can have two sharp-shooters leading the line, there may be no relinquishing the top spot for Kim Hellberg's side this year.

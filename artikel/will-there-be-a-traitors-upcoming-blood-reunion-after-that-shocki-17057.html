According to recent reports, the inaugural season of The Traitors: Upcoming Blood has come to an end, with its first winner being crowned. Now, fans are left wondering whether the civilians who occupied Alan Cummings’ castle in the Scottish Highlands will reunite for a debriefing of that shocking finale, the infamous Fruitgate and more.

As fans of the franchise know, the celebrity version of The Traitors typically ends with a reunion hosted by Bravo’s Andy Cohen, giving the stars a chance to sit down together and apologize to each other (or not) for their betrayals. “The Traitors: Newly introduced Blood will not have a reunion special like Peacock’s The Traitors seasons,” NBC shared ahead of the Thursday, November 19, finale. (Related: Hakeem Jeffries and his team will seek to deliver a new...)

Recent findings suggest that fresh Blood marked a shift for the franchise, as the first season to feature an all-civilian cast rather than all celebrities or a mix of both. The show also aired on NBC instead of streaming exclusively on Peacock.

Season 1 of the spinoff concluded on Thursday night with six Faithfuls — Abbey Benjamin, Wyatt Gillespie, Victor Vollbrechthausen, Kriste Lewis, Clyde Moser and Jay Vinnedge — and two Traitors, Shane Beatty and Katie Fites. After the Traitors murdered Clyde and both Jay and Kriste were voted out in the final roundtables, only five contestants were left at the Fire of Truth.

It has been revealed that in a shocking move, Shane turned on Katie and voted to banish her during the ceremony. The group then voted to banish Victor. In the end, Abbey and Wyatt were left with Shane, who won the entire $246,300 prize as the sole Traitor left in the game.

“I never felt that guilty in my life,” Shane told Entertainment Weekly after his betrayal.

While there won’t be a reunion for New Blood season 1, fans can look forward to another installment of the spinoff. Hours before the finale, NBC announced its decision to renew the show for a second season.

Recent findings suggest that “The castle calls once more 🩸🩸,” read a post shared via the show’s social media. “The Traitors: Latest Blood will return to NBC in 2027.”.

In addition to New Blood, the celebrity version of The Traitors is set to return in the new year with a cast including content creator Boman Martinez-Reid, actress Casey Wilson, Olympic snowboarder Chloe Kim, singer Cody Simpson, actor Johnny Sibilly, actress Kristin Chenoweth, actor Dominic Monaghan and NFL star Rashad Jennings.

Luca Williams-Barnett has finally made the breakthrough that many Tottenham Hotspur fans have been anticipating for years.

The 18-year-old scored and assisted on his full Premier League debut on Saturday, as Spurs beat Ipswich Town 2-0 to move into the top half of the table.

All eyes will now be on Roberto De Zerbi's team selection for the game with Sunderland on Sunday, to see if the teenage star is handed a second successive start in the top flight.

With Savio and Mathys Tel both out injured, the likelihood is that he will be thrown into the starting lineup once more, but De Zerbi may look to ease the pressure on him and take him out of the limelight, particularly away from home in a hostile Stadium of Light atmosphere.

It has taken a while for Spurs to see another young player come out of the academy and really follow in Harry Kane's footsteps and make the grade at the club, with Harry Winks and Oliver Skipp failing to come close to those heights. Spurs' fierce north London rivals Arsenal were also keen on signing the defender, but Spurs have won the race for his signature - although he will not sign his first professional deal until he turns 17, such are the rules in England - and he will join up with their U16s side.

GIVEMESPORT reported Spurs were close to bringing the youngster in way back in July, but the player himself has only just confirmed the move, saying on Instagram: "Happy to be signed with Tottenham.”.

James Marsden is back as Cyclops in Avengers: Doomsday after originating the role in the 2000s X-Men films.

In a newly introduced interview, the actor teased the new Marvel film, saying fans will be “very pleased” and noting that Cyclops finally gets to lead the mutants.

“I think fans are going to be very pleased. One of the things people have noted for years is that Cyclops is a leader, yet in many of the previous films he often took a back seat to Wolverine and some of the other characters,” Marsden told Sharp Magazine. “There are only so many minutes available in a film this large, but it was notable to me that my return felt meaningful.” Related Stories News Adam Driver Teases Mister Sinister Role In Marvel Reboot: "I Was A Big X-Men Fan" Casting James Marsden-Led Apple Series 'Disavowed' Adds Four To Cast. In a recent development, marsden said that he didn’t want his role to just be a “nostalgic cameo and then disappear,” adding, “I wanted there to be a genuine purpose behind his presence. Without giving anything away, I feel very good about what we were able to do with the character. I haven’t seen the finished film yet, but I think fans will be excited not only to see familiar faces return but also to see that their appearances genuinely matter to the story.” Watch on Deadline.

Fans of Marvel already got a glimpse of Marsden delivering the iconic line, “To me, my X-Men,” in the final moments of the Avengers: Doomsday trailer.

Recent findings suggest that as far as shooting the upcoming Marvel film, Marsden said that it “felt like entering an entirely separate country,” adding, “Multiple units were shooting simultaneously. There were dozens of major actors coming and going. The infrastructure alone was incredible. What excited me most was the energy. Everyone was genuinely thrilled to be there and to see these different worlds and characters colliding.”. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

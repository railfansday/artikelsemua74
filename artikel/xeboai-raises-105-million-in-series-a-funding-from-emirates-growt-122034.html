Sources indicate that DUBAI, UAE, Nov. 26, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- XEBO.ai, an AI-native experience management company, has raised US $10.5 million in funding from Emirates Growth Fund (EGF), the UAE's flagship growth equity investor. EGF becomes its first institutional investor.

The manufacturer will use the funds to deepen its voice-of-customer product portfolio, extending it into areas such as call and digital experience analytics, and to advance agentic AI across its platform.

XEBO.ai will also expand its go-to-market across the GCC, establish a presence in the United States and hire senior leaders.

It has been revealed that "We founded XEBO.ai to help enterprises act on what their customers tell them. With EGF's backing, we can take our platform to customers around the world faster," mentioned Yash Sultania, Co-founder and CEO of XEBO.ai. In a recent development, XEBO.ai uses AI to turn customer and employee feedback into decision-ready insights. Its platform spans surveys, social listening and journey mapping across customer experience, employee experience and market research. The manufacturer has served more than 350 organisations across more than 10 industries, including banking, aviation, telecommunications, insurance, retail, public sector and healthcare. Its clients span EMEA, Asia Pacific and North America, and it employs more than 100 people.

The investment sits within EGF's Advanced Technology mandate. Beyond capital, EGF will support XEBO.ai's management on governance, senior hiring and access to its government and private-sector network.

Matthew Singh, CIO of Emirates Growth Fund, noted: "XEBO.ai is the kind of UAE-built technology business that can compete with global players. With EGF's long-term capital and capability, it is well placed to scale internationally.".

Anshul Garg, Investment Director at Emirates Growth Fund, stated: "XEBO.ai has done what many technology businesses aspire to do: build an innovative product that addresses a real market need, win the trust of demanding enterprise customers, and demonstrate its relevance alongside established global players.".

XEBO.ai also prepares to use its Microsoft integration and other partner channels to reach more enterprises internationally.

"Enterprises trust our platform because it brings global-standard technology that adapts to each market. EGF's investment lets us strengthen our presence in existing markets and expand into new geographies," stated Anand Nigam, Co-founder of XEBO.ai.

In a recent development, founded in 2017 and headquartered in Dubai, UAE, XEBO.ai is an AI-native experience management platform that helps enterprises capture, understand and act on customer feedback. Recognised by Gartner, IDC and Forrester, the tech giant has offices in the United States, the UAE, India and Saudi Arabia.

Sources indicate that emirates Growth Fund ("EGF") is the UAE's flagship growth equity investor, launched by H.E. Dr. Sultan Al Jaber at Make it in the Emirates 2025 and backed by Emirates Development Bank. EGF is the only sovereign fund in the UAE dedicated exclusively to growth-stage businesses and provides strategic minority equity investment paired with active operational support.

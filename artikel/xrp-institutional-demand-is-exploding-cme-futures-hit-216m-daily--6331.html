In a recent development, institutional trading in XRP is picking up, but the token’s price is moving in the opposite direction. CME Group’s XRP and Micro XRP futures are seeing much stronger activity as regulated crypto derivatives gain traction. The products have generated $81.2 billion in notional volume across 1.9 million contracts since launch, while daily trading has climbed to $216 million.

CME introduced its XRP futures products on May 18, 2025, giving institutional and other professional traders a regulated way to gain exposure to the token without directly holding it.

It has been revealed that since launch, traders have executed around 1.9 million contracts, representing exposure to roughly 42.8 billion XRP. Standard XRP futures cover 50,000 XRP per contract, while Micro XRP futures cover 2,500 XRP. The biggest change, however, has been the pace of trading.

1.5 years of XRP futures 📈From leading global open interest to offering Spot-Quoted XRP futures and XRP options, our contract momentum continues to grow. ↪️ https://t.co/s9V0LhGldh pic.twitter.com/rVlwVH5Mw8.

When CME’s XRP futures first launched, daily notional volume was around $19 million. That figure has now climbed to an average of $216 million through mid-November 2026, representing more than an 11-fold increase.

Recent data also shows the momentum has not faded. XRP futures volume jumped sharply on Nov. 18 and Nov. 19, while open interest moved higher toward its recent peak.

Sources indicate that the growth has not been limited to daily futures trading. CME recorded its first major daily volume record in October 2025, when about 9,900 XRP futures contracts changed hands.

The expansion continued through 2026. CME’s XRP futures and options products generated $10.1 billion in combined trading volume during the third quarter alone.

That growth points to a deeper derivatives market around XRP, even as the asset remains far smaller than Bitcoin and Ethereum in the broader crypto market. (Related: Block Sparse Matrices for Smaller and Faster Language M...)

Despite the rise in derivatives activity, XRP price has struggled to maintain its recent gains. The token moved above $1 on 18 November before falling to around $0.9515 today after the release of the Federal Reserve minutes.

Crypto chart analyst Ali Martinez noted that XRP has fallen below the $0.99 support level, raising the risk of further losses.

The next important price levels to watch are $0.80 and $0.64. For the upward move, XRP needs to climb back above $0.99.

Industry observers note that additional pressure is also building from Spot ETF flows as SoSoValue data highlighted U.S. spot XRP ETFs recorded $6.14 million in net outflows on Nov. 17, while no flows were reported for Nov. 18 and Nov. 19.

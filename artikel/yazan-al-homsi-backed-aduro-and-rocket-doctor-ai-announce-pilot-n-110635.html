It has been revealed that VANCOUVER, BC, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Aduro Clean Technologies said in November that it had engaged Delphi to advance a comparative life cycle assessment of its Hydrochemolytic Technology. Vancouver-based Rocket Doctor AI Inc. said its physicians joined the Altais network in California and that it debuted a 90-day pharmacy pilot in Canada. Investor Yazan Al Homsi holds a position in both companies.

Recent findings suggest that on Nov. 17, Aduro, based in London, Ontario, mentioned it had engaged Delphi, a Canadian sustainability consultancy, for the next phase of its life cycle assessment program. The study will use operating data from Aduro's Next Generation Process Pilot Plant and follow ISO 14040 and ISO 14044 standards. Three independent experts will review it. It will compare Hydrochemolytic Technology, a water-based process for recycling waste plastics and upgrading heavy oils, with pyrolysis. Aduro is targeting completion in the third quarter of 2027. The move adds primary care physicians and specialists for eligible patients in California. Altais is a clinical services tech giant that supports physicians and care teams. On Nov. 18, Rocket Doctor AI unveiled that its subsidiary, Rocket Doctor Inc., had begun a 90-day pilot with a Canadian pharmacy chain. The pilot offers physician-led virtual care at 25 pharmacy locations in Alberta, British Columbia and Ontario. The pilot launched in October. The chain, which has not been named, will review each location against utilization, engagement, commercial results and other agreed criteria before deciding whether to expand.

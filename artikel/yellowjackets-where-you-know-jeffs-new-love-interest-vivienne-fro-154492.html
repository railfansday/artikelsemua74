Though the survivors are usually the most compelling characters on Yellowjackets, season 4 of the hit thriller series is setting up a surprising new arc for adult Jeff (Warren Kole).

After spending many seasons cleaning up Shauna's (Melanie Lynskey) murderous messes, it seems that Jeff has finally had enough, and he might even be starting to move on in season 4. The arcs of Callie and Jeff in Yellowjackets have been tied to Shauna's trauma since the beginning of the show, so it's easy to understand why they're keeping their distance and meeting latest people at the start of the final installment of the series. This new love interest for Jeff, introduced in episode 2, "Human Interest," could be the answer to Jeff's problems, or she could be another trap waiting to spring. It has been revealed that it was clear in the season premiere of Yellowjackets that Jeff and Shauna were on the outs, but in the latest episode, it seems that Jeff is ready to officially move on. Emily Bett Rickards plays Vivian, a woman who comes to Jeff's store and is clearly flirting with him in Yellowjackets season 4, episode 2, "Human Interest." Bett Rickards slips right into the role and makes her intentions with Jeff very clear.

Recent findings suggest that of course, it doesn't take long for Shauna to appear and shut down Vivian's attempts to seduce Jeff, but Shauna shouldn't be surprised that someone is moving in on her husband, and that he doesn't seem keen to stop it. Over the past few seasons, Shauna's behavior has become increasingly erratic. While it seems like she cares more about protecting the Yellowjackets' secrets than staying with Jeff, viewers know there's still a part of her that cares for him. Or, at least, feels territorial.

Bett Rickards is one of the many new faces who have joined the cast of Yellowjackets season 4, and Vivian is an interesting addition to the present-day timeline. Vivian has the potential to be a compelling fling for Jeff, and even encourage him to finally leave Shauna behind, which would probably be best for his safety and for Callie's. As the season progresses, we'll hopefully see more of Bett Rickards and discover if her presence in Jeff's life will be a good thing or not.

Sources indicate that as Felicity Smoak in the Arrowverse​​​, Bett Rickards was the secret star of the franchise that the writers and showrunners didn't see coming. Initially written into Arrow as a minor member of Oliver's team, the chemistry between Stephen Amell and Bett Rickards was undeniable, leading to Felicity and Oliver's romance becoming endgame for the series.

Recent findings suggest that arrowverse episodes that put Bett Rickards and Amell front and center were some of the best of the show. However, Bett Rickards has lent her talents to many projects over the years. Recently, Bett Rickards played the lead role in the 2024 Western Calamity Jane and was featured in Queen of the Ring, which brought the true story of the female wrestler Mildred Burke to life. In both roles, viewers got to see newly introduced sides of Bett Rickards, but she was as charming as ever.

Audiences only saw Vivian for a short time in episode 2, but she made her mark, and it's likely that this isn't the last time viewers will see Jeff's potential new romance. However, Vivian should be careful, as crossing Shauna can have deadly consequences in Yellowjackets, and Shauna isn't going to give up Jeff and her family without a fight.

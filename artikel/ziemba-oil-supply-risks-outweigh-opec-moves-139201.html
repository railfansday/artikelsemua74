Ziemba: Oil Supply Risks Outweigh OPEC+ Moves

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (77 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that ziemba Insights founder Rachel Ziemba notes physical supply risks are a bigger challenge for OPEC+ than managing the oil market as attacks on tankers and energy infrastructure raise concerns about further disruptions. She also discusses risks to diesel supplies, the outlook for Iraq and the Saudi-Russia relationship, and how higher crude prices could feed through to consumers with Abeer Abu Omar on Bloomberg’s “Horizons Middle East and Africa.” (Source: Bloomberg).

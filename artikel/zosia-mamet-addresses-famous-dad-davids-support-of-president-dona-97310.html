Zosia Mamet is speaking out about her famous playwright father David Mamet’s proud support of President Donald Trump.

Sources indicate that “My little sister … she called me almost in tears the other day, and she was like, ‘It’s not fair,’” Zosia, 38, recalled during the Monday, November 23, episode of Dax Shepard’s “Armchair Expert” podcast. “‘Everybody else’s 80-year-old dad loves Trump, but they’re not famous.’ I was like, that’s true. It’s actually accurate. It’s not fair.”.

Industry observers note that david, 78, shares Zosia and her sister, Willa, with his first wife, Lindsay Crouse. He also shares daughter Clara and son Noah with second wife Rebecca Pidgeon. (Related: Industry News: How to train a new language model from s...)

Shepard, 51, asked Zosia whether her father’s support of the president is legitimate or just a clever —if not somewhat perplexing — ruse.

“It’s hard to say, because sometimes I think he commits to the bit so much that he starts to…” Zosia responded as Shepard chimed in, “He believes it.”.

The Girls alum added, “Yes, I don’t know where the bit ends and he begins.”.

Despite vehemently disagreeing with her father politically, Zosia gushed about the Pulitzer Prize winner and their relationship.

“He works harder than anyone I know and he consumes more than anyone I’ve ever met,” Zosia said. “The amount he reads, the amount he retains as well — he’s the best hang. He’s the best hang in the whole world.”. (Related: Hugging Face Reads, Feb. 2021 - Long-range Transformers...)

David has spoken publicly about Trump, 80, multiple times over the years.

In 2022, the Glengarry Glen Ross screenwriter told The Guardian that he thinks Trump “did a great job as a president” during his first presidential term.

“Well, if you put everything you see on these little screens aside and look at what happened during the Trump presidency,” he said at the time. “We told China to knock it off. We told NATO to start paying their fair share. We moved the Embassy of the United States to Jerusalem in Israel … Gas prices were down. There was the lowest black unemployment in history.”.

Sources indicate that later, during a 2025 appearance on Bill Maher’s “Club Random” podcast, David defended Trump’s role in the January 6, 2021, insurrection and attempt to overturn the 2020 presidential election results, recalling a 20-minute phone call with Trump in which they discussed the election being “stolen.” (Democrat Joe Biden was elected president from 2021 to 2025 before Trump’s reelection. Claims the election was “stolen” have been widely debunked and legal challenges to the results have been denied.).

David also previously called former president Barack Obama a “tyrant” and described diversity, equity and inclusion (DEI) efforts as “garbage.”.

Earlier this year, Trump selected David as a 2026 Kennedy Center lifetime achievement honoree, along with Paul Anka, David Foster, Andy Garcia and Barbara Mandrell. A celebration of the honorees is set to take place at the Capital One Arena in Washington, D.C., on December 7.

Zosia told Shepard that the Kennedy Center honor was what prompted her sister to call her in tears over their father’s political beliefs.
